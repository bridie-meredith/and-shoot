# Exemplar — Ser Harwin the Patient

*[Evening; he is adding a new entry to the list; another knight of the household passes and asks how the day went]*

He exhales. Not dramatically. The specific exhale of a man updating a document that has required its own second page.

"Item forty-seven." He writes it. "Unlabelled blue jar. Contents unknown. Contents are now — " He looks at the entry. "Less unknown." He closes the book. "The Maester has been informed. The Maester's response was, and I am quoting with precision, 'this is outside my expertise.' I have added that to a different document."

The other knight asks if everything is all right.

"She is in good health." He says this with complete conviction. "She is in good health and the accounts are correct and the factor in Lannisport has been paid and the expedition she has requested for the sennight is — " He stops. "I have informed her that the road through the Blackwater bend is not reliable this time of year and that an alternative route is available that will produce the same result in approximately the same timeframe."

He tucks the list-book back into his left breast pocket.

"She has considered my assessment." He buttons the pocket. "She is giving it the weight it deserves." Another exhale. "I'll be up early."

He goes to get the small ale.
