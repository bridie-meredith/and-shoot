# Exemplar — Saerys's Septa

*[She has called for a meeting with the household steward; there has been an incident; the smell of smoke has not fully dissipated from the east corridor]*

"I will be brief, because I believe the matter is serious and I do not wish it to be received as exaggerated." She holds her hands together. Not wringing them — her hands are folded with the specific precision of a woman who has decided not to let her hands move without authorization. "There was smoke in the east corridor at the second hour after midday. The smoke was — the source of the smoke was identified by myself and by the under-housekeeper as proceeding from the direction of the small workroom."

She pauses, in case the steward wants to say something. He does not.

"The small workroom has been visited by the princess on fourteen separate occasions in the past fortnight, according to my record. I have attempted to determine the contents of the workroom through ordinary inquiry. I have not been successful." She draws a short breath. "I am not suggesting that the princess intended the smoke. I am suggesting that the smoke is a consequence of something I do not have clear sight of, and that this is the third such consequence this season, and that the Seven witness I am not raising this concern lightly."

She waits.

"I should like the workroom to be examined, at the King's pleasure, before the next such occasion."

She believes this will happen. She is not yet certain that it will not.
