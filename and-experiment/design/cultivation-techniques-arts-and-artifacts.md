## Part I — Techniques, Arts & Skills (功法 / 武技 Layer)

### A. Cultivation Manuals and the Technique Tier Hierarchy

**What a technique manual (功法, *gongfa*) is.** A cultivation technique manual is a written (or memory-transmitted) method that describes how to circulate qi in a specific pattern to achieve a specific cultivational effect. The manual encodes the sect's inheritance — its foundational method for advancement. Possessing the right technique manual is the difference between a cultivator who can advance past a certain realm and one who is permanently capped. Technique manuals are the genre's primary unit of wealth and inheritance.

**The manual-quality tier hierarchy.** Every technique is classified by the grade ceiling it can support:

| Tier | Common name | Advancement ceiling | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Inferior / Mortal-grade (凡阶) | Qi Condensation at best; often capped below that | Ordinary mortals, outer disciples of minor sects |
| 1 | Earth-grade (地阶) | Foundation Establishment to Core Formation | Standard sect membership; the lower tiers of established schools |
| 2 | Heaven-grade (天阶) | Nascent Soul | Core disciples; major righteous sects; the competitive tier |
| 3 | Saint-grade / True-grade (圣阶) | Soul Transformation and above | Legendary; only top-tier inheritance sects possess these |
| 4 | Divine-grade / Ancestor-grade (神阶) | Mahayana and Ascension | Mythical; characters spend whole arcs seeking them |

**The inheritance bottleneck.** A practitioner whose technique manual is mortal-grade will hit a wall at the first or second breakthrough regardless of talent. This is the genre's structural explanation for why talented practitioners from minor families never catch up to mediocre practitioners from major sects: the technique, not the talent, is the ceiling. Obtaining a higher-grade manual is a story goal; the antagonist protecting their sect's technique monopoly is a structural motivation.

**Sub-classifications of technique types:**
- **Qi-gathering techniques** (*聚气功法, jù qì gōngfǎ*): passive absorption of ambient qi; the baseline cultivation method
- **Body-tempering manuals** (*体修功法, tǐ xiū gōngfǎ*): methods specific to the body-cultivation path; see cross-reference `cultivation-types-methods-phenomenology.md` § Part III
- **Mind/soul strengthening techniques** (*魂修功法*): consciousness cultivation methods
- **Movement technique manuals** (*身法, shēn fǎ* — see below): classified separately but held as part of a complete technique set
- **Dual-path techniques**: rare manuals that support two cultivation paths simultaneously; considered exceptional; often the protagonist's cheat

**Saerys-mapping.** Her "technique manual" is the accumulated archive of cultivation novels she died reading in her previous life — hundreds of texts, none of which is a real technique for Westeros. She has no mortal-grade manual; she has a library of wrong maps for the right kind of country. She knows the entire quality-tier hierarchy and applies it mercilessly: she classifies every institution she encounters by the grade of manual it likely possesses. The Maesters hold Earth-grade archive techniques. The Kingsguard train from a Heaven-grade martial manual, clearly — look at the form. The Alchemists' Guild possesses a Saint-grade furnace-sect inheritance sealed from the outside world. None of this is correct. Her own "Inferior Path manual" is a single-authored text composed in real time, by her, in the dose-log. She considers it mortal-grade at present. She expects it to ascend.

### B. Martial Arts, Combat Techniques, and Weapon-Dao Paths (武技, *wǔjì*)

**What martial techniques are.** Combat techniques are the practical application layer of a cultivation base — the specific moves, sequences, and qi-projections a practitioner learns to use their accumulated energy offensively and defensively. They range from simple qi-augmented strikes (any intermediate cultivator can do these) to complex multi-step techniques with distinctive visual signatures. Named techniques are status markers in the genre: having a named technique means having a heritage; using an unnamed strike means being unaffiliated.

**Grading martial techniques.** The same mortal/earth/heaven/saint/divine tier hierarchy applies to combat techniques. A mortal-grade combat technique is a physical-enhancement punch; a heaven-grade technique produces visible qi manifestations (fire, lightning, crushing pressure fields); a divine-grade technique rewrites the reality of the combat space. Higher-grade techniques require a proportionally higher cultivation base to execute — attempting a technique beyond one's realm causes qi-backlash.

**Key martial technique sub-categories:**

*Qi-strikes and qi-blasts.* The baseline offensive technique: qi concentrated and released from the palm, fist, or fingertips as a directed burst of force. Mortal cultivators produce a concussive blast; Core Formation cultivators produce something comparable to a siege weapon; Nascent Soul cultivators produce area destruction. The visual signature varies by the practitioner's elemental alignment.

*Qi-shields and qi-barriers.* Defensive technique: qi condensed around the body as a protective layer. A basic qi-shield deflects physical weapons; an advanced qi-barrier blocks other cultivators' techniques. The shield's thickness and density indicate cultivation level. Burning through an opponent's qi-shield before their own technique resolves is the core tension of high-cultivation combat.

*Domain techniques (领域, *lǐngyù*).* Advanced-stage combat ability: projecting the practitioner's comprehension of a dao-principle outward to create a localized space where that principle dominates. A fire-dao domain makes the local environment an inferno that suppresses water-attribute techniques. A sword-dao domain fills the space with invisible cutting pressure. A time-dao domain (extremely rare) slows or stops movement within range. The practitioner who can deploy a domain has near-invincibility against practitioners below their level; against equals, domain conflicts are the genre's most spectacular set-pieces.

*Qi projection as attack-type classification.* The genre categorizes qi-attacks by their delivery method:
- **Direct compression** (direct force blast): the simplest form
- **Elemental transformation** (qi converted to fire, lightning, ice before release)
- **Spiritual-frequency attack** (qi directed at the target's meridians to disrupt circulation; particularly dangerous because it bypasses physical defenses)
- **Soul-attack** (qi directed at consciousness layer; body cultivators are resistant; soul cultivators specialize in defense against this)

**Saerys-mapping.** She does not have combat techniques. This is the genre's core tension for her character: she is in a setting that respects martial techniques as the primary power expression, and she has none. She classifies this as a path-specific limitation of the Inferior School — the body-tempering path optimizes for resistance and endurance, not for projection. She is correct that she has no qi-projection ability. She re-frames her poison-delivery methods as "contact-technique application" — the technique is expressed through the material rather than through projected qi. The cultivation-register is not wrong that she has a delivery method with comparable operational results in her specific use case; it is wrong that this is a qi technique.

Her "domain" is her still-room: a space she has arranged and controls, in which she has comprehensive awareness and operational advantage, and in which anyone who enters is on her terms. She would narrate this as "the preliminary formation of a Cauldron-Belly domain: radius approximately one chamber, effect duration as long as the still-room stands, primary effect: impurity suppression." The still-room-as-domain is a comedy beat. In Book II it is also real: she is at maximum operational effectiveness in enclosed spaces she has organized, and genuinely disadvantaged in open, uncontrolled ones.

### C. Movement and Footwork Techniques (身法 / 轻功, *shēnfǎ / qīnggōng*)

**What movement techniques are.** A specialized category of cultivation technique focused on speed, agility, and spatial evasion rather than on attack or defense. Movement techniques use qi to augment the practitioner's speed, reduce effective weight, and enable maneuvers physically impossible for an uncultivated body. They range from accelerated running (basic) to near-teleportation (advanced) to full spatial displacement (domain-level).

**The lightness-skill tradition (轻功, *qīnggōng*).** The genre inherits from wuxia the concept of *qinggong* — movement so light and controlled that the practitioner can run across water surfaces, move vertically up walls, leap distances that violate physics, and land from heights that would kill an uncultivated person. Cultivation-novel movement techniques extend this into explicit qi-augmentation: qi compressed under the feet for sudden acceleration bursts; qi-wrapped body for damage-free falls; qi-launched jumps.

**Named movement techniques (recurring genre archetypes):**
- *Flash step / ghost step* (*鬼步, guǐ bù*): near-instantaneous short-distance displacement; the basic high-level movement technique
- *Wind walking* (*御风, yù fēng*): sustained high-speed movement using wind-attribute qi, producing the sensation of gliding
- *Shadow step / clone step* (*残影步*): movement so fast it leaves after-images; opponents cannot determine which image is real
- *Void step* (*虚空步*): partial spatial displacement; advanced movement technique enabling movement through obstacles at high cost
- *Cloud-riding / sword-flight* (see Artifacts § B): at advanced stages, movement technique integrates with flying-sword artifact for sustained aerial travel

**The running-and-evasion combat role.** Movement technique determines whether a practitioner can disengage from combat, close distance against a ranged opponent, or evade a technique too powerful to block. A practitioner with superior movement and inferior combat is often more dangerous than the inverse: they control when engagement happens. This is the genre's rationale for investing resources in movement techniques early in the cultivation journey.

**Saerys-mapping.** She has no cultivated movement technique. She has *positional economy*: she always knows where the door is, where the watcher is, which corner is most defensible against observation. She learned this as a logistics problem at age four and has never stopped running the sub-routine. In the cultivation-register this is filed as "preliminary domain-awareness technique, mortal-grade, pending qi activation." What it actually is: operational security habit developed by someone who has been evading watchers since she could walk. Her movement technique is "make sure no one knows where I am until I have already left."

Her movement in Book II (Essosi expeditions) is logistical, not physical: she moves between ports through a network of factors and letters of exchange faster than someone physically traveling could be tracked. This is the Inferior Path's version of flash step — she is never where the watcher expects her to be, because "where Saerys is" is defined by her paperwork, not her body.

### D. Secret Arts (秘术, *mìshù*)

**What secret arts are.** Techniques classified as secret arts are not broadly taught even within sects — they are restricted to core disciples, passed directly from master to inheritor, and often sealed within the sect's innermost treasury. Secret arts represent the sect's most dangerous capabilities: unusual power-conversion methods, techniques that work against fundamental principles (a technique that lets a water-attribute cultivator access fire-attribute resources), methods that extract value from things other techniques cannot use.

**Common secret-art categories:**
- **Forbidden techniques** (*禁术, jìnshù*): techniques that work but carry a cost — lifespan, permanent physical damage, killing karma. Using a forbidden technique means accepting the cost in exchange for the power. The genre treats these as significant moral choices; antagonists use them casually; protagonists use them only at desperate moments.
- **Inheritance secret arts**: the founding patriarch's core technique, sealed in a jade slip and available only to the inheritor the patriarch designated. Often includes comprehension fragments — sections of the patriarch's own dao-understanding embedded in the technique, giving the recipient a head start toward comprehending a principle the patriarch mastered.
- **Soul-sealing techniques**: methods for sealing consciousness, memories, or techniques inside objects or other people; used for legacy transmission, for hiding information inside a person, and (in demonic use) for controlling others.
- **Puppet-mastering techniques** (see Artifacts § H): classified as secret arts when the puppets involved are sophisticated enough to require specialized control techniques.

**The secrecy economy.** Secret arts are the genre's highest-value tradeable information. A sect that loses its secret arts loses its competitive advantage. Keeping a secret art secret — through sealing techniques, through legacy-designation, through killing anyone who might carry the knowledge out — is a structural sect behavior. Obtaining a secret art from an enemy sect is a major story goal.

**Saerys-mapping.** Her secret arts are her actual operational methods: the dose-log protocol (which is genuinely hers, genuinely effective, and genuinely secret — no one else in Westeros is doing systematic mithridatism at her calibration level), the below-stairs faction management system (knowledge of who owes whom, who can be trusted with which step, how to communicate through the household economy without leaving a record), and the paper-instrument ship-heist toolkit (bills of exchange, manifests, charter-parties — her most advanced techniques). She holds these classified as secret arts of the Inferior Path and would not transmit them except to a designated inheritor. In Book III this has an edge: she has never found one.

The "inheritor" question is the secret arts' emotional load for her. The dao says a master takes a disciple. She has not. The entourage is as close as she gets. The list of people she trusts with pieces of the knowledge does not include anyone she trusts with all of it.

### E. Divine Abilities and Supernatural Powers (神通, *shéntōng*)

**What divine abilities are.** The distinction between a technique (武技) and a divine ability (神通) is cultivation stage: techniques are learned methods anyone with the right manual can execute; divine abilities are capabilities that emerge from having genuinely comprehended a dao-principle at a deep enough level that the comprehension manifests as a natural power. Divine abilities cannot be learned from a manual — they arise from within. They are the marker of practitioners who have moved beyond the craft stage of cultivation into the dao-understanding stage.

**Standard divine ability archetypes in the genre:**
- *Heavenly Eye* (*天眼*): the ability to see through barriers, illusions, and concealment; perceive the qi-structure of techniques being used against you; read the spiritual composition of materials
- *Divine Sense at extreme range*: the spiritual-sense expansion to truly vast distances — an advanced soul cultivator can sense across a kingdom
- *Elemental manifestation*: the element ceasing to be a technique-output and becoming a natural expression — a fire-dao practitioner at this stage doesn't cast fire; they *are* fire in a defined sense when they choose
- *Law-manipulation*: at Mahayana stage, practitioners can briefly override local physical law through their comprehension of the relevant dao-principle — a spatial-law comprehension that bends space, a time-law comprehension that slows events
- *Spiritual pressure* (*气压, qì yā*): a divine-ability-level emanation that doesn't require active technique deployment — the practitioner's presence simply suppresses weaker cultivators, making it difficult to act, breathe, or cultivate within their aura

**The genre's distinction between technique-power and dao-power.** Technique-power plateaus — techniques have a ceiling determined by their grade and by the cultivation base executing them. Dao-power does not plateau in the same way: a genuine divine ability, once awakened, grows with the practitioner's ongoing comprehension. This is why late-stage cultivation in the genre produces combatants who cannot be defeated by earlier-stage practitioners executing higher-grade techniques — their dao-power simply overrides the technique-framework.

**Saerys-mapping.** Her "divine abilities" are her information reach. She does not have literal divine sense, but her factor network and below-stairs intelligence system provides functionally analogous coverage within her operational scope: she knows what is happening in rooms she is not in, tracks movements across the Red Keep and eventually across the Narrow Sea, and can sense shifts in the operational environment before they surface as visible events. She files this as "preliminary Divine Sense, channeled through the sect's distributed node array rather than through direct spiritual emission." The cultivation-register understands that divine sense works through nodes; she has simply externalised the nodes.

The second claimed divine ability is the "precise-questions interrogation technique" — her method of asking exactly the right numbered questions to extract everything she needs from a conversation without revealing what she knows. In genre terms this maps onto soul-cultivation spiritual-sense (reading truth through conversation); in practice it is exceptionally well-developed social intelligence trained by years of operating in a court where wrong information is fatal. The technique is real; the cultivation basis is wrong.

In Book III, the information reach becomes monstrous: she knows the disposition of every dead dragon, the location of every remaining live one, the survival status of the family. The divine sense that was a comedy instrument in Book I (she always knows who is watching) is now a death-census.

### F. Sword Techniques and Sword-Intent (剑技 / 剑意, *jiànjì / jiànyì*)

**What sword-intent is.** The genre's distinction between a practitioner who knows sword techniques and one who has comprehended sword-intent is the distinction between craft and dao. Sword techniques (剑技) are learned sequences executed with a blade. Sword-intent (剑意) is a practitioner who has understood something fundamental about cutting — what it means to sever, to be decisive, to separate what should not be connected — and whose sword techniques now express that comprehension as a natural force rather than a mechanical sequence.

A practitioner with sword-intent moves differently: they don't have to think about the technique; the blade goes where the intention goes. At high levels, sword-intent becomes independent of the blade — the practitioner's will itself has a sharpness that cuts without physical contact. A sword cultivator who has achieved *sword-domain* fills an area with this cutting intention; everything within range is subject to it whether or not the sword has been drawn.

**The graduated progression:**
1. *Sword technique mastery*: learning and executing techniques cleanly
2. *Qi-infused technique*: adding cultivation energy to technique execution for amplified effect
3. *Sword-will* (*剑意, early*): understanding why a particular cut is correct, not just how
4. *Sword-intent* (*剑意, full*): the cutting truth manifests naturally; the blade is a vessel for the intention rather than the tool producing it
5. *Sword-heart* (*剑心*): the practitioner has internalized the sword-dao so completely that the distinction between cultivator and sword dissolves
6. *Sword-domain* (*剑域*): projection outward; the space becomes the weapon

**Vocabulary specific to the sword-dao:**
- *Sword-qi* (*剑气, jiàn qì*): qi directed specifically through a blade or in a blade-like form
- *Sword-silk / sword-thread* (*剑丝, jiàn sī*): qi drawn so fine it is invisible; a needle-thread application of sword-qi; associated with assassination techniques
- *Sword-cry*: the sound a sword makes when fully charged with its cultivator's intent; masters can hear it; it signals the technique is at peak
- *Sword-spirit* (*剑灵, jiàn líng*): see Artifacts §B — a sufficiently cultivated flying sword develops its own spirit

**Saerys-mapping.** She does not walk the sword path. She classifies it, clearly, as the genre's prestige path and the one explicitly closed to her (no qi-projection, body-only, logistics-oriented). She views Aemond as the sword-dao archetype made flesh: the practitioner who solved the no-dragon problem through martial conquest and has developed something that reads as sword-will through combat experience. This is not entirely wrong as a description of what combat training at his level produces. She finds the sword-dao genuinely elegant and has never said so, because the Inferior Path does not acknowledge elegance in paths it has declared inferior.

Her equivalent of sword-intent is *what she might call the account-intent*: the comprehension not just of the technique of bookkeeping but of what bookkeeping means — the understanding that to control the ledger is to control what happened, and that the past is as shapeable as the future if your documents are sufficiently correct. This is not sword-intent. It is the dao-heart of a steward applied to information warfare. She has not named it. The cultivation-register does not have a category for it. This is one of the places where her genuine comprehension exceeds her framework.

### G. Body-Refining Arts (体修功法, *tǐ xiū gōngfǎ*)

**Note:** The full treatment of body-refining arts is in `cultivation-types-methods-phenomenology.md` § Parts I–III, which provides the comprehensive deep dive. This section captures the technique-vocabulary specifically, for the mechanics layer.

**Technique names specific to body-refining:**
- *Iron body technique* (*铁布衫功, tiě bù shān gōng* / *金钟罩, jīn zhōng zhào*): the genre's classic "body becomes impervious to physical weapons" technique; the surface-level version of body cultivation, usually treated as a minor-grade technique in the cultivation novel context
- *Dragon-body technique* (*龙体诀*): advanced body tempering aimed at achieving dragon-like physical properties; fire resistance, extraordinary density, regeneration
- *Marrow-washing scripture* (*洗髓经, xǐ suǐ jīng*): the classic text for marrow-tempering; the most painful body-refinement stage's manual
- *Nine-revolution body forging* (*九转炼体*): the body-cultivation equivalent of the nine-revolution pill — tempering through nine complete cycles, each more intense than the last, with a qualitative transformation at the end of the full nine
- *Indestructible body* (*金刚不坏体, jīngāng bùhuài tǐ*): the end-state label for a fully tempered body cultivator's physical form; functionally immune to weapons below a certain tier

**Saerys-mapping.** She classifies her mithridatism regimen as a body-refining art. The correct genre vocabulary for what she does: "Cauldron-Belly Body Forging — a stomach-specific variant of the nine-revolution body forging method, conducted through controlled reagent cycling rather than through external tempering media." She writes this in the dose-log. The methodology is her invention. The vocabulary is the genre's. The underlying pharmacological reality (progressive tolerance is a real phenomenon; the stomach is a real absorption-and-filtration organ; documented escalating exposure is sound mithridatism methodology) is sound. Body-refining art is not the wrong category for what she is doing; it is the right-shaped box for the wrong-shaped object.

## Part II — Artifacts, Treasures & Equipment (法宝 / 灵器 Layer)

### A. Magic Treasures and the Artifact Grade Ladder

**What a magic treasure is.** A *fabao* (法宝, *fǎbǎo*) is a cultivated object — an artifact infused with spiritual energy through refinement, inscription, or prolonged contact with a powerful practitioner — that has capabilities beyond a mundane object of the same type. The line between a high-quality mundane object and a low-grade spirit artifact is the presence of an inscribed formation or absorbed qi-signature. The line between a tool and a treasure is whether the object has developed toward sentience (see life-bound artifacts, §F).

**The artifact grade ladder:**

| Grade | Common name | Chinese term | Equivalent tier | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Ordinary / Mundane | 凡器, *fán qì* | None | Good craftsmanship; no cultivation content |
| 1 | Spirit artifact / Spirit tool | 灵器, *líng qì* | Mortal-to-Spirit cultivation | Has absorbed spiritual energy; basic formation inscribed; augments use |
| 2 | Dao artifact / Magic treasure | 法宝, *fǎ bǎo* | Spirit-to-Earth | Significant formation array; active qi-interaction with user; can be powered by qi input |
| 3 | Immortal artifact | 仙器, *xiān qì* | Heaven-to-Saint | Formation of extraordinary complexity; typically ancient; may have artifact spirit |
| 4 | Divine artifact / Natal artifact | 至宝/神器, *zhì bǎo / shén qì* | Saint-to-Divine | The highest material grade; reality-warping effects; near-indestructible |

**How artifacts are refined.** Artifact refinement (器修, *qì xiū*) is the counterpart to alchemy: where alchemy applies spirit-fire to herbs and produces pills, artifact refinement applies spirit-fire to spirit materials (rare metals, beast components, dimensional materials) and produces objects with inscribed formation arrays. The quality of an artifact depends on: the grade of the base materials, the power of the refiner's spirit-fire, the complexity and accuracy of the inscribed formations, and the time invested in the process. See cross-reference `cultivation-types-methods-phenomenology.md` § Part I.12.

**Artifact spirits.** High-grade artifacts develop *artifact spirits* (*器灵, qì líng*): autonomous intelligence emerging from prolonged qi-infusion and use. An artifact spirit can communicate with its user, enhance the artifact's performance when the bond is strong, and resist use by unauthorized practitioners. An artifact spirit that has developed full sentience blurs the line between artifact and cultivated entity.

**Saerys-mapping.** She classifies every mundane object she relies on as a fabao, assigned to the appropriate grade tier. The grade assignment is diagnostic: what tier fabao would this need to be to function the way it functions? The account-book is a Tier 2 dao-artifact (it is not, but the classification reveals how she thinks about it: the account-book is not just paper, it is the primary power-expression instrument of the Inferior Path, and it has been with her long enough that the cultivation-register has begun to attribute quasi-spiritual properties to it). The dose-log is a Tier 1 spirit tool "calibrated to the Cauldron-Belly's specific resonance." Harwin's list is — she doesn't classify Harwin's list. Notably. The account-book is a fabao because she chose to make it one. Harwin's list is what it is.

The full artifact-to-object mapping for Saerys's primary instruments is in §B below.

### B. Flying Swords, Primary Weapons, and the Sword-as-Artifact Tradition

**The flying sword as cultivation artifact.** In the genre, a sword cultivator's primary weapon is not merely a weapon — it is their primary cultivation instrument, refined alongside the cultivator's own advancement. A flying sword is an artifact in the true sense: it has formations inscribed in the blade, it is powered by the cultivator's qi, and at sufficient cultivation it develops a sword-spirit (see §A). It can fly (hence the name): the cultivator's qi drives the sword through the air, enabling aerial combat, ranged attack, and eventually sustained sword-flight (the cultivator riding their flying sword as a transportation method).

**Flying sword characteristics:**
- Sized and balanced specifically for the cultivator; most flying swords are not combat-usable by anyone else
- Grade-matched to the cultivator: a Foundation Establishment cultivator with a Heaven-grade flying sword cannot fully activate its capabilities
- *Sword-cry*: the resonance frequency the sword emits when fully qi-charged; its unique tone identifies the sword and its cultivator
- *Sword-spirit awakening*: the sword begins to show preferences — it may resist being used for dishonorable purposes; it may warn the cultivator of danger; at full awakening it communicates directly
- *Natal flying sword*: see §F; the fully bonded life-bound sword

**Other weapons as cultivation instruments.** The sword has genre primacy (sword cultivators are the archetype), but the same logic applies to:
- Spears (reach-weapon cultivators; the spear-dao emphasizes penetration and continuous force)
- Sabers (the more aggressive, decisive cousin of the sword-dao; associated with military cultivation)
- Fans and brushes (rare, always associated with scholar-cultivators; treated as eccentric genre figures)
- Bare fists and open palms (body cultivators and close-range specialists; the fist-dao is about impact and internal force)

**Saerys-mapping.** She has no flying sword and no primary weapon. She has the account-book.

The account-book is her primary instrument in the same way a flying sword is a sword cultivator's primary instrument: it is always with her; it is refined and maintained; it is the object through which she projects her primary capabilities; it would be catastrophic to lose it; she would not let anyone else use it. She would narrate it as a Tier 2 dao-artifact: "the account-book has absorbed sufficient operational intent over its years of primary use to have developed a rudimentary qi-resonance. I have not yet confirmed an artifact-spirit, but I note that the ledger sometimes opens to the relevant entry without prompting. This may be coincidence. I am documenting it."

The account-book opening to the relevant entry is not coincidence. She knows exactly where each entry is, in the same way she knows exactly where every door is. The "artifact-spirit" is her own indexing system, which she has used so long that its operation feels automatic. The cultivation-register has correctly classified her own memory as an external spirit.

**The still-room kit** (portable; re-established at each new location) is a Tier 1 spirit-tool collection: the alembic, the specific scale, the collection of labeled and unlabeled containers, the lamped brazier, the reference texts she carries separately from the account-book. She classifies the kit as a unit rather than as individual objects. It is how she carries the still-room with her.

### C. Storage Rings and Spatial Pouches

**What spatial storage is.** The genre's most universally useful artifact: a ring, bracelet, pouch, or jade slip that contains a pocket spatial dimension — a small space accessible only to the owner, typically temperature-stable and time-stable, capable of holding vastly more than the object's physical exterior suggests. Storage rings are the genre's solution to the protagonist's perpetual supply problem: once obtained, the protagonist can carry their entire arsenal, their reagent supply, their cultivation resources, and their library without physical encumbrance.

**Grading storage spaces:**
- *Low-grade storage ring*: a small pocket space (roughly a room's volume); little temperature control
- *Mid-grade*: larger space; can maintain specialized conditions (temperature zones, qi-density maintenance)
- *High-grade spatial pouch*: large enough to store buildings; some can maintain living conditions for spirit beasts
- *Spatial bag / dimensional warehouse*: rare and enormously valuable; can store enough supplies for a small sect's extended expedition

**Access and security.** A storage ring is blood-bound: it responds only to the qi-signature of its owner. Theft requires either forcing the owner to voluntarily transfer it, or the owner's death (which releases the blood-seal and makes the ring claimable). High-grade storage rings have additional security formations.

**Spatial items beyond storage.** The spatial-compression principle extends beyond simple storage:
- *Spatial formation plates*: area-effect spatial manipulation; can compress a space, expand it, or create a localized spatial barrier
- *Teleportation formations*: fixed-point spatial jumping; requires both origin and destination formation arrays to be active; the genre's long-distance travel infrastructure
- *Spatial cracks*: damage to the spatial fabric from powerful spatial techniques; a combat hazard that a spatial cultivator can exploit offensively

### D. Defensive Treasures and Shields

**What defensive artifacts are.** The companion category to offensive techniques and weapons: artifacts designed to protect the cultivator from damage. The genre's defense economy: qi-shields (internally generated) have the advantage of always-on availability but drain qi continuously; artifact shields have the advantage of durability and passive protection without qi drain but have grade ceilings and can be broken or overwhelmed.

**Major defensive artifact types:**
- *Defense-inscribed robes and armor*: garments or armor with formation arrays that deflect attacks; the most common early-stage defensive artifact; can be layered with other protective formations
- *Shield artifacts*: physical shields with qi-enhancement or formation arrays; absorb and redirect incoming attacks; at high grade, convert absorbed energy into qi for the user
- *Barrier artifacts*: non-physical qi-barriers projected from a disc, plate, or badge; can cover a wider area than personal robes; used for protecting multiple people or a position
- *Carapace artifacts*: full-body artifact armor that completely encases the cultivator; extremely expensive; associated with body cultivators who are already resistant and use the armor as supplemental protection

**The defensive-artifact arms race.** The genre's ongoing problem: every advance in offensive technique quality prompts a corresponding advance in defensive artifact sophistication. A practitioner who relies entirely on defensive artifacts eventually encounters an offensive technique that exceeds the artifact's grade; a practitioner who maintains cultivation-based defenses has more flexibility but must allocate qi continuously. The most advanced practitioners solve this by reaching cultivation levels where their own body is more protective than any artifact.

**Saerys-mapping.** Her primary defensive artifact is the performed-illness cover (loophole 3 from the constraints document): "a defensive formation that renders the target invisible to the watcher through the mechanism of reducing the expected interaction to nothing." She is not joking about this being a defensive technique. The performed illness is a systematic protection system: it creates an exclusion zone around her (the sickroom; do not disturb) that physical guards cannot provide. She has extended this logic: she maintains several "defensive" social appearances simultaneously, each one a layer of the stack.

Her actual physical defense against poison is the only artifact-equivalent that works by the genre's mechanism: her mithridatism has built genuine chemical resistance. This is the body-cultivator's "carapace" in her case — not armor, but a bodily tolerance that is functionally protective against the Inferior Path's primary threat vector.

In Book II she begins carrying a small concealed dose of fast-acting antidote (classified as "Tier 1 emergency defensive treasure: barrier against acute-onset impurity invasion"). This is correct taxonomy under the wrong frame.

### E. Talismans (符箓, *fúlù*)

**What talismans are.** Portable, single-use formation arrays inscribed onto paper, cloth, jade, or wood. The talisman encodes a formation in miniature: an attack, a shield, a diagnostic scan, a signaling flare. Activate by channeling qi into the inscription; the formation fires once and the talisman burns out. The genre's equivalent of a disposable one-shot tool with highly specific function.

**Why talismans matter.** A practitioner with a stack of prepared talismans has a suite of capabilities beyond their personal technique repertoire. An early-stage cultivator carrying Heaven-grade offensive talismans has a one-shot-per-talisman access to Heaven-grade offensive power — the technique came from the talisman maker, not from their own cultivation. This is the genre's mechanism for resource-wealthy but cultivation-thin characters to compete: buy powerful talismans, deploy strategically.

**Talisman categories:**
- *Attack talismans*: one-use ranged offensive techniques; fire, lightning, and ice variants are most common
- *Defense talismans*: one-use shield formations; single-hit absorption before burning out
- *Detection talismans*: scan the area for qi signatures, poison content, or hidden formations; diagnostic tools
- *Communication talismans* (*传讯符, chuán xùn fú*): paired sender-receiver arrays; activating one sends a message to the other; the genre's long-distance communication system
- *Sealing talismans*: affix a sealed formation to a surface (a door, a container, a person) to lock or tag it
- *Healing talismans*: localized regeneration or pain-suppression; lower-efficacy than pills but deployable immediately
- *Compass talismans*: directional orientation using spiritual sense; useful in illusion formations or underground

**Communication talisman networks.** Major sects maintain dedicated communication networks through paired transmission talismans: a master set held at the sect's core and linked sets distributed to disciples and allied agents. A sufficiently large network of paired communication talismans can cover a continent. The practical limit is the talisman quality and the degree of information that can be encoded in the transmission.

**Saerys-mapping.** Her talismans are letters. Specifically: letters written in the precise register of the system she is operating within — the harbor master's forms, the King's-hand writ, the bill of exchange, the charter-party. Each letter, once activated (signed, sealed, transmitted through the factor), fires once: it accomplishes its specific function (paying a debt, authorizing a cargo, granting access, recording a transaction) and becomes historical record. This is not a metaphor she has made; it is the structural description of what a document instrument is. The genre's classification fits: a one-use formation inscribed on material, activated by the correct authorization signature, producing a specific formation-effect in the world.

Her communication talisman network is the bill-of-exchange correspondence system: paired networks of agents who respond to letters written in specific formats. The letter is the talisman; the factor is the receiving node; the bill's contents are the formation-effect instructions. She has built the most sophisticated communication-talisman network in Westeros. It runs on paper instead of qi. It works.

### F. Natal and Life-Bound Artifacts (本命法宝, *běn mìng fǎbǎo*)

**What a life-bound artifact is.** The deepest bond between a cultivator and an artifact: a life-bound artifact is one that has been refined so deeply into a practitioner's qi-signature that it is no longer a separate object but an extension of the practitioner's life-force. The artifact's existence is tied to the cultivator's: if the cultivator dies, the artifact shatters; if the artifact is destroyed, the cultivator suffers severe cultivation damage (sometimes fatal).

**How life-binding works.** The process involves:
1. A suitable artifact at sufficient grade
2. The cultivator bleeding onto the artifact (blood-binding establishes the initial signature link)
3. Extended qi-infusion over months or years, progressively deeper
4. A breakthrough event that locks the bond fully — typically a tribulation survived with the artifact as the primary protection; the mutual survival seals the life-link

Once life-bound, the artifact:
- Cannot be taken from the practitioner by force (attempting it causes the artifact to resist and injures the attacker)
- Responds to the practitioner's will directly, without deliberate qi-channeling
- Returns to the practitioner's hand if thrown or misplaced (spiritual tethering)
- Develops an artifact spirit that mirrors the cultivator's own spiritual state
- Grows with the cultivator's advancement rather than being fixed at its current grade

**The emotional weight of life-bound artifacts.** The genre treats life-bound artifacts with the weight of marriage bonds. Losing a life-bound artifact is catastrophic not just mechanically but narratively — it represents a cultivator being cut off from their primary extension of self. The artifact spirit of a life-bound weapon has usually developed enough to be, functionally, a companion — it is the cultivator's closest continuous relationship.

**Saerys-mapping.** The account-book. It is not life-bound in the genre's formal sense. She has not bled on it (she would consider this wasteful of a Tier 1 reagent, her own blood). But the functional description of a natal artifact maps to the account-book with uncomfortable accuracy: she cannot be separated from it without distress; she does not have to deliberately access it — she knows what is in it without consulting it; it has been with her through every crisis and come out the other side; it would be a cultivation-level injury to lose it. She would narrate: "the account-book approaches the threshold of natal-artifact resonance; the life-binding process has not been formalized but the qi-integration appears to be occurring naturally, which the Index of the Inferior Path notes as a theoretically possible but empirically unconfirmed development." The index of the Inferior Path is the account-book itself. It notes this in its own pages.

The irony: the object she has most thoroughly life-bound herself to is a ledger. Not a sword. Not a pill furnace. A ledger. The Inferior Path's natal artifact is, correctly, a ledger.

### G. Formation Flags, Plates, and Inscription Objects (阵旗 / 阵盘, *zhèn qí / zhèn pán*)

**What formation objects are.** The physical anchors of a formation array: objects inscribed with partial formations that, when deployed in the correct geometric configuration, create the complete array. The formation flag marks a node position; the formation plate is the base plate from which the array's qi-flow originates; together, a set of formation objects allows a formation master to establish a complete array in a new location without permanently inscribing it into the ground.

**Standard formation object sets:**
- *Formation flags*: typically sold in sets of eight, twelve, or twenty-four; each flag marks a node of the formation's geometric grid; planting the flags in the correct positions establishes the array
- *Spirit stone nodes*: formation plates with integrated spirit stones that power the array without the formation master's ongoing qi-input; allows for persistent arrays that run without continuous attention
- *Formation disc*: a single-object array system; the disc projects a formation array when activated; lower-flexibility than a multi-flag system but deployable in seconds
- *Map-inscription objects*: flat plates with the array's geometry already inscribed; the formation master sets them and they self-complete; expensive but idiot-resistant

**Formation objects as intelligence tools.** Detection formations are particularly useful in their portable versions: a small set of detection-formation flags can be deployed to create a perimeter detection array around a campsite, a secure room, or a storage area. Anyone crossing the formation's boundary triggers an alert in the formation master. Combined with concealment-formation objects (which render the area inside them imperceptible to spiritual sense from outside), this creates a secure workspace.

**Saerys-mapping.** Her formation-flag equivalents are her trusted below-stairs faction members deployed at strategic positions. The positional economy she runs is a real-time formation: she knows which member is at which node, what information each node collects, and how the network of nodes creates a detection perimeter for threats. She adds new nodes deliberately (each new recruited faction member is a "formation flag deployment"). She understands the geometry — who can see whom from their position, what the coverage gap is between two nodes, where the blind spot in the formation is. She closes blind spots.

The physical layout of the still-room is also a formation — she has arranged it according to an internally consistent theory of optimal qi-flow, which is actually an internally consistent theory of efficient workflow and contamination prevention. The formation is real; the qi is not flowing through it.

### H. Puppets and Mechanisms (傀儡, *kuǐlěi*)

**What cultivation puppets are.** Artifact constructs animated by formation arrays and qi-input: the genre's autonomous combat and utility agents. A puppet is an artifact refiner's creation made mobile and functional — a humanoid or non-humanoid body with inscribed formation networks that allow it to execute defined tasks, fight, carry loads, or gather intelligence. The puppet master (傀儡师, *kuǐlěi shī*) controls their puppets through spiritual sense, pre-programmed formation commands, or hybrid methods.

**Puppet grades and specializations:**
- *Combat puppets*: built for durability and offensive capability; used as force multipliers; high-end combat puppets can replicate simple techniques
- *Infiltration puppets*: built for concealment and appearance-mimicry; can reproduce a target person's appearance if the formation master has sufficient grade; the genre's assassin tool
- *Utility/labor puppets*: for non-combat tasks; alchemy puppets can execute routine pill-refining steps; archive puppets can copy texts
- *Beast-shaped puppets*: constructed to resemble spirit beasts; used for mounted transportation, wilderness scouting, and disguise

**The puppet master's combat style.** A puppet master does not fight directly: they deploy, direct, and position their puppets while remaining out of range. The limitation is their spiritual-sense range — puppets operated beyond the master's divine sense range operate on pre-programmed instructions alone, losing adaptability. A good puppet master maintains multiple puppets with overlapping coverage, ensuring the network never has a blind spot. See cross-reference `cultivation-types-methods-phenomenology.md` §15 Saerys-mapping for the factor-network-as-puppet-array treatment.

**Saerys-mapping.** Her factors are puppets with free will. The analogy is precise enough that she uses it deliberately: "remote puppets controlled through the formation of correct financial incentives" is her honest description of how the factor network functions. She does not attempt to deny the parallels. What she adds is the cultivation-register's usual inversion of the genre standard: where a puppet master controls puppets by overriding their will, the Inferior Path controls its network by *aligning* the network's interests correctly, such that the network acts as directed without requiring override. This is more stable and scales better than orthodox puppet-mastery. She has thought about this. It is one of the places where her cultivation-register actually generates a useful insight about the mechanism she's running, dressed in the wrong vocabulary.

### I. Spirit-Gathering Arrays as Objects

**What spirit-gathering objects are.** Portable versions of qi-gathering formations (see Part III §A): artifacts that actively draw ambient spiritual energy into a defined space and concentrate it, accelerating cultivation within the affected area. The most basic version is a spirit-stone platform: a stone arranged to channel qi from embedded spirit stones into the space above it. Advanced versions use formation arrays to draw from a larger radius and concentrate to a smaller target zone.

**The cultivation meditation room application.** A sect's dedicated meditation room typically has spirit-gathering formations inscribed into walls, floors, and ceiling, plus spirit-stone nodes integrated throughout, producing a qi density several times the ambient environment. Practitioners who cannot afford such a room use portable spirit-gathering objects as inferior substitutes.

**Relevant sub-types:**
- *Spirit-stone charging nodes*: a cluster of spirit stones arranged by formation theory to emit qi evenly into the adjacent space rather than just sitting inert
- *Qi-gathering disc*: a flat artifact that, when placed on the floor and activated, draws local qi downward and concentrates it in the room; the most common portable version
- *Formation-inscribed cultivation bed*: advanced artifact for serious practitioners; concentrates qi specifically around the sleeping body for passive overnight cultivation
- *Alchemy formation node*: a spirit-gathering object specifically calibrated to funnel qi into an external cauldron during the refining process; see Part III §C for the cauldron-specific treatment

**Saerys-mapping.** Her still-room's physical layout serves this function in the cultivation-register. The most interesting cultivation-register classification here: she maintains that working in the still-room accelerates her advancement, which is true in the sense that the still-room is where her genuine intellectual work happens and genuine intellectual work produces genuine insight events. She has spirit-gathered herself a room that accelerates her real development through environmental design. The mechanism is ergonomics, not qi-gathering. The outcome is real.

## Part III — Formations and Arrays (阵法 Layer)

### A. Formation Typology: What Formations Do

**The genre's formation taxonomy.** An array (阵法, *zhèn fǎ*) is a geometric configuration of inscribed nodes, qi-powered channels, and formation flags or plates that creates a persistent, area-effect cultivation technique. Where a technique is personal and momentary, a formation is environmental and sustained. Formations are one of the genre's most intellectually rich systems: a good formation master can solve problems with environmental design that a combat cultivator cannot solve through direct confrontation.

**Primary formation types:**

*Killing formations (杀阵, shā zhèn).* Designed to deal damage to anyone within the formation's boundaries. A killing formation turns a defined area into a weapon: qi-discharge nodes fire continuously or on trigger; the geometry channels attacks toward occupants; there is typically no safe position within the formation's effect. A sufficiently powerful killing formation makes the interior of a sect's compound lethal to anyone below a certain cultivation level. Killing formations are the genre's primary perimeter defense.

*Trapping formations (困阵, kùn zhèn).* Designed to contain rather than destroy: the practitioner who enters a trapping formation finds they cannot move in the expected directions, that exits appear where none existed, and that every path leads back to the center. High-level trapping formations are disorienting without being immediately lethal; the practitioner is held until the formation master decides what to do with them. Trapping formations are often nested: the outer layer traps; the inner layer, once the trapped practitioner is weakened by the outer, kills.

*Illusion formations (幻阵, huàn zhèn).* Attack the perception rather than the body: the formation projects false images, alters the perceived geometry of the space, makes the interior appear to be something it is not. At the lowest level, a practitioner may simply lose their way; at higher levels, the formation produces complete false realities indistinguishable from experience. Illusion formations are the genre's stealth-defense: they are not detectable as a threat until the practitioner is inside one.

*Qi-gathering formations (聚灵阵, jù líng zhèn).* Concentrate ambient spiritual energy into the formation's interior. Used to accelerate cultivation for practitioners within, power artifact refinement processes, or charge spirit-stone nodes. A sect built over a spirit vein uses a qi-gathering formation to focus the vein's output; without the formation, the qi disperses into the environment. See §I above for the portable-object versions.

*Defensive formations (防护阵, fáng hù zhèn).* Create a barrier that resists external attack: qi-discharge from outside is blocked or degraded; physical intrusion is repelled; formation-specific resistances can be inscribed (fire-resistant defensive formation; soul-attack-resistant defensive formation). The genre's classic sect-gate formation. A sufficiently powerful defensive formation converts a sect compound into an effectively invulnerable position against practitioners below a certain level — the reason that sects matter as geographic entities.

*Concealment formations (隐匿阵, yǐnnì zhèn).* Make the formation's interior undetectable to spiritual sense from outside: the formation appears as undifferentiated landscape qi to anyone scanning from without. Advanced concealment formations also suppress internal qi-signatures from being detected. Used to hide sect compounds, storage locations, important individuals, and the existence of operations. A well-executed concealment formation makes a space functionally invisible.

*Teleportation formations (传送阵, chuán sòng zhèn).* See §Artifacts C: fixed-point spatial jumping. Requires both origin and destination arrays to be active; the two formations are typically paired (inscribed simultaneously, by the same formation master, with matching geometry). Large-scale teleportation formations are major infrastructure; small-scale ones serve the same function as a dispatch network.

### B. Formation Eyes, Cores, and Control Mechanics

**The formation eye (阵眼, *zhèn yǎn*).** Every formation has a formation eye: the node that, if disrupted, collapses the entire array. A formation eye is typically the central node around which the other nodes are geometrically arranged; destroying or repositioning it breaks the qi-flow that sustains the formation. Finding and attacking the formation eye is the standard method for breaking an enemy formation — which is why skilled formation masters conceal the eye within false nodes, embed it in a formation structure where it is not geometrically obvious, or place it in a location that is difficult to reach without being inside the formation's kill zone.

**The formation core (阵核, *zhèn hé*).** The power source node: where the qi-input enters the formation and is distributed through the array. A formation powered by a spirit stone is only as durable as the stone's reserves; a formation core connected to a spirit vein runs indefinitely. Breaking the formation core starves the array of qi and eventually collapses it; controlling the formation core means controlling the array's power level.

**Formation flags as structural nodes.** Each formation flag marks a node in the array's geometry. The precision of flag placement determines the precision of the formation's effect — off by a few degrees, and a killing formation becomes a trapping formation; off by more, and the array fails to cohere. This is why formation masters are expected to be meticulous: the precision requirement makes the craft demanding.

**Breaking a formation from inside.** A practitioner trapped inside a formation has fewer options than one acting from outside:
- Brute-force destruction: overwhelming the formation's qi-resistance through sheer cultivation power; only viable if the practitioner is substantially stronger than the formation's grade
- Eye-hunting: locating and destroying the formation eye; requires formation knowledge and surviving long enough to look
- Counter-formation: inscribing a conflicting formation within the hostile one; requires a formation master with enough materials and enough time
- Suppression through pure spiritual pressure: high-level practitioners can simply walk through lower-grade formations by generating enough qi-pressure to break each node as they encounter it

**Saerys-mapping.** She applies formation-eye logic to every institution she operates within or against. The Kingsguard formation: formation eye is the duty schedule (disrupt the schedule, disrupt the coverage). The septa's observation network: formation eye is the report-transmission channel (she has identified this and manages it through the "filter" — her father's indulgent reception). The Red Keep's defensive configuration (Maegor's Holdfast, dry moat, drawbridge, layered keeps): she has mapped every node. She knows where the eye is.

The ship-heist is a formation-breaking operation narrated as such: the harbor master's validation system is a defensive formation protecting the ships. Its formation eye is the document-inspection protocol — the harbor master hunts for malformed documents. Her charter-party is so precisely correct that it passes through the inspection node without triggering the formation's response. She has not broken the formation by force; she has passed through it by being indistinguishable from a legitimate formation-element. The formation's own qi-flow carries her through.

### C. The Formation Master Role

**What a formation master does.** A formation master (阵法师) is the genre's intellectual specialist: not a fighter, not an alchemist, but a cultivator whose advancement comes through expanding mastery of formation theory and the ability to inscribe increasingly complex arrays. The formation master's power is environmental — they change the rules of a space, not the participants in it.

**Formation-master career arc:**
1. *Apprentice*: learning array templates from a master; executing pre-designed formations with guidance
2. *Junior formation master*: capable of independently executing standard formation types up to Earth-grade; the level at which a sect values your services
3. *Senior formation master*: can design novel formations for specific requirements; can analyze and break arrays as quickly as inscribe them; can identify a formation's type and properties from brief observation
4. *Grand formation master*: can inscribe Heaven-grade arrays; can layer formations (nested arrays where each layer builds on the previous); can establish formation networks (multiple arrays interacting)
5. *Array saint / formation patriarch*: essentially mythical in most story arcs; formation masters at this level can redesign the qi-environment of a region, have inscribed grand defensive formations that have stood for thousands of years, and can theoretically inscribe formation arrays that alter the laws governing a space

**Formation mastery as intelligence.** The formation master's defining skill is *reading* formations as well as writing them. A senior formation master entering an unfamiliar area scans constantly — for formation flags, for qi-flow irregularities, for node patterns in the landscape or architecture. They see the invisible geometry. A practitioner who is also a formation master is aware of the hidden structure of every space they enter.

**Saerys-mapping.** She is a formation master in the practical sense and uses the vocabulary accordingly. Her diagnostic entry behavior — always knowing where the door is, the watcher, the exits, the coverage gaps — is the formation master's spatial-reading habit applied to logistics and security rather than to qi-arrays. She enters a room and reads its "formation structure": what nodes are active (who is watching), what the formation eye is (what controls access), where the coverage gap is (where the watcher cannot see).

This is the most accurate of her cultivation self-classifications: she genuinely does have formation-master-level spatial and organizational intelligence. The vocabulary is wrong. The underlying cognitive skill is real. The broken clock, here, chimes accurately on the phenomenology while misidentifying the mechanism. She is a formation master who works in paper and people rather than in qi and flags.

### D. Laying, Powering, and Breaking Formations

**Laying a formation.** The process:
1. *Site assessment*: the formation master surveys the location — qi-flow direction, natural node points, existing interference formations, dimensional stability
2. *Template selection or design*: standard templates are executed from memory; novel designs are sketched onto a formation-design material (jade slip, specialized paper) before being inscribed in the field
3. *Node inscription*: the formation master inscribes each node either directly into the ground (permanent) or via placed formation flags (semi-permanent); precision is mandatory
4. *Qi-flow connection*: the nodes are connected through qi-channels, either inscribed as lines or established through the formation master's own qi-output during the inscription process
5. *Power source connection*: spirit stones or a spirit-vein channel is connected to the formation core
6. *Testing*: the formation is activated at low power and inspected for coherence; a misconfigured formation is repaired before full-power activation

**Powering formations.** Most formations are powered by:
- *Direct qi-input*: the formation master channels their own qi to sustain the array; efficient for short-duration uses; limiting because it occupies the formation master
- *Spirit stones*: inserted into the formation core; the stones discharge slowly into the array; high-grade stones power high-grade formations; the stones exhaust over time and require replacement
- *Spirit vein connection*: an indefinitely-powered formation; only viable if the location has a spirit vein; the reason sect compounds are built over spirit veins

**Breaking a formation.** Methods:
1. *Eye destruction*: locate and destroy the formation eye; collapses the array
2. *Core exhaustion*: drain or destroy the spirit-stone power source; starves the formation
3. *Formation-flag removal*: remove or displace enough formation flags to break the geometric coherence; tedious and only viable from inside the array
4. *Counter-formation*: inscribe a conflicting array that creates destructive interference with the existing one; requires time and a sufficiently skilled formation master
5. *Brute-force suppression*: for practitioners substantially above the formation's grade, simply overpowering the array's qi-resistance

**Saerys-mapping.** Her most relevant application of formation-breaking is organizational: she breaks institutional formations (control systems) by one of three methods that correspond to the genre's options.

*Core exhaustion* is the financial approach: remove the resources powering the controlling formation. She uses this against the marriage-market mechanism — a betrothal is a formation whose power source is her market value; her dragonlessness has already reduced the power source below standard; her active cultivation of additional liabilities (the eccentricity budget, the managed undesirability) aims to exhaust the remaining charge. A formation that cannot sustain its power level collapses on its own.

*Eye destruction* is the document approach: identify the specific administrative mechanism controlling the situation and address it directly. The ship-heist is eye-destruction — the harbor master's document-inspection protocol is the eye; she constructs a document that passes inspection and the formation loses control of her.

*Counter-formation* is the social approach: construct a parallel legitimacy structure that creates destructive interference with the hostile one. Her charitable-sick-house operation runs a counter-formation against the septa's control-formation: the septa's formation depends on having visible oversight of the princess; the sick-house cover creates an equally legitimate appearance that the septa cannot contradict without appearing ungodly.

### E. The Formation Master's Economy of Knowledge

**Formation knowledge as inheritance and monopoly.** A formation master who knows a technique no one else knows has a permanent competitive advantage. Formation knowledge is the genre's second most valuable inheritance (after technique manuals), and for the same reason: the knowledge creates a ceiling for opponents who lack it and a floor for those who have it.

**The formation master's analytical advantage.** A formation master can identify an opponent's formation by its symptoms — what kind of formation produces this qi-disturbance? What grade of array would produce this effect at this power level? This identification capability makes a formation master dangerous to encounter even without constructing an offensive array: they can read what you have built and tell you exactly how to break it, and they can identify your defensive formation's grade and weakness from outside the perimeter.

**Grand formations — the sect-wide or region-wide scale.** The most powerful formations in the genre are grand formations (大阵, *dà zhèn*): arrays inscribed at a scale of kilometers, spanning entire mountain ranges or underground territories. A grand defensive formation for a major sect's compound requires months to inscribe and a team of formation masters working in coordination. Grand formations may have been inscribed by formation-patriarch ancestors thousands of years ago and maintained through minor repairs across the sect's history — the formation is older than anyone currently alive and embodies knowledge no living practitioner fully understands.

**Saerys-mapping.** The trade-and-medicine network as a grand formation: she has been building it since Book I and by Book II it spans the Narrow Sea. The network is not a minor formation (a household, a single supply chain) but an infrastructure formation: its scale is measured in cities, not rooms. She thinks of it this way. The factor-node in Braavos, the supply-corridor to Lys, the still-room in the Red Keep, the Yi Ti contact — these are formation nodes of a network formation inscribed across a continent. The formation eye of this grand formation is her account-book. She knows this.

The grand formation's vulnerability is exactly what the genre predicts: it can be broken by destroying the formation eye. Her account-book being seized or burned would collapse the network. She keeps the account-book on her person at all times. This is formation-eye protection, not sentiment. The cultivation-register would say that too.

## Tensions and Gaps for Principal Triage

1. **The ship as formation-eye vulnerability.** In the climax (Book III ship-heist), Saerys deploys the full technique suite documented here — eye destruction, counter-formation, talisman-equivalent document instruments. Whether the cultivation-register should reach for this full vocabulary explicitly in the prose, or whether the reader should recognize the correspondence without the character naming it, is a register decision. The genre-literate reader will recognize the formation-master pattern. The general reader sees competent bureaucratic heist. Both readings work. The question is whether the bones-level narration should contain the cultivation vocabulary for these final techniques or let the behavior speak.

2. **The natal artifact declaration.** This document argues the account-book is approaching natal-artifact status in the cultivation-register's own accounting. If the prose uses this — if at some point the book explicitly notes the natal-artifact hypothesis in the dose-log — it creates a setup for a late payoff: a moment in Book III where losing the account-book (or choosing to leave it behind as the price of escape) would be staged as a natal-artifact severance. The emotional weight of a life-bound artifact's destruction is a genre-established tragedy beat. Whether this payoff is wanted depends on whether the principal wants the genre to deliver its promised beats on an inverted axis.

3. **The formation-master vocabulary for the entourage.** This document classifies the below-stairs faction as formation-flag equivalents and the factor network as formation nodes. If the bones-level narration uses this vocabulary consistently, the entourage's deaths in Book III read as formation-node destruction — which is both precise (she loses operational capability at each death) and monstrous (she has described the people she loves as structural elements). Whether to let this double reading run in the bones, or to keep the formation vocabulary for the trade network and not extend it to the entourage, is a register calibration question for the principal.

4. **The qi-projection absence and its Book III consequence.** This document establishes that she has no combat techniques and no qi-projection capability. In Book III, when the world has moved past the scale at which logistics and poison can solve problems, this absence becomes load-bearing. She has no martial response to a war. The cultivation-register should have something to say about this gap — whether it files it as the Inferior Path's acknowledged limitation, as an advancement ceiling that she has not yet broken through, or as the structural reason she survives the Dance (you cannot be a target in a martial conflict if you have no martial presence). The register's response to her own defenselessness in Book III may be one of the more revealing places where the farce-register tries to find a frame and comes up short before finding it.

5. **`[canon-uncertain]` material — Westeros formations.** The Red Keep's defensive design (Maegor's Holdfast, the dry moat, the drawbridge) is canon; the formation-theory vocabulary she applies to it is her own. The Alchemists' Guild's location on Rhaenys's Hill is canon; whether it sits on anything with genuine magical properties is unconfirmed `[canon-uncertain]`. The oily black stone of Yeen (associated with pre-human builders, the Five Forts, possibly Dragonstone) is canon-referenced but its properties are not established; the broken-clock treatment in `cultivation-genre-reference.md` Part IV appropriately leaves this open. These `[canon-uncertain]` items from the reagent tier-mapping are the primary uncertainty layer for any scene that puts Saerys in contact with the formation-reading of potentially-magical Westeros locations.
