## The chapter's job (what I.1 must accomplish)

**Dramatic shape: the founding spike.** This is the engine-start. Everything the series runs on is planted
here, and exactly one thread fires.

1. **Found the premise** — locked-inside POV; setting-blind / genre-aware; the two past-life layers (the
   nerd's rote facts; the recent cultivation-bender's tropes); **she is not clever, she is monomaniacal.**
2. **Found the power** — the gift-egg as a *source, not a pool* (GUARD-1 rev-4); sympathetic resonance; the
   real magic / wrong framework seam (the clock starts broken).
3. **Found the armor** — the cultivation cosmology as grief-scar (she has died once; the "dao" is a
   pre-emptive refusal to grieve). The wound is glimpsed **once** and buried.
4. **Found the cost** — R0: the kill that reads as a laugh. The curdle ladder's first rung.
5. **Arm the series** — plant the threads I.5 → III.6 will fire; start the four foil gags; open the
   scholar ladder.

**The one fire:** `FIRE[GIFT:ALICENT]` — the egg, given, becomes the source. (The chapter is otherwise
almost pure plant; it is the opener.)

## Scene-by-scene

> Four scenes, ascending to the R0 spike then landing the reverse-angle. Each carries: **Inside** (Gael's
> locked-in monologue), **Reverse-angle** (the Westeros-native read + channel + 😱/🤨/😂), **Gags / threads**,
> **State**.

### Scene 1 — *the diagnosis* (the locked-inside premise)
- **Where/who:** the crib; the wet-nurse and the nan in attendance; the ordinary-nursery routine running
  (the hour-by-hour day the plot will deviate from — `design/counterfactual-life/`).
- **Purpose:** install the POV and the premise. An adult mind, bored rigid in a newborn that can't yet move,
  works out it has died and been reborn; clocks the gift-egg in the crib **radiating *something*;** concludes,
  on recently-binged-novel logic, that this is an isekai and that thing is a spirit resource. Decides to
  cultivate.
- **Inside:** the breezy cultivation-monologue founds itself — precise in the jargon, wrong about
  everything; the wound glimpsed **once** (she will not attend a funeral, having already attended her own)
  and buried under enthusiasm.
- **Reverse-angle:** 😱 *wet-nurse (gossip):* a babe that never cries, lies open-eyed and rigid half the day
  "like a little corpse practicing"; won't take the night shift.
- **Gags / threads:** ⑥ (frets she's the villain-reincarnator; vows to be righteous) · ② proto (genre-brain).
  PLANT[ISEKAI-AWARENESS], PLANT[REINCARNATION-SICKNESS], PLANT[ACQUISITION-DRIVE] seeded.
- **State:** ARMOR established (the cosmology *is* the grief-scar); the *want* declared (transcend + be left
  alone).

### Scene 2 — *the first draw* (sympathetic resonance; the founding act)
- **Where/who:** the crib at night; the nan finds her mid-act.
- **Purpose:** found the power. She reaches for the egg, resonates with it, bleeds on a facet/chip and
  swallows — and something *answers* (the source seeds). The cultivation cosmology gets its founding
  "proof," which is real (the magic) and misread (the framework). The gift's backstory surfaces via nursery
  gossip: the Queen fought the King to put the egg in a girl's crib.
- **Inside:** she narrates "refining the resonance," certain she has begun the first revolution; she is
  *quoting novels,* not understanding.
- **Reverse-angle:** 😱 *nan:* finds her with bloodied gums mouthing the egg "like she means to eat it,"
  cooing.
- **Gags / threads:** ⑤ (the unsettling calm read as omen). **FIRE[GIFT:ALICENT]** (the egg given → seeds
  the source); PLANT[SYMPATHETIC-RESONANCE], PLANT[SOURCE-NOT-POOL].
- **State:** CAPABILITY-real seeds (the hoarded, real channel begins); the broken clock starts ticking
  (every fake effect will fail on schedule; she'll read each as a breakthrough).

### Scene 3 — *the hatching and the kill* (the R0 spike)
- **Where/who:** the crib; she is alone with it when it happens (the staging that lets adults misread).
- **Purpose:** the founding cost. She draws **harder** → the egg **hatches** → "a monster's in the crib with
  a baby" → she fights it, kills it (a scar, blood), and eats a claw. The R0 act — staged so neither the
  infant nor the reader registers it as a *kill;* it plays as a feral, funny survival beat. (The horror is
  retroactive after III.5.)
- **Inside:** genre-brain frames it instantly — she has *refined her first spirit-beast,* a tribulation
  passed; she is pleased, not horrified.
- **Reverse-angle:** 😱 *the nan, returning:* a comatose babe, a dead hatchling, blood — the only frame that
  fits is **cursed;** two nurses quit. 😂 *a practical guard:* "Good. One fewer dragon for the stables to
  feed." (Shushed.)
- **Gags / threads:** ① (her proud first title unlocks: **"Dragonbane"**) · ⑥ (the self-audit clears her on
  a technicality — it attacked first). PLANT[DRAGONBANE-EPITHET], PLANT[BESTIARY] (she begins logging the
  world by function), PLANT[DEATH-FLAG], PLANT[LESBIAN] (seeded lightly in the monologue's asides).
- **State:** **the cost-ledger opens at R0;** the kill is the first dead-thing→fuel gesture the ladder
  repeats at identical-shot scale to R4.

### Scene 4 — *the aftermath* (the reverse-angle close; arming the engine)
- **Where/who:** the nursery the morning after; the chorus, the maester (offstage, writing), Alicent.
- **Purpose:** land the reverse-angle and arm the series. The omen-frame snaps shut; the household's recoil
  **hands Gael exactly what she wants** — the *because-Westeros* engine fires on the page: superstition →
  **solitude.** The minds that will spend three books failing to catch her take their first reads.
- **Inside:** she registers only that she is being left more alone, and approves (more time for the work).
- **Reverse-angle:**
  - 😱→🙂 *Alicent (the first WTH):* terror at the blood and the dead thing → **flips** to proud "she's
    *strong*" (the mother's-narrative authored over the child who is actually there).
  - 🤨 *Maester Lorren (Citadel letter — scholar-ladder R0):* "A trance-state I can compare only to deep
    study or a greyscale stupor, yet she is in rude health. No diagnosis. I have requested more parchment."
    → travels up to Barth, who shelves it. *Far.*
  - *(structural)* the **staffing-problem** tweak: a new under-nurse will take the dawn watch but not the
    dead hours, "on account of the babe"; within a fortnight nobody takes the dead hours, and the rota has a
    hole shaped exactly like solitude — and no one says *cursed* aloud, because saying it would mean filing
    a report, and filing a report would mean staying late. (Cissa the counter-note: narrates the damning
    afternoon as "good as gold," wrong about the hours and not knowing it.)
- **Gags / threads:** ⑤ (the omen writing its own legend). PLANT[ALICENT-WTH], PLANT[SCHOLAR-LADDER].
  *Jaehaerys (offstage):* his "no egg for a girl" was overruled — the first thing he couldn't control.
- **State:** **SOLITUDE seeds** (the rota-hole); the refusal-arc anchored — she ends the chapter with fuel
  and the beginning of the solitude she craves, the wound sealed under the win.
