# Cultivation Library — Master Index

**What this library is:** Project-scoped authoring substrate for `and-experiment` (cultivation-reincarnation comedy, pre-Dance Westeros). This is the genre layer beneath `comedy-register.card.md` and `saerys-targaryen-behavior.card.md`. Those two cards define the *voice*; this library defines the *well* they draw from — the conventions, cosmology, vocabulary, paths, artifacts, dark paths, metagenre roots, and aphorism bank of the cultivation (xianxia/xiuzhen) genre at precision sufficient to make Saerys's broken-clock comedy land correctly.

**Hard fence (one line):** The broken clock stays broken — nothing in this library makes qi or cultivation magic real in the story; the only things that genuinely work in-story are mithridatism (mundane pharmacology) and uncontrolled ingestion of genuinely-magical Westerosi materials.

## Reading Order / Dependency Note

**Doc #1 is foundational.** `cultivation-genre-reference.md` is the root document — it defines the realm ladder, alchemy, spirit beasts, resources, social order, sects, moral axes, tribulations, the detachment doctrine, standard tropes, and the precision-of-wrongness vocabulary bank. Every other document in the library extends or cross-references it; do not read any other doc without reading doc #1 first.

**Docs #2–7 extend specific domains of doc #1** and are peer-level with each other. Each carries its own broken-clock mappings and candidate-card flags. Reading order within this tier:

- **#2 before #4 and #5:** Doc #2 (types/methods/phenomenology) covers the cultivation path taxonomy and body-tempering mechanics that docs #4 (techniques/artifacts) and #5 (cosmology/dark paths) assume.
- **#3 can be read independently** after #1; it covers the Westerosi side of the register-collision rather than the cultivation genre's internal structure.
- **#6 (metagenre/roots)** should be read after #1 and #2; it operates one level up from the in-world cosmology and requires familiarity with the genre conventions it taxonomizes.
- **#7 (aphorisms)** is the most standalone; it can be consulted in isolation once the voice is understood from #1.
- **#8 (maester's cabinet glossary)** is the granular apothecary-inventory layer — read #3 first for the load-bearing/magical substances; this doc is the exhaustive companion covering the full mundane and semi-magical cabinet. Standalone for any still-room / sick-house / poisoning scene; no prerequisite beyond knowing the broken-clock premise.

## Consolidated Open Rulings / Principal Tensions

Deduped across all seven docs. Items that recur across multiple docs are marked with the count of source docs; those are the high-priority calls.

### High-Priority (recur across 3+ docs)

**T-1: The oily black stone's actual properties and what happens if Saerys eats it.** *(Recurs in docs #1, #3, #5 — highest-priority call)*
Three options: (A) she never eats any — the horror is the appetite, not the consumption; (B) she eats a fragment; nothing happens; the clock stays broken; (C) she eats a fragment; something real happens — requires principal ruling on what. Option C has the most narrative weight but requires canon-uncertain commitment; Option A preserves maximum ambiguity. This decision gates the Book III ending's ambiguity and how to write the Yi Ti arc.

**T-2: The human-cauldron trope's explicit presence in prose.** *(Recurs in docs #1, #4, #5)*
How explicitly the human-cauldron's genre connotations should surface in the bones-level narration: latent (genre-literate reader catches it without statement; general reader sees horror without the name) vs. explicit (the narrative names the shape). Both readings work. Principal ruling determines the register calibration for Book III bones.

**T-3: The Daenys-as-dao-companion archetype vs. her Dance death.** *(Recurs in docs #1, #6)*
The genre archetype demands a sacrifice that triggers the protagonist's advancement. Daenys's death is political and arbitrary (the Dance; a war Saerys opted out of), not a cultivation-structured sacrifice. Does the narrative use the archetype's logic — the cauldron trying to metabolize a grief that does not metabolize — or land it as simply outside the register? Currently the blank-line scene implies the latter. Ruling needed before Bk III chapter-level bones.

### Medium-Priority (recur across 2 docs)

**T-4: The demonic-path isolation consequence — explicit or latent.** *(Docs #5, #6)*
The demonic path's standard cost (practitioners lose allies at accelerating rate) maps exactly onto what the Dance does to her found-family. Is this convergence explicit in the bones narration, or left as a structural undercurrent the reader supplies?

**T-5: Realm-ladder vs. project-tier-system reconciliation.** *(Docs #1, #2)*
The genre's nine-stage realm ladder and the project's Tier 0–4 structural scaffold are not formally mapped to each other. Should they be? (Tier 0 = Qi Condensation; Tier 1 = Foundation Establishment; etc.) Affects how the impersonator narrates her "advancement" claims at each book boundary.

**T-6: The gluttony-path framing for Book III.** *(Docs #2, #5)*
The gluttony/devourer path (§21) is the genre classification that fits what Saerys becomes by Book III. Should this surface explicitly in the prose (the narrative names the shape) or remain latent (the genre-literate reader recognizes it; others see the horror without the name)?

**T-7: Soul-erosion vs. blank-line interpretive question.** *(Docs #5, #6)*
Two available readings for the blank-line scene: (a) the heartless-dao practitioner's one-unclosed-attachment reading — it is specifically Daenys, the anomaly; (b) soul-erosion — the soul's structure is sufficiently eroded that the cultivation-register processing fails. Both readings are compatible but produce different emotional registers (grief vs. body-horror). Which is primary?

**T-8: The wildfire-fever as genuine magic vs. severe toxic reaction.** *(Docs #1, #3)*
Did the wildfire fever change something in Saerys's body beyond mithridatism-baseline? Three options: (A) changed only her mithridatism baseline — real but mundane; (B) changed something about her relationship to fire-magic — ambiguous, never confirmed; (C) the blank register rather than a registered change. Option A is most consistent with the broken-clock architecture.

**T-9: The dragon-egg ingestion as foundational ambiguity.** *(Docs #1, #3)*
Did eating the powdered cradle-egg produce any real effect? If yes: what? If no: the comedy is clean. The principal's ruling determines what the Book III beat can do with the identical-shot callback to this first moment.
