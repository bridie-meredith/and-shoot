## BOOK I — *The Inferior Path*

### I.1 · a-baseline
**Age:** infant–1yr. **Beat:** A dying reincarnated infant (mind/brain mismatch, seizures). Her mother
**Alicent the Queen**, against the withheld blessing of the King — her father **Jaehaerys** — slips a
*living, warm dragon egg* into the cradle to save her. She feels it *radiate*, clocks the impossible
silver-and-purple around her, and concludes she's in a magical world and must cultivate. The air is
dead to her sense; she needs a **source**, a baseline. She mimics the egg (nothing), can't crack it,
then chips a fragment and swallows it (a flicker). Insight — energy is **sympathetic**: a chip stands
for the whole. Another chip, a smear of *her own* blood, willing part = whole — and it **takes**.
Real source-radiance seeds in her (she calls it a "frequency"). The egg goes cold (drained; reads as
a dud → her dragonlessness) and is taken away. The infant lives, "a bit touched."
**Register:** SPIKE (loudest, so the III.8 silence reads). **Rung:** R0 (laugh now / horror later).
**Plants:** PLANT[NOTE] (Jaehaerys's verbal indulgence, aftermath) · PLANT[CAULDRON] · PLANT[SYMPATHETIC-RESONANCE] · PLANT[ACQUISITION-DRIVE] · PLANT[REINCARNATION-SICKNESS] · PLANT[GRIEF-REFUSAL] (death-refusal, glimpsed once) · PLANT[GIFT:JAEHAERYS] (he dotes on the miracle child).

### I.2 · the-pantry-heist  *(toddler bridge)*
**Age:** ~2–3. **Beat:** Barely walking, betrayed by her own baby hands. Her sense snags on the one
thing in the maester's stores that *glows* among inert jars — a genuinely-magical reagent. Her first
**heist in four beats**: SIGHT (the source — and that no one guards anything against a toddler) → PLAN
(an immortal mind running an op on toddler assets: the maester's rounds, the alibi of clumsiness) →
IMPLEMENT (toddles in, takes it by climbing/nerve) → REWARD (ingests it). Then the **OH SHIT**: it's
far more potent than she gauged — an uncontrolled breakthrough tears through a too-small body, nearly
fatal, nearly flaring into the open. She scrambles, frightened, to bury it.
**Register:** SPIKE→CHILL (the private rehearsal of the I.6 public scare). **Rung:** off-ladder.
**Plants:** PLANT[HEIST-METHOD] (sight→plan→implement→reward) · PLANT[MAESTER-MARK].
**Fires:** FIRE[GRIEF-REFUSAL] (death-refusal re-fires — she will not die again).

### I.3 · the-collector-princess  `[KEYSTONE]`
**Age:** ~6. **Beat:** Indifferent to the smallfolk (mere "mortals"), she pours her princess-privilege
into the only thing that matters — **getting materials**. She founds her **acquisition apparatus**: a
private workshop + first import line (cover: the eccentric scholar-princess), and brings the maester
(her mark since she could walk) to heel as supplier and screen. She runs a systematic **assay** — get
a thing, test if it *registers*, log it — including dosing herself with poisons (they don't register;
she builds real immunity anyway and files it "tempering"). Top of the want-list: **weirwood** and the
**pyromancers' fire-craft**. The empire is born here as a *side effect of her hunger*.
**Register:** HOLD→SPIKE (the maester out-administered by a six-year-old). **Rung:** R0.
**Plants:** PLANT[LEDGER] (account-book only she reads) · PLANT[FACTOR] (first bill of exchange — exotic-materials import) · PLANT[BELOW-STAIRS] ("the little master") · PLANT[DOSE-LOG] (self-assay → mundane immunity).
**Fires:** FIRE[ACQUISITION-DRIVE] · FIRE[MAESTER-MARK→dependent] · FIRE[NOTE→standing-license].

### I.4 · the-laundress's-boy  *(warm root — re-homed off charity)*
**Age:** ~6. **Beat:** A servant's child is dying. She runs no charity and tells herself she cares
nothing for mortals — yet *this* one is in front of her, and she finds she cannot watch a child die.
Three sleepless nights of **mundane apothecary craft** — the right tisane, an antidote, cool cloths;
*not* cultivation (she never spends energy outward, GUARD-1 rev-3) — and he lives. She files it
"karmic hygiene / cultivation merit." The reader sees the human under the armor — the single exception
that proves the rule, which she lies to herself about.
**HINGE established:** her craft **cuts both ways** — the same still-room knowledge that mended this
child is the knowledge that quietly kills (mundane, not a "healing hand"). Pays at I.7.
**Register:** RECOVER (lowest-volume scene — the warm floor the horror is measured against).
**Rung:** off-ladder. **Plants:** PLANT[WARM] (rootability anchor) · PLANT[HINGE].

### I.5 · tempering-the-cauldron  *(the materials TOUR)*
**Age:** ~6–7. **Beat:** The assay in full swing — a systematic tour of every material she can get,
each "tempering the cauldron" (the anti-Arya reveal: poison & pills, never a sword). The joke has a
TRUE floor: genuinely-magical things **work** — a chip of **weirwood** smuggled from a godswood (a
small heist of its own) registers, and *strangely* (old-gods magic behaves unlike fire-magic — a clue
she misfiles as "grade/purity"); mundane things (dung, a pebble) give nothing. Helaena (her younger
**sister**) bond deepens; riddle-twins as equals.
**Register:** SPIKE (comic high of early Book I). **Rung:** R0 sustained.
**Plants:** PLANT[NO-EAT] (pre-justifies the Bk II no-eat list) · PLANT[HELAENA] (the warm spot).
**Fires:** FIRE[CAULDRON] (retro-frames the antics) · FIRE[ACQUISITION-DRIVE] (the weirwood get).

### I.6 · the-furnace-sect  *(the PYROMANCERS)*
**Age:** ~7. **Beat:** The named target lands: she reads the Alchemists' Guild — the **pyromancers** —
as "a furnace sect" and goes after their fire-craft / wildfire. Something nearly burns; she eats a
shard of *real* magic → a 3-day "tribulation" fever → surfaces uncannily changed. For the first time
the adults are **scared, not charmed** — the warm cage shows its bars.
**Register:** SPIKE→CHILL (first CHILL — recovers but marked). **Rung:** off-ladder.
**Plants:** PLANT[BLACK-STONE-PRECEDENT] (the "ingest real magic → real, wrong effect" template; pyromancer contact opened).
**Fires:** FIRE[BROKEN-CLOCK-A] (the genuine ring that pays out and *isn't* funny).

### I.7 · the-impurity-expelled  `[FIRST-PEST]`
**Age:** ~6–7. **Beat:** A meddler moves to expose her workshop raids. He doesn't die loudly — a
lingering flux, slow recovery, a posting far from court, and he **never knows**. Saerys files him "an
impurity the cauldron expelled." The craft that kept the laundress's-boy alive (I.4) is now,
demonstrably, the craft that kills — the same still-room, turned.
**Register:** the prose shows the flux plainly (no straight-man). **Rung:** R2 (¾ comedy / ¼ chill).
**Plants:** PLANT[DE-PESTING] (the cold de-pesting reflex; rhymes to II.8).
**Fires:** FIRE[HINGE→R2].

### I.8 · impure-reagents  *(ward-child — re-homed to her household orbit)*
**Age:** ~7. **Beat:** A sick child in her household (the laundress's-boy echo) dies despite her
elixir. She files it "impure reagents" — and the locked POV lets one un-meme-able image slip: *she
remembers being the one who died, once* — then buries it. The literal account-book becomes the one
place a death is ever **entered**.
**Register:** RECOVER-INTO-COLD. **Rung:** R1 (ward-child filed cold — made personal). ARMOR: **first
visible seam** (the wound shows to the reader, still sealed to her).
**Plants:** PLANT[GRIEF-REFUSAL] (the pre-emptive-refusal mechanic, surfaced — fires at III.8).

### I.9 · the-fourth-bill  `[AGENCY-PATCH]`
**Age:** ~8. **Beat:** Reconciling her books she notices the loop — factor + maester + below-stairs
have compounded her bills into three unauthorized routes. First read: cosmological. Two pages later,
the turn — she sees the *machine*, and signs a fourth bill she doesn't need, **to feed it**. Accident
→ intent. The procurement network becomes a thing she *chose*.
**Register:** SPIKE. **Rung:** R0 (pure engine).
**Plants:** PLANT[EMPIRE] (the reagent multinational seed).
**Fires:** FIRE[LEDGER] (now a steering wheel) · FIRE[FACTOR→scaled].

### I.10 · the-sealed-parchment
**Age:** ~8–9. **Beat:** The furnace-fever fallout matures; the septa prepares to cloister her. She
talks her doting father — **the King, Jaehaerys** — into a *sealed* grant: "the princess shall have
what she requires for her good works." The warm cage hardens into a tool; she stops being his daughter
and becomes his auditor.
**Register:** HOLD. **Rung:** —.
**Plants:** PLANT[ORPHAN-SEAL] (this exact seal → III.1 orphaned → III.10 heist) · PLANT[GIFT:JAEHAERYS] (the gift at its final warm form, before the father-king spend).
**Fires:** FIRE[NOTE→sealed].

### I.11 · paper-wins  `[LOCK I · rung 1]`
**Age:** ~9 (BOOK I CLOSE). **Beat:** The septa holds a *true* accusation; Saerys produces the sealed
parchment; the master key overrules every chaperone below the King. **Paper wins.** Legally
ungovernable — and she thinks she achieved a breakthrough. Her toddler heist-method, executed full-dress
for the first time: sight → plan → implement → reward, at the scale of the court.
**Register:** SPIKE (comic apex + mechanism dress-rehearsal #1). **Rung:** R0 (triumphant farce; the
descent has not begun — the book boundary holds).
**Fires:** FIRE[NOTE→Lock-I] · FIRE[HEIST-METHOD] (Lock I, rung 1).
**Handoff → Book II:** the note can't reach past King's Landing; rarer materials need her own supply
lines → the roads open. Instrument: the I.3 bill of exchange. Appetite: the cauldron, always wanting a
higher grade.
