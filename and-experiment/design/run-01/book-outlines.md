# Three-book outline — run-01

One cohesive work in three books. Built from `treatment.md` + `constraints.md` +
the 122 AC asset set. Baseline locked (all swappable): **Saerys Targaryen**, youngest
of Viserys I & Alicent (b. ~116 AC), setting-blind reincarnator, dragonless,
idiot-savant, gay, locked-inside POV.

**Scale:** drafted as three ~27k books = one ~80k cohesive work (three linked
novellas). Each is structured so it can grow to full-novel length without re-architecting.

---

## THE COHESIVE LARGER ARC — why three books are one book

**The meta-question:** *Will the immortal ever come down and be mortal?*
Saerys spends three books building higher and higher walls against attachment and
grief — first a delusion (cultivation), then an empire (logistics), then forbidden
power (deep magic). Each book is the same refusal at a larger scale, and the larger
arc is whether the war finally breaks through it. It doesn't. She runs. The "larger
book" is a **tragedy wearing a comedy's clothes** — and the open door at the end (the
voyage) is the unanswered question of whether seeking and fleeing are the same act.

**The three rhymes that bind the books:**
1. **One method, three locks (escalating).** She solves every problem with a ledger.
   - Book I climax: paperwork/logistics defeats **the household** (she beats the *cage*).
   - Book II climax: logistics defeats **distance and the world** (she beats *scarcity*).
   - Book III climax: logistics defeats **the Crown, customs, and — in her head — death**
     (the bookkeeping ship-heist).
2. **The relationship ladder — gain, then lose.** Book I gives her the *family*
   (Viserys, Helaena, Aemond as foils). Book II gives her the *chosen family* (the lover,
   the entourage). Book III is the war taking everyone she was given in I and II — the
   people she gained are precisely the people she loses.
3. **The reagent / geography / tone ladder, in lockstep.** Tier 0–1 = the Red Keep =
   farce (Book I). Tier 2–3 = the world = adventure-with-teeth (Book II). Tier 4 = the
   forbidden edges + a sky of dead dragons = horror-then-cold-escape (Book III).

**The through-line joke, stated once:** the system trusts tedious paper over people, and
she is the only one patient enough to weaponize the tedium — against her septa, against
the harbor master, against mortality itself.

## BOOK I — *Spirit-Beast Essence*
**Span:** age ~3–9 (≈119–125 AC) · **Tier 0–1** · **the Red Keep** · **PURE FARCE**
**Engine:** soft-walls cage (ditch the septa) + *eccentric-royal latitude* ("it's the
dragon blood") + the **charity keystone** seeded.

**Movement 1 — The Heretic in the Cradle.** The inciting inversion: given a dead
cradle-egg and a replacement to *pray over nightly* (canon: Rhaena's beat), Saerys
doesn't pray — **she grinds it.** Establish the locked-POV gag (we hear the immortal's
monologue; the world sees a feral toddler gumming the holy thing). The antics ladder:
Dragonpit dung heist, weirwood shaved off the Kingsguard's Round-Room table, "meditating"
through lessons. The **confoundment engine** runs — maesters say prodigy-or-pica, the
Faith smells the demonic, Otto files her, **Helaena just nods along** (the one person who
finds her normal). Viserys is *delighted*; his love established as the warm cage.

**Movement 2 — The Sick-House.** The keystone clicks: she opens a **charitable
apothecary/sick-house** — unimpeachable piety that launders time, money, and supply lines
at once. She apprentices a maester for lab access and defeats his disapproval by becoming
indispensable to his accounts (*stewardship-as-foothold*). The first elixirs that *seem*
to work (broken clock). She starts building a below-stairs **faction, not a bribe list**.

**Movement 3 — The First Chill / the Furnace.** She gets her hands on **wildfire** and it
"gives someone fits"; something important nearly burns. She eats a shard of real magic,
burns three days with a "tribulation" fever, and surfaces subtly, uncannily *changed* —
the first time adults are **scared**, not charmed. The myth grows teeth.

**Foils:** Alicent (horrified mother), Otto (asset-filing grandfather), Aemond (the
dragonless brother who solved it by *conquest* — and now watches her), Helaena (the warm
spot), Viserys (the indulgent note that overrules the household — she clocks it as a
master key).
**Cost ledger:** the household stops finding her funny; the septa demands her removal;
the first person is hurt by her recklessness.
**Climax (lock #1 — beats the cage):** when the wildfire stunt threatens to get her
cloistered, she doesn't beg — she gets **a note in the King's hand** ("the princess is to
have what she asks for the sick-house") and the master key **overrules every chaperone
below him.** She has, on paper, made herself ungovernable.
**Handoff:** she resolves to build her *own* means and stop needing anyone's permission —
which means reaching past King's Landing. The roads open.

## BOOK II — *The Long Roads*
**Span:** age ~9–13 (≈125–129 AC) · **Tier 2–3** · **Essos → Yi Ti / Sothoryos** ·
**ADVENTURE WITH TEETH (the comedy takes its first wound)**
**Engine:** *factors & agents* (she sends men with papers; her body stays accounted-for) +
*performed seclusion* (a "sickly princess, not to be disturbed" who is actually weeks at
sea) + the **bill-of-exchange network** funding it invisibly. Precocity is a feature — the
adult-mind savant builds fast.

**Movement 1 — The Factor of the Dragon.** The sick-house matures into a realm-spanning
**trade-and-medicine network**; she becomes the reliable buyer everyone trusts (the trust
she'll later spend). The **entourage assembles** — a found-family of competent, exhausted
warriors babysitting a princess who strolls into mortal peril convinced she's invincible.
The **dragonrider girl** enters and stays: the one bond Saerys can't refine, transcend, or
meme — love as the thing the armor can't cover. Marriage-talk hardens into a real betrothal
threat (dragonless, at a discount, to someone worse), giving the love a blade over it.

**Movement 2 — The First Expedition.** She leaves (seclusion alibi) for a far reagent:
shade-of-the-evening in Qarth, Lysene poison-craft, a **Yi Tish herb** — the xianxia-
adjacent golden empire, where she feels eerily, wrongly *at home*. The adventure plays as
comedy until it doesn't.

**Movement 3 — The Wound.** A **bodyguard who believed in her dies** for an ingredient —
the cost made flesh, the first death the comedy can't meme away. She *refines the grief
into resolve* instead of feeling it (the tragedy, not the cure). She brushes the **deep-
magic frontier** — a whisper of the **oily black stone** (Yeen, the Five Forts) that even
her cultivation brain finds *wrong*.
**Foils:** the entourage (chosen family), the lover, distant Aemond hardening into the war
to come; in the background the **Green/Black fracture** tightens and Viserys ails.
**Cost ledger:** a life; the first crack in the immortal act; the lover now has everything
to lose.
**Climax (lock #2 — beats the world):** she pulls off the network's masterstroke — a
deal/extraction that no one of her rank, age, or sex should be able to execute, done
entirely through factors, bills, and forged-perfect paper across an ocean. She has, on
paper, made herself a power independent of dragons.
**Handoff:** she returns home rich, changed, and subtly colder — to a King's Landing that
is a held breath. The last page: **Viserys is dying.**

## BOOK III — *The Field of Corpses*
**Span:** age ~13–15 (≈129–131 AC) · **Tier 4 + the curdle + the escape** ·
**TRAGEDY / HORROR → COLD ESCAPE**
**Engine:** every loophole and instrument she's built, turned against the Crown itself
while the world ends around her.

**Movement 1 — The War She Never Studied.** Viserys dies; the shelter is gone; the Dance
erupts *around* her — the war she skipped the lessons for. She doesn't understand the
politics and doesn't try; she sees only that the realm has stopped supplying her. The
factions (Green by birth, neither by temperament) both reach for her — **Otto/Alicent**
want the asset; the Blacks want the medicine and the money. Her neutrality is no longer
allowed to exist.

**Movement 2 — The Windfall / the Forbidden Reach.** **Dragons begin to die** — her
lifelong reagent shortage solved by a national apocalypse; the windfall is everyone's
catastrophe, and harvesting a battlefield of dead dragons is where the farce **curdles to
horror.** Driven past the edge, she reaches at last for the **forbidden deep magic** (the
oily black stone / beyond-the-Wall ice-magic) — and the comedy is *gone*.

**Movement 3 — The Bill Comes Due.** The losses land at once: her **father** (already
gone), the **lover rides to war** (dragonriders *die* in the Dance), an **entourage member
falls**, **Helaena** — the warm spot — is broken by the war (canon gives Helaena a terrible
end; we orbit it). The question she's dodged since the cradle finally lands: *is ascending
alone over a field of corpses worth anything?*

**Climax (lock #3 — beats the Crown, customs, and death):** she does not break down and
mourn. She does what she always does — **she cooks the books.** She **steals an oceangoing
ship by paperwork**: the laundered hull that's "been the Crown's for a year" (mechanism A)
plus a **letter of marque** that lets her *repossess* it for a manufactured debt (mechanism
D). She never lays a hand on the ship; she takes it the way a steward balances a book, while
the men hunting a thief stare at a document more perfect than anything they've ever filed.
**The ending:** she sails off the edge of the map **without consent**, entourage in tow,
toward the deep-magic horizon she's chased all along (Sothoryos, Ulthos, the lands the black
stone came from). The unsanctioned voyage *is* her refusal of grief, dramatized — she will
not stay and be mortal; she absconds toward transcendence and leaves the wheel of dynasty
over the horizon. **Whether that's seeking or fleeing is the door left open** — and the
launch-pad for the voyage she always wanted.

## How this maps to the and-shoot pipeline (handoff)
"Three books forming one cohesive work" maps cleanly: **one series → three books**, each
book a chapter-set. At reverse-derivation, this doc becomes the **series chunk** (the
cohesive-arc section) + **three per-book deltas** (the book outlines), with the
acquisition/relationship/tone ladders as the series **state-axes** seed (transcendence↔
attachment, agency↔duty, the cost ledger of mortal lives spent).

## Swappable knobs
- **Title** (Book and series), **Saerys's name**, **the lover** (OC vs. Baela/Rhaena).
- **Scale:** three ~27k books, or expand any to full-novel length.
- **Book II's far reach:** Yi Ti vs. Sothoryos as the primary destination (or both, split
  across two expeditions if Book II grows).
- **How dark Book III goes** on Helaena and the harvesting-the-dead imagery.
