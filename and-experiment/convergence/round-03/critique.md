## 2. Guard audit — ALL THREE HONORED

### Broken-clock — HONORED
The Cauldron-Belly does NOT make her cosmology true. The outline is disciplined: it labels every
*real* effect as mundane-or-magic-material and keeps every *fake* effect failing on schedule.
- Only two things "work," both flagged in-text as exactly what they are: (a) **mithridatism =
  mundane pharmacology** (Bk I M2 dosing, lines 82–89; Bk III M1 failed assassination, lines
  263–274 — "twelve years of mundane mithridatism; she knows it is realm-tier immortality"); (b)
  **uncontrolled effects from genuinely-magical ingested materials** (the wildfire-shard fever, lines
  102–107, flagged "magic materials, not qi"; the black-stone appetite, Bk II M2 / Bk III M2).
- The *fake* effects fail on cue: the Yi Ti "immortality herb" is **expensive tea** and does nothing
  (lines 199–205), her cope intact ("the potency is latent"). The Yi Tish poisons do exactly what
  toxins do — "the one thing here that actually works" — while she credits her imaginary realm.
- She stays wrong about WHY throughout: every result is read as a cultivation breakthrough; the
  reader sources it mundanely. No beat implies her qi-theory is literally correct.
- **No FAIL condition triggered.** The clock stays broken; she is right twice a day and never knows
  which time it is (lines 272–274, exactly the guard's framing).

### Idiot-savant — HONORED
Lethality is walled into the gut and still-room; she stays a moron everywhere the plot and court
actually weigh.
- Competence is real and quietly lethal only in the unwatched domains (Tension check, lines 471–483:
  "she can kill a meddling under-septa and cannot read a room").
- She remains catastrophically incompetent at court, marriage (betrothal threat she barely registers),
  dragons (Aemond's martial answer is the road she explicitly does not take), succession (the only
  Targaryen who doesn't know there's a crisis), and war (treats two armies as a shipping nuisance,
  lines 259–262). The "last person in any room to grasp her own power" gag is preserved (line 130).
- Poison-competence is kept *low-body-count and cold*, never flashy, never a win-condition against a
  real antagonist (it "cannot touch Otto, the Greens, the war, or grief," lines 477–479). So it does
  not read as a power fantasy and does not make her too generally competent. The comedy-of-impotence
  lives exactly where it did before.
- **No FAIL condition triggered.**

### Curdle tie-in — HONORED
De-pesting via poison reads as the *same coldness* that feeds the gain-then-lose curdle.
- The single genuinely-new micro-beat — the Bk I M3 "first pest" (lines 108–114) — is deliberately
