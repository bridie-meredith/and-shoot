# Round 1 — GENERATOR C · LENS: STRUCTURE / CAUSAL SETUP-PAYOFF

*Chapterizing `round-03/fusion-v2.md` as a causal machine. Every chapter is a gear: it either
plants an instrument or fires one. The three per-book Locks are engineered backward from their
climax chapter and pre-planted at least one chapter (Lock III: one book) earlier. The three rhymes
— one-method-three-locks · gain-then-lose curdle · reagent↔geography↔tone ladder — are tracked per
book. Book II's Lock is the explicit dress-rehearsal for Book III's heist; the heist's four
mechanisms (A laundered hull · D marque-repossession · the orphaned seal · re-tasked crew) are
distributed across Bk III chapters so the finale **invents nothing.***

Convention: **PLANT[id]** = setup deposited · **FIRE[id]** = payoff collected. Curdle ladder rungs:
**R0** eggshell (laugh) → **R1** ward-child filed as reagents (laugh less) → **R2** the pest poisoned
(stop laughing) → **R3** Harwin balanced in the book → **R4** dead-dragon field harvested calm (horror).

## BOOK I — *The Inferior Path* (10 chapters)
**Tier 0–1 · Red Keep / Maegor's · PURE FARCE (spike-and-recover, T4) · age 3–9.**
**Lock I engineered backward:** the septa moves to cloister her → she defeats a true accusation with
the King's-hand note. So Ch.1 must plant the note, Ch.2 must plant the still-room/ledger, and the
note must be *cashed* (sealed) the chapter before Lock I fires. Book I's whole job: install every
instrument II–III will fire.

### I.1 — "the-christening-spoon"
**Movement:** M1, the grind-and-swallow / Cauldron-Belly declared.
**POV beat:** Saerys (3) grinds the dead replacement cradle-egg with a christening spoon and eats the
powder, lecturing inwardly on "refining the spirit-beast into the cauldron of my own belly across nine
revolutions"; the nursery sees a feral baby eating the holy relic.
**Mechanics:** the funeral she refuses (cause: she already attended her own) → the desecration that
declares her stomach a furnace. Viserys, delighted, indulges her.
**PLANT[NOTE]** Viserys's verbal indulgence ("give the child what amuses her") — the seed of the
master key. **PLANT[CAULDRON]** the self-label *Heavenly Demon Cauldron-Belly*. **PLANT[HELAENA]**
Helaena nods along — the one who finds Saerys normal.
**Curdle:** R0 (the foundational laugh). **DN:** —

### I.2 — "the-charitable-princess" `[KEYSTONE]`
**Movement:** M2, the sick-house keystone + poison-path + ledger.
**POV beat:** Saerys opens a charity apothecary — piety none can object to — and apprentices the maester
for lab access, conquering him by fixing his books until he can't function without her.
**Mechanics:** charity cover (cause) → unsupervised hours + supply lines + an account-book only she
reads (effect). She raids the trivially-easy still-room for nightshade/hemlock/monkshood/Lysene tears.
**PLANT[LEDGER]** the unreadable account-book. **PLANT[FACTOR]** first bill of exchange to a Braavosi
drug-agent (medicine + poison, one chain). **PLANT[BELOW-STAIRS]** the scullery faction ("the little

is formalized into a standing license she trades under.
**Curdle:** spike-and-recover farce. **DN:** DN-2 (cost-ledger cadence introduced).

### I.3 — "the-laundress's-boy"

**PLANT[WARM]** the reader's 80k-long root for Saerys. **PLANT[HINGE]** the poison hand and the healing
hand established as the *same hand* — the curdle's hinge installed beside the warmth.

## BOOK II — *The Long Roads* (10 chapters)
**Tier 2–3 · Essos → ONE far reach (Yi Ti) · ADVENTURE WITH TEETH (T4) · age 9–13.**

## BOOK III — *The Field of Corpses → The Open Door* (10 chapters)
**Tier 4 + curdle + escape · the forbidden edge · TRAGEDY/HORROR → COLD FLIGHT · age 13–15.**
**Lock III engineered backward:** she cooks the books to steal a ship — **(A) laundered hull · (D)
manufactured debt + letter of marque · the orphaned King's-hand seal · re-tasked crew.** The finale
(III.10) must invent nothing, so each of the four mechanisms is *staged into being* in an earlier
Book III chapter (or carried from Bks I–II). The blank line (T1) lands III.8; the curdle hits R4 at
III.5 (dead-dragon field); casualty ⊆ gift is collected across III.6–III.8.

**POV beat:** Viserys dies; the shelter and master key are gone; the Dance erupts around her. The

**Mechanics:** the signer dies (cause) → the seal becomes unrevokable, *enabling* the final heist
(effect). Both factions reach for her; neutrality ends.
**FIRE[ORPHAN-SEAL]** the I.9 seal becomes the orphaned license — planted two books prior, now armed.
**FIRE[VISERYS-DYING]** the II.10 last page pays off. **FIRE[GIFT:VISERYS]** the father, gone, un-mourned.

**Movement:** M1 cont. — the gallows-gag + idiot-savant guard, war-as-shipping-nuisance.

**PLANT[BROKEN-ROUTE]** a real route the war breaks → the ship-owner's undelivered goods → the

### III.3 — "the-insulting-grade" `[MITHRIDATISM PAYOFF]`

## Lens rationale (STRUCTURE / causal setup-payoff)

I built each book backward from its Lock, then forward from its plants, so the outline reads as a
machine with no orphaned parts. Three structural commitments drove every chapter choice:

1. **The three Locks are the same mechanism at rising audit-difficulty, each pre-planted at least one
   book early.** Lock I (I.10) defeats a *septa* with a note planted I.1 and sealed I.9. Lock II (II.10)
   defeats an *Essosi clerk* with a kit assembled II.4–II.9. Lock III (III.10) defeats *the Crown +
   customs + death* with instruments planted as far back as I.9 (the seal). The audit-difficulty climbs
   household → ocean → Crown; the *method never changes.* That is rhyme #1, made load-bearing across 30
   chapters.

2. **Book II's Lock is engineered as the literal dress-rehearsal so Book III's finale invents nothing.**
   II.9 assembles bill + manifest + factor-chain; II.10 fires them at half-stakes (a cargo, a clerk).
   Then every Book III heist mechanism is *staged into being in its own earlier chapter* — A in III.4,
   the orphaned seal in III.1, the manufactured debt across III.2→III.9, crew re-tasking on the II.9
   toolkit — so III.10 is pure collection, not invention. The reader has seen the trick work twice
   before it matters.

3. **The curdle ladder and casualty ⊆ gift are tracked as explicit FIRE chains.** Every casualty in
   Book III (Viserys III.1, Harwin already spent II.8, Helaena III.7, Daenys III.8) was a gift entered on
   a named earlier chapter's gift list. The one gesture climbs R0 (I.1) → R2 (I.6) → R3 (II.8) → R4
   (III.5) at identical-shot scale, so the horror is *revealed,* never switched on. The blank line (T1,
   III.8) is engineered as the *failure of the II.8 balancing beat* — the one account she cannot close —
   and DN-1's absent register is placed in exactly one scene, returning in III.10 to prove it was the
   break, not the new tone.

The orphaned King's-hand seal is the spine's longest causal arc: PLANT[NOTE] I.1 → sealed I.9 →
FIRE[Lock I] I.10 → orphaned III.1 → FIRE[heist] III.10. Three books, one prop, no orphan.

## Self-flagged weaknesses (where the structure bias costs the plan)

1. **Character-as-domino (the central risk of this lens).** Because I engineered every casualty as a
   pre-planted gift firing on schedule, the deaths can read as *clockwork rather than tragedy* — Harwin
   "had to" die in II.8 because II.7 charged it; Daenys "had to" die in III.8 because II.3 planted her
   want. The setup→payoff table is so clean it risks making the people feel like instruments the plan
   spends on cue. **Mitigation owed to Lenses A/B:** III.6 must give Daenys a scene that feels like *her*
   pursuing *her* life, not the plot loading the gun; II.8's wound must land emotionally before it lands
   structurally. My table proves the causality is sound — it does *not* prove the deaths feel inevitable
   rather than scheduled. That's a B-lens job.

2. **Tone-monotonicity / over-engineered descent.** My curdle FIRE-chain (R0→R2→R3→R4) is so legible
   that the macro descent risks reading as a *staircase you can see the top and bottom of from chapter 1*
   — predictable, joyless before its time. I placed the spike-and-recover beats (Yi Ti comic apex II.5,
   the gallows-gags III.2) but I treated them as *structural relief valves,* not as comedy with its own
   momentum. **Cost:** the comedy cadence (T4 / criterion 5) is the one thing this lens under-serves;
   chapters can each "have a laugh" on schedule and still not be *funny.* Defer the actual register-
   rhythm to Lens A — I've guaranteed the curdle *position* of every chapter, not its *aliveness.*

3. **Front-loaded Book I instrument-installation may starve it of standalone scenes.** Six of Book I's
   ten chapters exist primarily to *plant* (I.1, I.2, I.4, I.8, I.9). A pure-structure read risks Book I
   feeling like an elaborate setup lobby rather than a book with its own dramatic spine — the FROZEN
   pure-farce energy could thin into a checklist. I.3 (warmth) and I.6 (the pest) are the standalone-
   strongest; the rest may need A/B to give them present-tense life beyond their plant-function.

4. **Heist mechanism #2 (crew re-tasking) is the one mechanism not planted a full chapter early** — I
   resolved it onto the II.9/II.10 rehearsal toolkit and assembled it in-scene at III.10, which honors
   "invents nothing" only if the reader accepts ship's-articles as already-established. If a critic reads
   it as a finale-convenience, the fix is a one-line plant in III.4 (re-task a real crew for the laundered
   hull's plague run) — flagged, not yet placed, to avoid over-stuffing III.4.

5. **The agency-patch (T3, I.8) carries heavy structural load in a single chapter.** It must do
   accident→intent *and* set up the II.1 empire-discovery *and* stay comic (she's last to understand her
   power). Three jobs in one chapter is a density risk; if it cracks, the timeline-solve credibility
   (the whole "she didn't build it, she discovered it" defense) wobbles. Watch in fusion.
