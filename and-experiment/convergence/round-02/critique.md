## 2. Blocker audit

A blocker is retired only if the beat on the page does the job — not if the checklist claims it.

**BLOCKER 1 — the one break-scene (blank ledger line).** GENUINELY RETIRED. Bk III M3, "The one break."
The beat is not just asserted; it is mechanically constructed: Saerys has carried a *pre-drafted*
ledger-line for "if Daenys dies" since Book II (the armor's pre-emption made literal), so the blank is
the failure of a specific prepared instrument, not a generic pause. The register's absence is the event;
the re-seal ("she closes the book") is the choice that keeps the meta-question live. Works.

**BLOCKER 2 — the agency-patch beat.** GENUINELY RETIRED. Bk I "The agency-pivot." Two-stage read
(cosmological → mechanical) plus the deliberate fourth bill. The draft also harvests it as comedy ("she
is the last person in the room to understand her own power"), which is the T3 ruling's own grace note.
The beat does the work the ruling demanded: she becomes an operator on-page, not just a beneficiary.

**BLOCKER 3 — the Saerys-rootability beat (active warmth).** GENUINELY RETIRED, with a caveat I rule
below. Bk I M2, "active warmth": three sleepless nights nursing a laundress's boy who is *explicitly*
neither cover nor reagent — "he is not pious cover and not a reagent." The construction is exactly
right: she does the warm thing, the cope mislabels it as karmic hygiene, and the reader sees the real
motive under the cope ("she just couldn't watch a child cough"). This is active warmth at cost for
someone who can give her nothing. The beat exists and works. (Sufficiency-across-80k is a residual risk,
ruled in §4 — not a reason the beat fails to retire the blocker.)

**BLOCKER 4 — de-literalize the ledger.** GENUINELY RETIRED. The fix is structurally sound: grief-as-
accounting survives as *voice and behavior* (balances the book, files the dead, closes accounts) while
the literal entries are folded into a *real working instrument* — the household account-book that
launders the hours (Bk I) and the hull (Bk III), i.e. the same object that runs the empire and powers
the heist. There is no thesis-prop on a shelf for the reader to watch the author hold; the one literal
"enter the death" beat survives exactly once (the break-scene), which by rarity reads as climax, not
wink. This is the cleaner solve — the ledger is now load-bearing machinery, not symbol.

**BLOCKER 5 — Daenys's interior want.** GENUINELY RETIRED. "The lover" section gives her a want that is
*hers* and that mirrors the thesis: found a Velaryon cadet branch in her own name — the mortal mirror of
Saerys's immortality bid (both bids against being forgotten). Crucially it has *its own scenes* (courting
a marriage-alliance, banking a separate fortune so she is no one's asset, riding to war partly as
leverage for legitimization). She is "becoming someone when the war takes her, not waiting in a slot."
This both retires the blocker and deepens T2 — the blank line costs because Saerys lost a person mid-arc,
not a counter-argument.

All five blockers retired with a real beat on the page. The self-reported checklist is, in this case,
accurate — I verified each against the body, not the claim.

  planted ≥1 book before firing (note M1→Lock I; bill-of-exchange M2→Bk II; orphaned seal Bk I→Lock III;
  oily-black-stone Bk II M2→Bk III M2; Bk II Lock as explicit dress-rehearsal for Lock III). No orphan
  setups; casualty⊆gift enforced mechanically.
- **Drama — PASS.** Real stakes (betrothal-blade, the war, the dead); cost ledger is legible (gift list
  opened/grown/spent across all three books); curdle is EARNED via the four-scale identical gesture and
  the Harwin death chargeable directly to her vanity. The blank line is a genuine on-page armor-failure.
  (The "too quiet" risk is real but a tuning note, not a fail — see §4.)
- **Comedy — PASS.** The engine is the renewable register-vs-stakes mismatch, not a finite bag of bits;
  T4 licenses intra-book spikes so Bk II isn't starved; Bk III converts to gallows-voice ("same
  sentence, opposite effect") so the engine survives the curdle by changing function, not switching off.
  This is the one criterion both A and B individually failed; the fusion passes it.
- **Theme — PASS.** The meta-question lands as live: the armor demonstrably *can* crack (break-scene)
  and she *chooses* to re-seal it (closes the book), and the ending refuses to settle seeking-vs-fleeing.

  master-key is loophole #1 verbatim; sick-house is keystone loophole #2; heist instruments are the

  Helaena's canon-orbiting terrible end are both canon-true; Yi Ti is a legitimate far-Essos reach.
- **The three rhymes — PASS.** (1) one-method-three-locks: septa→ocean→Crown, the same document-beats-

Six of six PASS. Zero WEAK, zero FAIL.

## 4. Residual-risk ruling

The draft self-flagged five; I rule each decisively.

1. **Break-scene silence is hard to execute (absent register).** DRAFTING NOTE. The outline guarantees
   placement, the pre-drafted line, and the re-seal — everything an outline *can* guarantee. Whether the
   silence reads as genuine crack vs. magician's pause is pure prose execution, provable only in draft.
   Record it; do not hold the outline hostage. Carry forward.

2. **Cost-ledger may be too quiet / curdle leans on Harwin.** DRAFTING NOTE (with a pre-pinned patch).
   This is the one I scrutinized hardest, because the cost-ledger is B's most-praised mechanic and
   under-featuring it could cost the curdle a rung. But: the four-scale gesture is intact and the
   load-bearing curdle rungs (egg, ward-child, Harwin, dragon-field) all survive — the blocker-4 fix
   removed the *symbol*, not the *behavior*. Whether Book II needs one additional visible balancing beat
   is a calibration the prose layer can measure and the draft already names the exact fix (one more
   balancing beat in Bk II, no re-literalization). That is a tuning dial, not a structural hole. DRAFTING
   NOTE — carry the named patch forward as a watch-item for the bones stage.

3. **Daenys's cadet-branch want is canon-adjacent and could overgrow.** DRAFTING NOTE. A bastard
   founding a legitimate Velaryon line in the Dance era is plausible (legitimization authority exists in-
   world); the outline keeps it deliberately light to avoid competing with the spine. "Thin vs.
   restrained" is a prose-weighting call. Not a convergence blocker — the want exists, has its own
   scenes, and mirrors the thesis, which is all the blocker required. Record the overgrowth/thinness
   watch.

4. **Out-charactering guard is a single Bk I beat against whole-arc-warm Daenys.** DRAFTING NOTE. The
   ruling (BLOCKER 3) asked for *at least one* beat of active warmth; the draft delivers it plus the
   locked-in coping voice as continuous counterweight. "Is one beat *enough* across 80k" is explicitly a
   draft-provable question (does reader allegiance stay with the armored lead), and the draft pre-pins
   the patch (a second active-warmth beat in Bk II before Harwin dies). The ruling is satisfied at
   outline level; sufficiency is a prose measurement. DRAFTING NOTE — carry the pre-pinned Bk II patch.

5. **Yi Ti carries the Tier-4 forbidden-magic hinge on one seeded scene.** DRAFTING NOTE, leaning toward
   the most-watched of the five. This is C's own un-retired flag: the oily-black-stone payoff (Bk III M2)
   rests on a single Bk II M2 seed, which may be thin for that much register-shift. But it is *not* a
   convergence blocker: the seed exists, the payoff exists, and the causal link is on the page — the
   question is purely whether one seed carries enough weight, which is a setup-density tuning call
   resolvable at the bones stage by thickening the seed (e.g. a second glancing reference in Bk II or
   early Bk III) without touching the single-far-reach decision. It does not break structure, and
   thickening a seed is a bones-stage operation, not an outline re-architecture. DRAFTING NOTE — flag as
   the highest-priority seed to thicken; the single-reach FROZEN decision is protected.

None of the five is a convergence blocker. All four standing criteria the rulings touched are satisfied
by a real beat; the residuals are calibration dials (how loud, how thick, how many beats) that the prose
and bones layers exist to set — and in each case the draft has already named the patch, which is exactly
what a converged outline should do.
