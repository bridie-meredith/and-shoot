# Condition — Saerys's Formation Map of the Red Keep

Her internal cultivation reading of the Red Keep: nodes, formation eyes of each control system, coverage gaps, and her Book I vs. Book II state of understanding. Distinct from `loc-red-keep-interior`, which is the physical geography — this card is the cultivation overlay, the reading she imposes on that geography. It determines how she narrates every room she enters, every guard post she clocks, every exit she locates.

**Purpose of this card:** set-state consistency for all of Book I and the Red Keep scenes in Books II–III. She enters no room in the Red Keep without running the formation-scan. Bones authors need to know what she concludes from any given entry — what nodes are active, where the eye is, where the coverage gap is — so that her internal narration stays consistent across chapters.

**Hard fence:** The broken clock stays broken. She is reading a physical building using cultivation-formation vocabulary. The real-state column describes what the control systems actually are (the septa's schedule, the Kingsguard rotation, the household hierarchy). Her cultivation gloss is what she narrates. The formation-reading is not wrong about the geometry of control; it is wrong about the mechanism.

**Cross-reference for depth:** `and-experiment/design/cultivation-library/cultivation-techniques-arts-and-artifacts.md` §§ Part III.B (formation eyes, formation cores, control mechanics), Part III.C (the formation master role), Part III.D (breaking formations) — the doc from which this card's structural vocabulary is drawn. `loc-red-keep-interior` for the physical geography these readings overlay. `loc-maegors-holdfast` for the primary soft-cage formation she maps first.

## Description

From the first day she could walk the Red Keep's corridors, Saerys has been reading them as a formation master reads an array. Every space has nodes (watchers, choke-points, information-collection positions), a formation eye (the mechanism whose disruption would break the control system), a power source (the authority that sustains the formation), and coverage gaps (where the formation cannot see her). She maps these systematically and updates the map as her access expands. The formation-map is not a spatial diagram; it is a control-systems audit maintained in the cultivation-register.

---

## The Septa-Formation (Primary Surveillance Array)

**Formation type:** Detection + trapping (combined)  
**Formation eye:** The report-transmission channel — specifically, her father's reception of the septa's accounts. The septa's formation collapses without the channel between observation and consequence.  
**Nodes:** The septa herself; the attendants who report to the septa; the household staff who communicate attendance and whereabouts; the duty-schedule for who is with Saerys at which hour  
**Power source:** The Crown's general policy of supervised seclusion for young unmarried princesses  
**Coverage gaps:** Below the stairs (the septa does not go; she relies on intermediary reports); the still-room (the maester's domain creates a jurisdictional ambiguity the septa cannot resolve by direct inspection); the sick-house (the charity operation creates a parallel authority structure)  
**Her method for managing this formation:** she does not attack the nodes; she manages the formation eye — her father receives filtered reports (the septa's alarm is modulated by the father's indulgent reception). This is the counter-formation approach.

**Book I Bk-1-M2 note (Formation Eye confirmed):** When she plants the document with her father and it begins generating standing permissions, she has formally identified and addressed the formation eye. The septa's formation does not collapse — it is permanently managed from the eye.

## The Maegor's Holdfast Formation (Soft-Cage Formation)

**Formation type:** Defensive (for the Crown) / Trapping (for her)  
**Formation eye:** The drawbridge mechanism and the single-point access control  
**Nodes:** Kingsguard duty-station at the drawbridge; the dry moat's structural interruption of movement; the twelve-foot curtain walls creating a dead zone around the interior  
**Power source:** The Holdfast's physical design — this formation is inscribed in stone, not in qi; it cannot be broken by addressing a personnel node  
**Coverage gaps:** The formation has no gap for physical egress. It is effectively total for unauthorized departure. Her counter-strategy does not attack the formation; it creates a permission-category that the formation is not designed to detect (the sealed note is not a formation-break; it is a pass through the formation's own qi-flow)  
**Her method:** The document technique. The Holdfast's formation recognizes authorized egress (the drawbridge goes down when it should, for the right people with the right documentation). She constructs documentation that the formation's own processing treats as authorized. She does not break the formation; she becomes, temporarily, indistinguishable from a legitimate formation-element.

**Book I vs. Book II state:** In Book I she reads the formation as a Tier 1 trapping array and works within it. By the end of Book I she has a standing license that converts the trapping array into a conditional-release mechanism. In Book II she is outside the Holdfast entirely (Bk II Essos expeditions) and views it in retrospect as a successfully broken formation. Her notes from outside it are more confident than her notes from inside.

## The Kingsguard Formation (Perimeter Defense Array)

**Formation type:** Defensive + killing (combined; the killing layer is not aimed at her but she notes it)  
**Formation eye:** The duty schedule and rotation protocol  
**Nodes:** Individual Kingsguard at assigned posts; the watch-handoff system; the Captain's oversight of the rotation  
**Power source:** The Crown's martial authority  
**Coverage gaps:** The Kingsguard is not deployed against internal threats from the royal family; they are deployed against external threats. She is inside the defensive perimeter, which creates a structural blind spot — they watch outward, not inward.  
**Her method:** She does not interact with the Kingsguard formation directly. She maintains the below-stairs node array to track the formation's schedule, which gives her advance notice of presence-density changes.

**Her classification:** "the Sword Sect's deployment at the outer perimeter; Earth-grade defensive formation; the formation is not aimed at this practitioner and requires no counter-measure beyond schedule-awareness."

---

## The Alchemists' Guild Formation (External High-Value Formation)

**Formation type:** Defensive (external compound); Qi-gathering (her classification of the wildfire-production site)  
**Formation eye:** The Guild's membership and access control (who can approach the compound; what permission is required)  
**Nodes:** The compound gates; the Guild's relationship with the Crown (which provides their operating license)  
**Her reading:** "A Saint-grade furnace-sect formation concealing a genuine spiritual-ground; the formation eye is the Guild's relationship with the Crown's license-granting authority; I have approached the eye through the correct channel (my father's name + reagent interest) and the eye has opened; the formation's interior is now partially accessible"

**Book I M3 note:** The wildfire shard is what she obtains from this formation. The fever that follows is the first time the broken clock rings genuinely. Her formation-map updates: "Tier 3 material confirmed; the Alchemists' Guild formation contains genuine earth-grade-high reagents; the formation's qi-density reading is retrospectively validated." This is one of the few formation-map updates where she is right about the significant fact (there is something real there) and wrong about why.

## The Red Keep General Formation-Map (Full Survey)

**Book I state of understanding:**

| Location | Her formation classification | Real control system | Coverage gap she uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maegor's Holdfast | Trapping formation, stone-inscribed, single-eye at drawbridge | Physical seclusion structure with restricted egress | Document technique; standing license (Lock I) |
| The still-room | Domain formation (she claims it); jurisdictional ambiguity for others | The maester's professional territory; the septa cannot enter without cause | Maester's authority creates a buffer zone |
| The septa's corridor | Detection formation; nodes at each attendant station | Attendant-reporting network | Below-stairs node array (her own counter-formation) |
| Great Hall | Righteous-sect public-display formation | The court's ceremonial space; she is visible and watched | Irrelevant — she does not work in the Great Hall |
| The godswood | Low-detection zone; the weirwood itself is a "Tier 2 node" | A rarely-visited sacred space with minimal surveillance | Used for the few conversations she needs to have where no formation-flag is in range |
| The sick-house | Counter-formation she has constructed against the septa's primary array | Her own charity operation with its own access rules | She built this gap; it is her creation |

**Book II updates (Red Keep scenes in Bk II):**  
By Bk II, her father is dead (Bk III M1), and the formation-map is obsolete in its power-source readings — the formation eyes are the same structural points, but the power source (Viserys I's authority) is gone and the new power structure (Dance-era faction conflict) is a different formation entirely. She notes this as "the old formation's power source has been exhausted; the formation has not collapsed but its authority channel has been transferred to an unknown new core; the map requires a full re-survey." She does not have time to conduct the re-survey; Book III is the consequence.

## Formation-Master Reading Habits

In any room she enters, she runs this scan automatically (noted in her narration as a beat before engagement):

1. **Nodes active:** how many observers, what their positions, what coverage radius
2. **Formation eye:** what is the mechanism whose disruption would change her access or safety
3. **Coverage gap:** where can she stand, work, or speak that the formation cannot reach
4. **Power source:** whose authority sustains this control system; if that authority changed, how would the formation change
5. **Counter-measure:** she has one, or she notes she needs one

This scan is the bones-level behavior for any scene where she enters a new or changed space. It is not always explicit in narration — in familiar spaces it runs silently; in new spaces it is narrated.

---

## Sensory Impact

This condition is the lens through which she reads every space she inhabits. Its sensory signature: a slight pause on entry, eyes moving to the exits, the watchers, the positions. Not suspicious — she has been doing this long enough that it looks like nothing. The pause is the scan. It is always present.

---

## Duration

Persistent across all three books. The map is continuously updated. In Book III, when the Red Keep's control systems dissolve into Dance-era faction chaos, she has no reliable formation-map for the first time in her life and her narration reflects this as disorientation: "the formation's geometry is no longer stable; the nodes have been repositioned and the eyes are unclear."
