## OUTLINE

### I.1 · dragonbane

**Age:** infant (newborn → days). **Beat:** A reincarnated adult crammed into a newborn's body —
the mismatch causes illness. Queen Alicent bullies past Jaehaerys's misogyny (we see the argument)
and gives the sickly babe a dragon egg. The girl may be a stand-in/replacement for Gael (possibly
even named Gael — unsettled).

**Situation:** Trapped in an infant's body (initial conflict: adult mind, zero mobility). She has
nothing but time to meditate. She recognizes magic (the egg radiates *something*), concludes she's
been isekai'd, and decides to cultivate. Genre-awareness is explicit: she wonders if she's the
antagonist young master, jade beauty, villain-reincarnator. She resolves not to fall into tropes —
not cannon fodder, not first-defeat, not enemy-to-lover. She doesn't even like boys (future
conflict seed: lesbian in Westeros).

**Cultivation sequence (multi-day):**
1. Meditates, thinks she can feel something from the egg but isn't sure.
2. Forms a connection to it over time. Gets frustrated, gnaws/gums it.
3. Connection strengthens with physical contact + chewing.
4. Gets a hard wooden toy (expressed interest), hides it under blanket.
5. When unobserved: chips shell with toy, gnaws the chip, cuts soft infant gums on it, bleeds
   on it → spike of energy → swallows by reflex → feels warmth (qi). Immediately cultivates
   to keep the energy from dissipating / prevent qi deviation.
6. Hours pass in cultivation. Can't continue — monitored by worried nanny + Alicent.
7. Next day: prepared. Feels a sympathetic connection to the whole egg (without consciously
   naming the principle). Follows it, tries to draw the whole into herself.

**The hatch (crisis):**
- Her draw triggers a hatch. From her POV: a hostile beast in the crib with a helpless infant.
  *What is wrong with these people?!*
- She panics and fights. Uses her energy pool to weaken the hatchling + empower herself +
  strangle it. Gets scuffed up, gets a scar on her face.
- Defeats the beast. Feels her cultivation grow from the kill.

**Consumption (opportunity window):**
- She knows she must capitalize NOW — nobody leaves a dead monster with an infant (or do they?).
- Gnaws on the hatchling (works poorly). Hits it with the toy, breaks off a claw.
- Uses the sympathetic connection: consume the claw → through that, draw the hatchling's
  power into herself.

**Power system (what she thinks vs what's real):**
- She thinks: starting boost to pyromancy + body tempering.
- What's actually happening: she absorbed a SOURCE of power, not a pool. As she grows, this
  source continues to output new qi. Future need: expand the source or find compatible new
  sources (dragon-qi-compatible materials).

**End:** Alicent discovers a comatose infant with a dead hatchling and blood (from the struggle).
This is an ill omen. The girl gets an unladylike epithet — something like "Dragonbane."

**Register:** SPIKE (loudest — so III.8 silence reads). **Rung:** R0 (visible violence, not
hidden drain — GUARD-3 is rewritten here).

**Plants:** PLANT[SYMPATHETIC-RESONANCE] · PLANT[ACQUISITION-DRIVE] · PLANT[REINCARNATION-SICKNESS]
· PLANT[SOURCE-NOT-POOL] · PLANT[ISEKAI-AWARENESS] · PLANT[LESBIAN] · PLANT[DRAGONBANE-EPITHET]
· PLANT[GIFT:ALICENT] (she fights for the egg against Jaehaerys).

### I.5 · the-bond-breaker

**Age:** ~6. **Beat:** She decides to escape. Can't trust anyone. Needs strength first — cultivate
harder. Valyrian steel body tempering already superhuman (climb walls, crumble mortar). Conceals it.

**Escape preparation:**
- Hidey-hole in the Keep. Stashes stolen wealth.
- Targets the king's heirloom dagger (famous Targaryen piece, radiates MORE magic). Steals it.
- Eats it slowly — bites bits off with cultivated jaw. Slow going.

**The uproar:**
- Jaehaerys discovers the heirloom missing. SERIOUS. Several servants in the black cells.
  Innocent people punished for her theft.
- During the chaos: Daemon sneaks into her room. Corners her.

**The confrontation (what the world sees vs what happens):**
- World sees: she "defended herself," he tripped, hit his head. (True enough — he DID break
  into a child's room.) He survives, concussed, diminished. Can't contradict without admitting
  where he was.
- Reality: she senses a weak source inside him — his dragon-bond. Same energy type. She
  manhandles him, pins him, DRAWS the bond out via sympathetic resonance. Severs and consumes.
- The dragon dies. Somewhere distant, it drops. No visible cause. No connection to a six-year-
  old's "self-defense."

**The horror + pivot:**
- Drama: a dragon is dead, unexplained.
- Baelon and Viserys blame HER. Not for the draining (they don't know that) — but she's
  "Dragonbane," she was involved in the "incident" with Daemon, and now his dragon is dead.
  The reputation compounds: this girl is cursed, dangerous to dragons, dangerous to family.
  They can't prove causation but the association is damning.
- She feels GUILT — for the dragon, not Daemon. A living creature killed at a distance.
- HORROR at herself: too close to demonic cultivation. Consuming living bonds. The dark path.
  Xianxia has a word for this and it's the villain origin story.
- She CAN drain anyone with a dragon-bond. Surrounded by them. Staying = staying on the
  demonic path.

**The purification question + destination:**
- Can she purge her blood? Temper out the inbred draconic corruption?
- Needs a COUNTER-element: something that opposes dragon-qi.
- Weirwood. From the frozen North. Wood vs fire, old gods vs Valyrian.
- Destination locked: NORTH. She needs weirwood.

**But she doesn't bolt.** She needs to play it smart. She's IN the best intelligence resource
in Westeros (the Red Keep, the maesters, the library). She needs to know: where exactly is
weirwood? What routes exist? What's in the North? She starts planning properly — not a
reactive thief anymore, an operator.

**Register:** CHILL (she scared herself). **Rung:** R1 — a living creature killed through her
actions. Reader still sympathetic (self-defense) but the METHOD is wrong and she knows it.

### Interlude · alicent

**POV:** Alicent. External view of her daughter after Book I.

**Beat:** We see what MC looks like from the outside. A mother watching a child she doesn't
understand and is losing. The girl who killed a dragon at birth, who eats poison, who got
caught in the throne room with the skulls, who "defended herself" against a grown man, and
who has now gone completely silent and withdrawn.

Alicent wanted a daughter. What she has is something else — too still, too perfect, too quiet.
The uncanny beauty that should be a mother's pride is unsettling up close. The luminescent eyes
don't track like a child's eyes. The girl sits for hours doing nothing visible, and when she
emerges she is polite in a way that feels performed rather than felt.

The rift with Jaehaerys over how to handle her. His frustration (she's useless to him). Her
grief (she's losing something she can't name). The servants' whispered superstition. The dead
dragon nobody can explain. The diminished relative nobody pities.

Alicent loves her daughter. That's the tragedy — love aimed at something that can't or won't
receive it in the currency being offered.

**The new plan:**
- The Daemon match is dead (he's diminished, disgraced — can't explain why he was in
  her room). Jaehaerys needs a NEW betrothal for Gael.
- Revealed here through Alicent's POV: Jaehaerys is arranging a match. Not a prestigious one —
  whoever is giving him the most political trouble. A pacification marriage. Could be someone
  as lowly as Maidenpool. He's spending his daughter to buy a grumpy lord's loyalty.
- Alicent is furious/heartbroken — this is beneath their daughter. A princess used as a bribe
  for a minor lord. It reveals what Gael is worth to Jaehaerys: not much. A problem he can
  convert into a solution for a different problem.
- This is the opening pressure for Book II: Gael doesn't know yet, but the clock is ticking.

**Register:** CHILL (quiet horror from outside). **Structural role:** palate cleanser between
books; recontextualizes the reader's relationship to MC (we were rooting for her inside; from
outside she's frightening). Also delivers the Book II inciting pressure via Alicent's knowledge
of the betrothal plan.

## BOOK II

### II.1 · the-recluse (working title — playing smart)

**Age:** ~6-7. **Beat:** After the bond-breaker horror, she withdraws. Becomes a recluse —
focused on cultivating and purifying internally. Stays in her rooms. Meditates constantly.
Tries to refine out the draconic corruption / demonic taint. This worries her family.

**The family rift:**
- Jaehaerys: the girl needs to own up and mature. Be useful to the dynasty. Stop being a
  problem and start being an asset. His patience is spent — she's failed every test of
  normalcy and now she's hiding in her room. He wants a functional political piece.
- Alicent: wants an actual daughter. Not a tool (that's his frame) — a child she can love
  and be close to. The reclusion hurts her personally. She's losing her daughter to something
  she can't see or name.
- The rift between THEM deepens over what to do about her. Father vs mother, utility vs
  affection. She's the wedge.

**The draw-out (what breaks the reclusion):**
- She can't stay isolated forever. She allows herself to be drawn out by:
  - New materials (her acquisition drive is stronger than her fear — something exotic arrives
    at court? A gift? A trade shipment she senses?)
  - Learning new things (the library, the maester's knowledge, maps, geography). The scholar-
    princess cover begins here: she trades reclusion for "acceptable" studious behavior.
- This is strategic: she NEEDS maps (where is weirwood?), she needs to understand routes to
  the North, she needs samples of exotic substances. The library and maester's workshop are
  her intelligence-gathering apparatus.

**The tension:** every time she engages with the world, she's pulled between:
- Purification goal (stay clean, don't consume more dragon-stuff)
- Acquisition drive (that substance is RIGHT THERE and it's compatible)
- Escape planning (need information, need resources, need to know the world)

She's also interested in Dragonstone — senses it would be a powerful cultivation site.
Draconic materials are fine (objects, not people) — it's the incest-blood she wants to purify,
not dragon-qi generally. Dragonstone is a candy store she'd love to access.

**Register:** HOLD (internal work; the outside world pulls at her). **Rung:** off-ladder.

**Plants:** PLANT[SCHOLAR-PRINCESS] (the cover identity that gives her legitimate access) ·
PLANT[FAMILY-RIFT] (Jaehaerys vs Alicent over what she should be) ·
PLANT[DRAGONSTONE-DESIRE] (rich cultivation site; filed for later) ·
PLANT[NORTH-INTEL] (she's mapping her escape route through legitimate study).
**Fires:** FIRE[PLAYING-SMART] (reactive thief → strategic operator) ·
FIRE[OUTWARD-COMPLIANCE] (the mask evolves — compliance becomes a tool for access) ·
FIRE[MAESTER-MARK] (he becomes her tutor/resource, not just a target).
