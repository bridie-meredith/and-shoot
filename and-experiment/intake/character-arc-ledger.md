# CHARACTER ARC LEDGER — motivation · state-axes · the turn

**Companion to `intake/character-profiles.md` (who they *are*) and `intake/character-reactions.md` (how the
comedy fires).** This doc answers three questions for every load-bearing role, uniformly and checkably:

1. **Motivation** — the *want* (what they pursue on-page) and the *need* (the deeper driver underneath).
2. **State axes** — the measurable dimensions that *move* across the series (the seed for the substance
   state-axes / the pending run-03 state-ledger — `design/run-03/README.md` pending list).
3. **The turn** — the meaningful **growth or setback** each arc delivers (not a flat loop).

Every entry carries a **fence-check**: a confirmation that the trajectory respects the engine's hard fences.
The engine *requires* certain non-arcs (the three antagonist faces never reach the truth; Gael never comes
down and is mortal — she runs). For those, "the turn" is a **designed setback** (escalating, catastrophic,
or dramatic-irony), which satisfies "meaningful setback" without breaking the engine. A flat recurrence
would be the flaw; an *escalating* failure is the design.

Written to **live span-A canon** (Gael · b.84 AC · Jaehaerys father · Alicent mother · escape ~95 AC ·
the maid = Wylla). Series-level axes (from `memory.md` substance seed + the series chunk): **transcendence ↔
attachment** · **agency ↔ being-claimed** · **the cost-ledger** (mortal lives spent / the curdle R0–R4).

## TIER 1 — the two protagonists

### Gael Targaryen — protagonist *(the refusal-arc)*
- **Motivation — want / need.** *Want:* cultivate/transcend (the acquisition-drive) and be **left alone**
  (solitude to do the work). *Need (unspoken, refused):* the attachment and grief she has armored against —
  the cultivation cosmology is scar tissue from a death that already took everything once.
- **State axes.** (1) **transcendence ↔ attachment** — her core refusal; (2) **agency** — reactive thief →
  operator → master/escapee; (3) **method scale** — Lock I (body) → Lock II (the Keep) → Lock III (the
  world/sea); (4) **the cost-ledger** — R0 → R1 → R2 → R3 → R4 (her moral position degrades as the register
  stays breezy); (5) **the un-fileable bond** — the single interior axis: Wylla, the one thing the cauldron
  can't refine.
- **Trajectory.** *I:* infant reflex (R0) → thief-method born → the cage revealed → the demonic drain
  rejected (R1) → **the pivot, reactive → operator.** *II:* operator runs an empire she barely watches (R2);
  source-reveal (every theft was unnecessary — she reframes and continues, axis unmoved). *III:* master at
  full scale (R3) → the escape (R4 apex). Axes 1–4 all **escalate monotonically**; axis 5 holds flat-and-
  denied until the apex.
- **The turn — a *refusal-arc* with exactly one hairline crack.** This is the deliberate non-growth that
  makes the tragedy: across three books she builds **higher** walls (transcendence-wall up, attachment
  denied, cost-ledger climbing) and **does not come down.** The *only* interior state-change in the whole
  series is the **apex register-silence** (III.6): the breezy deflection finally *fails to arrive* over what
  Wylla becomes — the one crack in axis 5 — **and she does not act on it. She runs.** Growth is the reader's
  recognition; her own "arc" is the cost of refusing one. (Setback, moral: she keeps her hands clean only by
  spending the person she loves.)
- **Fence-check.** ✅ Honors *the clock stays broken* (the source-reveal moves no axis — she just reframes),
  *can-spend-but-won't* (three spends; no apex relapse), *simple-minded* (no strategic growth; the empire is
  incidental), and *she runs* (the one crack is unacted-on). The single crack is canon (profiles §"the bond
  the cauldron can't refine"; Wylla card §"the register-silence she alone notices").

### Wylla — supporting protagonist *(the cleanest arc — growth that is also the cost)*
- **Motivation — want / need.** *Want (early):* do the work well, keep her place and her neck. *Want
  (late):* the dao, the sect, and Gael — to be a *worthy* junior sister. *Need:* belonging / a faith to
  give herself to.
- **State axes.** (1) **knowledge-posture** — disbelief → willed incuriosity → devotion; (2) **role** —
  errand-runner → on-the-ground operator → co-conspirator → killer; (3) **faith** — skeptic → convert →
  true-believer; (4) **durability** — mortal → tempered.
- **Trajectory.** *II.1:* assigned; the *what-the-hell* sane outsider (the conversion clock starts). *mid-II:*
  the impossibilities pile up; she gets better at the work and more disciplined about **not asking**. *III.5:*
  the tempering makes the "magic" bodily, visibly real **on her** → faith sealed (junior sister). *III.6:*
  she kills the pursuer, framed (by her) as defending the sect; the witness to the apex silence.
- **The turn — bivalent: real growth that is simultaneously the deepest cost.** She gains agency,
  competence, courage, devotion — and **loses** the sane outsider's clarity (trades disbelief for a faith
  with no future) and her hands' cleanliness (she boards a killer so Gael's stay clean). The warmest and the
  most tragic arc at once.
- **Fence-check.** ✅ *Tempered ≠ cultivator* (durability axis moves; "real cultivation" never does — the
  clock stays broken for her too); *she stays*; *pre-romantic in text*; *survives* (epilogue fixed).

## TIER 1 — the antagonist (Westeros itself, three faces)

> Shared engine-fence: **none of the three ever reaches the truth.** Their "state change" is therefore not
> *knowledge* but **frustration → catastrophic / dramatic-irony failure**, escalating across the books. The
> comedy-review rule applies: each must *escalate,* never repeat flat.

### Otto Hightower — the predatory face *(the escalating-frustration setback)*
- **Motivation — want / need.** *Want:* read every actor as function + leverage; close the file on Gael.
  *Need:* to be the man who **sees clearly** (his whole pride).
- **State axes.** (1) **model-completeness** — every file closes except Gael's, the well with no bottom of
  the kind he knows; (2) **confidence** — certain → unsettled (correctly alarmed unsettles him more than
  panic would).
- **Trajectory.** II.2 unusual money he can't trace → II.5 reads the bestiary accurately, weaponizes the
  named entry (the leak), then the **decoy diary** jams his model (cover-or-genuine? he can't resolve it) →
  III.3 surveillance tightens → III.6 his one correct deduction (the maid matters) forces the interception.
- **The turn — the tragedy of competence: he wins every move and loses the game.** His single accurate read
  detonates a catastrophe he **never understands**; the warmth he reached for as a handle (a servant
  promoted to a name) is the exact thing that ruins everyone. Setback: maximal, and self-inflicted by his
  own clarity.
- **Fence-check.** ✅ *Never reaches the truth* (reaches "poisoner/Maegor," never "cultivator"); *not
  cartoonish* (correct, competent, dangerous *because* clear). State moves on confidence/model-completeness,
  not on knowledge.

### Jaehaerys I — the institutional face *(the dramatic-irony setback: objective-total, subjective-zero)*
- **Motivation — want / need.** *Want:* govern — file every person into a disposition, correct, arrange,
  dispose. *Need:* control; the pride of governance.
- **State axes.** (1) **control-illusion** — *held intact the entire span* (he never perceives the failure);
  (2) **involuntary unease** — the single thing that *does* move: a flicker he **refuses to dignify**,
  surfacing only as colder, more administrative reflexes.
- **Trajectory.** I.3 the Talk "works" (changes nothing) → II.4 the betrothal he's *proud* of = her clock →
  III.4 he accelerates the disposal → III.6 the realm's incoherent post-mortem.
- **The turn — the flattest *registered* arc and the largest *objective* setback in the cast.** He is the
  unwitting **architect of her escape** (the betrothal hands her the deadline and destination she was
  already fleeing to), and he **cannot even perceive it.** The setback is dramatic irony at full scale: total
  defeat, zero self-knowledge. The *only* movement we are allowed to show is axis 2 — the unease he won't
  name, leaking out as cooling reflexes — so he is not a literally-static obstacle but a man whose one true
  feeling never reaches his own awareness.
- **Fence-check.** ✅ *Cold, not warm* (warmth is Alicent's); *never reaches the truth* ("useless/dangerous,"
  never "cultivator"); *does not die before the escape*; *not a cartoon tyrant* (the horror is structural).
  The control-illusion deliberately does **not** move — that is the point; axis 2 carries the dramatized
  micro-setback (canon: reactions §"a flicker of unease he refuses to dignify").

### Septon Barth — the intellectual face *(the sharpening setback — the scholar ladder)*
- **Motivation — want / need.** *Want:* understand the anomaly-child; build the framework that contains her.
  *Need:* to think his way to anything (his pride and his danger, the same thing).
- **State axes.** (1) **proximity-to-truth** — the *only* antagonist axis that genuinely **moves toward** the
  answer: it *sharpens* across the scholar-ladder rungs R0 (I.1) → R5 (II.6); (2) **completion** — never
  reached.
- **Trajectory (the scholar ladder, Lorren → Barth).** R0 one odd data point → R1 immunity, wrong frame →
  R2 *consumed sorcerous material* (recoils from his own conclusion) → R3 a severing at distance (diverted by
  throne + Faith) → R4 reads the bestiary as a cosmology, the closest the truth ever comes → R5 *a source,
  not a pool* (the mechanism, with no subject) → R6 (III.6) the mind that could have caught them, kept too
  busy; dies 98 AC, the ladder ends one rung short, forever.
- **The turn — a real trajectory that is a designed setback.** He alone *changes toward the truth* (the most
  forward motion of any antagonist) and is fenced from arriving — far more maddening for a genius than never
  seeing at all. The dark note: the realm's one capable mind is kept too busy to finish the thought.
- **Fence-check.** ✅ *Never reaches the full truth* (the engine-fence; closest miss, not the catch); *a good
  man, not a predator* (the failure is poignant, not satisfying); *cleanly seated, dies 98 AC.*

## TIER 2 — the interlude narrator + recurring foils (each a setback arc, by design)

### Alicent — interlude narrator *(the recognition-and-grief setback)*
- **Motivation — want / need.** *Want:* mother a good daughter; protect her. *Need:* to **be a good
  mother** (her pride and her wound). *Coping mechanism:* a mother's narrative authored over the child who
  is actually there.
- **State axes.** (1) **recognition** — loving blindness → per-arc near-misses she flinches from → terminal
  recognition; (2) **the WTH escalation** — small frets → genuine what-the-hells → the break.
- **Trajectory.** I.1 frets (catastrophizes the harmless / misses the catastrophe) → Interlude the sustained
  external chill (*what is my daughter?*) → II.5 reads the bestiary, finds **her own entry** (near-break) →
  III.6 her daughter simply *gone* (the terminal WTH is grief).
- **The turn — escalating recognition that breaks her, and the tragic choice not to act.** Arc-question
  resolved: **knows-and-does-not-act** — the love that sees and still cannot move. A genuine, devastating
  setback.
- **Fence-check.** ✅ *Never reaches the truth* (the most poignant misread; tragic straight-man, not a fool);
  *the Interlude is the one external POV* (the WTH beats elsewhere are moments, not POV).

### Hobb — the Cassandra *(the attrition/flattening setback)*
- **Motivation / need:** report what he saw accurately; get the count right. **Axes:** accuracy (constant,
  always right) × credibility/voice (earnest → deadpan-defeated → silent). **Trajectory:** I.2/I.4 right,
  ignored, mocked, transferred → III.2 sees the rooftop ghost and **says nothing.** **The turn:** be right,
  be punished, go quiet, leave — a curdle in miniature; the system works *as designed* by removing its only
  honest witness. **Fence-check** ✅ accurate every time; never learns the *why*; one recurring figure.

### Daemon — the predator unmade *(the diminishment setback)*
- **Motivation / need:** appetite, entitlement, the predator's certainty. **Axes:** menace (apex → unmade) ×
  speech (can name what happened to him? → never, it convicts him). **Trajectory:** I.4 circles the
  uncanny-beautiful child → I.5 drained/severed, concussed, **gagged.** **The turn:** R1 made visible from
  outside — the apex predator unmade and locked out of his own story, a man nobody pities. **Fence-check** ✅
  the drain is unspeakable; diminished, not dead; never reaches the truth.

### Maester Lorren — the out-administered man *(the dependency setback)*
- **Motivation / need:** systematic knowledge; the document as shield. **Axes:** authority (the disapproving
  expert → an unwitting node in her network) × the record (accurate → metastasizing → a procurement trail he
  funds). **Trajectory:** I.2–I.5 out-administered (his still-room, his filing) → II.6 reads the whole ledger
  and finds the six-year network served **no purpose**; files nothing. **The turn:** the first proof the
  administrative conquest works *without violence* — defeated by his own impeccable bookkeeping. **Fence-
  check** ✅ a real maester (out-administered, not a fool); never names the dependency; no warmth.

### Septa Aldith — the endorsement-trap *(the one-way-ratchet setback)*
- **Motivation / need:** moral legibility; everyone sortable into the Seven's frame; composure. **Axes:**
  alarm (correct, rising) × standing-to-warn (ratcheted shut by her own endorsement). **Trajectory:** I.3
  scandalized → her one kind true word ("a stillness I have not seen in grown septas") is amplified by the
  realm's telephone game into sainthood → she **cannot ring the alarm without confessing she rang the bell
  for sainthood** → phased out via betrothal-prep. **The turn:** right, earnest, properly motivated, and
  structurally defeated by what she can't see; the reader laughs *and* knows she was right. **Fence-check** ✅
  she is right (not a fool/villain); does not lose register; never understands the mechanism.

## Roll-up — the shape of the whole cast

| Role | Motivation (want → need) | Headline axis-move | The turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gael** | transcend + be left alone → refuse grief | walls **rise** (transcendence/cost-ledger); bond-axis flat-then-cracks | **refusal-arc**; one unacted crack (apex silence); she runs |
| **Wylla** | keep her place → belong / a faith | disbelief → devotion; mortal → killer | **growth = cost**; the warmest and most tragic arc |
| **Otto** | close the file → see clearly | confidence: certain → unsettled | **setback**: wins every move, loses the game, never understands |
| **Jaehaerys** | govern → control | control-illusion **held**; unease leaks as cold reflexes | **dramatic-irony setback**: total defeat, zero self-knowledge |
| **Barth** | understand her → think his way to anything | proximity-to-truth **sharpens**, never arrives | **sharpening setback**: closest miss, one rung short forever |
| **Alicent** | mother her → be a good mother | recognition: blind → terminal | **setback**: sees, breaks, does not act |
| **Hobb** | report accurately | credibility → silence | **attrition**: right, punished, gone quiet |
| **Daemon** | appetite / certainty | menace → unmade | **diminishment**: predator gagged |
| **Lorren** | knowledge / the document | authority → unwitting node | **dependency defeat**: out-administered |
| **Aldith** | moral legibility | standing-to-warn ratcheted shut | **endorsement-trap**: right and disarmed |

**Two consistency guarantees this ledger enforces:**
1. **Every major character has a captured motivation (want + need) and a trajectory that *moves* an axis** —
   no role is a flat loop. The recurring foils escalate (ratchet/attrition), not repeat, per the comedy
   review's escalation-not-recurrence rule.
2. **The two designed non-growth roles are dramatized, not absent.** Gael's refusal is given exactly one
   measurable crack (the apex silence) so the static monomania *means* something; Jaehaerys's blindness is
   given exactly one involuntary tell (the cooling reflexes) so his objective-total defeat has an on-page
   movement. Both fixes consolidate existing canon (profiles + reactions) rather than invent it.
