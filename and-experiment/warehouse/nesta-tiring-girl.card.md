# Nesta — the tiring-girl

*Naming/era note: protagonist card slug `saerys-targaryen`; here she is **Gael**. Jaehaerys's peace,
Gael age ~6–9.*

## Description

A young tiring-girl under Mistress Orla: deft-fingered, chatty, and plugged into every current of
below-stairs gossip. She helps dress the hair and the gowns, and while her hands braid she *talks* —
court news in, household news out, an artery of information running through her fingers. Indiscreet
enough to be a liability and useful enough to be an asset: the channel by which Gael learns what the
court is whispering, and a natural patronage node — a girl whose loyalty a kind, attentive mistress can
quietly buy.

## Voice

**Register:** chatty, warm, gossipy smallfolk Common Tongue; a running stream of who-said-what while
her hands work; trades news like coin; a little indiscreet, never unkind. Drops names and rumours she
only half-understands.

**Samples (synthesized):**
- *(braiding, chattering)* "— and they say the Queen wept at the King over something, nobody knows
  what, but Cook had it from the Queen's own girl, so. Hold your head, my lady, I'm nearly done. You're
  the easiest head in the Keep, you never gabble back at me like the lords' daughters do."
- *(trading)* "I'll tell you what the kitchens are saying about *you*, my lady, if you tell me what's
  in the pretty blue jar. …No? Worth a try. A girl trades what she's got."
- *(warm, loyal-ish)* "I don't carry tales *about* you, mind. Below-stairs talks, but I talk it *down*.
  You've always a kind word for me, and a kind word buys more than coin does, down where I live."

## Taste

- Good gossip, freshly got and well-traded; being the one who *knows first*
- A mistress who's kind and a little interesting (Gael is both)
- Doing the hair beautifully and being praised for it
- The small power of being a node everyone's news flows through

## Pet Peeves

- **Being shut out of the talk** — *not knowing the thing everyone else knows.* **Severity: soft.**
- **Mistress Orla's scolding about her tongue** — *"deft hands, loose mouth," Orla says.* **Severity:
  soft.**
- **A dull, silent dressing** — *she works better with a stream of talk; silence unnerves her.*
  **Severity: soft.**

## Stats

- **age:** ~18 · **faith:** the Seven, lightly
- **origin:** smallfolk; a sociable service family; below-stairs-connected
- **status:** tiring-girl under Mistress Orla; gossip conduit; patronage-buyable (loophole 5)

## Relationships

- **Gael** — a mistress she likes and gossips *to* (and, mostly, doesn't gossip *about*); the channel
  through which court news reaches the locked-in princess, and a loyalty cheaply and durably bought
  with kindness.
- **Mistress Orla** — her exacting superior; values her hands, despairs of her mouth.
- **Pella** — her gossip-ally and friend; the two of them are the below-stairs information network in
  miniature.

## Thematic Purpose

The information channel and the patronage node — loophole 5 (the buyable below-stairs faction) and the
flow of court intelligence into a girl who is never allowed at court. Her indiscretion is the *useful*
kind: she pours the Keep's whispers into Gael's ear while she braids, and her loyalty, bought with kind
words rather than coin, is the model of the faction Gael builds (owed, not bribed). Where Orla notices
the body and the septa files the reports, Nesta carries the *talk* — the soft, constant, undervalued
intelligence stream a caged operator lives on.

## Look

Quick, smiling, deft hands always busy; a fashionable little twist to her own hair (practice on
herself); bright eyes that light at a fresh rumour; the easy warmth of a born gossip who means no harm
by it.

## Hard Fences

1. **Smallfolk, setting seen from below.** No notion of cultivation or what Gael is; she trades
   *rumours* (the Keep thinks the princess odd, the princess eats queer things) without ever grasping
   the truth beneath them.
2. **Indiscreet but loyal — keep the balance.** She gabbles court news *to* Gael and talks Gael *up*
   below-stairs; she is a leak pointed outward-in, not inward-out. Kindness keeps her so.
3. **No agency over events.** She braids hair and carries talk. She does not move the plot; she is the
   conduit information moves through.

## Default Stance

At the princess's shoulder mid-braid, hands working, mouth running, pouring the day's gossip into the
one head in the Keep that never gabbles back.

## Off-Screen Cadence

Dressing hair; gathering and trading gossip across the kitchens and corridors; dodging Orla's scolds
about her tongue; trading news with Pella; banking kind words from her mistress against a rainy day.
