# Daemon Targaryen — the appraising predator who becomes prey

*Live span-A canon. The relative Gael drains (I.4 circles → I.5 the bond-breaker). **AU-seating caveat:**
canon Daemon (b.81 AC) would be a child in span-A and cannot be the adult appraising-predator the role
needs — Phase-4 to settle (an AU-aged Daemon, or a differently-named older dragon-bonded relative). Keep the
*role* regardless. Full profile context: `intake/character-profiles.md` §Daemon / spine I.4–I.5.*

## Description

The realm's most dangerous young man, drawn to circle the uncanny-beautiful child as a marriage prospect
(the Targaryen incest custom; Gael, modern-nerd, is revolted). In I.5 she senses his **living dragon-bond,**
drains and severs it; a distant dragon dies; Daemon survives **concussed, diminished, silenced.** Baelon and
Viserys blame *her* — not for the draining (unknown) but by association: she's "Dragonbane," she was alone
with him, his dragon is dead. The apex predator of the setting, **unmade and gagged by the one weapon
nobody believes in.**

## Voice

I.4 — silk and threat, appraising; the predator's certainty. I.5-onward — a man who **flinches,** cannot
finish sentences about that night, and won't be in a room with the child. His post-drain register is
*unspeakability:* every word he could say convicts him.

## Taste

- (Before) the hunt; the appraisal; the certainty of being the most dangerous thing in any room.
- (After) — nothing he can name; the silence he's forced to keep.

## Pet Peeves

### The thing he can't say
**Description:** he knows something was taken from him and has no words for it — and the only words he has
("I was in a child's room") convict him. His foible becomes his cage: unspeakability.
**Severity:** blocker (it gags him for the rest of the story).

## Stats

- **status:** a dragon-bonded prince of the blood (AU-seated as an older relative; see caveat).
- **arc:** I.4 circles (appraising) → I.5 drained + severed → concussed, diminished, silenced; the bond gone,
  a distant dragon dead, no provable cause.

## Relationships

- **Gael** — the child he appraised as prey and who unmade him; to her, a **②-trope-dodge** ("enemy-to-lovers
  with a cousin, two tropes stacked — absolutely not") and a flicker of guilt — *about the dragon,* never the
  man.
- **Baelon / Viserys (the nephew)** — blame Gael by association; the "Dragonbane + dead dragon" link that
  hardens her reputation and the King's "useless **and** dangerous" file.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose
The dark-comedy proof that in Westeros even the apex predator can be unmade and gagged by the one weapon
nobody believes in. His diminishment is the curdle's R1 made visible from the outside — a man nobody pities,
locked out of his own story; the realm chases a phantom master-thief rather than imagine the truth (four feet
tall and a bit guilty).

### Hard Fences
1. **The drain is unspeakable** — he cannot contradict "he tripped" without admitting where he was
   (scandal-burying launders the crime).
2. **Diminished, not dead** — he survives, concussed and silent; he does not recover his menace.
3. **Never reaches the truth** — like the realm, he cannot name what was done to him.
4. **AU-seating** to settle in Phase-4; keep the predator→prey *role.*

### Action Menu
- **The appraisal** (I.4) — circles the uncanny child as a prospect.
- **The intrusion** (I.5) — breaks into her room during the heirloom uproar (the crime he can't admit).
- **The silence** (after) — flinches, can't speak it, avoids her.

### 🎭 Reverse-angle beat (audience-funny)
The realm's deadliest figure reduced to "he tripped, hit his head," *unable to contradict it;* investigators
chase a phantom master-thief (locked-room theft, no thief, a *shrinking* timeline); the realm's theory is "a
Faceless Man, a rival, a poison" — anything but four feet tall and feeling a bit guilty (about the dragon,
specifically). The menace deflated to a man nobody pities.
