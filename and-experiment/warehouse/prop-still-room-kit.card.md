# Prop — The Still-Room Kit

The portable reagent case she re-establishes at every location. The fixed still-room at the Red Keep is the permanent domain; this is its mobile shadow — smaller, more deliberate, and more revealing about what she actually considers essential.

In her cultivation-register: a Tier-1 spirit-tool collection, classified as a unit rather than individual objects. "The alembic, the specific scale, the ceramic containers — these function as a unit; their individual function is the unit function; they are operationally incomplete without each other." The domain-is-the-practitioner insight she arrived at on the Essosi voyage is one of her better operational observations: her methods live in her, not in the Red Keep's stone.

**Cross-reference:** `loc-still-room` is the fixed space this kit re-establishes in. `cond-maesters-cabinet` lists the specific substances populating the variable-components compartment. `prop-dose-log` travels with the kit but is kept separate from it — she does not store them in the same case.

---

## Physical Description

A fitted leather traveling case, exterior resembling a maester's instrument kit — deliberately. Outer surface is dark-oiled leather with brass clasp catches; the resemblance to a senior maester's instrument case is exact enough that it has passed three household inspections without being opened. Interior: her own partitioning system, built from thin wooden dividers fitted to the case dimensions and lined with packed wool. Each component has a defined position. She can unpack and repack in under four minutes in darkness — she has timed this.

The case is roughly the size of a large saddlebag, heavier than it looks. When full for a long voyage (the Yi Ti crossing), it required two below-stairs faction members to carry it to the dock, which she filed as "the cauldron's impedance grows proportional to tier advancement." The total weight, including a full variable-component load, is approximately thirty pounds.

There is a secondary external compartment on the case's back face, accessible without opening the main case, containing a folded cloth, a small roll of catgut suture, and a sealed vial of spirits of wine — her immediate-response kit for the kind of emergency that does not wait for full case deployment.

## Fixed Components (Carried Every Time)

These never leave the case. The case travels when they travel; if the case is separated from her, these are gone.

**The travel alembic:** Quarter-scale of the fixed still-room alembic. Glass body, copper fitting head and condensing arm, a small brass-lidded receiver flask. Sufficient for producing spirits-of-wine-concentration distillate from wine; for distilling essential-fraction extracts from plant material at the small-batch level. The glass body has one repair — a hairline crack along the lower curve sealed with a copper-backed binding she applied herself; it has held for three years and she checks it before every deployment.

**The precision scale:** Her personal scale, calibrated at age eight to a standard she established by weighing documented samples against each other until she had a reference set. The scale uses bronze counterweights in a custom set she had made. No other scale reads the same way she does; the dose-log's grain-weight entries are entries on this scale's measure. She does not trust other people's scales. She has documented this position in the dose-log.

**Ten ceramic containers with sealed lids:** Glazed ceramic, small enough to fit in one palm. She relabels them with her parallel-system cards before each deployment — the cards are small squares of treated cloth, not paper (damp destroys paper labels; cloth holds), attached with a small wire loop through a hole in the label. The standard Kit contains one "reserved" container that begins each deployment empty and is assigned a use within the first operational day.

**The lamped brazier:** A collapsible brass brazier, approximately the size of a large fist when folded. Burns specific charcoal she sources locally at each destination through the below-stairs faction's established channels or factor-network equivalents. The required charcoal is a slow-burning, low-ash type; she has documented three local substitutes (one Braavosi, one Pentoshi, one shipboard) and their adjustment factors. The brazier's temperature control is managed by charcoal quantity and vent position; she has calibrated this to a degree that the lamped brazier gives her approximately 80% of the fixed still-room brazier's controllability.

**The reference extract:** A transcribed extract of the dose-log's current tolerance thresholds — not the log itself, which stays separate. The extract is the operational fast-reference: what she is currently tolerant to, at what dose, with what margin. Updated before each departure. The current-tolerance page also includes a short table: "if separated from the kit," listing the minimum substitutes available from a standard Westerosi market or harbor apothecary that can maintain her regimen for up to fifteen days at reduced tier.

**The blank supplement:** Twelve blank pages, sewn into a small pamphlet, for new entries during travel. New entries are transcribed into the dose-log proper on return. During two voyages she has run out of supplement pages; the Yi Ti voyage produced entries on the margins of the reference extract that she transcribed verbatim, annotating: "administrative tribulation, minor."

## Variable Components (Assembled Per Deployment)

These are restocked for each deployment and consumed during it. The specific load depends on: deployment duration, estimated tier-advancement target, and calculated margin.

**The current-tier substance supply:** Whatever the dose-log's active regimen calls for, in sufficient quantity for the deployment duration plus one-third margin. For a two-week voyage, she carries three weeks of supply. For the Yi Ti crossing (weeks at sea plus extended stay), she carried two months of supply plus an emergency-contingency set of early-tier training substances that she could acquire locally if the main supply ran out.

**Location-specific additions:**

*Essosi deployment (Braavos, Pentos):* Included the Tears of Lys full-concentration series (dose-log entries 100–115 were completed during this voyage). Also the first basilisk-venom preliminary (entry 89; aborted at early-onset; the vial she aborted was re-sealed and remains in the case at the relevant concentration). The Essosi load was the heaviest variable set she has traveled with; she hired an extra below-stairs-equivalent porter through the local factor contact.

*Yi Ti deployment:* Included the poison-master's case as an integrated addition — a separate fitted case (see functional state below) acquired in Yi Ti. The variable set for Yi Ti was lighter than the Essosi set because she had anticipated sourcing locally (correct; the Yi Ti market for exotic toxins exceeded her expectations). The poison-master's case integrates with the standard kit but is held separately and has its own labeling system.

*Red Keep operational:* The fixed still-room is the primary; the kit in Red Keep mode is deployed only during periods when the still-room is inaccessible (one such period during Book I Lock I, approximately three days, when the maester's tower access was restricted pending the wildfire-stunt inquiry).

## Re-Establishment Protocol

The first hour in any new location. Before unpacking anything else, before assessing the local situation, often before sleeping:

1. Find where the still-room will be: light, airflow, external access, heat-source position.
2. Unpack in defined order: brazier first (establish heat), scale second (calibrate to local surface), alembic third (position for light and cooling), containers fourth (line in order by reference extract assignment), reference extract fifth (read, confirm, note any changes from last reading).
3. Run a calibration check: one small known preparation at a documented concentration; verify against reference extract notes. The calibration check adds approximately twenty minutes and is not skipped.
4. The space is the still-room when the calibration check is complete.

She has executed this protocol in: a maester's guest chamber in Braavos, a factor's two-room flat in Pentos, a ship's first-mate cabin on the Essosi voyage, a single-room accommodation in a Yi Ti caravanserai, and the interior of a sea-chest in Book III when no other surface was viable. The sea-chest deployment produced the worst calibration-check results she has documented (motion, humidity, temperature variance) and a note: "the still-room holds. The domain is the practitioner."

## Sensory Hooks

- The leather of the case carries the still-room's third chemical register — the cumulative ambient presence of years of compound work has transferred to the case's exterior; she can smell the kit from across a room when the case has been closed for more than a day
- The ceramic containers make a specific soft sound when the case shifts — a faint porcelain-on-wool click that she has learned to distinguish from the sound the scale's counterweights make (a finer, metallic clink); in a dark or tense situation, she can assess the kit's state by sound alone
- The travel alembic's copper fittings have a patina that reads orange in warm light and grey in cold light; she considers this a reliable ambient-light indicator and notes it in the calibration-check log entry
- The case's weight when full is the single most consistent physical object in her life — same weight, same center of gravity, same distribution; she is aware of it as a constant against which other objects and situations are measured

## Portability

Carried by porter when traveling; carried by her directly when operational within a location. She does not leave the case in the hands of household staff or general ship's crew. It travels in her cabin, in her assigned room, within the scope of her personal oversight. If she must leave it unattended, she sets three observable tells — the clasp catch angle, the position of the secondary compartment's cloth, the label on the front-facing ceramic container — that she checks on return.

---

## Carry State

Weight: approximately thirty pounds full; approximately eighteen pounds with minimal variable components. Too heavy for extended personal carry; requires porter or assigned stable location. Concealment: the maester's-instrument-case exterior is the primary concealment; it has passed inspection as such. Noise: the ceramic-on-wool sound described above; she manages this by maintaining the wool packing, which she replaces when compressed.

---

## Functional State

**Full operational:** the kit is at sea-voyage-ready load. All fixed components present; variable components at full deployment quantity; reference extract current. This is the kit's state when departing the Red Keep for an extended operation.

**Active deployment:** variable components being consumed; the kit is operational at the current location. The calibration protocol has been run. The lamped brazier is in use or recently used.

**Depleted/restock pending:** one or more variable components below the safety margin; factor contact has been engaged to resupply. The case still functions but the active regimen is at reduced pace. She documents depleted-state entries in the supplement with an asterisk.

**The Yi Tish poison-master's case:** Acquired in Yi Ti (Book II, Movement 2). A separate, smaller fitted case with its own label system (partially translated into her notation; some labels remain in Yi Tish script with phonetic annotations she is still rendering into her system). The case contains the exotic toxin set she ate through as "advanced cauldron-seasoning." It integrates with the standard kit but is never stored inside it — carried alongside as a second case. By Book III it has been fully incorporated into the regimen's variable-component rotation, and the remaining contents represent her highest-tier documented tolerance frontier.
