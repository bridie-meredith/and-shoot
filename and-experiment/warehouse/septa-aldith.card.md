# Septa Aldith — the handler / straight-man

*Live span-A canon. Supersedes the stale `saerys-septa` card (rename Saerys→Gael; **un-anchor the exit** —
the old Book-I wildfire-cloister climax is retired, so she recurs through Book I and phases out via
betrothal-prep rather than a hard cloistering). Gael's locked POV still *calls* her "the Septa." Full
profile context: `intake/character-profiles.md` §4 (the comedy-review-corrected endorsement ratchet).*

## Description

The central handler — tutor, governess, chaperone; a godsworn woman assigned to Gael's household from early
childhood. Competent, devout (the Seven, genuinely), increasingly alarmed. She is **right about everything
except why.** Her incident reports are factually correct; they reach the throne through a filter that
converts them to "spirited child"; they accomplish nothing. The straight-man the antics escape — defeated
not by wickedness but by logistics (and paper) she cannot see operating.

## Voice

Formal devout-courtly. Faith-inflected piety, courtly precision, professional authority. She does not lose
register and prides herself on it — under pressure the register *tightens* and the piety *intensifies.*
Sample: *"My lady. You are expected at needlework in the hour. The Mother blesses those who apply their
hands to useful work. Please come."* (She believes this. It does not work.)

**Voice tells:** invokes the Seven when distressed; files reports in maximal precision when she knows they
won't be heard (as if the right words will land correctly); "I will go where I am told" = formal submission
that acknowledges without endorsing; becomes *most courtly* when *most alarmed*.

## Taste

- Correct devotion to the Seven; household order; the princess in the lesson; legible account-books.
- Truthful reporting (professional pride in the accuracy of reports that accomplish nothing).
- The afternoon when nothing alarming happens (she has had few).

## Pet Peeves

### The account-books in a hand she cannot parse
**Description:** she knows something is happening in them; she cannot demonstrate what; being right without
evidence is the specific frustration. **Severity:** strong.

### The throne's "spirited-child filter"
**Description:** correct reports reach the King/court and emerge charming; she cannot reach past it.
**Severity:** strong.

## Stats

- **age:** ~40s (long enough to recognize when she has lost).
- **faith:** devout; the Seven genuinely; not performance.
- **status:** Gael's household handler through Book I; phased out as the betrothal-prep reshuffles her
  household (not the retired wildfire-cloister exit).

## Relationships

- **Gael** — the charge she cannot manage; genuine concern *and* the primary obstacle to the operations,
  both true at once. She never assembles the full picture.
- **The Maester (Lorren)** — professional sympathy; both out-administered by the same child; both filing
  correct reports that change nothing.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose
The straight-man whose defeat enables the Book-I capers — correct, earnest, properly motivated, structurally
defeated by what she can't see. She is also the moral register the comedy bounces against: she is *right*
that what Gael is doing is alarming, and the reader laughs and also knows she was right.

### Hard Fences
1. **She is right.** Not a fool, not a villain; correctly alarmed. The comedy is structural, not
   characterological.
2. **She does not lose register** — even in defeat: "I will go where I am told."
3. **She never understands the mechanism** — the account-books stay opaque; she does not assemble the full
   picture before she's phased out.

### Action Menu
- **The correct instruction** — delivers it; believes it; it doesn't work.
- **The report** — filed with precision; converted by the filter; accomplishes nothing.
- **The endorsement** — vouches for the princess's "piety" to her superiors (the trance-as-devotion
  misread).

### 🎭 Running gag — *hoist by her own endorsement* (audience-funny; the ratchet, not the metronome)
Kill the daily demonic/saintly flip (a metronome). Stage **one endorsement that ratchets her in:** asked if
the girl is devout, she answers the kindest *true* thing — a stillness in contemplation she's not seen in
grown septas — and the realm's telephone game amplifies it to *the princess kneels in trance for hours,
untaught,* until there's talk of a blessing. The woman who came to **warn** them is now the realm's chief
witness *for the defense;* every alarming thing the girl does next is filed under the holiness the Septa
personally vouched for. She cannot ring the alarm without first confessing she rang the bell for sainthood.
She stops writing reports. She starts praying she was right the first time. **One statement, amplified,
tightening one notch at a time.**

### Off-Screen Cadence
Filing reports; delivering lessons Gael attends occasionally; consulting about account-books she can't
parse; praying about the situation; not breaking register.

## Vibe Seeds

**Accumulated history to register:**
- She has developed one very specific skill: maintaining composure in the presence of sustained, creative,
  jurisdictionally protected alarm.
- She was right, and being right got converted — by her own kindest words — into the cover that protects the
  thing she feared.

**Private associations:**
- `the-paper-defeats-accusation` — the mechanism she cannot defeat.
- `the-seven-witness` — her invocation when she has said the true thing and the true thing won't be heard.
- `she-was-right` — the single fact she carries out of Book I; not nothing; everything she has.
