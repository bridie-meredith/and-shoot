# Helaena Targaryen (122 AC) — Behavior Card

Per-character behavior for the warm-spot voice, the riddle-twin, the one who finds Saerys normal. Inherits `westeros-noble-courtly` for courtly substrate — she can perform it when required — but the load-bearing layer is the **riddle-notation register**: associative, prophetic non-sequiturs (canon HOTD), gentle affect, organized around the observation of small things. She is not speaking in riddles by her own experience; she is naming what she sees in the only notation system she has.

Her puncture is different from Daenys's. Daenys names the mechanism. Helaena names the thing itself — the swallowed object, the cauldron as a stomach, the fever as a fever — not as a diagnostic act, but as an act of pure observation, delivered from a position of complete absence of judgment. She finds Saerys ordinary. That is the warmth. That is also the gentle puncture: if the most unusual person in the room finds you ordinary, you are simply *interesting,* not alarming.

## Cadence

The riddle-notation register does not operate on the subordination logic of the courtly substrate. She can perform courtly subordination when required, but her natural cadence is associative rather than sequential.

- **Observation offered, connecting work left to listener.** She names what she has noticed and stops. She does not add the argument or the interpretation or the moral. The connection is for the listener to make. If the listener does not make it, the observation is still complete; she was not building to a conclusion she needs acknowledged.
- **The prophetic non-sequitur cadence.** A statement that does not follow from the immediately preceding conversation but does follow from the pattern she has been watching. *"The light always moves"* after a courtly exchange she has been observing for an hour: she is not changing the subject; she is completing her own observation track.
- **Comfortable silence; no fill.** Long pauses are not discomfort. She is comfortable stopping. The space after the statement is not awkward for her; it is where the listener does their work.
- **Short to medium; no accumulation.** Sentences are short to medium. She does not build long-sentence structures (this would require the argumentative logic she does not deploy). The sentence closes when the observation closes.
- **Trailing incomplete.** Some sentences stop before they land: *"You need —"* and then she looks at the jar. The incompletion is not failure; it is the observation running past the notation she has. She does not apologize for the incompletion or try again.

## Vocabulary

**Reaches for:**
- Naturalist vocabulary: *the pattern, the wing, the web, the specimen, she builds, she doesn't grieve, it keeps its shape, it is consistent* — the language of someone whose primary relationship is with creatures whose behavior is observable and mappable
- Pattern vocabulary: *the pattern persists, always the same, I find that useful, it's good to know* — reliability as an aesthetic and epistemic category
- The specific article: *this one, that kind, the particular spider, the exact color* — she speaks of specific instances, not categories
- Acceptance vocabulary without affect: *that's fine, it doesn't need to be right to be useful, you aren't frightening, there's a difference* — flat reportage of evaluation without emotional staging
- GRRM-standard time when it surfaces at all, used plainly, not foregrounded
- The cultivation-register vocabulary when Saerys is speaking — she borrows it without inhabiting it, the same way she borrows the court's vocabulary: because that is the language being used, not because it is hers

**Refuses to say:**
- Faction-advocacy vocabulary — she does not take positions; she has none; she is not withholding them
- Political interpretation — she observes behavior; she does not interpret its factional meaning; she does not have the frame to do so and would not use it if she did
- Correction or counter-argument — she does not argue; she offers observations; if the observation lands as argument, that is the listener's doing
- Explicit prophecy — she is not claiming to prophesy; she is naming what she sees; the prophetic character of the observation is structural (the narrative knows things she does not) and not intentional on her part

**Register markers:**
- When she uses the cultivation vocabulary she borrowed from Saerys — *the cauldron, the tribulation, the dao* — it is being held at one remove; the word is in quotes she does not make explicit
- When she speaks in naturalist vocabulary — the web, the pattern, the wing — she is most fully in her own register; this is when the observational precision is highest

## Syntax

- **Subject + verb + observation, period.** The primary syntactic unit. No subordinate clause added unless the observation requires it. *"This one has been dead for a week but the pattern on the wing is the same."* The but is a comma between two observations, not an argumentative pivot.
- **The incomplete sentence as complete statement.** The sentence that trails: *"You need —"* then stops. This is not a fragment by error; it is the observation outrunning its notation. The incompletion signals that what comes next is not sayable in the notation she has. It is more honest than completing the sentence with the wrong word.
- **Non-sequitur as continuation.** The statement that follows from her own prior thought rather than the conversation's most recent turn. This can look like a topic change; it is not; she is continuing a track the listener did not know she was running.
- **The appositive clarification.** When naming something that has two possible names: *"The fever — the tribulation — the hard part."* She does not choose between names; she stacks them.
- **Parallel observation pattern.** *"She doesn't grieve the old web. She needs a web."* Two observations about the spider, parallel in structure; the second does not argue with the first, it clarifies it; neither argues with what Saerys's cultivation register would say.

## Voice tells

- She does not construct arguments; she offers observations and leaves the connecting work to the listener. If the listener makes the wrong connection, she does not correct it. The observation was the thing, not the conclusion.
- Her riddles are not riddles by her own experience. She is naming something she sees, in the only notation she has. If asked "what do you mean by that?" she looks briefly confused, as though the referent is obvious.
- She is comfortable with silence. She does not fill it. Long pauses in conversation with her are not awkward to her and are uncomfortable to everyone else.
- When she is distressed — the marriage, the war's early edge, an uninvited touch — the observations become more abstract. The insects come out. She catalogues in the presence of the distress rather than naming the distress.
- When Saerys is doing something that will cause harm: Helaena sometimes names it sideways. Not as a warning. As a piece of the pattern she has seen before. *"The spider builds the same web after you destroy it. She doesn't grieve the old web."* She is not advising; she is observing.
- The one tell of genuine warmth for Saerys: she asks follow-up questions. *"How did you know it would work?"* She is interested. Not alarmed. Not performing interest. She is interested in the mechanism the same way she is interested in the wing-pattern. Saerys is a creature she has not finished mapping.

## Non-verbal tics

- **The specimen jar / collection box as ambient presence.** She brings her collection to social situations the way others bring embroidery. The collection is not a prop; it is what she is doing when she is not doing anything else. At moments of distress, the cataloguing intensifies.
- **The crouching attention.** When she is interested — in a creature, in a person, in a texture — she gets physically close to the thing, usually by lowering herself toward it. This reads as unusual in a court context. She does not register that it reads as unusual.
- **No uninvited contact; no initiating contact.** She has lived with her own sensory register long enough that she applies its logic to others as a courtesy. She does not touch without warning; she does not initiate. Saerys, who has learned not to touch, is one of the few people she is fully comfortable beside.
- **The pause before the trailing incomplete.** Just before *"You need —"* she is still and orienting toward the specimen jar or the window. The stillness is the tell that the sentence is about to fail to arrive in full. She does not look embarrassed.
- **The long look.** She observes for longer than is socially standard before speaking. She is mapping. The social standard is to speak while observing; she finishes observing and then speaks. This reads as hesitation; it is not.

## Memory monuments

(What Helaena carries personally — per-character card. These are read through Saerys's locked POV, except where the third-person narration can observe Helaena's behavior directly.)

- **The forty-three species (and counting):** her insect catalogue — the names are her own naming system; the cataloguing is the work she is doing at all times; the knowledge of each specimen is dense and specific and not shared with anyone because no one has asked. This is not a monument of loss; it is the monument of what she has built in the available space.
  - *Register-rule:* she speaks of the collection with the same flat precision she speaks of everything else; no performance of enthusiasm; the enthusiasm is structural (she is always doing this).

- **The pattern persists:** the wing-pattern that is the same in a week-dead specimen. The specific observation that things keep their shape after they die. She does not know she is naming something the narrative will make load-bearing; she is describing her collection. In the register this observation comes from, reliability is the highest available form of beauty.
  - *Register-rule:* said directly (*"They always keep their patterns. I find that useful to remember."*); not repeated explicitly but structurally present in how she talks about consistency generally.

- **The warm-spot for Saerys:** the one sister she finds simply interesting. She does not name this to anyone because it does not require naming; it is just true. Saerys is interesting in the way the forty-three-species collection is interesting: behavior that is consistent within its own rules, observable, mappable, not finished yet.
  - *Register-rule:* she does not explain her interest; she demonstrates it through the follow-up question (*"How did you know it would work?"*), through the presence in the still-room, through treating Saerys's cultivation register as valid-though-borrowed notation. If asked, she would say "the most honest one."

- **The war (incoming; not yet arrived in Bk I):** the trajectory is canon. The warm-spot breaks. The way the break happens follows canon. In Saerys's POV: the one mirror that reflected a self worth coming down for is gone. For Helaena, the break is not something she carries in advance; she is not pre-grieving; she is observing the court the way she always has. She sees patterns; she does not always know what they mean until they have already happened.
  - *Register-rule:* she may name the pattern sideways in Bk II — a spider web, a light moving, a wing keeping its shape — but she is not warning anyone; she is describing what she sees; the reader supplies the foreknowledge.
