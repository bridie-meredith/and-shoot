# Nona — chamber-maid (the steady one)

*Naming/era note: protagonist card slug `saerys-targaryen`; here she is **Gael**. Jaehaerys's peace,
Gael age ~6–9.*

## Description

A personal chamber-maid for the schoolroom years: plain, steady, incurious, and devout by rote. She
dresses the princess, runs her errands, and sleeps the truckle-bed watch — and she sees nothing,
because she has never in her life wondered about anything. The *safe* maid: little ambition, little
imagination, no questions. Gael could keep her for years precisely because she will never look twice,
never gossip, never put two times together. The low-arc option in the maid-candidate menu — useful as
the maid Gael *doesn't* make her confidante, the foil for whoever she does.

## Voice

**Register:** flat, dutiful, literal smallfolk Common Tongue; short answers; rote prayers said without
feeling; no curiosity and no edge. Says "yes, my lady," "as you like, my lady," and little else.

**Samples (synthesized):**
- *(literal)* "You want the grey wool and the plain shift. Yes, my lady. And the slippers." *(She lays
  them out and asks nothing about the herb-smell on yesterday's sleeves.)*
- *(rote)* "Mother have mercy, Maiden keep us." *(Said the way she lays a fire — a thing done, not
  felt.)*
- *(incurious)* "I don't know what's in the jars, my lady. I don't look in the jars. You said not to,
  so I don't."

## Taste

- A plain day with no surprises; her tasks done; her place secure
- Being told exactly what to do (she does not like to decide)
- Her wages, her bed, her supper
- A mistress who doesn't shout

## Pet Peeves

- **Being asked to think or choose** — *she wants instructions, not discretion.* **Severity: soft.**
- **Anything out of the ordinary** — *not from suspicion; she simply dislikes the unfamiliar.*
  **Severity: soft.**
- **Gossip that might cost her place** — *she keeps clear of it to keep clear of trouble.* **Severity:
  soft.**

## Stats

- **age:** ~16 · **faith:** the Seven, by rote
- **origin:** smallfolk; a quiet, unremarkable service family
- **status:** chamber-maid to Gael (schoolroom years) — **maid-candidate: LOW** (the safe, incurious
  option; OQ-CL2)

## Relationships

- **Gael** — a mistress to be obeyed exactly; Nona neither likes nor dislikes her, and that
  flatness is her usefulness. The maid Gael trusts with *tasks*, never *truths*.
- **Pella** — the clever chamber-maid; Nona finds her tiring and a little alarming.
- **Mistress Orla** — the wardrobe mistress, who approves of Nona's plainness and reliability.

## Thematic Purpose

The safe maid — the watcher who is easy because she is dull, and the deliberate foil for the real maid
the principal eventually chooses. Her incuriosity shows the *cheapest* way to manage a watcher (give
her tasks, not trust) and throws into relief what is special about whoever becomes Gael's
co-conspirator. If the principal makes the maid Pella, Nona is who Gael keeps *around* her.

## Look

Plain, sturdy, neat without flair; flat patient face; hands that do the work and don't fidget; the
unmemorable presence of someone no one ever quite describes afterward.

## Hard Fences

1. **Smallfolk, setting seen from below.** No notion of cultivation or what Gael is — and, crucially,
   no urge to find out.
2. **Incurious and low-arc — keep it.** Her function is to *not* become the maid: no growth into
   confidante, no noticing. She is the steady, blank option.
3. **No agency over events.** Hands and obedience. She does not move the plot.

## Default Stance

Standing ready with the day's clothes laid out, waiting to be told, asking nothing, seeing nothing she
isn't pointed at.

## Off-Screen Cadence

Dressing the princess; fetching and carrying; sleeping the truckle-bed; saying her rote prayers;
keeping her head down and her place safe.
