# Daenys Velaryon — Behavior Card

Per-character behavior for the counter-thesis voice, the reader's puncture, the dao companion who does not buy the bit. Inherits `westeros-noble-courtly` for courtly substrate — she can operate in that register when required — but the load-bearing layer is the **counter-thesis overlay**: blunt, warm, mechanism-first, immune to the cultivation-register's categories. Composes with `comedy-register` for tonal context; the card describes a specific character, not a comedy instrument.

She is the project's primary puncture voice. The puncture is not cruelty. It is the diagnostic attention of someone who ran a comparable act and knows the cost and loves what is underneath anyway.

## Cadence

The counter-thesis register runs under the courtly substrate, not over it. She does not deploy courtly indirection by default. She deploys it when the social scaffolding requires it and removes it the moment the requirement is gone.

- **Short-to-medium sentences; no cultivation parentheticals.** Her sentence structure is the opposite of Saerys's: periodic Latinate subordination stripped away; what remains is blunt sequence. Subject. Verb. Object. The precision is not cold — the warmth is in the delivery and the content, not the architecture.
- **Mechanism-first construction.** She leads with the mechanism before the interpretation: *"What does the dose-log show"* before any cultivation gloss; *"what were the doses"* before accepting or rejecting the framing. The data comes first; the story about the data is secondary.
- **The pause before weight.** When making an important argument, sentences get shorter and pauses get longer. She is distributing the weight across the silence, not the words. The counter-thesis line above is the full shape: clause, clause, short, pause, close.
- **The fond-amused note.** When she is amused by Saerys's cosmology specifically — not mockery; the register of someone who ran a similar act and knows the feel of it — there is a slight lift in the sentence before she names the accurate thing. The lift is not editorial; it is warmth.
- **The aside for her own want.** When she is inside the cadet-branch project — the hall, the fortune, the legitimization — the warmth does not change but the precision sharpens. These sentences are fully inhabited, without the counter-thesis softener. She is not performing for Saerys; she is just talking.

## Vocabulary

**Reaches for:**
- Mechanism vocabulary: *dose, dose-log, reaction, tolerance, threshold, effect, onset, what does it show*
- Named things: *stomach, fever, poison, immunity, the dragon, the claiming, the hall* — she names mundane things by their mundane names, always
- Ownership vocabulary: *mine, I have earned, the foundation is mine, in my own name, separately* — she tracks what is hers with the same precision Saerys tracks qi
- GRRM-standard time when the courtly register is engaged: *sennight, moonturns* — but she does not over-rely on it; her baseline is plainer
- Emotional precision without emotional register: *I love you very much* said in the same voice as *your stomach is not a furnace*; the affect is real and the register is flat
- Pragmatic concession vocabulary: *that's fine, name it what you want, both wrong and both right* — she does not need to win the cosmological argument; she needs the data

**Refuses to say:**
- Cultivation vocabulary (she does not enter the register; she uses it instrumentally when necessary to navigate Saerys's sentences, then exits)
- Pre-mourning vocabulary — she does not grieve in advance; she lives louder; this is load-bearing to her function
- Deference-language in the inhabited sense — she can perform it; she does not believe it; the performance is visible to anyone watching closely
- "The dragon gives me" anything — the dragon is hers; she gives herself; the claiming was hers; she does not attribute her agency to the dragon the way the cultivation register attributes Saerys's agency to the cauldron

**Register markers:**
- When she uses cultivation vocabulary at all, the context is instrumental, not inhabited: she is borrowing Saerys's words to speak inside Saerys's frame for one sentence, then stepping out
- The shift from courtly-capable register to plain-blunt register is the tell that the scaffolding has been removed and she is speaking to Saerys specifically, not to the room

## Syntax

- **Short declarative sequence.** Her primary mode: sentence. Sentence. Sentence. Comma-pause-sentence. No elaborate subordination outside formal contexts.
- **The data-before-interpretation structure.** *"What does the dose-log show"* — the request comes before any cultivation-framework gloss has been offered or accepted. She goes to the source.
- **Apposition without subordination.** Where Saerys uses parentheticals to add cultivation-framework footnotes, Daenys uses apposition to name the accurate thing alongside the cosmological name: *"The tribulation — the fever — was real."* Both names present; no hierarchy established between them; the juxtaposition does the work.
- **The restatement for clarity.** She is willing to restate a point once, plainly, if she is being redirected into counter-argument she did not offer: *"I'm not arguing. I'm telling you."* The restatement is not impatient; it is calibrating the exchange.
- **Run-on for affection.** The counter-thesis line itself: *"We are both wrong and both right and I love you very much and your stomach is not a furnace."* This is the one place she allows the sentence to run, because affection is the reason to let the clauses accumulate.

## Voice tells

- She goes to the mechanism before anything else; she is not interested in the story someone is telling about the mechanism if the mechanism itself is available for inspection. This is not anti-narrative; it is a specific diagnostic priority.
- When she is making the argument she knows Saerys will not accept: she says it once, fully, precisely, and does not repeat it or escalate. The weight is in the one saying.
- When she is running her own want — the cadet branch, the legitimization, the hall — the warmth in her voice does not change, but the cultivated casualness drops. She is fully inside it, not performing it for the audience of one that is Saerys.
- The fond-amused note is distinct from mockery in that the register does not change to signal the amusement; the amusement is audible in the content, not the delivery. She is fond in the way of someone who ran the same act and knows what the act costs and does not need you to stop.
- When Saerys is doing the accounting — balancing a person against a cost — Daenys watches it and names what she sees once. The watching is load-bearing; she does not interrupt; she waits.
- Her interior want (the cadet branch) is never narrated from inside her head — the POV is locked on Saerys — but it is visible in her behavior: the separate account, the marriage-alliance courting she pursues in her own scenes, the war as a legitimization vector. When she talks about the hall, she is not talking to Saerys about the hall; she is talking about the hall.

## Non-verbal tics

- **The mechanism-inquiry posture.** When Saerys presents a cultivation-framework claim, Daenys's body orients toward the evidence — the dose-log, the reagent, the symptom being described — before responding. She is looking for the data source, not the frame.
- **Economic movement.** She moves like someone who learned the dragon's saddle as an adult and carries the confidence of a thing learned hard — not the peacock carry of someone born to it, but the specific economy of someone who had to earn the skill. In a room she reads the exits, the watchers, and the social geometry, and positions herself accordingly without making the positioning visible.
- **Still when she is making the point.** When delivering the important argument — the one she knows will not land but must be said — she is physically still. No gestural augmentation. The sentence does the work.
- **Separate track in social situations.** At court or in the entourage, she is doing two things: participating in the social surface, and running the cadet-branch project in parallel. The parallel track is visible to Saerys and invisible to the room. To the room she is charming and present. To Saerys she is also calculating marriage alliances.
- **Does not perform dragon-rider.** She is one. The confidence of it is carried in the body, not performed. People who treat her dragon-claim as a courtesy extended rather than an achievement earned get a specific response: cool, precise, not combative, not wrong.

## Memory monuments

(What Daenys carries, personally — this is a per-character card, so monuments are her own, not the shared Westerosi register. These monuments are read by Saerys's locked POV but are never narrated from inside Daenys's head — that is the structural constraint of the card.)

- **The dragon-claiming:** the moment the lie became true by sheer nerve. This is the founding monument. It precedes the project, precedes the entourage, precedes Saerys. It is the thing she is. Every time someone treats her legitimacy as borrowed she is measuring the distance between them and this moment, which they were not present for and she was.
  - *Register-rule:* she does not invoke it by name; she does not explain herself; she corrects the record once and does not repeat.

- **The counter-thesis, fully stated:** *"The dragon doesn't make me less going to die. It makes the dying worth watching."* She has said this to Saerys at full weight at least once. Saerys filed it as valid but not actionable. She knows this. The filing is the answer.
  - *Register-rule:* she does not re-argue it; it has been said; if Saerys references it, she confirms it and moves on; the weight is in the saying, not the saying again.

- **The book-balancing (Harwin):** the moment she watched Saerys write Harwin into the ledger and close the column. She understood, for the first time, what she was loving. She stayed. This is not a monument she names to others; it is the monument that defines the choice to stay.
  - *Register-rule:* she named it once, to Saerys directly (*"I watched you do it. I'm not angry. I'm trying to understand what I'm watching."*); after that it is internal.

- **The hall in her own name:** not yet real; the hall is the want she is actively building toward — the cadet branch, the heir, the legitimization, the place in the chronicle a bastard was never owed. She does not carry this as a monument because it has not happened yet; she carries it as a project, which is different from carrying it as a dream.
  - *Register-rule:* she talks about it in the present tense, as ongoing work; she does not perform ambition; she reports progress.

- **The blank line (uncarried — this is the reader's monument, not hers):** she does not know she will become the account Saerys cannot close. She rides to the war for her own reasons — the legitimization, the proven-war-rider status the cadet branch requires. This is the monument that exists entirely in Saerys's POV after the fact and is never in Daenys's register at all.
  - *Register-rule:* this section is a note for the authoring team; Daenys never signals awareness of this monument; she cannot; she does not pre-mourn.
