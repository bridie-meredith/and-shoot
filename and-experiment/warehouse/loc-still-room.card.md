# Location — The Still-Room

The operational center of Saerys's project. The place that smells like her. The space she has built inside the space she was given.

In her cultivation-register, the still-room is the Inferior Path's first completed domain formation — a space she has arranged according to an internally consistent theory of optimal qi-flow that is actually an internally consistent theory of efficient workflow and contamination prevention. The "qi-gathering formation" is ergonomics. The formation is real; the qi is not flowing through it.

**Cross-reference:** The still-room is physically embedded in `loc-red-keep-interior` (Maester's Tower section). This card describes it as a distinct space, extends beyond what that card covers, and adds the portable dimension — the still-room kit she re-establishes at each new location.

---

## Geography

Inside the Maester's Tower, one level below the maester's primary library and consulting room. Stone walls, stone floor, small high windows that admit limited but predictable light. Access via the main maester's-tower corridor, or via the back stair — a servants' delivery stair that connects this level to the supply area of the outer ring and is not documented in any household plan she has accessed.

The still-room is described as "the maester's still-room" in household records. She obtained access through a combination of persistent curiosity (she was seven; she simply kept appearing in it), competence-as-foothold (the maester's supply ledgers were chaotic; she fixed them; he could not function without the fixed ledgers; the dependency became operational cover), and the maester's specific personality (he disapproves of her presence but cannot articulate a ledger-based objection). By the time he thought to formally revoke access, he had already lost the administrative capacity to manage without her.

## Layout

**Fixed dimensions and features:**
- Cold-water basin along the east wall: stone trough fed by a pipe; fills on a regular interval she has used to calibrate work-sessions without a candle-clock; the trickle of refilling water is audible and useful as a timer
- Drying racks along the north wall: her use has restructured these; the original arrangement followed a Citadel-standard system she replaced at year two; the new arrangement is cultivationally accurate and functionally incomprehensible to anyone using the standard system; the maester has accepted the rearrangement because the materials he uses most frequently are now, somehow, more accessible
- Brazier (fixed position, south wall): small; for heating compounds; the charcoal she uses is a specific grade she sources through the below-stairs faction because the standard charcoal burns dirty
- The maester's labeled jar-rows: three shelving units she has not replaced but has extended with additional shelving she installed herself using materials from the supply area; the label system is dual — the maester's labels on the front of each jar; her parallel relabeling system on small cards she slides behind the jars, visible only to someone who knows to look behind them
- Her work table (center): added by her, not original to the room; the surface is marked with years of compound work; specific stains are documented in the dose-log as "calibration markers" for batch consistency
- The maester's supply cabinet (east wall, adjacent to basin): originally locked; she solved this within a moonturns of acquiring still-room access by identifying the household's spare-key location; she restocks items she removes with near-identical replacements where possible; the cabinet's inventory is known to her in detail; the maester's knowledge is an approximation

**The back stair (critical secondary feature):**
A servants' delivery stair connecting the still-room level to the supply delivery area of the outer ring. Stone steps, no light source (she has memorized the step count and the one loose step at position eleven). The stair is structurally sound but unknown to anyone she has not told. The septa does not know it exists. She found it at age six behind a storage rack she moved while looking for something else; she moved the storage rack back and has moved it herself every time she uses the stair since.

The back stair is the most significant architectural exit in her operational geography because it provides movement between the still-room and the outer supply area without crossing the monitored maester's-tower corridor. All incoming materials that cannot be explained by normal supply chains arrive this way.

**The maester's library (above, functionally adjacent):**
Not part of the still-room proper but operationally linked. She has been rearranging the library since she was five. The rearrangement is "cultivationally accurate" by her taxonomy — substances sorted by reagent tier, not by Citadel classification; texts sorted by utility to the Inferior Path, not by subject or author. The maester accepts this because the texts he uses most frequently are now more accessible. He does not know why.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**Primary register:** dried herbs — complex, seasonal, layered; base notes from the drying racks (elder herb, lavender from the previous season, bunches of something that came from the Braavosi factor); middle notes from the jar-rows (the specific clay smell of particular containers she prefers for certain preparations); surface notes from whatever she is currently working on

**Secondary register:** recent compound work — sharper, more volatile, highly variable by project; the still-room on a passive day smells different from the still-room an hour after she has been heating something; she uses the difference to assess whether anyone else has been in the room while she was absent

**Tertiary register:** the third chemical layer — the register she has introduced over years that the maester cannot identify and has stopped trying to; she knows what it is; it is the cumulative ambient presence of the dose-log regimen, which has left its trace in the room the way a practitioner leaves a trace in their cultivation space; a faint clean-acid smell under the herbs, metallic when the brazier is active; the still-room carries her in the air in a way that the persona card notes she carries the still-room on her skin

**Sound profile:** the cold-water trickle on refill (her primary timer); the brazier's low heat-breath when active; occasional settling of the structure (stone building; predictable sounds she has mapped); the absence of the corridor's wax-and-cedar formal atmosphere means sound behaves differently here — this room does not have tapestries and the acoustics are the bare stone acoustics, which carry sound further and less selectively than the formal spaces

**Light:** the small windows admit a predictable angle of light at predictable hours; she uses the light position as a secondary time marker; the direct morning light falls on a specific jar-row she has arranged for that reason

## The Portable Still-Room Kit

The still-room is not fixed to the Maester's Tower. She has developed a portable version that she deploys whenever she is away from the Red Keep for an extended period — the Essosi voyage in Book II, the ship in Book III, any location where she is operating for more than a few days.

The kit is carried in a fitted case she had made to her specifications by a craftsman she found through the below-stairs faction. Exterior: a sturdy leather traveling case that resembles a maester's instrument kit (which it is not). Interior: subdivided with partitions of her own design.

**Fixed components (carried every time):**
- A small alembic (glass body, copper fittings; not the still-room's main alembic, which is fixed; this is a quarter-scale travel version)
- A precision scale (her own standard; she established her unit of measure at age eight and has used it consistently; no other scale reads the same way)
- Ten small ceramic containers with sealed lids (she relabels these for each deployment)
- A lamped brazier (collapsible; the only portable heat source in the kit; requires specific charcoal, which she sources at each location through established channels or substitutes with documented adjustments)
- A copy of the dose-log's summary pages (not the log itself; a transcribed extract of the tolerance thresholds she needs for the current deployment; updated before each departure)
- A blank-leaf supplement for new entries during travel

**Variable components (assembled per deployment):**
- The substance supply for the current cultivation tier — whatever the dose-log's current protocol calls for, sourced for the deployment duration plus a margin she calculates from prior voyage lengths
- Location-specific additions: the Essosi deployment included Tears of Lys full-concentration series and the first basilisk venom preliminary; the Yi Ti deployment included the poison-master's case (a separate instrument set she acquired there and integrates with but does not conflate with the standard kit)

**Re-establishment protocol:**
When she arrives at a new location, the still-room is re-established before anything else — before unpacking, before assessing the space, before sleeping. The first hour in any new location is finding where the still-room will be: where is the light adequate, where is the air flow correct, where is the access from outside possible, where is the heat source positioned. Once this is determined the kit unpacks and the space becomes the still-room. She has done this in a maester's chamber in Braavos, in a two-room factor's flat in Pentos, in a ship's cabin on the Essosi voyage, and in a single-room accommodation in Yi Ti.

The Yi Tish poison-master's case (acquired Book II, travels with her from that point onward) integrates with the kit but is held separately — its own fitted case, its own labeling system (she is still translating some of the labels into a system she fully controls). The case expanded the reagent set significantly and introduced substance handling protocols she has documented as "advanced Inferior Path technique variants, originating in the Eastern school."

In her cultivation-register, the portable kit is classified as a Tier 1 spirit-tool collection: "the alembic, the specific scale, the collection of labeled and unlabeled containers, the lamped brazier, the reference texts — classified as a unit rather than individual objects, as their function is the unit function and the individual components are operationally incomplete."

**The Essosi precedent:** Re-establishing the still-room in Essos demonstrated that the cultivation domain is the practitioner, not the location. She narrates this as "the domain expands with the practitioner's advancement; the formation seeds itself wherever the practitioner operates for sufficient duration; the formation eye is the practitioner." The domain-is-the-practitioner insight is one of the places where the cultivation register produces an operationally useful observation: her methods are indeed portable because her methods live in her, not in the Red Keep's stone.

## Hazards

**The compound failure:** something she is working on produces an unexpected response — smell, color change, audible reaction. The question is whether anyone outside the still-room registered it. Two events of this type have escalated: the wildfire-shard fever (the most serious compound failure; three days of consequences; documented in dose-log entry 31) and a less-serious but more visible reaction in Book II that required rapid sanitization and a cover story involving the maester's "experimental new preparations"

**The inventory discrepancy:** the maester performs periodic assessments of his supply cabinet; her restocking protocol covers most deviations; the residual risk is a discrepancy she has not yet replaced catching his attention; her mitigation is the ledger-dependency (he cannot effectively assess inventory without her ledger support, which she controls the frequency of)

**Back stair discovery:** if anyone finds the stair, its value as a covert access route ends immediately; the stair's existence is maintained by the rack that conceals its entrance, which she replaces exactly each time; one incomplete replacement would expose it; she has drilled the rack-replacement until it is automatic

**Unknown third-party access:** the still-room is hers in practice but his in title; a sufficiently senior instruction to the maester could reassign it; she maintains the ledger-dependency as the primary guard against this; as of Book I's climax, the King's-hand note provides a supplementary guarantee

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- The cold-water trickle stops: pipe blockage; she has now lost her primary work-session timer and must estimate
- The brazier's charcoal is wrong grade: someone substituted standard-grade charcoal for her specific grade in the last below-stairs delivery; the temperature control is off; the current compound batch is suspect
- The back stair rack is slightly out of position: she set it; someone moved it; someone has been on the stair; she does not know when or who
- The maester's jar-row has been disturbed: one jar is in a slightly different position than she left it; this may be the maester retrieving a supply; it may be something else; it will take her ten minutes to verify that nothing has been accessed
- The light angle is wrong for the current operation: cloud cover, unexpectedly; the calibration she was using for a specific preparation requires the morning window light; she must wait or adjust
- Helaena in the doorway: how long has she been there, and what has she registered; her observations are not always about what they appear to be about
- The still-room smells wrong: not her wrong-smell; a different wrong-smell; someone has been in here, not to access the cabinet, but to stand and breathe; she does not know who or why
