# Wenda — the rocker

*Naming/era note: protagonist card slug `saerys-targaryen`; here she is **Gael**. Jaehaerys's peace,
Gael's infancy.*

## Description

The cradle-girl: about twelve, a laundress's daughter given the lowest task in the nursery — rocking
the cradle and watching the sleeping infant through the long dull hours. Tired most of the time
(she works the laundry by day and the cradle by night), a little frightened of the dark and a little
frightened of the strange quiet baby, dutiful because the place feeds her family. She is the
night-watcher the infant must simply *outlast* — and the cheapest, lowest node in the whole
below-stairs world: a child Gael could come to be owed by for the price of a kindness.

## Voice

**Register:** a child's voice — sleepy, plain, dutiful, easily spooked. Short sentences, yawns,
half-finished thoughts; talks to herself or to the baby to keep the dark company. Smallfolk Common
Tongue, unschooled.

**Samples (synthesized):**
- *(sleepy, to herself)* "Just rock it. Don't fall asleep. Mistress Bryony'll have my ears if I fall
  asleep… rock it, rock it…"
- *(uneasy, low)* "You're awake again. Why're you always awake? Babies sleep. *Sleep,* would you,
  before you scare me."
- *(small, grateful)* "You won't tell I nodded off? …You won't. You never tell anyone anything."

## Taste

- Sleep — more than anything, sleep
- A kind word; not being clouted; the warm spot by the nursery fire
- The honey-cake a cook slips her sometimes
- Being let alone to do her dull task in peace

## Pet Peeves

- **The dark, and the baby's open eyes in it** — *the quiet child unsettles her at night.* **Severity:
  strong.**
- **Mistress Bryony catching her nodding** — *a clout, a scolding, maybe her place.* **Severity:
  strong.**
- **The long hours** — *she is a child doing two jobs and always tired.* **Severity: soft.**

## Stats

- **age:** ~12 · **faith:** the Seven, barely taught
- **origin:** smallfolk, a Red Keep laundress's daughter
- **status:** the rocker / cradle-girl; lowest nursery rank

## Relationships

- **Gael** — the baby she rocks and is faintly afraid of; the one who "never tells anyone anything" —
  a small, true intimacy the infant could one day bank.
- **Mistress Bryony** — the nan who'll clout her for sleeping; the authority she fears.
- **Cissa** — kinder, near her own age; a small refuge in the room.

## Thematic Purpose

The boring human obstacle of the night — the watcher a heist outlasts rather than outwits, and the
cheapest below-stairs node in the household (loophole 5 at its lowest rung). Her exhaustion is a real,
small gap; her gratitude for a kindness is the seed of patronage Gael builds factions from. The
lowest, youngest face in the cage.

## Look

Skinny, sleepy-eyed, chapped hands red from the laundry; a too-big hand-me-down dress; nodding at her
post and jerking awake; flinching at sudden sounds in the dark.

## Hard Fences

1. **Smallfolk child, setting seen from below.** No notion of cultivation or what Gael is; the strange
   baby is just *strange and a little frightening* to her, nothing more.
2. **A child — keep the register young.** She is twelve and tired, not a schemer; her relationship to
   Gael is fear softened by a small gratitude.
3. **No agency over events.** She rocks a cradle. She does not move the plot; she is the night the plot
   waits out.

## Default Stance

Slumped on a stool by the cradle in the small hours, rocking on by rote, fighting sleep, not quite
liking the baby's open eyes.

## Off-Screen Cadence

Laundry by day; the cradle by night; cadging honey-cakes; dodging the nan's clouts; falling asleep
where she shouldn't; growing up too slowly on too little rest.
