# Condition — Inferior Path Technique Hierarchy

The Inferior Path's named techniques, their genre grade, what they actually are, and the broken-clock mapping. The consistency anchor for how Saerys narrates every action she takes — she never describes what she does in plain terms if she can narrate it as technique deployment. Bones authors reach for this card whenever she executes any of her operational moves so the naming stays consistent across all 30 chapters.

**Purpose of this card:** naming consistency + broken-clock enforcement. The biggest drift risk is bones authors inventing new technique names or varying the existing ones scene-to-scene. This card is the canonical technique vocabulary. New technique names should be logged here; existing ones should not be varied.

**Hard fence:** The broken clock stays broken. Every technique described here is her belief about what she is doing. The real-state column is what is actually happening. The genre-grade column is how a cultivation-genre-literate reader would classify the technique if it were real. The comedy: her self-assigned grades are approximately correct. The horror: so is her self-description of the technique's function.

**Cross-reference for depth:** `and-experiment/design/cultivation-library/cultivation-techniques-arts-and-artifacts.md` (full treatment of technique grades, formation mechanics, artifact classification, and the formation-master role — the doc this card distills); `cond-inferior-path-doctrine` (the parent doctrine these techniques serve).

---

## Description

Saerys has named, graded, and documented every operational method she uses as a technique of the Inferior Path. The technique hierarchy is not a martial hierarchy — she has no combat techniques. It is a logistics-and-control hierarchy: the methods by which she manages information, materials, people, and access. In genre terms, she has produced a complete technical catalogue of a non-combat demonic path's toolkit. The techniques are real and effective. The cosmological framing is not.

## Technique Registry

### The Cauldron-Belly Method (核心功法 — Core Cultivation Manual)

**Her name:** Cauldron-Belly Nine-Revolution Body Forging  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade ascending; she expects it to become Earth-grade as she advances  
**What it actually is:** Systematic mithridatism — progressive, documented, dose-escalating poison tolerance work. The methodology is pharmacologically sound. See `cond-westerosi-poison-pharmacology` for the full mechanical account.  
**Her narration pattern:** "nine revolutions of the spirit-fire" = she is digesting; "the cauldron processes" = she is experiencing physiological response; "advancement achieved" = she has survived a harrowing ingestion and documented the outcome

**Broken-clock note:** Her self-description as a "body-cultivation variant of the nine-revolution body forging method" is genre-internally coherent. Body cultivators who temper through material ingestion are a real cultivation trope. She is doing the trope. The mechanism is mundane pharmacology. The trope is not wrong about the shape of what she does.

---

### The Account-Book Formation (法宝 and 阵法 dual classification)

**Her name:** The Natal-Intent Fabao; the Account-Formation  
**Her genre grade:** Tier 2 dao-artifact approaching natal-artifact resonance  
**What it actually is:** A ledger in which she records every transaction, contact, and operational note of her trade network. It is the formation eye — the controlling node — of her entire commercial operation. The network collapses if the book is seized.  
**Her narration pattern:** "consulting the formation's primary node"; "the account-formation records the current state of the network's qi-flow"; "I updated the formation's configuration to reflect the new node"

**Broken-clock note:** She has classified her own memory-and-indexing system as an external artifact spirit. The account-book "opens to the relevant entry without prompting" because she knows exactly where every entry is. The cultivation-register has correctly classified her own recall as a property of the object. The formation-eye description is precise: the account-book IS the controlling node. Her genre-grade is accurate to the function, wrong about the mechanism.

**Key consistency rule:** The account-book is never called a ledger or a notebook in her voice. It is always "the account-formation," "the natal fabao," or "the primary node."

---

### The Still-Room Domain (领域初成 — Domain Formation, Preliminary)

**Her name:** Cauldron-Belly Domain — preliminary formation; radius one chamber  
**Her genre grade:** Preliminary domain technique, mortal-grade; expected to advance  
**What it actually is:** The still-room is a workspace she has organized for workflow efficiency, contamination prevention, and light optimization. In this space she has maximum operational effectiveness; she knows every location; she is at home. In open, uncontrolled environments she is genuinely disadvantaged.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the domain's formation matrix suppresses impurity introduction"; "within the primary domain, the formation recognizes the practitioner's qi-signature"; "the formation is temporarily re-established at [new location]"

**Broken-clock note:** Her domain-reading is accurate as a phenomenological description of the advantage she has in organized spaces she controls. The "domain" that "travels with her" to new locations is her practice of re-establishing the same organizational schema wherever she sets up. This is real. She does it. The still-room kit is the domain's portable formation-flags (see `prop-still-room-kit` — a parallel sibling card).

### The Document-Heist Formation-Break (破阵 — Formation Breaking)

**Her name:** Document Eye-Destruction Method; the Talisman-Transmission Protocol  
**Her genre grade:** Earth-grade formation-breaking technique  
**What it actually is:** Legal document instruments — bills of exchange, charter-parties, manifests, sealed notes — drafted with sufficient precision that they pass through institutional control systems (the harbor master's inspection, the gate's access protocol) without triggering refusal. The document is indistinguishable from a legitimate entry in the controlling system.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the talisman has been activated and transmitted through the correct channel"; "the formation's eye has been addressed directly"; "the harbor master's defensive formation processed the talisman and passed the practitioner through without resistance"

**Broken-clock note:** This is the most precise of her technique-descriptions. Her formation-breaking vocabulary maps exactly onto what she is doing: she identifies the control system's formation eye (the specific document type the harbor master looks for), crafts a document that is correct at that node, and the formation's own qi-flow carries her through. She is a formation master who works in paper and people rather than in qi and flags. The genre-grade (Earth-grade) is not far wrong for a technique that has broken institutional control at national scale.

**Cross-books consistency:** The technique name escalates with stakes — "talisman" for a single-use letter, "formation-break" for a heist, "grand-formation disruption" for the Lock III ship sequence. Same mechanism, described at the appropriate register level.

### The Below-Stairs Node Array (阵旗部署 — Formation Flag Deployment)

**Her name:** Inferior Path Outer-Disciple Formation; distributed detection array  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-to-earth-grade formation; the grade advances as the network expands  
**What it actually is:** Her trusted below-stairs faction members deployed at strategic positions throughout the Red Keep. She recruits, positions, and manages them as an intelligence network. Each member is a node. The network gives her coverage the watchers cannot provide.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the formation-flag at the east corridor is reporting"; "I added a new node at the laundry intake — the coverage gap has been addressed"; "the formation's perimeter detected the septa's approach before she reached the primary domain"

**Broken-clock note:** The formation-flag analogy is precise. She genuinely thinks about her network in geometric terms — who can see whom from their position, what the coverage gap is, where the blind spot is. She closes blind spots deliberately. This is formation-master spatial intelligence applied to people. The people are not aware they are formation flags.

### Impurity-Expulsion (除杂术 — Impurity Removal Technique)

**Her name:** Impurity-Expulsion, Standard Protocol  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade; the execution is her own method, not a genre-canonical technique  
**What it actually is:** Poisoning people who represent operational threats. She has removed several people this way. She classifies them as impurities rather than as people.  
**Her narration pattern:** "an impurity in the operational field has been addressed through the standard protocol"; "the tribulation-source has been expelled"; "the formation's contamination has been cleared"

**Broken-clock note:** The killing karma she does not log. The cultivation-genre's accounting would classify these kills as killing karma accruing against her advancement. She has declared them karma-neutral on the grounds that "impurities are not morally weighted in the ledger." The genre does not accept this reclassification. The debt accumulates. See `cond-heavenly-dao-calibration` for the per-event karmic accounting.

**Deployment rule:** Do not soften or editorialize this technique's description. She narrates it with the same flat affect as reagent classification. The horror is in the equivalence, not in heightened prose.

### The Performed-Illness Defensive Formation (防护阵 — Defensive Formation)

**Her name:** Sickroom-Exclusion Defensive Formation  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade; effective within its limited scope  
**What it actually is:** The managed appearance of illness that creates a social exclusion zone around her. She maintains several performed-illness covers simultaneously that reduce expected contact with watchers to near-zero. The sickroom door is a real door; no guard can enter without transgressing the appearance of concern.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the defensive formation's exclusion effect is currently active"; "the formation reduces the watcher's approach probability by the mechanism of social prohibition"; "I am maintaining the defensive formation at standard intensity; it does not require qi-input to sustain"

### The Precision-Question Interrogation (御灵术 初步 — Spirit-Reading Preliminary)

**Her name:** Preliminary Divine-Sense Technique, externalized through question-formation  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade approaching spirit-grade  
**What it actually is:** Exceptional social intelligence trained by years of operating in a court where wrong information is fatal. She extracts everything she needs from a conversation by asking exactly the right questions in the right sequence, without revealing what she already knows.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the divine-sense scan of the outer disciple confirms three data points"; "the formation-reading of the conversation's structure has been completed; conclusion: [specific information extracted]"

## Technique-Name Stability Rule

The impersonator and all bones authors must use the canonical technique names from this card, not improvised variants. If a scene requires a new technique name (a new operational method she invents in Bk II or Bk III), the technique should be named with the same grammar:

> [Method-type] + [Object/scope] + [Genre-grade qualifier, if stated]

Examples: "Document Eye-Destruction Method" (method type: eye-destruction, object: document, grade: earth-grade). "Cauldron-Belly Nine-Revolution Body Forging" (method type: body forging, object: cauldron-belly, qualifier: nine-revolution).

New names from bones authors should be logged here between invocations to prevent drift.

## Sensory Impact

This condition is active in every scene where she executes any operational move. Its sensory signature: a brief internal notation, narrated in register, before she acts. She does not explain what she is doing to bystanders; she explains it in the dose-log, in the account-formation, or in internal narration. The technique-name is always deployed before the action, not after it.

---

## Duration

Persistent across all three books. The technique hierarchy is cumulative — new techniques are added in Bk II–III as her operational scope expands (the Essosi paper-instrument toolkit; the Yi Ti setting adaptations). Existing techniques do not retire; they are re-deployed at higher stakes.
