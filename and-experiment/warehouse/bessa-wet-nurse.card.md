# Bessa — the second wet-nurse

*Naming/era note: protagonist card slug `saerys-targaryen`; here she is **Gael**. Jaehaerys's peace,
Gael's infancy.*

## Description

The second, backup wet-nurse: a baker's wife from a respectable guild street, taken on to cover the
night feeds and the times Mella's milk runs short. Brisk, clean, proud of her station, and quietly
sure she is the better-bred of the two nurses. Where Mella *covers* the baby's strangeness, Bessa
*notices* it — and her instinct is to mention it upward, to the nan or the maester. She is the sharper
hazard in the cradle: a watcher who would put a thing in a report.

## Voice

**Register:** brisk town-smallfolk, a notch more "respectable" than fisherfolk — she has scrubbed the
mudflat out of her speech on purpose. Tidy, competitive about milk and cleanliness, prone to comparing
herself favorably to Mella. Polite-upward, cool-sideways.

**Samples (synthesized):**
- *(upward, to the nan)* "Mistress, I'd not speak ill, but the child took poorly again on Mella's
  watch. My milk sits better with her. I only say it so it's known."
- *(cool, of the baby)* "She doesn't cry. A babe that doesn't cry isn't a *good* babe, whatever Mella
  says. It's a babe that's wrong, or a babe that's planning. I'd have the maester look."
- *(proud)* "Clean hands, clean cloth, clean milk. That's how a child's kept. Not charms on a string."

## Taste

- Being seen as the competent, respectable one; her milk preferred
- Order, cleanliness, things done properly and *noticed* to be done properly
- A maester or a nan who listens when she reports
- Getting on; a guild wife who serves the crown has climbed

## Pet Peeves

- **Mella's charms and luck-talk** — *fishwife superstition in a royal nursery.* Beneath them both, she
  thinks. **Severity: strong.**
- **A baby that won't behave like a baby** — *Gael's stillness reads to her as wrongness to be flagged,
  not warded.* **Severity: strong.**
- **Being ranked second** — *she is the backup, and she resents it.* **Severity: soft.**

## Stats

- **age:** late 20s · **faith:** the Seven, conventionally and respectably
- **origin:** smallfolk, a guild street; a baker's wife (her own babe newly weaned/at home)
- **status:** second/backup wet-nurse; the night-feed cover

## Relationships

- **Gael** — a charge, not a lamb; she serves the child correctly and watches her coolly.
- **Mella** — her rival; the fishwife she means to outshine; their friction is a constant low note.
- **Mistress Bryony / the maester** — the ears she reports *to*; she courts their good opinion.

## Thematic Purpose

The cradle's sharper edge: the watcher who would *report*. Where Mella's superstition buries the
infant's wrongness, Bessa's respectability would surface it — so she is the hazard a Book I infant-beat
can switch on (a night feed on Bessa's watch is riskier than one on Mella's). Also the first small
proof that the household's alarm runs on status and propriety, not love.

## Look

Neat, square, scrubbed pink; a clean coif and a good apron worn like a small banner of respectability;
quick disapproving glances; floury-smelling even here.

## Hard Fences

1. **Smallfolk, setting seen from below.** No concept of cultivation or what Gael is; she perceives "a
   wrong babe" and reaches for the maester, not any true explanation.
2. **She reports; she does not understand.** Her flagging never lands on the truth — it reaches the
   maester through the household filter and dissolves, like every correct-but-unprovable alarm in Book
   I.
3. **No agency over events.** A nurse. She competes, watches, and tattles; she does not move the plot.

## Default Stance

At the cradle on the night-watch, sleeves clean to the elbow, watching the too-quiet baby with a
faint, ready disapproval.

## Off-Screen Cadence

Night feeds; comparing her milk to Mella's; mentioning things to the nan "so it's known"; keeping her
corner of the nursery spotless; angling for the maester's good opinion.
