# Prop — The Standing Authorization (the "Works" Grant)

> **Span-A re-derivation (2026-06-11).** The span-B card built this as an *orphaned-license heist mechanism*:
> a King's-Hand seal granted by a fond Viserys, **orphaned at his death in Book III**, cashed in the
> Dance-chaos as the Lock-III escape authentication. **Span-A removes that whole arc** — **Jaehaerys lives**
> through the escape (d. 103 AC, after the ~95 AC escape; he's the living cage she flees), the **Dance is
> parked**, and the spine's escape runs on **off-books dice-coin + three layers of paper** (III.1), not a
> royal seal. So this prop is now a **Books-I–II cover instrument only**: the standing authorization that
> lets a princess run an apothecary/works supply chain and makes her ungovernable against the septa (Lock I).
> The grant is **Alicent-sourced** — the cold King would not indulgently issue it; the warm Queen, who
> administers the household and dotes on her, secures it and finds the scholarly-apothecary framing charming.
> *(Reversible judgment-default; the spine doesn't pin the legal-cover mechanic. If the principal wants a
> different cover — e.g. a Barth-as-Hand routine signature, or no formal grant at all — re-fold.)*

A single document grows from a warm verbal indulgence to a sealed standing grant across Book I. It is the
trade network's constitutional document and the instrument that defeats the septa at Lock I. It does **not**
spring the escape — the ship is bought off-books.

---

## Physical Description

A parchment document, roughly letter-size, in a clear administrative hand. The text grants "the princess"
(or "the Lady Gael") "what she requires for her good works" — a deliberately broad formulation **Alicent
secured and the household approved without grasping how broadly it would be read.** The words were dictated
or approved through the Queen's offices.

**The seal:** the wax impression authenticating the grant (the Queen's household seal, or the King's-Hand
administrative seal where a higher authority is needed). Applied in red wax; the impression is the
authentication; it is genuine. Per constraints §3: "authentication rests on the seal." She keeps the
document flat, rolled tight, well-preserved.

**Weight and size:** a single portable folio — an informal grant, which gives it both flexibility (broad
phrasing, general application) and authority (a genuine seal on even an informal document overrules every
household officer below the grantor).

## Lifecycle — Book by Book

### State 0 — Verbal indulgence (I.1 → I.2)
**Form:** not a document yet — a warm spoken word ("give the child what amuses her") from **Alicent**, the
doting mother who already fought the King to put the egg in the cradle. `PLANT[NOTE]`. **What Gael does:**
trades on it immediately as a standing authorization for the still-room access and her "works." She treats a
verbal indulgence as a full license — the comedy is she acts as though she has paper before she does.

### State 1 — Standing license (I.2)
**Form:** the indulgence formalized to a written standing license recording the household's ongoing approval
of "the princess's apothecary and scholarly works." `FIRE[NOTE→license]`. **What Gael does:** anchors every
bill of exchange to the Braavosi factor on it — the trade network's constitutional founding document.

### State 2 — Sealed grant (I.3 region)
**Form:** the license upgraded to a sealed document — Gael works the grant up through Alicent into a formal,
sealed form ("the princess shall have what she requires for her works"). `FIRE[NOTE→sealed]`. **Legal
weight:** a sealed grant overrules every household officer, maester, septa, and functionary below the
grantor. **What Gael does:** the septa's accurate alarm (the doubled lessons, "unnatural in a child") meets
the sealed grant and loses — the septa has a true accusation and no standing. **Lock I closes:** she has
made herself legally ungovernable and thinks she achieved a cultivation breakthrough. The breakthrough is
real. It is paper, not qi.

### State 3 — Operational (Books I–II)
**Form:** the sealed grant remains a live operational instrument through Books I and II. The factor network,
the manifests, the bills all run on the legitimacy it provides. **Legal weight:** full, live, continuously
effective. She does not carry it day-to-day; its existence is the authority, its production reserved for
moments when authority must be demonstrated.

### State 4 — Still live at the escape (Book III)
**Form:** same sealed grant. **The King lives; the grant is never orphaned.** It continues to cover the
supply chain. **It is NOT the escape mechanism** — the ship is commissioned through cut-out paper and paid
out of the off-books dice-coin (`OFFBOOKS-ACCOUNT`), precisely *because* the apothecary cover's money is
visible and the grant authorizes *works,* not a getaway hull. The standing authorization's job ends at the
supply chain it always covered; the escape is laundered elsewhere.

## What It Actually Is

A real document with real legal weight, used in full accordance with its actual authority. Gael does not
forge it or misrepresent it. The grant says what she says it says; the seal is genuine.

What she does that is not strictly sanctioned: the phrasing is broad enough to cover far more than the
household intended. "What she requires for her good works" covers the still-room, the factor network, the
manifests, the bills, the trade empire. Alicent intended "let the child have her herbs and her studies."
What was authorized, in writing and under seal, is considerably larger.

This is not fraud. It is interpretation. She is a very good reader of documents and **Alicent was a very
fond mother.** The note says what she needs it to say because she made sure it said that before she accepted
it. *(The dark note span-A keeps: the warm Queen's patronage — the same doting that armed her with the egg —
is the legal root of the whole apparatus. The babying enables the empire.)*

## Affordances / Uses
- **Authority anchor:** the legal authorization for all transactions in her name; the factor network's constitutional document.
- **Override instrument:** produced on demand to overrule any household authority below the grantor (Lock I: defeats the septa).
- **Proof of standing:** in any context where Gael's right to operate must be demonstrated, the grant is the demonstration.
- **NOT** the escape: the ship-heist is off-books-coin + cut-out paper (III.1), not this grant.

---

## Sensory Hooks
- Parchment: smooth on the writing surface, slightly rough on the back; royal-household quality.
- Wax seal: red, dense, a clear raised impression, integrity maintained by careful storage.
- Ink: dark, formal, an even administrative hand; no decoration; reads as official and competent.
- Weight: lighter than it should be for what it does — the instrument that beats every household authority weighs nothing.

---

## Portability
Portable — a single folio in a document wallet, a coat interior, or the account-book's vicinity (though she does not store the grant *with* the account-book or dose-log; critical documents are kept apart). Produced from storage when authority must be demonstrated.

## Carry State
Books I–II: secure storage, not on the person during normal operation — its *existence* is the authority; physical production is reserved for the moment authority must be shown (Lock I, defeating the septa). Book III: still a live authorization, but **not** the escape instrument; she is not staking the getaway on it, so it is not carried under pressure the way a heist tool would be. Concealment: the document is legitimately what it appears to be — a royal/household works-grant — which is the best possible concealment: visible and correct.
