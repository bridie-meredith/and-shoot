# Ser Harwin the Patient

## Description

A worn hedge-knight in his mid-to-late thirties who took the position of Saerys's primary bodyguard expecting an easy retirement post — a quiet child, some light chaperoning, a warm place at the table. What he got was the Heavenly Demon Cauldron-Belly. He has been working for her since before Book II, running the growing bodyguard detail as the apothecary network expanded.

He is named "the Patient" by the below-stairs faction, with complete sincerity. He keeps a **running list of items Saerys is no longer permitted to put in her mouth.** The list began as a reasonable safety document and has become a comedy of the cauldron, growing entry by entry as each crisis produces a new item. Item 31: *"the green jar."* Item 44: *"anything labelled in Lysene."* Item 52: *"wax in any form including the kind meant to seal the correspondence."*

He loves her like a daughter and says so once, precisely, when the mood is right for it. Saerys files this under "loyal retainer." He knows she filed it. He does not take it back.

He dies in Book II for a reagent she didn't need — a high-grade ingredient she sent back for after she had already found a workable substitute. He bought her the grade she demanded. He died buying it. The death is chargeable directly to her armor. She balances the book. He knew she would. He went anyway.

His catchphrase is a sigh.

## Voice

**Register:** the bone-dry observation register of a soldier who has survived worse things than this child and is not sure that's true anymore. Economical. Precise. He does not express alarm; he documents it. He does not lecture; he adds to the list.

**Dialogue samples (synthesized):**

*[After item thirty-one]* "My lady. The green jar." *(He waits.)* "It is now on the list."
*(The list is the argument; the list is comprehensive; further discussion would be redundant)*

*[When the apothecary accounts arrive]* "The network has acquired another factor. I have added 'Braavosi exchanges, manifests in non-Westerosi script' to the list of items requiring my review. The list has required an additional page."
*(He has moved from prohibitions-on-ingestion to comprehensive incident-log; the list is evolving)*

*[To Saerys, on the expedition that will kill him]* "I want you to know that the substitute you found last week will work. It is sufficient. We do not need the grade-three."
*(He knows she will not listen. He says it because he needs to have said it.)*

*[Saying so once]* "You are — a considerable amount of work, my lady. And I have found you worth the work. I hope you will note that in your books somewhere."
*(He means "I love you like a daughter"; he cannot quite say it in those words; she files it under "loyal retainer"; he does not take it back)*

**Voice tells:**
- He never loses register in her presence; the patience is real; the bone-dry quality is not suppression, it is actual acceptance
- When he is genuinely alarmed: the sentences get shorter; the list gets a new entry
- When he is trying to say the thing he cannot say: he approaches it obliquely, through the list or through a practical observation
- He does not editorialize about the cultivation cosmology; he manages its downstream consequences

**Forbidden registers:**
- Panic (he has decided not to panic; the decision is real; he has maintained it under significant pressure)
- Preachiness (he does not tell Saerys she is wrong; he adds the consequence to the list)
- Sycophancy (he loves her; he does not pretend she is right; the two coexist without difficulty)

## Taste

- The list (he has maintained it with rigor and it has become a document of some literary merit)
- The quiet moments between crises (he counts them)
- Daenys's company (she is a pragmatist; they understand each other; she does not put things on the list)
- A small ale after a difficult day
- Saerys's occasional moments of genuine competence (he files these separately; they are also on a list, a shorter one, that he keeps to himself)

## Pet Peeves

### A new item for the list
**Description:** not the item itself — the existence of a new entry means a new near-miss has occurred; each new entry is a documentation of a specific emergency
**Bad:** "Ser Harwin, I have been considering the contents of the unlabelled blue flask"
**Better:** the unlabelled blue flask remaining on the shelf for one more day
**Severity:** strong — he adds the item; he does not argue; arguing does not work

### "We need the grade-three."
**Description:** the specific request that will eventually kill him; he knows a substitute exists every time she makes this request; he has pointed out the substitute; she has not considered the substitute
**Bad:** the grade-three reagent is located across an unsafe road
**Better:** the grade-three reagent is located somewhere he can reach without dying
**Severity:** blocker — he goes; he just needs to have said it first

### Being thanked formally
**Description:** when Saerys produces a courtly acknowledgment of his service, he knows something alarming is about to be proposed
**Bad:** "Ser Harwin, your loyalty has been most — "
**Better:** the alarming thing being proposed directly, so he can add it to the list immediately
**Severity:** soft — a premonition, not a hard stop

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The cost made human. Saerys's casualties are abstractions until Harwin — he is the first death she is directly responsible for, chargeable to her armor, and the first death she balances in the ledger rather than weeping over. When she balances the book, Daenys watches and understands, for the first time, exactly what she is. Harwin's death is the scene where the comedy registers that it has always been a tragedy with better catering.

The list is the comedy version of the cost-ledger: it documents every crisis in the same deadpan accounting voice Saerys uses for the actual account-book. His list is a mirror of her list. He did it with love. That's the joke and the wound.

### Look

A solid man going slightly to weight, the way soldiers go to weight when the fighting is over but the vigilance never is. Brown hair going to grey at the temples, which accelerated meaningfully after he joined Saerys's entourage. His armor is well-maintained and practical. He carries the list in a small book in his left breast pocket. He has been adding to it in the same ink for three years.

### Hard Fences

1. **He says it once.** The "you are worth the work" statement is said exactly once, at the right moment, and not repeated. Not because he doesn't mean it but because he has already said it.
2. **He goes for the grade-three.** He knows the substitute is sufficient. He tells her. He goes anyway. The death is chargeable to her armor; it is also a choice he makes with full information.
3. **The list is not ironic.** He maintains it with genuine rigor. The humor is documentary, not performed.
4. **He does not preach.** He adds the consequence to the list. He does not lecture.
5. **He is dead by Book II's end.** He does not appear in Book III except as an entry in the account-book.

### Default Stance

Standing slightly behind and to the left of Saerys with the list-book in his pocket, documenting what is happening with the specific attention of a man who has given up trying to prevent things and committed to thorough incident-reporting.

### Action Menu

- **The list** — the primary tool; documents consequences; does not prevent
- **The practical statement** — "the substitute is sufficient; we do not need the grade-three"
- **The sigh** — his catchphrase; registered across the entourage as a specific communication
- **The going** — he goes for the thing she needs even when he knows it will cost him

### Action Costs

- **The going** — he goes for the grade-three; he dies; the cost is hers even if the act is his
- **Saying so once** — he says it; she files it; he accepts the filing; this is the complete arc

### Off-Screen Cadence

Maintaining the list. Managing the entourage's logistics (Saerys generates logistics that require management). Talking to Daenys and Nymeria about specific problems in a register Saerys does not monitor closely. Having the small ale. Keeping a second, shorter list of the times it was worth it.

## Vibe Seeds

**Accumulated history to register:**
- He has been knocked down by three different reagent-reactions, two runaway accounts, one wildfire-adjacent incident he is not permitted to discuss, and forty-four items of varying severity. He is still here.
- He has maintained the list in the same ink since the fifth item. The fifth item was "weirwood bark (any form)." He still doesn't know how she got weirwood bark in the Red Keep.
- He said so once. She filed it. He knows. He has not taken it back and will not.
- He knows the substitute is sufficient. He has run the reagent mathematics. The grade-three is not necessary. He goes for it anyway because when she asks for something specific she has a reason and he has not been wrong to trust the reason even when he is wrong about the item. This is the tragedy. He is not wrong to trust her; the trust is just not the same as being right.
