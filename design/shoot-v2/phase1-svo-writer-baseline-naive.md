narrator: taylor-hebert-westeros
goal: Show how an unattached ward becomes a named, flagged entry in the lord's administrative record under a customary-authority refusal.

1 The morning is bright on the sept flagstones
2 Taylor is tired from the night before
3 Mira speaks to the yard loudly that the officer will want the unattached ones first
4 Edric positions himself at the gate post and looks out at the road
5 Taylor reads Mira's tone and notes the warning
6 The census officer enters through the sept gate with his clerk
7 The clerk is carrying a heavy ledger and an ink case
8 The officer announces his commission
9 Taylor moves into the yard with the assembled wards
10 Taylor counts twelve wards in the line
11 The officer works through the wards, dictating names and ages and blood-claims to the clerk
12 The clerk writes carefully into the ledger
13 The officer reaches Taylor at the end of the line
14 The officer doesn't ask anyone else this question
15 The officer asks Taylor for her name and age and wardship
16 Taylor states her name and age and that Septon Osmynd holds her wardship
17 The officer requests the septon to step forward
18 The sept doors are closed and no one comes out
19 Taylor takes out the septon's written letter and presents it
20 The officer reads it and folds it back and hands it to Taylor
21 The officer states that without a sworn guardian present the attestation is insufficient
22 Taylor turns to Mira and asks her to stand as witness
23 Mira looks at the yard stones and says nothing while everyone watches
24 Taylor turns to Edric and asks him to step forward and speak for her
25 Edric does not speak and steps back through the sept door
26 The yard is silent
27 The clerk asks Taylor whether she has been assessed before
28 Taylor knows this question was put to no other ward
29 The clerk writes Taylor's entry into the ledger and marks a double-stroke notation in the margin
30 The officer states that Taylor is on the provisional labor-eligibility list pending cognitive assessment by the traveling maester
31 The officer and clerk exit through the gate with the ledger sealed in the case
32 Taylor stands alone in the yard holding the letter
33 Taylor feels the moment the window closes
