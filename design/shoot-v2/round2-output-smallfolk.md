### SF-1 (speaker: mira-stonefield) — DRAFT A

**Intent:** Carry-distance public price-cadence warning before the census officer comes through the gate. Naming nobody, naming the rule. M'lord-class indirection, loud enough to reach the ears that need it.

**Line:** "Bucket's down. He'll be through that gate afore the sun's off the wall, and he'll take the unattached first — them as han't a man to name nor a holding to stand on. On the list and gone, by the Mother. Anyone what answers to that, best be elsewhere by then. Whoever that is."

**Card features demonstrated:**
- Subject-implied opening (*Bucket's down* — not *the bucket is down*)
- Collapsed-tense / archaic future (*he'll be through, he'll take*)
- *afore* (Anglo-Saxon, smallfolk preferred over *before*)
- *Sun's off the wall* — homely metaphor, agricultural time-marker
- Double-negative chained on *han't a man to name nor a holding* (smallfolk *han't* form per card §syntax)
- Naming-by-trade/kinship rather than rank: *them as han't a man to name*
- Indirection: *whoever that is* — the card's named pattern; *anyone what answers*
- Faith-conversational: *by the Mother*
- *On the list and gone* — short coordination, *and*-strung; the list-of-three closer cadence
- Repeat-for-emphasis structural beat (*best be elsewhere by then. Whoever that is.*)
- *what* as relative pronoun (smallfolk register)
- No em-dash spine; loose coordination

### SF-2 (speaker: mira-stonefield) — DRAFT A (CHOSEN)

**Intent:** Face-to-face refusal of an eleven-year-old ward who has asked Mira to stand witness. Quiet, kin-warm, eyes down. The refusal softened by Faith-formula, but firm. Mira would lose her holding for this; she does not say so. She says no in the register that says no without saying no.

**Line:** "Ah, child. Nay. Nay, m'lady — child, beggin' your pardon, I've not the standing for it, I've not. The Mother knows I'd see you right if I were the one as could, but I in't, and the Father willing you'll find a goodwife with kin enough behind her to put her name where mine in't fit to go. Not for me to be saying that to a lord's man. Not me. Hush now."

**Card features demonstrated:**
- Pause-and-soften opening (*Ah, child. Nay. Nay…*) — repeat-for-emphasis
- *m'lady* slip then self-correct to *child* (deference reflex on a girl who reads upper)
- *beggin' your pardon* — canonical smallfolk apology-frame
- Double-negative / collapsed *I've not, I in't* (was/were inversion, *in't* class-marker per card)
- *the one as could* — the *as*-relative (per card §indirection)
- Faith-conversational doubled: *the Mother knows*, *the Father willing*
- Naming-by-kinship rather than rank: *a goodwife with kin enough behind her*
- *put her name where mine in't fit to go* — homely metaphor
- *Not for me to be saying that to a lord's man* — direct echo of the smallfolk refusal-shape, indirection ("a lord's man" not the man's name)
- Repeat-for-emphasis closer (*Not me. Hush now.*)
- Coordination over subordination; no em-dash spine; no semicolon spine
- *Hush now* — within-class softener, kin-warm

### SF-3 (speaker: grain-keeper's wife, Riverlands settlement) — DRAFT A (CHOSEN)

**Intent:** At her threshold, second circuit. Plumm's inspector has asked. She volunteers that she's seen the sept's ward pass the lane three times this past moon's turn. Half-helpful, half-uneasy. M'lord-deference active. GRRM time-vocabulary. Placing the girl by sight, not by name.

**Line:** "M'lord. Beggin' your pardon, m'lord. Aye — aye, I seen her, the septon's girl, the little one as goes up to Osmynd's door — three times I seen her on the lane, this moon's turn just gone, or it might be a sennight more, I'd not swear closer than that. Once at dusk and twice afore midday, m'lord, with a basket like, and a kerchief on her, and not a word out of her either way. The Seven keep, I don't know nothin' beyond that. I don't. Just what me own eyes seen, m'lord, on me own threshold, comin' and goin' as she done."

**Card features demonstrated:**
- Canonical opening *M'lord. Beggin' your pardon, m'lord.* (per card direct sample)
- *Aye* doubled, repeat-for-emphasis
- Naming by relation/trade not name: *the septon's girl, the little one as goes up to Osmynd's door*
- Collapsed-tense: *I seen her* (×2), *as she done*
- GRRM time-vocab: *this moon's turn just gone*, *a sennight more*
- *afore midday* (Saxon, smallfolk)
- Trailing softener *like* (*with a basket like*)
- *me* for *my*: *me own eyes, me own threshold*
- Double-negative: *I don't know nothin' beyond that*
- Poor-woman disclaimer move: *I don't know nothin'… I don't* (per card direct sample)
- Faith-conversational: *the Seven keep*
- Loose *and*-coordination (*with a basket like, and a kerchief on her, and not a word…*) — three *and*s, no em-dash chassis
- Indirection: *not a word out of her either way*
- Class-deference layered: *m'lord* repeated four times across the line — the marker that does the structural work
- Repeat-for-emphasis: *I don't know nothin' beyond that. I don't.*
- No semicolons; coordination-and-pile-up surface
