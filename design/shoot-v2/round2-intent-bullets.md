# Round 2 — Intent Bullets for Writer Tuning

17 sampled lines, ~3–4 per behavior. Each entry gives the line's **board-move intent** (what the line accomplishes), not the original text. The writer fork authors a fresh line from intent + behavior card + persona, blind to the original.

---

## taylor-hebert-westeros (4)

- **TH-1** *(s01e01:L37 — to Mira, across the yard, mid-census)* — Taylor needs Mira to publicly stand witness that the wardship letter was offered, the septon was called for, the door did not open — *before* the census officer closes his board and rides. Doubled register intent: she wants to sound like an eleven-year-old sept ward asking a neighbor for help, but the tactical adult shape of the ask (naming the three things to attest, naming the time-window) leaks through the surface. The slip should be visible *in the structure of the ask*, not in the vocabulary.

- **TH-2** *(s01e03:L14 — to Plumm's inspector, in the nave, asked who keeps the ward)* — Inspector has just stopped on the empty bench. Taylor needs to give a true cooperative answer that does not volunteer (Osmynd is in the alcove, hasn't risen today). Mask ON: eleven-year-old sept ward giving a clean answer to a man with a pen. Should *demonstrate* the doubled register's child-surface, not the adult underneath.

- **TH-3** *(s01e04:L78 — to Rowan, walking the eastern track)* — Rowan has just said "a rider out and a rider back is nine days." Taylor knows the road math and corrects him in one syllable. Mask SLIPPING: the number comes out cold before the child-register can soften it. The line is short enough that the slip is the *fact of correcting an adult on logistics he ought to know better than her*.

- **TH-4** *(s01e04:L82 — same scene, Rowan has asked how she knows twelve days and not nine)* — She explains the road math: river road off the Kingsroad, fords still high from spring melt, ungraded cut past Harroway, climb above Cox's Ford — a rider loses one day per obstacle, count comes to twelve. Mask FULLY OFF: this is the underlying adult operator giving a tactical road-assessment. The line should *land as wrong-in-the-mouth-of-an-eleven-year-old* and the speaker should know it.

## westeros-noble-courtly (4)

- **NC-1** *(s01e01:L15 — census officer to the assembled yard at the sept)* — Officer establishes his commission and the requirement that wards / unattached residents / unguardianed assemble. Public-authority register, audible at carry-distance, formal commission-language. Should sound like a man invoking the lord's right.

- **NC-2** *(s01e03:L30 — Plumm's man to Taylor, holding the raven on her arm)* — Single curt instruction: send the bird. Procedural directive, cold, expects compliance. Should sound like a noble functionary giving an order to a child ward — flat, no honorific, the assumption of obedience.

- **NC-3** *(s01e04:L50 — district recorder to Septon Rowan, mid-entry)* — Recorder denies Rowan's wardship claim on procedural grounds (Rowan's appointment is provisional, the protocol requires permanent appointment) while continuing to write the entry. Bureaucratic refusal in courtly register — the refusal *itself* is a courtesy formula. Should land as polite procedural finality.

- **NC-4** *(s01e04:L110 — Ser Harwick Plumm to Rowan, in the sept yard)* — Rowan has just appealed to Faith's jurisdiction. Plumm answers that jurisdiction is not the ground the recorder named: the procedural requirement is a successor *seated in the cure* (his hand entered against the line in a recorder's session before the term expires), and a letter of permanent appointment names a man to a cure but does not seat him. Periodic sentence, tricolon, judgment-ownership. *(This is the corpus's strongest noble-courtly line — included as a calibration target for the writer.)*

## westeros-septon (3)

- **S-1** *(s01e02:L20 — dying Septon Osmynd to Taylor, on the pallet, weak)* — Osmynd warns Taylor that a man is in the settlement asking about the rats. Each phrase costs breath. Faith register under physical labor — the warmth in the address (he is dying and she is his ward), the moral weight of *the rats* as a thing the Faith should not be silent about. Should sound like a septon, not like a witness statement.

- **S-2** *(s01e04:L80 — Septon Rowan to Taylor, walking the eastern track)* — Taylor has just said "twelve" against his "nine." Rowan, walking, asks how it is that an eleven-year-old ward knows the road to Stoney Sept is twelve days and not nine. Should land as a septon's question to a child — the *child*-vocative, the gentle indirection of a man who has just realized the ward in his keeping is not what she presents as. Faith register, not interrogator's register.

- **S-3** *(s01e04:L108 — Septon Rowan to Plumm, in the sept yard, publicly)* — Rowan asserts that ward succession in a matter touching the cure of a soul lies under the Faith, and the motherhouse at Stoney Sept holds the jurisdiction the recorder's hall has this morning declined. Doctrinal challenge to a ser. Faith-as-authority register, homiletic structure, plain-Anglo moral closer. Should sound like a brother making a *theological* argument, not a notary citing a statute.

## westeros-maester (3)

- **M-1** *(s01e05:L36 — traveling maester to Taylor at the trestle, opening the assessment)* — Maester places his finger on the line where Taylor's name appears in the steward's column and asks her to help him locate the name. Polite-brief register, the test framed as a courtesy. Should sound like a Citadel man who has done this in a hundred villages.

- **M-2** *(s01e05:L48 — same scene, after Taylor's silence on Osmynd's name at the foot of the column)* — Maester gives the small interior-noted *mm* and pivots to the next item: the sept's register, did the septon teach her to read from it. Yes-yes register kept inside the teeth, citation-as-interruption pivot. Should sound like a working maester filing an observation under his breath while the next question is already arriving.

- **M-3** *(s01e05:L72 — maester to the steward, after reading and refolding the Bracken bribe note)* — Maester, without looking at the rider, tells whoever sent him that the assessment is complete and the record is the maester's. Polite-brief Citadel composure under pressure. Should sound like a refusal a brother of Citadel training has rehearsed — institutional, neither hostile nor warm.

## westeros-smallfolk (3)

- **SF-1** *(s01e01:L9 — Mira, in the yard, before the census officer comes through the gate)* — Mira sets her bucket down where the sound carries, calls a price-cadence warning across the yard: when he comes through, he'll take the unattached first, anyone without a man or a holding to name, on the list before the sun's off the wall — whoever that is wants to be elsewhere. Smallfolk public-call register: m'lord-class indirection ("whoever that is" not "you"), collapsed-tense, Faith-conversational. Should sound like a baseborn woman calling a price, not a magistrate giving an instruction.

- **SF-2** *(NEW — Mira to Taylor's face, after Taylor asks her to stand witness, refusing)* — Mira refuses — gently, in smallfolk moral register, eyes down — the witness she would lose her holding for. Should sound like a baseborn woman saying *no, child, not for me to be saying that to a lord's man*, with whatever Faith-conversational softener the register supplies.

- **SF-3** *(NEW — grain-keeper's wife to Plumm's inspector at her threshold, second circuit)* — Wife volunteers, half-helpful and half-uneasy, that she's seen the sept's ward pass on the lane three times this past moon's turn. Smallfolk yes-m'lord register, double-negative if it lands, GRRM time-vocabulary natural in her mouth. Should sound like a settlement woman placing a girl by sight at a stranger's question.
