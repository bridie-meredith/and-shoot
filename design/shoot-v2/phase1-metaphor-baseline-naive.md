## Author's note

**@33** — The door staying shut over Osmynd is the episode's first public defeat for Taylor and the memory-flag fires here (dying-tutor cluster). The closed-door allegory earns figurative reach because the memory callback licenses it: the door is not just a door, it is the shape her history gives to rooms that will not yield. One entry, minimal extension. The allegory stays in-register — no Earth-Bet vocabulary, no proper nouns, renders as physical + architectural only.

**@64** — The two parallel margin-marks are the episode's bureaucratic climax (tensometer t=3, state-update @64 the irreversible registration). The dark-humor allegory earns its fire here: the ledger's pre-judicial authority — the marks arrive as if the record already contained her outcome and is merely confirming it. The tone is grim and slightly sardonic, which is in Taylor's register without naming her interiority. The figuration is in-register (Westerosi administrative form; no Earth-Bet reference).

**@73** — The threshold-shadow at the episode's close carries the third memory callback (locker-displacement) and the feeling-flag (breath empties). The simile earns the fire because "the shadow takes her" is already partway figurative in the proto-line ("steps into the shadow of the frame") — the simile completes the gesture rather than adding decoration. The "body that has stood at one before" phrase does the memory callback work without naming what that prior threshold was. Sparse, inward, closes the episode's figurative arc.
