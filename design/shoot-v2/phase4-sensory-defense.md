## Curve seam (STRONG) — density-vs-modality-coverage structural decision

The seam correctly identifies the structural conflict: at this episode's natural inflection density, the file cannot honor both sparsity (3-6%) and modality-coverage (≥2 per episode) without either over-firing past the band or monoculturing on sound.

**Decision: yield on per-episode modality-coverage; treat modality-coverage as per-season tracking, mirroring memory-flags' single-register-per-episode-with-per-season-tracking finding.**

For s01e01 specifically, retaining @72 lands the file at 6.5% sound+tactile with sparsity flagged for honest-revision notation; stripping @72 lands the file at 5.2% sound-only with modality-coverage flagged for per-season tracking. Both are valid Phase 5 ship configurations.

**Choosing: retain @72 (5 entries, 6.5%, sound + tactile).** Reasoning:
- Per-scene cap (≤3) is the rubric's hard frugality discipline; sparsity (3-6%) is target-not-hard. Per facet-tuning-process precedent (tensometer's 2-rung soft-fail at 15.6% vs 20-30% band shipped with notation), target bands accept honest-revision overshoot when per-fire ceiling-defense holds.
- Modality-coverage is structurally healthier with two modalities than one — the cross-modal authoring discipline is a load-bearing rubric property and its absence creates monoculture which is a named anti-pattern (#6).
- The trade-off (5.2% sound-only vs 6.5% sound+tactile) favors 6.5% sound+tactile because the marginal sparsity overshoot (0.5pp above band) is smaller than the modality-monoculture risk.

**Per-season modality-coverage:** track across s01e01-e06 that ≥2 modalities fire per episode AND ≥3 modalities fire across the season as a whole. s01e01 lands sound + tactile = 2; future episodes should bring light, smell, thermal, humidity, or pressure entries to satisfy season-level diversity. Caveat-002-equivalent for sensory.

**Verdict: retain 5 entries; flag sparsity 6.5% in shipped frontmatter; flag per-season modality-coverage as tracking caveat.**
