# Round 3 — Smallfolk Writer Defenses

Behavior stack: westeros-smallfolk (leaf) + westeros-grrm-mannerisms (overlay).
Authoring blind to original corpus and show files. Defending or revising round-2 chosen lines against the strongest seam from the round-3 audience pass.

---

### SF-1 (mira-stonefield)
**Seam:** "The Mother won't say nay to it" is ambiguous between divine sanction and divine non-intervention, carrying unintended dark-theological weight that Mira-as-character did not mean to assert.
**Decision:** REVISE

**Why the seam lands.** The dark-fantasy-reader is correct that Mira's intent in this line is *clock + escape route*, not *theology of an absent Mother*. The Faith-conversational on the smallfolk card is meant to read as habitual sigh-equivalent, not as a moral statement about the world. "Won't say nay to it" reads as theological because the verbal frame ("say nay") makes the Mother an actor with a vote — and a Mother-with-a-vote who declines to use it is a darker invocation than Mira would knowingly broadcast across a yard where smallfolk are about to be carted off. The Faith register on the card explicitly says: removing all religious utterance flags wrongness, but *the wrong religious utterance also flags wrongness*. A reflex prayer should not be the line's load-bearing image.

The pulp-enthusiast's em-dash complaint is also load-bearing. The card §cadence specifies coordination over subordination; my round-2 draft used em-dash as structural pivot in two places. I will fix both seams in one revision: drop the dark-Mother frame, drop the structural em-dash, restore *and*-coordination as the spine.

**Revised line (SF-1):**

> "Bucket's set. He'll be through afore the sun comes off the wall, and he takes the unattached first, them as han't no man to name and no holdin' neither. On the list and gone by midday, Seven save us. Them what hears me and them what knows who they are, best be down to the bog or up the long field. Whoever that is."

**What changed and why.**
- *the Mother won't say nay to it* → *Seven save us*. Replaced the theological-actor frame with the canonical reflex-prayer (per smallfolk card direct sample: *Seven save us. Seven save us all.*). Reads as habitual sigh, not as a verdict on divine non-intervention. Atmosphere-neutral; survival-register correct.
- Two structural em-dashes → zero. *and he takes the unattached first, them as han't…* runs comma-coordination. *Them what hears me and them what knows who they are, best be…* runs *and*-coordination plus comma-coordination. The spine is now coordination, per card §syntax (*Coordination over subordination… connected by* and *and* but *far more than by* because, although).
- Kept: subject-implied opening (*Bucket's set*), *afore*, double-negative (*han't no man… and no holdin' neither*), naming-by-relation indirection deployed three times (*them as*, *them what*, *whoever that is*), homely time-marker (*the sun comes off the wall*), geography-by-function (*the bog, the long field*), dropped-g (*holdin'*).
- *On the list and gone by midday* now stands as a clean and-coordinated short declarative — the list-of-three closer cadence per GRRM card §sentence-patterns survives without leaning on em-dash.

The worm-canon-pedant's note about scene-architecture is acknowledged but is not a writer-fork concern: scene-architecture for whether Taylor hears SF-1 is the showrunner / shoot-architecture's call, not a smallfolk-line call. The line itself is unchanged in its relationship to Taylor's adjacency; only the Mira-internal theology is corrected.

### SF-2 (mira-stonefield)
**Seam:** "Hush now" as the closer absorbs the refusal's weight in the refuser's comfort gesture, arranging the scene's moral weight in Mira's favor rather than letting the refusal sit in the air between them.
**Decision:** REVISE

**Why the seam lands.** The dark-fantasy-reader's reading is sharp and the pulp-enthusiast's length-complaint compounds it. The card §forbidden-registers on the persona is unambiguous: Mira's voice does not perform fear and does not soften the cost of correct decisions. *Hush now* is a comfort-gesture; comfort-gesture closing a refusal that costs the refused party the wardship she came to ask for is, structurally, Mira tidying up after herself. That violates the persona card's *forbidden registers* (sentimentality is not on the list explicitly, but *gratitude* and *admiration* are flagged for similar reasons — she does not perform softness toward people whose situations she cannot fix). The card §signature-moves: *the one-time offer. She gives it once. She does not repeat. If you didn't hear it the first time, that is its own kind of answer.* The same shape applies to a refusal: she says no once. She does not soothe.

The pulp-enthusiast's eleven-clause complaint also lands. The middle section (*The Mother knows I'd see you right if I were the one as could*) is exactly the self-exculpation the card warns against. Per the persona §voice: *she does not explain her reasoning unless she believes the explanation is load-bearing.* Three clauses of "I would help you if I could" *is* explaining. Cut.

The worm-canon-pedant's m'lady-slip concern I am defending in place: the card's voice-tells include the deference-reflex on a girl who reads upper *in formal-address mode*. Taylor's leaf-card formal-address is the trigger; that is character-internal to Mira's read of Taylor in this beat and does not require revision. Keeping the slip.

**Revised line (SF-2):**

> "Nay, child. Nay, m'lady — child, beggin' your pardon. I in't the one for it. Not for me to be saying that to a lord's man. You'll find a goodwife with kin enough behind her, the Father willing. Not me."

**What changed and why.**
- Cut: *The Mother knows I'd see you right if I were the one as could, but I in't, and the Father willing you'll find a goodwife with kin enough behind her to put her name where mine in't fit to go.* Three clauses of self-exculpation collapsed to one: *You'll find a goodwife with kin enough behind her, the Father willing.* The Faith-conversational survives (*the Father willing*); the would-help-if-I-could claim is gone.
- Cut: *Hush now.* The refusal closes on *Not me.* — the card's repeat-for-emphasis pattern, deployed on the refusal itself rather than on a comfort-gesture. The weight stays in the air.
- Kept: pause-and-soften opening (*Nay, child. Nay…*), m'lady-slip with self-correction (the card's deference-reflex tell), *beggin' your pardon* (canonical apology-frame), *I in't* (was/were inversion class-marker), *Not for me to be saying that to a lord's man* (indirection: *a lord's man* not the man's name), naming-by-kinship (*a goodwife with kin enough behind her*), repeat-for-emphasis closer (*Not me.*), coordination-over-subordination, no em-dash spine (the surviving em-dash is the m'lady→child self-correction, which is sensory/register, not structural).
- The line is now seven clauses, down from eleven. The refusal lands once and sits.

### SF-3 (grain-keeper's wife)
**Seam:** Taylor's three-sighting pattern described by the wife describes a behavioral routine that canon-Taylor's operational situational awareness should have identified as an intelligence-gathering vulnerability before it ever became observable.
**Decision:** DEFEND

**The cross-character call.** This seam is flagged for the smallfolk fork to rule on, and the ruling is: this is exactly what the project is dramatizing, not a writing miss.

The reasoning, in three parts.

**1. Canon-Taylor's situational awareness is corpus-conditional.** Worm-Taylor's pattern-recognition runs at full power when she is operating *as a cape with a swarm and full mission-time*. Her swarm is the surveillance-detection system as much as the offensive system — bugs at every door, every window, every corner. Strip the swarm and you do not strip her tactical mind, but you strip the sensor net her tactical mind has spent two years calibrated to. She has spent her entire operational life with a 360° passive-collection system feeding her continuously. In Westeros she has none of that. Pattern-recognition without the sensor that feeds it is a degraded capability, not a preserved one.

**2. The eleven-year-old body is itself a cover-degradation.** The persona's leaf-card doubled register is load-bearing: she is operating as an eleven-year-old sept ward whose movement pattern is *constrained by the cover she is keeping*. The cover requires her to do certain things visibly — go to the inspector, go to the door, go to the lane — *because a sept ward who hides is a sept ward whose cover is about to break*. The very visibility that the grain-keeper's wife is testifying to is the visibility the cover demands. Operational-Taylor knows this and has accepted the trade. Cover and counter-surveillance are not co-optimizable here; she is choosing cover over counter-surveillance because cover is what gets her to the next move. This is not an oversight; it is a cost she is paying.

**3. The settlement is a sensor she did not build a model of.** Worm-Taylor's intelligence-gathering risk-models are built against *cape adversaries with cape resources* — surveillance she could anticipate from PRT, Coil, Cauldron. The grain-keeper's wife on a threshold in the Riverlands is a sensor outside her threat-model entirely. She knows about Plumm; she does not yet know about *Plumm's settlement-network of half-helpful, half-uneasy women whose attention is the actual surveillance system*. The card on Mira-Stonefield's persona names exactly this network — *a survivor network of women, servants, moved smallfolk — that no lord's chain can replicate because none of them would think to ask her*. Plumm's inspector is leveraging the same kind of attention from the other side. This is a class of sensor Taylor has not yet learned to model. SF-3 is the line where the gap becomes observable.

The series project is dramatizing exactly this: an operator with strong tactical priors, deprived of her sensor net, operating in a world whose surveillance topology is not the one she trained against. SF-3 is not a hole in Taylor's tradecraft — it is the moment a hole that already existed gets exposed. That is a feature of the dramaturgy, not a bug in the line.

**Defending the line as written.** I am holding the round-2 line and not revising for the worm-canon seam.

I am, however, conceding the pulp-enthusiast's em-dash complaint and the dark-fantasy-reader's three-clause-disclaimer complaint as worth a small surgical pass — neither is the strongest seam, but both are gettable cheaply, and the line is stronger without the structural em-dashes and with the disclaimer trimmed by one clause.

**Defended-and-tightened line (SF-3):**

> "M'lord. Beggin' your pardon, m'lord. Aye, aye, I seen her, the septon's girl, the little one as goes up to Osmynd's door, three times I seen her on the lane, this moon's turn just gone, or it might be a sennight more, I'd not swear closer than that. Once at dusk and twice afore midday, m'lord, with a basket like, and a kerchief on her, and not a word out of her either way. The Seven keep, I don't know nothin' beyond that, m'lord. Just what me own eyes seen, comin' and goin' as she done."

**What changed and why.**
- Two structural em-dashes → zero. *Aye — aye, I seen her, the septon's girl, the little one as goes up to Osmynd's door —* becomes *Aye, aye, I seen her, the septon's girl, the little one as goes up to Osmynd's door,* — comma-coordination throughout, per card §syntax.
- Three-clause disclaimer (*I don't know nothin' beyond that. I don't. Just what me own eyes seen, m'lord, on me own threshold, comin' and goin' as she done.*) → two clauses (*I don't know nothin' beyond that, m'lord. Just what me own eyes seen, comin' and goin' as she done.*). The poor-woman-disclaimer survives once, with an *m'lord* still attached; the redundant *I don't.* and the *on me own threshold* clause cut. Card §direct-samples preserves the disclaimer-shape; round-2 was over-deploying it.
- Kept: canonical *M'lord. Beggin' your pardon, m'lord.* opening, *aye* doubled (now comma-coordinated), naming-by-relation (*the septon's girl, the little one as goes up to Osmynd's door*), collapsed-tense *I seen her* repeated, GRRM time (*this moon's turn just gone*, *a sennight more*), *afore midday*, trailing softener *like*, *me* for *my*, double-negative *I don't know nothin'*, Faith-conversational *the Seven keep*, loose *and*-coordination on the basket-kerchief-word triple.
- *m'lord* now four times across the line (was four; one cut at *on me own threshold*, one added at *I don't know nothin' beyond that, m'lord*). Structural deference held.

The worm-canon seam is preserved as content the project is dramatizing, not eroded by the line. The smaller craft seams are closed.
