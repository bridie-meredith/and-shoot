## Author rationale (naive, schema-only)

> Lines 1-10 are environmental staging and character placement with no stakes yet visible, so they land at 1. I reached for 2 when an actor's action became legible as a move with consequence — the officer entering, the line forming, the ledger being positioned — because those beats make the power differential visible even before it activates. I reached for 3 at any moment where the outcome could not be taken back or where the scene's central conflict compressed into a single physical beat: the officer's gaze locking on Taylor (@23), Taylor physically blocking the officer's path (@37-39), the confrontation over the letter (@40-41), the stylus marking the margin (@64), the officer's final spoken verdict (@66), and Taylor crossing the threshold at the end (@77). The sustained 2s in the late-middle section (roughly @50-63) felt like aftermath pressure — something was just decided but the shape of it is still resolving — which I treated as stakes-visible-but-not-rupturing. The closing walk to the door (@70-76) I kept at 2 rather than 1 because Taylor is still inside the officer's jurisdiction until she crosses; @77 lifts to 3 as the irrevocable act.
