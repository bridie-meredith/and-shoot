## What the user-supplied requirements added

### Pre-Phase-0 nudges (handoff message + first response)

The user supplied **eight rubric-shaping nudges** before Phase 0 dispatch (the most pre-Phase-0 absorption of any facet to date; absorbs feeling's seven and extends):

1. **Sparsity 0-3%.** Zero-fires-per-episode acceptable; ≤2 fires per s01e01-class on 77 beats. Hard ceiling 3%; floor 0%.

2. **Multi-signal licensing from {memory, feeling, tens}.** "Metaphors should also use signals from tensometer and memory/feeling flags." Multi-justification ≥2 layers from this set; anchor must be memory OR feeling (those are content-licensing); tens is supporting.

3. **Per-scene cap ≤1 cross-character.** "One metaphor per scene cap." Hard cap, no exceptions.

4. **Functional registers narrowed to callback + dark-humor.** Memory-flags' four registers (realization / grim humor / social commentary / painting characterization) narrow to two at metaphor: callback and dark-humor. Painting-characterization without callback is anti-pattern (AP9). Social commentary registers at memory-flag but not at the figurative-render layer.

5. **Reading A scope (explicit comparisons only).** Idioms-with-meaning ("the door takes her") are out of scope; verb-personification stretches without comparator are out of scope. Reading A floor is high; metaphor is a load-bearing comparison the proto-line + cited facets do not already carry, not a figurative wisp.

6. **Hybrid mechanic + dialect audience, independent gates.** Pulp-enthusiast secondary calibration is load-bearing for figurative-reach taste — the most calibrated ear for whether figurative reach earns its keep vs feels grafted. Worm-canon-pedant primary; dark-fantasy-reader secondary; pulp-enthusiast secondary (figurative-reach-taste-bearing).

7. **Transitive audience-meaningful via memory-flag co-cite.** Audience-meaningful inherits from the memory-flag anchor; if the cited memory-flag was correctly refused on audience-meaningful upstream, metaphor at that beat cannot license. Resolution to memory-flags caveat-005 (NI @48 audience-meaningful retroactive evaluation): @48 stays correctly refused; metaphor at @48 cannot license; transitive resolution.

8. **Single-anchor allegory only.** Multi-beat structural allegory collapses to single anchor (strongest beat) or refuses at this facet. Multi-beat allegory is a stitcher concern, not a facet entry.

## What worked

1. **Schema-revision-at-ship pattern transfers and extends to capstone.** Sensory shipped a §rename + content-shape revision. Feeling shipped a §field-retirement + scope-expansion. Metaphor ships a §formal-content-shape-introduction (the `licensed-by:` field, formerly proposed in rubric only, now schema-authoritative) + Reading A scope formalization + transitive audience-meaningful encoded into schema text. Same commit lifecycle. Pattern: when the rubric mandates structure the schema doesn't currently encode, schema edit ships at Phase 5 with the locked facet file. Metaphor adds a third variant: not a rename, not a retirement, but a **formal-introduction** of structure that was previously implicit.

2. **Refuse-by-default editor discipline holds end-to-end.** Phase 1 baseline at 3 fires (1.3% over the ≤2 ceiling) with all three on AP1/AP4/AP7 was the contamination pattern. Phase 2 at 1 fire (1.3% inside band; only @73 fires) was honest restraint. Phase 4 at 2 fires (2.6% inside band; @52 + @73) is honest revision. The editor's job is to *prevent* metaphor; the rubric's licensing layer enforces that the editor's taste call must clear five independent gates (anchor + multi-justification + Q1 + Q2 + functional-register).

3. **Monument-scope determination (Phase 4 load-bearing finding).** The Phase 2 fork's @52 REFUSE rested on a hidden premise: that memory-flag anchors license only the image-domain their own text deploys. Phase 3 surfaced this as Seam 3; Phase 4 corrected it: **memory-flag anchors license from the full semantic domain of the cited monument**, not only from the memory-flag's image-domain. The flagstone is memory:2's chosen image; the monument-emma-betrayal is the licensing source. This principle generalizes: any future facet that consumes memory-flags as licensing inherits the monument-scope reading, not the image-scope reading.

4. **Hard-fence as label-fence (Seam 2 determination).** Phase 3 Seam 2 pressed: is AP5 a label-fence (proper-nouns-only) or a figure-fence (the Earth-Bet structural figure itself)? Phase 4 committed: **label-fence**. The Earth-Bet doubled-register that the worm-canon-pedant's voice-fidelity review approved is the intended mechanism — Earth-Bet monument resonance carried through structural figure into prose, with the label fenced off. A figure-fence reading would make every Earth-Bet-glossed memory-anchor self-defeating at metaphor (since the whole point is to license metaphor that activates the monument's resonance). The schema text is updated to encode this.

5. **AP4 as domain-gate (Seam 1 determination).** Phase 3 Seam 1 pressed: is AP4 a domain-level gate (vehicle-tenor-specific) or a structural-level gate (logical-recognition-shape-blocking)? Phase 4 committed: **domain-level**. A structural-level reading would bar the entire callback register from the facet (since callback is definitionally a recognition-of-prior-acquaintance structure). The rubric intends AP4 to prevent restating the memory-flag's specific figure (its vehicle + image), not to prevent participating in the same semantic occasion.

6. **Hybrid mechanic + dialect-audience review with independent gates held cleanly.** Mechanic catches form, AP-axis sweeps, multi-justification, cross-facet contract, anchor verification. Dialect catches voice-register fidelity (worm-canon-pedant POV calibration; dark-fantasy-reader Westerosi register; pulp-enthusiast figurative-reach taste). Independent signal confirmed: mechanic accepted both fires at Phase 5; dialect accepted both fires at Phase 5. Pulp-enthusiast's Phase 2 advisory ("something" indeterminacy) was absorbed in Phase 4 with a documented named-cost-object search (four candidates evaluated: coin / weight-stone / key-ring-token / reflexive-breath; all excluded for AP5 or AP6 grounds).

7. **`licensed-by:` field is load-bearing for audit.** Phase 2 mechanic flagged that the licensing structure is "not verifiable from entry notation" under the schema-current shape. Phase 5 audit confirmed: the `licensed-by:` field makes licensing auditable from the entry alone, without cross-file scan. The field narrows anchor verification to a targeted lookup (specific upstream ID) rather than a full-file scan. Schema-revision-at-ship rationale: the field is structurally load-bearing, not cosmetic.

8. **Transitive audience-meaningful is the right gate.** Memory-flags caveat-005 (NI @48 audience-meaningful retroactive evaluation) was deferred at sensory and feeling tunings. Metaphor's transitive licensing forced resolution: if a cited memory-flag was correctly refused on audience-meaningful, metaphor at that beat cannot license. The transitive gate operates by construction — there is no need to re-evaluate memory-flags @48 separately. The metaphor cannot reach @48 because no memory-flag exists to anchor it. Caveat-005 absorbed via design rather than rubric retune.

9. **The Phase 1 naive editor's three fires (@33, @64, @73) were diagnostic.** The naive baseline at @33 chose the AP4-dominant figure (door-as-shape; mirrors memory:1's own figure). The naive baseline at @64 chose the AP1-out-of-union figure (tens=3 peak with no memory or feeling anchor). The naive baseline at @73 chose the AP4-domain-collision figure (threshold-shape-recognition; mirrors memory:3's own figure). All three are textbook contaminations the rubric anticipates. The Phase 2 fork avoided all three by reading the rubric and the eligible-beat union before authoring — exactly the per-category-fork-with-rubric pattern the process doc specifies.

10. **The figure at @73 (breath-as-deposit) was the rubric-correct answer to the @73 question.** Phase 1 chose threshold-shape (AP4-collision with memory:3). Phase 2 chose breath-as-deposit (Q1 clear, AP4 clear by domain, AP5 clear by label-fence). Phase 3 pressed both gates hard. Phase 5 confirmed. The breath-figure is licensed by feeling:4 (somatic anchor), structurally mirrors memory:3 (threshold-callback), tens=1 supports, voice-register matches. This is what a clean facet-consumer fire looks like: triple-anchor + functional-register + voice-fidelity + Q1+Q2 transitive.

## Co-deployment note

Per dialogue, loc-state, tensometer, narrator-interest, state-updates, memory-flags, sensory, and feeling packages: writer + reviewer ships as a co-deployed unit. For metaphor-flags, the unit has FOUR components reflecting the single-fork-author + hybrid-review architecture:

- **Writer (single editor fork; refuse-by-default).** Loads behavior pack + variant + rubric + corpus + cross-facet locked files. Two-pass authoring (per-beat decisions on the eligible-beat union → file-shape audit). Per-beat decisions evaluate Q1 + Q2 + multi-justification (≥2 layers from {memory, feeling, tens}) + AP1-AP13 sweep + functional-register + per-scene cap. File-shape audit verifies sparsity (0-3%), per-scene cap (≤1 cross-character), schema content-shape (`licensed-by:` field), anchor verification against locked upstreams, voice-register fidelity. Editor refuses by default; the editor's job is to **prevent** metaphor, not produce it.

- **Reviewer (mechanic auditor).** Single mechanic auditor with rubric as authority. Per-fire verdicts (CORRECT / INCORRECT-{AP-axis} / REFUSE-CORRECT). Per-skip verdicts (SKIP-CORRECT / SKIP-MISSED). File-shape verdict (SHAPE-OK / SHAPE-FAIL). Cross-facet contract pre-ship check (anchor verification; pre-rendering vs contradiction distinction; beat saturation noted as stitcher concern not correctness fault).

- **Reviewer (dialect audience, voice-fidelity-only mode).** Worm-canon-pedant primary (POV cape-trained-doubled-register awareness; base-card register fidelity; AP5 label-fence vs figure-fence calibration). Dark-fantasy-reader secondary (Westerosi-overlay register; political-figure period-coherence). Pulp-enthusiast secondary (figurative-reach taste; the most calibrated ear for whether figurative reach earns its keep — load-bearing for metaphor specifically). Per-fire verdicts: VOICE-OK / VOICE-FAIL / VOICE-MIXED with citation to behavior-pack / persona-card §. Calibrated per-character. Domain restricted to voice fidelity; does not adjudicate firing decision, AP-axis, multi-justification, Q1/Q2, per-scene cap, sparsity, or cross-facet contract.

- **Verdict combination.** Mechanic + dialect verdicts are independent gates. Both must pass for ACCEPT. They cannot substitute. Phase 5 confirmed independent-signal architecture working: mechanic catches AP-axis + multi-justification + cross-facet + monument-scope + AP4-domain + AP5-label; dialect catches voice-register + figurative-reach taste.

- **Adversarial pass (Phase 3).** Mechanic auditor in hostile mode, one strongest seam per fire plus per-skip seams plus cross-facet seam plus curve seam plus hard-fence seam plus per-entry indeterminacy seam. Catches what passes naive mechanic + dialect review. The Phase 3 monument-scope SKIP-MISSED on @52 (Seam 3) is the most load-bearing seam in the run-set so far — it surfaced a structural rubric reading that retroactively governs all downstream facet-consumer authoring.

The four parts (editor-fork + mechanic + dialect + adversarial-pass) are not separable. The editor's refuse-by-default discipline only works because the mechanic auditor tests anchor + multi-justification + AP-sweeps. The dialect audience's voice-fidelity verdict only works because the editor produces entries whose figurative reach is rubric-licensed (not just rubric-compliant). The mechanic auditor's strict rubric is only meaningful because the editor can produce entries that survive monument-scope + domain-AP4 + label-AP5 challenge. The adversarial pass surfaces what the others accept.

The schema-revision-at-ship pattern is now confirmed across **three facets** (sensory + feeling + metaphor). The pipeline tolerates schema-revision-at-Phase-5 cleanly: the locked facet file uses the new shape; the schema text is revised in the same commit; the rubric § Locked notation flags the schema edit at Phase 0 so the schema change is anticipated by Phase 1. **Recommendation locked: future facet tunings should evaluate at Phase 0 whether the schema content shape needs revision; if yes, ship in same commit as Phase 5 facet file.**

The monument-scope determination at metaphor Phase 4 is the **first capstone-derived structural finding** that retroactively governs upstream rubrics. Specifically: any future facet that consumes memory-flags as a licensing layer inherits the monument-scope reading (anchor licenses the full monument's semantic domain, not just the memory-flag's image-domain). This finding generalizes beyond metaphor and should be referenced in the facet-tuning process doc next time it is updated.
