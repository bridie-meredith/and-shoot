# Treatment — run-01 (first synthesis)

Built from `idea-board.md` + `outlines.md` (Outline A) + `constraints.md` + the
reusable 122 AC asset set. This is the first *concrete* assembly — "what it looks
like when you take all of it and make something." Everything is swappable.

**Working titles:** *The Reagent Princess* · *No Dragon Required* · *A Heretic's
Grindset* · *The Dragonless* (straight option).

---

## Logline
A Targaryen princess who failed to get a dragon — and who is secretly a dead
cultivation-novel addict who doesn't know she's in Westeros — decides that if she
can't *ride* the sacred beasts she'll *grind them into immortality pills*, and
accidentally builds a realm-spanning trade-and-medicine empire to feed the habit,
right up until the Dance of the Dragons hands her a sky full of corpses and takes
everyone she refused to admit she loved.

## The lock (baseline)
- **Saerys Targaryen**, youngest of Viserys I & Alicent, b. ~116 AC. Setting-blind
  reincarnator (cultivation-genre brain, zero Westeros knowledge). Dragonless.
  Idiot-savant. Gay. Locked-inside POV — we hear the immortal's monologue; the world
  sees a feral child eating rocks.
- **Family as straight-men (cards exist):** mother **Alicent** (horrified), maternal
  grandfather **Otto** (files her as an asset), brother **Aemond** (solved
  dragonlessness by *conquest*; she "solves" it by deciding dragons are food — perfect
  foils), sister **Helaena** (the odd insect-princess — the one person who finds Saerys
  *normal*; the warm spot the war will break), father **Viserys** (the good king whose
  indulgent note is both her cage and its master key, and who never understands either).
- **Inciting inversion (canon-anchored):** like the real Rhaena, Saerys is given a dead
  cradle-egg and a replacement to *pray over nightly*. She doesn't pray. **She grinds it.**
  Her first reagent is the holy thing she was meant to worship.

## Structure — four novellas (~20k each), Outline A, escalating off the tonal ladder

### N1 — "Spirit-Beast Essence" (Tier 0 · age 3–7 · the Red Keep) — PURE FARCE
**Reagent:** dragon dung, shed scale, hatched eggshell; weirwood shaved off the
Kingsguard's Round-Room table (White Sword Tower). **Loophole engine:** ditching the
**septa** (soft walls, not bars); *eccentric-royal latitude* ("it's the dragon blood").
**Spine:** the antics ladder — the cradle-egg grind, the Dragonpit dung heist (canon:
a dragonless princess may visit the building full of everyone else's dragons),
"meditating" through lessons, licking dragonglass. The **confoundment engine** runs:
maesters say prodigy-or-pica, the Faith smells the demonic, Otto files her, Helaena
just nods along. **Relationship:** Viserys is *delighted* his daughter is so adventurous
— his love established as the warm cage. **Turn / first chill:** she eats a shard of
*real* magic, burns three days with a fever she calls a "tribulation," and surfaces
*changed* in some small uncanny way (broken clock) — the first time the adults are
**scared** instead of charmed. The myth grows teeth.
**Cost paid:** the household stops finding her funny. The septa requests her removal.
**Cards:** `cond-kl-court-state-122ac`, `cond-kl-geography-122ac`, `loc-dragonpit-*`,
Alicent / Otto / Helaena / Viserys, the (to-build) Saerys + septa.

### N2 — "The Furnace Sect" (Tier 1 · age 8–12 · KL guilds) — FARCE + FIRST STAKES
**Reagent:** the maester's apothecary; **wildfire** from the Alchemists' Guild.
**Loophole engine `[KEYSTONE]`:** she opens a **charitable sick-house** — unimpeachable
piety that launders *time, money, and supply lines* at once. The trade-and-medicine
empire is born as an alibi. (She apprentices a maester for lab access; defeats his
disapproval by being indispensable to his accounts.) **Spine:** the **wildfire caper** —
she gets her hands on the substance, it "gives someone fits," and something important
nearly burns. **Relationship:** the **dragonrider girl** enters (an OC companion, or a
resonant canon pick — Baela/Rhaena, flagged). Saerys loves the embodiment of the one
thing she failed at and calls "beneath her." **Marriage-talk begins** — betrothed young,
at a discount (dragonless), to someone worse: a real threat to a girl who, unknown to
all, has private reasons to refuse. **Turn:** the wildfire stunt forces Viserys to either
punish or protect her; his indulgent **note overrules the whole household** — and she
realizes that note is the master key. She resolves to build her *own* means and stop
needing permission. **Cost paid:** the first person gets hurt by her recklessness;
Aemond starts watching her, because he knows the shape of a dragonless Targaryen with
something to prove.
**Cards:** `loc-eastern-quarter-apothecary`, `oc-maester-edwyn` + `westeros-maester`,
`oc-broken-maester` (off-leash alchemist option), Aemond-122ac, the (to-build) love
interest.

### N3 — "The Long Roads" (Tier 2–3 · age 12–15 · Essos → Yi Ti / Sothoryos) — THE COMEDY TAKES ITS FIRST WOUND
**Reagent:** shade-of-the-evening (Qarth), Lysene poison-craft, a **Yi Tish** herb (the
xianxia-adjacent golden empire — she feels eerily *at home*), Sothoryi jungle materials.
**Loophole engine:** *factors & agents* (she sends men with papers; her body stays
accounted-for), *performed seclusion* (a "sickly princess, not to be disturbed" who is
actually three weeks at sea), the bill-of-exchange network funding it invisibly. **Spine:**
the trade revolution matures into real power, and the **entourage assembles** — a found-
family of competent, exhausted warriors babysitting a princess who walks into mortal peril
convinced she's invincible. The first true **expedition** for a far reagent. **Turn / the
wound:** a **bodyguard who believed in her dies** for an ingredient — the cost made flesh,
the first death the comedy can't meme away. She brushes the **deep-magic frontier** (a
whisper of the oily black stone — Yeen, the Five Forts). **Relationship:** the love deepens
into the one bond she can't refine or transcend. **Turn home:** she returns powerful and
subtly colder — and King's Landing is on the brink. **Cost paid:** a life, and the first
crack in the immortal act (she *refines the grief into resolve* instead of feeling it —
which is the tragedy, not the cure).
**Cards (to-build, library-promotable):** entourage trio, Essos/Yi Ti/Sothoryos location
+ condition set, the far-reagent props.

### N4 — "The Dance" (Tier 4 + the curdle + the escape · age 15–17) — THE FARCE CURDLES TO HORROR
**Spine:** **Viserys dies** → the shelter is gone → the war she never studied (she skipped
the lessons) erupts *around* her. **Dragons start dying** → her lifelong reagent shortage is
solved by a **national apocalypse** — the windfall is everyone's catastrophe. She reaches at
last for the **forbidden deep magic** (the oily black stone / beyond-the-Wall ice-magic) and
**the comedy is gone.** **Losses:** her father; the lover rides to war (dragonriders *die* in
the Dance); an entourage member falls. The question she's dodged since the cradle finally
lands: *is ascending alone over a field of corpses worth anything?*
**The ending — the bookkeeping escape `[the payoff]`:** she does not break down and mourn.
She does what she always does — **she cooks the books.** Her real power was never cultivation;
it was the logistics empire. She **steals an oceangoing ship by paperwork** — the laundered
hull that's "been the Crown's for a year" (mechanism A) plus a **letter of marque** that lets
her *repossess* it for a manufactured debt (mechanism D) — and sails off the edge of the map
**without consent**, entourage in tow, toward the deep-magic horizon she's chased all along
(Sothoryos, Ulthos, the lands the black stone came from). She never lays a hand on the ship;
she takes it the way a steward balances a book. The unsanctioned voyage *is* her refusal of
grief, dramatized — she will not stay and be mortal; she absconds toward transcendence and
leaves the wheel of dynasty over the horizon. **Launch-pad for the voyage she always wanted.**

## What makes it hang together (the rhymes)
- **One method, three locks.** Logistics defeats the **septa** (the cage), the **harbor
  master** (the ship), and — in her deluded head — **death** itself. She solves every problem
  in the story the same way: with a ledger.
- **The good king's love is mechanical irony.** Viserys's indulgent note is the master key
  that springs the cage he keeps her in; he fails to understand his daughter exactly as he
  fails to settle his succession. Micro rhymes with macro; both kill him.
- **Charity is the spine.** The sick-house alibi turns toddler rock-eating into a realm-
  spanning empire with no implausible step — and "tending the sick" is genuinely how a
  medieval noblewoman accrued autonomy, money, and supply lines.
- **Cultivation is grief-armor.** The whole bit — transcend attachment, refuse mortality,
  it's all "tribulation" — is a child's refusal to feel. The Dance is the bill. The escape is
  her paying it in the only currency she'll accept: she runs.
- **Dragonlessness is the engine, not a wound to heal.** She never gets a dragon and never
  needs one; her power has nothing to do with the thing her family worships. The dragonless
  girl out-survives the dragonriders by being the one Targaryen who treated the magic as
  *materials.*
