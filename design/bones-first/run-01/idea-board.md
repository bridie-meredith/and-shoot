## Concept `[STICKING]`

Genre mash: **cultivation-reincarnation comedy** dropped into **a universe that
absolutely does not run on cultivation logic** — pre-existing setting, real rules,
funny friction. Reference vibe: PJO / DxD / HP — settings with enough mystic
"wiggle room" that a cultivator could *almost* fit, which is what makes the
misfit funny instead of impossible.

**The protagonist is a dimwit, not a genius.** A cultivation-novel addict who
died and woke up somewhere new and immediately declared "I'm gonna cultivate" —
and just... does that. Confidently incompetent. Builds his whole life around
tropes he half-remembers. Dunning-Kruger immortal-in-training.

**Chosen world:** Westeros, in the build-up to the Dance of the Dragons, while
the key players are still **kids**. Comedy first. Then the cultivator **ages up**
into the actual Dance — and the comedy darkens as the realm slides toward a real
war he's too trope-brained to take seriously, until it's real.

## Tone `[STICKING]`
Comedy that earns tonal complexity. Starts pure farce (kids, low stakes, a child
solemnly "gathering spiritual qi" on a battlement). Tightens as canon politics
close in. The age-up is the tonal hinge.

## The engine `[STICKING]`

The joke is **mistranslation**. The protagonist runs every input through
cultivation-novel logic; Westeros answers in feudal blood-and-fire logic.

- **Core running gag — THERE IS NO QI HERE.** His entire practice is built on a
  resource the world does not contain. He meditates to "gather spiritual energy"
  and just gets cold, hungry, and lightly ill. He refuses to accept this. Every
  failure gets re-explained ("my meridians are still impure," "the spiritual
  density of this continent is low," "this is a tribulation").
- **Broken-clock seam.** Westeros DOES have real mystic seams — dragons,
  Valyrian blood-sorcery, prophecy/dragon-dreams, weirwoods, wildfire. So once in
  a while the nonsense *accidentally* lines up with something real, which (a) is
  funny and (b) keeps him convinced he's right. This is the plot motor.

### Friction map (cultivation trope → Westeros reality)
| He thinks it is... | It actually is... | Comedy |
|---|---|---|
| Spirit-beast contract | Bonding a dragon | Sometimes *works* — dragons are basically spirit beasts. Terrifying for everyone else. |
| Bloodline-purity cultivation | Targaryen incest dynasty | He *approves*, earnestly, of their "clan bloodline discipline." Everyone uncomfortable. (Keep light, played for cringe-comedy not leer.) |
| Righteous sect | The Faith of the Seven | He keeps trying to "spar for sect ranking" with septons. |
| Scholarly/alchemy sect | The Citadel / maesters | Maester potions = "pill refining." Leeches = "impurity expulsion." |
| Alchemy furnace | The pyromancers' wildfire guild | A literal alchemy sect with an exploding furnace. He wants in. |
| Qi / meridians | (nothing) | The void at the center of his whole system. |
| Breakthrough to a new realm | Just... growing up / a normal political win | He self-declares "breakthroughs" that are puberty and luck. |
| Heavenly tribulation | A storm, a fever, an actual prophetic dream | He survives a fever and announces he's "ascended a realm." |
| Face / "do you know who I am" | Feudal honor & precedence | This one MAPS. Westeros runs on face too — so he accidentally *fits in* here. |
| Immortality cultivation | Targaryen dynasty-as-immortality obsession | His pitch lands with exactly the wrong people. |

## Protagonist `[OPEN]` — the decision everything keys off

Options on the table:

- **A — Original Targaryen kid (OC prince/princess).** Max comedy freedom; real
  cast become straight men; his very existence is a wildcard that bends the Dance.
  Born ~113 AC → ~16 at the Dance (129). Needs a genuinely novel name (NOT a
  reused library OC name — see CLAUDE.md name-novelty note).
- **B — Reincarnate into a REAL Dance kid (e.g. Aemond).** His canon intensity
  re-read as cultivation-protagonist seriousness: claiming Vhagar = "spirit-beast
  taming arc," the sapphire eye = "dao-eye won through tribulation." Very funny,
  instant stakes — but heavy canon entanglement and rewrites the war's outcome.
- **C — Reincarnate into a minor/obscure real kid** (a Velaryon, a lesser
  Targaryen, a court ward). Middle ground: canon resonance, looser handcuffs.

(Leaning A for a first prototype — the comedy breathes and we're not fighting
canon — but B is the spicier, higher-resonance pick.)
