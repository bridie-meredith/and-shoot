# P28 barren-navigator

LOGLINE: On a generation ship governed by whoever holds the most descendants, a barren navigator who alone holds the star-charts is being voted off the council she needs to land the ship.

LENS: political

## Premise trajectory (5-component path)
- motivation: Seren needs to stay on the Navigation Council long enough to execute the final course correction — a six-week burn sequence only she knows how to calculate.
- anchor: The council is majority-rule among heads of family lines; Seren has no family line and holds her seat by historical appointment, which the council is now voting to revoke.
- escalation: She negotiates a deal with a minor family head — she teaches his daughter the calculation method in exchange for his bloc's votes keeping her seated.
- trade: Seren teaches the full method before the vote is secured; the family head takes his daughter's new competence to a larger family bloc and uses it to leverage a better deal elsewhere.
- irony: The method is now in three hands besides hers; the council votes her out anyway; the ship will land, but Seren will not be on the council that claims credit for landing it.

## World rules (the magic-tech that makes the engine work)
The ship was designed for a two-hundred-year transit; it is in year one hundred and eighty-three. Governance shifted forty years ago from technical meritocracy to Lineage Quorum: council votes are weighted by the number of living descendants a council member can document, verified by the ship's census registry. Seren was appointed navigator under the old system; she holds no descendant-weight and therefore a fractional vote. The final course correction requires a seventy-two-step calculation sequence — involving stellar parallax readings taken from the navigator's station, cross-referenced against the ship's positional drift — that was never formalized in the ship's documentation because Seren's predecessor taught her verbally and destroyed the written version to prevent political interference. The calculation lives in her hands and her memory. The council cannot remove her from physical access to the navigator's station without a security vote, but they can remove her from the Quorum — stripping her legal authority to execute any command, including course correction.

## Substance signature
- state_axes: position (Legal authority on the council, eroding to zero); legibility (the calculation method, currently locked in Seren's memory — her value proposition); social-tether (Seren's alliance network, which she is spending to survive)
- cost_ledger: Each vote Seren buys with partial method-disclosure earns one more council session — cost: the knowledge spreads and her leverage shrinks. Teaching the full method to the family head's daughter earns a promised bloc — cost: the knowledge is no longer hers exclusively, and the promise was verbal. Staying at the navigator's station through the burn sequence without legal authority — cost: everything she does will be attributed to whichever family head authorizes her action retroactively.
- antagonist_pressure: R1 — the largest family bloc introduces the revocation motion; Seren needs twelve votes to block it and has four. R2 — her four allies are each approached privately and offered descendant-registry upgrades in exchange for switching; two switch. R3 — the family head who took her full method teaches it to his daughter publicly, in a council session, demonstrating the ship no longer needs Seren specifically.
- dramatic_shape: Setup — the revocation motion lands on Seren's desk; she counts votes and finds she needs eight more / Escalation — she works the corridors, trading partial method-steps for commitments that erode as fast as she makes them / Midpoint reversal — she discovers the family head she is negotiating with has already taught her method to his daughter, three days ago, from notes he took during their sessions / Cost spike — the public demonstration in council; the room sees that Seren is replaceable / Climax — Seren sits at the navigator's station and runs the burn sequence alone, on the night before the revocation vote, without legal authority, because the window closes in eleven hours and she does not know if whoever replaces her will calculate it correctly / Aftermath — the ship enters final approach; the council ratifies the course in the morning and assigns the credit to the family head's daughter, who ran the verification sequence.

## Protagonist arc
Starts as a person whose sole remaining value is knowledge she has kept locked in her memory, believing that lock is protection. Ends as a person who ran the calculation alone, in the dark, for a council that will not acknowledge she did it — the single decision being to execute the burn without authority rather than let the window close while she waited for a vote she was going to lose.

## Beat outline (4-6 beats)
1. The revocation motion is read aloud at council; Seren counts hands and gets to four including her own fractional vote; she walks out without speaking and goes to the star-chart room to verify the burn window is still eleven weeks out.
2. She meets with family head Ossian in his quarters and offers him the first twelve steps of the calculation method in exchange for his bloc; she watches him copy them by hand and does not stop him.
3. She discovers — from a third party, in a corridor — that Ossian's daughter Vey presented the twelve steps to two other family heads two days ago as her own independent derivation.
4. Seren goes to Ossian and teaches Vey the remaining sixty steps in a single four-hour session, in exchange for a verbal promise of eleven votes; she watches Vey's handwriting fill a notebook.
5. In the council session three days later, Vey runs the first forty steps on the central board as a demonstration of competency; Ossian moves that Seren's seat be given to Vey.
6. Seren enters the navigator's station at 0200, eleven days before the window closes, and runs the full seventy-two steps alone; she logs the burn execution under her own credentials; in the morning the council reviews the telemetry and assigns ratification credit to the Navigation Committee, a body Seren is no longer a member of.

## Concrete engine (DEC-0115 fence)
Seren sits at the navigator's station and reads parallax numbers off the forward scope, writes them on the calculation sheet in column order, cross-checks each against the drift log from the week prior, and keys the burn command into the propulsion board when she reaches step seventy-two. The engines fire. She feels the vibration in the deck plates under her feet. She sits there until the burn completes and then logs off and goes to her bunk.

## Novelty claim
The protagonist's power is purely procedural knowledge that cannot be verified as hers once transmitted — the political engine runs on the gap between "who knows the method" and "who can prove they knew it first"; the road-to-hell is that teaching it was both the only play she had and the act that ended her.
