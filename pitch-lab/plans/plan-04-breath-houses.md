# P04 breath-houses

LOGLINE: On a tide-locked world where noble houses compete by who can hold their breath longest underwater, a commoner girl who never surfaces is adopted as a weapon — and discovers the houses drowned the last person like her to keep the rankings honest.

LENS: political

## Premise trajectory (5-component path)
- motivation: Sable wants to lift her family out of the drowning-labor tier by winning a single ranked dive for a noble patron.
- anchor: Lord Cray adopts her as his house's diver, giving her a title, a uniform, and a chain around her ankle.
- escalation: Sable beats every house record and Cray moves toward controlling the rankings board; rival houses close ranks against him.
- trade: Sable agrees to throw a dive to prevent a war between houses — surfaces for the first time, publicly, on command.
- irony: By performing limitation she doesn't have, she ratifies the system that drowned her predecessor and ensures the next person like her will be killed too.

## World rules (the magic-tech that makes the engine work)
The world is permanently tide-locked: one hemisphere faces the ocean moon and sits under perpetual tidal flood; noble houses hold territory measured in air-pockets above the waterline. Rank is determined by the Depth Register: annual timed dives in the Trench Courts, witnessed and clocked by guild timekeepers. A diver surfaces when their lungs give out. Sable's body processes dissolved oxygen directly through her skin — she feels no urge to surface and leaves no breath-bubbles. The mechanism is visible: timekeepers watch for bubbles, rivals watch for flinching, and Sable produces neither. The previous person with this trait was held under by three house guards until she stopped moving; her death was recorded as "an ill-timed cramp."

## Substance signature
- state_axes: capability (up then deliberately suppressed); position (commoner → house weapon → complicit insider); moral-framework (instrumental loyalty → active ethical betrayal)
- cost_ledger: First win earns family's debt clearance — cost: Sable is legally Cray's property, not his ward. Each subsequent record earns house rank — cost: a rival diver is demoted to drowning-labor tier, someone she has met. The thrown dive ends the inter-house conflict — cost: Sable publicly demonstrates she can be controlled, making her permanently useful as a leash rather than a diver.
- antagonist_pressure: R1 — rival house challenges the timekeeping (procedural). R2 — rival house bribes a timekeeper to foul her next dive (personal danger). R3 — three houses form a bloc and present Cray with the drowned predecessor's guild record, offering him a quiet exit if he retires Sable (existential threat framed as mercy).
- dramatic_shape: Setup — Sable dives for Cray and doesn't surface for forty minutes / Escalation — houses begin targeting her family and her patron / Midpoint reversal — she finds the drowned woman's guild record hidden in Cray's study / Cost spike — Cray tells her the thrown dive is the only move that keeps her alive / Climax — she surfaces on command in front of the Trench Courts / Aftermath — she is celebrated; the system is intact; she has made herself safe by making herself controllable.

## Protagonist arc
Starts as a body with a capability no one has catalogued, believing the capability is her way out. Ends as a body whose capability is now catalogued, leashed, and politically load-bearing — the single decision being the moment she chooses to surface, not because she must, but because Cray asked her to.

## Beat outline (4-6 beats)
1. Sable is pulled from drowning-labor when a Cray scout watches her work an underwater salvage job for three hours without surfacing; she is brought to the manor and timed in a courtyard pool.
2. Her first Trench Court dive breaks the standing record by twenty minutes; Cray's house jumps two ranks; Sable's family is handed a clearance document she is not allowed to read.
3. A rival diver corners Sable after a session and shows her a scar on his wrist — the chain-mark from a house that retired him when he became inconvenient.
4. Sable breaks into the guild archive and finds the predecessor's record: cause logged as cramp, witnessed by two Cray retainers, entry co-signed by the current Lord Cray's father.
5. Cray lays out the throw in plain terms: surface at the thirty-minute mark, lose the ranking point, and the bloc disbands; refuse and he cannot protect her from what the bloc will do.
6. Sable surfaces at thirty minutes. The crowd cheers her endurance. The chain stays on.

## Concrete engine (DEC-0115 fence)
Timekeepers kneel at the pool edge and watch for breath-bubbles rising to the surface — that is the only instrument. Sable goes under and the water stays flat. Rivals lean over the rail and watch her face through the glass panel in the Trench wall, waiting for the muscle-flinch that precedes a surface push. When she throws the dive, she pushes off the bottom herself, breaks the surface herself, gasps on purpose. The gasp is audible. The timekeepers write down thirty minutes and two seconds.

## Novelty claim
The political engine runs entirely through a biological tell that is also an absence — no bubbles, no flinch — making the protagonist's power legible only as a negative space that witnesses argue over; the road-to-hell is her learning to fake the tell rather than hide it.
