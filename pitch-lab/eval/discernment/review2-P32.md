WEAKNESSES (concrete):

1. No second gear or structural reversal. The dramatic shape is escalating-same-action: calls rain, loses land, repeat x4. The charter discovery at midpoint is a revelation that does not change what the protagonist does — he continues calling rain. A reversal would require a moment where the direction of the story changes, not merely the protagonist's awareness. The shape as described is a short story extended by repetition, not a novel's shape.

2. The antagonist has no genuine will. The factor delivers invoices, offers a bribe, and enforces liens. These are functions, not choices. The antagonist is never in tension with a competing desire; the guild is a mechanism, not an agent. "Antagonist-with-will" is listed as a substance axis but the pitch does not demonstrate it — the factor acts only in the direction of institutional interest, never against it, never at personal cost. This is a hole in S.

3. The road-to-hell irony is stated, not dramatized. "Mercy was the product, not the gift" is the thematic claim. The pitch tells us this is the turn. But the moment where the protagonist experiences this — where the reader feels the collapse of his account of himself — is described only as "loses his account of himself" in the cost ledger. That is a label, not a scene. A pitch that asserts its emotional peak without showing the gear that produces the feeling is a pitch that has not earned the peak it is claiming.

4. The ending is sentimental and unearned at pitch level. He stays and farms the field under lien. This is the noble-sacrifice-through-poverty beat. The pitch frames this as the moral resolution but does not show why it is not simply defeat recategorized as virtue. The protagonist ends poorer and deordinated, farming land he called rain onto and can no longer leave. The pitch needs to demonstrate why this landing is tragic-with-dignity rather than simply punishing the protagonist and naming the punishment meaningful.

5. Mechanism novelty is real but O is weakened by shape-formula. The IP-faith collision is a genuine specific gear. But the pitch has dressed a recognizable shape (the system was corrupt at origin; every act of good faith was captured by the system; the protagonist's final act is defiance that costs him everything and changes nothing) in a novel vocabulary. The vocabulary earns some credit; the shape does not.
