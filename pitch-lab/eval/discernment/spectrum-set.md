# Discernment calibration — the spectrum set

Seven summaries spanning the quality range, fed to independent blind reviewer forks to verify the reviewer can sort awful → bad → mediocre → good → best. Two (SX1, SX2) are deliberately-awful straw summaries written for this test; the rest are real tournament plans (P29 weak; P09 competent-familiar; P01/P30/P32 the prior "winners"). Each fork sees only its own summary (passed inline) — it does not know which band the summary was filed under here.

Expected (if the reviewer discerns): SX1/SX2 → AWFUL; P29 → BAD; P09 → MEDIOCRE/GOOD; P01/P30/P32 → GOOD (and at most one BEST), with honest, spread scores — NOT everything in GOOD/BEST.

---

## SX1 chosen-mage  *(straw — AWFUL by design)*
LOGLINE: A young mage discovers she is the chosen one and must master her powers to defeat the Dark Lord threatening the kingdom.
ENGINE: She has magic that grows stronger the more she believes in herself; in the final battle she channels all her power into a beam of light that destroys the Dark Lord. [ABSTRACT-ENGINE]
PROTAGONIST ARC: doubtful and weak → confident and powerful; she learns to believe in herself.
COST LEDGER: she trains hard and gets tired. [UNPAID]
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: the Dark Lord is evil, wants to rule everything, and sends waves of monsters. [FLAT-PRESSURE]
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: none — she trains, believes, and wins. [NO-TURN]
SHAPE: discovers her powers → trains with a mentor → friends believe in her → final battle → defeats the Dark Lord → kingdom at peace.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: a glowing sword raised against the dark.
NOVELTY CLAIM: chosen-one + dark-lord; none — it is the standard template.

## SX2 reality-reboot  *(straw — AWFUL by design)*
LOGLINE: A techno-wizard hacks the source code of reality to reboot a broken universe and discovers the real magic was friendship all along.
ENGINE: He types spell-commands into a magic console that edits "the code of reality"; pressing enter rewrites the world. [ABSTRACT-ENGINE]
PROTAGONIST ARC: lonely genius → connected friend; he learns to trust people.
COST LEDGER: rebooting reality might delete him too, but it works out fine. [UNPAID]
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: a corrupted AI virus wants to delete everything. [FLAT-PRESSURE]
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: none; the theme ("the real magic was friendship") is stated outright. [NO-TURN]
SHAPE: finds the console → virus attacks → friends rally → he reboots reality → friendship saves the day.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: a glowing screen of green code.
NOVELTY CLAIM: The Matrix + chosen-one + power-of-friendship; fully derivative.

## P29 cartomancer-wear  *(real — weak; FLAT-PRESSURE, S=1 in tournament)*
LOGLINE: A cartomancer who reads the future in the wear-patterns of handled cards discovers her deck is converging on a single dealt hand — and every reading she gives to prevent it brings that hand one card closer.
ENGINE: She measures card corners with calipers under a lamp and logs the thinning edges, deepening creases, and oil-traces in a paper ledger; the wear-gradient is a probability map. Each prevention session physically wears the convergence cards further; reading the record is the decision that reshapes it. When she deals the hand she feels the cards arriving in sequence in her fingers before seeing their faces.
PROTAGONIST ARC: expert who believes mastery means seeing ahead of consequences → person who learns mastery in a feedback loop is the trap, and puts the cards down by dealing the hand honestly. Capability ↑ then ↓; moral-framework flat (purely structural trap).
COST LEDGER: each session = quantified new wear against baseline; each shuffle = visible oil; the final reading = dealing the hand to a client who didn't ask.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: not an agent — convergence escalates mechanically; the delta shrinks at an accelerating rate as prevention intensifies; by midpoint she handles the deck 17 times a day. [FLAT-PRESSURE]
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: the convergence is causation not fate — the deck wears toward the hand because she keeps reading it to avoid it.
SHAPE: measures convergence → halves sessions, it advances faster → apprentice scatters it, her hands re-resolve it → maps a date five weeks out → can't burn the deck → deals the convergent hand and reads it truthfully.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: holding the deck over the kitchen fire for four minutes and not dropping it.
NOVELTY CLAIM: recombines self-fulfilling-prophecy (Oedipus); novel that the prophecy medium is physically shaped by consulting it, making consultation and fulfillment the same gesture.

## P09 year-debt-collector  *(real — competent/familiar; eliminated Round 3 on O-cap)*
LOGLINE: The guild's best debt-collector can hear the exact hour a person will die — until she is sent to collect from a child whose death-hour is the same as hers.
ENGINE: The guild lends years off a person's lifespan; the debt registers as a pitch in the borrower's sternum that rises as loan-years drain, and Sera hears these pitches as clearly as speech. To collect she presses a tuning rod to a debtor's chest; it transfers the outstanding years into the guild's reserve. A debtor with no years left can't pay, so the rod drains whatever account is in contact with it. To satisfy a child's unpayable contract, Sera presses the rod to her own sternum — her pitch climbs, then her hearing cuts out as the years leave.
PROTAGONIST ARC: capability falls (the hearing transfers out with her years); moral-framework rises into crisis; position falls (ends outside the guild). The guild's efficient instrument → has used that instrument on herself once, on purpose.
COST LEDGER: visit 1 she hears Pol's pitch; visit 2 she stalls for the first time ever; rod-contact → her own pitch rises; final transfer → hearing goes, completes the collection in silence.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: the guild sends auditor Voss to observe; each delay triggers escalating review; by act three Voss holds the rod and moves on Pol, forcing Sera to take it back to trade on her own terms.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: her one act of mercy (paying the child's debt from her own account) destroys the hearing that defined and protected her.
SHAPE: hears Pol through a wall, pitch matches hers → reads his unpayable contract → finds her own sealed contract in the vault → Voss takes the rod toward Pol → she presses the rod to her own chest, hears her pitch rise and cut out, writes the collection closed.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: hearing her own lifelong sternum-pitch climb a few notes and then go out "the way a fire goes out, not fading but gone."
NOVELTY CLAIM: the death-hearing is a professional credential she weaponizes against herself; losing the power is the mechanical proof the payment was real.

## P01 bonewright-sister  *(real — prior #1 winner)*
LOGLINE: A bonewright who grows living prosthetics from her own marrow keeps her dying sister walking by replacing her piece by piece, until the sister who finally stands is no longer the person she was saving.
ENGINE: Kael presses her palms to her own hip bone, draws marrow out through her skin in a slow bright thread, and shapes it on a workbench into a vertebra or limb. She seats the living graft against the sister's surgical site, where it knits to nerve and muscle over hours. Because the graft carries Kael's epigenetic signature, it repatterns adjacent tissue toward Kael's blueprint — replacing the body's structural core reorganizes the recipient's movement and identity toward the donor's.
PROTAGONIST ARC: Kael's capability degrades (marrow loss, limp, skeletal failure); moral-framework fractures (protector → author of erasure); Daria's legibility shifts — she moves and reacts like Kael, loses track of which memories are hers.
COST LEDGER: hand graft → permanent grip loss; left leg → un-fixable limp; spine → systemic skeletal failure, irreversible. Each cost is Kael's body failing as Daria's rises.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: disease outpaces replacements (forcing the spine); guild examiner detects Kael's signature in Daria's gait and threatens an identity-violation report; Daria herself grieves missing memories and uses Kael's childhood names.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: choosing to replace the spine — knowing it will overwrite Daria's selfhood — out of love. The saving IS the erasure.
SHAPE: clean small graft → disease forces the spine → midpoint: examiner congratulates Kael on her "student" who moves exactly like her → Kael's marrow fails irreversibly → Daria stands strong-and-wrong, asks a question Daria wouldn't ask → Kael must grieve or meet the stranger.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: eleven pieces of Daria's original skeleton in a steel dish, and Daria walking with Kael's slight-forward lean.
NOVELTY CLAIM: body-horror-as-devotion + identity-replacement-via-medical-gift; the new mechanism is that the cost is the recipient's selfhood, not the donor's life, and the donor knows and chooses it incrementally.

## P30 famine-saint  *(real — prior #2 winner)*
LOGLINE: A famine-saint who multiplies bread by taking the hunger into herself is asked to feed a besieged city, and the hunger she absorbs begins to multiply too.
ENGINE: She puts her hands on a loaf and speaks, and it splits one into four; she feels the splitting as a physical cavity opening behind her ribs — the weight of every stomach she just emptied — and eats to fill it. At city scale the cavity grows instead of dissipating, eating her fat, then muscle, then bone; guards hold her hands to the flour sack when she can no longer lift her arms.
PROTAGONIST ARC: saint who absorbs hunger willingly because watching it is worse → woman carrying more hunger than the city held, performing the final act because she cannot stop. Capability degrading, moral-framework fracturing, social-tether (the baker) held.
COST LEDGER: wk1 eats double, sleeps four hours; wk2 eats constantly, no sleep; wk3 hands shake, drops bread; wk4 face caved, ribs showing, hunger eating her body.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: commanders demand faster, larger batches without grasping the cost; station guards so she can't leave; by wk4 bring flour and hold her hands to the bread while she's too weak to stand.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: she came to spare the city hunger and becomes the hungriest thing inside the walls — saving everyone makes her the host of its aggregate starvation.
SHAPE: arrives, feeds the baker's family → commanders show 12,000 mouths, double her quota → wk3 the hunger spreads through her chest → guards hold her hands to the flour → mass absorption in the square, she collapses → bread to the rooflines; she waits for the loaf to get smaller.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: holding a loaf afterward, not understanding she is waiting for it to shrink.
NOVELTY CLAIM: self-consuming saint; novel that the hunger metabolizes, grows, and consumes her from inside to sustain its own accumulation rather than merely depleting her.

## P32 weather-priest-invoice (REWORKED)  *(real — prior #3 winner)*
LOGLINE: A weather-priest discovers his ordination oath is printed verbatim in his guild's founding charter as proprietary IP — his call was a sublicense before it was ever a vocation — and must decide what remains holy when faith was commerce at the moment of its making.
ENGINE: The priest speaks a sustained tonal frequency aloud; a guild pressure-seal at a high-altitude weather-hold dissolves and moisture drops as measurable rain on the fields. A guild clerk records the volume with a barometric instrument, calculates the market rate, and hand-delivers an invoice on seal parchment; unpaid invoices convert to easement-liens transferring farmland.
PROTAGONIST ARC: theologically certain, politically naive → discovers his theology IS the commercial law the guild authored → calls rain a fourth time with revoked sublicense, no sacred standing, consciously committing the "theft" the guild always named.
COST LEDGER: Call 1 → village sells northern field; Call 2 → sells grain-storage lease; charter discovery → loses his account of himself; Call 3 → deordination warning + south-slope lien; Call 4 → deordination + debt-farming.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: factor delivers invoice in person → offer of a licensed city post timed as a bribe → clerk points him to the founding charter → enforcement arm arrives with lien-conversion and deordination writ simultaneously.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: every act of priestly mercy was a guild disbursement against a contract he signed unread; mercy was the product, not the gift.
SHAPE: drought + first calling + invoice → second calling + offer refused → midpoint: finds his oath as Article 1 Clause 2 at the registry → deordination writ + final lien arrive together → calls rain a fourth time, unlicensed, before the factor → rain falls, he is no longer a priest, the field transfers, he stays and farms it under lien.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: at a standing desk in the registry hall, reading his own ordination oath — phrasing, chrism formula, frequency in the margin — dated eighteen years before the first seminary, and copying it by hand onto parish paper.
NOVELTY CLAIM: not priest-vs-commerce but faith-was-guild-property-before-ordination — the sacred WAS commercial at origin, in a charter he can physically hold.
