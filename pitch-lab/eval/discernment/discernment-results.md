## Why nothing reached BEST

Every strong plan lost the same two axes: **D** (dramatic shape) and **A** (aliveness), for the same structural reasons — escalation-without-reversal, and the best image filed as HOOK metadata instead of staged inside the story. Two strong plans (P01, P30) also left the **unavoidability** hole open (a smarter protagonist could sidestep). These are not flaws in the *concepts* — the concepts are genuinely strong — they are flaws in how the **generator** builds a summary. Which is exactly the signal the loop is supposed to produce.

## Generator-insight synthesis (the load-bearing output)

Across all seven reviews, the independent forks converged — without coordinating — on seven systemic generator faults. Ranked by how many forks independently named them:

| # | Generator fault | Named by | Fix → GEN-v2 |

| G1 | **Conflates escalation with dramatic shape** — a `[setup → escalate → collapse]` SHAPE scores D=3 forever because escalation ≠ reversal. | SX1, SX2, P29, P30, (P09) | Add a mandatory **REVERSAL** field distinct from SHAPE: name one moment audience expectation *inverts*, not just intensifies. |
| G2 | **Files the best image as HOOK metadata** instead of staging it at a named SHAPE beat; aliveness concentrated in one late image. | P29, P09, P30 | Stage the single best image *inside* a SHAPE beat; require ≥1 concrete image per major section (engine + shape), not one capstone. |

## Escalation L3 (harsher) — "still not critical enough"

The L2 forks above scored the strong plans GOOD (18–19). The principal judged that still too soft: a strong-but-**unproven pitch** landing at GOOD is itself inflation, because a pitch is potential, not achievement. L3 forks were re-run with: *this is a pitch, not a story; the hard part is unproven; credit only what is demonstrated; treat unproven payoff as a liability* — plus new attacks (PITCH-NOT-STORY / FORMULA / SENTIMENTALITY / SUSTAINABILITY). Result:

|---|---|---|---|
| P01 bonewright | 19 | **12/25** | BAD |
| P30 famine-saint | 18 | **12/25** | BAD |
| P32 weather-priest | 19 | **13/25** | BAD |

Every L3 fork landed the FORMULA attack (the "magic mechanism whose use IS the tragic cost / road-to-hell" template is a cliché shape even when the mechanism is novel → caps originality), PITCH-NOT-STORY (interiority + earned emotion + novel-length sustainability all unproven), and SUSTAINABILITY (one transaction repeated at rising magnitude is escalation, not reversal — a short-story engine inflated to novel scale). This is the bar the principal wanted: clever pitches are exposed as *unproven and formulaic*, not waved through as "good."

## Field-critic (non-blind) — the criticism independence structurally cannot make

Blind forks each see one plan, so none can see that **the whole field is one shape**. The field-critic read the four strongest together (`field-critic.md`):

- **Shared formula:** "every pitch is a mechanism whose operation IS the harm — a power that saves, feeds, purifies, or collects only by consuming the wielder or the saved, delivered as tragic irony where devotion and destruction are the same act." One trick repeated, not four explorations of it. All four even share a hook convention (a physical image the protagonist can't yet interpret, shown at the moment of completed loss).
- **Range deficit (severe):** absent entirely — comedy, hope, forward-motion adventure, a protagonist who WINS, mystery, ensemble, tonal variation. *"A principal choosing from this field is choosing which flavor of 'capacity = harm' to write for a year."*
- **Sentimentality (most→least bought):** P30 > P01 > P09 > P32.
- **Harsh ranking:** **P09 > P32 > P01 > P30**, and crucially: *"None of these is best in absolute terms. P09 is best-of-field in a field where that designation is damning."*

This overturns the gauntlet's verdict (which had P01 #1) — because the gauntlet rewarded surviving attacks, while the field-critic judges against the *space of possible pitches*, where this whole field occupies one cramped corner.

## The corrected verdict

The earlier `best-3/` (P01, P30, P32 at 24/25) was a **gauntlet artifact** and is retired. The honest finding:

1. Under independent brutal forks, no pitch in this field reaches GOOD, let alone BEST — they are clever, concrete, formulaic, and unproven (BAD–MEDIOCRE).
2. The *field itself* fails on range — the real defect is upstream, in the 33-prompt brief and the generator, which over-selected one tragic-irony shape (G8 below).
3. If forced to rank, **P09 > P32 > P01 > P30**, with the explicit caveat that "best here" means "least-cramped in a cramped field."

| # | Generator fault (added at L3 / field-critic) | Fix → GEN-v2 |
|---|---|---|
| G8 | **The brief over-selected one shape** — rewarded tragic-irony-mechanism, never solicited comedy / hope / a winnable plot / a human antagonist / moral ambiguity, so 33 prompts converged. | Require explicit **category coverage** (tragic / comic / ambiguous / adventure / mystery as slots to FILL), a human antagonist in ≥⅓, ≥1 prompt where the protagonist wins and that win creates a forward problem, and a **structural-sameness penalty** so convergence is punished, not rewarded. |
| G9 | **Mistakes "premise implies depth" for "pitch demonstrates depth."** | Require the pitch to demonstrate a REVERSAL and one beat of interiority, not infer them from concept quality. |
