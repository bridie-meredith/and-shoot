BAND: BAD

ATTACKS LANDED:
- PITCH-NOT-STORY: landed hard. The pitch demonstrates a mechanism and a cost ledger; it does not demonstrate interiority, voice, or the capacity to sustain a novel. The protagonist's inner life is a single sentence ("watching it is worse"). Everything credited here is structural architecture, not proven execution. The hard parts — why she made this bargain in the first place, what she wants other than to stop watching, whether she has a self distinct from her function — are entirely unproven. This is a premise card, not a character.
- FORMULA: landed. "Magic whose USE is the tragic cost" is the most overproduced shape in dark-fantasy pitching. The self-consuming healer, the price-extraction sorcerer, the gift that devours the gifted: the market is saturated. The pitch's novelty claim ("hunger metabolizes and grows rather than merely depleting her") is a calibration of an existing dial, not a new mechanism. The SHAPE is formula even if the specific wasting-curve is somewhat fresh.
- SENTIMENTALITY: landed. Besieged city, 12,000 starving mouths, famine-saint who cannot stop even unto death — this is stock pathos assembled from pre-certified suffering icons. The hook ("waiting for the loaf to shrink") reaches for earned strangeness but is undercut by the surrounding scaffold of maximally legible tragedy. The cost ledger ("face caved, ribs showing") is clinical inventory of a suffering saint; it activates pity on demand, which is the definition of cheap.
- SUSTAINABILITY: landed. The engine is one transaction iterated to collapse. Week 1 → Week 2 → Week 3 → Week 4 → collapse. There is no reversal: the shape is linear escalation with a terminal. "Can she sustain 12,000 mouths?" is answered immediately by the cost ledger, which tells us she cannot. The novel's only remaining question after page 20 would be HOW she collapses, not WHETHER or WHY. A short story can carry this; a novel requires the protagonist to have decisions, deflections, recoveries, relationships that change the stakes — none of those are in the pitch.

WEAKNESSES (>=3 concrete):
1. Antagonist has no will, only demand. The commanders are placeholders who "demand faster, larger batches without grasping the cost." An antagonist who does not grasp the cost is not an antagonist with will — they are a structural pressure device. There is no scene in which the antagonist's goals and the protagonist's goals are in genuine conflict; the antagonist does not want anything the protagonist could negotiate with. This makes the "antagonist pressure" axis a false fill — the dramatic engine is not antagonist pressure but protagonist self-destruction. The pitch does not know the difference.
2. Reversal is absent. The pitch names escalation at each stage but no reversal — no moment where the protagonist's relationship to her power, her choice, or her situation inverts. "Arrives willing, ends involuntary" is described as a turn but is in fact the slow completion of the opening premise. A reversal would require her to want something different mid-story, to make a choice that backfires in an unexpected direction, to be wrong about something she was certain of. The cost ledger forecloses surprise: readers know week 4 from week 1.
3. The hook image is disconnected from what the pitch earned. "Holding a loaf, not understanding she is waiting for it to shrink" is a genuinely strange image — dissociation, a body that has learned to read food as a threat signal even when the crisis is over. But the pitch does not earn this: the cost ledger describes physical collapse, not psychological drift. The image requires interiority — a formed self that has been deformed — and the pitch has not built a self for the reader to care about losing. The final image is borrowed from a more interior story than this pitch actually describes.
4. The famine-saint archetype is borrowed without transformation. The pitch imports the suffering-intercessor figure (voluntary bodily sacrifice for collective good, female saint body as site of communal absorption) without doing anything structurally new with it. The theological/mythic weight is activated for free. The novelty claim rests entirely on the metabolic specificity of the cost, not on any reconception of who this person is, why she chose this, or what that choice says about anything beyond itself.

SUMMARY INSIGHTS (2-3, axis + exact change):
- D (dramatic shape): score should drop from whatever the prior reviewer gave to 2. The pitch has escalation but no reversal, and the single-move engine eliminates the novel's capacity for genuine dramatic turns. To raise D, the pitch must name a moment where the protagonist is WRONG about something consequential — not just physically depleted but narratively surprised.
- O (originality): score should drop to 2. "Cost-extraction magic consumed its user" is a genre template; the metabolic specificity of hunger-that-grows is a new dial setting, not a new mechanism. To raise O, the mechanism must do something the archetype structurally forbids, not merely do it with more anatomical precision.

GENERATOR INSIGHT (1-2 systemic):
- This pitch received a GOOD (18/25) from the prior reviewer because the cost ledger is written with unusual clinical specificity (week-by-week physical degradation is genuinely detailed) and that specificity was mistaken for demonstrated interiority and dramatic substance. Clinical inventory of suffering is NOT the same as earned suffering. The generator's strength is architectural precision; its weakness is substituting structure-of-suffering for a character who has a stake in being alive beyond their function. Any pitch from this source should be checked: does the protagonist want something OTHER than to do the thing they do? If not, the interiority is missing regardless of how specific the cost schedule is.
- The FORMULA attack is the hardest to defend against at the pitch stage because the mechanism's novelty (hunger-that-metabolizes) distracts from the shape's familiarity (self-consuming gift). A reviewer charmed by the mechanism will miss that the shape is standard. Flag mechanism-novelty claims for shape analysis before crediting them.

ONE-LINE VERDICT: A clinical cost schedule wearing the clothes of a character study — the pitch has built the scaffold of tragedy without building the person the tragedy happens to.
