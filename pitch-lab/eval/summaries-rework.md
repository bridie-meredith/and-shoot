PLAN: P03 healing-wasps (REWORKED)
LENS: penitential
---
LOGLINE: A field-surgeon who carries a hive of healing wasps in his chest saves lives at the cost of stealing, from a nearby villager, the memory that proves the disease was ever real.
ENGINE: Davan unlatches a brass port beneath his collarbone; three to five wasps crawl out across his fingers onto a patient's wound, sting the infection, and return — fever breaks, necrosis reverses within hours. Within half a day, one person inside a two-mile radius permanently loses their clearest memory of being sick or watching sickness; the hive strengthens with each cure until one treatment can blank a household's memory of a season. Fully concrete and picturable.
PROTAGONIST ARC: Believes healing is unconditional good → understands the cure only buries, not undoes → closes the chest-port on a conscious, frightened, curable patient and lets him die remembering. Moral-framework ↓, social-tether ↑-then-↓.
COST LEDGER: Cure 1 → innkeeper forgets husband's fever week; Cure 5 → Rem forgets his father was ever ill; Final → whole village forgets the original patient existed. Escalation paid and specific.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: The plague re-emerges in the south well; each cure-round leaves the village less able to recognize symptoms, so the outbreak accelerates exactly as the hive grows more potent. Escalating, not flat.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: The surgeon who wanted to erase his failure succeeds — the village forgets the death he caused and forgets it was his fault. Present.
SHAPE: Davan heals freely → cures compound, forgetting spreads → Rem reaches mid-sentence for his dead father's name, hand closing on nothing, listeners watching him grope → cured woman walks to the tainted well and drinks because the danger-memory is gone; Davan sees the whole loop happen in front of him → he closes the latch and the patient dies remembering.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: Rem reaching mid-sentence for his father's name, hand closing on nothing, the table going still as he gropes and finishes with a different word.
NOVELTY CLAIM: The memory-cost is stripped from the healer and levied on bystanders in a medically specific, subject-mirroring way — building a collective-amnesia antagonist the protagonist manufactures with every moral act.

PLAN: P32 weather-priest-invoice (REWORKED)
LENS: political
---
LOGLINE: A weather-priest discovers his ordination oath is printed verbatim in his guild's founding charter as proprietary IP — his call was a sublicense before it was ever a vocation — and must decide what remains holy when faith was commerce at the moment of its making.
ENGINE: The priest speaks a sustained tonal frequency aloud; a guild pressure-seal at a high-altitude weather-hold dissolves and moisture drops as measurable rain on the fields. A guild clerk records the volume with a barometric instrument, calculates the market rate, and hand-delivers an invoice on seal parchment; unpaid invoices convert to easement-liens transferring farmland. He calls rain; it falls; a document arrives; a field changes hands. Concrete and picturable.
PROTAGONIST ARC: Theologically certain, politically naive → discovers his theology IS the commercial law the guild authored → calls rain a fourth time with revoked sublicense, no sacred standing, consciously committing the "theft" the guild always named. Position erodes, moral-framework fractures.
COST LEDGER: Call 1 → village sells northern field; Call 2 → sells grain-storage lease; charter discovery → loses his account of himself; Call 3 → deordination warning + south-slope lien converts, guild sells rain back at premium; Call 4 → deordination + debt-farming. Paid, escalating.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: Factor delivers invoice in person → offer of a licensed city post timed as a bribe → clerk points him to the founding charter → enforcement arm arrives with lien-conversion and deordination writ simultaneously, sublicense revocable. Escalating.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: Every act of priestly mercy was a guild disbursement against a contract he signed unread; mercy was the product, not the gift. Present.
SHAPE: drought + first calling + invoice → second calling + offer refused → midpoint: finds his oath as Article 1 Clause 2 at the registry → deordination writ + final lien arrive together → calls rain a fourth time, unlicensed, before the factor → rain falls, he is no longer a priest, the field transfers, he stays and farms it under lien.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: At a standing desk in the registry hall, reading his own ordination oath — phrasing, chrism formula, frequency in the margin — dated eighteen years before the first seminary, and copying it by hand onto parish paper.
NOVELTY CLAIM: Not priest-vs-commerce but faith-was-guild-property-before-ordination — the sacred WAS commercial at origin, in a charter he can physically hold; his final act is the first time his deed and the guild's description of it exactly agree.
