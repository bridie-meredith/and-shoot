PLAN: P01 bonewright-sister
LENS: relational
---
LOGLINE: A bonewright who grows living prosthetics from her own marrow keeps her dying sister walking by replacing her piece by piece, until the sister who finally stands is no longer the person she was saving.
ENGINE: Kael presses her palms to her own hip bone, draws marrow out through her skin in a slow bright thread, and shapes it on a workbench into a vertebra or limb. She seats the living graft against the sister's surgical site, where it knits to nerve and muscle over hours. Because the graft carries Kael's epigenetic signature, it repatterns adjacent tissue toward Kael's blueprint — replacing the body's structural core reorganizes the recipient's movement and identity toward the donor's.
PROTAGONIST ARC: Kael: capability degrades (marrow loss, limp, systemic skeletal failure, two years from collapse); moral-framework fractures (protector → author of erasure); social-tether to Daria inverts as Daria becomes less Daria. Daria: legibility shifts — she moves and reacts like Kael, loses track of which memories are hers.
COST LEDGER: On-page and escalating: hand graft → permanent grip loss; left leg → an un-fixable limp; spine → systemic skeletal failure, irreversible. Each cost is Kael's body visibly failing as Daria's visibly rises. Concrete and paid.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: Three escalating rounds: disease outpaces replacements (forcing the spine); guild examiner detects Kael's signature in Daria's gait and threatens an identity-violation report; Daria herself begins grieving missing memories and using Kael's childhood names. Rises cleanly.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: Choosing to replace the spine — the point of no return — knowing it will overwrite Daria's selfhood, doing it anyway out of love. The saving IS the erasure.
SHAPE: Clean small graft (control believed) → disease forces the spine → midpoint: examiner congratulates Kael on her "student" who moves exactly like her → Kael's own marrow fails irreversibly → Daria stands strong-and-wrong, asks a question Daria wouldn't ask → Kael must grieve or meet the stranger.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: Eleven pieces of Daria's original skeleton in a steel dish, and Daria walking with Kael's slight-forward lean.
NOVELTY CLAIM: Recombines body-horror-as-devotion + identity-replacement-via-medical-gift (both known); the new mechanism is that the cost is the recipient's selfhood, not the donor's life, and the donor knows and chooses it incrementally from love.

PLAN: P02 star-cartographer
LENS: vocational
---
LOGLINE: The empire's last star-cartographer discovers that the constellations she charts are a language, that reading them aloud rewrites the sky, and that her life's masterwork is slowly erasing the stars sailors steer by.
ENGINE: She stands at the observatory window and speaks a constellation's full syllable-string aloud from her atlas; the specific named stars physically move across the dome and stop in a new, permanent position, verifiable by sextant. Sailors taking their next bearing on those stars are wrong by exactly the degrees the stars moved and strike reefs. Voice → stars relocate → charts go false → ships wreck. She cannot partial-read without scattering stars into noise.
PROTAGONIST ARC: capability rises (reads farther, controls more sky) → legibility falls (the mapped sky her identity rests on ceases to exist); social-tether frays (apprentice's route darkens, trusting sailors die). COST LEDGER: Each reading: one star-cluster gone permanently → one sea-lane uncharted → sailors die there. Weaponized readings buy the empire tactical wins at the cost of the only sky she knows. Concrete, escalating, paid on-page (red ink per dead star).
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: Empire reassigns her from preservation to erasure; she complies once; navy produces a politically-justified kill-list of enemy port-stars; her apprentice's ship is declared "acceptable loss" if she finishes the northern sequence. Escalates to the personal.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: She reads the northern sequence — knowing her apprentice is on that route — because the empire offers no third option; the reroute order arrives three hours too late.
SHAPE: Dutiful charting, notices readings change sky → empire weaponizes her → midpoint: apprentice's route is next on the list → she reads anyway, his ship goes down → climax: she reads the empire's own home-port stars as her last bargaining chip → aftermath: blank sky, teaches dead reckoning.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: Crossing out real stars in her atlas with one line of red ink each, the night after they vanish from the sky.
NOVELTY CLAIM: Constellation-as-language is known (Le Guin-adjacent True Speech); the inversion is the engine — *completing* the masterwork *is* the destruction; the protagonist isn't tempted off-mission, the mission is the catastrophe.

LENS: penitential
---
LOGLINE: A field-surgeon who amputated a plague-arm one hour too late now carries a hive of healing wasps in his chest; every life he saves costs a villager the memory that proves the disease was ever real.
ENGINE: Davan unlatches a brass port beneath his collarbone; three to five wasps crawl out across his hand onto a patient's wound, sting the infection, and return. The patient heals within hours (fever breaks, necrosis reverses). Within half a day, one person within two miles loses their clearest memory of being sick or witnessing sickness — a precise, permanent gap they don't feel go. The hive strengthens per cure; eventually one treatment blanks a household's whole season.
PROTAGONIST ARC: moral-framework degrades (each cure re-argues the same wrong trade); legibility falls (village loses its record of the plague); social-tether rises then falls (bonds with the dead man's son, then erases the son's memory of his father). COST LEDGER: Cure 1 → innkeeper forgets her husband's fever week; Cure 5 → the son forgets his father was ever ill; Final → village forgets the original patient existed. Levied on bystanders, escalating, concrete.
ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: The plague re-emerges in the water table; each round of cures leaves the village less able to recognize symptoms, so the outbreak accelerates exactly as the hive grows more potent. Self-amplifying loop.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: Curing freely — the moral act itself — manufactures the collective amnesia that lets the plague kill again; saving people is what dooms them.
SHAPE: Heals a child freely → cures compound, forgetting spreads → the son can no longer name what killed his father → Davan sees the hive will erase the death entirely → must let a patient die or erase the last witness → he closes the port; the patient dies remembering.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: A villager pausing mid-sentence and losing the thread — "a gap where a particular winter used to sit."
NOVELTY CLAIM: The memory-cost is stripped from the healer and levied on bystanders in a medically specific way that mirrors the cure's subject — a collective-amnesia antagonist the protagonist manufactures with every moral act.

---

PROTAGONIST ARC: capability rises then is deliberately suppressed; position moves commoner → house weapon → complicit insider; moral-framework moves instrumental loyalty → active ethical betrayal. ANTAGONIST PRESSURE: R1 rival challenges timekeeping (procedural); R2 rival bribes a timekeeper to foul her (personal danger); R3 three houses form a bloc, produce the drowned predecessor's record, offer Cray a "merciful" exit. Escalates procedural → physical → existential.
ROAD-TO-HELL TURN: Surfacing on command to prevent a house-war ratifies the system that drowned her predecessor and guarantees the next person like her dies too — performing limitation she doesn't have.
SHAPE: Pulled from drowning-labor → first dive shatters the record → rival shows her his chain-scar → she finds the predecessor's "cramp" record co-signed by Cray's father → Cray lays out the throw as her only survival → she surfaces at thirty minutes; crowd cheers; the chain stays.
HOOK / ALIVENESS: Timekeepers kneeling at the pool edge watching flat, bubble-less water; the deliberate, audible fake gasp at thirty minutes.
NOVELTY CLAIM: The political engine runs entirely on a biological tell that is an absence (no bubbles, no flinch), legible only as contested negative space; the road-to-hell is learning to fake the tell rather than hide it.
