## Gate result

**All 33 PROMOTE.** No plan fell below the Tier-1 floor. The field is uniformly strong — the summarizers reported zero `[ABSTRACT-ENGINE]`, `[UNPAID]`, or `[NO-TURN]` flags across all 33, only three `[FLAT-PRESSURE]` (P11, P17, P29). This is a *good problem*: it means selection happens at the hard tiers, not here.

## Observation → PI-05

Tier 1 promoted everyone, so as a cut it did no work on this field. That is not a rubric failure — Tier 1 is *meant* to be generous and only catch the broken — but it tells us the difficulty must ramp faster than a flat +3-per-round when the field over-performs. Round 2 therefore re-scores the whole field at a **skeptical** stance with explicit anti-inflation calibration anchors (see PI-05), compressing the bunched 19-24 band so the top separates. Logged to process-improvements.md.
