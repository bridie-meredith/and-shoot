## Gate

**5 PROMOTE** straight into the final contender pool: P01, P10, P19, P22, P30.

**4 ELIMINATE** — P05, P09, P26, P31, all at exactly 19, all on the same disqualifier: **Originality capped at 3 because the engine is a nameable archetype** ("deserter becomes what she fled," "debt-collector sacrifices self," "grief-object harms the community," "deserter endangers hidden community"). Each is a genuinely well-built plan; the cut is purely that the *mechanism* is recognizable. Note this is the single most common Tier-3 killer (see PI-09) — concreteness and substance were near-universal in this field; originality is what actually separates.

**6 REWORK** — P03, P04, P18, P20, P23, P32, each one axis short with no disqualifier and a *specific, actionable* fix logged in the scorecards.

## The iterate-to-best decision (PI-10)

This is the round where "rework until best" earns its keep. I carry **two** rework candidates into a lift attempt rather than all six, because the final pool already holds 5 clean promotes and the rework budget should go where it can change the *winner*, not pad the shortlist:

- **P03 healing-wasps** — Round 2's evaluator called it "the strongest plan in the field"; Round 3 dinged it only for having no *exceptional* axis (all 4s). The two rounds disagree, which is exactly the case rework should adjudicate. Fix: two scene-level lifts (A: a physical memory-gap beat; D: make the midpoint realization a visible event).
- **P32 weather-priest-invoice** — held back solely by O=3, but with the single most surgical fix in the field: reframe so the *ordination ceremony itself was a guild contract*, converting "priest vs. commerce" into "priest whose theology was commercial property before he held it" — a genuinely un-nameable engine.

The other four reworks (P04, P18, P20, P23) are logged with their fixes as **honorable mentions / not carried** — strong plans whose lift wouldn't displace the top cluster. P23 (silence-translator) and P20 (grief-archivist, best A-image in the field) are the strongest of these.

## Carry into Round 4 (Tier 4 BESTEST, adversarial)

Pool: **P01, P10, P19, P22, P30** + **reworked P03, P32** (if the reworks clear Tier 3 on re-score). Round 4 runs the 4-attack adversarial gauntlet (generic / naive-follow / unavoidability / one-image); only plans surviving all four pass. Target: best 3.
