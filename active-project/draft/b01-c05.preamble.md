*I had given the four-ward sheet to the courier in the cooper's yard, and the arrangement that ran through him was now a thing I had named to myself and walked back from in the same hour. The accounting closed. The harm I could prevent was still the only column the accounting would close. The coverage from the four wards held overnight. This morning the count reached past the stitch-house lane into a ward where the alleys lean up toward the hill — ground I had not walked before, ground that abuts the lower servant-passages of the Red Keep. The method was the same. The substrate was about to be different.*

*The arrangement bought protection for a fourteen-year-old ward of a Hightower cadet branch placed in Alicent's household. Her name was Sera. Her standing rested on a question about her parentage that Otto's rivals at court could surface to damage him; Otto kept the question quiet by knowing what was moving before they did. The intelligence I routed through the courier was the form that paid for the quiet. I had agreed to this. I read court bodies now.*
