The flies came back with the heat-signatures and the beetles came back with the threshold-count. I left the drain angle. The angle was the farthest south point in the lane, the gap where the two tenement walls pinched, and from there every approach down the forty paces was visible before it arrived.

The fever-cluster came back too. Three signatures across two alleys, the day's unresolved load. The three would be priced before sundown or not at all.

I fanned the insects out down the alleys and the ward delivered up the heat-signatures and I took the alley-mouth at the north end where the line-of-sight ran both ways, and the insects crossed the threshold-stones into the Hook.

The feed held four hundred bodies.

The alley-back dropped out before I turned my head. The whole back of the lane went silent in the feed and the silence was the receipt for the range I had not yet committed to take.

I extended the range.

The stitch-house-lane precinct gave way and the feed ran past the forty paces into the Hook beyond. The four hundred had been the ceiling and it had just been paid for in the back of my skull. The cost rose with the range.

I drew the line. The prohibition was the prohibition I had set myself. The line I drew here was the line that would be asked of me again, and I drew it in the same hand. It held at the ceiling and not past it.

The insects filled the Hook.

The foot-traffic knotted the ward-junction — a corner in the Hook where three or four alleys gave onto one another under a single precinct-ward's reach.
