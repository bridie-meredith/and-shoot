*I am twenty years old by the calendar I came in with, and the year here is 122 AC — late in Viserys-the-First's reign, though no one in Flea Bottom counts the king's years, only feast, shortage, and the lord's levy. I live in King's Landing now, in the slum south of the three hills. I came here with a rule. Be useful without taking control. Do not do, again, what I once did at the end of the world I came from.*

*Flea Bottom is the city's lowest ground, the warren south of Aegon's Hill where the smallfolk live who count their coin in copper — pennies for a small meal, stars for a cup of water or a courtesy paid. I pay copper stars for a corner-room off the Hook, a curving lane near the river where the most marginal of Flea Bottom keeps to itself, and I mend nets to pay for the next week.*
