The flies came back with the heat-signatures, the beetles with the threshold-count. I left the drain angle while the returns were still arriving, and the body went where the morning sent it: north along the lane toward the alley-mouth, forty paces of wall I had walked enough times to walk without watching.

The fever-cluster came back too. Three heat-signatures across two alleys — the day's unresolved load, and one that would be priced before sundown or not at all. I had told myself that twice already.

The insects fanned the alleys and the ward delivered the heat-signatures and I took the alley-mouth and the insects crossed the threshold-stones, and none of it required watching either, because the hands of the work were not the hands.

Four hundred bodies sat in the feed. The count was even. The count was the gauge by which the count stayed even.

The alley-back dropped from the feed before the head turned to ask why. The dropout was the receipt — the back of the bill, signed in advance, for a range I had not yet committed to take.

I extended the range.

It was paid for in the back of the skull, and it was the same prohibition I had set myself when I sat down with the count the first time. The line held at the ceiling and not past it. I drew the line where the line had been drawn before, in the same hand.

The insects filled the Hook. The foot-traffic knotted the ward-junction — a corner in the Hook where three or four alleys gave onto one another under a single precinct-ward's reach.
