*The arrangement holds. The count runs the Hook and four wards past it now, and the coverage extends, and the harm I can prevent is still the only column that closes. I know the stitch-maker's days the way I know the lanes — Wren keeps the Hook's children fed and clothed, and the feed has been threading her street since the boundary moved, and I have not had to ask it to. The courier I had without a name has one now; I filed it last circuit and did not ask whose errands he runs. This morning the circuit is routine. The Jarvis line will run at its standard interval.*
