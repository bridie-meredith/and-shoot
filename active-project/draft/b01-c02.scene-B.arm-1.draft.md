Days of it, and the pattern came. The insects came back from the lane carrying a body the map had not yet had a slot for — a woman crossing the same thresholds on the same sweeps, day after day, returning as a junction-signature before she returned as anything else.

The tallow smoke from the stitch-house east wall rolled at floor-level into the lane, lamps lit, the work-day running. The coverage map anchored on that threshold and waited.

The beetles came back from the stitch-house door, the weight on stone repeating itself — same foot first, same pause at the sill, same sequence under the same lintel, morning after morning. The weight read as one body, not many. The pattern read as one body.

I took the drain angle again and let the insects work the lane. The body was already crossing the thresholds outward, going wherever she went in the mornings, and the feed gave her to me in the same order both ways.

Then the alley admitted her, and I yielded the alley-mouth — the lane-mouth I had been holding from the south, the same threshold she was coming through from the north, the two of us occupying it from opposite sides for the moment it took her to pass. The alley-mouth had been priced as exposure before I'd taken it, and the yield was calculated before her body arrived in it.

The insects returned her function-signature. The map gave her a slot — connector-type, the corner her foot-pattern made readable. The slot was the only place she fit in it, because the slot was the only language the map had.

I turned from the alley-mouth. The discipline held. The alley-mouth was not where I would learn anything I did not already have.

The insects filed the ward-junction contact under its function-label, and the gesture moved in a hand that knew the motion already — knew it from another accounting under another architecture, the hand reaching for the slot before the mind had finished naming what it was filing. The ledger had a new entry and no signature against it.

The feed thinned.

The insects reached the far margin of the junction-lane, and the ward-traffic past it stopped being readable — the coverage map closing on its outer edge, attenuation as boundary, the negative space inside the accounting now the same shape as everything else in it.
