Days of it, and the pattern came.

The insects returned a body the map had not yet carried a slot for — a body that crossed the ward-junction at the same stones at the same hour, sweep after sweep. The tallow smoke marked the stitch-house lane that morning the way it had marked every morning since the work-day in the precinct had started running before mine: the lamps lit on the east wall, the smoke rolling low at the lane-floor, the day already moving by the time my feed was up. The coverage map anchored on the stitch-house threshold and held.

The beetles returned the threshold-crossings and the threshold-crossings repeated the weight-on-stone sequence — the same gait, the same stones in the same order, pass after pass.

I took the drain angle.

The ward-junction body crossed the thresholds. She came down the alley from the precinct side toward the lane-mouth, and the alley admitted her — the same alley-mouth I held line-of-sight on from forty paces south, the threshold occupied from both sides at once, her body crossing into it and mine looking down it. I yielded the alley-mouth. The yield was already priced. The alley-mouth had been carried on the ledger as exposure since the second sweep, and the cost of standing in it when a ward-junction body crossed had been billed and paid before her body arrived in it. The insects returned the function-signature. The map assigned the junction-body the connector-type slot.

I turned from the alley-mouth. The alley-mouth was not the place I learned anything I did not already have. The discipline held.

The insects filed the ward-junction contact.

The ledger took the entry and there was no signature against it. The body had been carried into the map under a function-label without consent and without contact, and the gesture of filing moved in a hand that knew the motion — that had moved this way before, in another accounting, under another architecture, and would move this way again because the motion was the architecture and the architecture was mine. I did not stop the filing. The entry stood.

The insects returned the junction-lane attenuation. The feed thinned past the corner, the body-count falling off where the alley-network frayed into the broader ward, and the insects reached the junction-lane edge. The map had a hard outer margin there — not a failure of the sweep but the second register of the same accounting. The place where the ward-traffic was no longer readable was itself an entry, and the entry sat at the edge of the map, and held.
