## Where the prose fails

### 1. Apparatus-register dominates the middle (c02–c05)

The voice prime visible in the c01 prologue blocks and the c07 ledger-stylus passages does not hold across the body of c02–c05. Those chapters read as logistics-system operating instructions: "the feed returned," "the count," "the insects returned," "the accounting closed" — a litany of register-tics rather than load-bearing nouns. By c05 the reader is being asked to do interpretive work on near-mechanical prose. This is exactly the failure class PROP-0022 / PROP-0023 are trying to surface. The retroactive c05 verification said the chapter was ALIVE; the cold-read here says: not across the sub-section.

### 2. Sensory grounding is named but not embedded

Tallow smoke, drain water, fish-cart, lane-cold, the chandler's candle-smell — sensory specifics are present at the *anchor* level (the chapter labels them), but they get filed alongside count entries in the same processed register. They do not break the apparatus-rhythm. c06 (Septon corner, cold stone, smoke at foot level) and c07 (the cold coming through the soles) are the only sustained sensory grounds that read as inhabited rather than logged.

### 3. The c06 four-names beat has no on-page consequence

The sub-section's most loaded action — Taylor sealing the form with four Black-faction ward elders by name, the cost-axis crossing the trade was designed to surface — receives **zero follow-through** in c07. c07's prologue says "the circuit is routine" and the chapter pivots to an abstract ethics argument with the Septon. The reader sets the book down at c06 expecting blood downstream; c07 opens with quiet. This is the largest narrative ball drop in the seven chapters. Either c07 needs to register the consequence, or c06's sealing needs to be re-framed as one trade among many (which it isn't — the chapter is structured around it as a singular axis crossing).

### 4. The Rushwick courier attack (c05) is also unprocessed

Three figures coordinate an enforcement-incident in front of Taylor; she files the body-record. The act lands as evidence the apparatus is recognizing the route. But the courier doesn't recur. The attack doesn't recur. The Rushwick ward doesn't recur in c06–c07. As staged it should be either the doorway to Otto-tier substrate or the moment the apparatus refuses neutrality — currently it is the moment-and-then-nothing.

### 5. Time skips are not signaled cleanly

Calendar drifts: c01→c02 = three weeks (prologue). c02→c03 = compressed days. c03→c04 = one day. c04→c05 = unspecified (presumably weeks of the arrangement running). c05→c06 = no signal. c06→c07 = two months (prologue). A reader hits c07 unsure how long the seven-chapter span covers. Best guess: ~3 months. Should be cleaner.

### 6. Bridges live entirely in the italic prologues

Every chapter opens with an italicized first-person summary doing all cross-chapter context work. Once the prologue ends, the prose body resets. The body is self-contained per chapter — exactly the cold-read whitespace finding. Across the sub-section the bridges are scaffolding that hasn't been internalized into the body.

### 7. Wren's c06 "There's a way past" is the strongest line and barely earned

The one-line dialogue from Wren in c06 is the strongest beat in the sub-section: a stranger extends care while standing outside Taylor's ledger, and Taylor immediately prices the field she leaves blank. But she has been **absent** from on-stage attention since c01 — feed-flag in c02, not-written-down in c04, no presence in c03/c05. Her c06 line should land like a struck bell. Currently it lands like a stranger you were told about in chapter one.

### 8. The Septon walks on cold in c07

Halvard is described as a fixture Taylor has tracked for two months. He is on-page for the first time in c07. The Wenna Cobb name — the founding entry, structurally — drops in his dialogue with no prior on-page anchor. Halvard's argument is the strongest scene of the seven, but he should not be entering the sub-section as a stranger to the reader at chapter seven.

### 9. The contempt axis hasn't surfaced

Per the series trajectory the contempt-toward-elite axis fires at d05 (early in the book) and articulates at d09. By c07 some early contempt-onset would be expected. The cold-utilitarian register is dialed; the contempt is absent. May be by design (the trajectory targets d05 deeper in b01) — but worth flagging.

### 10. Dialogue is sparse, which compounds the apparatus problem

Across ~13.5K words: meaningful dialogue exchanges happen at three points (c03 Jarvis, c06 Wren one line, c07 Septon). Otherwise the prose is interior + observation. With the apparatus-register dominating that interior, the lack of voice-traffic shows.
