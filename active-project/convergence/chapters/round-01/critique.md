# Round 1 — CRITIC · chapter-outline convergence (fusion, not selection)

*Deconstructing gen-A (comedy/pacing · 30ch · 10/10/10), gen-B (emotional-arc · 25ch · 9/8/8),
gen-C (structure/causality · 30ch · 10/10/10) against the 7 chapter-level convergence criteria and
the FROZEN / T1–T5 / DN-1..6 placement requirements. The accepted spine is `round-03/fusion-v2.md`;
chapters MUST realize it and I do not relitigate it. This pass mirrors the parent loop: the critique
produces the fusion target a Round-2 pass writes up in full.*

**The 7 criteria (chapter level):** (1) every chapter has a clear job + advances the spine; (2) setups
precede payoffs, no orphans; (3) the per-book Lock climax lands and is set up; (4) the curdle ladder +
tone macro-descent hold; (5) comedy cadence sustains (T4); (6) the DNs + T-rulings each placed in a
named chapter; (7) chapter counts give each book a coherent ~27k shape.

## 1 · Per-outline deconstruction

### gen-A — COMEDY / PACING-RHYTHM (30ch · 10/10/10)

**Single strongest move:** the *register-refreshed-three-ways* thesis, made structural. A names the
exhaustion risk explicitly — a register-vs-stakes gap that escalates by bigger antics burns out by
Book II — and solves it per-book: Book I refreshes via *new instruments* (each chapter installs a tool;
the joke is the gap between what she thinks she builds and what she builds), Book II via *the
entourage's registers* (Harwin's no-eat list, Nymeria's anthropology, the banker's misread, Daenys's
flat puncture — II.6 is the proof-of-concept: a recovery chapter that stays funny on *zero* new
escalation), Book III via *gallows* (same register, inverted function). This is the only candidate that
treats "does the comedy survive 80k" as an engineering problem and answers it at the chapter grain.

**What works:** the explicit `SPIKE / RECOVER / HOLD` notation per chapter, mapped to a stated intra-book
rhythm (criterion 5, best-served here by a wide margin). The deliberate placement of recovery chapters
(II.6, II.7) *before* the Harwin wound (II.8) so the reader is warm when he dies. The two gallows-SPIKE
chapters (III.1, III.2) parked at the top of Book III to *prove the engine survives the tone-turn before*
the curdle-apex silences it. DN-6 calibration (¾ comedy / ¼ chill) is handled correctly — keep the
*monologue* bright, let only the reader do the math.

**What fails against the 7 criteria:**
- **Criterion 4 (tone macro-descent) — over-corrected into a hole.** A's own weakness #1: Book III is
  back-loaded with quiet — III.4–III.10 is HORROR×2 → GRIEF×2 → BREAK → COLD×2, only two register-up
  chapters then six near-silent ones. Six low-volume chapters risk the back third reading as a different
  book. This is the *inverse* of C's tone-monotonicity problem and just as real.
- **Criterion 1 / relationship spine (Saerys↔Daenys).** A's weakness #2 is load-bearing: A keeps Daenys
  *apart* to preserve the engine (Daenys-present = puncture = less farce), giving us "they recognize each
  other" (II.3) and then largely separate until she dies. This starves the relationship the blank line
  depends on — the single biggest cost of the comedy lens.
- **Criterion 7 (book shape).** A's weakness #4 concedes 10/book is tight for everything Book I must
  install; A floats splitting I.3/I.4 to push Book I to 11 but doesn't commit.

**Most dangerous flaw:** the comedy lens *under-emotionalizes the Daenys account* (B's core competency)
while *over-quieting Book III* — so the back third risks being simultaneously airless AND under-grieved.
A is the best engine and the weakest heart.

### gen-B — EMOTIONAL-ARC / RELATIONSHIP-CADENCE (25ch · 9/8/8)

**Single strongest move:** the **gift→spend ledger charted up-front** as the load-bearing spine —
"the story is not 'she builds an empire,' it is 'she opens five love accounts and closes four of them
by sailing away.'" Each gift is *given in a named warm chapter* before it is *spent in a named cold one*,
and the spends are *spaced to climb the curdle ladder rather than cluster*. This makes **casualty ⊆ gift
visible as a structure, not asserted in a checklist** — and it is the cleanest realization of that
FROZEN beat in any of the three.

**What works:**
- The **III.5/III.6 break split** — the absence (III.5, register simply *gone*, DN-1) given its own
  scene, and the *choice* to re-seal (III.6) given a separate beat to keep the meta-question live (T5).
  This is the correct structural reading of T1: the wound and the re-choice are two different jobs.
- The **cost-ledger made audible by giving the *balancing* its own chapter twice** — I.7 (ward-child,
  the armor's first seam) and II.7 (Harwin's closed account, witnessed by Daenys) — so the curdle never
  rides solely on Harwin's death (DN-2, best-served here).
- **Daenys's interior-want chapter (II.3, "A Hall of Her Own")** given on its own so the blank line costs
  a *person becoming someone*, not a slot (BLOCKER 5). The laundress's-boy beat (I.4) is the single
  load-bearing rootability anchor and is correctly isolated.
- The counter-thesis line is *planted as a setup that pays the meta-question* ("the dragon doesn't make
  me less going to die; it makes the dying worth watching" → III.4/III.7).

**What fails against the 7 criteria:**
- **Criterion 5 (comedy cadence) — the known cost.** B's weakness #1: Book III is almost wall-to-wall
  spend (III.1 father / III.3 horror apex / III.4 Helaena / III.5 Daenys), only III.1's gallows-gag and
  III.2's "insulting grade" carry the engine. B *flags* that A's lens must pressure-test whether the
  gallows-voice survives chapter-to-chapter — i.e., B knows it under-served T4 in Book III.
- **Criterion 7 (book shape) — 25 chapters is light.** B's weakness #3 concedes 9/8/8 = 25 ≠ ~30, and
  that the Yi Ti pair and the Lock III heist mechanics may need splitting to hit ~27k/book and give the
  A+D heist room to breathe. B prioritized emotional legibility over word-count shape and flagged it.
- **Criterion 2 (heist setup-payoff) — under-distributed.** B collapses the entire A+D heist into a
  single chapter (III.7, "A Document More Perfect"), which is structurally thin: the four mechanisms
  (laundered hull / marque-repossession / orphaned seal / re-tasked crew) are *asserted* rather than
  *pre-staged across Book III chapters* the way C does. This is the heist's room-to-breathe problem B
  itself named.
- **DN-5 under-served** (B's weakness #2): I.6 (first pest) with no straight-man present risks tipping
  too cold under B's chill bias.

**Most dangerous flaw:** **chapter starvation in the back half.** Compressing every emotional beat to a
clean single chapter is legible but leaves the *mechanism* (the heist, the Yi Ti reach) under-roomed and
Book III under-paced. B is the best heart and the thinnest machine.

### gen-C — STRUCTURE / CAUSAL SETUP-PAYOFF (30ch · 10/10/10)

**Single strongest move:** the **PLANT/FIRE setup→payoff table** + **building each book backward from
its Lock**. C's orphaned-King's-hand-seal arc is the spine's longest causal chain made fully legible:
`PLANT[NOTE] I.1 → license I.2 → sealed I.9 → FIRE[Lock I] I.10 → orphaned III.1 → FIRE[heist] III.10`
— three books, one prop, no orphan. The closing table proves *every PLANT resolves and every FIRE traces
to a named PLANT* (criterion 2, decisively best-served).

**What works:**
- **Lock placement + the explicit dress-rehearsal.** C engineers Lock II (II.10, "the-clerk") as the
  *literal* half-stakes rehearsal — a cargo, an Essosi clerk — with the kit *assembled the chapter before*
  (II.9), so Book III's finale invents nothing. This is the cleanest realization of the same-mechanism-
  rising-audit rhyme (criterion 3).
- **Heist pre-distribution.** Every Book III heist mechanism is *staged into being in its own earlier
  chapter*: orphaned seal III.1, laundered-hull manifest III.4, manufactured debt III.2→III.9, crew
  re-tasking on the II.9/II.10 toolkit — so III.10 is pure collection. The four-mechanism finale is
  *built*, not *asserted* (the direct fix to B's heist-compression flaw).
- **The blank line engineered from the balancing beat** — T1/III.8 staged as the *failure of the II.8
  balancing gesture* (the one account she cannot close), and the rhyme-checks per Lock (method / curdle
  rung / register ladder all confirmed per book).
- The **broken-clock template planted I.5** ("ingest real magic → real wrong effect") that the Book III
  black-stone reach fires against — a structural guard the other two leave implicit.

**What fails against the 7 criteria:**
- **Criterion 5 (comedy cadence) — explicitly deferred.** C's weakness #2: the curdle FIRE-chain
  (R0→R2→R3→R4) is so legible the descent risks reading as "a staircase you can see the top and bottom
  of from chapter 1." C treats spike/recover beats as *structural relief valves, not comedy with its own
  momentum* — and concedes "chapters can each 'have a laugh' on schedule and still not be funny." C
  guarantees curdle *position*, not *aliveness*, and defers register-rhythm to A.
- **Criterion 1 (people, not dominos)** — C's weakness #1, the central risk: engineering every casualty
  as a pre-planted gift firing on schedule makes the deaths read as *clockwork rather than tragedy*
  (Harwin "had to" die in II.8 because II.7 charged it; Daenys "had to" die because II.3 planted her
  want). The table proves causality is sound; it does *not* prove the deaths feel inevitable rather than
  scheduled. C explicitly owes this mitigation to B.
- **Criterion 1 (Book I as a setup lobby)** — C's weakness #3: six of Book I's ten chapters exist
  primarily to *plant* (I.1/I.2/I.4/I.8/I.9), risking Book I reading as an elaborate setup lobby rather
  than a book with its own present-tense spine.

**Most dangerous flaw:** **tone-monotonicity / character-as-domino** — the structure is so legible the
reader can feel the gears, both in the predictable descent and in deaths that arrive on cue. C is the
best machine and the least *alive*.

## 2 · Cross-candidate synthesis

The three lenses are almost perfectly complementary — each is strongest exactly where the others are
weakest. A=engine, B=heart, C=machine. Fusion is not selection; it is **C's skeleton, B's account
ledger, A's register rhythm.**

### KEEP (attributed)

- **C's PLANT/FIRE setup→payoff table + backward-from-the-Lock construction** — the spine of the
  converged skeleton. The orphaned-seal long arc, the per-Lock rhyme-checks, and the no-orphans guarantee
  are the structural baseline. *(criterion 2, 3)*
- **C's heist pre-distribution across Book III** — orphaned seal III.1, laundered-hull manifest staged
  before the finale, manufactured debt staged III.2→finalized late, crew re-tasking on the rehearsal
  toolkit. The finale invents nothing. *(criterion 2, 3)*
- **C's explicit Lock II dress-rehearsal** (cargo/clerk at half-stakes, kit assembled the chapter prior)
  and **the broken-clock template plant (I.5 → fires at the black-stone reach).** *(criterion 3)*
- **B's gift→spend ledger chart** as the relationship-cadence overlay on C's skeleton — each gift given
  in a named warm chapter, spent in a named cold one, spends spaced up the curdle ladder. This is the
  *answer to C's character-as-domino flaw*: the same casualty schedule, but every gift gets a *warm
  present-tense scene first* so the spend reads as loss, not as the plan firing a planted gun. *(crit 1, 4)*
- **B's I.7/II.7 double-balancing-beat** (DN-2 audible cost-ledger given its own chapter *twice*) and
  **B's III.5/III.6 break-split** (the absence and the re-seal as two scenes — DN-1 + T1 + T5). *(crit 6)*
- **B's Daenys interior-want chapter** (II.3, BLOCKER 5) and **the isolated laundress's-boy anchor**
  (BLOCKER 3, DN-3). *(criterion 6)*
- **A's register-refresh-three-ways** (instruments→entourage→gallows) as the *aliveness layer* over
  C's curdle positions — the direct fix to C's "funny on schedule but not funny" deferral. *(criterion 5)*
- **A's SPIKE/RECOVER/HOLD pacing marks per chapter** and **A's two-gallows-SPIKE-at-the-top-of-Book-III**
  move (III.1, III.2 prove the engine survives the tone-turn) — the fix to B's airless Book III. *(crit 5)*
- **A's "recovery chapters placed *before* the wound" discipline** (warm before Harwin dies). *(crit 4,5)*

### CUT

- **A's strict 10/10/10 with Daenys held apart** — the engine-preservation instinct that starves the
  central love account. Replaced by B's relationship cadence.
- **B's 25-chapter compression** — too light for ~27k/book and starves the heist of room. (See COUNT below.)
- **B's single-chapter heist (III.7)** — replaced by C's pre-distributed four-mechanism build.
- **C's treatment of spike/recover as mere "relief valves"** — replaced by A's engine-with-momentum.
- **C's Book I as a six-chapter plant-lobby** — softened by importing B's I.4 warmth-anchor placement and
  A's "each chapter is a *present-tense* comic set-piece that *also* plants" framing. Keep all the C
  plants; reframe their chapters so they have standalone life (A/B's mitigation, owed to C by C's own #3).

### CHAPTER-COUNT RULING

**30 chapters, split 10 / 10 / 10.** Rationale:
- The ledger's own target is "~9–11 chapters/book … ~30 total" — 30 is the center, not the ceiling.
- B's 25 is the *only* outlier and B itself flags it as a deliberate trade it expects C to overrule
  ("C's structural lens may need to split … I prioritized emotional legibility over the word-count shape").
- A and C *independently converged on 10/10/10* — two of three lenses, from opposite priorities, landed
  on the same shape. That is strong signal.
- 10/book is the only count that gives **both** B's emotional beats their own chapters **and** C's heist
  its pre-distributed room. B's 25 was light *because* it merged the heist into one chapter and merged
  give/spend beats; restoring C's distribution requires the extra 5.
- The one tension (A/C's "10 is tight for Book I's installs") is resolved *not* by going to 11 but by the
  fusion reassigning load: the converged Book I keeps 10 by letting the warmth/pest chapters (I.3-warmth,
  I.6-pest) double as standalone scenes rather than splitting the plant chapters (kills C's lobby problem
  in the same move). Books II and III at 10 each give the entourage cadence (B) and the heist
  distribution (C) full room. **10/10/10 = 30.**

### FIX (what no single outline solved)

1. **The "domino vs warmth" reconciliation** — neither A (apart) nor C (scheduled) solved it; only the
   *fusion* does, by laying B's warm-present-tense gift scenes over C's causal schedule. Each death keeps
   its planted cause AND gets a warm scene first. This must be a stated drafting directive, not just a
   chapter list.
2. **Book III pacing under tragedy** — A back-loads quiet, B goes airless, C uses relief valves. The fix
   is A's *front-loaded* gallows pair (III.1–III.2) + **one gallows beat braided into the late spend
   stretch** (both A's weakness #1 and B's weakness #1 ask for exactly this) so the engine's pulse stays
   detectable right up to the break.
3. **The Saerys↔Daenys connective tissue** — A flags it starved; the fusion must add at least one *warm,
   together* Daenys beat between the meet (II.2/II.3) and the want-chapter, so the relationship is lived,
   not just recognized-then-killed. B's II.2+II.3 give two; the fusion keeps both and the gallows-warmth
   of III.3 (A) as the third live current.
4. **Betrothal-threat orphan** — A's weakness #6: planted (II.1/II.3 "blade over the love") but never
   cashed; A let it dissolve into the war. **Ruling: explicitly retire it on-page** — Viserys's death
   (III.1) removes the signer who could have forced the match, so the war *visibly* makes the marriage
   moot. One line in III.1 converts an orphan into a confirmed retirement. (See Placement Audit.)
5. **Crew re-tasking plant** — C's weakness #4: the one heist mechanism not planted a full chapter early.
   **Ruling: take C's own offered fix** — a one-line plant in the laundered-hull chapter (re-task a real
   crew for the plague-run) so all four mechanisms are pre-staged, not just three.

## 3 · TENSIONS + rulings

**T-α · Comedy-cadence (A) vs C's tone-monotonicity staircase.** A wants the engine alive per chapter;
C's legible R0→R4 descent risks a predictable staircase. **RULING: A wins on *register*, C wins on
*position*.** The curdle *rung* of every chapter is fixed by C's ladder (non-negotiable — it is the
FROZEN one-gesture-four-scales structure). The *register* of every chapter is A's to set. They are
orthogonal: C says *which rung*, A says *how alive*. The fusion carries C's rung tags AND A's
SPIKE/RECOVER/HOLD marks on every chapter. The staircase stops being visible-from-chapter-1 because A's
refresh-three-ways keeps the *texture* unpredictable even as the *rung* climbs predictably.

**T-β · Character-as-domino (C) vs relationship warmth (B).** C's schedule makes deaths feel clockwork;
B's give-before-spend makes them feel like loss. **RULING: B's cadence governs, C's causality survives
inside it.** Keep every C plant (the gun is loaded on schedule) but require B's warm present-tense scene
for each gift *before* its spend. The death is both causally inevitable (C) and emotionally surprising
in its weight (B). This is the single most important fusion move (see Verdict).

**T-γ · The Book III gallows-quiet stretch vs sustaining the engine.** A bets six quiet chapters land
harder; B goes airless and flags it; C uses relief valves. **RULING: keep two full gallows-SPIKE chapters
at the top (A's III.1–III.2), let the curdle-apex (III.4–III.5) silence the engine for the first sustained
stretch, braid ONE gallows beat into the late spend run (III.6–III.7 region) to keep the pulse detectable,
then go truly silent at the break (III.8) and COLD at the close (III.9–III.10).** This honors A's bet (the
silence is powerful) while answering A's *own* worry (six-in-a-row reads as a different book) with the
single braided beat both A and B requested. The break's silence stays the loudest beat precisely because
the engine was audible right up to it.

**T-δ · 10/book "tight for installs" (A/C) vs Book-I-as-lobby (C).** Both pull toward more Book I rooming,
but in opposite directions (A: split for cadence; C: the plants crowd out drama). **RULING: neither split
nor pad — *dual-purpose* the chapters.** Each Book I chapter is a present-tense comic/warm set-piece that
*also* plants. I.3 (laundress's boy) and I.6 (the pest) are already standalone-strong; the fusion writes
I.1/I.2/I.4/I.8/I.9 as *scenes first, plants second* (A's instinct without the split). 10 holds.

**T-ε · DN-5 under-straight-man (B's chill) vs DN-6 ¾-comedy calibration.** B risks the first pest tipping
cold; A nails the ¾/¼. **RULING: A's calibration governs the first-pest chapter** (monologue bright, reader
does the math) and **C's staging note governs DN-5** (where no straight-man is present, the prose shows the
flux plainly even as she narrates qi). Both are honored without conflict.
