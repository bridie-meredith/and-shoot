## 5 · Residual-risk ruling

The draft honestly self-flagged six risks. Ruling each as CONVERGENCE BLOCKER / DRAFTING NOTE / NON-ISSUE:

1. **III.5–III.8 four near-consecutive low-volume chapters (III.7 airless risk).** **DRAFTING NOTE.** The
   architecture already mitigates it correctly per T-γ: front-loaded gallows trio (III.1–III.3), the III.6
   braid, the III.8 loud silence. III.7 being the trough before the break is a *defensible structural
   choice* — a threadbare grief chapter between a braided-gallows chapter and the absent-register break is
   not an architecture defect; it is the descent doing its job. Whether III.7 reads airless on the page is
   a register/prose calibration (candidate: a second faint gallows pulse, or confirm the threadbare
   register is load-bearing). Carry as a drafting note to the bones stage; do not re-architect. NOT a blocker.

2. **III.6 triple-duty density.** **DRAFTING NOTE.** Density of function in one chapter (want-pays /
   cruel-adjacency-arms / ledger-line-plants / braided-gallows) is real but it is a prose-execution risk
   (the braided gallows reading bolted-on), not a structural orphan or missing job. The functions are all
   correctly placed; the watch is on the tonal seam in the writing. Bones/facets pass holds the seam. NOT a
   blocker.

3. **Gift→spend cadence legible as a chart.** **DRAFTING NOTE.** This is the T-α legibility risk applied to
   the relationship ledger, and the draft's own defense is correct: A's register-refresh keeps each spend's
   texture distinct (R3 cold relief / R4 horror / ABSENT silence). The fix lives in prose register, not
   outline structure — the draft says so explicitly. The architecture is sound; aliveness is checked at
   bones/stitch, not here. NOT a blocker.

4. **Viserys's I.9 warm-gift is the weakest of the four pairs.** **DRAFTING NOTE.** T-β is technically
   satisfied (a doting father gives out of love before the spend). The thinner warmth is a calibration
   matter — bank one explicitly tender father-beat in I.1/I.2 if the spend needs to land harder. This is a
   prose enrichment within an existing chapter, not a chapter-architecture change. NOT a blocker.

5. **POV strain at II.3 and III.6 (Daenys's interior want under locked-inside-Saerys).** **DRAFTING NOTE.**
   The draft resolves it at the architecture level — Daenys's interiority lives in what Saerys *observes and
   fails to understand* (the baffling alliance, the separate fortune), not in Daenys's head. The locked POV
   is preserved structurally; holding the line is a bones/prose discipline. NOT a blocker.

6. **The braced FROZEN touch (grind-AND-swallow).** **NON-ISSUE.** I.1 on the page does the swallow as the
   same gesture one inch further into the body (grinds the relic, eats the powder, files the prayer as a
   funeral), not a new beat. Confirmed against the FROZEN inciting-grind-AND-swallow requirement. Nothing to
   carry.

**No residual risk rises to a convergence blocker. All are prose/register calibration, deferred to the
bones stage with the notes recorded above.**
