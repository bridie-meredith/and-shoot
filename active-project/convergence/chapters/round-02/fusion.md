# Round 2 — CHAPTER FUSION (the converged outline, written in full)

*One continuous 30-chapter (10/10/10) outline realizing `round-01/critique.md` §5 and its rulings.
This is fusion, not selection: **C's PLANT/FIRE skeleton** carries the spine, **B's gift→spend cadence**
is laid over it (T-β — every death keeps its planted cause AND gets a warm present-tense scene in a
named chapter BEFORE its spend), and **A's register-refresh-three-ways + SPIKE/RECOVER/HOLD/GALLOWS
marks** ride every chapter so the descent's texture stays unpredictable even as its rung climbs
predictably (T-α). Curdle rung = C's position (FROZEN ladder, non-negotiable); register = A's to set;
warmth = B's. POV is locked-inside Saerys throughout. The spine, FROZEN list, T1–T5, the Cauldron-Belly,
the poison-path, and mithridatism are realized verbatim from `round-03/fusion-v2.md` — not relitigated.*

**Curdle ladder (the one gesture, dead-thing→fuel, identical-shot, rising scale):**
`R0 cradle-egg (laugh)` → `R1 ward-child filed as impure reagents (laugh less)` → `R2 the pest quietly
poisoned (stop laughing)` → `R3 Ser Harwin balanced in the book (wince)` → `R4 field of dead dragons,
refiner's calm (horror)`.

**Register marks (A):** `SPIKE` comic peak · `RECOVER` deliberate warmth/quiet · `HOLD` steady register
at steady stakes · `GALLOWS` cultivation-register over horror (same sentence, opposite effect) ·
`ABSENT` the one break, register simply gone.

**Engine refreshed three ways (A), so the gap never repeats its own trick:** Book I via *new instruments*
(each chapter installs a tool; the joke is the gap between what she thinks she builds and what she builds);
Book II via *the entourage's registers* (Harwin's no-eat list, Nymeria's anthropology, the banker's
misread, Daenys's flat puncture); Book III via *gallows* (same register, inverted function).

## BOOK I — *The Inferior Path*

feral baby eating God off a spoon.
**Register:** `SPIKE` — full farce, register-vs-stakes gap at maximum (DN-1 *armed*: the register is
loudest here so its later absence reads).
**Rung:** R0 (the foundational laugh).

### I.2 · the-charitable-princess `[KEYSTONE]`
**Movement:** M2 — the sick-house keystone; poison-path opens; ledger born.
**Beat:** She founds a charity apothecary — piety none can attack without looking wicked — and apprentices
the maester for lab access, conquering his disapproval by quietly fixing his books until he can't function

only she can read is born. Reverse-trope: where the realm expects needlework or a filched sword, she raids
the trivially-easy still-room for nightshade, hemlock, monkshood, a stoppered jar of Lysene tears, and
begins eating micro-doses "to temper the cauldron against every poison before refining the heaven-tier
ones" — sick for a week the first time, files it as "the cauldron repelling its first tribulation," keeps
going.
**Register:** `HOLD → SPIKE` (the maester-conquest gag).
**Rung:** R0.
**SETUP:** PLANT[LEDGER]; PLANT[FACTOR] (first bill of exchange to a Braavosi drug-agent — medicine and

### I.3 · the-laundress's-boy
**Movement:** M2 — the rootability anchor (the single load-bearing warmth beat; isolated, B's placement).
**Beat:** A laundress's boy is dying of a wet cough the maester has written off; he is neither pious cover
nor reagent — the system has no line for him. Saerys sits up three sleepless nights running clean broth and
a poultice she made herself, *certain* it is karmic hygiene ("the dao punishes the cold-hearted; no
disciple ascends across the corpses of those who asked her for help"). The reader sees the true thing under
the cope: she got off the path, at real cost, for one small person who could give her nothing. He lives.
She files it as a cultivation merit.
**Register:** `RECOVER` — the book's deepest breath, lowest-volume scene.
**Rung:** off-ladder (the warm floor the horror is later measured against).
**SETUP:** PLANT[WARM] (the reader's 80k root — keeps Daenys from out-charactering the lead); PLANT[HINGE]
(the poison hand and the healing hand established as the *same hand*, side by side, so the curdle has its
pivot — the gut that cures the boy is the gut she is teaching to kill).
**PAYOFF:** the DOSE-LOG gut (I.2) re-cast as a healing instrument. **DN/T:** DN-3 (the named
laundress's-boy beat, placed).

### I.4 · tempering-the-cauldron

**Register:** `SPIKE` (the reverse-trope is the comic high of early Book I).
**Rung:** R0 sustained, broadening.
**SETUP:** PLANT[NO-EAT] (Harwin's future no-eat list is pre-justified here — the behavior the list will

### I.6 · the-impurity-expelled `[DN-6 FIRST-PEST]`

## BOOK II — *The Long Roads*

### II.3 · a-hall-of-her-own
**Movement:** M1 — Daenys's interior want; the warm-together beat (the connective tissue A flagged missing).
**Beat:** **Daenys Velaryon** — base-born Velaryon who *claimed a wild dragon off Dragonmont nobody
expected her to*, the inferior path *flown* — is warm, blunt, unafraid of the rock-eating-and-swallowing
princess, and sees through the act faster than anyone, because she also remade a whole life on a lie she
had to make true by nerve. They recognize each other. And she has a want of her own: she courts an alliance
Saerys finds baffling, banks her own small fortune *separately* ("I will not be anyone's asset"), and eyes
a future war as the one arena where a proven war-rider can finally *demand* legitimization — to found a
Velaryon **cadet branch in her own name**, the mortal mirror of Saerys's immortality (both bids against
being forgotten). Saerys cannot parse why anyone would build *forward into descendants* when one could
simply refuse to die — and that friction is the lived warmth between them.
**Register:** `RECOVER → HOLD` — the warmest live current of the book; character-driven; the counter-thesis
given teeth.
**Rung:** R0.
**SETUP:** PLANT[DAENYS-WANT] (the separate fortune + alliance + war-as-leverage — BLOCKER 5; this is what
makes the blank line cost a *person becoming someone*, not a slot); the counter-thesis line — *"the dragon
doesn't make me less going to die; it makes the dying worth watching"* (pays the meta-question).
**PAYOFF:** Daenys's entrance (II.2) deepened into a lived bond. **DN/T:** DN-3 (Saerys-warmth, the
connective beat); BLOCKER 5. *(POV note: Daenys's interiority lives in what Saerys observes and fails to

## Residual risks (honest — to seed the re-validation critic)

1. **III.5–III.8 still carries four near-consecutive low-volume chapters** (HORROR → GRIEF+gallows-pulse →
   GRIEF → ABSENT). The T-γ braid (one gallows pulse in III.6) and the front-loaded gallows pair (III.1–III.3)
   are the mitigation, but a critic may judge the III.7 grief chapter as the airless one — the only chapter
   in the late stretch with neither a gallows beat nor the break's loud silence. Candidate: braid a second
   faint gallows pulse into III.7, or confirm III.7's threadbare register is load-bearing as the trough
   before the break.

2. **III.6 does triple duty** (Daenys's want pays / cruel-adjacency arms / ledger-line plants / braided
   gallows), echoing C's I.8 density flag. If it cracks, the braided-gallows beat is the first thing to read
   as bolted-on rather than felt. Watch the warmth-vs-gallows tonal seam inside the single chapter.

3. **The gift→spend cadence is now legible *as a chart*** — the same legibility risk T-α flagged for the
   curdle staircase. The defense is that A's register-refresh keeps each spend's *texture* distinct (R3 cold
   relief / R4 horror / ABSENT silence), but a critic reading the gift→spend table cold may still feel the
   accounts being closed on a schedule. The fix lives in prose register, not outline structure; flagged so
   the re-validation pass checks aliveness, not just position.

4. **Viserys's "warm gift chapter" (I.9) is the weakest of the four pairs** — it is the cage hardening into
   a tool, warmth already half-converted, not a clean warm beat like II.2 or II.3. T-β is technically
   satisfied (the doting father gives out of love before the III.1 spend), but the warmth is thinner than
   Harwin's or Daenys's. If a critic wants the father's spend to land harder, I.1/I.2's indulgence may need
   one more explicitly tender father-beat banked earlier.

5. **POV strain at II.3 and III.6** (Daenys's interior want under locked-inside-Saerys). Resolved here by
   keeping Daenys's interiority in what Saerys *observes and fails to understand* — but a bones/prose pass
   must hold that line; any drift into Daenys's head breaks the locked POV the blank line depends on.

6. **The braced FROZEN touch** (grind-AND-swallow upgrade) is inherited from fusion-v2 and not relitigated
   here, but a chapter-level critic re-reading I.1 should confirm the swallow reads as the same gesture one
   inch further into the body, not a new beat — the one place the integration touches a FROZEN item.
