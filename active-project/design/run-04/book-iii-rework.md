| Front | What it is | Gael's misread |

## III.1 · the commission — `[SHIP · CLOCK · OTTO]`
1. **(SHIP)** She orders the hull — designed from her room off the **reagent map**, commissioned through three
   layers of paper, paid in off-books dice-coin. The shipwright quotes *years.*
2. **(CLOCK)** Otto's II.7 vise has set the wedding early and firm; the Starks send word and a date forms. She
   runs years-of-build against months-to-bride — the math is murder.
3. **(OTTO)** His II.8 manhunt is *live* (gold cloaks + his own watchers turning over the disappearance), so every
   coin she moves and door she opens happens **under an active investigation** — which she misreads gloriously:
   an orthodox sect's inquisitor has come to purge the demonic Quiet Coin, and she resolves, gravely, to *help him.*
- **Comedy/frame:** the long con as a procurement project (④); the agent incurious about whose coin (because-Westeros — nobody imagines a child). **Cultivation:** the ship filed as the only domain no sect can reach. **Awfulness:** she coolly builds an exit while the investigation churns up the bodies she left.

## III.2 · the excursions — `[SHIP · GANG · FACTOR]`
1. **(SHIP)** The build pulls her citywide — shipyard, harbor, timber-yards — and the night-roof traffic births the
   **"ghost of the Red Keep"**; Wylla covers ("the princess is at her meditations and must not be disturbed").
2. **(GANG)** Sensing the Quiet Coin distracted and overextended, rival houses test the empire she can no longer fully
   mind — a smashed den, a leaned-on factor; she answers a few, fast and quiet, but can't be everywhere.
3. **(FACTOR)** The ship-money has swollen past safe trust; one of her own pit-factors starts skimming and dreaming
   bigger — the first chafe (Quint, seeded).
- **Comedy/frame:** the meditation alibi (⑤); the haunting comically *sharpens* the watch (a commended captain). **Cultivation:** body-tempering hard in the stolen hours (the I.4/I.5 gains compounding). **Awfulness:** every outing bets Wylla's neck; the smallfolk operators hold the exposure.

## III.3 · the greedy factor — `[FACTOR · GANG · OTTO]` › **R3**
1. **(FACTOR)** **Lothar Quint**, grown greedy, can't produce the phantom buyer behind the ship-money and **leans on the
   maid**; Gael removes him — paper, the maester's supply chain, the still-room — **colder than R2,** his death-flag
   cashing (⑦ *"I'm only doing this for my family"* → *"the account is closed."*) › **R3.**
2. **(GANG)** Quint's grab had cracked the house open, and a rival crew pounces on the wobble — internal rot and external
   threat fuse; she puts two fires out at once, and the violence leaves a louder trail than usual.
3. **(OTTO)** That trail is the lead his manhunt needed — a "natural" death + a gang-flare circling the same maid he'd
   already flagged in the bestiary; he draws the line toward Wylla and the Quiet Coin and tightens. **Her handling feeds the hunter.**
- **Comedy/frame:** Quint "offboarded" (④); ① her reputation does the intimidating. **Cultivation:** the gluttony/devourer-path shape surfacing (obstacles removed as casually as reagents eaten). **Awfulness:** Quint's dependents ruined behind him — collateral, none on her ledger; clean-hands doctrine holds (poison ≠ the demonic path).

## III.4 · the deadline — `[CLOCK · OTTO · SHIP]`
1. **(CLOCK)** The wedding accelerates hard — **Jaehaerys** is packing her north; she burns her last delay-capital playing
   the **sickly waif** too delicate to travel (the babying weaponized one final time).
2. **(OTTO)** Having drawn his line in III.3, he closes — surveillance peaks, melodramatically certain; he maneuvers to
   **expose her before the wedding carries her out of reach.**
3. **(SHIP)** The hull is *behind;* the two countdowns cross. She must accelerate the build while stalling the wedding —
   both at once, both maxed.
- **Comedy/frame:** the realm wrote her cover (an unkillable superhuman feigns delicacy and they *believe* it because the legend says the scholar-princess was "always delicate"); ⑤/②. **Cultivation:** she clocks that **the Targaryens aren't strong but for their dragons** — to her cultivator's eye the court that cages her is a room of soft mortals, which steels the decision to simply leave. **Awfulness:** a child stalls her own forced marriage by performing illness, and the *only* reason it works is the realm's contempt for her.

## III.5 · the tempering — `[the GIVE · SHIP · OTTO/CLOCK = why-now]` › **the anti-curdle break**
1. **(the GIVE)** The one outward spend — she runs her **own qi into Wylla** to make her sea-durable, deadpan as logistics
   ("an untampered mortal is a liability at sea"). It **costs her real cultivation — she chose to be weaker** for one person.
   GUARD-1's third and final spend.
2. **(SHIP)** Framed as voyage-prep — crew and maid readied for open water; the ship now days from ready.
3. **(why-now: OTTO/CLOCK)** The wedding is imminent and Otto is at the threshold — now-or-never; the tempering happens
   under the gun, not as a calm gift.
- **Comedy/frame:** "a tonic for the voyage" (③④); Wylla's awe and brief fear of what her princess *is* (warmth pouring in, the body changing), then she never asks (some doors you don't open). **Three-frame:** Gael sincere ("investing in the asset") / the reader sees love / Wylla feels the weight. **Cultivation:** the genre-logic — she "clears Wylla's death-flag" by tempering her, the rationalization for the spend she'd never otherwise make. **Irony:** the gift that "saves" Wylla is exactly what makes her able to kill at III.6.

## III.6 · the narrow escape — `[OTTO · CLOCK · GANG/FACTOR loose end]` + **the LOCKED absent-arrival** › **the apex**
1. **(CLOCK × SHIP)** The wedding party arrives to collect the bride exactly as the hull comes ready — collection and launch
   collide at the water.
2. **(OTTO)** His one correct deduction puts his man at the dock to intercept; he wins every move and loses the game.
3. **(OTTO × GANG/FACTOR loose end)** The man at the waterline is **Otto's** — but he is there because a loose end of the
   underworld she built (a rival she once spared / a removed factor's vengeful associate) **sold the Quiet Coin's movements
   into the investigation.** The front she could never fully close becomes Otto's eyes; his instrument is who reaches the dock.
   **The tempered maid kills the interceptor by her own hand** (the apex; Gael's gift made her able). Otto wins every move and
   loses the game. They sail.

> **LOCKED BEAT — the absent-arrival (the one crack).** On the deck, the continent sliding back, Gael reaches for the
> cultivation-gloss to *file* leaving her mother — the line that should arrive (*"Mother: an attachment severed; the dao
> requires —"*) and **it doesn't come.** A blank where the register should be. She doesn't name it; she looks at the water;
> she goes on. The only sign she will miss anyone, and she won't let herself know it. (Wylla, beside her, sees her mistress
> go quiet and doesn't ask.) *A pulled-forward micro-version of the Dance-book blank-line device, per principal lock 2026-06-13.*

- **Comedy/frame:** the realm's incoherent post-mortem — survivors disagree; the final theory is a foreign prince, a god, a conspiracy, *anything* but "she built a boat and left," because a princess with **agency** is the one thing it can't imagine (sexism, the last cheat code). Far at **Dragonstone, Alicent** gets the terminal "what the hell" — her daughter simply *gone* — and never learns she was the shield. **Cultivation:** gluttony-path apex shape; the kill files under her oldest maxim — *waste nothing; everything has a use.*

## Epilogue
Two girls on a ship, the continent behind them, the warm south ahead. Both desires satisfied; one of them now a killer;
the **absent-arrival** the last note we hold. Door left open for the parked Dance book (her longevity intact).
