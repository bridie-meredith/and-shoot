### Audience review (attempt 1)

**dark-fantasy-reader:**
ACCEPT with one flag.
- Scene C lands correctly — the maester is an assessment machine, not a mentor figure; the yes-yes lean is the only warmth and it is unsettling. The notebook going into the satchel without Taylor knowing what was written is exactly right.
- Scene D: the Bracken move is clean and I want it slightly harder. His man arriving and being refused by the maester is good institutional texture. But Bracken's counter-claim entering the record is happening off-page (through a functionary). I want to see the document enter the world — a messenger carrying the sealed claim, someone reading it, a hand signing it into the ledger. The board has to visibly close, not just be reported as closed.
- Scene E: the letter read aloud in company at the castellan's hall is the right shape for the irreversible close. Ensure the reading is done in front of named characters or at least in a room with witnesses — not a private reading.
- Flag: Scene F's closing is currently passive — Taylor receives word. The scene needs Taylor doing something with that word, even if the something is only registering the fauna sense around the hall and reading the shape of what she cannot see inside. She needs to be active in the close, not just informed.

**pulp-enthusiast:**
REJECT.
- Scene B is filler. Nine bullets of two people in a room at night with no active threat and no board change. The motherhouse letter is already established as futile (we know the road math from e04). Watching Rowan write it does not change the board. Cut this to 2 bullets max or skip it entirely.
- Scene A is fine at 5 bullets but the "jump forward to morning" rationale needs to be in the plan header, not implied.
- Scene D has complication density but it's spread too thin. The Bracken man + maester refusal + counter-claim filing is three beats that should hit consecutively without breathing room. Currently there are beats between them where nothing escalates.
- Scene E is the payoff and it's correct. The letter read in company is exactly the irreversible close I want. Do not delay getting there.
- The closing (Scene F) works IF Taylor is doing something — receiving word is passive, which is a flat landing after a strong Scene E. Give her a move, even a failed one.

**worm-canon-pedant:**
ACCEPT with flag.
- Maester assessment reads correctly as detached documentation, not recognition of Taylor as exceptional. The "notation of interest" framing in the Citadel report is exactly right — clinical, not warm. The yes-yes reflex firing without verbal acknowledgment is canonically correct for this character.
- Taylor's management of apparent literacy speed during the assessment is correctly threaded — she slows her reading, she allows herself to be seen hesitating, she gives the maester slightly less than full performance. This is Taylor's cognitive-age-gap management in practice.
- Flag: the wardship document Taylor is reading in Scene B — the plan currently has her reading it at full adult speed in private, which is fine (no one is watching), but the plan bullet should not SAY she reads it at adult speed. Keep the bullet action-only: "taylor reads the castellan wardship instrument." Interior processing belongs to the impersonator, not the plan.
- Flag: Bracken's motivation for suppressing the maester's findings is not specified in the bullets. This is CORRECT — motivation must not appear in bullets. But the plan log should note that Bracken's reasons (whatever they are — his own plan for Taylor's use, a prior deal with Plumm that the notation disrupts, a grudge against the Citadel's placement network) are for the impersonator to embody, not for the bullet to name. This flag is a confirmation, not a correction.
- Ward-of-administration language: confirm Scene D and E use "ward of the castellan's administration" not "Bracken's ward" or "Plumm's ward." The contest is between two claims over an administrative ward, not a personal ward.
