# Episode Plan — s01e01
## Title: The Ledger Opens
## Chunk: The impressment census officer arrives at the sept and logs Taylor as an unattached ward with no blood claim and no sworn guardian capable of asserting one — the septon is too ill to appear, and his written attestation is refused as insufficient. Taylor is placed on a provisional labor-eligibility list pending the traveling maester's cognitive assessment, and the window for being overlooked closes the moment her name enters the census ledger.

## Script

[Scene 1 — Sept outbuilding, sickroom]
1. STUDIO: The sickroom is a small stone cell off the sept's main hall — one shuttered window, two tallow candles burning low, a pallet where Septon Aldric lies. His breathing is audible. The morning light leaks around the shutter's edges.
2. taylor-hebert-westeros: sets a clay cup of water on the floor beside the pallet
3. septon-dying-protector: turns his head toward Taylor and says the census rider came through Millfall yesterday
4. taylor-hebert-westeros: goes still
5. septon-dying-protector: lifts the folded letter from beneath the coverlet and holds it toward Taylor — his written attestation of guardianship, sealed
6. septon-dying-protector: says the letter is what he can give her and he does not know if it will be enough

[Scene 2 — Sept common yard]
7. STUDIO: The yard outside the sept's main entrance — packed earth, a well, three smallfolk standing in the shade of the wall. A cart track leads to the road. Edric Cray stands near the well. Mira Stonefield stands at the wall's corner with a clear view of the road approach. Rymer's horse is tied at the far post, its rider absent from the yard.
8. edric-cray: watches the road without watching it
9. mira-stonefield: says the census officer does not accept letters from men who cannot stand in front of him
10. taylor-hebert-westeros: holds the sealed letter at her side

[Scene 3 — Sept entrance, census]
11. STUDIO: A census officer in Hightower-administrative livery arrives at the sept gate on horseback with a clerk and a ledger. He dismounts. The clerk opens the ledger on a portable writing board. The officer's manner is efficient, not hostile. His efficiency is the threat.
12. taylor-hebert-westeros: steps forward and presents the sealed letter
13. census-officer: takes the letter, reads it, folds it shut, and returns it — says a written attestation without the guardian present is not a recognized assertion under current impressment rules
14. taylor-hebert-westeros: says the septon is ill and cannot leave his bed
15. census-officer: says that changes nothing and asks her name
16. taylor-hebert-westeros: gives her name
17. census-officer: dictates to the clerk — one unattached female ward, no blood claim, no sworn guardian present — provisional labor-eligibility, cognitive assessment pending, traveling maester's circuit

[Scene 4 — Sept gate, ledger closed]
18. STUDIO: The clerk writes the final entry, blots it, and closes the ledger. The officer mounts. The cart track is empty of dust; nothing has changed visibly. The yard is still. The letter is in Taylor's hand.
