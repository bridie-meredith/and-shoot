# Episode Plan

chunk: Three simultaneous arrivals, close-cut. Jack, three months in, makes his first mistake — a Reach lordling notices that the traveling merchant seems to know things he shouldn't. Dennis, day one, lands in a Stormlands market town, uses his power publicly, and starts the time-witch rumor. The Crawler-dragon is seen from a ship in the Narrow Sea and the sighting is reported to King's Landing.
theme: The world starts keeping files on things it does not have words for.
actors: [jack-slash, dennis-stansfield, crawler]
change: Jack has a lord's steward investigating him. Dennis has seeded the first public rumor about a time-witch. Crawler's existence is in writing, bound for King's Landing.
constraints:
  - Jack does not reveal what he is without extreme dramatic justification
  - Dennis/Clockblocker's humor armor always running; silence is the signal
  - Crawler has no human language at series open
  - No cape knows the others exist at series start
  - Shard-host lock absolute: no new bonding, no transfer
  - Powers work with environmental friction; shard adapts expression

## Script

### Scene 1: Jack

1. STUDIO: Great hall, minor Reach lordship — tallow candles lit, late afternoon light angled through arrow-slits, two hounds under the trestle table, Lord Edwyn Fossoway-minor and his steward at the far end, two men-at-arms flanking the door
2. jack-slash: sets his merchant's satchel on the table and names the road he traveled from, with the bridge toll he paid at Tumbleton
3. STUDIO: the steward's quill sits idle; Lord Edwyn's left hand settles flat on the table
4. jack-slash: lists the goods he carries — spice-wax, a bolt of undyed wool, one jar of Arbor pickled fish — and names a price per item, unhurried
5. Lord Edwyn: asks what other roads the merchant has traveled this season, casual, picking at a candle drip
6. jack-slash: names the road south through Cider Hall and the one through Longtable, then mentions the bad stretch past the mill near Aldric's ferry crossing
7. STUDIO: the steward's quill touches the page and does not move; Lord Edwyn's eyes stay on the candles
8. Lord Edwyn: asks whether the ferry crossing is commonly called by a man's name, voice still easy, and says he had not heard it called that
9. jack-slash: says the ferryman's son used it, two days back on the road, and names a number of coins for the crossing without breaking the price discussion
10. Lord Edwyn: holds the silence for three breaths, then nods once — names a fair price for the wool and nothing else, asks Jack to leave the spice-wax for inspection until morning
11. STUDIO: Lord Edwyn's left hand lifts from the table; he nods a second time, to the steward
12. steward: closes the ledger and crosses to pour wine, back turned to Jack
13. jack-slash: agrees, pockets the coin offered, and gathers the remaining goods into the satchel
14. STUDIO: Lord Edwyn watches Jack's hands while Jack buckles the satchel; one of the men-at-arms opens the hall door and holds it

### Scene 2: Dennis

15. STUDIO: Stormlands market town square, midday, overcast — livestock pen to the left, bread stall at the near corner, a well and stone coping at center, a cart horse tied to a ring-post, crowd of thirty-odd moving between stalls
16. dennis-stansfield: takes a position at the bread stall and holds out his hands, palms up — no coin, no goods, just the offer of labor
17. STUDIO: the bread-stall woman looks at his hands, then at the sacks behind the stall, and points; dennis-stansfield picks up the first sack
18. dennis-stansfield: carries four grain sacks from the cart to the stall-back, slow, gloves on, and accepts a half-loaf and a cup of well water
19. STUDIO: the cart horse shies hard left; the cart swings on its pin and drives the nearside wheel into the well coping; a boy's arm is pinned between the wheel rim and the stone
20. STUDIO: crowd noise crests; the boy's mother shouts from the bread stall; a sell-sword near the pen turns but does not step forward
21. dennis-stansfield: pulls his right glove off with his teeth and lays his bare palm flat on the cart's side-board
22. STUDIO: the cart freezes — wheel locked mid-arc, spilled grain hanging, the boy's arm still pinned but the pressure stopped
23. STUDIO: crowd falls quiet; the sell-sword faces the cart and has not moved; the septon three stalls back has turned
24. dennis-stansfield: reaches with his other hand and pulls the boy free of the wheel, then pulls the glove back on
25. STUDIO: the cart releases — wheel drops, grain scatters, horse lurches and steadies; the boy's mother reaches her son and pulls him back
26. septon: says the old word to the boy's mother, low, not moving toward dennis-stansfield
27. STUDIO: the market resumes noise around them; dennis-stansfield stands at the center of a small open circle of people who have not moved back in

### Scene 3: Crawler

28. STUDIO: large sea-cave, two-thirds down coastal cliffs, Stormlands — cave mouth open to the Narrow Sea, grey morning light, salt-smell, wave sound below; Crawler at rest near the cave entrance, visible from the water
29. STUDIO: a trading cog appears at distance, sails running before an offshore wind, heading south along the coast
30. crawler: shifts position in the cave mouth, orienting toward the cog
31. STUDIO: on the cog's deck, maester Pylos raises a brass looking-glass and holds it trained on the cliffs for a long count; the captain and two sailors turn toward him
32. STUDIO: the cog adjusts bearing two points west, adding distance from the cliffs; a sailor at the rail points toward the cave mouth and pulls his hand back
33. crawler: turns away from the water and moves into the cave interior
34. STUDIO: below deck, maester Pylos opens his writing case; the cog holds its new heading away from the coast
