# Episode Plan

chunk: The traveling maester conducts his cognitive assessment, and the results — literacy, numeracy, and a pattern-recognition performance he has not recorded in a ward of her age and station — go into his report to the Citadel as a notation of interest. Ser-aemon-bracken, who has his own reasons to want the maester's findings suppressed, moves to acquire Taylor's guardianship through a counter-claim to Plumm's; the two claims are now in active administrative contest, and a letter from ser-edwyn-celtigar's office arrives at the castellan's hall requesting a summary of all census anomalies in the Harrenhal district.
title: The Notation
theme: Documentation is the trap that closes without announcing itself; every notation is a door shutting.
actors: [taylor-hebert-westeros, westerosi-traveling-maester, ser-aemon-bracken, oc-castellan-harrenhal, septon-rowan]
# note: ser-harwick-plumm does not appear on-stage; his standing claim is a prior administrative record referenced in Scene C/D but he is not physically present. Removed from active cast list.
change: At episode open Taylor holds the wardship document and the administrative contest has not begun. At episode close Bracken's counter-claim is in the castellan's ledger, the Celtigar inquiry is in the castellan's hall, and a Citadel notation of interest on Taylor's assessment is in the maester's report — three new documents have entered the world and none of them can be recalled.

## Plan header note — timeline jump

Episode opens at the e04 closing tableau (sept yard, late afternoon, wardship document in Taylor's hand, Plumm departed, Rowan in yard with folio). The plan holds this tableau for 4 bullets, then jumps forward to the following morning when the maester's circuit cart arrives in the settlement. The jump is justified: the intervening night contains no board changes. Rowan writes the motherhouse letter; Taylor reads the wardship document; neither action alters the administrative position. The jump is noted in Scene A's opening STUDIO bullet.

## Script

- STUDIO: Sept yard, late afternoon. Plumm's departure track is empty. Taylor stands at the threshold-stone, wardship document in hand, wax seal intact. Rowan stands in the yard with the motherhouse folio under his arm. Osmynd's body is on the altar table inside through the open sept door. The neighbor woman is seated with the body. The bench is bare stone.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: "The next recorder's session in this district closes before any motherhouse letter can come back over that road."
- septon-rowan: "I am writing to the motherhouse tonight regardless."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: sets the wardship document on the threshold-stone, seal-up, and does not pick it up again.
- STUDIO: Jump forward. The following morning. The wardship document is in Taylor's inventory. The folio is under Rowan's arm. The sept yard is empty; the neighbor woman has gone. Osmynd's body remains on the altar table inside.
- STUDIO: The sept exterior. Early morning, grey light. A cart's sound on the eastern track — two wheels, one horse, unhurried pace. The maester's circuit cart enters the settlement road.

- STUDIO: The settlement common. The maester's steward unrolls the census scroll on the headman's table, set up in the open air at the common's edge. The headman stands at the table's end. Settlement smallfolk gather at the common's margin, the way they gather for official proceedings.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: steps down from the cart and sets his satchel on the headman's table, still closed. His chain is inside the traveling cloak. He does not open the satchel yet.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: stands at the table's edge and lets his attention move across the gathered smallfolk — faces, postures, who is looking at the scroll, who is looking away.
- STUDIO: The steward opens the census scroll to the Harrenhal precinct's sept-district entries. Taylor's name is on the provisional labor-eligibility line. The maester's name is not on it; the maester has his own extract in the satchel, unmoved.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: opens the satchel, produces his auxiliary extract and the small notebook. Places both open on the table beside the steward's scroll. The chain comes out of the cloak.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: "The ward of the sept at this precinct — Taylor, entered as a provisional labor-eligibility pending cognitive assessment. Is the ward present?"
- septon-rowan: "She is." Steps forward to stand beside Taylor.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: looks at Rowan, at the folio under his arm, and at Taylor. His eye does not linger. He pulls the small chair out from the headman's table and sets it across from his own. "Come and sit."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: crosses the common and sits in the chair at the distance the maester has set. Hands on her knees. Eyes at the table edge.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: draws the census scroll toward him and turns it to face Taylor. "The ward Taylor — perhaps you could help me locate the name in this column." His finger rests on the scroll without pointing.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: her eye goes to the scroll. Moves along the column at a pace two beats slower than her read-speed. Her finger settles on a name three lines above her own.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: "Yes, yes — and the entry below it, the numbers in the margin."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: reads the numbers at the same controlled pace. Names them.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: makes a mark in the small notebook. Does not look up. "And the name on the line at the foot of the column."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: finds the line and reads the name without moving her lips.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: "Mm." Writes in the small notebook. Closes the scroll. The embedded test is over. He does not say so. "The sept's register — the septon taught you to read from it?"
- taylor-hebert-westeros: "Yes, ser."
- westerosi-traveling-maester: "And numbers — accounts, tallies."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: "The sept's grain record. The tithe columns."
- westerosi-traveling-maester: produces a folded sheet from the satchel — a column of figures, a sum line at the foot. Sets it on the table facing Taylor. "The sum at the foot — is it correct?"
- taylor-hebert-westeros: looks at the column. Lets a beat pass. "No, ser. The third entry is carried wrong."
- westerosi-traveling-maester: leans forward a fraction. The yes, yes is not spoken aloud. He writes in the small notebook.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: sets a second sheet on the table — a pattern of marks, not letters or numbers. A series of shapes arranged in rows, some present, some absent. "Which mark follows this one."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: looks at the pattern. Does not move for two full beats. Names the mark.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: writes in the small notebook for longer this time. His pen does not pause.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: closes the small notebook and sets it in the satchel. Closes the satchel. "Thank you." Stands. The interview is over.
- STUDIO: The maester moves to the steward's end of the table. They speak at low volume. The steward makes an entry on the census scroll under Taylor's provisional line. The maester's small notebook does not come back out; the notation is already inside it.

- STUDIO: The settlement common, mid-morning. The maester's cart stands at the common's edge. A rider arrives from the western track — not Plumm's livery. He dismounts at the common's margin and crosses to where the maester is still at the headman's table reviewing the census extract with the steward.
- ser-aemon-bracken: the rider presents a folded note to the maester's steward. The rider does not speak.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: takes the note from the steward, reads it, folds it, returns it to the steward. His posture does not change.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: "Tell whoever sent you that the assessment is complete and the record is mine." Does not look up from the extract.
- ser-aemon-bracken: the rider waits one beat, takes the note back, and returns to his horse.
- STUDIO: The rider mounts and rides west. The maester does not watch him go.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: finishes the review of the extract with the steward. The steward rolls the census scroll. The maester lifts the satchel by the strap and does not look back at the settlement.
- STUDIO: The cart moves east. The small notebook is in the satchel. The assessment record travels with it toward the next circuit posting, and eventually toward Harrenhal's outgoing raven-carry.
- STUDIO: The western track, late morning. A second rider from the same western direction, not the same man. This rider carries a sealed document with a wax impression — not Plumm's device.
- ser-aemon-bracken: the second rider stops at the headman's house. A settlement man directs him east, toward Harrenhal.
- ser-aemon-bracken: the rider does not stop at the sept. He continues east on the track toward the Harrenhal road.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: in the sept yard, tracks the second rider's departure east by passive fauna feed — horse-weight on the track, direction, pace. The rider is not stopping.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: returns inside the sept. Sets the wardship document on the chancel shelf.
- septon-rowan: "What was the second rider carrying."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: "East. Toward the hall."
- STUDIO: The Harrenhal road, afternoon. The second rider reaches the outer gate. A castellan's-office functionary receives him in the gatehouse passage.
- ser-aemon-bracken: the rider presents the sealed document. The document carries the Bracken device on the wax.
- STUDIO: The functionary breaks the seal and reads the document in the passage. He carries it to the castellan's office anteroom. A junior clerk opens the district ledger to the sept-precinct entries.
- ser-aemon-bracken: the counter-claim enters the ledger — ward Taylor of the sept at the Harrenhal precinct, a competing claim of administrative wardship filed under Bracken's name against the existing Plumm proceeding. The clerk's hand enters the date and the filing party. The ledger stays open.
- STUDIO: The clerk sets the ledger to the dispatch side of the table. The Bracken counter-claim is now a recorded administrative contest alongside Plumm's standing claim. Both entries are on the same page, in the same ledger, in the same ink.

- STUDIO: The castellan's hall anteroom, late afternoon. A third rider arrives at the Harrenhal outer gate — the Celtigar livery on his cloak, three red crabs on white. He carries a sealed letter.
- STUDIO: The rider is admitted. The letter travels from the gate through the anteroom passage to the castellan's receiving room. Oc-castellan-harrenhal is at his table with two functionaries present.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: takes the letter. Breaks the Celtigar seal. Reads it in silence.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: sets the letter flat on the table and reads it aloud, at the pace of a man reading an official document to subordinates who will be required to act on it: "Ser Edwyn Celtigar, by commission of the Small Council, requests a full accounting of all census anomalies logged in the Harrenhal district in the current census period — unresolved status classifications, unusual capability notations, and any matter flagged for review but not yet closed — to be prepared and dispatched by raven to the address named in the annexed sheet within ten days of receipt."
- STUDIO: The two functionaries receive the reading in silence. One of them opens a writing case.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: "Bring me the sept-precinct entries. Both of them."
- STUDIO: The functionary with the open writing case crosses to the dispatch shelf and returns with two documents: the Plumm proceeding and the Bracken counter-claim, both on the same ledger page. The castellan receives them.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: reads the ledger page. Sets it beside the Celtigar letter. Both are on his table now. Neither can be un-received.
- STUDIO: The castellan's hall. The receiving room holds three people and two documents and an open writing case. The Celtigar letter is official. The contested wardship is official. The ten-day clock starts from the reading.
- STUDIO: Harrenhal exterior, late afternoon. The outer gate is closed. The Celtigar rider is in the gatehouse. The Bracken rider departed an hour ago on the western track. The eastern track is empty.

- STUDIO: The sept exterior, late afternoon. The yard is quiet. Rowan is inside the sept. Taylor is in the yard.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: passive fauna sense delivers the harrenhal bell tower ravens — their late-afternoon configuration, overhead, within passive range at the edge of her radius.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: directs the raven toward the Harrenhal outer gate. The bird lifts from the bell tower and crosses the half-league to the castle wall.
- STUDIO: The raven lands on the castle wall's outer face, above the gatehouse. It is visible to anyone looking up from the gate passage. Cost: taylor-hebert-westeros impersonator declares per cond-fauna-control-rules.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: receives the bird's feed — the closed gate, the Celtigar livery on the gatehouse rider's horse, the weight of two horses still in the yard, the shape of the passage below.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: holds the feed for one breath. Returns the bird to the bell tower.
- septon-rowan: comes into the yard. "There was a second rider. I saw him from the sill."
- taylor-hebert-westeros: "The Celtigar livery. He is still inside."
- septon-rowan: does not speak.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: goes back inside the sept. Picks up the wardship document from the chancel shelf. Sets it down again.
- STUDIO: The sept yard. Late afternoon grey, failing light. The bench is bare stone. The eastern track runs toward Harrenhal. The castle towers are visible to the north, half a league. The raven is back in the bell tower. The wardship document is on the chancel shelf. Both claims are in the castellan's ledger. Celtigar's letter is on the castellan's table. The ten-day clock is running.
