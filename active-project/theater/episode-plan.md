# Episode Plan

chunk: Ser-edwyn-celtigar's inquiry forces the castellan to resolve the contested guardianship before the crown agent's arrival makes the dispute visible at a higher level; Plumm, with the faster administrative machinery and the existing documented file, wins the resolution before Bracken's counter-claim can be heard. Taylor is formally named a ward of the castellan's administration, assigned to Plumm's operational remit, and the name given to her function — intelligence-gathering asset, fauna-behavior resource — is not one she chose and cannot now be ungiven.
title: The Function Named
theme: The moment of institutional capture — smallness of the act, permanence of the record.
actors: [taylor-hebert-westeros, septon-rowan, oc-castellan-harrenhal, ser-harwick-plumm, westerosi-traveling-maester]
change: Taylor opens contested — two names on one ledger page, ten-day clock, no resolution. Taylor closes named — ward-of-administration, function written, clock consumed by the resolution that beats her.
constraints:
  - cond-fauna-control-rules: active fauna deployment costs declared. Passive surveillance (continuous low-level input) is structural and undeclared. One active extended deployment in SCENE 1 — single raven directed to the Harrenhal gatehouse road, under 5 minutes, cost: nil (within clean window). Any deployment beyond passive in SCENE 5 flagged.
  - cond-westerosi-customary-authority: ward-of-administration language throughout. No personal-ward framing anywhere in the plan. Plumm's claim is administrative, not personal — "assigned to Plumm's operational remit" not "given to Plumm."
  - cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure: all fauna use reads as omen/animal behavior to Westerosi observers. The raven's positioning in SCENE 1 reads as a bird settling; the rat-cleared grain store cited in records reads as vermin behavior. No observer names it as controlled.
  - Plumm's resolution is administrative (faster machinery + existing documented file), not physical confrontation with Bracken.
  - Bracken remains off-stage. His counter-claim is an administrative fact, not a physical presence. His claim is procedurally deferred — the hearing proceeds without his representative present because the resolution is issued before the window for counter-hearing opens.
  - The naming of Taylor's function — "intelligence-gathering asset, fauna-behavior resource" — must be uttered and written in a way she cannot un-hear. This is the irreversible season close.
  - Episode delivers: Resolved into involuntary structure (S1 escalation spine terminus).
  - Ser-edwyn-celtigar or his advance rider arrives at or near episode's close — after the resolution is sealed — confirming the crown visibility is live for S2.

## Script

### SCENE 1 — Before dawn. Sept yard. The clock made visible.

- STUDIO: Sept yard predawn. No lanterns lit. Cold. The sept building hulks behind. A single raven perches on the yard's gatepost — not roosting, watching the road north toward Harrenhal.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: presses her back against the sept wall and holds still.
- STUDIO: The raven turns its head. The road north is empty. The Harrenhal gatehouse lantern is visible as a distant smear of light half a league off.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: counts the gatehouse lanterns and does not move.
- STUDIO: A second light joins the first at the gatehouse. Then a third. Rider preparation, not a watch change.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: releases the raven from the gatepost.
- STUDIO: The bird lifts and crosses the road northward. Cost: within clean window, under 5 minutes active. The yard is empty again.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: goes inside before the light changes.

### SCENE 2 — Morning. Septon Rowan's room in the sept. The ward's position named aloud.

- STUDIO: Rowan's room. Small, the dead septon's old writing case on the table, a half-drafted letter beside it. Rowan sits. Taylor stands near the door.
- septon-rowan: sets his pen down and does not pick it up.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: states that riders gathered at the gatehouse before dawn — three lanterns, not a watch.
- septon-rowan: says the castellan sent word last night; the ten-day window is at two days.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: asks what the castellan's word said.
- septon-rowan: says the castellan's word said the contested guardianship creates an administrative irregularity and the irregularity cannot stand if the crown's office sends a formal inquiry.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: asks which claim the castellan intends to close.
- septon-rowan: says he does not know. Says Plumm sent a man to the hall last evening with a sealed packet; he heard this from the sexton.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: holds the doorframe and says nothing.
- septon-rowan: says his letter to the motherhouse is not yet answered; he has no instrument that reaches the hall table.
- STUDIO: The letter on the table is three lines unfinished. The pen is dry.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: asks Rowan to send the letter anyway.
- septon-rowan: says it will arrive after.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: says to send it anyway.

### SCENE 3 — Late morning. Harrenhal hall antechamber. Plumm files the packet.

- STUDIO: The castellan's hall antechamber. Two clerks at a side table. The westerosi-traveling-maester stands near the window with his satchel closed, not working. A third clerk carries a sealed packet toward the inner door.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: enters from the inner room and takes the packet without sitting.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: breaks the seal and reads the covering letter standing.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: does not move from the window.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: says to the senior clerk: Plumm's documentation is complete.
- STUDIO: The senior clerk opens the ledger to the contested-guardianship page. Two names are visible on the same line — Plumm's claim and Bracken's counter-claim.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: asks the maester if the cognitive assessment notation is confirmed in the Citadel copy.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: says the notation went by raven to the Citadel twelve days ago; it is confirmed.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: tells the senior clerk to note the time of Plumm's filing and the present hour.
- STUDIO: The clerk writes the time. The inner door opens. Ser Harwick Plumm enters the antechamber, stops, and waits.

### SCENE 4 — Continuous. Harrenhal hall antechamber. The faster machinery wins.

- ser-harwick-plumm: stands inside the doorway and says nothing.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: tells Plumm his documentation is in order.
- ser-harwick-plumm: inclines his head.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: says the counter-claim from Bracken's office has not filed a complete packet within the response window; the clock ran from the day of original filing.
- ser-harwick-plumm: says he is aware of the clock.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: produces a folded paper from inside his cloak and sets it on the senior clerk's table without speaking.
- STUDIO: The paper is the maester's countersigned copy of the cognitive assessment notation. The clerk takes it.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: says the irregularity requires resolution before a formal inquiry from the crown's office can be received; the Celtigar letter named a deadline.
- ser-harwick-plumm: says he is prepared to receive the resolution today.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: says the resolution will be entered in the administrative record as follows: Plumm's claim stands, Bracken's counter-claim is deferred for want of complete filing, the ward reverts to the castellan's administration under Plumm's operational remit.
- STUDIO: The senior clerk's pen is already moving. The ledger is open. The column for "resolved" is blank for approximately four seconds.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: tells the senior clerk to note the ward's function designation in the operational column.
- STUDIO: The clerk looks up once, then writes.

### SCENE 5 — Midday. The sept yard. Taylor receives the summons.

- STUDIO: Sept yard. Full light. Rowan stands in the doorway. A castellan's rider is in the yard, horse reined. Taylor is at the well with a bucket.
- STUDIO: The rider carries a sealed notice folded twice with the castellan's device pressed into the wax.
- septon-rowan: takes the notice from the rider and breaks the seal himself, in the yard, before the rider leaves.
- septon-rowan: reads it once. Reads it again.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: sets the bucket down and waits.
- septon-rowan: says she is summoned to the hall before the vesper bell to hear the administrative resolution read into the record.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: asks if Bracken's counter-claim was heard.
- septon-rowan: says the notice does not mention Bracken. Says Plumm's claim is sustained.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: asks what the notice says about her function.
- septon-rowan: does not answer immediately.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: asks again.
- septon-rowan: reads from the notice: ward-of-administration of the castellan's office, assigned to operational remit of Ser Harwick Plumm, function designated: intelligence asset and fauna-behavior resource, per the traveling maester's assessment notation.
- STUDIO: The rider has not left. He is waiting for acknowledgment.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: says: understood.
- STUDIO: The rider turns and goes. The yard holds the silence for a moment.
- septon-rowan: says he is sorry.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: picks up the bucket and goes to fill it.

### SCENE 6 — Vesper. Harrenhal hall. The record closed.

- STUDIO: The castellan's hall, late afternoon light from high windows. The senior clerk stands at the lectern with the ledger. Oc-castellan-harrenhal sits at the head table. Ser-harwick-plumm stands to one side. Two hall functionaries witness. Taylor stands in front of the lectern with Rowan beside her.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: tells the senior clerk to read the resolution into the record.
- STUDIO: The clerk reads: designation of ward-of-administration under the castellan's office, operational assignment to Ser Harwick Plumm, function: intelligence-gathering asset and fauna-behavior resource, effective this date, per cognitive assessment notation countersigned by the traveling maester of the Citadel circuit.
- westerosi-traveling-maester: stands near the window, satchel closed. Does not look at Taylor.
- STUDIO: The clerk's pen scratches the final entry. The ledger page is full. Taylor's function is in the record.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: asks if the ward acknowledges the designation.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: says: yes.
- oc-castellan-harrenhal: tells the clerk to note the acknowledgment and close the record for this matter.
- STUDIO: The clerk writes. Closes the ledger. The ledger goes under his arm.
- ser-harwick-plumm: approaches the lectern and takes the countersigned copy from the clerk.
- ser-harwick-plumm: looks at Taylor for the first time.
- ser-harwick-plumm: tells her she will receive instructions in the morning.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: holds his gaze and says nothing.
- septon-rowan: puts one hand briefly on Taylor's shoulder, then removes it.
- ser-harwick-plumm: turns and walks out.
- STUDIO: The hall begins to empty. The senior functionary douses the far brazier. Taylor and Rowan are left standing at the lectern in cooling light.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: does not move until the last functionary has gone.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: asks Rowan if the letter to the motherhouse is sent.
- septon-rowan: says it went this morning.
- taylor-hebert-westeros: says that is good.
- STUDIO: Taylor walks to the hall's side door. Stops with one hand on the frame. From the bell tower outside, three ravens lift and settle on the hall's eave — not responding to anything a person in the hall would have caused.

### SCENE 7 — Night. The road from the south. The crown's arrival.

- STUDIO: The road south of Harrenhal, full dark. A rider at speed, cloak carrying the Celtigar device — three red crabs on white. Behind the rider, the lanterns of a retinue: twelve horses, a baggage mule, torches. Moving north.
- STUDIO: The Harrenhal gatehouse lantern comes up. The gate opens. The rider passes through.
- STUDIO: The sealed resolution sits in the castellan's records room, logged, closed. The Celtigar retinue is inside the walls.
