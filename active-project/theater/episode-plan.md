# Episode Plan

chunk: Three simultaneous snapshots — Jack's first mistake with a lordling, Dennis's day-one power exposure creating the time-witch rumor, the Crawler-dragon seen from a ship and reported to King's Landing.
theme: The moment before anything is known — three people at the edge of discovery, none of them knowing the others exist.
actors: [jack-slash, clockblocker, crawler]
change: Jack's cover develops its first hairline fracture; Dennis creates the time-witch rumor that will outlast him in the Stormlands; a written sighting report of the black dragon begins traveling toward King's Landing.
constraints:
  - Jack does not reveal what he is without extreme dramatic justification
  - Jack's broadcast shard fires returning only noise — no parahuman reads
  - Clockblocker's humor armor always running; silence is the signal
  - Dennis wears gloves constantly; must remove them to use power
  - Dennis arrived three days ago; no cover, no money, no documentation
  - Crawler has no human language at series open
  - Crawler is horse-sized, iridescent-black, adaptive fire
  - Westerosi characters process cape powers through existing frameworks (sorcery, curses, gods)
  - Capes have no knowledge of each other at series start

## Script

### Scene 1 — Jack / Reach minor lordship, late afternoon

- STUDIO: Reach minor lordship great hall, late afternoon light through arrow-slit windows; a long table with the remains of a meal; Lord Edwyn Fossoway-minor (a young lordling, not the main Fossoway line, ambitious and watchful) seated at the head; Jack positioned across the table in the posture of a merchant concluding a deal; two household men-at-arms by the door.
- jack-slash: Jack lays the terms of his cloth-trade arrangement with practiced ease, adjusting a subtle imbalance in the proposed weights in the lordling's favor — a small gift that costs Jack nothing, designed to be noticed and filed away as generosity.
- STUDIO: Lord Edwyn Fossoway-minor leans forward slightly, tapping a finger on the table, and asks how a traveling merchant knows the quality difference between Myrish work and Lyseni imitation by touch in dim light — a detail he had mentioned only once, in passing, the day before.
- jack-slash: Jack holds the pause one half-beat longer than a merchant ought to before answering; he offers a smooth explanation about his years in the Free Cities, watching the lordling's face for whether the hesitation registered.
- STUDIO: The lordling nods, accepts the explanation, signals his steward to close the arrangement — but his eyes track Jack's exit with the specific quality of attention that means the file has been opened, not closed.

### Scene 2 — Dennis / Stormlands market town, midday

- STUDIO: Stormlands market town, the main square at midday; a livestock market in progress; Dennis standing near a bread stall, having just obtained a half-loaf through a small labor exchange he negotiated this morning; three market women are arguing about price near a goat pen; a sell-sword sits on a water trough watching the crowd.
- clockblocker: Dennis tears a piece of the bread and thinks, not for the first time today, that he would kill for something to drink that isn't river water, and then catches himself wondering what exactly that joke would even mean here, and eats the bread.
- STUDIO: A cart horse spooks at a sound from the pen and lurches sideways; the cart pins a boy against the well coping; the boy shouts; the crowd turns.
- clockblocker: Dennis is already moving before he thinks about it — Ward reflex — and he strips the right glove off his hand as he runs because the cart wheel is about to pin the boy's leg and he needs to stop something.
- clockblocker: He gets a hand on the cart wheel and time stops for it — the entire cart frozen in mid-lean, horse included, a tableau of arrested motion in the middle of the square — and the boy scrambles free.
- STUDIO: The sell-sword on the water trough is on his feet; the market women have stopped arguing; a septon at the edge of the square has raised his hands; the boy's mother is staring at the frozen cart with an expression that begins as relief and ends somewhere else; nobody is speaking; the cart hangs in the stopped instant at an angle that cannot exist without something holding it.
- clockblocker: Dennis puts his glove back on, says something about the cart being stuck in a rut like he has seen this exact thing a dozen times, and the joke doesn't land because nobody here knows what a rut actually looks like when it stops a cart, or if it does, they are not thinking about ruts right now.
- STUDIO: The cart releases — whatever duration it was, it ends — and falls back to its original angle with a crack of wood and a startled shriek from the horse; in the confusion Dennis moves to the edge of the square; the septon is already talking to the mother; the phrase the septon uses is not a technical designation but the market will carry it by tonight: the boy was saved by something; the question of what that something was is already becoming a story.

### Scene 3 — Crawler / Stormlands coastal cliffs, dusk

- STUDIO: Stormlands coastal cliffs, the large cave mouth two-thirds down the cliff face, late afternoon sliding into dusk; a merchant cog visible at distance on the Narrow Sea, running south with a light wind; the cave smells of salt and something scorched; outside the cave mouth the light is going orange-grey; Crawler is visible in the cave mouth as a shape against the darker interior, iridescent-black scales catching the last light in ways that do not look like any color the sailors have a word for.
- crawler: The dragon in the cave mouth tracks the ship with the fixed, cataloguing attention of an animal assessing range and threat potential — not hunger, not aggression; the ship is too far and the wind is wrong; but the shard is notating: configuration, speed, crew count estimated from movement at the railings.
- STUDIO: On the deck of the merchant cog, a sailor at the stern rail stops coiling rope and stares at the cliff face; he calls out to the bosun; the bosun looks; the bosun calls the ship's factor; the factor comes up from below with a traveling maester making passage south to King's Landing.
- crawler: The Crawler does not move from the cave mouth; movement invites engagement; the calculus is simple — they are too far for immediate threat; the assessment continues; the fire in his chest is warm and not yet ready.
- STUDIO: The maester raises a looking glass; he holds it for a long time; when he lowers it his face has the specific stillness of a man arranging an account of something he has seen into language he can use; he tells the factor he will need ink and vellum before the ship reaches the next port; the ship adjusts bearing slightly west, gaining distance from the cliffs; nobody on deck says the word dragon out loud, but the maester is already composing the first sentence of his report in his head.
