# Episode Plan

chunk: Three simultaneous arrivals, close-cut. Jack, three months in, makes his first mistake — a Reach lordling notices that the traveling merchant seems to know things he shouldn't. Dennis, day one, lands in a Stormlands market town, uses his power publicly, and starts the time-witch rumor. The Crawler-dragon is seen from a ship in the Narrow Sea and the sighting is reported to King's Landing.
theme: The world starts keeping files on things it does not have words for.
actors: [jack-slash, clockblocker, crawler]
change: Jack's cover is cracked — a lordling's steward has instructions to investigate. Dennis's power is witnessed by a crowd and named by a septon; the rumor is in motion. Crawler has been seen and measured; a maester's report is sealed and en route to King's Landing.
constraints:
  - Jack does not reveal what he is without extreme dramatic justification
  - Dennis/Clockblocker's humor armor always running; silence is the signal
  - Crawler has no human language at series open
  - No cape knows the others exist at series start
  - Shard-host lock absolute: no new bonding, no transfer
  - Powers work with environmental friction; shard adapts expression

## Script

### Scene 1: Jack

1. STUDIO: Great hall of a minor Reach lordship — tallow candles lit, late afternoon light through arrow-slits, two men-at-arms near the door, dogs under the long table, Lord Edwyn Fossoway-minor seated at the hall's head with his steward standing at his left
2. jack-slash: delivers the merchant's pitch — bolts of undyed wool, Arbor pickled fish, spice-wax, quoted low, terms favorable, arrival through Tumbleton
3. STUDIO: Lord Edwyn nods, taps the table twice with two fingers — his left-hand tell — and glances to the steward
4. jack-slash: names the steward's brother Aldric to close the familiarity
5. STUDIO: the steward's quill lifts off the ledger and does not return
6. STUDIO: Lord Edwyn holds the pause, then smiles, confirms the arrangement, and gestures for wine to be brought
7. jack-slash: accepts the cup, holds it, does not drink
8. STUDIO: Lord Edwyn angles toward the steward and says one word, too low for the table to carry
9. STUDIO: the steward sets the quill down, closes the ledger, and exits through the side passage
10. jack-slash: makes exit pleasantries — road schedule, next destination, thanks for the hall's courtesy
11. STUDIO: the men-at-arms near the door shift weight; neither moves aside until Lord Edwyn raises a hand
12. jack-slash: crosses the hall and passes through the main doors into the courtyard
13. STUDIO: the hall doors close behind him; inside, the steward has not returned to the table

### Scene 2: Dennis

14. STUDIO: Stormlands market town square, midday, sky overcast — livestock pens along the eastern wall, bread stall at the square's center, a water trough beside the well coping, a sell-sword leaning against the well post
15. clockblocker: stacks crates behind the bread stall until the stallholder counts out two loaves and waves him off
16. STUDIO: a cart horse shies at a dog running through the pen-gap — hooves find no purchase on the wet stone, cart lurches sideways, boy pinned between cart wheel and well coping
17. clockblocker: pulls off one glove and presses a bare palm flat against the cart's side panel
18. STUDIO: the cart freezes — wheel mid-skid, horse foreleg lifted, harness chains suspended without tension
19. STUDIO: the boy drops to the ground clear of the wheel and does not move for a moment
20. clockblocker: pulls the glove back on
21. STUDIO: the cart is still frozen — wheel locked mid-arc, horse foreleg suspended, harness chains hanging without tension; the market square goes quiet around it
22. STUDIO: the freeze releases on its own — horse lurches forward, harness drops, wheel strikes the coping with its full weight
23. STUDIO: the sell-sword straightens off the well post and watches Dennis without moving toward him
24. STUDIO: the septon crosses to the boy's mother and says a word in her ear — an old word, pre-Andal root, not part of the Seven's liturgy
25. STUDIO: the boy's mother looks at Dennis, then at the septon, then does not look away from Dennis

### Scene 3: Crawler

26. STUDIO: sea-cliffs, Stormlands coast — large cave two-thirds down the cliff face, Narrow Sea grey and flat below, morning light diffuse; a trading cog working east a quarter-mile out, three sailors on deck, a young maester at the rail
27. crawler: moves to the cave mouth and orients on the cog
28. STUDIO: maester Pylos raises a brass looking-glass and holds it on the cliff face; two sailors turn toward where the glass points
29. crawler: steps fully into the open light of the cave mouth — full body at scale above the sea
30. STUDIO: a sailor points toward the cave mouth and pulls his hand back; the helmsman adjusts bearing two points west without a spoken order
31. crawler: turns away from the water and moves back into the cave interior
32. STUDIO: maester Pylos goes below; the looking-glass does not come back up
33. STUDIO: below decks, maester Pylos has parchment out and a quill inked — the first line of a report addressed to the Citadel and copied to the Hand's office in King's Landing
