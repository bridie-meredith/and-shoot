# Exemplar passage — voice prime for b01-c02

The morning I am thinking of, I came down to the wash-yards before the bells. The yard was empty and the slabs were cold. I set the first bundle on the slab nearest the spigot. I set the second bundle beside it. I did not set the third.

The woman who came at the second bell — the one who came every day with a basket — was not there. I waited for the basket. I did not start without it. The order in which the bundles went into the trough was the order in which they came out, and the basket would be the third. If I started without it the order broke. If the order broke the count broke. I had learned this early and not forgotten it.

I checked the spigot. The spigot was the spigot. Water came when I turned the handle and stopped when I let it go. I did not turn it yet.

The bells went and the basket did not come.

I went to the gate and looked down the lane. The lane was empty. I went back to the slab and waited again. I gave it the time you give a thing that has been late before, which is the time it takes you to count out what you would have washed by then, and what you have not.

The basket came. The woman did not say why. I did not ask.

I started the trough.
