This is not set-dressing with a benign label attached; it is a description of structural exposure. The dark-fantasy register is preserved.

A place is older than any person who uses it; it names itself by geography, not by function. If anything, the physical specificity is better dark-fantasy texture than leaving it abstractly institutional.
