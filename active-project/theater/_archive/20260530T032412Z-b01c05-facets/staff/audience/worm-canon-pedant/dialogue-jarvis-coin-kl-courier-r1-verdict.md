### Seam attack — Entry 8

**Attack:** The sidecar claims the patron-circumlocution return ("the man I serve") is a deliberate courier-tier discipline move — post-naming at c03 @16, Jarvis does not re-flash Otto's name in a public yard. That claim holds internally. But look at what the line actually does: it delivers a four-part routing confirmation (acceptability of terms, volume control, interval control, substance form). That is a *lot* of operational content for a "courier-tier" operative.

From a Worm-adjacent tradecraft lens: courier-tier in Worm is transactional-confirmation-only. The person who negotiates or confirms complex multi-axis terms is *above* courier-tier — that's a handler or a case officer. Jarvis is framed as a physical-exchange operative (thirty seconds to three minutes; arrive, transmit, receive, depart). The four-sentence routing confirmation is either: (a) consistent with his function if it was pre-scripted by his patron, in which case this is simple recitation and the flatness makes sense, or (b) a handler-level function Jarvis shouldn't be performing.

The card says: "He handles the physical exchange of intelligence products at the boundary layer." It also says: "Rate holds. The next ask is in the envelope." That dialogue sample suggests he *does* carry verbal protocol updates. So pre-scripted recitation reads as within scope.

**Seam strength:** Soft. The attack is conditional — if the text never shows the scripted nature of this confirmation, a reader might mistake Jarvis for a handler-tier operative, which would flatten the functional distinction. The seam is in staging/vibes, not in the dialogue itself. The dialogue line does not overclaim; it is what a courier would say if pre-scripted. Seam stays soft.

**Prescription if filed:** NI or vibes at bone 9 should mark this as scripted-recitation rather than improvised negotiation. The dialogue carries it; the surrounding narration needs to. Not a dialogue revision — a staging observation. Carry to Phase 5b cross-facet.

### Seam attack — Entry 9

**Attack:** "Same place. First bell. Three days." — three facts, clean, calibration-matched. The attack from my lens: this line is *identical in structure* to c03 @31 ("Same place. First bell. Day after.") with only the interval word changed. The sidecar calls this a feature — calibration-anchor match, shape-replication, deliberate. I grant the structural defense.

But: the interval changed from "Day after" (c03 — next day) to "Three days" (c04). The scene-context is: this is the *first* report delivery (bone 29-32 in the bones file shows Jarvis arriving at the cooper's yard, Taylor delivering the report-sheet, Jarvis pocketing it). So at the end of b01c04, the next-contact interval established is three days. At the end of b01c03, it was one day ("Day after").

This is not a dialogue problem — it's a continuity-tracking observation. If the arrangement at c03 established "day after" as the meeting cadence, and c04's exchange sets "three days" as the next interval, that's a shift. Worm-tradecraft lens: changing the interval after the first delivery is a normal operational calibration. No flag on the dialogue itself. But if both c03's "Day after" and c04's "Three days" refer to the *same* initial contact establishment, that would be a continuity error — the arrangement is being established twice with different intervals.

Reading the bones: c03 @7 is Taylor's acceptance scene (entries 1-7 of the dialogue file header-marked b01c03). c04's entry 8-9 are the first actual delivery confirmation. These are different exchanges: c03 sets up the arrangement *in principle* (terms, patron identity, substance scope); c04 is the *first delivery execution* and sets the operational cadence going forward. "Day after" in c03 @31 was the next-contact after the acceptance conversation, not the delivery cadence. "Three days" in c04 @9 is the delivery cadence post-first-delivery.

That reads cleanly — different interval for different purpose (acceptance-followup vs. delivery-cycle). No continuity error. Seam collapses.

**Seam strength:** NONE. Attack self-refutes on close reading. The structural defense holds.
