## Zero-entries verdict

The silence is load-bearing.

This chapter is held-axis discipline as substance. The constraint isn't "no metaphors because the rubric says so." The constraint is that the held-axis staging is what the chapter IS — the discipline of not elevating is the only axis-contribution available in scenes B and C, and the one axis-move in scene A (Taylor's capability +0.5) already has its figurative register fully occupied by NI:1 through the geometric-overlay figure.

A metaphor in a chapter like this would be the author flinching. Reaching for elevation at a quiet-peak to reassure the reader that something meaningful is happening. I've seen that move. It's how you announce that the substance isn't doing the work. Here the substance is doing the work, and the NI facet carries the figure the chapter needs, and the metaphor facet correctly stands down.

That's earned silence. Not evasion.
