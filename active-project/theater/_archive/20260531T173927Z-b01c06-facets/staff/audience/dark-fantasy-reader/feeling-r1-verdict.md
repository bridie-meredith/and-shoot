A feeling-flag that fires at the moment a surveillance system logs a person as a node is making a claim: that this event has a cost, that the cost is registered in the body, that the body carries it without announcing it. If the flag cannot make that claim good, it is sentiment dressed as restraint.

"Her shoulders hold where they were." The verb is "hold." Not "stiffen," not "drop," not a direction — hold. Holding still is an active suppression. The body is containing something. The insect-feed filed a ward-junction contact: a human being has been catalogued as a structural element in a coverage map. The shoulders hold. That stillness says: she knows what just happened, she is not going to let it register, and the not-letting-it-register costs something. That is consequence propagating. Not announced — just present in the body.

This is the shape of dark fantasy suffering done correctly: the wound shows as a posture, not as a statement. The character does not say "I have just done something I came here to not do." The shoulders say it. Expressed:no — no one in the scene sees this. The reader sees it and no one else does. That is the correct information architecture for this kind of interiority: the world does not know, the body knows, the reader knows.

"Hold" would be sufficient for stillness; "hold where they were" means they are holding their exact position — not adjusting, not shifting weight, not making any of the micro-compensations you make when you are comfortable.
