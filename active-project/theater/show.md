The sparrows on the eaves held the boots past the gatepost and past the bend and held them into the longer distance the eave-line gave them, the warm forward mass of three men and a sealed case running smaller in the feed at the pace a floor of feathers can carry a sound, and where the eave-line ran out the bell-tower ravens took the line at their angle and held it at the longer reach, the case-and-boots running smaller still under the ravens' arc, and I did not move my feet and I did not shift my weight and the cooperative-child stillness held in my body the shape it had taken at the doorway and held it through the inspection and was holding it now around the empty center of the nave, and the breath through the wall came at the worse interval and I counted what it did not do, and the line under the ravens went smaller and smaller and then went under the threshold the ravens could hold, and the feed gave me nothing where the boots had been, and Rowan's road-weight stood in the corner of my vision where it had been since the page took his name, unmoving, his pack still on his shoulder, and into the place where the line had ended my throat moved once — a swallow, dry, the small involuntary closing and opening that happens in a throat that has been held closed for an hour, the muscle doing for itself what I had not told it to do — and the chin stayed at its quarter-inch and the feet stayed on the rushes and the mouth stayed closed, and the next breath through the wall came at the worse interval, and the silence the ravens had handed back was a silence the sparrows could not pick up again.
