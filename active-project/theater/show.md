The sound from the next room was worse. She knew it before she was fully awake — the wet pull of it, the catch and the not-quite-release — and she had the bowl in her hands and was moving before she had decided to move.

She set the clay cup on the floor beside the pallet — careful, no sound — and when she straightened, he had already turned his head.

"Millfall," Aldric said. His chest lifted wrong, the wet pull audible before the next word came. "Census rider. Yesterday." He stopped. When he had breath again: "By terce if he rode fast. Midday if he did not." His eyes found her face and held. He had said the thing. There was nothing left to add.
