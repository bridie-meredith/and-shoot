# KL Witch-Label Formation — 122 AC (Insect-Control Trigger)

## Description

Governs the social and institutional process by which Taylor's insect-control capability generates a witch-label in King's Landing at 122 AC. The trigger mechanism for this project is observable insect-network anomaly — animal massing, directed insect movement, rat behavior at variance with normal scavenger patterns — not any foreknowledge or behavioral-pattern trigger. The KL institutional escalation sequence (Watch → noble quarter → Hightower network) is the same as in prior versions of this card; only the trigger and the capability description change to match this project's power-config.

## Sensory Impact

The witch-label is not a sensory condition but its formation produces observable social effects: behavioral changes in witnesses (avoidance, crossed signs, increased Watch surveillance), changes in how Taylor is addressed or not addressed in Flea Bottom, and eventually the arrival of institutional attention in forms Taylor can track through her insect network.

## Duration

Progressive. The witch-label does not form instantly; it accumulates across act one as Taylor's insect-network use becomes observable to enough witnesses across enough incidents. Once formed at street level, it propagates upward through the institutional channels at the latencies described below. It cannot be reversed within the book's timeline once it reaches the noble-quarter or Hightower-network layer.

## Trigger Mechanism — What Generates the Label

**The label forms from observable insect-network anomalies, not from any behavioral or cognitive tell.** The specific observable patterns that generate the witch-label are:

- **Animal massing:** Insects or small fauna assembling near Taylor in quantities or patterns not explained by ordinary food-source attraction. Flies that gather and do not disperse. Ants that trail toward her rather than away. Rats that approach rather than flee.
- **Directed movement:** Insects moving against prevailing flow (upstream of smells, toward noise rather than away, in geometric patterns). Any witness who sees insects moving in a visibly directed way near a specific person will file this observation in the witch-category.
- **Correlated events:** Taylor is present; something happens (an observation she shouldn't have made, a piece of information she has that she shouldn't have); insects were observed near her before the event. The correlation between insect-anomaly and anomalous information or capability is the social mechanism that binds the label to the person rather than to the event.
- **Sustained absence of insect nuisance:** Taylor is not troubled by flies, gnats, or vermin in situations where any Flea Bottom resident would be. The absence is itself an anomaly — witnesses notice that the flies don't land on her, that the rats don't come near her table, that she sits in the midden-adjacent alley without acquiring vermin.

**What does NOT trigger the label for this project:** Behavioral foreknowledge expressed as a "flicker" — involuntary behavioral tells produced by near-future knowledge — is not a trigger in this project because Taylor does not have that capability. The pattern-recognition precog surfaces as unusual analytical ability (she draws correct inferences quickly), which Westerosi observers may interpret as "she knows things" but this is a secondary effect, not the primary trigger mechanism. The primary trigger is always the observable insect-behavior anomaly.

## Label Formation Stages

**Stage 1 — Street-Level Formation (Flea Bottom, Hook).**
Individual witnesses accumulate observations. No single observation forms the label; the label forms when a critical mass of corroborating observations circulates through the Hook's gossip network. Timeline: days to weeks from first significant observable insect-anomaly event. The label that forms is the folk-category: *witch*, *the touch*, *cunning-craft*. This is not immediately dangerous — the Hook has its own witch-category and the label does not automatically attract hostile action. It attracts attention, some avoidance, some opportunistic interest (people who want something from "the witch").

**Stage 2 — Watch Observation Layer.**
Watch patrols in the Hook observe Taylor in context of the street-level label. Watch officers with informant networks hear the folk-category reports. The Watch's institutional vocabulary launders "witch" into "person of unknown provenance with behaviors inconsistent with ordinary vermin response." Watch informant-reporting latency for non-emergency anomalous-person observations: days to weeks after stage 1 is established. The Watch report is filed, not acted on immediately. It enters the Watch's passive intelligence picture of the Hook.

**Stage 3 — Noble Quarter / Court Transmission.**
Watch intelligence on anomalous-behavior persons in Flea Bottom reaches the noble quarter through two channels: formal Watch captain reporting to the city's administration, and informal informant networks that feed households in the noble quarter. The transmission latency is weeks to a month after stage 2. At this stage the intelligence is fragmentary — "unusual foreign woman in the Hook, animal behavior reports" — not yet a threat assessment.

**Stage 4 — Hightower Network Capture.**
Otto Hightower's intelligence network in KL is calibrated to surface exactly this category: foreign nationals of unknown provenance with unusual capabilities or anomalous observable behaviors. Once the stage 3 intelligence reaches noble-quarter households, Otto's network collects it within days. His network writes the intelligence-category report (per `cond-westerosi-witness-vocabulary`: not "witch" — "foreign national, capability of unknown nature in the area of animal behavior, assessed as potential intelligence asset or vulnerability"). This is the point at which Taylor's witch-label translates into operational attention from the most sophisticated observer available in 122 AC KL.

**Stage 4 is the first-trade setup.** Otto's network capture of the witch-label report is the mechanism by which Taylor comes to Otto's attention, which is the precondition for the first-trade beat (Taylor becomes Otto's intelligence asset in exchange for his protection of Lucerys in the succession calculus). The witch-label is not an obstacle to Taylor's objective; it is the path to it, and the path to the first auditable mistake.

## KL Institutional Escalation Rules

**The escalation sequence is not reversible.** Once stage 2 Watch observation is established, Taylor cannot suppress the label by modifying her behavior — the observation is already in the informal intelligence picture. She can slow escalation by reducing observable insect-anomaly events, but she cannot zero it.

**Faith escalation is a parallel path, not a stage in the sequence.** Septon or Most Devout involvement does not follow from the Watch-to-noble-quarter path; it requires a separate trigger (Taylor performing an observable insect-anomaly event near Faith-affiliated observers, or Faith informants in the Hook reporting the label to septon institutional attention). Faith escalation is more dangerous to Taylor than Hightower network capture but slower and less certain. It is not a required story element but is available as a pressure mechanism.

**The label does not prevent Taylor's court access.** In KL's political climate, unusual capability is as likely to generate patron interest as it is to generate suppression. The Hightower network's assessment of Taylor as a "potential intelligence asset" is the institutional translation of the witch-label into utility. Otto Hightower is not frightened by witchcraft; he is interested in capability.
