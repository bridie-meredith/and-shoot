# Velaryon Household — King's Landing Townhouse (Patron's KL Premises)

## Geography

A noble townhouse on the upper slope of the Hill of Rhaenys, in the shadow of the Dragonpit's hill but positioned at the city's respectable tier — above Flea Bottom, below the Red Keep. The Velaryon name carries sufficient weight in King's Landing that their KL premises occupy a street where other noble-affiliated households have secondary residences: lords who maintain KL addresses for court attendance, merchants who have been ennobled, bannermen with standing business at the city. The street is wide enough for a cart with room to spare. The houses are stone-fronted, maintained, gate-locked.

The townhouse is not Velaryon primary — their seat is Driftmark, their island. This is the KL address for court periods, for Rhaenys and her household's attendance at Viserys's court when required. It runs on a permanent small staff even when the household is absent from the city. It is the address where a patron like Rhaenys receives faction contacts whom she cannot, for various reasons, receive at the Red Keep itself.

Distance from Flea Bottom: forty to fifty minutes on foot, ascending. Taylor's route, when a retainer collects her, goes east from the Fish Gate margin, then north through the eastern quarter, then up the hill-slope streets. The journey is a visible social gradient — the streets widen, the refuse decreases, the proportion of people who look at Taylor and find her out-of-place increases.

## Layout

**Exterior approach:**
A stone gate set in the property wall — iron-worked, opening to a small forecourt where a cart could turn around. The gate has a porter's station inside; there is always someone at it when the household is in residence. The forecourt is cobbled and slightly elevated above the street, so arriving guests step up when they enter and have a brief moment of exposure before the gate closes behind them.

**Receiving room (Taylor's access space):**
The room where Taylor is brought is the formal receiving room off the main entrance passage — not the hall, not the solar, not any room deeper in the house. It is the room designed for receiving people whose status has not yet been established: sufficient furniture to communicate that the household is not impoverished, not comfortable enough to invite extended stays, lit by two windows facing the forecourt so that the occupant of the chair behind the table always has the light at their back.

Rhaenys receives Taylor seated. The chair is behind a table. Taylor is shown to a low chair on the other side — lower than the receiving chair by a deliberate half-foot, the most common architectural trick for establishing hierarchy without requiring anyone to say anything about hierarchy. There is no second table, no food offered at first meetings, no invitation to remove outer garments. The room is warm in a managed-warmth way: the hearth is lit but banked, enough to prevent the cold from being a distraction, not enough to be a comfort.

**Adjacent passages Taylor can identify:**
The receiving room opens to the entrance passage, which runs toward the courtyard at the back of the house. There are two doors in the passage: one to a secondary anteroom used by the household staff, one to stairs descending toward the kitchens. The courtyard at the rear has a postern gate into the next alley — Taylor notes this in the first three minutes of her first visit.

**Rooms Taylor does not access:**
The solar, private apartments, the household chapel. The house has two additional stories above the receiving floor. Taylor has no operational picture of these spaces.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**Smell:** Clean in the way that distinguishes noble maintenance from poverty maintenance: the absence of smoke residue (proper hearths that draw), the presence of dried herbs in the floor rushes, the faint cedar-and-oil smell of maintained furniture. Underneath this, sea-adjacent: the Velaryon household carries a faint salt-timber note from their maritime primary, present in the oils used on the furniture and in the clothing of the staff who rotate between Driftmark and KL. This smell is specific enough that Taylor will later, in other KL spaces, register it as a Velaryon presence marker.

**Sound:** Quiet relative to Flea Bottom — this is the most immediately noticeable thing. The street outside is not silent, but the house's stone walls absorb it. The household's internal sounds are staff-movement sounds: footsteps on flagstone with a specific cadence (people who have worked in this space and know where each floor-sound is), the distant sound of a kitchen operating. Nothing raised. Nothing in a hurry. The quiet is a power demonstration as much as a fact of construction.

**Visual:** The receiving room is furnished in Velaryon colors without being garish about it — sea-green present in the upholstery fabric, the household's personal motif in the carved arms of the receiving chair. Good candles in brass holders, supplementing the window light. The table between Taylor and Rhaenys has nothing on it at initial meetings: no documents, no refreshments, no props. This is deliberate. The table is an architectural fact, not an invitation.

**Temperature:** The managed warmth of a room that is attended to, not ambient. The receiving room is slightly warmer than the entrance passage, which is slightly warmer than the street outside. Each threshold is a degree of curated welcome.

## Fixed Props

None card-level. Furniture is architectural.

## Exits

- **Forecourt gate to street (primary exit):** The way Taylor arrived. The porter observes departures. This exit returns her to the hill-slope street and the descent back toward the eastern quarter and Flea Bottom.
- **Courtyard postern (secondary, noted by Taylor):** The back gate to the alley behind the house. Taylor registers this exit before Rhaenys enters the receiving room. She does not use it; she registers it.
- **Entrance passage toward interior (not Taylor's exit):** Deeper into the house. Staff use it. Taylor notes which direction the footsteps come from when Rhaenys approaches.

## Hazards

**Social intelligence cross-contamination:** Taylor's visits to this address are visible to anyone watching the street. The Velaryon townhouse is not a clandestine location. A woman from Flea Bottom being escorted through this gate by a Velaryon retainer is a legible social event to anyone in that street who understands Velaryon household politics. The Black faction uses this address; so do faction observers from the Green faction. Taylor's association with Rhaenys becomes a documented social fact from the moment of her first visit.

**The power differential as operational pressure:** The room is designed to keep Taylor in a specific posture. The lower chair, the controlled warmth, the empty table — these are all mechanisms for maintaining the patron's interpretive authority over the meeting. Taylor reads the room correctly; this means she is also aware that she is being managed, which adds a layer of self-consciousness to every exchange. The flicker, if it fires during a receiving-room consultation, happens in a space where Taylor's reaction will be fully witnessed by someone who has a stake in interpreting it as confirmation of her amplification value.

**Retainer escort dependency:** Taylor does not come here voluntarily. She comes when a retainer is sent to collect her. This means her access to the patron is controlled by the patron's availability, not Taylor's operational timing. She cannot arrive here urgently with new information; she can only receive a summons and attend.

**Dragonpit proximity:** The townhouse is on the Hill of Rhaenys's upper slope. The Dragonpit is on this hill. From the forecourt, on a clear day, the Dragonpit's outer wall is visible above the next tier of rooftops. Taylor noticed this on her first approach. The flicker-pressure gradient from the Dragonpit may reach this location — lower than at the exterior approach, but present.

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- The retainer arriving at Taylor's lodging to escort her up — the specific choreography of being collected (what the retainer says, what he does not say, whether there is urgency or routine in the summons)
- Another visitor departing the townhouse as Taylor arrives through the forecourt gate — a Black-faction contact Taylor does not yet know by name, whose presence tells her something about what the meeting will be about
- Staff movement in the entrance passage during the consultation — a household member passing who should not have been passing at this hour
- Rhaenys receiving Taylor standing rather than seated — the single deviation from established protocol that signals a change in the patron's assessment of the relationship
- The postern gate in the courtyard being ajar when it should be latched — an irregularity Taylor notes but cannot explain during the meeting
- The Dragonpit-direction smell reaching even into the receiving room on a specific wind condition — Taylor's flicker pressure spiking in the patron's presence, exactly as the patron's theory predicts, for reasons the patron does not understand
- A sound from the upper floors during consultation — someone in the private apartments who has not been introduced, whose presence Taylor can infer but not confirm
