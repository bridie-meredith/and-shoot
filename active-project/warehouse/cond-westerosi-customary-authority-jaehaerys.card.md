# Westerosi Customary Authority — Riverlands Smallfolk Social Physics at 84–101 AC (Jaehaerys I Era)

## Description

The behavioral constraints governing how Riverlands smallfolk interact with authority figures at 84–101 AC. This card defines the social physics of Taylor's operating stratum — the default postures, the available compliance behaviors, the specific costs of deviation from expected norms, and what various kinds of wrongness look like in a smallfolk child in this environment.

This is the ambient behavioral layer that everything in Taylor's daily life runs against. It is not a special circumstance; it is the water.

**Key era difference from the 120 AC config:** This is Jaehaerys I's long peace. The lord's men are functionaries, not soldiers in a war footing. Suppression of smallfolk deviation is documentary and procedural before it is physical. The record is the threat.

## Sensory Impact

The social physics of deference are physically embodied. When a man-at-arms arrives in a village common, every adult smallfolk body undergoes a postural shift within seconds: eyes drop, weight shifts back, shoulders relax to signal non-threat. The absence of these behaviors is visible immediately.

This physical vocabulary is the tell that Taylor cannot entirely suppress. The result is a performance that is close but slightly off — a beat late, a fraction wrong in the direction of the movement, eyes that drop and then return too fast.

## Duration

Persistent. Era-stable for the full 84–101 AC project window.

## Default Postures by Authority Type

**Armed men (men-at-arms, household guard, mounted escorts):**
- Eyes down on approach
- Short answers, nothing volunteered
- Physical positioning: stepped back and to the side
- No asking questions of the armed man
- Requests directed toward the figure in charge, briefly and formulaically
- Era note: under Jaehaerys's long peace, men-at-arms in a market town are tax-collection escorts. The deference rules are the same; the immediate physical risk of deviation is lower; the documentary risk is higher.

**Septons:**
- Different register: horizontal rather than vertical. Meet eyes with appropriate humility.
- Era note: the Faith Militant was suppressed ~50 years before story open. No organized Faith violence permitted. A septon's authority is moral only. His tools: letters, charity access, preaching, social pressure.

**Maesters:**
- Institutional rather than physical authority.
- A maester in administrative mode has quasi-administrative authority; physical deference rules apply at reduced intensity.
- Era note: Citadel at institutional peak. A maester's anomalous finding travels upward through the chain quickly and efficiently.

**Lords and stewards:**
- Full physical deference. Eyes down, stance receded, do not speak unless spoken to.
- In practice, smallfolk address the steward (Aldric Pryor for Ryger's interests), not the lord directly.

## Literacy as Red Flag

The available cover stories for Taylor's literacy: (1) Sept education through Septon Rowan; (2) Trade education from her father's dyer-with-accounts practice. The cover is plausible and defensible under casual inspection; it degrades under sustained scrutiny.

Taylor's specific exposure: she reads at adult speed, does accounts-level arithmetic, writes in a practiced hand. A maester spending more than ten minutes with her on any administrative task will notice.

## The Competence Problem

A child who speaks to authority with adult confidence is misread in one of three ways:
1. Mentally affected (touched) — protective and exposing.
2. Taught by someone with an agenda — draws attention to the patron.
3. Dangerous — something pretending to be a child.

Taylor can pass as mentally affected (strange but harmless) if she is careful. She cannot pass as a normal child.

## Era-Specific Authority Architecture (84–101 AC)

**Jaehaerys I long peace:** The administrative apparatus records more than a distracted, overextended one. Lower immediate physical threat; higher documentary threat.

**Tully suzerainty stable:** Functional, well-oiled escalation path. Taylor's escalation will climb this chain through normal institutional reporting.

**House Ryger of Willow Wood:** Minor Tully bannerman. Local representative is Aldric Pryor (steward). Interest in Fairstead is entirely administrative.

**No Faith Militant:** Zero organized Faith violence permitted. Any scene implying Faith-organized violence or armed Faith investigation is a fence violation.

**Citadel at peak:** Maesters' soft-surveillance function (literacy detection, anomalous-findings reporting) operates efficiently.

## Available Resistance Modes for Smallfolk

Delay, disappear, claim illness, invoke competing authority, comply correctly, or direct refusal (not viable for Taylor at series open; changes as she ages).

## What Deviation Costs

In a stable administrative apparatus:
- First deviation: Pryor makes a note. Open flag.
- Accumulated deviations: Taylor is treated as a pattern; routed to higher review through normal channels.
- Patterned-response: Steward pays attention on every visit.
- Policy: The lord has directed systematic attention.

See `cond-suppression-policy-progression` for the full escalation structure.
