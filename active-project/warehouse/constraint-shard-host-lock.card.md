# Constraint: Shard-Host Lock

## Description

A shard can only act through its arrived host. It cannot gain new hosts. The host they arrived with is the only host they will ever have in this world.

## Sensory Impact

No scene condition. This is a hard mechanical law.

## Duration

Permanent. Absolute. No exceptions.

## Interaction Notes

- If the host dies in Westeros, the shard goes dormant. It does not move on.
- The shard cannot be stolen, transferred, or forced into another person.
- A Westerosi person touching a cape does not trigger.
- The Cauldron is not here. There are no new parahumans. The capes who arrived are the only parahumans.
- This law is load-bearing for all power-mechanics scenes: no cape can grant powers, create new capes, or spawn secondary parahuman threats through their power alone.
- The exception to consider: Noelle's clone production. Clones would not be parahumans — they would be Noelle's copies, not new shard-hosts. Clones with Noelle's power expression would be Noelle's power expression, not independent shards. This distinction may matter if clone production occurs.
