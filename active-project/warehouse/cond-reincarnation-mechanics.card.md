# Reincarnation Mechanics — Base Rules (All Westeros Projects)

## Description

Governing constraint card for the terms of Taylor Hebert's reincarnation. Defines what carried over, what the reincarnation created anew, what it permanently closed, and what paths do and do not exist. The foundational rules apply to all Westeros variants including the dragon-gate-foreclosure project.

**For dragon-gate-foreclosure:** Load `cond-reincarnation-mechanics-dragon-gate` as the operative arrival-mechanics card. This card's specific content (11yo body, Harrenhal-area arrival, Septon Dying Protector, etc.) is the taylor-hebert-westeros config and is NOT operative for this project. The foundational rules below apply.

## Foundational Rules (Apply to All Projects Including dragon-gate-foreclosure)

**Full episodic memory carried over.** Taylor carries complete recall of her life as Taylor Hebert. Not impressions or fragments — intact autobiographical memory.

**Semantic knowledge carried over.** Earth-Bet knowledge (capes, Shards, Worm canon) and Fire & Blood as text.

**Return is closed.** There is no route back to Earth-Bet. Not through Planetos-native magic, not through any Shard mechanism. Physical fact, not reluctant acceptance. Taylor's internal model does not include a return scenario.

**The body is real.** Not a projection, temporary vessel, dream, or simulation. Death in Westeros is permanent. Injury is permanent.

**Taylor is the only displaced soul.** No other isekai arrivals in this project.

**Memory completeness is non-negotiable.** No degradation, no selective forgetting under stress, no fading over time.

**The reincarnation was involuntary.** No discovered purpose, patron deity, or mission. The absence of explanation is permanent.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-reincarnation-mechanics-dragon-gate`:** That card governs the Dragon Gate config specifics (19yo body, mid-catastrophe arrival, no prior occupant, Watch encounter at arrival). This card provides the foundational rules. Both should be loaded together.
