# Maester Network Behavior — Citadel-to-Lord Soft Surveillance at 84–99 AC

## Description

How the Citadel-to-lord maester network operates as a soft surveillance and information system in the Riverlands at 84–99 AC. Covers: the placement function, ravens as the fast channel, literacy and anomaly detection, how anomalous findings travel upward, the Citadel's institutional peak under Jaehaerys's reign, and what Septon Barth's death (~99 AC) means for the network's future behavior.

This card governs how Taylor's anomalous characteristics (extraordinary literacy, unusual cognition, the ignition event) can travel through the maester system.

## Sensory Impact

The maester network has no ambient sensory expression. Its presence is felt as potential: the awareness that anything a maester observes is entered into a system that extends from the nearest castle to Oldtown. Taylor knows this system is there.

## Duration

Persistent across the project window. Stable throughout 84–101 AC. Septon Barth's death (~99 AC) reduces collaborative efficiency between maester network and Faith network.

## The Placement Function

Every lord of any consequence has a maester. For House Ryger's scale: they likely share access to a maester with other minor Tully bannermen, or use a traveling maester for episodic consultations rather than having one in residence. A traveling maester arriving in Fairstead is a significant event, not routine.

## Ravens as the Fast Channel

Ravens carry messages between castles that have raven rookeries. The raven system does not extend to market towns — Fairstead does not have a raven rookery. Information about Taylor that travels through the raven network travels through the lord's hall first. Pryor controls what enters the raven network from Ryger's end.

## Literacy Detection and Anomaly Flagging

**What triggers a maester's attention:**
- Reading at adult speed or comprehension when stated education should not produce that level
- Handwriting that is practiced and consistent rather than laborious
- Accounts-keeping ability indicating regular arithmetic practice
- Valyrian script recognition (significant flag — no plausible innocent explanation for a smallfolk child)
- Questions that imply adult-level understanding of subject matter

**Taylor's specific exposure:** She reads at adult speed, does accounts-level arithmetic, writes in a practiced hand. She is actively suppressing her Valyrian script recognition. The cover (father's dyer-with-accounts practice + septon supplementation) holds for casual inspection; it degrades under sustained scrutiny by someone who knows what extraordinary literacy looks like.

**What happens when a maester notices:** He notes the anomaly in his administrative record. If significant enough, he writes a letter to Oldtown, framing it as an interesting observed case. More immediately: he mentions the anomaly to the lord he is serving. The two tracks (Pryor's administrative track + maester's observation track) converge at the lord level.

## How Anomalous Findings Travel Upward

1. Local observation: a maester encounters Taylor in Fairstead.
2. Lord's report: the maester reports to the lord whose administrative mission he is supporting. Pryor receives this as confirmation of his existing file.
3. Citadel archival record: the maester writes a letter to Oldtown. Arrives in weeks. Enters the archive.
4. Pattern detection at Oldtown (if applicable): multiple letters from multiple maesters may generate a response letter of guidance.
5. Citadel-Faith cooperation: during Barth-era, a maester might mention his observation to the local circuit septon during a shared administrative meeting. This is the overlap point where Rowan's theological track and the maester's analytical track might briefly intersect.

## The Citadel at Institutional Peak (84–99 AC)

Lord's maesters are well-resourced and their counsel is taken seriously. The Citadel's archival systems function efficiently. Letters that reach Oldtown are actually processed. The collaborative information-sharing with the Faith is at its peak.

As the series progresses toward Barth's death (~99 AC), this peak begins to erode. Maesters and the Faith operate more independently by the Council horizon. This means the information convergence (maester observation + Faith observation triangulating on Taylor) is more likely to occur early in the series than late.

## The Traveling Maester Walk-On

The `westerosi-traveling-maester` character represents a maester arriving in Fairstead approximately at the ignition aftermath (86–87 AC), contracted for specific administrative tasks. His job: assess the Ashford household's administrative compliance and observe the Ashford girl specifically. He will notice her literacy within minutes of a sustained interaction. His report to Pryor and his letter to the Citadel constitute Taylor's first entry in the Citadel's archival system.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-suppression-policy-progression`:** The maester's observations feed into Pryor's file at Stage 1–2. The convergence of Pryor's administrative file and the maester's anomaly report is one of the triggers that can move Pryor from Stage 2 to Stage 3.

**With `cond-faith-of-seven-jaehaerys`:** The Citadel-Faith collaborative model means maester and Faith information tracks can intersect. This intersection is more likely in the Barth-era (84–99 AC) than after.

**For auditor use:** The maester network does not take direct operational action against Taylor. It generates records and reports. If a scene implies a maester actively directing suppression of Taylor (rather than reporting upward), that is a role violation.
