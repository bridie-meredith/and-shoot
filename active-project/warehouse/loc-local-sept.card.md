# The Fairstead Sept (Septon Rowan's Seat)

Small stone sept on Fairstead's south edge. Seven-sided tower, two stories. Septon Rowan's full-time parish seat (NOT a circuit septon — he is resident here). Taylor's literacy-origin: she used the sept's four book collection for reading practice. Taylor maintains a passive surveillance unit (fly in the baptismal basin) since ~85 AC. Rowan's correspondence with his Septon-Superior travels through here. Full card at library source.
