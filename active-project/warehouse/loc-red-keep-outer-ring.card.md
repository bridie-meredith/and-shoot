# Red Keep — Outer Ring (Working Copy)

Source of truth at cards/locations/loc-red-keep-outer-ring.card.md

## Geography

Outermost layer of the Red Keep: outer walls, gatehouse approaches, outer baileys, servant and garrison quarters. Not inner castle.

**Access:** Not by physical proximity — by range expansion. Taylor's 300m radius does not reach from Flea Bottom at story open. At near-ceiling range (~1.5km, late s2), the outer ring becomes accessible from her Flea Bottom anchor.

## Key Notes

- Not available until late s2 / early s3 range expansion
- Insect substrate is thinner (castle pest management) than Flea Bottom
- Taylor's range at ceiling covers outer ring at degraded precision
- Possible Valyrian magical residue producing anomalous insect behavior (observable, not interpretable)
- Surveillance location only — not a traversal location
