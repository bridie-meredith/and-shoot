# Monument: Cost Borne by the Unconsenting

## What this monument is

A behavior-pack monument family for Taylor Hebert's memory-flags system. Names the class of displacement-cue that fires when Taylor registers that a cost of her actions has settled on someone who did not choose to be inside the radius of what she did. The cost may be informational (witnessing something they cannot unknow), social (being associated with anomalous events), or physical. The key element is that the bearer did not agree to carry it.

Monument class: Earth-Bet displacement. The cue draws on Taylor's prior-world experience of this pattern — the accumulation of people who were near her decisions without choosing to be, who ended up carrying weight she generated. She does not name the prior-world instances. She recognizes the ledger entry.

## Monument trigger

A bystander, witness, or third party who has received some portion of the consequence of Taylor's action without having been a participant in the decision that created it. The trigger is the specific moment of recognition: Taylor accounting for the cost that has landed on someone who was not consulted.

Typical triggers: a civilian witness who saw something that cannot be unseen; an associate who absorbs ambient suspicion by proximity; a person whose life was altered by Taylor's routing, deployment, or presence without their knowledge.

## Displacement-cue form

The cue must:
- Name the cost-landing without naming Earth-Bet specific events or persons.
- Foreground the unconsenting quality — the key word is that the bearer did not agree to carry this.
- Use ledger-register language (Taylor's internal accounting frame) if appropriate to the beat, but the monument is the unconsenting-bearer pattern, not the ledger per se.
- Not introduce Earth-Bet proper nouns, facility names, or named individuals from prior-world.

The cue can reference: faces she did not pick, counts set by others, costs that settle without negotiation, the gap between what was chosen and who is paying.

## Rubric positioning

Memory-flags quiet-beat discipline: this monument is a quiet-beat instrument (the recognition of unconsenting cost is a slow accounting, not a peak event). Peak-bone fires are default-forbidden. Settle-tail and scene-gap positions preferred.

Per-scene cap: 1 fire per scene maximum. Doubled-register requirement: must appear alongside at least one Westerosi-clamp monument in the same file.
