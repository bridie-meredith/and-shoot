# Constraint: Arrival Mechanics

## Description

Three arrival modes exist. The shard selects mode based on optimal power expression environment.

## Sensory Impact

No sensory impact — this is a law governing the form of arrival, not a scene condition.

## Duration

Permanent. Arrival mode is set at arrival and cannot change.

## Interaction Notes

- Type-A (adult drop-in): full Earth-Bet memories, power-shaped new body, knows they died.
- Type-B (reincarnated): no episodic Earth-Bet memory, shard-dreams as only contact with prior life, power re-emerging through childhood.
- Type-C (dragon-born): no memory, no language at birth, shard fully operational, dragon body is the body.
- Mode determines what the character knows, what they can do, and how much grief they carry about their prior life. These three variables are load-bearing in all character scenes.
- Type-B arrivals grow up. Time passes. The tinker power re-emerges as they develop. This means the Riley arc spans years of the series.
