See library card at `cards/locations/loc-dragon-gate-block.card.md` for full content.

## Critical facts for scene use

**Role:** PRIMARY operational territory for the entire book (months 1–36). Taylor's full world for three years.

**Geography:** ~300m radius from gate arch. Eastern landward entrance to KL. Gate arch → market alley system → ward sept → dockyard-road edge. Mixed-class: travelers, merchants, guild-registered traders, Watch, craft operations. NOT Flea Bottom; NOT Red Keep district.

**Taylor's range from center:** ~150m hard constant. Gate arch, market alley (inner), Mira's workshop, contact's premises, guardhouse — all within range when centrally positioned. Block exits (gate arch east; interior streets west) are at range edge or beyond when Taylor is positioned centrally.

**Insect ambient:** ~20,000 insects in continuous contact when centrally positioned. Gate-traffic flies, wall-seam beetles (old stone), market-area fauna, rats in storage. Taylor knows the full population to individual-creature level within 3 months.

**Sensory vocabulary (key):** Gate-traffic smell (animal, dust, dried-dung). Market cooking. Rope-fiber-and-lanolin (Mira's workshop). Harbor salt on eastern wind. Evening: amber lanterns across inner alley.

**Sound:** Gate arch amplifies cart wheels. Market's continuous commerce wash. Watch patrol foot-rhythm (distinguishable from civilian). Metalmonger's hammer (acoustic baseline of inner alley).

**Exits:** Gate arch east; interior streets west; north ward boundary; south to dockyard-road. All exits are at or beyond Taylor's range cap from block center.

**Hazards:** Watch omnipresence (patterned, Taylor has mapped it); gate-traffic political temperature (Dance's approach bleeds in through faction-sigil carriages); witch-label contingent stability; persons-risk sweep protocol (Act 3 primary hazard).

**The Hand's carriage (Act 2 canon glimpse):** Passes through gate arch identifiable by sigil. Watch clears staging yard. Market alley quiet. Carriage does not stop. No canonical figure speaks on-page.
