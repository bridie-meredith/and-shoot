# Series Tone — Dragon Gate Foreclosure (126–129 AC)

## Description

Project-scoped tone law for the dragon-gate-foreclosure project. Derives from `cond-series-tone-constraints` (library base card) with re-specification for: single-book not multi-season structure; patron-dialect-as-structural-horror not clinical-register-as-structural-horror; foreclosure-as-bureaucratic-processing not foreclosure-as-research-arc-terminus; no Khepri unlock; no glass-candle research arc; no s4 multi-subject experiment. The contemplative-procedural-horror register from the base card carries; the specific form of the horror is the 6-rung ladder's momentum and the final bureaucratic processing rather than the self-erasing research log.

## The Core Tone Law

**Grimdark with honest weight, not aestheticized cruelty.** Bleakness must be earned by causal chains the reader can reconstruct. Every cost in this book — the Rung-3 workers receiving nothing, the Rung-5 physician suspended, the contact dying in custody, Mira dying in the sweep expansion, Taylor processed and dead — must arrive as the consequence of a causal chain that was visible in prior scenes. No cost arrives from ambient bleakness or world-cruelty unmoored from the ladder's accumulation.

**Tragedy-of-competence pacing.** Taylor is competent. The model she uses is competent. Each rung deploys competent judgment applied through a model that has a structural failure mode she cannot see. The horror is that the competence is real and the failure mode is real and they are not incompatible. The pacing is structural deceleration into catastrophe — not pulp escalation, not ambient suffering, not grimdark indulgence. Each act should feel slower than the one before in the specific way that foreclosure feels: each degree of freedom closing leaves fewer options, and the absence of options feels heavier the later in the arc it accumulates.

**Interiority is heavy.** The book's dread lives between what Taylor sees and what she decides to do about it. The insect-ambient-register is her primary perceptual tool and her primary prose mechanism. What the insects report and what she decides to do with the report is the interior action of the book. Court-intrigue exterior, character-horror interior — but in this project the "court-intrigue" is the Watch-precinct and guild-liaison institutional register, not a noble court. The institutional register is the exterior surface; Taylor's interior is the source of the book's dread.

**No moral narration.** The book does not tell the reader what to think about Taylor's choices. The 6-rung ladder does not carry commentary. The Rung-3 workers receiving nothing is not accompanied by narrative judgment. The Rung-6 contact's death is not accompanied by narrative acknowledgment of what the death means. The final state is the verdict. The reader reconstructs the causal chain from the evidence and arrives at the verdict without being told.

## The Specific Form of This Horror

**Patron-dialect as structural horror.** The horror in this project is not the clinical log's self-erasure (as in flea-bottom-dance). It is the model's operational invisibility: Taylor builds, reports, calibrates, and the reports travel through channels whose momentum she cannot see from inside her range-cap. The horror accumulates in the gap between what she intends and what the patron-channel does with her intent. The gap is never named by Taylor. It is named by the reader.

**Foreclosure as bureaucratic processing.** The specific form of the foreclosure at the book's close is: processed, not executed; detained, not martyred; intake-numbered, not condemned. Taylor is not the protagonist of her own death in this book. She is the subject of an administrative procedure. The mode of death — not dramatic, not violent in the performed sense, bureaucratic — is the structural complement to the mode of her work: building patron-channel infrastructure, one accurate report at a time, using the institutional processing machinery she had available. The machinery runs correctly on her.

**The absence of a villain.** No human in this book is the antagonist. The captain calculates. The steward applies categories. The sweep officer runs procedure. The ward septon holds his pastoral assessment. No one commits a personal act of cruelty against Taylor. The antagonist is the patron-machinery itself: the Watch persons-risk infrastructure plus the Hightower patronage chain plus the Faith-of-the-Seven Green-adjacency — operating through faces whose interiority is closed to Taylor and to the reader. The foreclosure lives in the structure, not in any person's choices.

## Act-Level Tone Specifications

**Act 1 (months 1–12):** Surface calm. The calm belongs to Taylor's perception, not to the world. The faction architecture is already present; she cannot see it from inside her block-cap. The prose in Act 1 should have the specific quality of a person who is building something real, in good faith, without access to the structural information that would change the building. This is not ironic — the good faith is genuine. The reader does not yet have enough information for the dramatic irony to be fully active. Act 1 should feel like competent, earnest groundwork. The dread is retrospective.

**Act 2 (months 13–26):** Terrain-tightening. The cost-per-intervention rises each quarter. Watch cooperation becomes more contingent. Block-network loyalty-splits appear that earlier groundwork didn't map. The Act 2 tone is the specific experience of a working plan meeting increasing resistance from structural conditions the plan doesn't account for. Taylor attributes the tightening to the political temperature. The political temperature is real. The attribution is incomplete. The required recognition-failure beat (Rung 3 enforcement-overshoot) must land at the Act 2 level as a named cost without named cause — the reader sees the model producing the outcome; Taylor sees the steward's category limits producing the outcome.

**Act 3 (months 27–36):** The last pivot missed and the close. Act 3's tone is the specific experience of a structural process completing. Not tragedy-as-fall, not tragedy-as-revelation, but tragedy as completion: the mechanism built over three years running to its final output. Taylor's interior at Rung 6 must register the felt break without naming the model. The book's final pages — the street-crier announcement of Viserys's death, the three days between the contact's detention and Taylor's arrest, the detention, the death — should be rendered at the same procedural tempo as the model's earlier rungs. The tempo is the tonal point: the close does not feel different from the earlier operations because it isn't different. It is the same operation. The difference is the variable it runs on.

## Forbidden Tone Registers

- Pulp escalation: any scene that amps the stakes by adding dramatic violence, personal cruelty, or thriller-pacing to events that should read as institutional procedure.
- Aestheticized bleakness: the Watch guardhouse cold, the cell block dark, the procedural death — these are not to be rendered with literary suffering-appreciation. They are rendered as what they are: the conditions of a bureaucratic hold.
- Warmth in the final arc: no scene in Act 3 should provide Taylor with warmth, community validation, or recognition that she was "right in trying." The foreclosure is structurally honest: the mechanism runs correctly on its builder. No compensatory warmth.
- Stated verdict: no prose articulating the book's moral position. The reader arrives at the verdict. The prose provides the evidence. The evidence is sufficient.
- Taylor as protagonist of her own death: the final image is external, observational, silent (insects reporting from outside the isolation cell). Any version of the close that centers Taylor's internal experience of dying is a structural violation.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-watch-persons-risk-sweep`:** The sweep card defines what must happen. This card defines how it must sound.

**For auditor use:**
- Any scene rendering institutional procedure as personal drama. Tone violation.
- Any scene providing Taylor with Act 3 warmth or community validation. Register violation.
- The book's final image rendered as Taylor's interiority. Structural violation.
- The book's verdict stated in narrative voice. Thematic violation.
- Pulp-pacing in any rung event. Tone violation.
