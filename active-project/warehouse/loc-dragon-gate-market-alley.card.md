## Critical facts for scene use

**Role:** Rung-1 and Rung-2 setting. Ambient block-life geography for the full 36-month duration. ~80m primary working frontage; Taylor has the full alley in range from any central position.

**Layout:** East end (gate-approach adjacent, grain stalls, Watch turning-point). Central alley (established trade row: cloth trader, metalmonger, Mira's workshop south side, short-weight merchant north side, dye-goods trader). West end (interior-city street, quieter, Nolla's water-carrier route). Side passages off alley to rear courtyards (one leads to Daveth's counting-house).

**Sensory vocabulary:** Grain-area: dusty-sweet with sour-waterlogged note (Rung-1 context). Central: metalmonger coal-and-iron, rope-workshop fiber-and-lanolin. Acoustic baseline: metalmonger's hammer. Watch patrol footstep rhythm (the primary institutional-presence indicator). Evening: amber lantern-rope across alley width.

**The insect register:** Full alley in range at 150m diameter with central positioning. Taylor has individuated the alley population to creature-level within 3 months. Water-carrier's twice-daily route, patrol interval, grain-delivery disruption — all mapped.

**Exits:** East end (outer market zone, gate approach, Watch patrol staging yard boundary). West end (interior-city street, at range edge when Taylor positioned at east end). Side passages (one to Daveth's courtyard; others to residential and storage).

**Hazards:** Watch patrol east-end turning-point (Taylor has timed around this from month one). Rung-1 grain stall back-area (scene of first patron-dialect deployment). Rung-2 short-weight stall (north side, central — stall remains after guild fine). Evening lantern-rope (fire risk in wind or poor maintenance).

**Rung-1 mechanics:** Taylor reports the waterlogged grain as riot-risk to the Watch captain — not as illness. First deployment of the patron-dialect model. Physical scene: grain stall back-area storage, east end.

**Rung-2 mechanics:** Taylor reports the short-weight merchant as guild-stability risk. Report is accurate. Outcome: guild fine and partial restitution. Stall remains. The patron-dialect model's second successful pass.
