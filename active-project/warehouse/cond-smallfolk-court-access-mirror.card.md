# Smallfolk-to-Court Access Mirror — Patron Bridge Mechanics (Pre-Dance, ~125 AC)

## Description

How a Flea Bottom smallfolk woman reaches a noble patron's attention and what she can and cannot access as a result. The governing principle: the patron does not bring Taylor to court-level spaces; the patron brings court-level attention to Taylor's location. The consultation runs on Taylor's terrain or at neutral ground. What Taylor can access expands through the patron's umbrella; what she cannot access without the umbrella is precisely the territory where the bad act must occur (the Dragonpit interior) — meaning the access chain for the bad act runs through the patron and must be traced clearly in the narrative.

## Sensory Impact

The access constraints are felt as a specific quality of friction — the hesitation before any upward movement in the city's geography, the calibration exercise Taylor runs before approaching any point above her social level. With the patron's umbrella active, that friction reduces to a specific kind of attention (the retainer's presence, the factor's vouching word) rather than a genuine barrier. Without the umbrella, the friction is a wall.

## Duration

Active from the point of the patron-Taylor relationship's establishment to the point of the patron-collision. After the collision, the patron's umbrella status becomes uncertain; this card governs the umbrella's operational mechanics when it is active.

## How the Patron Bridges the Access Gap

In Westerosi feudal-political practice, a smallfolk person does not climb to a patron — the patron reaches down. The mechanism:

**The factor intermediary**: The patron's household employs factors (practical agents who manage the household's business operations in the city) who operate below the noble-quarter and interact with the lower-city economy as part of their function. A factor can appear in Flea Bottom, conduct business, and carry a message or an invitation without flagging as a notable event. The factor is the first link in the chain.

**The retainer escort**: When the patron wants a direct meeting with Taylor, a retainer (trusted household employee, a man-at-arms of the household's personal guard, not a City Watch figure) appears at Taylor's location and escorts her to the meeting point. The escort is both access-mechanism and status-signal: the retainer's livery (the patron's household insignia, however modest) is what makes Taylor's presence in a given space legitimate to any Watch checkpoint.

**The consultation venue**: The patron does not hold consultations with Taylor at the Red Keep or at any formal court venue. The meetings occur at: the patron's KL premises (a townhouse or rented factor house), the Sept of Baelor's outer courts (neutral and accessible), or — in urgent circumstances — at a market-district inn or factor house with sufficient privacy. The patron comes down to Taylor's accessible range, not the reverse.

**This is structurally load-bearing**: The story's access chain for the Dragonpit interior (the kill location) must pass through the patron's umbrella. Taylor does not enter the Dragonpit alone. She is brought. Any scene implying she enters the Dragonpit independently is a violation of the access physics.

## What Taylor Can Access With the Patron's Umbrella

**With an active patron umbrella, Taylor can access:**

- **The Dragonpit exterior approach**: The patron's retainer escort passes the Watch checkpoint at the Dragonpit road. Taylor can reach the exterior approach — the outer grounds, the access road, the peripheral structures around the main Dragonpit complex.
- **The Dragonpit interior (with direct escort)**: Interior access to the Dragonpit requires either a documented rider or the escort of a household that has authorized access. The patron's household has such access as a Rhaenyra-adjacent (Black-faction) noble family. Taylor enters the interior specifically as a member of the patron's party, not as an independent actor.
- **The opposite-number**: The patron's arrangement of a meeting with the opposite-number (for the purposes of the patron's amplification-theory experiments) is the mechanism through which Taylor learns of the opposite-number's existence and enters their social proximity. Taylor cannot reach the opposite-number independently — the opposite-number moves in noble circles Taylor cannot enter unescorted.
- **The cousin (cost-bearer)**: Similarly, Taylor's contact with the patron's collateral cousin (the cost-bearer child) runs through the patron's household social space. The patron brings Taylor into contact with the household; the child is present in that space. Taylor's care-adjacent relationship with the child is not an independent attachment but an outgrowth of patron-household proximity.
- **Neutral meeting spaces**: The Sept's outer courts, market-district factor houses, and similar spaces are accessible with or without the patron's umbrella, but the patron's umbrella makes the meetings less visible (a retainer's escort naturalizes Taylor's presence in any of these spaces).

## What Taylor Cannot Access Without the Patron's Umbrella

**Taylor cannot independently access:**

- Any formal court venue (the Red Keep's halls, the Small Council's operational spaces, any room in the Red Keep beyond the public entry areas)
- Any Targaryen household's premises without an explicit invitation mediated through the patron
- The Dragonpit's interior operational areas (where the dragons are actually housed) without accompanying escort from a household with documented access
- Direct communication with noble-tier figures without a factor or retainer intermediary
- The patron's formal political operations (the patron brings Taylor information; Taylor does not participate in the patron's political planning sessions)

**Why these constraints matter for the story:**

The access constraints ensure that Taylor's involvement in the Dragonpit scene (the kill) is structurally mediated by the patron's umbrella. The patron brought her. The patron's wrong-theory-of-Taylor is what made the patron bring her to the Dragonpit in the first place. The patron's instrumental use of Taylor is what created the access to the bad act's location. When the consequences of the bad act unfold, the patron cannot avoid complicity in the access that made it possible — this is the structural seam where the collision lives.

## The Access Chain: Flea Bottom to Dragonpit Interior

For auditor use — the complete chain that must be narratively present or implied:

1. Taylor in Flea Bottom, operating in the informal economy (weeks of labor-web presence)
2. Factor intermediary appearing on the patron's behalf (first contact)
3. Initial consultation meeting at neutral ground or the patron's accessible premises
4. Establishment of the patron's working theory (amplification hypothesis) through early meetings
5. Retainer escort to the Dragonpit exterior approach (first dragon-proximity access)
6. Dragon-proximity session: patron observes Taylor's reaction, interprets as theory confirmation
7. Retainer escort to Dragonpit interior (the operational areas) for the bad act's staging
8. The bad act occurs inside the patron's umbrella and with the patron's instrumental authorization

Each link in this chain must be traceable. If any link is missing, the bad act's causal responsibility is ambiguous — which is cost-evasion (world-notes.md, binding: "the causal chain from Taylor's kill → wrong-rider outcome must be UNAMBIGUOUS in the rendered story").

## Patron-Taylor Consultation Format

The operational format of Taylor-patron consultations, derived from the access physics:

**Terrain**: The patron's premises (a KL townhouse or rented factor space) or neutral ground (Sept outer courts). Not the patron's primary court-level residence if that residence is at or near the Red Keep.

**Escort**: Always a retainer escort for Taylor. The patron does not meet Taylor publicly without this intermediary layer — the meeting is arranged, not spontaneous.

**Communication mode**: The patron speaks through the same factor/retainer system for routine communication (messages, schedule changes). Direct meetings are arranged, not ongoing. Taylor does not have open access to the patron's household.

**The asymmetry**: The patron has full access to Taylor's operational space; Taylor has limited access to the patron's. This asymmetry is standard for the relationship type (noble patron / smallfolk asset) and is part of the structural basis for the patron's wrong-theory — the patron can observe Taylor in the patron's controlled settings but cannot verify Taylor's behavior in settings the patron does not control.

The retainer's livery is a credential the Watch reads. This card describes the mechanism; the feudal-physics card describes the institutional framework the mechanism operates within.

**With `cond-patron-amplification-theory-mirror`:** The patron's umbrella is what enables the bad act. The amplification-theory is why the patron created the umbrella in the first place. Both cards are load-bearing for understanding the causal chain.

Any scene where Taylor independently arranges meetings with the opposite-number or the cost-bearer cousin without patron mediation is a violation. Any scene where the patron holds court-level consultations with Taylor in formal Red Keep spaces is a violation.
