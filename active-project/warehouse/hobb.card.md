# Hobb — the Cassandra witness

*Live span-A canon. One recurring figure across the books: the still-room boy (I.2/I.4) who is mocked and
"transferred" → ends up a City Watch guardsman (III.2). The transfer IS the bridge between his Book-I and
Book-III appearances. Full profile context: `intake/character-profiles.md` (Hobb / the Cassandra witness).*

## Description

A low servant — the still-room boy who keeps the maester's inventory; later, by way of the transfer, a
City Watch guardsman. The realm's least-credited eyes, and ⭐ the spine's designated truth-teller: he keeps
**accurately** seeing the impossible (the jewelry-biting, the wall-climbing, the dropped poison-levels) and
is **never believed.** "The boy who cried demon" — twice, correctly. His arc: be right, be ignored, stop
reporting, transfer as far from her as he can get.

## Voice

Plain smallfolk Common Tongue, increasingly flat. Starts earnest; ends resigned. *"I counted right. The
levels are down. The child glows. I'm not saying it again."* The register **flattens by degrees** across
the books — earnest → mocked → deadpan-defeated → silent.

## Taste

- Getting the count right (his one pride).
- A quiet post, far from the princess.
- Being believed, once — which he learns to stop wanting.

## Pet Peeves

### Being right and punished for it
**Description:** every accurate report earns him ridicule and a worse posting. The system removes its one
honest witness. **Severity:** blocker (it ends his reporting).

### The word he has no language for
**Description:** he sees exactly what happens and has no way to say it that doesn't sound insane, and no
standing to be heard. **Severity:** strong.

## Stats

- **age:** a youth/young man across the span.
- **origin:** smallfolk; still-room boy → (transferred) City Watch.
- **arc:** right → ignored → punished → transferred → silent (by III.2 he sees the rooftop "ghost" and says
  nothing).

## Relationships

- **Gael** — the thing he keeps accurately seeing and is never believed about; he never learns *what* she is,
  only that he is always right and always punished for saying so.
- **Maester Lorren** — his nominal superior at the still-room; the maester documents up the chain while
  Hobb is mocked down it (the two halves of "truth surfaces, the explanation never does").

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose
The human form of the series' law: **truth surfaces; the explanation never does.** His attrition into
silence is a curdle in miniature — the comedy is the structural repetition (right-ignored-punished), the
weight is that the system works *as designed* by removing its only honest witness.

### Hard Fences
1. **He is accurate, every time** — never a fool or a liar; the comedy/tragedy is that accuracy is punished.
2. **Never learns the truth** — he sees the *what,* never the *why.*
3. **One recurring figure** — the still-room boy and the III.2 watchman are the same Hobb (the transfer is
   the bridge).

### Action Menu
- **The report** — accurate, specific, disbelieved.
- **The count** — he gets it right; the levels are down; nobody acts.
- **The silence** — by III.2, having learned, he sees and says nothing.

### 🎭 Running gag — *right, ignored, punished* (escalating, not flat)
Each report is *more precisely correct* and earns *more ridicule and worse postings;* his register flattens
toward deadpan defeat. **Escalation, not a loop:** the one time someone half-believes him it's the wrong
person at the wrong moment (it accelerates the cage), so the lesson he draws is *stop reporting.* By III.2
the watch is *commended* for the discipline the "ghost" instills — pinned on the very captain who once
mocked and transferred him — and Hobb, on that watch, sees her plainly and **says nothing.**

### Off-Screen Cadence
Counting stock; being scolded; drinking a little; later, walking a watch route, keeping his eyes down,
deciding again not to report what he saw.

## Vibe Seeds

- He learned the rational lesson the hard way: in this realm, the truth is not a thing you are rewarded for
