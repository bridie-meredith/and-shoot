# Monument: Conquest-Charter Language

## What this monument is

A behavior-pack monument family for Taylor Hebert's memory-flags system. Names the class of displacement-cue that fires when Taylor encounters Westerosi institutional language — edicts, charter-terms, declarations, struck decisions — and recognizes the resonance with prior-world experience of words that outlive the authority that made them.

Monument class: Westerosi-clamp (not an Earth-Bet displacement; a Planetos-specific historical resonance). The monument is in the texture of the world Taylor now inhabits, not in what she left.

## Monument trigger

A formal decision inscribed, struck, or annulled — any moment where a word is written down and then undone, superseded, overwritten, or where language is shown to have persisted past the hand that set it. The trigger is the specific quality of Westerosi institutional speech: conquest edicts that remain law after the conqueror dies, succession charters that travel through dynastic crises, treaty-language that outlives the treaty.

Taylor recognizes this shape because her prior experience includes language of exactly this kind — words that structured lives, defined enemies, established obligations, and then continued to function as if the author still lived to enforce them. She does not name the prior-world source. She recognizes the shape.

## Displacement-cue form

The cue must:
- Produce the shape of the monument without naming the Earth-Bet source (e.g., "a tongue that outlived the hand that set it").
- Connect the Westerosi institutional act to Taylor's prior recognition of the same pattern.
- Not introduce Earth-Bet proper nouns, dates, or named events.
- Fire on the resonance between the specific Planetos act and the general monument-family, not on the act itself.

The cue can reference: charter language, struck decisions, edicts that travel, words that function without their authors.

## Rubric positioning

Memory-flags quiet-beat discipline: this monument prefers settle-tail or scene-boundary fires. Peak-bone fires require explicit displacement-clamp construction AND defensible resonance-not-action argument (see rubric-memory-flags §Licensing-discipline / peak-bone exception).

Per-scene cap: 1 fire per scene maximum. Doubled-register requirement: must appear alongside at least one Earth-Bet displacement monument in the same file.
