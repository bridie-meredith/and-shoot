# Patron Dialect Operational Model — Dragon Gate Foreclosure (126–129 AC)

## Description

The operational belief Taylor carries and uses across all six rungs of the 6-rung ladder: "speaking the patron's dialect" — the practice of calibrating true reports into patron-register language so that accurate information reaches the people who can act on it effectively. The model treats patron categories (Watch riot-risk, guild stability-risk, steward sedition-risk, Watch persons-risk) as NEUTRAL TRANSMISSION CHANNELS rather than as ENFORCEMENT MECHANISMS WITH THEIR OWN MOMENTUM.

This is the mechanical spine of the foreclosure. It is what Taylor does, not what she thinks about doing. She does not name the model. She does not reflect on it. The model is invisible to her as a model; it is legible to her only as calibration, as accuracy, as knowing your audience.

**Auditor primary load card.** Every scene at or near a rung event must load this card and verify: (a) the model is operating without Taylor naming it; (b) the framing choice she makes is accurate as a true report; (c) the patron-category she delivers to is not evaluated by Taylor for its own momentum; (d) the model's output is not re-attributed by Taylor to the model — it is attributed to the patron's category limits or the patron's enforcement overshoot.

## Sensory Impact

None for Taylor. The model has no sensory expression Taylor can access. It is invisible as a structural feature of her cognition. Its presence is felt in the prose as a slight precision in her framing choices — the reports that are always slightly more categorically crisp than the facts strictly require — that the reader can see and Taylor cannot name.

The model's downstream consequences have sensory impact: the dockworkers receive nothing (Rung 3); the contact dies (Rung 6). These are the external sensory events. The model itself is absence: the absence of Taylor examining the gap between her intent and the patron's action.

## Duration

Persistent across all three acts and all six rungs. The model does not change. It does not degrade or escalate. It applies identical cognitive structure to each rung. The rungs vary in target, scale, and patron-channel; the model is constant. This constancy is what makes the 6-rung ladder a ladder rather than a series of unconnected events.

The model's first failure of registration — the felt break at Rung 6 — is interior-only and must not be articulated as recognition of the model. See §Model Falsification section below.

## The Six-Rung Ladder

All six rungs deploy the same model. The ladder is binding and fully specified. Deviations in rung sequence, target, or patron channel are auditor-flagged fence violations.

**Rung 1 (Month 4 — seed rung):**
- Target: tanner's apprentice's grain-watering practice (a public-health problem, not a security problem)
- Framing: riot-risk (the Watch captain's category)
- Channel: Watch captain
- Outcome: small clean success — the practice is addressed, the immediate harm is resolved
- Model function: first deployment; establishes the model as working; Taylor experiences this as: accurate information delivered in patron-register produces the correct outcome; the framing felt like a translation choice
- Auditor check: the riot-risk framing is accurate in the sense that public-health failure in a gate district is a riot-risk factor; it is not the most accurate framing; the most accurate framing (illness-risk) is not the Watch captain's active category; Taylor's choice is calibration, not fabrication

**Rung 2 (Months 9–10):**
- Target: merchant short-weight practice (a consumer-fraud problem, not a guild-stability problem)
- Framing: guild-stability risk (the guild liaison's category)
- Channel: guild liaison
- Outcome: merchant fined; buyers see partial restitution
- Model function: second deployment; success; Taylor experiences the model as confirmed by evidence across two patron-channels
- Auditor check: the guild-stability framing is accurate as a long-run prediction; it is not the closest available frame; Taylor's choice is still calibration, not fabrication

**Rung 3 (Months 14–15 — REQUIRED ENFORCEMENT-OVERSHOOT BEAT):**
- Target: dockworker slowdown (a wage-dispute, not a sedition-adjacent problem)
- Framing: sedition-adjacent (the lord's steward's category)
- Channel: lord's steward (Aldric Fenwick)
- Outcome: slowdown ends; workers receive nothing; Taylor BLAMES THE STEWARD'S CATEGORY LIMITS, NOT HER OWN FRAMING
- Model function: enforcement-overshoot seed planted; the steward's sedition-adjacent tools are not wage-dispute tools; the workers receive nothing because the model delivered the report into a category that cannot produce a wage-dispute resolution; Taylor does not register this as a model failure — she registers it as a category-limits problem
- REQUIRED PROSE BEAT: the steward's visible calculation (the one moment rendered as behavior) shows him applying the sedition-adjacent category correctly; his decision is not cruel; it is procedurally accurate for the category it received; the overshoot is structural, not personal
- Auditor check: Taylor must be shown registering the workers-receive-nothing outcome AND attributing it to the steward's category limits; she must NOT attribute it to her framing; she must NOT show awareness that the framing was the cause; any prose that shows her privately acknowledging the framing as the cause at Rung 3 is a fence violation (the recognition is reserved for Rung 6 interior-only)

**Rung 4 (Months 18–19 — MIRA RUNG):**
- Target: smuggling rumor about the Black-adjacent contact (the contact's cross-faction movement pattern, real and documented)
- Framing: Watch-risk language (framed to Mira, who acts at Watch level)
- Channel: Mira — the genuine-attachment, NOT an institutional patron; this is the model operating on a person Taylor values
- Outcome: contact protected off-record; Taylor sees the model "working" on someone she cares about
- Model function: the most dangerous rung; the model succeeds while operating on a non-institutional-patron channel; Taylor registers this as evidence that the model works even in the informal register; the fact that she used the model on her genuine attachment without noticing that she used the model on her genuine attachment is the foreclosure mechanism at Rung 4
- REQUIRED SHOOT-LEVEL CONSTRAINT (from dramatist): the business-partner's CONCRETE MUTUAL DEPENDENCY with the contact must be established in this rung's scene; not co-presence; the Rung-6 absence is the cost; the cost only registers if Rung 4 did the bond-and-stakes work
- Auditor check: Mira must act on the Watch-level framing, not on a personal favor; the model must be shown working through Mira, not around her; Taylor must not register that she used the model on Mira; Mira's protection of the contact must be a Watch-level action, not a warmth action

**Rung 5 (Month 24):**
- Target: unlicensed physician's treatment practice (unlicensed-practice problem, not a poisoning problem)
- Framing: poisoning-risk (the guild auditor's category, for records access)
- Channel: guild auditor
- Outcome: physician suspended pending review
- Model function: the model produces an outcome that is harmful to the physician (suspension) in service of getting records access; Taylor experiences this as an acceptable cost for the access; she does not register the physician's suspension as equivalent to the dockworkers' outcome at Rung 3; she attributes the suspension to the auditor's enforcement threshold, not her framing
- Auditor check: the physician's suspension must be rendered as a consequence Taylor is aware of but does not fully internalize; the model's pattern should be visible to the reader by Rung 5 as an accumulating structure; Taylor must not yet register the accumulation

**Rung 6 (Months 27–28 — RECOGNITION-FAILURE BEAT / CLOSING RUNG):**
- Target: Black-adjacent contact's border-crossing pattern (real, documented, persons-risk-relevant by Watch category)
- Framing: smuggling-of-persons-risk (Watch-persons-risk category, for record-review access)
- Channel: Watch officer (persons-risk function) — NOT the captain directly; the report travels through the correct chain
- Outcome: contact detained; detention triggers Watch sweep escalation Taylor did not author and cannot interrupt; contact killed in custody during the wider M27–28 Watch sweep of persons-risk detainees; THE NAMED IRREVERSIBLE COST
- Model function: the model runs correctly on the most accurate framing Taylor has ever delivered; the contact's border-crossing pattern IS a persons-risk indicator; the Watch persons-risk protocol runs correctly on it; Taylor cannot interrupt the protocol once the detention begins because the protocol's momentum is institutional and the patron-channel she used has no reverse gear
- INTERIOR REQUIREMENT: at Rung 6, Taylor's interior must register the collapse of the model — the felt break, the moment "something has gone wrong that my dialect cannot name." This is INTERIOR ONLY. It must NOT be articulated as "her reports were heard as authorizations, not calibrations." See §Model Falsification section.

## Model Falsification (Interior-Only, Rung 6)

**What must happen:** At Rung 6, Taylor's interior must register the break — the felt collapse of the operational model. Something has gone wrong. She does not have words for what has gone wrong. She cannot name the mechanism.

**What must NOT happen:** The prose must NOT articulate the meta-statement that her reports were heard as authorizations rather than calibrations. The recognition is reader-inference from the 6-rung consequence chain, not Taylor's stated conclusion. This is non-negotiable per literary-snob C4 (theme-as-structure, not theme-as-statement).

**Auditor enforcement:** Any prose where Taylor articulates the model's failure mechanism — any version of "I was not calibrating, I was authorizing," "the patron channel was not neutral," "I was sending instructions, not information" — is a C4 fence violation. Flag immediately. The violation is the most critical prose failure mode for this project.

**What is permitted in Taylor's interior at Rung 6:**
- The felt break — a sensory/cognitive event with no available label
- The specific wrongness — this outcome is not like the prior outcomes; there is a shape to the wrongness she cannot name
- The insect-ambient-register of the contact's last hours, rendered externally through her range-cap: no interiority available, only the ambient sense of presence-to-absence
- The attribution failure: she tries to attribute the outcome to the persons-risk protocol's momentum; the attribution doesn't close the felt break

**What is not permitted:**
- The meta-recognition stated as Taylor's conclusion
- Any formulation that closes the felt break into understanding
- Any formulation that would allow Taylor to say "I see now what I was doing"

The reader sees the pattern across six rungs. Taylor sees the outcome at Rung 6 and cannot name what she sees. The gap between reader-recognition and Taylor-recognition is the book's central irony and its structural horror.

## The Wrong Pivot at Months 27–28

Rung 6 IS the wrong pivot that closes the last viable choice. Taylor took this rung BECAUSE the model worked at Rung 4 (Mira confirmed the model works in the informal register). The pivot was available — she could have used the Faith channel, the foreign-merchant channel, the dockyard-guild channel, or could have not pursued the border-crossing intelligence at all. She did not see the pivot as a pivot because the model told her this was just another rung.

**Auditor enforcement:** Any scene implying Taylor was unaware she had alternatives at Rung 6 is a fence violation. She had alternatives. She did not use them because the model's prior success made the Rung-6 application feel like the correct calibration. The foreclosure is structural: the model's accumulated credibility is what removes the pivot from Taylor's visible decision space.
