## Top three flags

- **The rule-break is the chapter and it's underlit.** "The propagation went where she had told it to go, and the telling was a thing she would not name" — this is the load-bearing beat. She declared the rule three paragraphs earlier. She broke it under pressure. That deserves its own line, its own breath, its own recoil. Right now it's syntactically equal to the handcart geometry. Promote it.
- **Geometry-prose fatigue.** "Angle-gap," "angle-wall," "lane-mouth," "morning geometry," "the geometry of it" — the noun stack is doing atmosphere at the cost of momentum. I skim. Cut by a third and the tactical beats hit harder.
- **Oswyn's recognition is told twice in the same paragraph.** "The chin lifts the way it lifts in the country's older stories..." and then "The elder lifted his chin, the elder had taken the lane-mouth a week before he would have the word for it" — same beat, two passes, and the country's-older-stories framing is doing work I don't have context for yet. One clean pass on him noticing her. Save the mythic framing for when I've earned it.
