## Cluster notes

- **Dense-paragraph cluster (P1, P4, P5, P8, P9, P10):** All share the same architecture — italic prologue, three lyric interior paragraphs, then two large block paragraphs handling rescue and aftermath. They differ only by which fragments of repeated language they over- or under-emphasize. They read like minor variants of one template.
- **Sectioned-with-breaks cluster (P2, P3, P12):** Use horizontal rules or extra paragraph air to isolate the rescue dialogue from the surrounding setup. This cluster reads measurably faster and clearer; the same words breathe differently with structural whitespace around the speech acts.
- **First-person-leaning cluster (P6, P8):** Both lean harder on the "I" register through the middle of the scene. P6 commits to it and gains voice; P8 oscillates back into third-person and loses it.
- **Repetition-of-formula cluster (P1, P11):** Both repeat the "chin-lift filed her in a category" and "the chin lifts the way it lifts in the country's older stories" sentences nearly verbatim within a few lines of each other, producing a stuck-loop feeling at the close.

## Outlier notes

- **P7 is the strongest outlier on the low end.** It's roughly a third the length of the others, missing the italic prologue and the three rule-of-the-game interior paragraphs entirely. As an event sequence it parses; as a chapter it reads like notes. It also contains the only clear pronoun error in the set ("Oswyn lifted her chin").
- **P2 is the strongest outlier on the high end** on the dimension of *pacing*. It is the only variant that lets the three dialogue lines exist on their own breaths and that puts a structural break between the held-count opening, the rescue, and the post-rescue recognition. Same materials, different cadence — and the cadence wins.
- **P11 is an outlier on overstatement.** Most variants end on the ward's quiet registration; P11 ends on a sentence that names the antagonist-pressure ("witch-label assembling, stage one") in the narrative voice. The rest of the chapter earns that read; P11 announces it.
- **P6 is an outlier on voice confidence.** It contains the chapter's strongest single sentence about the protagonist's transgression ("the prohibition cracked without being acknowledged as cracking... before permission") and treats the elder's recognition as the elder's interior accounting rather than as omniscient narration.

## What mattered most

Across these twelve renderings of an identical event sequence, the dimension that moved variants up or down the ranking most reliably was **pacing through whitespace** — whether the prose allowed structural air between the held-count opening, the rescue, and the post-rescue recognition. Every variant had access to the same vivid materials (the drain water, the tallow smoke, the chin-lift, the eleven-year-old who watches cloth before she cuts it). What separated the top three from the bottom three was not which materials were present but whether the rescue dialogue was given room to land or was packed inside a 200-word block alongside the crowd compression, the insect propagation, and the world-fact glosses. The bottom of the ranking is dominated by variants that either ran the same image through a second time within a paragraph (P1, P11) or compressed three speech acts into one undifferentiated list (P5), and the top is dominated by variants that simply put a blank line between the count holding and the cart blocking. Sensory richness, interior monologue, and metaphor variation all mattered less than I expected; cadence mattered more.
