## 2. The load-bearing finding

**For chapters where the substrate is system-description (compound-noun-heavy, parallel-clause bones), the user's taste wants the voice to RESIST the substrate, not amplify it.**

V2 porter-active and V0 baseline both *respected* the bones' inherent cadence — porter's clipped "I lifted them. I moved them. I set them down." rhythm transferred straight into Taylor's "I extended the range. I drew the line. I held at the ceiling." chain, which is exactly the metronome Fork B postop named as the failure mode. V0 with no prime defaulted to the bones' own rhythm because nothing was counterweighting it.

V1 market-observational and V3 dusk-reticent both *intervened* against the bones' rhythm — variance in sentence length, embodied digressions, and the willingness to let a quiet sensory line do paragraph-of-statement work. V1 did this with the most discipline; V3 did it but lapsed into double-naming recognitions ("the line drawn here is the one that will be asked of me again; I did not know that yet").

This generalizes the lesson the cross-persona experiment hinted at: the prime's job is not to "match" the chapter's register but to *complement* the bones' weaknesses. A coverage-map chapter's bones will be compound-noun-heavy by necessity (the chapter is about the map); a prime that respects-and-transmits that load produces prose that becomes the map. A prime that counterweights produces prose where the person reading the map stays visible.

## 4. Pairwise differentials (3 most informative)

**V2 porter-active vs V1 market-observational** (rank-4 vs rank-1; opposite voice outcomes on identical bones)

Same event sequence, opposite outcomes. V2 lets parallelism eat the narrator; V1 breaks parallelism deliberately ("Then the alley-back went silent" / "I extended the range anyway"). The prime mechanism that produced V2 appears to *amplify* the bones' inherent cadence; the one that produced V1 *counterweights* it. **For a chapter this compound-noun-heavy, counterweight wins.** This is the load-bearing finding.

**V0 baseline vs V1 market-observational** (rank-3 vs rank-1; closest in quality)

V0 has the single best sensory sentence in the field (the drain-angle geometry); V1 has the best embodiment ("the skull knew the price"). V0 loses on the three-"line" closer; V1 closes on "the map had its first name for that node" — singular, resolved, not gestural. **Mechanism difference**: V0 preserves clause-level physicality but inherits the bones' closing-cadence; V1 restructures the close. The prime's value is concentrated at endings.

**V3 dusk-reticent vs V1 market-observational** (rank-2 vs rank-1; closest in intention)

Both reach for embodiment via the hand. V3 over-symbolizes the hand and double-names the recognition. V1 holds the same image to one beat. **This is the cleanest demonstration that the prime can either trust an image once or compulsively re-explain it.** An inward-weighed prime (V3) drifted toward theme-statement; an observational-with-hands prime (V1) stayed disciplined.

## 5. Recommendation

**Keep the V1 market-observational exemplar wired** at `active-project/theater/voice-exemplar-b01-c02.md`. The current wiring is the tournament winner.

**Do NOT promote V1 to series-level default** (`active-project/voice-exemplar.md`) yet — V1 was authored specifically against c02's compound-noun-heavy substrate; whether it generalizes to chapters with different substrate-rhythm (dialogue-heavy chapters, action chapters, court-political chapters) is unknown. The library Robinson voice-exemplar.md remains the project default; V1 stays per-chapter for now. Promote after K chapters of consistent winning per the proposal-tournament-mechanism deployment plan.

**Caveats** flagged for future tournaments:
- V3's failure mode (inward register drifting into theme-statement) suggests inward primes need an explicit "no double-naming recognition" fence in the prime authoring guidance.
- V2's failure mode (clipped active rhythm amplifying bones' metronome) suggests the prime's cadence-shape should be evaluated AGAINST the bones' default-cadence-shape, not in the abstract. A prime that "matches" the chapter's energy may be exactly the wrong prime.
- V0's strong rank-3 finish suggests "no prime is better than the wrong prime" — a useful prior for chapters where no clearly-superior exemplar has been authored: ship un-primed rather than ship with a register-mismatched prime.
