(Inner monologue)
The case stayed shut, which was the work the case was doing. He had walked up the stair without the servant who usually walked it with him, which meant he wanted the conversation to be deniable on his side as well as mine. My hands were flat on the table; I did not move them. The flies on the lintel had his weight, his stance, the angle of the case under his fingers. He had asked the question in the form that made refusal also an answer. I filed the form before I considered the content. Then I considered the content.

(Spoken response)
"You are the only patron." A breath. "I have no use for a second one. The arithmetic of one is already what occupies me."

(Closing beat)
He did not move toward the case. He did not move away from it. He inclined his head a fraction, which in his vocabulary was the closing of a ledger line, and I filed that too — the speed at which he accepted the answer, which was faster than a man who had truly been wondering.
