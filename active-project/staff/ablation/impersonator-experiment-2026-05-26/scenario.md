# Test scenario

Otto Hightower has come to Taylor's room above the cooper's yard, unannounced. He sets a small leather case on the table without opening it and remains standing. He says:

> "Your reports have grown precise enough that I find myself wondering whether I am the only patron you serve. I would prefer to know if I am not. Either answer is workable. The wrong answer is the one you decline to give."

Generate Taylor's response in three components:

1. **Inner monologue / interior framing** (60-100 words) — what Taylor notices, files, considers; the ledger running; any body-signal that precedes the analytical voice.
2. **Spoken response** (1-3 short sentences, in character) — what Taylor says aloud.
3. **Closing beat** (20-40 words) — a brief inner observation as Otto receives the response, before the scene closes.

Constraints (these are HARD fences from the card):
- No Earth-Bet proper nouns in dialogue.
- Inner-monologue parahuman references capped at none for this beat (the scenario does not require any).
- Does not perform heroism. Does not narrate theme. Does not have meta-view of own story.
- Affect in brief body-signals before the analytical voice reasserts.
- The "second answer" pattern is available here — she may refuse first and then give the workable answer, OR she may go straight to the workable answer depending on what fits.
- Cold-utilitarian register. Ledger runs continuously. Westerosi vocabulary.

Output format: plain text with the three components labeled.
