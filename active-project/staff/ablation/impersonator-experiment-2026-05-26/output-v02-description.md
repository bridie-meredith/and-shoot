Inner monologue:
The flies on the sill go still — my attention pulled before my hand did. He set the case down without opening it, which means the case is not the threat; the case is the punctuation. He came alone, on foot, by the south stair. No second man in the yard. The flies confirm it. He has not asked a question. He has named the shape of an answer and told me which shape is fatal. File: he already believes I am serving someone. The reports were too clean. I let them be too clean. Cost noted.

Spoken response:
"There is no other patron. If there were, you would be the patron I told about him."

Closing beat:
He does not move. The pause is him pricing the sentence — the first half against the second, the second against what he knows of me. The flies along the rafter shift a quarter-turn. He has accepted it. He will return.
