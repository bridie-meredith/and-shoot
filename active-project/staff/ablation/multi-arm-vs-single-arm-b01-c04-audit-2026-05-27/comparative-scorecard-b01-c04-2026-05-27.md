## Pairwise differentials

- **Rank-1 (P3) vs Rank-4 (P1): max spread.** Structural difference is staging vs. aggregation. P3 commits to small physical facts — chest-height, inner seam of coat, the wall opposite the rope-walks, the brim-touch — and lets the cost / acceptance / network-expansion read off the staging. P1 aggregates events into single recognition-sentences that announce the shape of what is being recognized ("the steady recognition that the shape was a shape that would hold the next cycle and the cycle after that"). P3 trusts the bones; P1 narrates the meaning of the bones.

- **Rank-1 (P3) vs Rank-2 (P2): closest at top, deciding edge.** Both have person-in-voice and some embodiment. P2 has the single best embodied cost-moment ("shoulders set into it, breath shortened against it"). P3 has consistent embodiment across the chapter — the handoff is staged, Wren is acted upon (not just observed), the spatial choices in the cooper's yard are reasoned. P3 wins on Reward #6 (setup→payoff actually dramatized at the receipt-display) and Reward #7 (commits to specifics where P2 still falls back on compound-noun proxies in scenes 2 and 3).

- **Rank-3 (P4) vs Rank-4 (P1): closest at bottom, distinguishable?** Yes — distinguishable on opposite failure modes. P4 fails by list-decomposition: §64–68 turns the report's contents into an enumerated inventory of categories, and the "no prefect-weight; no household-guard register; no court-tier register" triple-negative is the most explicit fake-closure in the set. P1 fails by aggregation: serial "and" coordination glues events into single 60-word sentences that the narrator then names the significance of. P4's listing makes the rendering legible at the cost of dramatization; P1's aggregation buries the dramatization in syntactic accumulation. Both miss the same craft target — let events earn their meaning — but along different vectors.

All four variants share the same bones-vocabulary fingerprint (compound-noun saturation, the "I mapped X" / "the feed returned Y" call-cadence, the ledger preamble) which suggests the upstream bones are doing more naming-of-meaning than the rubric tolerates — the stitch-mode can mitigate but not erase the tic-density baseline. The spread P3→P1 maps cleanly onto a single editorial axis (commit to staged physical specifics vs. aggregate into recognition-sentences), and the strongest variant is the one that most aggressively breaks the bones' nominalization habit and substitutes hand-heights, wall-positions, and brim-touches.
