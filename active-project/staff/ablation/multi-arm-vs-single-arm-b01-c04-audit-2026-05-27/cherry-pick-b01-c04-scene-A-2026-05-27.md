## Assembled cherry-pick scene

The tallow-damp off the lane-caulking reached me before the yard did. The cooper's workers held the smallfolk-hours murmur at the near shed, the third bell already gone quiet, the predawn flat. I walked into the yard and took the shed-wall and put the timber at my back and the open ground between me and the lane-mouth across, because three things at once is what the work asks, and the shed-wall asked for two of them, and the lane-mouth asked for the third.

The feed had him a span before the lane-mouth did. The gait that came in through the count was the gait already mapped, settled in its weight, settled in its step-interval, and the feed returned him to me a moment before the lane-mouth gave him to my eye. Jarvis Coin entered the lane-mouth.

I said, "Yes. The terms hold with two changes. You will have patterns — what moves through the streets, what does not, where the seams sit — not raw report of who said what to whom. The interval is mine to set. The volume is mine to set."

The yes went across the yard-air in the cadence of a settled count, parameter carried, prohibition still held. My hand closed on the shed-wall at my back.

I held my feet.

He said, "The man I serve takes those terms. The volume yours. The interval yours. The substance pattern-reports from the wards you walk. Same place. First bell. Three days."

The cadence carried receipt without the conditional that would have left the yes open. The receipt-form settled before my body finished holding.

The feed held the hook-range around him.

Jarvis Coin exited the lane-mouth.

I exited the cooper's yard.
