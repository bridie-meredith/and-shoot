Late morning shifted the air at the junction-mouth before the lane gave itself a name. The tallow-damp off the lane-caulking thinned and went over to a heavier register, the middens-discard sitting on the throat, the three alleys feeding the junction at the lane's near end and the discard-air sitting heaviest where they met.

I entered Pig Tallow Lane — the first Flea Bottom ward beyond the Hook, named for the rendering trade no other would carry, three alleys feeding a waste-middens junction at its mouth.

The work extended. The insect-range opened one ward outward, and the suppression-load that came with it settled at the weight I had already priced for it, and the feed widened, and the held weight beneath my breath did not move.

I mapped the junction-agitation. The discard-air carried the body-count without the bodies; the count came in through the feed at the cadence of three alleys feeding one mouth.

A carter parked the middens cart at the near alley. Penny-a-barrel work; the wheel-creak settled and went quiet.

The feed returned the gait before the body did. The step-interval came in already mapped, settled in its weight, the routing accounting for him before the eye took him in — Oswyn Mudway, the elder. I did not let the next thought finish.

I mapped the interval. Step-cadence, lane-position, range-from-junction; the count took him and held him at the reach the work had set.

Pig Tallow Lane returned ward-tier bodies only at the count I held. No prefect-weight, no household-guard register; only the foot-traffic the ward carried in its own hours.

The stitch-house frames marked the second ward. Frame-pitch and frame-spacing came back through the feed at the cadence of a lane that lived by needle-work, and the second-ward boundary settled at the reach.

The feed returned Wren.

I held my feet.

I mapped the second-ward junction. Frame-pitch, lane-mouth-width, the reach the work had taken; the count carried the ward at the same hand that had carried the first, and I did not turn the count toward the stitch-house lane.
