*The crowd had broken open around the stitch-maker's ward, and the hands had gone up before mine did. After that I went back to the drain angle and did not feed anyone for three weeks. I held the count at its edge by the cost of holding it. What I did instead was start to listen — at first because subsistence-reading is what survives in the Hook, and then because the listening had a shape I had begun to extend on purpose. The rule was the same rule. Reading was not directing. I had not crossed the line.*

What I had built I did not yet have a name for. The flies and beetles told me where heat clustered and where foot-traffic knotted. I held a count of bodies in my head — who was sick, who was hungry, which alley junction emptied before dark and which one never did. I called it a coverage map when I was being plain with myself; I called it an accounting when I was not. The ledger was where it closed at end of day. The rule was that I read and did not direct. That was the line. The instrument was harm-reduction. That was how it stayed permitted.

The flies came back with heat-signatures. The beetles came back with the threshold-count. I left the drain angle — the gap where the two tenement walls pinch, the farthest south point, where every approach along the forty paces of lane arrives before it arrives — and put the fever-cluster out ahead of me: three signatures across two alleys, the day's unresolved load, a price to be paid before sundown or not at all.

The insects fanned the alleys. The ward delivered the heat-signatures and I took the alley-mouth, the lane-mouth opening line-of-sight both ways — into the lane behind, out to the alley-traffic ahead. The insects crossed the threshold-stones. They held four hundred bodies in the feed.

Then the alley-back went silent. The dropout came before my head turned; it was the receipt for the range I had not yet committed to take. I extended anyway. The four-hundred ceiling was already paid for in the back of my skull — the cost rising with the range, landing as a felt pressure, somatic and unmistakable, the billing threshold announced in the body before the map confirmed it. I drew the line at the ceiling and not past it — the same prohibition I had set myself, the same line, self-authored and still held. The insects filled the Hook. The foot-traffic knotted the ward-junction — a corner in the Hook where three or four alleys gave onto one another under a single precinct-ward's reach — and the line drawn here was the line that would be asked of her again.
