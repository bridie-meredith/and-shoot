# s01 content beats — draft 2

# schema: drama-sized continuous beats; no episode delimiters; no slugs; no count target.

---

1. Taylor wakes in Tya's body three days after the fever broke — the family has been sitting the vigil for something that did not come back as expected. The tanner-father counts the signs against the girl at the table: no flinch at the dogs, wrong angle when she turns to face the door, the salt at the morning meal she reaches past without hesitation. The tanner-village's category for her is already closing before she has said ten words. The possibility of walking out of this house as a recognized member of this community is foreclosed on the first morning.

2. Taylor takes inventory of her range from inside the tanner-family's yard: 300 meters of insects, fully present, immediately. She walks the perimeter of the village's insect ecology twice in the first week, through wall-sense alone, and maps what she has. The map is smaller than anything she has ever worked from. The shard is running local-only — no network, no multiplier bandwidth, no external reference. What she has is what she has.

3. The tanner-mother brings a meal and pauses before setting it down. She begins a song — three notes of something private, a tune she would have used only with Tya, a family-interior reference that a stranger would have no way of knowing. Taylor does not finish the song. She does not look up at the right moment. The mother's face closes on a new fact: whatever came back through the fever, it does not have the song. The mother sets the bowl down and does not ask again. The foreclosure, from the mother's side, is complete. This is not the same foreclosure as beat 1 — the father measured behavioral tells; the mother has now run her own test and received her own answer.

4. The tanner-father withdraws one degree further each week. He assigns the girl tasks at the edge of the yard, away from the pits where the family's skilled work happens. By the fourth week, he has reordered the labor distribution so she occupies a functional slot but touches nothing central. He does not announce this. It is accomplished through routing, not declaration. The withdrawal is already a structural fact before Taylor identifies its shape.

5. The village's "Tya who came back wrong" category reaches the lord's man on his quarterly pass — not as an accusation, as background information a reeve mentions in passing: the tanner-family's girl came back from the fever but isn't right. The lord's man records it in his notes as "tanner's daughter, fever-return, behavior irregular." The record exists before Taylor knows the lord's man visited. The file is opened in the most minor possible register, by someone who has no idea what he recorded.

6. Taylor chooses King's Landing. The tanner-elder, operating from Flea Bottom, takes a trade-reference from the tanner-family and places her in the labor web as an unskilled hand with "reliable and strange." It is a transactional routing, not a rescue. She arrives at loc-flea-bottom-base on a market day, maps her 300m radius in the first forty-eight hours, and identifies the eastern-quarter apothecary as a surface worth watching. The man who works in the upper room keeps unusual hours and reads aloud while he works. She does not know yet what she has found.

7. The range at 300 meters covers Flea Bottom's immediate block, the market-side junction, the Fish Gate margin, and the apothecary's upper room at 250 meters. She runs the full perimeter twice and catalogs what the range contains: population density by alley, insect ecology by micro-zone, the tanner-elder's labor-web traffic at the junction, the broken maester's working pattern through the south wall's beetle colony. What the 300m boundary does not contain is the rest of King's Landing. Every direction beyond the perimeter is dark. The dark is the primary operational fact of s01.

8. The dock-runner moves a cargo through the Fish Gate margin on a night when the Watch patrol has shifted earlier than its usual hour. Taylor's network already knows the Watch moved; she does not act on the information, does not warn the runner, watches through the wall-insects as the runner recalculates the route and finds a clean exit. The runner surfaces at the edge of her range an hour later, recalibrated. Neither of them speaks about it. Two days later, the runner approaches the market-side junction looking for specific information about the Watch pattern. The tanner-elder routes the inquiry to Taylor. It is the first transactional exchange in the Flea Bottom network. The runner does not know what she is. The information passes cleanly.

9. The tanner-family makes the walk to King's Landing for the first time since Taylor left — the mother and father, arriving at the market-side junction with leather goods for the trade and a specific intention to check on the girl. The father watches how she holds herself among strangers. It is not the tells he catalogued in the village — salt, dogs, door-angle. It is something else: the way she orients toward each new arrival before turning to the person speaking to her, a half-second scan that the village frame has no category for. It is not body-wrong in the way a fever-return is body-wrong. It is strategic, and the father does not have a word for strategic. He leaves the market-side junction with a new problem he cannot name. The mother saw it too and is quieter on the walk home than she was on the walk in. The transactional surface is confirmed open. The family has learned something about her they did not know when they arrived, and it has not made anything simpler.

10. Taylor maps the Flea Bottom whisper chain's architecture through three weeks of network-ambient listening: which junction conversations travel to the Fish Gate margin, which do not; who the relay nodes are; the tanner-elder's position as membrane between the labor web and the informal credit economy. She begins routing small pieces of information through the chain — weather-pattern data useful to dock workers, Watch-movement timing useful to carters — without identifying herself as the source. The chain accepts the information and transmits it. The network does not know it has a new node. The stranger-leavings frame has not reached Flea Bottom yet; she reads here as "odd but functional."

11. The range expands for the first time past its initial perimeter — not by direction or effort, but as a threshold crossed during a sustained network operation lasting most of a night. When Taylor walks the perimeter the next morning, the sphere has extended approximately thirty meters in all directions. The extension is small enough to be unmeasurable without a reference point she has carefully maintained. She marks the new edges in her log with grid notations she invented for the purpose. The log notes the expansion factually, the physiological cost (headache, onset four hours post-operation, duration six hours), and the ambient conditions that preceded it. The notation is exact. The range is expanding.

12. A Flea Bottom family is evicted from a two-room dwelling in the alley two blocks south of the market-side junction — a lord's-man enforcement action over unpaid rent, carried out by three men who break the door latch and move the family's possessions into the alley. Taylor's network covers the alley; she observes the event in full through insects on the door lintel, the wall between adjacent rooms, and the alley itself. The family's neighbors watch from doorways and do not intervene. Taylor does not intervene. She logs what she observes with notation that includes the children's ages and the specific amount of the rent debt. This entry will not exist in the log by s03 — a fact that has no weight yet.

13. The tanner-family makes a second visit. This time the father speaks directly: the family has a claim on the girl's labor under Crownlands customary practice — a daughter working in the city owes a portion of her wages to the family that raised her. He states the amount. He states the mechanism. He does not raise his voice. The claim is not a threat; it is a statement of customary law in 125 AC Crownlands terms, accurately presented. Taylor cannot pay it and sustain her operating costs. She cannot pay it and it not be registered as payment toward an obligation she does not hold. She makes partial payment. The transactional surface does not close — it formalizes. The village-claim arc has an instrument now.

14. The range crosses 400 meters during a sustained relay operation in autumn, when the insect density in Flea Bottom is at seasonal peak. The expansion carries the radius noticeably north toward the Street of Steel and northeast toward the eastern-quarter proper. Taylor walks the new perimeter the following day and records it. The range expansion log entry is clinical: new perimeter, date, ambient conditions, headache duration. What the expanded radius now contains: a second block of labor-web traffic, two additional alley networks, and a broader slice of the eastern-quarter approach. The maester's upper room remains inside the radius with margin to spare.

15. The broken maester's side-alley door is used at an unusual hour — before first light, a visitor arriving without announcement. Taylor's network catches the visitor's movement: heavier tread than the maester, pauses twice in the stairwell, enters the upper room and speaks at a register too low for clear acoustic capture through the beetle colony. The conversation lasts forty minutes. The visitor leaves before the district is awake. Taylor records the event in her log as an anomaly in the maester's access pattern and notes that she cannot identify the visitor. This is the first time the maester registers as more than ambient monitoring data — the visit makes him a variable whose behavior she cannot fully model from the south-wall relay.

16. The broken maester arrives in Taylor's operational picture as a named presence: she determines, through acoustic fragments accumulated across weeks, that he is chain-stripped — stripped by the Citadel, not retired — and that he is keeping a systematic record of something in his upper room. She cannot read the record. She can hear pen-scratch and occasional spoken phrases and the specific rhythm of a man who has spent decades organizing information in a particular format. She does not have a category for what she has found. She records it as "subject: chain-stripped maester, upper room apothecary, eastern quarter, consistent high-density notation activity." He is now a named entry in her log.

17. The tanner-family's third visit is unannounced. The mother arrives alone, without the cloth bundle, without trade goods. She sits across from Taylor in the market-side junction's edge-space and does not speak for a long time. She says: your father wants to know if you're coming home for the cold months. Taylor says she is not. The mother nods — the nod of someone confirming a thing already known. Then she says, without being asked, that she has stopped lighting the vigil candle. She says it the way a person states a practical decision, not a confession. She stopped when it became clear that waiting for Tya was not the same as waiting for what came back. The mother has been doing something with the eighteen months of unresolved grief, and she has now named it: she has stopped. She leaves without staying for the meal. The village-claim has now demonstrated it does not require a formal mechanism to recur — it comes because it comes — but the grief it carries has changed shape since beat 3. What the mother brings now is not the vigil. The vigil is over.

18. A Hightower intelligence clerk makes a routine pass through Flea Bottom on assignment — a low-tier operative in the Hand's information apparatus, tasked with mapping the labor web and identifying non-native residents with unusual capabilities or affiliations. King's Landing's smallfolk district is a known source of useful information and a known harbor for foreign-connected agents. The clerk does not know what he is looking for in specific terms. He is building a population register. He speaks to the tanner-elder, who describes the girl as "trade-referenced from the Crownlands, reliable, strange, no known affiliations." This is accurate information. The clerk records it. Taylor's insect network covers the conversation at the junction. She observes the clerk writing in his book, observes his departure through the Fish Gate, and records it in her log as "lord's-apparatus intelligence contact, junction, recorded the elder's description of me." She does not know what apparatus the man works for.

19. The range crosses 500 meters in a second expansion event — incremental, not threshold-event, a gradual extension through the weeks of early winter as the shard's local-reseed stabilizes further. The new radius pushes the perimeter past the Street of Steel to the north and deeper into the eastern quarter to the east. Taylor marks the new edges. The broken maester's observation surface now includes the ground-floor apothecary as well as the upper room — she has the whole vertical. The range log is specific: dates, perimeter markers, physiological cost per extension.

20. A second clerk — a different man from the first, moving through the eastern-quarter adjacent streets — stops at the apothecary below the broken maester's rooms and asks the owner a set of questions about the district's recent unusual residents. Taylor's network covers the exchange through insects in the apothecary's doorframe: the clerk describes a behavioral profile — Crownlands-origin woman, unskilled labor entry, trade-referenced, unusual behavioral register — and asks whether the owner has seen anyone matching it. The apothecary owner names two people and Taylor is one of them. The clerk writes in his book, thanks the owner, and leaves. Taylor records it: "second lord's-apparatus intelligence contact, eastern-quarter apothecary, owner identified me by description." She still does not know what apparatus this is. The clerk carries a file entry. The file entry, when it returns to the Hand's apparatus, will be categorized under the wrong framework — she does not know this and has no path to knowing it. The file exists. It is already wrong. What cannot remain unchanged after this beat: the possibility of Taylor being wholly unknown to the lord's apparatus operating out of the Red Keep is gone.

21. The tanner-elder receives a request he cannot route without summoning Taylor to the junction — a specific task requiring knowledge of the Fish Gate Watch pattern and the ability to move information through the dock-adjacent labor web without leaving a visible trace. The routing happens at the junction, visible to the surrounding labor web. The elder's transaction acknowledges, by the way he frames the ask, that the girl has become a node in the network — not a name, not an affiliate, but a functional position that the network routes around. Taylor accepts the task. Moving the information without a visible trace requires burning through a portion of her insect network in the dock-side alleys — a cluster she has maintained as a passive observation surface — in order to route the hand-off through coverage she controls rather than through contacts she cannot monitor. She completes the task and is paid. The dock-side cluster will need weeks to reseed. Her position in the Flea Bottom network has changed from "placed by the elder" to "routed to by the network" — and the node that made the change possible is now thinner than it was before she accepted.

22. The broken maester descends from his upper room in mid-morning and walks the alley below Taylor's window — his occasional walk, the warm-bodied presence she recognizes by gait. Her insects track him to the eastern-quarter market, to a specific stall selling dried beetles and preserved specimens, to a conversation with the stall-keeper about insect behavior anomalies in the Flea Bottom adjacent area. The stall-keeper mentions that the insect population in certain alleys has been behaving with unusual coordination — he cannot explain it and does not try. The maester buys nothing and returns to his upper room. That night, pen-scratch continues for six hours past his usual stopping point. He is writing about what the stall-keeper described. Taylor knows this through the beetle colony. She does not know what conclusion he is approaching. She records it: "maester attended eastern-quarter market, purchased nothing, spoke to dried-goods vendor re: insect coordination anomaly, returned, extended work session." She does not record what she thinks the extended session means.

23. The tanner-father appears at the Flea Bottom labor-web junction without the mother — alone, in the middle of a trade day, without trade goods. He approaches the tanner-elder and states that the family's claim on the girl's wages is a matter of customary record with their lord's man. The elder receives this information without expression. He records nothing. After the father leaves, he routes the information to Taylor: your family has registered the wage claim with the lord's apparatus. The claim now has an external instrument. Taylor cannot make it go away by paying it off — it exists in a lord's-man record independent of any transaction she makes with the family directly. The village-claim arc has closed around her from the outside: an open transactional surface that is now also a formal record she has no access to and cannot modify.

24. The range reaches approximately 600 meters in late winter — a third extension event, this one during a sustained overnight operation covering the Fish Gate margin comprehensively. The log records the expansion, the headache duration, the specific new geography now inside the perimeter. The broken maester's upper room is now more than 200 meters inside the sphere's edge. The Red Keep's outermost approach is still four hundred meters beyond her current ceiling. The distance is known; the math is in her log. The log does not record the distance to the Red Keep.

25. A third operative — more senior than either prior contact, moving in the outer-ring service approach of the Red Keep, not Flea Bottom — sends a messenger to the tanner-elder at the market-side junction asking for a written account of the Crownlands girl's behavior and affiliations. The elder receives the request, says nothing to anyone, and delivers a written account three days later through a middleman. Taylor's network observes the messenger at the junction, observes the elder's response, and cannot observe what the elder writes or where it goes after the middleman takes it. She records: "Hand's-apparatus contact to the elder, formal written request re: my affiliations, outcome unknown." The written account reaches the Hand's records and completes a file. That file is categorized under the wrong framework — a misread that will not be corrected in s01 because no one in the apparatus has a better category to offer. Taylor does not know the file exists. She knows she is being recorded. The two things are not the same.

26. Taylor walks the Flea Bottom perimeter in the early morning, three days after the junction messenger came and went — the full circuit, every alley, the Fish Gate margin, the south wall where the beetle colony runs densest. The walk takes two hours. She opens her log at the base afterward and writes two entries side by side: the village-claim, now in a lord's-man record she cannot access; and the Hand's-apparatus contact, now a file she cannot read. Two external records. Two separate apparatuses. The lord's-man who holds the wage-claim record and the Hand's-apparatus that built the file have no knowledge of each other — they are different institutional tracks, different scales of apparatus, neither cross-referencing the other. She writes this down because it is what the data shows. Within four days of each other, two unrelated recording instruments closed around her. She does not have a model that explains this as anything other than coincidence. Her log does not speculate. What the log holds is what the perimeter walk holds: the village-claim open and formal, the Hand's file dormant and miscategorized, the maester's pen-scratch audible three blocks north, the dock-side insect cluster still thin from the burn-down three weeks ago. The architecture has changed. She knows that. She does not know what file she is in.
