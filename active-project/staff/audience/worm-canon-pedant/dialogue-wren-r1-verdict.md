A child who is making a pure proximity argument would say "not near you" or "not on you." The specificity of "hand" implies she watched Taylor's hand in relation to the fly distribution, which is the same close-attention-to-insect-behavior-around-Taylor's-hand that the card dialogue-sample names as the capability tell.
