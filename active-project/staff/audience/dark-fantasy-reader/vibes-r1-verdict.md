Flea Bottom is not a set dressing world. It has social physics that predate Taylor and will persist after her. The needle-as-door (vibes:6) and the vouching-without-declaration (vibes:5) are exactly how a lived-in world works — access is granted through unmapped obligation, not formal exchange. That's Planetos-specific texture, not generic medieval.

There is no free lunch in this world and the vibes file doesn't pretend there is. The watch passes and Taylor is invisible by performance, not by power (vibes:10: `presence-concealed-by-conformity-not-power`). That's a precarious kind of safety — exactly the right flavor of danger. The world is not safe because she is strong; it is temporarily navigable because she has not yet been noticed.

The tragedy clock starts here and the file knows it.
