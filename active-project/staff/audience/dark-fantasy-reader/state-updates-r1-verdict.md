# Dark Fantasy Reader — State-Updates Adversarial Reading

## Persona frame

Sits back and waits to be convinced. Not cynical — patient. Reads state-updates not as a mechanic ledger but as evidence that the world pushes back — that changes cost, that the field mutations actually mean something to the terrain. When an entry fires, this reader asks: does this change matter? Will it propagate? Does the world that caused this change feel real, or does it feel like a stage-direction in a system where nothing actually bites?

Fatigue trigger: stops reading environmental description when the setting stops feeling hostile. State-updates is where the world's bite is recorded — if the entries read like bureaucratic notation on events that have no weight, this reader disengages.

Hot-button relevant here: a world that feels like set-dressing rather than a place that was there before the protagonist. State-updates is supposed to demonstrate that Flea Bottom's social physics, the Watch's patrol cadence, Coll's block-register, and Taylor's insect-sense discipline are real systems that mutate — not narrative furniture.

## File-level concern

This reader's concern is not density — it is texture. Twenty entries on 26 bones means the state file is dense enough to raise the question: does the world feel like it is tracking real consequence, or does it feel like a system generating log entries? The env entries and the Coll/Wren entries feel earned. The Taylor knowledge entries in bulk (seven entries across @8, @12, @15, @22, @24, @25, @26) make Taylor's environmental reading feel very granular. That granularity is accurate to her capability — she is reading at that resolution — but the state-updates file that records it risks making Flea Bottom feel like a map Taylor is filling in rather than a place she is living in.

This is an authorial-register concern, not a rubric violation. The rubric doesn't ask whether the state-updates file reads poetically. But for this reader, the test is whether the world pushes back, and seven knowledge entries on 26 bones suggests Taylor is not being pushed back against — she is acquiring smoothly. The prohibition-catch at @24–@25 is the exception, and it is the best moment in the file precisely because the world (in the form of her own rule) resists her acquisition impulse. The rest of the knowledge entries are clean acquisitions without friction.
