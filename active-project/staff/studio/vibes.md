# Series Vibe-Cloud — series-plan revision
# Preliminary section authored at step 1c. Extensions added at series-plan step.
# Format: world-key → register/associations the series activates around it.

## World-keys (decided premise)

- `riverlands-jaehaerys-peace`: long peace, stable Tully suzerainty, Bracken-Blackwood feud as background constant, ferries and tributaries as commerce nerves, the realm has not seen Targaryen civil violence in living memory
- `fairstead`: composite — market square, sept, ferry, craftsman district, lord's-man garrison outpost; Blue Fork tributary; ~250–350 souls; House Ryger of Willow Wood as distant feudal weight
- `house-ryger`: minor Tully bannerman; not cruel, thorough; presence felt through Aldric Pryor (steward), not the lord himself; functionary state as primary face of authority
- `feudal-extraction`: tax collection as the institution's regular pressure on smallfolk; the table on tax day, the weighing stone, the armed retinue positioned for intimidation; legible coercion that is not personal
- `faith-of-seven-jaehaerys`: post-Faith-Militant suppression by the king (~50 years prior); no organized faith violence permitted in this era; theology operates without armed enforcement; sincere parish-level Faith with bureaucratic institutional layer
- `maester-network`: Citadel at institutional peak; ravens as the fast channel; literacy detection and soft surveillance; Septon Barth's death (~99 AC) as the closing of an era of cooperative governance
- `targaryen-dynasty-99ac`: peak — many living dragons, Old King aged and aging, Great Council 101 AC as the structural inevitability not yet triggered; offstage in Fairstead's daily life
- `great-council-horizon`: ~17 years out from story-open; Taylor knows what it is; nobody in Fairstead does
- `the-dance-horizon`: ~30 years out; Taylor knows it follows the Council regardless; nobody else does
- `smallfolk-political-physics`: subjugation as internalized state; learned invisibility; the posture of non-threat; informal credit and whisper networks; collective compliance as armor
- `westerosi-superstition-frame`: omens, curses, blessings, signs from the Seven, old-gods residue at the Riverlands border; uncanny phenomena are categorized through this frame, not through "magic"
- `low-magic-backdrop`: dragons distant and Targaryen-only; wargs are northern rumor; glass candles are Citadel legend; no daily supernatural friction; Taylor's swarm-sense reads as uncanny / low-grade madness against this

## Protagonist-keys (Taylor-specific)

- `passive-sense-texture`: always-on, ambient-not-directed, she-knows-without-choosing-to-know; cannot be turned off; near-zero physical cost; her continuous radius-check is the operational baseline
- `active-control-cost`: child-body ceiling — ten minutes to headache, twenty to nosebleed, thirty to blackout; physical cost meaningful and recoverable; she rations
- `foreign-ecology-friction`: the insects are wrong; the map is being rebuilt; precision degrades at range and in new terrain; eighteen months into recalibration at story open
- `shard-thumb`: judgment biased toward escalation, away from de-escalation; Taylor cannot detect this as external; reads the pull as her own nature; not a driver, a weight
- `gold-morning-load`: geological; always present; never content; the floor under everything; mass fauna death lands through this register
- `dinah-debt`: live moral wound — she used a child for a calculation she has not finished judging; activates around any child she would have to weigh in a coalition equation
- `family-as-vector`: the warm constraint; her parents have legitimate intimate claim; she cannot keep them out without generating the cost OQ-9/C names
- `child-performance-grooves`: years of running adult cognition under a child's external register; mostly invisible, slips are sharper when they happen
- `foreknowledge-load`: knows the Great Council is coming, knows the Dance follows; broad strokes accurate, specifics fuzzy; cover story degrades under sustained smart questioning
- `escalation-instinct`: cape-war-trained at sixteen; recalibrating for a world without parahuman infrastructure; the Shard's thumb makes recalibration cost more than it should
- `literacy-as-exposure`: reads at adult speed and comprehension; manages appearance of child-speed; continuous low-grade drain in observation scenes

## Coalition-keys (the people Taylor's failure modes will cost)

- `intimate-cost`: mother and father — the first targets of the self-isolation pattern; the cost extracted in S1 early
- `coalition-cost`: Mira, Rymer, the proto-coalition Taylor builds in S1 mid → S2; the cost extracted as her control-as-substitute-for-trust pattern compounds
- `institutional-cost`: lord's apparatus, Faith framework — the cost extracted late as Taylor's organizing reaches scale
- `cost-migration`: outward — Taylor pays personally early, coalition pays mid, institution pays late; the structural promise of OQ-9/C
- `peer-authority-earned`: Mira-Stonefield-Jaehaerys absorbs the community-elder function; Taylor must earn community-legitimacy and information-access from one person, simultaneously, not sequentially
- `non-convert-witness`: Rymer Hedge — watches without becoming an acolyte; the workman-register that does not convert
- `theological-frame-imposed`: Septon Rowan — building category-around-Taylor without Taylor's consent; Faith-witness as complication, not antagonist
- `child-mirror`: oc-child-peer — Clem Ferris, the social-surveillance cost of performing-as-child; family alone cannot deliver this friction

## Institutional-keys (the apparatus that escalates against Taylor)

- `the-steward`: Aldric Pryor — first institutional record, single escalating face, suppression-policy spine; thorough not cruel
- `tax-collection`: the regular pressure that triggered Taylor's involuntary swarm; the structural mechanism by which extraction becomes legible coercion
- `record-as-clock`: the steward's first note is the first entry in a file that grows; the antagonist clock is documentary, not personal
- `suppression-policy-progression`: incident-response → patterned-response → policy → action; mid-S3 deadline for policy-level institutional answer

## Tonal-register keys

- `social-warrior-escalation`: organizing-as-engine; coalition-building as primary tactic; recursive escalation of stakes as narrative spine; not chosen-one fantasy; not court-romance
- `tragedy-of-correct-diagnosis`: she is right; being right costs her and everyone around her; the catharsis prohibited before the Dance
- `costs-become-final`: the Council close as stress-test; what survives survives in damaged, diminished, or transformed form
- `ratchet-not-spiral`: escalation is structural — stakes rise and the nature of the cost changes; not the same fight at higher volume
- `westerosi-witness-frame`: locals read Taylor's swarm through superstition (old gods, Seven-as-ward, fear-of-the-dark), not as generic strangeness
- `pulpy-momentum-with-weight`: pacing-readable, not slow-burn-only; escalation visible early; institutional response visible early
- `interiority-without-confession`: Taylor's internal failure modes are visible to the reader through behavior; she does not narrate herself; the audience reads her by what she does and does not do

## Series-plan extensions (added at series-plan step)

### Season-arc tonal keys

- `s1-depth-before-breadth`: Taylor's early organizing is narrow and intentionally invisible; the ignition is involuntary; the first season's dramatic shape is quiet infrastructure laid against the steward's growing file; intimacy-as-exposure is the quiet danger
- `s2-coalition-discovers-the-withholding`: the season's emotional register is what it feels like to trust someone who cannot stop calculating you; the loss is not betrayal — it is the recognition of what was never offered
- `s3-institution-as-lever-and-trap`: Taylor presses the Faith as a counter-institutional tool; the register is cold institutional maneuvering; the season ends on the gap between what operates under pressure and what does not
- `s4-nothing-left-to-shield`: at the Council all managed distance collapses; the register is reckoning-without-resolution; the season closes on what survives, not what is saved

### Structural tonal keys

- `control-calculus-as-tax`: Taylor's operational logic is not a flaw she overcomes; it is a continuous levy on every person who trusts her; the story tracks who pays and when
- `documentary-antagonist`: Aldric Pryor's file is the antagonist clock; the threat is never personal — it is bureaucratic accumulation; each season's suppression-stage is a new entry in the record
- `faith-without-consent`: Septon Rowan builds a theological category around Taylor; the register is pastoral and sincere; the complication is that Rowan's framing is not wrong, exactly, and not Taylor's to refuse
- `knowledge-as-loneliness`: Taylor's foreknowledge cannot be shared without destroying her cover; the people around her are not stupid — they are working with the information they have; the register is that being the only one who sees the whole shape is a kind of isolation that organizing cannot fix

## Season 1 (~84–88 AC) extensions

Deltas from the series vibe-cloud — what is foregrounded in S1 specifically. Series-level keys remain active; these extend or intensify them at the season scale.

### Fairstead as total world

- `fairstead-as-complete-map`: in S1 Taylor's operational radius is Fairstead entire — market square, sept, craftsman district, ferry dock, the common, her parents' workshop; she knows where every fly is; the geography is small and named; the season lives inside this containment
- `named-smallfolk-texture`: faces, debts, feuds, seasonal rhythms, the wool factor, the reeve's household, who owes whom and who talks to whom; Taylor's passive-sense populates this map continuously; the season treats Fairstead's ~250–350 souls as a known quantity
- `ferry-as-institutional-throat`: the river-ferry dock is where census, tax, and official travel enter; institutional pressure arrives wet and by water; Taylor watches the dock the way a trapper watches a gap in the fence

### Pre-ignition stealth management (~84–86 AC, Taylor age 7–9)

- `performance-of-ordinariness`: the dominant register of the season's first half; Taylor managing child-speed literacy, child-register curiosity, child-appropriate emotional display; the drain is continuous and invisible; slips are the dramatic material
- `two-years-of-not-being-seen`: the operational baseline before ignition; doing nothing legible to power is its own active discipline; the season foregrounds the cost of restraint, not only the cost of action
- `father-as-unwitting-cartographer`: Edwyn Ashford's trade relationships and gossip channels are the social map Taylor reads through; she extracts information from his household role without his awareness; the register is instrumental affection
- `sept-as-cover-and-tool`: attending the sept is publicly legible as piety; it also provides literacy access, Rowan's information network, and observation cover; Taylor uses the form without the content

### The ignition event's involuntary signature (E05, ~86 AC)

- `involuntary-as-defining`: the swarm rises before Taylor decides to act; this is the season's spine; the loss of control is the hinge the entire season bends around; everything before bends toward this; everything after bends away from it
- `tax-collection-as-political-frame`: the ignition is not "child with strange power" but "child with strange power that activated against institutional extraction"; Pryor's note reads it this way from the first; the political valence is stamped at origin
- `pryor-note-as-documentary-birth`: the steward's first entry in the incident log is Taylor's institutional birth; she does not know the exact content of the note but she knows the note exists; the season closes on whether the note has traveled

### Maester's letter as closing instrument

- `institutional-tier-crossing`: when the note reaches a maester it has crossed from local-incident to networked-surveillance; the season closes on this tier-crossing; what changes is not the threat level but the scope of the apparatus that knows
- `ledger-cover-as-structure`: the maester arrives under cover of routine ledger work; Taylor must not appear to recognize what his visit is; the closing episode's dramatic pressure is performing normalcy at the highest possible cost

### Intimate-cost foregrounding

- `elara-as-warm-obstacle`: Elara Ashford is not antagonistic — she loves her daughter; the register is warmth as pressure; she senses something is wrong without being able to name it; Taylor's management of her mother is the intimacy-cost primary vector
- `managed-distance-as-exhaustion`: Taylor cannot close the distance with her parents without exposing her operational baseline; the cost of distance is real but the alternative costs more; the season tracks this as a continuous low-grade loss, not a single dramatic rupture
- `elara-moves-without-permission`: late S1, Elara takes action outside Taylor's managed frame — she goes to Rowan with questions Taylor did not anticipate; the gap stops being managed from one side only

### Mira-recruitment as S1 coalition-seed

- `slow-earn-dual-axis`: Mira Stonefield is simultaneously a community-elder Taylor needs access through and a peer-ally Taylor cannot afford to instrumentalize further than she already is; the recruitment must earn on both axes simultaneously or it earns on neither
- `mira-as-ignition-witness`: Mira was in the market when the swarm rose; her account of what she saw is the first community elder to hold that information; her decision about what to do with it is the first test of the recruitment's depth
- `patience-as-discipline`: Taylor's instinct is to move faster; the Shard weights toward direct approach; the Mira arc is the season's most explicit demonstration of Taylor suppressing the escalation-pull; the slow earn is a choice she has to re-make weekly

### Rowan's theology-building as parallel pressure

- `rowan-without-malice`: Septon Rowan is sincere; his framing of Taylor is pastoral, not predatory; the complication is that his category-building is not Taylor's to refuse and not wrong exactly; the pressure is structural, not personal
- `category-before-consent`: Rowan names Taylor's gift within his theological frame before Taylor has decided what the gift is; this is the season's version of being defined by your context; it is not the same threat as Pryor's note, but it occupies the same structural position
- `taylor-watches-and-waits`: Taylor is aware Rowan is building a frame; she cannot stop it without leaving the sept; she does not know what to do; the season ends without resolution here — this is an S2 pressure

### Clem-as-mirror

- `clem-sees-without-processing`: Clem Ferris (reeve's son, ~age 8) notices things about Taylor that adults miss, because his processing is social rather than institutional; he has no vocabulary for what he notices; the friction is that a child's noticing is harder to manage than an adult's
- `peer-register-as-genuine-pressure`: Taylor can manage adults through their frames; Clem operates without a frame she can subvert; the child-mirror is the one witness whose honesty is structurally guaranteed
- `dinah-debt-activation`: Clem's proximity activates the Dinah-debt register — Taylor cannot stop running the coalition equation on a child; the moral wound is live every time she chooses how much to manage him
