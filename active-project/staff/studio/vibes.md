# Series vibe-cloud — taylor-in-westeros
# Built at series planning stage. Active throughout all seasons.

  body: [alien-integration, the-self-that-persists, constraint-discovery, apex-predator-learning-itself]
  # Taylor's dragon form is never background. It is always present as the register of everything.
  # What can she do. What does it cost. What does it feel like to be this. These questions are live.

  insects: [home, surveillance-that-no-one-sees, the-taylor-layer, proof-of-continuity]
  # The bugs are how the audience knows it is Taylor and not just a dragon. They are the
  # Taylor-proof. Every scene where she uses them is a scene where identity is being asserted.

  the-patch: [what-she-is-building, small-at-first, contested-by-everything, the-series-question]
  # This key is the narrative engine. What is she protecting. Why. What will it cost.
  # The patch gets larger across seasons. The Dance tests whether it is large enough.

  catastrophe: [she-knows-the-calendar, the-window-is-open, not-her-job-to-stop-it, ticking-clock]
  # 19 years. She knows. Nobody else does. The asymmetry is the clock.
  # The dramatic question beneath every season: how much has she built before the clock runs out?

  legitimacy: [power-without-category, the-claim-that-will-not-die, building-outside-the-system]
  # She has no house, no rider, no institutional recognition. The Targaryen claim persists.
  # She is building leverage while the legal framework insists she cannot.

  fire: [tool-not-symbol, irreversibility, Gold-Morning-adjacent-weight, used-sparingly-means-something]
  # Dragon fire is the most visible thing she can do. It is also the thing that reminds her
  # of Scion. She uses it when she decides to. The deciding is always load-bearing.

  violence: [grammar-not-exception, fluent-in-a-different-dialect, Westerosi-register-familiar]
  # Westeros speaks force. She is fluent. The grammar is recognizable even if the dialect differs.
  # She does not require violence to be exceptional. She requires it to be correctly priced.

  loss: [Gold-Morning-scale-beneath-everything, Westerosi-losses-are-real, no-audience-for-the-grief]
  # What happened on Earth-Bet is the sub-register. The Westerosi losses are not diminished by
  # having survived something larger. They are losses. She tracks them.

  survival: [still-here, cost-not-counted, what-is-this-survival-for, the-series-question-again]
  # She survived Gold Morning. She is surviving in Westeros. The question underneath:
  # what is she building this survival toward? The series ends when she has an answer.

  the-clock: [19-years, each-season-costs-some-of-it, the-Dance-is-coming, she-knows]
  # Explicit. The clock is always present even when it is not mentioned.
  # Every season of building is a season of the clock running.
