- L1: "made my first transactional pass with the dock-runner through the tanner-elder"
  reason: "transactional pass" reads as slightly generic narrator-summary rather than canon-Taylor's clinical noun-locked register; "pass" wants a more specific operational noun (contact / handoff).
  scene: opening exposition (pre-scene-A)
  category: voice

- L5: "My father went still. ... My father went still again, and my mother went still."
  reason: three "went still" beats in seven lines. The body-track is canon-register, but the repetition reads metronomic in tight space — peaks @4 (father step-back is bone-noted at @22 in scene-map) lose differentiation when both stillnesses are reported with the same verb.
  scene: A
  category: variance

- L5: "I answered, 'Goodman. I have not forgotten.' ... I answered her, 'Goodwife. The road is yours.'"
  reason: Taylor addressing her parents as "Goodman" / "Goodwife" is register-correct for the Westeros placement and consistent with the Tya-shaped-debt framing — flagging only because the editor should confirm intent: Taylor's voice will sound out-of-character to readers who don't read the exchange as deliberate distance-marking. The text earns it on a careful read; a single body-cue elsewhere in the scene confirming the cost of that address would lock it.
  scene: A
  category: voice

- L13: "He turned and faced me square, working pitch, the placement-look set on his face. The carter rolled out of the junction."
  reason: "He" antecedent ambiguous on first read — could be the elder (who just spoke) or a previously unintroduced carter. "Working pitch" is not parsable on first pass; reads as a Westeros-trade idiom the prose hasn't established. The carter then "rolling out" of the junction lands without prior placement in the scene — blocking unclear.
  scene: E
  category: confusion

- L15: "I exhaled. ... I exhaled again. ... and exhaled."
  reason: three exhales in one paragraph. The 400m range expansion is supposed to peak here (scene-F peak-bones @77=2, @78=2); the repeated exhale reads as a stand-in for the physiological cost rather than a varied body-signature. Worm-canon Taylor body-tracks the cost in specifics (jaw clench, sinus pressure, dry mouth) — three identical exhales flatten the peak into refrain.
  scene: F
  category: pacing

- L15: "the wasps were on the Fish Gate — the southern dock-side gate of King's Landing, the city's busy fish-trade mouth — holding the perimeter"
  reason: the in-line gloss ("the southern dock-side gate of King's Landing, the city's busy fish-trade mouth") reads as narrator-explanation outside Taylor's clinical register. Taylor names her geography in operational terms; she does not pause to tourist-tag a gate for the reader. The gloss is fine as exposition but the placement inside the relay-quartet breaks the fauna-relay refrain's percussion.
  scene: F
  category: voice

- L17: "I woke and held my eyes still, then opened the log, wrote the entry, and closed it."
  reason: one-line scene (G) lands clean but the "held my eyes still" gesture (also reused in L23) wants differentiation — second appearance feels like the same verb is doing the same job twice.
  scene: G

- L19: "His man broke the latch — and the alley-murmur cracked. The bar gave. His man carried possessions out."
  reason: "His man" twice in three sentences with the lord's-man already named at line-open creates a stuttering possessive chain (lord's-man → his man → his man). The bar / latch separation is also unclear — were they two fastenings or the same one?
  scene: H

- L21: "(What the Crownlands called the customary wage — the unwritten claim a family carried for the labour a returned child owed them, a tally without paper.)"
  reason: parenthetical narrator-explanation. Taylor explaining a custom to the reader in a clean parenthetical is borderline — canon-Taylor does explain, but typically inside her observational frame, not in a bracketed aside. The aside lands as exposition seam rather than thought. Editor should consider folding the gloss into Taylor's beat ("I knew the wage-claim. Two coppers a moon...") or trimming.
  scene: I
  category: voice

- L23: "I walked the perimeter and wrote the entry — wrote it again — flexed my hand. I woke. The stylus dropped. I held my eyes still."

As written the reader hits "I woke" before any sleep-state is established, then "the stylus dropped" appears as if she dropped it after waking. Scene-J peak is @125=3 (stylus-drop as physiological cost of 400m); the peak loses its load when the cause/effect ordering is fogged.

  category: confusion

Two narrator-explanation seams (Fish Gate gloss at L15, customary-wage parenthetical at L21) read as exposition welded into Taylor's clinical frame rather than emerging from it. The protected patterns (log-trio, three-note-buildup, cardinal-quartet) all land — preserve them. Carry-forward live watches (insect-network/glass-candle theory; maester distance precision) are not in s01e02 scope and remain unresolved at execution.
