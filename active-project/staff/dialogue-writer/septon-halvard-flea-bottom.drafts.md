## Anchor b01c07s02n05 — the thesis, arrived at

Objective: name what is wrong with the Lane man's arrangement, working it through honestly, not aimed at Taylor.

Scene state: Halvard has been describing Derry's fever, the missing herbs, the unaffordable maester. He turns — not abruptly — to a man up the Lane who runs errands for a household that pays him in kind not coin. He does NOT know he is describing Taylor's arrangement. Plain accounting. Neither complaint nor appeal. Thesis arrives WITHOUT being announced as thesis. Pausal, slow register. Plain-Anglo. NO Seven-name homily here (that would make it a sermon — wrong; this is a man thinking aloud about a neighbour). The "names the wrong, does not prescribe" tell is honored: he names that the debt compounds and where the slower way costs; he does NOT tell her what she should do.

### Draft A (chosen — matches on-disk entry 1)
> "There's a man up the Lane runs errands for a house that pays him in bread, not coin. Good bread. His children eat. I've no quarrel with a man feeding his children. But the house pays him that way because it suits the house, and every loaf he takes, he takes on the house's terms, and the terms don't get smaller for the taking. They stay the size they were. A thing built crooked doesn't come straight because you lean on it gently. It grows crooked at the rate it was always going to grow. The slow way costs him more now — he'd go hungry a while, refusing. It costs him less after. The debt doesn't sit there gathering."

chosen-mark: ✓
why this over B/C: A lands the thesis the way the brief requires — *arrived at*, not declared. Opens on the concrete neighbour (same Lane as Derry), grants the man his good faith first (*I've no quarrel with a man feeding his children* — the sin-and-grace ledger run plainly, crediting the good before naming the wrong, exactly the card-core move), then works the accounting to the substance heart: the corruption compounds *at the rate it was always going to grow* — not faster, not more, AT THE RATE. The "costs more now / costs less after / the debt doesn't sit there gathering" closer names the wrong and the price of refusing it WITHOUT prescribing (he never says *and so you must refuse*). The "thing built crooked... lean on it gently" image carries "even mitigation compounds the corruption" without abstraction or jargon.

## Anchor b01c07s03n06 — acknowledging his own cost

Objective: acknowledge the cost of his own position honestly without retracting it or claiming she is wrong.

Scene state: AFTER Taylor counters with a specific named death — the body the slower method cost. Halvard absorbs it; he does NOT have an answer that closes the question. He KNOWS the slower method has a body count. He has NOT resolved the cost. He has decided not to endorse the faster method because the interest accumulates and he cannot see where it stops. He is telling her the cost HE has decided to pay — honestly — NOT telling her she is wrong. Pausal. Honest about the limit of his knowing (card-core). Plain closer.

### Draft A (chosen — matches on-disk entry 2)
> "I know the slow way has a cost. I'll not pretend I don't. You've named it, and I'll carry the name. I've buried by the slow way before. What I can't do is the other thing — take the quick way and watch the debt go on growing past where I can see the end of it. I don't know where it stops. That's the truth of it. I haven't an answer that makes your dead breathe. I've only the one I can live beside."

chosen-mark: ✓
why this over B/C: A meets all three objective constraints in order. (1) Acknowledges the cost honestly: *I know the slow way has a cost ... I've buried by the slow way before.* (2) Does NOT claim she is wrong: *You've named it, and I'll carry the name* — he accepts her named death as real and binding, never countering it. (3) Does NOT retract: *What I can't do is the other thing* — he holds his position and says why (the debt growing *past where I can see the end of it*). The closer pair (*I haven't an answer that makes your dead breathe. I've only the one I can live beside.*) is the actor-core honesty about the limit of his knowing — he names the cost HE has decided to pay rather than telling her what to do. Plain-Anglo throughout; the doubt is real and the position holds.
