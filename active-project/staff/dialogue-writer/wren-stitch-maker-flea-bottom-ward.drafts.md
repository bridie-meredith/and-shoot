**Draft A** — "Them nets is hard, an't they?"
- *rejection note:* Carries the right cadence (question-tag, *is*-with-plural, subject-implied) but the question-tag *an't they* is invitation-to-agree and pulls toward small-talk-with-a-peer. Wren is opening across an unfamiliarity gap; the invitation-to-agree compresses that gap too fast and reads as more familiar than the first-meeting state supports.

At first meeting, before the not-asking choice is even formed, the more in-register move is to route the question through the elder — the trained-attention child opens with the relation she can place (Coll, the trade) before naming the stranger she cannot yet place.
