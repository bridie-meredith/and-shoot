# Season 1 Plan — Taylor in Westeros
## Season: s01 | Timeline: 120–122 AC

### Season Drama
Three opening pressures converge and close the window of unobserved development, forcing Taylor into involuntary structure before she has built anything that can survive being named.

### Season Chunk
The impressment census, the dying septon's collapse, and the Hightower castellan's consolidation converge before Taylor has any cover; her fauna network becomes visible to a castellan's man who writes reports, and the only move that preserves her is one that names her as useful to the wrong person. The window of unobserved development closes with her inside a structure she did not choose and cannot control.

### Escalation spine
Named → Pattern documented upstream → Witnessed anomaly + intercession backfires → Cover removed + succession triggered → Competed over + crown visibility → Resolved into involuntary structure.

### Episode Chunks

**s01e01 — 120 AC**
The impressment census officer arrives at the sept and logs Taylor as an unattached ward with no blood claim and no sworn guardian capable of asserting one — the septon is too ill to appear, and his written attestation is refused as insufficient. Taylor is placed on a provisional labor-eligibility list pending the traveling maester's cognitive assessment, and the window for being overlooked closes the moment her name enters the census ledger.

**s01e02 — 120 AC**
Ser-harwick-plumm's man makes his first circuit of the Harrenhal shadow settlements, recording anomalous activity — a rat-cleared grain store, ravens roosting against their pattern, a girl present at three separate locations where vermin behavior was reported unnatural. His first written report does not name Taylor directly but describes a set of conditions that point to a single source, and the report goes to the castellan's records before Plumm receives his copy.

**s01e03 — 120–121 AC**
The inspection visit triggered by Plumm's report arrives at the sept, and the inspector finds Taylor with a raven perched on her arm in a posture no trained bird takes with a stranger — a concrete, witnessed anomaly that Plumm's man records and sketches. Septon-rowan, arrived as the dying septon's replacement, attempts to intercede and succeeds only in putting his own name in the inspector's record alongside Taylor's, broadening the documented exposure rather than narrowing it.

**s01e04 — 121 AC**
The septon dies, and his death simultaneously removes Taylor's last institutional cover and triggers the sept's ward-succession protocol: unattached wards with no sworn guardian revert to the nearest administrative authority, which in this district is the castellan's household. Ser-harwick-plumm moves within the same week to formalize Taylor's status as a ward of the castellan's administration before any competing claim can be filed, and the mechanism that will make her useful to him is the same documented anomaly that made her visible in the first place.
*E4 planning note: Taylor must make an active attempt to intervene in or delay the succession mechanism before it closes — the episode cannot be passive witness.*

**s01e05 — 121 AC**
The traveling maester conducts his cognitive assessment, and the results — literacy, numeracy, and a pattern-recognition performance he has not recorded in a ward of her age and station — go into his report to the Citadel as a notation of interest. Ser-aemon-bracken, who has his own reasons to want the maester's findings suppressed, moves to acquire Taylor's guardianship through a counter-claim to Plumm's; the two claims are now in active administrative contest, and a letter from ser-edwyn-celtigar's office arrives at the castellan's hall requesting a summary of all census anomalies in the Harrenhal district.
*E5 planning note: Episode must close on an irreversible board-worsening action — a document reaching the wrong desk, Bracken's counter-claim filed publicly and locking a timeline, Celtigar's letter read aloud before the castellan in company — not on stalemate.*

**s01e06 — 121–122 AC**
Ser-edwyn-celtigar's inquiry forces the castellan to resolve the contested guardianship before the crown agent's arrival makes the dispute visible at a higher level; Plumm, with the faster administrative machinery and the existing documented file, wins the resolution before Bracken's counter-claim can be heard. Taylor is formally named a ward of the castellan's administration, assigned to Plumm's operational remit, and the name given to her function — intelligence-gathering asset, fauna-behavior resource — is not one she chose and cannot now be ungiven.
