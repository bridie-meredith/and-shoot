# Season 1 Plan — The Unclaimed Thing (~110-113 AC)

## Season Drama

A thinking dragon appears in contested territory with no name, no rider, no institutional position, and full knowledge of everyone else's future; the season is the escalating sequence of human responses to this anomaly, from smallfolk terror to hedge-knight curiosity to lord-calculation to crown dispatch.

## Episode Chunks

**S01E01 — Hatching**
Taylor cracks out of a dragon egg in a forest clearing with full human consciousness and a body she has no map for; the egg's presence is immediately a political emergency for every party within ten miles. The collision is between her need to survive the first hours (hide, assess, move) and the immediate mobilization of local actors to find what hatched; what cannot remain unchanged after it is whether a small dragon with no rider can go undetected in a world that notices everything.

**S01E02 — First Contact**
Rymer the hedge knight follows the trail and is the first human to establish sustained proximity without immediate flight or attack; both parties have severely incomplete models of the other. The threshold is whether a non-verbal dragon can communicate enough to prevent a miscalculation; what cannot remain unchanged after it is either the relationship or the options it represents.

**S01E03 — The Maester's Report**
The traveling maester achieves proximity close enough to form a correct hypothesis about Taylor's cognition; he writes a raven to the Citadel. The collision is between his institutional obligation to report and the first evidence that reporting may produce a response she cannot survive; what cannot remain unchanged after it is the number of people who know what she is.

**S01E04 — The Septon's Sermon**
Septon Rowan preaches his first structured theological account of the dragon; the sermon immediately produces smallfolk action (pilgrimage, rumor-propagation, a young man who announces he will seek the sign). The collision is between Rowan's intent to provide meaning without harm and the simple fact that meaning produces motion; what cannot remain unchanged after it is the size of the human footprint approaching her territory.

**S01E05 — Ser Aemon's Calculation**
Ser Aemon Bracken arrives at the tower hill with four men to assess and decide. The collision is between his calculation and the dragon's; what cannot remain unchanged after it is whether she has an ally or another enemy with a garrison.

**S01E06 — The Territory Claim**
Taylor demonstrates the ruined tower hill is hers through behavior, bug deployment, and a calculated minimum use of fire; local actors must decide whether to accept this arrangement or contest it. The collision is between the Westerosi reflex to contest a claim without a house behind it and the practical reality that contesting this one is expensive; what cannot remain unchanged after it is whether she has a defensible base.

**S01E07 — The Word Reaches**
News of the unclaimed dragon reaches King's Landing; the Small Council authorizes investigation; Ser Edwyn Celtigar is dispatched. The collision is between the administrative time required for the crown to act and the rate at which the local situation is already setting itself; what cannot remain unchanged after it is the number of players with a direct mandate to resolve the anomaly.

**S01E08 — The Agent Arrives**
Ser Edwyn and his retinue begin lord interviews; Ser Aemon is first; the alliance between dragon and bastard knight is now a political fact. The collision is between Edwyn's administrative competence and a protocol set that does not cover this situation; what cannot remain unchanged after it is whether Ser Aemon's alliance position is a liability or leverage.

**S01E09 — First Encounter**
Ser Edwyn makes his first formal approach with the crown's authorization; it fails — she does not respond to Targaryen approach protocols. The collision is between the crown's legitimate legal right and the dragon's refusal to be a legal object; what cannot remain unchanged after it is whether Edwyn escalates, negotiates, or waits for instructions that will not arrive in time.

**S01E10 — The Offer**
Edwyn makes the administrative offer (rider, legitimate position, terms of compliance); the impasse is total — acceptance requires her to be a mount and she is not a mount. The collision is between the crown's need for resolution and the structural impossibility of resolution by any means Edwyn is authorized to use; what cannot remain unchanged after it is whether the next response from King's Landing escalates to destruction.

## Season Notes

- Taylor cannot speak verbally in S1. Communication is through behavior, bug deployment, and demonstration. No shortcuts.
- Raven travel time: King's Landing is 1+ weeks away by raven. The crown's response is not fast.
- E06 fire use: aftermath must be visible and costly-looking. A burned area, animals fled, the septon's congregation seeing something not easily categorized as divine.
- S1 ends with structural deadlock between Taylor and the crown's legal claim. S2 will escalate this.
