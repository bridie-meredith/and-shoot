## Chapter count rationale

**Selected: 20 chapters.**

Twenty sits at the midpoint of the 18-22 range, preserving both the hard floor margin and a buffer against over-inflation. The 14-delta trajectory distributes across three zones: setup (d01-d06) receives chapters 1-9, nine chapters for six deltas — this front-loads embeddedness before the lock zone, directly addressing the dramatist Phase 5 attempt-2 watch-item that setup must build attachment before collapse. Lock (d07-d10) receives chapters 10-16, seven chapters for four deltas — adequate space for the non-extractable confirmation sequence at d10, which requires more than one chapter of escalation to avoid feeling abrupt. Cascade (d11-d14) receives chapters 17-20, four chapters for four deltas — one delta per chapter, compressed, which satisfies the pulp-enthusiast watch-item (cascade zone must read as acceleration, not slow burn). At 18 chapters the cascade zone would still be four chapters but setup would compress to seven, likely under-developing the embeddedness; at 22 the lock zone would sprawl. Twenty is the cleanest load distribution.
