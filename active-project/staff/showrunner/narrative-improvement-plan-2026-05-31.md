## Per-item authoring spec

### Item 1 — c07 four-names consequence

**Where:** c07 chapter open, before the cart-blocked-lane line.

**What:** 1-3 bones registering the morning-after feeling of the sealed
form. Three candidate shapes (pick one or combine):

- **Absence-on-count.** Taylor's morning Hook-feed returns an absence
  at a recurring node — a body she has filed for two months that did
  not cross its threshold this morning. The count records the gap. She
  does not name what she knows.
- **News-in-the-murmur.** At the chandler's-storehouse corner the
  smallfolk murmur carries an off-rhythm word about a thing-that-
  happened-uphill in the night. Taylor files it without engaging. The
  Septon hears it too.
- **Substrate-shift in gait.** A patrol-pattern through the Hook the
  feed has not seen before. Bodies in lanes that previously held
  Black-faction routing. The new bodies file as filled-slot replacement.

**Bone-gate criteria to honor:**
- `EVENT-CONCRETE` (PROP-0022 spine-legibility): the bone is a concrete
  physical observation, not "she felt the consequence."
- `axes_in_motion`: register `moral_legibility_to_self -X` (the
  consequence is on-page; the ledger entry is undeniable now).
- `cost-paid`: the morning's emotional weight is the cost.
- `opposing-force-visible`: Halvard's voice carrying the news IS the
  opposing force; the chapter that already exists becomes the
  reckoning, not a separate ethics seminar.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- The Septon argument in c07 reads as arriving *because* of the
  consequence rather than separate from it.
- Cold-read says: c06 → c07 transition feels like a single accelerating
  pressure, not two adjacent unrelated chapters.

### Item 2 — Wren plant in c03 + c05

**c03 plant (morning market):** One bone, passive. Taylor's feed
returns Wren crossing through the salt-fish stall sightline before
Jarvis enters. Filed under the same recurring-body anchor she opened
in c02. No contact. No eye-meet. The body filed alongside the others
the market returns. Bone shape: passive observation; no axis movement.

**c05 plant (evening review):** One bone, passive. Taylor's Hook-feed
flat-read returns Wren's print held a length longer than the others.
The accounting closes; the print stays held. She does not name the
extra length. Bone shape: passive observation; no axis movement
(the held-extra-length IS the absence-of-axis-movement that lands).

**Acceptance criteria:**
- c06 Wren-route-offer reads as the closing of a four-month silence
  the reader has watched Taylor maintain.
- Cold-read says: "there's a way past — I been through" lands like a
  struck bell.

### Item 3 — Halvard body + Cobb founding entry

**Halvard body plant (c04 or c05):** One bone, passive. Taylor's feed
returns the Septon at his corner — body filed as fixture, his gait
mapped a hundred times, exchanged the nod a person gives a fixture
they have decided to greet. No contact. (Mirrors c07's "the nod a
person gives a fixture they have decided to greet" line and earns it.)

**Cobb founding entry plant (c01-c06; pick one earliest viable):**
One brief mention in a prologue or interior accounting block. The
"first name in the count" framing as a thing Taylor has carried since
before coverage came up. A child's name, a lane, a season-back fever.
The Cobb name does not need to be spoken — Taylor's ledger-arithmetic
can carry "the first name" anonymously and c07 makes the name explicit
on stage.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- c07 Halvard scene reads as an argument with someone the reader
  knows by reputation, not a stranger.
- Wenna Cobb lands as the name Taylor has been carrying, not a name
  authored for c07.

### Item 5 — Calendar pass

**Where:** Every chapter prologue. Add one calendar/season anchor per
prologue. Candidates:

- **Faith calendar.** The Maiden's name day. Father's day. A sept
  observance.
- **Market-day.** "Salt-day." "The fishmongers' three-day." (Hook
  vernacular; the rhythm of fixed market rotations.)
- **Weather/season.** Late-summer dust. The first cold. The wind off
  the bay turning.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- A reader can rebuild the calendar across the sub-section without
  digging.
- The two-months elapsed by c07 reads as carried, not announced.

**Bone-gate impact:** None — prologues are not bone-gated. Edit lands
at `/and-write` Phase 7 emit (prologue authored alongside scene-one
bones per convention) without re-running Phase 6.
