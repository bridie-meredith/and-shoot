## 1. Carriers by perspective

### perspective: protagonist

Taylor Hebert is the sole protagonist carrier. All nine protagonist-perspective axes are carried by Taylor; no split needed. POV hard fence (first-person only; non-Taylor chapters = marked interludes) makes this non-negotiable.

Axes carried: moral-framework (3→1), capability (3→7), position (1→1 rise-fall), social-tether (2→1), relational-anchor-status (3→1), moral-legibility-to-self (7→4), political-register-toward-elite (5→9), knowledge (3→8), agency (5→1).

No additional protagonist carriers. Taylor must do this alone; any second protagonist POV dilutes the claustrophobic irony of the contempt-without-refusal ending.

---

### perspective: antagonist

**Otto Hightower** (named in series chunk) is the primary antagonist carrier. He carries:
- moral-framework (antagonist: 7→8) — his pressure is calibrated asks, never threats; the pressure is being right about the arithmetic
- agency (antagonist: 7→9) — factional control over dynastic outcomes escalates through Taylor's deliveries
- knowledge (antagonist: 7→9) — intelligence holdings grow with each delivery
- social-tether (antagonist: 7→9) — factional apparatus cements cross-layer dominance
- position (antagonist: 6→8) — factional standing from advisory-from-outside toward Hand-in-fact
- political-register-toward-elite (antagonist: 7→8) — elite self-regard, structurally reinforced dynastic prerogative
- moral-legibility-to-self (antagonist: 6→7) — fully self-confirming political-actor self-knowledge

Otto alone is sufficient as the named antagonist-pressure source. The other axis (capability, antagonist: 5→8) and relational-anchor-status (antagonist: 5→6) move through the Green-faction apparatus — not through a second named antagonist but through the institutional structure Otto commands.

**Do we need additional named antagonist carriers?**

One additional named carrier is structurally required, not for axis coverage, but for embodiment of the apparatus pressure that Otto cannot carry in person:

- **Aemond Targaryen** (or a Green-faction enforcer figure): the capability and agency axes require a named face that can enact coercive consequences on [protect-target]'s position. Otto proposes and calculates; the coercive arm executes. Without a named coercive carrier, the apparatus-pressure on Taylor feels abstract and non-physical. Aemond is the canonical option (present at 122 AC court; capable of coercive acts; does not require much page-space to function). Walk-on/low-frequency use sufficient.

- **Alicent Hightower**: optional. She is the household anchor of Otto's political standing at 122 AC court and her presence gives [protect-target]'s protection logic its specific shape inside the Red Keep. Whether she is a named role or remains backstage is a [protect-target] identity question — if [protect-target] is embedded in Alicent's orbit, Alicent needs at least two scenes. If [protect-target] is a more peripheral figure, Alicent can remain background. Flag for margit to resolve at Phase 2 alongside [protect-target] identity.

Confirmed antagonist carriers: **Otto Hightower** (primary, named), **Aemond Targaryen** (secondary, walk-on), **Alicent Hightower** (conditional on [protect-target] identity — see section 5).

---

### perspective: supporting

Supporting carriers serve two structural functions:

**Function A — [cost-bearer]**: the un-priced relationship. Carries relational-anchor-status (world: 3→1) and the social-tether (world: 4→1) decline in its most particular form. Specification in section 4 below.

**Function B — witness-mirror**: one carrier who briefly sees what Taylor is becoming and names it, even once. This is the moral-legibility-to-self axis from outside — the world's collective moral legibility (3→2) needs a single on-stage representative capable of speaking it. Candidate options:
- a Flea Bottom figure who has known Taylor long enough to see the change (smallfolk-register, witness-vocabulary)
- a broken maester with chain stripped, above-apparatus enough to read the pattern, named once and not again

One witness-mirror slot. Not a confidant. One scene, one naming, then silence or disappearance. The axis requires a witness; the register requires the witness not to be Taylor's friend.

**Function C — [protect-target]**: court-tier figure whose survival anchors the trades. Carries position (world) and dynastic-stability pressure as embodied cost of the arrangement. Specification in section 5 below.

Supporting carrier count: [cost-bearer] + witness-mirror + [protect-target] = 3 slots. Each distinct. No overlap permitted — if [cost-bearer] serves as the witness-mirror, the relational weight collapses the witness function into sentiment; the naming must come from outside the attachment.

### perspective: world

World-perspective axes need institutional embodiments — on-stage representatives whose movements carry the world's state without becoming POV characters. These are not protagonists or antagonists; they are witnesses and fixtures.

**community substrate + smallfolk-agency axes (social-tether world 4→1, agency world 3→1):** One to two Flea Bottom fixtures — recurring presences who establish and then lose the community substrate. The cond-westerosi-witness-vocabulary behavior applies here; these characters use a specific witness register. One block-fixture type (seen regularly, does not speak to Taylor's interiority) and the [cost-bearer] character together carry this axis.

**dynastic-position axis (position world 4→1) + knowledge world (2→2 flat):** The dynastic position decline is backstage-structural; it does not require a named world-perspective carrier beyond what Otto and the apparatus already embody. The knowledge-world axis (smallfolk-systematic-awareness) starts and ends at 2 — it stays flat. No named carrier needed.

**collective-moral-legibility (moral-legibility-to-self world 3→2) + political-register world (4→2):** The maester-chronicler archetype (cond-maester-chronicler-voice behavior) carries this in a coda-only capacity. One scene or interlude at close. This is the "witness who chronicles but does not act" function that the prompt-binding calls for under the witness archetype.

**institutional-capacity decline (capability world 4→3):** Carried implicitly by the institutional apparatus's behavior across the book; does not require a named on-stage carrier.

World-perspective named carriers: one block-fixture Flea Bottom presence (recurring low-frequency), one maester-chronicler (coda only). The [cost-bearer] character also carries community-substrate as the most specific world-axis instance.

## 3. Tonal / voice register requirements

**TAYLOR-HEBERT:** Cold-utilitarian interiority. Ledger runs on-page. Affect suppressed, not absent. Inner monologue rare on Earth-Bet proper nouns; the Khepri-residue is named internally in her own private register, not translated into Westerosi vocabulary. Theme never spoken aloud. Register: precise, economical, audit-trail prose.

**OTTO-HIGHTOWER:** Gravitas without menace. He never threatens; the menace is the arithmetic. His dialogue is proposal-register — framing, not demanding. The cond-maester-chronicler-voice is adjacent but not identical; Otto is active, not archival. Register: institutional, unhurried, exact.

**AEMOND-TARGARYEN:** Coercive instrument. Minimum dialogue. Register: curtness with implied violence. Not a talker. His function is embodied consequence, not argumentation.

**ALICENT-HIGHTOWER (if activated):** Court-tier surface legibility — Taylor can observe her and the observation earns contempt-specificity. Register: formal court-tier, warmth-as-performance. The political-register-toward-elite axis earns its articulation from characters like Alicent providing specific, observable material.

**COST-BEARER:** Smallfolk register. Witness-vocabulary per cond-westerosi-witness-vocabulary. Gallows humor permitted; no sentiment performance. The un-priced quality of this relationship must be present without being telegraphed — the character has to read as genuinely attached, not as structural-symbol. Age band and relational vector specified in section 4.

**PROTECT-TARGET:** Court-tier; political-register surface; does not know they are being protected. Their dialogue and behavior must be legible enough to Otto's apparatus to justify ongoing trades, and specific enough that Taylor's contempt toward them (if they are elite) earns particularity. Register determined by identity — see section 5.

**WITNESS-MIRROR:** Either smallfolk-register (if Flea Bottom placement) or stripped-institutional (if broken-maester type). One function: see the repetition and name it. Register must be different enough from Taylor's interior that the naming lands as external rather than internal monologue.

**FLEA-BOTTOM-FIXTURE:** Witness-vocabulary per cond-westerosi-witness-vocabulary. Non-interpretive. Reports what is present. Does not explain or theorize. Register: ambient, observational, subsistence-focused.

**MAESTER-CHRONICLER:** Archival voice per cond-maester-chronicler-voice. Deduction from record. Cold precision. The closing irony of the book's institutional accountability is carried here — the ledger that Taylor ran privately is echoed in the archival record that runs impersonally. Register: formal-archival, counterfactual inference, no affect.

## 4. [cost-bearer] specification

**Structural requirements (from series chunk + signature):**
- Dies in Dance's opening Flea Bottom violence — this is a hard fence
- The relationship must be un-priced: Taylor refuses to enter [cost-bearer] into any ledger calculation from d02 onward
- Present from near-open (d01 rescue establishes initial attachment)
- Becomes load-bearing in the network by d08 without knowing it — moves in parts of Flea Bottom where Taylor's insects risk triggering the witch-label
- Identified by Otto's network at d11 as a potential courier-adjacent use-vector — Taylor intercepts this, screens it, does not tell [cost-bearer]
- At d14: dies in the streets Taylor had mapped, in the violence the trades enabled

**Age band:** Child or early adolescent (8–13). This is required, not optional. The closing-image irony requires the cost-bearer to be someone whose value to Taylor is purely relational — not a political asset, not a functional partner, not someone who could be traded for — and Westerosi smallfolk children in Flea Bottom carry the community-substrate axis with the specificity the signature demands. An adult cost-bearer risks becoming a foil or ally; a child in this age band reads as genuinely outside the ledger.

The prompt-binding names the lyra-register archetype for this slot — specific ward child as closing-image cost-bearer. The boundary-scope names the street-runner-child (trust-test) and attached-smallfolk (single relationship Taylor will not sacrifice) as adjacent archetypes. All three point to the same profile.

**Relational vector:** Found-kin or attachment-by-rescue. Taylor rescues [cost-bearer] from acute danger at d01 (first deployment of insect-sense). The attachment forms because [cost-bearer] attaches to Taylor — not because Taylor pursued it. Taylor's refusal to price the relationship reads as atonement-working at d02; it reads as catastrophic miscalculation at d14. The relational vector must be asymmetric in a specific way: [cost-bearer] trusts Taylor completely; Taylor cares but refuses to account for them.

**What un-priced means concretely:** [cost-bearer] moves freely through Flea Bottom, including in areas adjacent to Taylor's insect coverage. Taylor routes around using [cost-bearer] as an intelligence source to Otto. She covers [cost-bearer]'s movements but does not tell them. She treats this as protection (d11). She does not obtain consent. She does not tell [cost-bearer] what the network is, who Otto is, what the trades mean. The relationship is authentic from [cost-bearer]'s side and surveilled-but-unacknowledged from Taylor's side — the surveillance is the repetition, even at the intimate scale.

**Gender and social specificity:** Open. Flea Bottom placement requires smallfolk class — no noble blood, no Faith attachment, no guild-level economic standing. Specific trade-family background (tanner, renderer, dyer, stall-keeper) grounds the witness-vocabulary register. Margit should select from smallfolk-child archetypes without reusing Nessa from prior series-plan path brainstorm, per name-novelty requirement.

## 5. [protect-target] specification

**Structural requirements (from series chunk + trajectory):**
- Court-tier; Green-faction adjacent or structurally within Green-faction's orbit
- Sufficiently vulnerable that Otto's protection requires an ongoing asset (not a one-trade transaction); the vulnerability must be real enough that Taylor cannot simply assess it as resolved after d03
- Survival is structurally meaningful to the succession calculus — Otto trades his intelligence architecture to protect them because their death would damage or complicate the Green position; the protection is not purely personal sentiment
- Taylor never becomes friends with [protect-target]; the arrangement is at distance, mediated through Otto; [protect-target] does not know Taylor exists
- The protection logic must sustain 14 deltas of escalating asks — meaning [protect-target]'s vulnerability must have a structural source that does not resolve: faction threat, succession adjacency, legitimacy question, or exposure risk that renews with each dynastic maneuver

**Identity options (margit must decide; three candidate shapes):**

*Option A — a young Green-faction-adjacent figure whose legitimacy is contested:* A child or adolescent in the Green orbit whose claim, survival, or position the Black faction would benefit from eliminating. Not a central canon figure (Aegon, Aemond, Helaena are too load-bearing to the Dance's own plot mechanics). A figure who is structurally meaningful to Otto but not to the canonical war's turning points. This allows Taylor's protection work to be effective (keeping them alive) while the Dance ignites anyway on separate structural logic.

*Option B — Alicent's household figure:* Someone in Alicent's personal orbit whose exposure would damage Alicent's standing and therefore Otto's. This activates the Alicent-Hightower conditional slot from section 2 — Alicent becomes a named role at low frequency. Concrete: a ward, a companion, a young woman of the household whose past or parentage Otto needs suppressed.

*Option C — a maester or factional clerk:* A figure in the Small Council's apparatus with knowledge of the Green faction's internal communications that, if captured or turned by the Blacks, would compromise Otto's position. Lower emotional register; the protection is purely instrumental from Otto's side. Advantage: makes Taylor's intelligence function feel more mechanical, which suits the cold-utilitarian register. Disadvantage: a purely instrumental [protect-target] weakens the tragic irony of Taylor's sacrifice — she trades everything to protect a ledger entry, not a person.

**Recommended shape:** Option A or B. The trades need to feel like Taylor is protecting someone — not just a piece of paper. Option A is preferred if it can be invented without canon collision; Option B is preferred if Alicent-activation is desirable for other scene purposes. Option C is a fallback.

**What Otto must gain from the trades specifically:** Each time Taylor delivers intelligence that prevents a threat to [protect-target], Otto's factional standing strengthens by one increment — the trades are not charity, they consolidate Green position over Maegor's Holdfast and the Small Council. This means [protect-target]'s survival is the carrot and the mechanism: keeping them alive gives Taylor a reason to keep delivering, which keeps Otto's faction advancing. The trades work. That is the catastrophe.

## 6. Recommended total roster size and rationale

**Confirmed named actors:** 7 (Taylor, Otto, Aemond, [cost-bearer], [protect-target], witness-mirror, Flea-Bottom-fixture, maester-chronicler = 8 including coda-only).

**Conditional:** Alicent (1 additional if [protect-target] embeds in her orbit).

**Recommended total: 8 confirmed, 9 if Alicent activated.**

**Rationale:** Single-POV single-book tragedy. Every actor who is not Taylor or Otto is a pressure-surface or an axis-carrier. The 14-delta arc runs through d01–d14 across 18–22 chapters; most chapters will carry 2–3 axis-moves; each axis-move needs at most one on-stage carrier. Expanding beyond 9 risks:
- diffusing the community-substrate decline across too many named smallfolk, weakening the particularity of [cost-bearer]'s death
- adding court-tier faces that multiply the political-register-toward-elite axis without deepening it (contempt earns specificity from few faces observed closely, not many faces observed briefly)
- creating confidant-pressure on Taylor through sheer cast mass — this POV requires Taylor to be essentially alone inside her own accounting

The maester-chronicler is coda-only (one scene; no active character pressure). The Flea Bottom fixture is low-frequency (recurring ambient, no sustained dialogue arc). Aemond is walk-on at structural crisis points only. These three carry their axis function with minimal page-mass.

The load-bearing creative work at Phase 2 is the identity resolution for [cost-bearer] and [protect-target]. Once those are named, the rest of the roster is margit-filter from existing planetos library candidates — Otto, Aemond, and Alicent all have existing library entries.
