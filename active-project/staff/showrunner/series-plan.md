## Path brainstorm — round 1

```
path-1 (lens: relational):
  motivation: Taylor stays operative in King's Landing because Nessa — a Flea Bottom girl of eight who attached to her in the first week — is the one person who receives her without fear, and Taylor cannot leave someone she has already made dependent.
  anchor: Nessa — Flea Bottom girl, approximately eight at 122 AC; the one relationship Taylor's moral accounting refuses to price, resident in the Hook from scene one, closing-image cost-bearer.
  escalation: Taylor builds an insect-network intelligence apparatus covering the Hook and surrounding wards, positioning Nessa's block as a protected node — a place no faction moves through without Taylor knowing; she sells this coverage to Otto as calibrated intelligence, buying Nessa's street physical safety with each trade.
  trade: Taylor accepts becoming Otto's readable asset — feeding him the information that consolidates the Green faction's succession grip — because every piece she hands him buys another season where Nessa's alley stays outside the war's footprint.
  irony: The intelligence Taylor feeds Otto accelerates the succession calculus that ignites the Dance; when the dragons come to King's Landing, the Flea Bottom wards Taylor's network marked as protected are the first to burn — Nessa dies not despite Taylor's protection architecture but inside it, in the exact streets Taylor had mapped, in the fire Taylor's trades made possible.
```

```
path-2 (lens: political):
  motivation: Taylor holds no position at open and refuses to seek one — she treats institutional invisibility as the only safe operating condition for a cold-utilitarian with parahuman residue in a court she cannot fully read.
  anchor: Nessa — Hook-resident girl, eight years old, resident in Taylor's Flea Bottom circuit from scene one; the one relationship Taylor will not run a formal cost-benefit on.
  escalation: Otto Hightower identifies Taylor's insect-network intelligence capability and offers her a position — not a title, not a rank, but a function: calibrated information-delivery to the Green faction in exchange for his quiet protection of Lucerys in the succession calculus. Taylor accepts. The first trade is the auditable mistake.
  trade: To sustain the position and keep Lucerys alive, Taylor progressively feeds Otto intelligence that consolidates Green-faction institutional control — each delivery is priced as acceptable because it is smaller than a war. By the time Taylor understands that the position has made her the instrument of the exact dynastic strangling she meant to prevent, she cannot refuse it without triggering the Lucerys exposure she was preventing in the first place.
  irony: Taylor's political usefulness to Otto depended on her being outside any formal faction — anonymous, unreadable, with no institutional loyalty the Blacks could exploit. The position she refused to hold was the only thing that made her safe to use. The moment she accepted the function she was protecting against, she became the mechanism of the war she delayed; and when Dance ignites and Flea Bottom burns, the position that was never formal enough to protect Nessa is the reason Nessa is in the street.
```

```
path-3 (lens: interior):
  motivation: Taylor's compulsion is control-through-accounting — she cannot stop converting people into variables in a prevention ledger, because the moment she stops calculating she has to feel what Gold Morning cost her.
  anchor: Nessa — the one entry in the ledger Taylor refuses to price, which makes her the blind spot the accounting cannot see.
  escalation: Each trade Taylor makes to protect Lucerys requires she calculate more people as acceptable losses, hardening the compulsion until she is running the same utilitarian triage that made Khepri, not the person who survived her.
  trade: Taylor delivers Rhaenyra's network to Otto because the accounting says Rhaenyra's survival is less probable than Lucerys's — and the calculation is correct, and the calculation destroys the only faction that would have kept Nessa's street intact.
  irony: The compulsion Taylor used to escape Gold-Morning's guilt — keep counting, keep preventing, stay functional — is exactly what she was when she ended a world; she does not become Khepri at the close, she reveals she never stopped.
```

```
path-4 (lens: penitential):
  motivation: Taylor is atoning for Khepri — for the mass override of human will she performed at Gold Morning; she came to Westeros with no plan except to be useful, to heal, to do good without taking control, because the last time she took control she made fifty thousand people into instruments.
  anchor: Nessa — the Flea Bottom girl Taylor keeps out of the ledger, the one relationship she refuses to price, her proof that she can protect without possessing.
  escalation: The atonement work requires Taylor to become exactly what she atoned for — she rebuilds the Khepri architecture in miniature, threading an intelligence network through Flea Bottom, reading bodies and routing information and moving people without their knowledge, because the alternative is Lucerys dies and the war comes and Nessa burns in it.
  trade: The protection network she builds to save Nessa from the Dance becomes the information infrastructure Otto Hightower needs to consolidate the Greens' position; Taylor's penitential smallfolk-care is the instrument of the Flea Bottom violence she was preventing.
  irony: She came to King's Landing to stop being the person who overrides human will for good reasons; she leaves having overridden more human will, for better reasons, to a worse end — the atonement was the repetition.
```

```
path-5 (lens: escape):
  motivation: Taylor wants out of the obligation she carried into Westeros — she came to prevent one death, not to become an instrument of court politics, and by act two she is trying to engineer the conditions under which she can simply disappear: Lucerys protected, Nessa placed somewhere safe, herself gone before the war she can feel building catches her inside it.
  anchor: Nessa — the one tether she cannot abstract; Taylor's exit is conditional on Nessa being clear first.
  escalation: Every move Taylor makes to secure the exit (buying Nessa safe passage, negotiating release from Otto's network, acquiring the coin and cover to vanish) requires giving Otto one more piece of calibrated intelligence, which makes her more embedded, more necessary, and more surveilled — the exit door moves farther the harder she walks toward it.
  trade: To finally buy free, Taylor gives Otto the one piece of intelligence that would secure her release — the location and timing of Lucerys's planned movement, framed as a peace-routing — which she has calculated will go through a channel that keeps him safe; Otto routes it through a channel that does not.
  irony: The act she performs to disappear — the final trade she tells herself closes the account and frees her — is the act that kills Lucerys, lights the war, and puts Nessa in the path of the violence Taylor spent the whole book trying to step out of.
```

path-6 (lens: vocational):
  motivation: Taylor's craft is organizational pattern-reading — she sees how complex systems move, where the pressure points are, which node to remove or insert to shift the whole; Khepri-residue insect control is the physical instrument of that same logic at ground level.
  anchor: Nessa — the one node in Taylor's network whose value she refuses to calculate, and who therefore never appears in any of the trades.
  escalation: Each intervention Taylor makes is more structurally correct than the last; Otto recognizes her pattern-reading as the intelligence asset he has been missing, and the more accurately she works, the more load-bearing her position inside his architecture becomes — she becomes the system she is trying to redirect.
  trade: To keep Lucerys protected inside the succession calculus, Taylor must give Otto calibrated intelligence about the Blacks' internal movements — the good craft (accurate threat-mapping) is the instrument that hands the Greens the organizational advantage they need to survive long enough to start the war.
  irony: The skill that makes Taylor uniquely capable of preventing the Dance is the same skill that makes her uniquely useful to the faction driving it; her competence is not wasted on the wrong cause — it is the wrong cause's most effective tool, and the ledger she keeps proves it, line by line, if read backward from Nessa's death.
