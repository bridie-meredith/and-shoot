# Series Plan — Dragon Gate Foreclosure (one-book series)

Detail companion to `memory.md`. Slug-only references throughout (no titles).

---

## Theme

Foreclosure as structure. Road to hell paved with good intentions. Each defensible choice closes off a better outcome that was still possible. The book's final state is its verdict — theme is structure, never statement.

## Series question

Whether competence and accurate observation are enough to keep one's hand clean inside an institution one cannot audit. The answer the book gives is structural, not stated.

## Series drama

Taylor Hebert, post-Khepri, lands range-capped to ~one city block in King's Landing pre-Dance and rebuilds operational agency on a single Dragon Gate market block. Her working model — "speaking the patron's dialect" — calibrates true reports into Watch, guild, and steward registers, treating those registers as neutral channels. The Hightower patronage architecture beneath the captain harvests her labor without her knowing. The Dance is the off-page weather that finally activates the persons-risk infrastructure her ladder has helped construct; the same infrastructure runs to completion on the contact she vouched for and then on her.

## Plot

- **Start:** Late summer 126 AC. Taylor arrives mid-market-fire at Dragon Gate. Insects respond visibly to her distress; three gold cloaks and a septon witness; septon shouts "witch." Held overnight in the Dragon Gate guardhouse, questioned in Old Valyrian she does not speak, released as "lunatic-not-worth-paperwork" on a captain's word. The witch-label is permanent operational infrastructure.
- **Protagonist arc:** 36-month back-loaded compression.
  - Act 1 (months 1–12) — grief-recovery + first block-network seeded under unreadable-to-Taylor friction. Calm of Act 1 belongs to her perception, not the world.
  - Act 2 (months 13–26) — faction temperature bleeds into street-level. Cost-per-intervention rises each quarter. Operational resistance accumulates as terrain-tightening.
  - Act 3 (months 27–36) — last viable pivot at M27–28 under accumulated weight. Pivot closes wrong from the logic of everything built before. Viserys dies ~M36 off-page. War begins off-page. Book closes on first irrecoverable street-level atrocity directly consequent on the wrong pivot.
- **6-rung patron-dialect ladder:** M4 seed (apprentice/grain-watering/Watch/riot-risk) → M9–10 (merchant/short-weight/guild/stability-risk) → M14–15 (dockworker/slowdown/steward/sedition-risk — REQUIRED ENFORCEMENT-OVERSHOOT BEAT) → M18–19 (Mira-rung; smuggling rumor in Watch-risk language; contact protected off-record; Taylor sees the model "working" on someone she cares about) → M24 (physician/unlicensed treatment/guild auditor/poisoning-risk) → M27–28 RECOGNITION-FAILURE (Black-adjacent contact; smuggling-of-persons-risk to Watch officer; contact detained; Watch persons-risk sweep escalates; contact killed in custody during a wider sweep).
- **Mira's death:** ON-PAGE in the Watch persons-risk sweep expansion, late Act 3.
- **End:** Taylor processed by the same Watch sweep three days after the contact's death. The captain signs her paperwork. She dies in the Dragon Gate guardhouse — not executed, not martyred, processed. Final image: her own range-capped insects registering no movement inside the building she is in. Viserys is dead off-page; the war has begun off-page; her paperwork reflects the new regime's intake numbers as administrative realignment.

## Laws / Lore / Behaviors (full set in world-notes.md and warehouse condition cards)

LAWS:
- Earth-Bet does not intrude. No portals, no other capes, no Tinker tech.
- Westerosi cruelty (chattel-smallfolk, dynastic warfare, dragonfire-as-policy, judicial torture) is the local register; not flagged as anomaly.
- The book is one series, one arc, one closing. The ending IS the verdict.
- Taylor's insect-control is range-capped to ~one city block (~100–150m, hard constant). Khepri-vector inert. AU divergence from canon shard behavior must be legible in prose, not silently elided.
- The witch-label is permanent operational infrastructure. Never lifted, never corrected. Only layered.
- The patron-dialect model is invisible to Taylor as a model. The prose never names it. Reader-inference only.
- The model-falsification meta-statement ("her reports were heard as authorizations, not calibrations") MUST NEVER appear in prose. Lit-snob non-negotiable.
- No moral narration. The book does not tell the reader what to think about Taylor's choices.
- The captain is opaque. No interiority rendered.
- No named family for Mira or for the contact (Daveth). Smallfolk are substrate, not protagonist-fodder.

LORE:
- Setting: King's Landing, late Viserys I, 126–129 AC. Pre-Dance.
- Dragons exist and are bonded political weapons. Magic is rare. Faith of the Seven is the establishment religion; Green-adjacent via Hightower patronage.
- Taylor's first faction-gravity is Watch-track via the Captain of the Dragon Gate precinct. His promotions came through Hightower-controlled channels.

BEHAVIORS:
- Taylor's power presents ambient-not-directed. She knows without choosing to know within her cap.
- Taylor reaches for the lever that works when the alternative is watching people die. This is reflex, not malice.
- Taylor carries Khepri-weight. She is not Skitter-era; the prose must not write her as if she were.

## Seasons (slug only; no titles)

### s01 (only season; this is a one-book series)

**Chunk statement:**

The Watch's persons-risk infrastructure — built on Taylor's contracted labor and filed reports — runs inside a Hightower patronage chain that harvests the same data it appears to commission, invisible at every level until the Dance activates it as a targeting apparatus.

The collision produces a closed loop: the infrastructure turns on the contact Taylor vouched for, then on Taylor, with no external authority positioned to audit the institution that ran both operations.

**Season scope:** the full arc as detailed under "Plot" above. The 36-month back-loaded compression, the 6-rung ladder, Mira's on-page death, Taylor's bureaucratic processing at close. Season planning (drama / vibe-cloud delta / content beats) is owned by `/and-season s01` Phase 1; per-episode boundaries are produced by interpretive split during that command.
