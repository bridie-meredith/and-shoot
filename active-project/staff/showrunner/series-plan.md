# Series Plan — Taylor in Westeros
## Slug: taylor-hebert-westeros

### Series Drama
A parahuman survivor with complete foreknowledge of the Dance of Dragons starts eleven years old and nameless in the Riverlands shadow of Harrenhal, nine years before the war ignites. The succession fracture expands on its own momentum; the feudal order punishes every step of accumulation by someone with no legitimate claim; and the person who once stopped an apocalypse must build from zero while watching the machinery of the next one turn.

### Series Question
Can someone who already paid the full price once build enough from nothing to matter when the second catastrophe arrives — and should they?

### Theme
The cost of building when everything resists you — and what you become in the process of building it anyway.

### Season Chunks

**Season 1 — 120–122 AC**
The impressment census, the dying septon's collapse, and the Hightower castellan's consolidation converge before Taylor has any cover; her fauna network becomes visible to a castellan's man who writes reports, and the only move that preserves her is one that names her as useful to the wrong person. The window of unobserved development closes with her inside a structure she did not choose and cannot control.

**Season 2 — 122–126 AC**
Positioned inside a minor Riverlord's household, Taylor's escalation reflex surfaces during a border conflict and burns the cover she spent two years building — her intervention is too precise, too decisive, and the lord she served survives only because she violated every instruction to stay unseen. The household survives; her anonymity does not; and the factions that were not watching her before are watching now.

**Season 3 — 126–129 AC**
Green and Black recruiting pressure reaches the Riverlands simultaneously; Taylor's intelligence network is the most complete picture of Riverlands disposition either faction has, and both sides move to acquire it — meaning acquire her. The last neutral space collapses when the broker she used to stay unaligned is killed, and the season ends with Taylor holding the network alone, with no structure around her and the Dance nine months out.

**Season 4 — 129 AC onward**
The Dance ignites and dragonfire strips the Riverlands of every established authority in weeks; Taylor's ground-level network is the only coherent intelligence picture in the theater, and every surviving lord, both Targaryen factions, and the smallfolk she built it through all have claims on what she does with it. Her neutrality — the one thing that kept her alive and free — cannot survive the war's first season, and everything she built from nothing now becomes the mechanism by which the catastrophe consumes her.

### Escalation spine
Anonymity (S1) → Invisibility (S2) → Neutrality (S3) → Sovereignty (S4) — each lost in sequence, each loss irreversible, the network simultaneously her only asset and the instrument of her exposure.

### Forward flags
- S4 end-state is open: season 4 episode planning must commit to a specific landing (survival-at-cost, transformation, or destruction) — not a blocker now, required for S4 planning.
- S1 pulp-enthusiast condition: episode planning for S1 must distribute complications across all episodes, not concentrate payoff at the finale. The castellan's-man-writing-reports must be a running escalating process, not a single end-of-season reveal.
