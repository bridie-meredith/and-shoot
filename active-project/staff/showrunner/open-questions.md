# Open Questions
# Internal. Written in step 1a. Ordered by dependency. Each resolved OQ moves to world-notes.md.

## Dependency-ordered

### OQ-1: Power state at arrival
Does Taylor arrive with her power intact, partly recovered, or absent? Subordinate questions: if intact, is it full-range (insects + Khepri-control) or reduced (insects only)? If absent, does it recover during the book? This decision shapes every plot vector — recruitment, identification, intervention capacity.

### OQ-2: Arrival circumstance and identity-handle
How does she arrive in King's Landing — body-only stranded in a Flea Bottom alley, or with some artifact/condition that signals foreign origin? What does Westeros initially read her as: foreign smallfolk, suspected sorceress, captured stranger, refugee from across the Narrow Sea? Dependent on OQ-1 (an obviously powered arrival forces faster identification).

### OQ-3: Time-of-entry within the pre-Dance window
Does the book open early in Viserys's decline (more political slack, slower clock) or late (Viserys dying, weeks-to-months before Aegon II's crowning)? Dependent on the desired ratio of slow-burn intervention to acceleration. Determines whether Viserys-death is mid-book or near-endpoint.

### OQ-4: Faction proximity at story open
Which faction's gravity does Taylor enter first — Green (Hightower / Alicent / Aegon II camp), Black (Rhaenyra / Daemon camp), neutral street-level (smallfolk / Flea Bottom / Faith), or third party (Velaryon / Sea Snake / merchant)? Determines who finds her and how she becomes politically legible. Dependent on OQ-2.

### OQ-5: The form of the "first bad thing for good reasons"
What is the inciting good-intentioned bad thing that establishes the pattern? It must be small enough to be defensible at the time, large enough that the reader registers it as the first step on the road. Subordinate: is it interpersonal (she kills/maims someone to save someone else), structural (she leaks information that destabilizes a brake on the war), or epidemiological (she does something to the city that helps the smallfolk but hands a faction a tool)? Dependent on OQ-4.

### OQ-6: Closing-shape — what the verdict looks like
What is the final state the book closes on? Subordinate: does Taylor survive? Does she understand what she has done? Does she succeed in any sense, even an ironic one? Is the Dance prevented, redirected, accelerated, or unchanged? The closing-shape constrains every prior beat because the foreclosure pattern only resolves into a specific final state. Dependent on OQ-3 and OQ-5.

### OQ-7: Cast scope
How many named recurring non-Taylor characters does the book carry? Subordinate: which of the canonical pre-Dance figures (Viserys, Otto, Alicent, Rhaenyra, Daemon, Larys Strong, Criston Cole, Helaena, Aegon, Aemond, the Sea Snake) appear on-page versus off-page; how many original-character smallfolk anchor Taylor's attachment-network. Dependent on OQ-4 and OQ-6.
