# Open Questions
# Internal. Written in step 1a. Ordered by dependency. Each resolved OQ moves to world-notes.md.

## OQ-1: What form does Taylor reincarnate as?
Options: human (low-born or other), beast (dragon? direwolf? large predator?), other (weirwood-touched? maegi? something liminal?).
Stakes: This determines the entire dramatic vocabulary of the series. Beast → apex predator / monster's POV. Human → legitimacy-hunger, infiltration, faction play. Other → wild card.
Dependency: All other OQs depend on this.

## OQ-2: What era specifically within "leadup to Dance or earlier"?
Options: Deep leadup (~100-110 AC, early Viserys I reign), immediate leadup (~120-128 AC, succession crisis visible), or earlier (Jaehaerys era ~50-100 AC, less locked-in timeline).
Stakes: Determines how much she can anticipate vs. how far the catastrophe is from landing. Closer = more tension; earlier = more freedom.
Dependency: Depends on OQ-1 (beast lifespan vs. human lifespan matters for "how early").

## OQ-3: What region and social position does she start in?
Options: King's Landing (high-stakes court, dense politics), the Reach/Riverlands (contested ground, military action), the North/Wolfswood (isolated, dangerous, far from the Dance), Dragonstone or Driftmark (Targaryen heartland, near the fault line).
Stakes: Determines her first adversaries, first allies, and what escalation looks like in episode 1.
Dependency: Depends on OQ-1 (beast in the North vs. human in King's Landing are different stories) and OQ-2 (some locations are more relevant at certain eras).

## OQ-4: Does she retain parahuman capabilities?
Options: Full toolkit retained in some adapted form (Skitter-equivalent in this world), partial/degraded (tactical cognition only, no bugs), or none (pure skill and memory, no powers).
Stakes: Determines the series power ceiling and whether she's a superhuman actor or a very dangerous ordinary person. Affects pacing and escalation ceiling.
Dependency: Depends on OQ-1 (a beast body may be the power; a human body without powers is a very different story than one with them).

## OQ-5: What is her initial situation and entry point?
Options: Infant/child reincarnation (slow start, memory-but-helpless phase), adult reincarnation (immediate action capability), mid-crisis entry (she arrives just as something is already on fire).
Stakes: Determines episode 1 dramatic pressure and how fast "fast" actually is.
Dependency: Depends on OQ-1, OQ-3, OQ-4.
