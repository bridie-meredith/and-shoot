# Open Questions
# Seeded at 1a from prompt-binding.md remaining-open parameters. Dependency-ordered.
# Each resolved OQ moves to world-notes.md.

## OQ-1: Mechanism of Taylor's arrival in Westeros
Admissible: portal, reincarnation (born into Westerosi body), post-death afterlife / second-life, Shard-mediated transit, or unexplained / in medias res. Sets the frame for power state, knowledge of context, and social position. Earliest in chain.

## OQ-2: Taylor's power state post-Gold-Morning / post-transit
Admissible: full Queen Administrator + multi-trigger swarm-sense, severed from QA but retaining residual capability, swarm-sense only at human-baseline insect availability, no powers at all (skill and memory only), or altered/repurposed by transit. Depends on OQ-1 (mechanism may impose state).

## OQ-3: Time of arrival relative to story-open
Admissible: just-arrived (days/weeks; outsider posture), embedded for months/years (established but still foreign), or arrived as a child / grew up in Westeros (assimilated, native-seeming). Depends on OQ-1 (some mechanisms imply some windows).

## OQ-4: Knowledge of Westeros context at story-open
Admissible: ignorant (no foreknowledge of Targaryen factions or Dance), partially informed (knows current court, not future events), fully prescient (knows the Dance is coming and roughly when), or selectively prescient (knows fragments / misremembers details). Depends on OQ-1 and OQ-3.

## OQ-5: Political position in King's Landing at story-open
Admissible: smallfolk, hedge knight's ward, household servant, courtier (low rank), foreign sellsword, mystery figure / suspected witch, captive, or hidden advisor to a faction. Sets the social register and access surface. Depends on OQ-3 and OQ-4.

## OQ-6: Targaryen-faction proximity
Admissible: Rhaenyra's circle (Black faction), Aegon II's circle (Green faction), Viserys I's court (pre-split), both / floats between, or neither / outsider to court. Determines who the false-ally Targaryen-patron is. Depends on OQ-5.

## OQ-7: Dragon involvement — does Taylor interact with dragons; is a load-bearing bad act dragon-adjacent
Admissible: dragons are central (a bad act involves a dragon directly — bonding, killing, manipulating, preventing a hatching, etc.), dragons are present but adjacent (Taylor orbits them without acting on them), or dragons are backdrop only (the Dance is dragon-war, but Taylor's acts don't touch them). Depends on OQ-5 and OQ-6.

## OQ-8: The specific load-bearing "bad thing done for good reason"
Admissible: assassination of a key figure, regicide, manipulating succession, suppressing or amplifying a prophecy, raising/binding/exposing something supernatural, poisoning, exposing a secret, saving the wrong person, or a chain of smaller acts whose aggregate is the bad act. Depends on OQ-5, OQ-6, OQ-7. This is the spine of the plot.

## OQ-9: The "later turns out to be bad" mechanism
Admissible: directly causes the Dance, accelerates the Dance, makes the Dance worse than it would have been, butterflies a different catastrophe instead of preventing the Dance, or ruins / kills a specific person whose loss is the cost. Depends on OQ-8.

## OQ-10: Whether the Dance is the engine or the backdrop of the story
Admissible: story climaxes inside the Dance (Dance is the engine; the catastrophe Taylor caused unfolds on-page), or story closes before the Dance ignites with Taylor's act as the strike-match (Dance is the backdrop; the catastrophe is implied / chronicler-framed). Depends on OQ-8 and OQ-9.

## OQ-11: Cost-bearer specifics — who exactly is the named innocent in Taylor's care
Admissible: a smallfolk child, a low-status adult (servant, hedge knight, septa), a Targaryen child whose protection Taylor undertook, a dragon-bonded innocent, or an OC tied to Taylor's specific Westeros placement. Depends on OQ-5 and OQ-8.

## OQ-12: End-state valence
Admissible: bad-place (Taylor alive and broken, or dead, with the Dance ignited as her doing), ambiguous-place (cost paid, recognition complete, future uncertain), or pyrrhic-good (something is saved but at a cost that makes the saving Pyrrhic). Depends on OQ-9 and OQ-10.

## OQ-13: Closing image / final beat type
Admissible: chronicler's verdict (maester writes the entry that confirms cause), Taylor's recognition tableau (the moment she sees what she did), the cost-bearer's fate as final beat, the Dance's first ignition (a specific event opening), or a stranger / opposite-number's mirroring beat. Depends on OQ-12.

## OQ-14: POV / narrative voice
Admissible: first-person Taylor, close-third Taylor exclusively, multi-POV including the chronicler / false-ally / cost-bearer, or frame-narrated (the maester is the narrator and Taylor is rendered in third). Affects how recognition lands.

## OQ-15: Tone register
Admissible: grim-literary (Westeros baseline), tragic-elegiac (mournful, post-event grammar), ironic-cold (surgical chronicler distance), or black-comic (dark humor as cope). Sets prose temperature throughout.

## OQ-16: Audience cohort
Admissible: Worm-literate (assumes reader knows Gold Morning, QA, Skitter), ASOIAF-literate (assumes reader knows the Dance, the Targaryens, prophecy structures), both, or neither (story is self-contained, both contexts loaded in-text). Affects how much of the Worm and Westeros lore is rendered explicitly.

## OQ-17: Supporting cast composition
Admissible: all invented OCs, canon Targaryens (which — Rhaenyra, Aegon II, Viserys I, Alicent, Daemon, Otto Hightower, Criston Cole, Strong, Velaryon), other canon ASOIAF figures, and / or any Worm characters carried over (likely none given "post Gold Morning solo" reading; OPEN). Depends on OQ-5 and OQ-6.

## OQ-18: Moral framing
Admissible: narrative endorses Taylor's reasoning (she was right; tragedy is unearned), ironizes it (she was wrong but for understandable reasons; tragedy is recognition), or condemns it (she was wrong and the narrative judges her). Sets the chronicler's posture and the reader's invited stance. Depends on OQ-12 and OQ-13.
