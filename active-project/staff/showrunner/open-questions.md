## OQ-1: Reincarnation form — human, beast, or other?
This is the first dependency: every downstream decision (power form, entry location, faction relationships, story register) changes based on whether she wakes in a human body, a beast body, or something stranger.
- Human: she is a person in a social world from the start; the action register is political and physical.
- Beast (e.g. dragon, direwolf-equivalent, something apex): the first arc is body-horror-adjacent; the world's categories are wrong for her from the start; the action is survival and then emergence.
- Other (e.g. something liminal — a skinchanger host, a creature without clear category): opens the widest space but requires the most definition.
Depends on: nothing (this is the root question).
Inputs to: OQ-2 (power form), OQ-3 (entry location), OQ-4 (story spine).

## OQ-2: Power form in the new body — carried, mutated, or stripped?
If she retains her shard-link through reincarnation (soul persists = shard persists in altered form):
- Option A: Power carries fully — she controls arthropods in Westeros. This is the simplest read and the one the conditions cards (condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology) already support for a prior project.
- Option B: Power mutates based on new body — if she's a dragon, maybe she controls something different; if she's a beast, the shard adapts to the new nervous system.
- Option C: Power is dormant at reincarnation and must re-establish — the first arc includes re-discovering she has capability at all.
Depends on: OQ-1 (form determines what mutation looks like).
Inputs to: OQ-4 (story spine), OQ-5 (faction danger level in early episodes).

## OQ-3: Entry location — where in Westeros does she wake?
Options span: King's Landing and its environs (high density, high politics, high danger), the Crownlands or Dragonstone (Targaryen heartland, dragon proximity), the Reach or the Vale (more insulated, slower entry into macro politics), the North (distant from the Dance, potentially slower escalation), the Stormlands or Westerlands (faction-specific entry points).
The brief wants fast escalation, which pushes toward locations close to the Dance's center of gravity (Crownlands, Dragonstone, King's Landing).
Depends on: OQ-1 (form constrains where a beast can plausibly be found).
Inputs to: OQ-4 (story spine), OQ-5 (who finds her first and what they do about it).

## OQ-4: Series spine — what is the story building toward?
The brief-expansion mapped six framings. These are not all compatible with the same protagonist arc. The choice here determines:
- What counts as winning
- What the season structure looks like
- What gets built against Taylor and when
Options from brief-expansion: Warlord emergence, Tragedy of competence, Mercenary genre piece, Cult-of-power genesis, Monster-as-protagonist, Dual-register body-horror.
The brief's genre register (fast, pulpy, escalation) best fits Warlord emergence, Mercenary piece, or Cult-of-power genesis. Tragedy of competence is slower. Monster/body-horror can be fast but requires form = beast.
Depends on: OQ-1, OQ-2 (form and power determine available spines).
Inputs to: season chunk structure, series question.

## OQ-5: Era precision — Dance of Dragons lead-up or earlier?
"Or earlier" spans a huge range — Aegon's conquest (~1 AC) through the Dance (~129 AC) is 130 years of history. The lead-up to the Dance specifically (~110-129 AC) is a defined historical period with known factions (Alicent vs. Rhaenyra, the greens vs. the blacks). "Earlier" could mean:
- Jaehaerys I era (~48-103 AC): the long peace, a relatively stable period with fewer external escalators — but Taylor's knowledge of "the Dance is coming" would be far-horizon rather than immediate.
- Viserys I era (~103-129 AC): the slow deterioration before the civil war; factions forming; Alicent and Rhaenyra's conflict hardening.
- Just before the Dance (~120-129 AC): maximum political pressure, dragon count highest, and the war-clock is loudest.
The brief wants fast escalation, which pushes toward later in this range — but the interesting version of the "she knows what's coming" engine may need a longer runway.
Depends on: OQ-4 (story spine determines how much runway we need for the pre-catastrophe window).
Inputs to: which specific historical figures and factions are on the board, series season count.
