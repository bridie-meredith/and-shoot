# Open Questions — Dependency-Ordered
# Internal. Written by showrunner in step 1a. Resolved in 1b. Each resolution appended to world-notes.md.

## OQ-1: ARRIVAL MECHANIC [RESOLVED — see world-notes.md]
What form do arriving capes take, and what do they retain?
Brief allows: reincarnated infant, dragon-hatched, adult drop-in with power-shaped body, or other.
Dependency: Must be decided before casting, because arrival mode affects what characters can do in scene 1.

Options considered:
A. Single mode for all arrivals (cleaner, but loses variety)
B. Mode determined by the shard / power type — dramatic variation, shard-as-agent angle
C. Three-mode system: adult drop-in, reincarnation, dragon-born — all three coexist, shard selects
Advocate: C. Three modes gives maximum dramatic range and makes the shard an active creative agent, which feeds the series theme about shards having their own agendas.
Decision: C adopted. Shard selects mode based on best expression environment.

## OQ-2: SHARD BEHAVIOR NEAR WESTEROSI MAGIC [RESOLVED — see world-notes.md]
How do shards react to magic they've never encountered?
Dependency: OQ-1. Must know arrival mode before knowing how shard-host relationship is stressed.

Options considered:
A. Shards are inert near magic — clean, but wastes dramatic potential
B. Shards are actively hostile to magic — interesting but forecloses nuance
C. Shards are fascinated and destabilized — want to interface, can't, produces involuntary host pressure
Advocate: C. Weirwood network as near-miss Entity-analog is the richest angle; shard that almost recognizes something and can't process the failure is scarier than one that simply fights.
Decision: C adopted.

## OQ-3: POLITICAL POSITION OF ARRIVED CAPES [RESOLVED — see world-notes.md]
Do the capes know about each other? Does anyone in Westeros know about them?
Dependency: OQ-1 (affects how findable a type-C arrival is vs. a type-A).

Options considered:
A. Capes start known to each other, allied by default — forecloses season 1 discovery drama
B. Capes are scattered and unknown, must find each other — strong discovery arc, higher stakes for first contact
C. One faction knows (Faceless Men, Maesters, Targaryens) — interesting but premature for season 1
Advocate: B. The isolation and first-contact moment is too valuable to skip.
Decision: B adopted.

## OQ-4: POWER FUNCTION IN WESTEROS [RESOLVED — see world-notes.md]
Do powers work the same, or does Planetos suppress/alter them?
Dependency: OQ-1, OQ-2. Needed before any action scene can be planned.

Options considered:
A. Full suppression — strips the whole premise
B. Full power, no change — flat, no dramatic friction
C. Powers work, but environmental friction for Earth-Bet-specific expressions; shard adapts
Advocate: C. The adaptation process is itself dramatic — a cape whose power was calibrated to North American bugs finding what works in Westeros is a scene.
Decision: C adopted.
