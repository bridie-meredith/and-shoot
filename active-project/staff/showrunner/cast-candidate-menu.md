### Age-fit assessment for 122 AC
The existing card describes Aemond as the most dangerous Targaryen antagonist of the Dance period. The Dance begins 129 AC. At 122 AC, Aemond is approximately 10–11 years old (born c.110–112 AC per F&B/HOTD). He lost his eye — and claimed Vhagar — in a childhood event (c.120 AC per canon timeline). So at 122 AC:
- He has claimed Vhagar (the claiming event is canonical at approximately age 10)
- He is not yet the One-Eyed Prince of the Dance era — he is a child who has just done something extraordinary and dangerous
- He is not a walk-on capable of independent coercive action on behalf of a faction in the way the cast-brief envisions

**Age-fit verdict: MISMATCH.** The cast-brief requires a "walk-on coercive arm" capable of enacting consequences on [protect-target]'s position. A 10–11 year old, even one who has claimed Vhagar, cannot function as Otto's independent enforcement instrument at 122 AC. The One-Eyed Prince persona — the cold, precise coercive instrument — belongs to the Dance era (129+ AC), not to 122 AC.

**Resolution options:**

| option | slug | notes |
|---|---|---|
| A: Aemond-122AC-variant | `aemond-targaryen-122ac` | Author a new 122 AC-specific variant. At this age he is present, Vhagar-bonded, and capable of radiating threat via the dragon's presence — even a child riding the largest dragon in Westeros is a coercive instrument. The variant card would need to address: 10–11yo register, the dragon as the coercive weight rather than his own authority, limited dialogue, Otto-directed rather than independent. Not-yet-authored. |
| B: Alternative Green enforcer | A member of the Kingsguard or an Otto-affiliated household knight acting as the coercive arm, with Aemond's Vhagar presence as the structural threat (offstage but implied). Aemond functions as background structural menace; the walk-on enforcement figure is a different named character. | This is more period-accurate but requires authoring an additional character. |
| C: Accept 122AC Aemond at reduced role | Use `aemond-targaryen` as-is but constrain his function to: Otto's leverage (the implication that Vhagar exists and Aemond will ride it when older); not active enforcement. The "coercive arm" is the dragon's existence via the boy who holds it. | Reduces functional walk-on load but works for low-frequency structural-crisis-point appearances. |

**Recommendation:** Option A (`aemond-targaryen-122ac`) or Option C. Option A preserves the cast-brief's intent most cleanly and creates a distinct card that does not retroactively modify the Dance-era scant card. Option C requires the cast-brief's "walk-on coercive arm" to be reinterpreted as "coercive presence through dragon-claim rather than direct enforcement" — plausible at 122 AC, less dramatically sharp.

**Provisional selection for menu:** Option A flagged as preferred. The existing scant `aemond-targaryen` card is noted as the canonical base; the 122AC variant would compose from it with age-specific modifications.
