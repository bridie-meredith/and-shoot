## Story-type pick
Slow-prevention-tragedy (mirror-of-canon)

reason: This shape forces the end-requirement to be earned structurally — Taylor's interventions become the proximate cause of the rupture she prevented, so the "road to hell" chain is reconstructible by the reader beat by beat rather than announced at the close.

## Archetype picks
- protagonist characterization: cold-utilitarian — the theme's irony bites hardest when Taylor's moral accounting is explicit and systematic, because every trade she accepts narrows the exit the reader is watching for.
- foil: naive-idealist — the foil's survival or death functions as a calibration instrument for Taylor's cost, giving the reader a legible yardstick without a mirror-image that flattens the contrast.
- false-ally: patron-with-agenda (rhaenys / rhaenyra register) — protection-for-use structure puts Taylor inside the dynastic machinery she is trying to forestall, which is the structural irony the story-type requires.
- true-ally: attached-smallfolk — a single relationship Taylor will not sacrifice anchors the cost-of-prevention chain and doubles as the test the thematic spine names: the good intention has a face, and when the face pays, there is no abstraction left.
- antagonist-as-person: hightower-intelligence-architect (otto) — an opaque institutional opponent with legible function but no apex-chain position creates friction without resolving into polemic; the reader can describe what he does without hearing a present-day critique over it.
- antagonist-as-institution: Targaryen dynastic logic itself — the war is coming regardless is the structural antagonist the slow-prevention shape requires; the institution has no face to bargain with and no weakness Taylor can exploit, which is what makes each "right" choice tighten the noose.
- victim-by-which-cost-is-paid: specific-ward-child (lyra register) — a named closing-image cost-bearer makes the systemic loss particular and irreversible; the camera-widening move of the ironic-causation register lands only if the cost has a face the reader has been holding since early.
- witness: maester-chronicler — coda-only archival voice is structurally load-bearing here: the chronicle frame confirms the end-requirement's retroactive revelation and gives the reader the wider-camera without requiring the narration to state the theme.
- opposite-number: a canon Dance-igniter whose path Taylor's choices accelerate — this makes Taylor's prevention-logic visibly convergent with the ignition she is preventing, which is the dramatic irony the slow-prevention-tragedy shape runs on.

## Notes
The cold-utilitarian protagonist and the patron-with-agenda false-ally create a tension worth watching: Taylor's systematic moral accounting makes her readable to a patron who operates the same way, which risks collapsing the false-ally friction into alliance-of-convenience before the betrayal lands. The screen-writer should preserve asymmetry in what each is willing to trade. Advisory only.
