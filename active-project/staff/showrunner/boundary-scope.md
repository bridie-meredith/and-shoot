### STORY TYPE (that fits the boundaries)

- Tragedy-of-agency (classical). Protagonist's competence + good motive is the engine of the bad end; structure rewards the audience's growing dread as each "right" choice tightens the noose.
- Ironic-causation chronicle (Greene-tragedy / Le-Guin-Omelas register). Protagonist solves the local problem; the camera widens to show the cost was systemic and the solution caused it.
- Slow-prevention-tragedy (mirror-of-canon). Protagonist tries to prevent Dance of Dragons; her interventions are the proximate cause of its ignition, or of an equivalent worse rupture. Strongest fit to "before Dance of Dragons" timing.
