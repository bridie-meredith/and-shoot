attempts: 1

Insects respond visibly to her distress; three gold cloaks and a septon witness; she is dragged to the Dragon Gate guardhouse, questioned in Old Valyrian she doesn't speak, held overnight, released as "lunatic-not-worth-paperwork." Operational territory: Dragon Gate market-and-gate block (merchants, travelers, City Watch — NOT smallfolk-only). The witch-label is permanent operational infrastructure; she cannot correct it without disclosing what she actually is.

## OQ-4: Faction proximity at story open — RESOLVED

## OQ-5: Form of the first bad-thing-for-good-reasons — RESOLVED (attempt 2)
screen-writer advocate: round 1 — three options proposed (A: Convenient Witness / framing-for-effect; B: Name She Gave / information-as-currency / Mira-attachment; C: Sanction She Borrowed / captain's-model-upgrade). Round 2 — accept-with-condition on refined A: (i) plant Watch enforcement-overshoot earlier (steward/workers rung is natural candidate) so the M27–28 sweep is surprising-yet-inevitable rather than authorial machinery on cue; (ii) recognition-failure beat must include Taylor's internal articulation of the model's collapse, not only the external consequence.

attempts: 2
decision: Refined Option A. Seed at month 4 — Taylor reports the tanner's apprentice's grain-watering, frames it as riot-risk rather than illness, because riot-risk is the category the captain acts on. Taylor names her practice internally as "speaking the patron's dialect" — operational belief that a true report in the wrong register goes unheard, so calibrating register is service-to-accuracy not distortion. The model treats patron categories as NEUTRAL TRANSMISSION CHANNELS, not as ENFORCEMENT MECHANISMS WITH MOMENTUM. Six-rung ladder: M4 seed (apprentice/Watch); M9–10 merchant short-weight/guild; M14–15 dockworker slowdown/lord's steward (workers get nothing; Taylor blames steward's category limits, not framing — THIS IS THE DRAMATIST-REQUIRED EARLIER OVERSHOOT); M18–19 Mira rung (smuggling rumor framed in Watch-risk language; contact protected off-record; Taylor sees the model "working" on someone she cares about; blind spot becomes load-bearing while still invisible to her); M24 unlicensed physician/poisoning-risk/guild auditor; M27–28 RECOGNITION-FAILURE — Black-adjacent contact's border-crossing pattern framed as smuggling-of-persons-risk to a Watch officer for record-review access; contact detained; detention triggers Watch persons-risk sweep escalation Taylor did not author; contact killed in custody. Taylor experiences the model's collapse internally (the felt break — something has gone wrong that her dialect-of-calibration cannot name); the prose never makes the meta-statement.

Binding conditions carried forward into series-plan:
- C1 (dramatist): The M14–15 steward/workers rung must show patron enforcement-logic overshooting Taylor's intended outcome in a deniable way she explains away. This is the planted seed of the M27–28 sweep mechanic.
- C2 (dramatist): The M27–28 falsification must land on the BELIEF, not only on the contact — Taylor's interior must register the collapse of "speaking the patron's dialect" without the prose articulating it as such.
- C3 (worm-canon-pedant): Mira must be established credibly in prose before the M18–19 rung — emotional weight is a delivery obligation.
- C4 (literary-snob, non-negotiable): The model-falsification meta-statement must NEVER appear in the prose. Reader-inference only. Theme-as-structure not theme-as-statement.

screen-writer advocate: three options — (A) Taylor alive, still operating, has not understood, last image: insects two alleys over register a woman with Mira's gait stopping; (B) Taylor alive, understands the dialect was wrong but not that the dialect was her, paralyzed, last image: beetle on windowsill; (C) Taylor dead, processed by the same Watch sweep, last image: her own range-capped insects registering no movement inside the building she is in.
dramatist: accept C as structural winner (foreclosure-pure — the mechanism she built runs correctly on her; her surveillance apparatus outlives its author and reports faithfully on her own disappearance; final image external/observational/silent enforces theme-as-structure). Accept A with concern (foreclosure-complete via end-state = start-state; trim the ambient crier-proclamation detail). Revise B (recognition-tragedy/anagnorisis shape, not foreclosure — character looks at the crack, model has cracked; the form this project is explicitly not).
audience: 3/3 accept C. Worm-canon-pedant: PRT-analog institutional grinding, no martyrdom, no execution, no ceremony — the shard doesn't mourn, it counts. Dark-fantasy-reader: world's verdict in final image, not protagonist's experience of it. Literary-snob: terminus mode (bureaucratic-not-dramatic) structurally enforces the theme-as-structure non-negotiable. A passes 2-of-3 (lit-snob revise on Mira-gait-as-prepared-detail risk). B revised 2-of-3 (dark-fantasy + lit-snob): "choosing not to know what it knows" NAMES the meaning, converts structural foreclosure into narrated tragic choice — violates the Round 7 non-negotiable directly.
attempts: 1
decision: Option C. The book closes on Taylor dead in the Dragon Gate Watch guardhouse. Sequence: the detained Black-adjacent contact (Rung 6, M27–28) names Taylor as his source before dying in custody. The Watch officer running the persons-risk sweep is Green-adjacent via the captain's network. The captain, calculating that a foreign-lunatic-with-insect-witchcraft now attached to persons-risk intelligence is liability rather than asset, signs the paperwork. Taylor is arrested three days after the contact's death, detained under persons-risk protocols, and dies in the Watch guardhouse — not executed, not martyred, processed by the same sweep she helped create. Last image: her own insects, range-capped to the block, registering no movement inside the building she is in. Viserys is dead off-page; the war has begun off-page; her paperwork reflects the new regime's intake numbers as administrative realignment.

Binding execution obligation (audience): the insect-ambient-register must be a consistent prose mechanism from early in the book. The closing image of range-capped insects registering her own stillness must be earned by prior use of the insect-register as the operational mode of her perception — not introduced at the terminus as a device. Screen-writer and shoot stages must track this obligation. Act 1 (months 1–12): grief-recovery, first block-network seeded, granular hostility present but unreadable to Taylor — the calm is her perceptual limitation, not world-flatness. Act 2 (months 13–26): faction temperature bleeds into street-level operations; cost-per-intervention rises each quarter; operational resistance accumulates as terrain-tightening. Act 3 (months 27–36): last viable pivot at month 27–28 under accumulated weight; pivot closes wrong from the logic of everything built before it; Viserys dies ~month 36 off-page or as news-from-the-Keep, war begins, book closes at first irrecoverable street-level atrocity directly consequent on the wrong pivot. Required by dramatist (condition): plant one explicit Act 2 recognition-failure beat where Taylor acts on her old model of the city and pays a named irreversible cost — the mechanical spine of the accumulation. This is recorded as a binding pacing constraint.
