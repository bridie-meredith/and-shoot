# 1b — Open Question Resolution Log

Each OQ runs: screen-writer proposes 2–3 concrete options as decided facts → dramatist + audience review in parallel → decision recorded → world-notes appended.

## OQ-1: Power state at arrival — RESOLVED
screen-writer advocate: three distinct options proposed — (A) insects-only, full canon range; (B) no power at arrival, stress-recovery; (C) range-capped to ~one city block.
dramatist: accept C (structural winner) / revise A (collapses tragedy of competence into attentional failure) / revise B (recovery-as-reward inverts foreclosure).
audience: accept C unanimously (worm-canon-pedant conditional on AU divergence being legible in prose; dark-fantasy-reader on cap reading as physical fact not plot brake; literary-snob on theme-as-structure satisfied). Split on A (worm accept / dark+lit revise) and B (dark accept / worm+lit revise).
attempts: 1
decision: Option C — Taylor's power is intact but spatially range-capped to roughly one city block. AU divergence must be legible in-prose (per worm-canon-pedant). The cap is treated as physical fact throughout (per dark-fantasy-reader). Using-people-as-reach-extenders is the structural foreclosure mechanism (per literary-snob and dramatist).

## OQ-2: Arrival circumstance and identity-handle — RESOLVED
screen-writer advocate: three distinct options proposed — (1) Flea Bottom drain-mouth, unwitnessed, filed as vagrant; (2) Dragon Gate market riot, publicly witnessed, filed as witch/lunatic; (3) Maegor's Holdfast refuse slope, captured, filed as foreign spy.
dramatist: accept Option 2 (structural winner — label-as-lever IS the foreclosure mechanism; rise-peak-fall without coincidence) / revise Option 1 (flat-constant-deflation; requires contrivance to escalate vertically) / revise Option 3 (peak-without-rise; positional pressure unearned).
audience: ACCEPT Option 2 unanimously across all three personas. worm-canon-pedant: insects-respond-to-distress demonstrates ambient-not-directed in action; observer mislabel chain (witch/lunatic) makes AU divergence legible without exposition. dark-fantasy-reader: world acts first, dismissal-as-lunatic compounds rather than clears, septon's "witch" is culturally grounded earned grimdark. literary-snob: witch-label is tragedy-of-competence inscribed as social mechanism before page one — she cannot correct the misread without disclosing what she is, which forecloses everything the misread enables. Options 1 and 3 both REVISE (3/3) — vagrant has no structural weight; spy opens the horizon instead of closing it.
attempts: 1
decision: Option 2 — Taylor arrives mid-catastrophe at a market fire near Dragon Gate. Insects respond visibly to her distress; three gold cloaks and a septon witness; she is dragged to the Dragon Gate guardhouse, questioned in Old Valyrian she doesn't speak, held overnight, released as "lunatic-not-worth-paperwork." Operational territory: Dragon Gate market-and-gate block (merchants, travelers, City Watch — NOT smallfolk-only). The witch-label is permanent operational infrastructure; she cannot correct it without disclosing what she actually is.
