# 1b — Open Question Resolution Log

Each OQ runs: screen-writer proposes 2–3 concrete options as decided facts → dramatist + audience review in parallel → decision recorded → world-notes appended.

## OQ-1: Power state at arrival — RESOLVED
screen-writer advocate: three distinct options proposed — (A) insects-only, full canon range; (B) no power at arrival, stress-recovery; (C) range-capped to ~one city block.
dramatist: accept C (structural winner) / revise A (collapses tragedy of competence into attentional failure) / revise B (recovery-as-reward inverts foreclosure).
audience: accept C unanimously (worm-canon-pedant conditional on AU divergence being legible in prose; dark-fantasy-reader on cap reading as physical fact not plot brake; literary-snob on theme-as-structure satisfied). Split on A (worm accept / dark+lit revise) and B (dark accept / worm+lit revise).
attempts: 1
decision: Option C — Taylor's power is intact but spatially range-capped to roughly one city block. AU divergence must be legible in-prose (per worm-canon-pedant). The cap is treated as physical fact throughout (per dark-fantasy-reader). Using-people-as-reach-extenders is the structural foreclosure mechanism (per literary-snob and dramatist).

## OQ-2: Arrival circumstance and identity-handle — RESOLVED
screen-writer advocate: three distinct options proposed — (1) Flea Bottom drain-mouth, unwitnessed, filed as vagrant; (2) Dragon Gate market riot, publicly witnessed, filed as witch/lunatic; (3) Maegor's Holdfast refuse slope, captured, filed as foreign spy.
dramatist: accept Option 2 (structural winner — label-as-lever IS the foreclosure mechanism; rise-peak-fall without coincidence) / revise Option 1 (flat-constant-deflation; requires contrivance to escalate vertically) / revise Option 3 (peak-without-rise; positional pressure unearned).
audience: ACCEPT Option 2 unanimously across all three personas. worm-canon-pedant: insects-respond-to-distress demonstrates ambient-not-directed in action; observer mislabel chain (witch/lunatic) makes AU divergence legible without exposition. dark-fantasy-reader: world acts first, dismissal-as-lunatic compounds rather than clears, septon's "witch" is culturally grounded earned grimdark. literary-snob: witch-label is tragedy-of-competence inscribed as social mechanism before page one — she cannot correct the misread without disclosing what she is, which forecloses everything the misread enables. Options 1 and 3 both REVISE (3/3) — vagrant has no structural weight; spy opens the horizon instead of closing it.
attempts: 1
decision: Option 2 — Taylor arrives mid-catastrophe at a market fire near Dragon Gate. Insects respond visibly to her distress; three gold cloaks and a septon witness; she is dragged to the Dragon Gate guardhouse, questioned in Old Valyrian she doesn't speak, held overnight, released as "lunatic-not-worth-paperwork." Operational territory: Dragon Gate market-and-gate block (merchants, travelers, City Watch — NOT smallfolk-only). The witch-label is permanent operational infrastructure; she cannot correct it without disclosing what she actually is.

## OQ-4: Faction proximity at story open — RESOLVED
screen-writer advocate: three options — (A) Watch-track via the Gold Cloak captain who signed her release, private Green-allegiance at one remove via Hightower-controlled promotion channels; (B) Faith-track via the Dragon Gate septon who flagged her, reversed-to-protector through observed competence, Faith hierarchy Green-patronized; (C) Velaryon merchant-track via a Lyseni factor recruiting her as block-watcher, Velaryon connection one step removed.
dramatist: accept A (conditional) — only option where first-faction-gravity and the required Act-2 recognition-failure beat are the same mechanism; the captain's deniable-sensor-node framing does setup work invisibly. Revise B (recognition-failure beat is diffuse; "tolerates misreadings" is character-texture not causal chain). Revise C (fuse too long for back-loaded compression; operational cleanliness leaves Act 2 understaked).
audience: 3/3 accept A. Worm-canon-pedant: ambient-not-directed legible as quiet compliance to Watch observer; no demonstration scene; grief-shaped failure-to-audit-patronage has direct Worm precedent. Dark-fantasy-reader: hostile-not-helpful infrastructure from day one; world acts on her, not for her; earned-bleakness payoff with full Act 1 cost. Literary-snob: faction-character readable in operational texture scene by scene; doubled-accumulation irony between Taylor's model and the reader's; foreclosure inscribed in infrastructure, not argued by narrator. 3/3 revise B (B-3-ways: wobbles witch-label; world-softens-toward-protagonist; theme-by-statement). 3/3 revise C (active-block-watcher breaks ambient-not-directed; reprieve-not-terrain; irony-as-surprise-not-accumulation).
attempts: 1
decision: Option A — Watch-track. First contact is the Captain of the Dragon Gate precinct (gold cloak captain who signed Taylor's release). His promotions came through Hightower-controlled channels — Green-adjacent at one remove, undeclared. He releases Taylor as a deniable sensor-node for ward-level disturbances; her witch-label and lunatic dismissal make her low-cost, deniable, and not on any official roster. Social handle Taylor gets through him: "the one the captain lets alone" — half-step above witch. She does not know the captain's faction-pull and cannot see it from inside her block-cap. Required follow-through: Act 2 recognition-failure beat (per OQ-3 dramatist condition) is the moment her Watch-built network is revealed to have been Green intelligence infrastructure all along — a Black-adjacent contact she vouched for is burned by her own network's data flowing upward through the captain. Act 1 (months 1–12): grief-recovery, first block-network seeded, granular hostility present but unreadable to Taylor — the calm is her perceptual limitation, not world-flatness. Act 2 (months 13–26): faction temperature bleeds into street-level operations; cost-per-intervention rises each quarter; operational resistance accumulates as terrain-tightening. Act 3 (months 27–36): last viable pivot at month 27–28 under accumulated weight; pivot closes wrong from the logic of everything built before it; Viserys dies ~month 36 off-page or as news-from-the-Keep, war begins, book closes at first irrecoverable street-level atrocity directly consequent on the wrong pivot. Required by dramatist (condition): plant one explicit Act 2 recognition-failure beat where Taylor acts on her old model of the city and pays a named irreversible cost — the mechanical spine of the accumulation. This is recorded as a binding pacing constraint.
