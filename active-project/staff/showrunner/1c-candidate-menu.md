### (A) Lead — Taylor Variants

**Existing Taylor persona cards in the library:**

| slug | world/era | one-line read |
|---|---|---|
| `taylor-hebert` | earth-bet (base card) | The canonical Worm mid-canon baseline. Full quality. This is the source from which any Westeros variant should be composed or derived. Directly reusable as the base card in a composition. |
| `taylor-hebert-westeros` | planetos — **120 AC**, Viserys I era, Harrenhal shadow, age 11, orphan ward of dying septon | **Wrong era by ~36 years.** Built around the impressment-census-120ac condition, Harrenhal geography, and the septon-dying-protector as opening hook. **Assessment of gap and recommended path:**

No existing card matches the decided baseline: *mid-canon Worm Taylor, reincarnated into a Westerosi smallfolk child body at ~84 AC, age 7–8, daughter of craftsman/tradespeople in a Riverlands river-market town, with full swarm-sense and foreign-ecology friction, Jaehaerys I era.*

The prior variant (`taylor-hebert-westeros`) was built for a substantially different project configuration. Its value as a composition source is partial:

- **Carry forward:** psychological profile, voice rules, Gold Morning weight, vibe seeds (accumulated history and private associations sections), swarm-sense mechanics, the child-performance problem, the literacy-as-exposure problem, stats block structure, trigger list.
- **Must differ:** arrival age (7–8 not 11), birth station (craftsman family, not orphan ward), geographic anchor (Riverlands river-market town near Gods Eye / Blue Fork, not Harrenhal shadow), institutional protection structure (family, not septon), era (Jaehaerys I not Viserys I), condition references (new set for 84–101 AC project), relationships (parents, market-town peers, no septon-dying-protector, no castellan-harrenhal), ignition context (tax-collector event at age ~9, ~86 AC), opening references should be to new condition and location cards not the 120 AC set.
- **New material required:** the child-in-family setting (what it costs her to perform as a craftsman's child over seven to nine years before ignition), the market-town social physics at 84 AC (Jaehaerys peace, less actively contested terrain than 120 AC Riverlands), the foreknowledge load being longer (she has roughly 15 years of runway, not 9), the Shard-as-behavioral-weight framing, the specific community she will organize rather than the census escape she needs to survive.

**Recommended action:** Author a new variant card `taylor-hebert-jaehaerys` (or similar) composed from `taylor-hebert` as base, drawing selected sections from `taylor-hebert-westeros` where they apply, and writing new sections for the 84 AC configuration. This is a composition + substantial original authoring task, not a simple copy-and-adjust. The prior variant (`taylor-hebert-westeros`) should be noted as the prior-project card, not retired — it remains in library as provenance for the prior project.
