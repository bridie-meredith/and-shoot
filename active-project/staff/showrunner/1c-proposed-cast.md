## Carry-forward constraint to s1 planning (from dramatist viability check 2026-05-10)

The broken maester's structural load (sole witness, s4 foreclosure documentation, s2 naming-and-not-being-heard) requires a PRIOR audible interaction with Taylor. Provision at least one s1-or-early-s2 scene where Taylor hears the maester (through her insect-network, via fragment she overhears, or via direct adjacency) and adjusts her behavior in response. This makes the s2 naming-not-heard event read as deterioration rather than first-contact. Without this earlier beat, the s4 documentation has no felt weight. This is a plan-level constraint for /and-season-plan s01 — not a cast composition change.
