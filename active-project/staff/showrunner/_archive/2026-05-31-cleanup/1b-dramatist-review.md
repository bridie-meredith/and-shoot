Two archetypes unplaced by the 9 OQs:

Either place it as a distinct character or explicitly fuse it into Rhaenyra before /and-series commit. Implicit fusion is not sufficient — the function must be load-bearing.

2. **Opposite-number (canon Dance-igniter)** — not placed. The convergence line between Taylor's prevention-logic and the ignition-path has no body to carry it. Aemond is the canonical candidate. Must be placed before /and-series; convergence must be planted in act one, not introduced at the ignition beat.

## Story-type fit

Configuration produces slow-prevention-tragedy. Has not drifted to assassin-thriller or intrigue-drama. Mirror-of-canon sub-type intact: declarative objective, named target, auditable first-mistake beat, "both" end-locus.

## Single-book viability

Four beats across 18–24 chapters at 3–5 scenes per chapter is viable without compression. Retroactive-revelation is satisfied by backward reconstructibility, not a late data-dump. Seven story-years require chapter-level ellipsis — consistent with first-person close POV.

## Cold-utilitarian asymmetry

Partially preserved but shifted. Rhaenyra is currently passive — a resource being depleted rather than a patron with an active agenda. The patron-with-agenda archetype requires the patron to be moving. Without an active Rhaenyra agenda putting pressure on Taylor's choices, the betrayal loses structural weight and the archetype collapses into patron-as-passive-object. The operative Taylor-false-ally friction is now Taylor-Otto, which is valid; Rhaenyra must still carry independent agenda pressure or the archetype is not doing its job.

## Carry-forwards (advisory — not blockers for /and-series)

1. Place naive-idealist foil or declare explicit fusion into Rhaenyra with load-bearing function before /and-series commit.
2. Name opposite-number before /and-series commit; plant convergence line in act one.
3. Give Rhaenyra an active agenda — a specific thing she wants that puts pressure on Taylor's choices throughout, not only a trust that can be violated at the end.
