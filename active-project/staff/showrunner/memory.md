series:
  theme: Can competence protect anything against civilizational fire?
  laws:
    - Soul persists through reincarnation; Taylor remembers everything including Gold Morning
    - Taylor is a dragon; hatched ~110 AC in the Riverlands; grows across the series
    - Insect-control power persists via shard-soul-link; full mechanics in condition-shard-in-dragon-body.card.md
    - Two toolkits: insect layer (covert) and dragon layer (visible); choice between them always load-bearing
    - Genre register locked: fast, action, pulpy, dramatic, tension, escalation
    - Taylor escalates to match trajectory; does not wait for fatal
    - Taylor protects her patch; not trying to stop the Dance; behavior constant across series
  lore:
    - Setting Riverlands Westeros ~110 AC; Dance of Dragons (~129-131 AC) is series horizon
    - Targaryen claim: every unclaimed dragon is crown property; persistent; unenforceably-in-practice
    - Riverlands contested; Bracken-Blackwood feud; always in the middle of every war
    - Taylor knows Dance history; nobody in Westeros does yet; asymmetry is the clock
    - Arthropod ecology: see condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology; seasonal variation applies
  behaviors:
    - Taylor does not perform weakness, loyalty, or heroism — pays loyalty as fixed cost
    - Taylor clinical under crisis; seams show only in pauses
    - Taylor does not seek legitimacy from institutions; builds leverage instead
    - Taylor is not trying to stop the Dance; protecting the patch is the goal
  plot:
    start: Taylor hatches in the Riverlands with full memory of who she was, into a world that will try to categorize, claim, or kill her
    end: The Dance of Dragons arrives and tests everything she built; the series question is answered
    protagonist_arc: From "what am I now" to "I know what I am and what I am protecting and whether that was enough"
    series_question: Can she protect something worth keeping before the fire arrives?
  cast_roster:

    - septon-rowan: cult-of-power engine; builds theology around Taylor without authorization; card at active-project/actors/septon-rowan/card.md
    - westerosi-traveling-maester: institutional observer; first institutional encounter with the anomaly; card at active-project/actors/westerosi-traveling-maester/card.md
    - mira-stonefield: smallfolk intelligence network; Riverlands survivor; card at active-project/actors/mira-stonefield/card.md
