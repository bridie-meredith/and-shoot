### Naive-idealist-foil flavor

**Pick: (a) Flea Bottom septon — moral presence only, Faith register.**

Reasoning: The foil's axis-carrier function is moral_legibility_to_self — a mirror for Taylor's rationalization, not a parallel plot actor. A Flea Bottom septon has on-page access throughout all three acts (tendance of the sick and the ward's dying is a constant presence in Flea Bottom regardless of court politics), requires no institutional integration with the Otto-channel or Black-faction plots, and operates at a register Taylor cannot dismiss as ideological opposition. Options (b) and (c) both risk overlapping with Rhaenyra's road-not-taken function (see Rhaenyra decision below): a minor Black-faction ward figure would duplicate Rhaenyra's faction-embodiment, and a court-adjacent figure in a different institutional position would dilute the contrast between Taylor's cold-utilitarian register and the foil's principled slowness. The septon is the cleanest contrast — Faith vocabulary rather than political vocabulary, presence-and-tending rather than network-building, refusal of the calculus on principle rather than inability to access it. The counter-argument Taylor stops engaging at d09 is harder to dismiss when it comes from someone who never had the option to take the fast path.

### Rhaenyra elevate y/n

**Decision: ELEVATE.**

Reasoning: The world-notes carry-forward is explicit and hard — "Give Rhaenyra an active agenda putting pressure on Taylor's choices throughout — not only a trust to be violated at the end." This is not a nice-to-have flag; it is a carry-forward advisory from Phase 1b with named dramatic stakes. Without elevation, Rhaenyra functions as a background name in Taylor's accounting, which the carry-forward expressly rejects. On axis coverage: she carries political_register-world from the Black side — the faction whose position Taylor's intelligence deliveries actively undermine — and she provides the road-not-taken contrast that Alicent/Aemond/Criston, all Green-faction, cannot provide. The ironic structure requires the reader to feel the Black-faction's claim as a live alternative that Taylor is foreclosing, not merely as a named faction in Taylor's ledger. Rhaenyra on Dragonstone at 122 AC (observable primarily through court-rumor, intelligence inference, and possibly one interlude-adjacent scene) does not produce scene-count pressure — her function is information-economy rather than direct scene contact. She does not overlap with the naive-idealist-foil (flavor (a) septon): the septon is a register contrast at the smallfolk level, operating from Faith and presence; Rhaenyra is a political-register carrier at the court level, embodying the succession axis Taylor is working against. Distinct slots, distinct functions, no structural conflict.

Margit will author a `rhaenyra-targaryen-122ac` addendum at Phase 4 following the same pattern as `aemond-targaryen-122ac`, encoding: age ~25, Dragonstone base, Laenor-married + Laena-Velaryon-dead (120 AC), Jacaerys and Lucerys as young children, active succession agenda, observable primarily through court-rumor and intelligence channels.

### Helaena elevate y/n

**Decision: OMIT.**

Reasoning: Helaena's texture-and-dread function (anomalous register; dreamspeak that files as an error in Taylor's cold accounting) is a genuine atmospheric asset. However, three conditions combine to favor omission. First, the foil selection (Flea Bottom septon, flavor (a)) already introduces a register that disrupts Taylor's contempt-accumulation model from the smallfolk layer — a second register-disruption at the court layer risks diluting the effect of both. The septon's disruption is grounded in presence and physical tending; Helaena's is dreamspeak-oblique; neither is redundant, but stacking them in the same arc compresses the disruption into predictable texture rather than keeping it as a punctuation event. Second, Helaena carries no required axis and margit would need to author a full card from scratch with no library base. The authoring cost is real and not justified by an axis-delivery function. Third, at 11 roles already (including the elevated Rhaenyra), scene-count pressure is a live concern: the brief names Helaena as explicitly "droppable under scene-count pressure," and scene-count pressure is exactly the condition that applies. Helaena can be flagged for revival at `/and-substance book` if the chapter plan produces observation sequences where her anomalous register would deliver strong axis-texture without requiring additional bones budget.

They do not: the septon operates at the smallfolk level through presence and Faith, and his contrast is register-and-method. Rhaenyra operates at the court level through faction-embodiment, and her contrast is political-allegiance-and-consequence. Different layers; no structural conflict.
