## A. Time-Window

~88–94 AC. Taylor opens the season at age 11 (~88 AC) and closes it at age 16–17 (~93–94 AC). The season spans approximately five to six years. The adolescent-body ceiling transition falls at approximately ~91 AC (age ~13–14), at the midpoint of the arc — committed as a named internal threshold event at Beat 3.

---

## B. Season Drama

The steward's quarterly monitoring cycle converts Mira's market-day gatherings from a social nuisance into a named target on Pryor's institutional perimeter — and Taylor's counter-response is to build depth and geographic breadth before the patterned-response apparatus finishes naming what it is watching. The arc turns on a single structural liability: the Shard's weight toward escalation and the coalition's expanding exposure travel in the same direction at the same time, and the people Taylor recruited to trust her are sitting inside that convergence without knowing the shape of what she withholds from them. By season close, Mira publicly dissolves her market-day gatherings under the monitoring record's direct institutional pressure — the relationship does not break, but the structure Taylor built around Mira does not survive, and what remains is smaller and harder to rebuild.

---

## C. Season Question

What does Taylor give up — and who does she cost — in the window between her first named monitoring record and the moment when building more is the same action as spending the people she built it with?

## D. Season Content Beats (continuous content guidance)

The beats below describe the dramatic territory the season covers. They are continuous content guidance; the /and-season Phase 2 writer treats them as a flat aggregate. Phase 4 splits that aggregate into actual episodes (multiple of 3; this plan targets 6) by interpretive cut.

### Beat 1 — Pryor's perimeter (anchor: ~88–89 AC, Taylor age ~11)

Pryor's quarterly monitoring cycle is an established structural fact in Fairstead — the reeve has acknowledged receipt of the report three times in writing, the ferryman carries it on a fixed schedule that Taylor's passive-sense has logged as a named pattern, and on the fourth report-arrival day the garrison captain posts a sentry on the ferry dock during the handoff. The dock sentry is new — it was not there at the previous three arrivals — and Taylor's passive-sense logs it as a first perimeter-hardening event: the monitoring cycle is no longer only a documentary record, it is acquiring a physical face. What cannot remain unchanged: the dock sentry is a specific observable change to Fairstead's institutional landscape — a physical fact that did not exist before this beat and that alters what is possible in Beat 2 (Mira's gatherings now have an armed presence on the dock at the same time the ferry carries the report, which means the town's throat is watched on report-days in a way it was not before).

### Beat 2 — coalition expansion pressure (anchor: ~89–90 AC, Taylor age ~12)

Taylor expands the coalition's geographic reach along the Blue Fork road network — a wool factor in a tributary village, a millward's wife two stops east — while Pryor's monitoring field begins naming Mira's gatherings as its new perimeter; the threshold is whether Taylor holds the expansion under the monitoring cycle's rhythm or lets the Shard push her past the pace she can defend. What cannot remain unchanged: Mira's gatherings are now named in Pryor's quarterly report — not as a threat, but as a location — and the coalition's outer members know they are on a record they cannot read.

### Beat 3 — body-clock event (anchor: ~91 AC, Taylor age ~13–14) — season spine transition

The adolescent-body ceiling shifts: the cost curve that had capped active control at three minutes (mild), ten (headache), twenty (nosebleed) lengthens proportionally as Taylor's physical body crosses its adolescent threshold. The transition is not a gift — the new ceiling is higher, but the Shard reads the lengthened cost-curve as license to push further before Taylor decides to pull back. What cannot remain unchanged: the old ceiling was a hard wall Taylor could point to and hold; the new ceiling is a moving threshold that requires continuous recalibration, and the Shard's behavioral-weight immediately begins loading toward the new limit. The transition is not public, but one specific small indicator — Taylor's insects work a grid they could not hold last autumn, and the difference is registered as a quiet operational shift she names to herself without naming to anyone else.

### Beat 4 — garrison copy (anchor: ~91–92 AC, Taylor age ~14–15)

Pryor sends the fifth-quarter report with a written instruction that the garrison captain acknowledge receipt by hand — institutional protocol becoming visible: a paper trail requiring a named military officer's signature, not just a ferryman's transit record. Rymer Hedge, on courier duty for a day-trip between Fairstead and the garrison outpost, carries the acknowledgment packet back. He reads nothing; he delivers the packet; he is not in Pryor's coalition. But after the delivery Rymer's behavior shifts — he rides the Fairstead-to-ferry road on the same schedule he always has, but his posture changes at the garrison waypost, his stops along the road shift by a quarter-hour, and the smallfolk he speaks to at the road's edge are different smallfolk than he spoke to before; Taylor's passive-sense logs the delta without being able to source it, the way it logs weather-changes before the rain arrives. What cannot remain unchanged: Rymer has moved from ground-level non-convert witness to a named behavioral anomaly in Taylor's operational-tracking register — passive-sense has logged the shift; Taylor does not know why he changed, only that something changed and she cannot account for it. The coalition-inside-category distinction is still intact; Rymer does not know about it; but Taylor is now carrying an unresolved anomaly in a member she trusted precisely because he held his shape. **Structural note (body-clock active-control differentiation carry-forward):** Beat 4 is the first post-transition beat where the new Shard-surface-area is operational in Taylor's decision-making — the lengthened cost-curve from Beat 3 is now a live variable in how far she can push surveillance and response. A response-bone must commit to differentiating this new cost-register from the pre-transition baseline: the reach Taylor now has (extended insect-grid coverage of the road corridor, sustained passive-sense through the garrison outpost's insect-wrong zone) is what makes the behavioral-delta logging possible, and the aggregate writer must show the difference between what the old ceiling would have returned and what the new ceiling returns here — not as exposition, but as a bone showing the physical cost of the extended hold.

### Beat 5 — confrontation (anchor: ~92–93 AC, Taylor age ~15–16) — season peak

The monitoring record reaches a sufficiency threshold: Pryor's quarterly file on Mira's gatherings is thick enough that it does not require incident-response, it requires a directive response — and the directive takes the form of a summons to the reeve (not to Mira, to the reeve), instructing him to officially discourage large gatherings on market days pending a lord's-hall review of public assembly patterns. Taylor learns of the summons before the reeve acts. The Shard loads toward intervention; Taylor has new ceiling; the coalition is at its widest radius. The collision is whether Taylor's attempt to interpose herself between the reeve and the directive is operational or is a cost she is running on Mira's behalf that Mira has not authorized. What cannot remain unchanged: Taylor makes an active intervention attempt — it is legible, named, and either succeeds or fails in a way that commits to the peak's consequence. **Structural note (body-clock cost-register differentiation required here):** B5 is the first beat where Taylor deploys the new Shard-surface-area in a confrontation context. The response-bone must distinguish the new active-control ceiling from the pre-transition baseline — not merely assert the expanded capacity, but mark the cost-shape: what the extended hold does to Taylor's body that the old ceiling would have foreclosed, and what the Shard reads as license in the aftermath.

### Beat 6 — dissolution (anchor: ~93–94 AC, Taylor age ~16–17) — season close / INTERLUDE narrator: mira-stonefield-jaehaerys

Mira dissolves the market-day gatherings publicly and without Taylor's advance knowledge — she does it in the open, in the market square, in the language of ordinary prudence, in front of the wool factor and the fishwife and the town reeve. The dissolution is not a rupture; Mira does not break with Taylor; she makes the institutional cost legible and takes it off the record before Pryor's directive can force the reeve to formalize it as an enforcement action. The structure Taylor built around Mira does not survive. What cannot remain unchanged: in the dissolution's direct aftermath, a new face arrives at the sept — a young woman from a tributary village, carrying a letter of introduction to Septon Rowan from a Faith chapter-house functionary two towns east. She arrives the day after Mira's public dissolution, her purpose unstated, her connection to the chapter-house's literacy-extension work unnamed. Taylor's passive-sense logs her presence; her insects have mapped the route she traveled; but the source of the introduction and the purpose of the visit are not visible from the road-edge where Taylor stands. The season closes on that logged presence — a thread in motion that the dissolution itself may have made possible, or may have foreclosed, and that S3 will open on rather than on the aftermath of the dissolution.

## H. Structural Notes for Aggregate Authoring

- **Active cost ceiling by beat:** Child-body ceiling (~3/10/20-minute actor-card constraints) applies through Beat 2. The transition event at Beat 3 shifts the cost curve upward; the new ceiling applies from Beat 3 forward. Beat 1 and Beat 2: passive-sense only. Beat 3: the transition event itself (passive demonstration, not active deployment). Beat 4: extended passive-sense hold at new ceiling (the garrison-road insect-grid run); this is the post-transition differentiation bone. Beat 5: first confrontation deployment at new ceiling; response-bone required marking the new cost-shape. Beat 6: no active swarm event; passive-sense terminal logging at beat-close.
- **Suppression-policy stage at S2 close:** patterned-response. Pryor's quarterly file has thickness; the directive to the reeve has been sent; the apparatus has vocabulary for what it is watching. Policy-level suppression begins in S3. S2 close must not overstate — this is the end of patterned-response, not the beginning of formal policy.
- **Mira coalition arc:** initiated at S1 witness-inquiry beat (debt unnamed, not a coalition). S2 Beats 2–5 expand and stress the coalition; Beat 6 closes the market-day structure without closing the relationship. The S3 opening inherits Mira as a transformed ally — structurally diminished, relationally intact, motivation not fully legible to Taylor.
- **Rymer anomaly carry-forward:** Taylor's passive-sense has logged Rymer's behavioral shift at Beat 4 without sourcing it. This is an open operational variable entering S3. The aggregate writer must not resolve it — leave it as logged-but-unsourced. S3 episode planning will deploy or dismiss it.
- **Body-clock cost-register differentiation (carry-forward mandate):** Two named differentiation requirements: (a) Beat 4 passive-sense extended hold — show what the new ceiling returns that the old could not hold; (b) Beat 5 confrontation deployment — mark the new cost-shape as a body-resident consequence, not as an expanded capability assertion. The differentiation must land in bones, not in header description.
- **B6 forward-thread (carry-forward):** The tributary-village newcomer logged at season close is not resolved at season-plan level. S3 episode planning must assign her function before the B6 bones are authored. Three options remain open: (1) she is a Faith chapter-house literacy-extension placement, forwarding Septon Rowan's network into S3's Faith-leverage arc; (2) she is a Pryor-adjacent monitoring plant, and her arrival is not coincidence; (3) she is neither, and the timing is the coincidence Taylor cannot afford to trust. Season planning does not decide; S3 episode planning decides. The requirement at this stage is only that the logged-presence bone is specific enough to anchor whichever function S3 assigns.
- **Coalition-discovers-the-withholding distribution (dramatist execution constraint, iteration 2 review):** the series-plan commitment that "the people Taylor recruited to trust her discover the specific shape of what she withholds" is distributed across B2 (gatherings on record without coalition-side authorization), B4 (Rymer behavioral anomaly Taylor's own passive-sense cannot source — withholding visible in her inability to explain her own observations), and B6 (Mira dissolves; Taylor cannot identify a different move). The B6 Mira interlude must make the arithmetic legible as Mira's recognition of the specific shape of the withholding — not just "the institution pressed hard enough," but Mira understanding what Taylor held back and when. If B6 renders the dissolution as institutional pressure alone, without Mira's recognition of the gap between what Taylor knew and what Taylor shared, the series commitment is not met. This is execution-level for the aggregate writer.
- **Rowan theology arc continuity:** S1 bequeathed two open Rowan threads (pastoral claim on Taylor; Elara-Rowan convergence). S2 does not close either. Beat 6's sept-adjacent newcomer arrival keeps the Rowan thread active as a site rather than as an actor. If the aggregate writer needs Rowan to appear, confine him to Beat 5 (as a peripheral institutional presence during the reeve summons) and Beat 6 (receiving the newcomer's letter). Do not open a new Rowan arc in S2 — that is S3 work.
- **Interlude count (carry-forward):** Beat 6 is the single S2 interlude. Confirmed as load-bearing by dramatist iteration-2 review. Iteration 1's B3 Pryor interlude was removed; the apparatus now arrives only through its physical/social effects on Taylor's map (sentry, summons, garrison protocol, Rymer's anomaly), which the dramatist accepted as more disciplined than direct Pryor-POV access. Do not add a second interlude in S2 at aggregate authoring level without routing back to screen-writer.
- **Audience carry-forward flags:**
  - pulp-enthusiast: B1 close is a named physical board-change (dock sentry added) — the aggregate writer must render this as a specific observable event Taylor's insects map, not as a retrospective summary of the institutional cycle. The ratchet fires at beat-close when the sentry is first logged, not when Taylor has processed what it means.
  - pulp-enthusiast: B6 forward-thread newcomer must land as a board-change that B6 dramatizes — the insects return her presence from the road-edge, the arrival is concrete, the ambiguity is in the sourcing not in the event.
  - worm-canon-pedant: B4 behavioral-anomaly logging must land as an operational-tracking response-bone — Taylor's swarm-sense registers the Rymer delta in physical-specific terms (road stops, posture, interlocutors) before the beat closes. The absence of a sourcing-explanation is canonical; the absence of a logged response is not.
  - worm-canon-pedant: body-clock differentiation bones required at B4 (passive-hold) and B5 (confrontation deployment) — see structural notes above. These are the cost-register response-bones the carry-forward demands.
  - dark-fantasy-reader: B6 interlude must render the dissolution as world-pushback, not managed-darkness. Mira's arithmetic is the suppression apparatus doing what it does — not Taylor's coalition failing to manage it. The newcomer's logged presence should feel like a door the dissolution left ajar, not a consolation.
