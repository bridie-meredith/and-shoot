### Decision narrative

**Scene 1 (b01c01s01) — baseline:** Scene 1 carries no axis motion and exists to make the prohibition feel like labor, not policy. Taylor holds the line at subsistence range by active effort. This makes the scene 2 deployment legible as a break rather than an increment — it matters that holding cost something every morning. Scene 1 also plants Wren without naming her: the stitch-house smell, two lanes over, is the first appearance of the relational anchor before it is a cost. All axes held; density low end (0.6-0.7); stakes_axis moral_framework because the scene's conflict is the ongoing pressure of sustained suppression. The prohibition is intact here — the test is duration and effort, not a crisis.

**Scene 2 (b01c01s02) — rescue and deployment:** The capability Δ concentrates here because the chapter chunk specifies the deployment is instinctual. Moral_framework is held rather than moved because Taylor does not file the breach as a breach while it is happening — "she does not make a decision, or if she does she does not file it as one." The held status reflects Taylor's interiority, not the objective fact. This is how the chapter goal ("instinct that survives every prohibition") is dramatized without announcing it. Stakes_axis: moral_framework — the conflict is whether the prohibition survives contact with a dying child; it does not, but Taylor does not know that yet. Social_tether-prot-rise is held because Oswyn's awareness of Taylor as a ward-category has not yet formed during the rescue itself; it forms in the aftermath. Wren is in the crowd and absent from Taylor's calculus — relational_anchor_status held as structural dormancy. Density high (0.75-0.9) because this is the chapter's hinge action.

**Scene 3 (b01c01s03) — aftermath and planting:** Social_tether-prot-rise moves here because the ward-embedding is the aftermath of the rescue, not the rescue itself — Oswyn's composing-the-word look, the ward's category shift from anonymous-foreign-woman to known-by-sight. Stakes_axis: social_tether-prot-rise — the conflict is whether Taylor remains invisible versus whether the ward's categorization process runs. The ward wins. Relational_anchor_status is held as load-bearing dormancy: Wren is named (stitch-house smell, Taylor not looking toward it), and the reader now has two reference points for the anchor before it is a cost. Moral_framework is held with "crack visible only in retrospect" per the chapter chunk's explicit instruction. The three-scene rising shape: s01 establishes the cost of maintenance; s02 breaks the prohibition without the protagonist noticing; s03 shows the ward noticing what the protagonist did not. The chapter ends with Taylor categorized, the witch-label in assembly, and Wren's presence planted — before any of these are legible as costs.
