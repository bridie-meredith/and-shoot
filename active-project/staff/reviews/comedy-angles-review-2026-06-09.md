# Comedy-angles review — `intake/character-reactions.md`

**Date:** 2026-06-09 · **Target:** `active-project/intake/character-reactions.md` (per-character reaction
design for the Gael spine) · **Method:** three independent critic forks, one per bound audience lens,
each briefed to judge the *comedy* harshly and specifically (what doesn't land, not just what does) and
to return concrete staged replacements. Reviewers were blind to each other.

**Reviewers:** ① Youjo-Senki lens (sardonic dramatic-irony native) · ② progression/cultivation-isekai
genre native (gag-freshness) · ③ literary-snob lens (does the comedy *mean* anything).

---

## Headline verdict (all three converge)

The doc is a strong comic **engine** with ~⅓ of its bits firing as actual jokes; the rest **describe a
funny situation and trust the situation to be the joke.** Youjo: *"a blueprint of comedy is not comedy."*
Literary: *"three brilliant garments and seven mannequins."* Genre-native: *"strong bones, lazy meat."*

The recurring failure mode is **asserted, not staged** — the doc writes "the comedy is X" where a beat
should be. The recurring success mode is **the cope performed on the page** (the maester's syllogism; the
bestiary entry).

## Cross-cutting findings (2-of-3 or 3-of-3 agreement → load-bearing)

|---|---|---|---|

| F3 | **Recurrence ≠ escalation.** Only the **bestiary (③)** and **Hobb** genuinely build toward a payoff. ① and ④ are *recurrence cosplaying as escalation* — the doc/worksheet *claim* they peak but the staging never makes the apex deployment bigger/different. | Genre-native + Youjo + Literary | HIGH |
| F4 | **④ corporate-euphemism is dead/flat.** The single most played-out bit in the genre; run at one amplitude. "Stating the rule of the joke (blander = darker) is a tell it's gone mechanical." | Genre-native (lead); Youjo (via the offboarding) | HIGH |

| F6 | **Announcing labels.** "The tragic engine," "intimacy amplifies wrongness," the bolded "screw-turn" — the doc admires its own trick; risk is the announcing voice migrating into prose as on-the-nose framing (a Rule-22 hazard on the *thematic* vector). | Literary | MED |

## Findings unique to one lens (still adopted)

- **U1 (Literary) — the curdle is over-leveraged on one gag.** The bestiary (③+④) does almost all the
  apex-seeding; most per-character tweaks are self-contained Book-I bits that won't curdle. If ③ wobbles,
  the apex has no paper trail. → **Seed curdle into more foils.**
- **U2 (Literary) — no foil witnesses the silence.** The project's most sophisticated idea is that
  tragedy = the *register failing* (the joke that can't arrive). The doc seeds the jokes and forgets to
  seed a foil *present at that collapse.* → **Build the apex-silence witness** (Wylla is the natural one).
- **U3 (Genre-native) — source-not-pool undercuts the empire-comedy motive.** II.6 reveals every
  acquisition was unnecessary, yet the whole Book II workplace comedy rests on her *needing* materials,
  and **no foil ever lands on "she didn't even need any of this."** → **Let the source reveal incriminate
  a coper too.**
- **U4 (Genre-native) — ① title-collector is named, never staged here; the roll-up over-credits it.**
  Its real joke is the *ranking / disappointment at a weak epithet* (gacha-pull logic), which is
  Gael-internal POV, not foil-reaction. → **Stop crediting ① as a foil beat; flag it as POV-layer.**
- **U5 (Genre-native) — ⑥ self-audit is criminally underused** (the bible's funniest gag; lands once, on
  Otto's decoy-diary "a real villain would've been caught with worse"). → **Add ⑥ where cheap.**

## Divergences adjudicated

- **Daemon's ②-dodge.** Genre-native: *freshest beat in the doc.* Youjo: *"a label, not a beat."* →
  **Both right:** the *deflation* (gagged predator who can't speak the crime that convicts him) is strong;
  the bare "files him as a ②-dodge" is weak. **Resolution: stage the line, kill the label.**
- **Maester's Valyrian-steel syllogism.** Youjo: *top-tier.* Genre-native: *clever-horror, not funny;
  documenter-sounds-insane is genre-default.* → **Keep the syllogism (it lands for the irony reader) and
  add the genre-native's escalation** (the *bureaucracy itself* metastasizes) so it's funny, not only clever.

## What all three agree actually LANDS (protect these)

1. **Alicent's own bestiary entry** — "Mother — sentimental, useful for egg-procurement, low threat."
   Unanimous #1. The laugh and the wound are the same words; the irony is load-bearing on the emotion.
2. **The Septa hoist by her own endorsement** — being right converted into self-incrimination by her own
   coping mechanism. The thesis working.
3. **The Maester's syllogism** (Youjo) / **Hobb's attrition into silence** (Literary) / **Daemon's
   unspeakable diminishment** (Genre-native) — each lens's third pick differs; all are keepers.

## Action list → applied to `character-reactions.md` (revision 2026-06-09)

1. **Break F1 (monotony):** add a **Tempo map** — assign each foil a distinct comic *register* (fast
   one-liner / slow grind / undignified-physical / cold deadpan / tragic near-miss) so they stop rhyming.
   Vary the *tempo*, not just the cope.
2. **Fix F2 (weightless marks):** give each Book-III mark a **virtue/love to betray** — the agent's
   craftsman's-pride in the hull; Quint's desperation for his family; the banker's self-flattering cope
   run to a monstrous conclusion (endows a scholarship that funds her → bestiary "Vendor (self-funding)").
3. **Fix F3 + F4 (escalation; ④):** add the rule **euphemisms get *blander* as deeds get darker** — apex
   murder = the most boring word in the book (a calendar line), not "vendor dispute." Stage ① ranking if
   kept; otherwise demote it in the roll-up (U4).
4. **Fix F5 (staged not asserted):** replace the nursery, septa, factor, banker, and post-mortem
   assertions with the critics' concrete staged beats (the "takes notes" image; the one-statement-amplified
   ratchet; the useful-tip-from-the-man-about-to-be-dissolved; the scholarship; three named wrong theories
   + the binned true report, "the realm prefers the war").
5. **U1/U2/U3:** seed curdle into more foils; **add the apex-silence witness (Wylla)**; **add a
   source-reveal cope-incrimination** (a foil half-realizes the empire had no purpose).
6. **F6 (labels):** soften the announcing labels; let the beats operate.
7. **U5 (⑥):** add self-audit beats where cheap (Quint "polite, so not the villain").
8. **Maester:** add the metastasizing-bureaucracy escalation atop the syllogism.
