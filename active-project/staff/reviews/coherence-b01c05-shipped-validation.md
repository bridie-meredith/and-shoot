The non-intervention is the moral-cost the chapter is built to deliver, not an oversight.
- **Is the PROSE incoherent?** No. The prose renders the recurring-body status (line 23, "filed three mornings running"), the held discipline (line 25), and the non-intervention (the wall-line hold, the filing) coherently and on-page. The reader CAN follow that Taylor watches and does not act, and that this is deliberate.
- **BUCKET: NOT A WEAVE-GAP.** This is coherent prose rendering a challenging design. The discomfort of a protagonist who observes enforcement-violence and files it neutrally is the chapter's intended substance (road-to-hell chain-shape; the licensed-exception running without a switch — vibes:9/vibes:10). The design carries inherent cold-read risk (a reader may dislike the non-intervention), but that is a design property the principal accepted, not a prose defect.

- **Severity / routing:** ADVISORY. Do NOT route to revise. The discomfort is contract-authorized.

#### (b2) Feed-mechanics opacity (what the "feed" is mechanically)
