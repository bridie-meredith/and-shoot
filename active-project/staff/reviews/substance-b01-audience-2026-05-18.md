## Per-reviewer verdicts

### cape-fic-reader

verdict: REVISE

findings:

- **c04/c05 (d03 split) — refusal-as-formality risk.** Otto appears, receives the refusal "without pressure," adjusts his posture "as though making a note," and leaves. Between c04 and c05, no antagonist-pressure event is described — the calculation dissolves the refusal on its own. The chunk correctly says the refusal holds "long enough that the second answer is a choice" and that Taylor "can see both answers clearly, she has not been coerced." But for the refusal in c04 to feel like real load rather than a narrative beat that makes c05 look like a choice, some antagonist action or world-event should arrive between the two chapters that makes the calculation possible and pressing. As written, the calculation in c05 would have run whenever Taylor ran it — the c04 refusal delays it by one chapter without anything in the world changing to justify why the calculation became decisive now rather than at c04. The load is on the arithmetic alone, and the arithmetic was always going to win. The refusal needs to feel like it was genuinely holding something back, not marking a beat before the inevitable.

- **c11 (d09) — articulated-contempt mechanism approximate.** This is the largest single-chapter move on the political-register axis (+1.5, 6→7.5) and the book's contempt-hinge. The chunk describes the mechanism as "a sustained intelligence sequence — tracking a specific political play through multiple court feeds over several days." What she observes is given in category terms: "briefings received but not read, instruments signed but not understood, dynastic prerogative performed as though the smallfolk burning in the eventual war are a weather event." These are accurate categories, but the contempt arrives as a conclusion that needs a specific threshold-crossing observation — one specific feed-moment that completes the accumulation. "She names whose names" is in the right register but it's a placeholder for a roster and a specific triggering moment. As written, the +1.5 contempt-axis move is asserted to arrive from "several days" of sustained observation rather than shown to arrive from a specific beat. The contempt is told as a conclusion; it needs one moment where it lands.

- **c16 (d13) — single-axis thinness.** One axis in motion (+1.0 political-register). The chapter earns its keep through the Wren-no-accounting beat (Taylor looks at Wren and runs no accounting; the non-entry is the final form of non-pricing) and the final-delivery-is-legible-and-not-relief sequence. Both beats are present. But a chapter positioned between the book's climax sequence and the burn, carrying only one axis in motion, risks reading as a marking-time bridge despite being labeled "falling." The existing beats are load-bearing; the chapter needs to ensure none of those scenes feel transitional.

- **Chapters that pass cleanly:** c01 (baseline correctly established), c02 (rescue costed with witnesses), c03 (removal-from-model enacted not asserted), c06 (criterion-is-the-problem correctly shown), c07 (resentment-as-color correctly pre-verbal), c08 (criterion-displacement named without reversal), c09 (arrangement-naming + ledger-fluency-as-problem), c10 (Wren as load-bearing without ledger entry, witch-label constraint correctly operating), c12 (no-exit + courier detention + first-recognition suppressed — the book's clearest consequence chapter), c13 (second-recognition rationalized + Wren-stitching close), c14-c15 (full deployment two-beat split works), c17 (attempt-and-failure, not just failure), c18 (Corvan not filing a verdict is earned).

notes: The axes roll up correctly. The cost ledger is consistently applied — the specific ledger entries (cl-otto-trade, cl-network-position, cl-unpriced-cost-bearer, cl-protection-buys-consolidation) are correctly anchored to chapter-level moves throughout. The two-vector relational-anchor-status mechanism (cl-unpriced-cost-bearer operating through Taylor's refusal to price Wren + cl-protection-buys-consolidation operating through each protect-target extension) is visible in both vectors across the chapter plan and the compounding is readable. The social-tether-as-trap mechanism (Flea Bottom embedding becomes the thing that makes Taylor visible to Otto and non-extractable) is correctly architected across c02→c06→c12. These are the plan's structural strengths. The two flagged chapters are the places where the mechanism description needs more specificity before scene-authoring begins.

### dark-fantasy-reader

verdict: REVISE

findings:

- **c04/c05 (d03) — Otto not pressing reads as enemy pausing at a convenient moment.** The hot button is enemies who pause at convenient moments. Otto receives the refusal and leaves. The chunk correctly notes he has learned "where the threshold is." But between c04 and c05, no texture of Otto continuing to operate is shown — not even Taylor registering, in her political-tracking, that Otto's apparatus is still moving calibrated to what he has heard. The refusal pause has to be read as Otto having made a specific subsequent calculation that the reader can identify in world-texture, even if they cannot see Otto's interiority directly. As written, the pause is characterized only by Taylor's accounting of the encounter as "prohibition held; function declined; position unchanged." That's correct from Taylor's register, but the dark-fantasy-reader's concern is that an antagonist who is as calibrated as this chunk describes Otto to be does not simply wait between c04 and c05 for the arithmetic to do his work. The calculation becoming decisive in c05 should correspond to something Otto did after c04 — an action that Taylor reads as part of the same political sequence, even if she doesn't name it as pressure. Without that texture, Otto between c04 and c05 is absent from the field in a way that feels like a pause at a convenient moment.

- **c11 (d09) — contempt as conclusion needs one specific image.** The trajectory says the contempt "arrives not as a feeling but as a conclusion." This is correct for the register. But "briefings received but not read, instruments signed but not understood, dynastic prerogative performed as though the smallfolk burning in the eventual war are a weather event rather than a consequence" is the category of observation, not the observation itself. The dark-fantasy-reader needs one specific image from the court-layer feed that the chunk plan names — the specific moment that completes the accumulation. Not because the accumulation is wrong, but because "sustained sequence" plus category description is asking the screen-writer to find the landing-image on their own. The +1.5 on political-register is the book's largest single contempt-axis move; it should not rest on approximate mechanism. The chunk plan should name what specifically she saw that day — the one brief she watched received that made the conclusion arrive, not as resentment but as legibility.

- **c03 and c08 — interior-accounting chapters without Westerosi texture.** Both chapters are nearly entirely interior. This is tolerable for this register, but both chapters end on Taylor's self-narration rather than on any world-consequence visible outside her interiority. C03 ends on "The reader knows what she has done." C08 ends on "She tells herself this, too, is the atonement working." These are correct as closing registers for Taylor's POV, but the dark-fantasy-reader wants at least one brief moment in each chapter where Westerosi world-texture grounds the interiority — not as action, but as the world continuing to be what it is around Taylor's accounting. This is a scene-level note rather than a chunk-level finding, flagged for the screen-writer's attention: do not write these chapters as pure interiority without at least one anchoring detail of the world-as-place.

- **c12 — passes the test most cleanly.** The courier detained by Taylor's route-map is the consequence-propagation event this reader has been waiting for since c06. The courier is not dead — detained, questioned — but the accounting on them is legible and Taylor runs it and closes it. The suppression of the first-recognition (filed and continued) is enacted not asserted. This is the book's clearest consequence chapter and the benchmark for the others.

- **c17 — the attempt-and-failure structure is correct.** Taylor threads every insect she has through the ward and cannot reroute the violence. The reason is structurally explained ("not a courier path, it is a faction's bladed answer"). The failure is caused, not given. The charting of the streets is the mechanism of Wren dying in them. This is the kind of consequence-propagation the dark-fantasy-reader needs to see in a burn chapter: the protection apparatus fails not because it wasn't tried but because what it was built for was not the shape of the threat.

- **c18 — earned bleakness.** Corvan not filing a verdict because he does not have the data is correct. The hook-adjacent blocks being especially lethal completing the coverage-center irony is the right kind of Westerosi-chronicle close. The ledger-of-the-empowered as the document Corvan is reading without knowing what it is — the reader's recognition, not the maester's — is the earned bleakness this story requires.

notes: The Aemond walk-ons (c10, c14, c17) are correctly staged — each shifts a named plot-axis, none overstay, the escalation from presence to operative to Dance-trigger tracks correctly. The witch-label as operational constraint is Westerosi-specific and correctly applied. The book's overall consequence-propagation architecture is sound; the two flagged chapters (c04/c05 and c11) are the places where the screen-writer needs a specific image or event to anchor the mechanism.

### worm-canon-pedant

verdict: REVISE

findings:

- **c05 — information path for Sera Hightower survival-probability model is not established.** The c05 chunk says the calculation runs with "the information she has now assembled — Sera Hightower's position in the succession calculus, the probability distribution on her survival without an inside adjustment, the downstream consequences of that particular loss." C04's handoff describes Taylor as having begun "systematic Flea Bottom political-layer reading to track whether Otto resurfaces" — a ground-layer information-gathering mode. A probability model on Sera Hightower's court-tier survival position requires court-layer information Taylor does not yet have a documented path to at the c04/c05 seam. Taylor at this point has: a picture of Otto's Green-faction network from the relay-chain he used to find her, her nascent systematic reading of Flea Bottom political layer, and the information Otto himself conveyed in their meeting. The c04 chunk describes their conversation as "direct: he has a function that requires her specific capability and offers a specific exchange." Did Otto in that meeting give Taylor enough court-layer information about Sera Hightower's position that Taylor can model her survival probability? The chunk doesn't establish this. If that's the source, it should be named in c04's handoff or c05's chunk text as "the information Otto provided in their meeting." If Taylor built the model from her own reading in the gap between c04 and c05, the chunk should describe what she was reading and how it gave her court-tier visibility. As written, this is a lore-leak risk: at scene-authoring time, Taylor will know something about Sera Hightower's survival odds without a traceable in-world path for that knowledge.

- **c11 — "she names whose names" needs a roster stub.** "She names whose names — in the accounting, in the entries, in the lines of the ledger she has been keeping." This is the correct register. But "whose names" is a placeholder. At scene-authoring time the screen-writer will need to decide whether Taylor is naming Alicent Hightower, Aegon the Elder, specific Small Council members, Otto himself, or a named subset of the Green-faction inner circle. The chunk plan does not establish who is in scope for the contempt-naming. This is a scene-authoring dependency that should be pre-resolved at chunk level — either name the roster here or establish that the contempt-naming is general (she names the faction as a collective, not individuals). The distinction matters for Taylor's register: canon-Taylor's contempt tends to be specific-to-persons-responsible rather than general, which would require individual names.

- **Capability mechanics — consistently maintained.** The distinction between surveillance+unconsented-instrumentalization-of-movement-patterns (what Taylor is doing) and nervous-system-override (what Khepri did) is correctly preserved across all 18 chapters. No chapter describes Taylor directing a person's body. The "compound-eye awareness" and "insect-feed" framing is consistently Westerosi-adjacent. The witch-label constraint is correctly used as the operational limit that drives the Wren-as-anchor mechanism. The multi-shard hijack capability is correctly absent. No lore violations on capability.

- **Taylor's cold-utilitarian register — maintained.** The accounting voice, the prohibition-as-load-bearing-architecture, the refusal to name emotions as emotions, the care-registered-as-methodological-decision — all correct for canon-Taylor's source register. The AU divergence (post-Gold-Morning, Khepri-haunted, penitential) is named in the series document and does not produce in-canon contradictions at this level of the plan. Taylor's ignorance of Westerosi court-tier politics is correctly played — she is reading what she can read from insect-feed and inferring from there, not knowing things she has no path to knowing (except at the c05 seam, which is the flagged concern).

- **Otto's meeting in c04 — AU-tolerance.** Otto appearing personally in plain wool rather than through an intermediary is AU-tolerant per the project's boundary-scope. No flag.

- **c09 moral-framework intermediate labeling.** The axis labels (first-sanctioned-exception → systematic-override-rationalized → irrevocable-Khepri-repetition) map to trajectory d03/d07/d12 milestones but are intermediate values (2.0, 1.5, 1.0) within the one/five/nine rubric's end-anchors. The intermediate labels are internally consistent with the chunk plan's mechanism descriptions. Not a hard flag — noting for scene-authoring: the bone-gate reviewer at /and-write Phase 6 should evaluate moral-framework bones against the trajectory milestone (d03/d07/d12) rather than against the axis end-anchors alone.

notes: The worm-canon-pedant's primary concern is the c05 information path. This is the kind of error that does not show up in structural review but becomes a scene-level problem when the screen-writer writes Taylor running a model on Sera Hightower's survival odds without establishing where she got the court-tier picture to run it from. If the information came from Otto in the c04 meeting, add one line to c04's handoff naming it. If it came from Taylor's systematic reading in the c04-c05 gap, add one sentence to c05's chunk describing what she was reading to build the picture. The fix is small; the lore-leak risk without it is real.

## Aggregated findings (2-of-3 surfaced)

**Hard findings:**

- **[SUBSTANCE-SUSPECT: political-register-toward-elite — c11]** — flagged by cape-fic-reader and dark-fantasy-reader. The articulated-contempt arrival (+1.5, the book's largest single-chapter political-register move) is described in approximate mechanism terms: "sustained intelligence sequence," "several days," category descriptions of court behavior observed. No specific threshold-crossing image is named in the chunk that shows the contempt arriving as a conclusion rather than deepening as a resentment. The chunk needs one specific observable moment from the court-layer feed that the screen-writer can use as the landing-beat. Without it, this chapter risks being authored as "contempt deepened enough that Taylor named it" — asserted, not enacted.

- **[STRUCTURAL-GAP: d03 split, c04 refusal / c05 acceptance]** — flagged by cape-fic-reader (refusal-as-formality risk) and dark-fantasy-reader (Otto not pressing reads as enemy-pause). The refusal in c04 is formally correct but structurally thin: Otto leaves, nothing happens in the world between chapters, and the arithmetic dissolves the refusal in c05 on its own schedule. The refusal needs to hold against something — either a world-event in the gap that raises the cost of refusal, or a texture of Otto continuing to operate that Taylor reads in her political-tracking (making the calculation not just arithmetic but Otto-plus-arithmetic), or some mechanism that makes the calculation become decisive now rather than at c04. As written, the c04 refusal is a gesture that delays c05 by one chapter without earning the delay. Compounded by worm-canon-pedant's adjacent finding: the probability model Taylor runs in c05 requires court-layer information about Sera Hightower's position that has no documented acquisition path in the c04 handoff.

**Strong recommendations (1-of-3, surfaced as advisory):**

- **[c05 information path — worm-canon-pedant]** — Where did Taylor get the court-tier picture of Sera Hightower's survival probability? If from Otto's c04 meeting, name it. If from Taylor's independent reading in the gap, describe what she read. This is a scene-authoring dependency that should be resolved at chunk level to prevent a lore-leak.

- **[c11 contempt-naming roster — worm-canon-pedant]** — "She names whose names" needs to be resolved to either a specific roster (Alicent, Aegon the Elder, named Small Council members) or an explicit acknowledgment that the contempt-naming is collective (the faction, not individuals). Canon-Taylor's contempt register is person-specific, not categorical; the screen-writer needs a decision from the chunk plan.

- **[c03 and c08 Westerosi texture — dark-fantasy-reader]** — Both interior-accounting chapters close on Taylor's self-narration without a grounding world-texture beat. Scene-level note for the screen-writer: do not author these chapters as pure interiority; at least one beat in each chapter should anchor the accounting in a specific Westerosi sensory or social detail.

## Notes / recommendations for revise

It should be a single observed act — something the Green-faction inner circle does in a specific scene that Taylor can name in the ledger as the conclusion-arriving-moment rather than the resentment-deepening-moment. The distinction matters: resentment was already named at c07; articulated contempt at c11 requires Taylor to have concluded something she had not yet concluded, not just to have seen more of the same. The chunk should name what that conclusion-completing act is.

2. **c04/c05 seam:** Choose one of the following:

3. **c11 "whose names" placeholder:** Resolve this to a roster stub or a collective-naming decision before scene-authoring begins. This is a small addition to the chunk text.
