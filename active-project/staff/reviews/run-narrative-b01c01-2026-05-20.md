## At a glance

The active project is **taylor-westeros-good-intentions**: a one-book Worm/ASOIAF crossover in which post-Gold-Morning Taylor Hebert lands in 122 AC King's Landing with one operating rule — *be useful without taking control* — and Otto Hightower prices that rule out of her, one calibrated trade at a time, until her insect-network has rebuilt a moral rhyme of Khepri in the service of a Green-faction succession architecture whose Dance she cannot reroute when it ignites.

This was the first end-to-end run of the **substance overhaul** pipeline — the variant of the system in which every authoring stage produces a "substance contract" before any prose is written: a declared signature of state axes (moral-framework, capability, position, social-tether, relational-anchor-status, moral-legibility-to-self, political-register-toward-elite, knowledge, agency), a cost ledger of which axis-trades the story actually pays for, and per-chunk **state-delta targets** that govern chunk sizing top-down — series → book → chapter → scene → bone. The animating idea under test: that if you size every chunk by *how many points of state must move in it*, and gate every bone on whether it moves a declared axis through a declared cost, the bones-first authoring layer becomes the structural authority and the facet/stitch layers become surface-rendering services. Chapter b01c01 is the first chapter to be carried all the way to a stitched draft under that contract — 599 words, three scenes, one POV, opening the operating rule and the un-priced child it will fail to save.
