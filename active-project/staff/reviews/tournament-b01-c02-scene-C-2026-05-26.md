## Why winner won
Arm-1 inverts the bones' compound-noun parallel-clause default instead of amplifying it: the peak @40 lands as a true single-sentence standalone, @39 and @41 are isolated, @42's line-drawing is rendered as the discipline of not-looking (the recognition-holding-suppression pattern enacted, not summarized), and @43's body-closure has distinct physical content (chest, shoulder, brick, heel) rather than echoing @42 in parallel-clause register. The scene-bridge at @30 ("I noticed the slant before I noticed I had noticed it") is the strongest person-in-voice line in either variant and does the loc-state:9 / sensory:2 work without exposition-fold. The ledger trio @44-@46 fuses but ward-junction is differentiated per narrator:11 ("The last one cost more to close than the others. The cost did not appear in the closing"), and the exhale paragraph @47 trails into dusk to seal vibes:10 sealed-not-resolved without editorializing.

## Why runner-up lost
Arm-2 amplifies the bones' parallel-clause cadence into the prose itself, so the compound-noun stacks ("lane-mouth sweep", "fever-cluster corner", "ward-junction entry") never get torqued into action and the "feet... feet... head... head" repetition reads metronomic rather than load-bearing. The @43 closure stays in nominal-clause register ("the wall at my back, the gap in shade, the shoulder against the stone where the stone met the spine") so the body is described rather than enacted, and the bare "I exhaled." @47 leaves vibes:10 under-realized against the scene-map's sealed-not-resolved spec.
