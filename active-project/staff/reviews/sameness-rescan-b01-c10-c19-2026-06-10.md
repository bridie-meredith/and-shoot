## Method

Read all ten current drafts (b01-c10 … b01-c19) end to end. For each chapter recorded: opening move, central scene-move, and **closing gesture** (the load-bearing sameness axis). Closings classified into two buckets:

- **DESK-TIC** = chapter ends at the feed-station / working surface on a stylus-or-pen-or-palm-against-the-writing-surface gesture (the literal recurring tic).
- **EXIT / OTHER** = chapter ends elsewhere (door, lane, trough, stairs, leaving the room) or on a non-desk-gesture beat.
