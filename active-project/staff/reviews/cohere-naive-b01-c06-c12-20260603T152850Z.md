# Q6 — apparatus-register cumulative load (LOAD-BEARING) — CAUTION

**The openings now read as distinct, not as a repeated formula** — this is the genuine win of the revision. Each italic prologue does a different job: c06 sets up a form about to break ("What Otto asked for next would not keep the form"); c07 is a meditation on a cost going quiet ("a cost you no longer feel is the one that has finished its work"); c08 is two terse lines; c09 names Wren and the indexing she didn't consent to; c10 is a self-interrogation ("it would ask whether that was true"); c11 names the arrangement and points at the one observation left unrouted; c12 narrows to the two open columns. They open on different emotional and structural footing, and the sept-bell/season cues vary the texture. That formula problem is solved. **The CAUTION is the body register, not the openings.** The technical accounting language stays readable through c06–c09 because each chapter has a human anchor — Sera's debt, Septon Halvard's argument, the Cobb girl, Wren. But c10–c12 tip toward "data-entry in riddles": dense strings like "the geometry holding all around the place his mark had gone," "the single axis-move settling both," "the fifth-ward circuit," "the aggregate-shape entry... the harm-prevention column... the breach column" stack columns/entries/registers with diminishing human ballast. **It tips in c12** — the final chapter is the most abstract of the seven, closing on "the older word... not filed with it," and a first reader has to work to feel what is at stake rather than parse it. Not airless enough to FAIL — the Corwick thread and the Wren-as-boundary mechanism carry real weight — but the closing third leans hardest on the register the prior read flagged. Verdict: improved off the prior FAIL, lands at CAUTION.
