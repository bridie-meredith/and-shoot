## Rationale

**On the magic-fence pressure — does the softened framing relieve it?**

Partially. The `nine_means` reframe ("surveillance + unconsented instrumentalization, not control-override") is a meaningful clarification of the sin — it names what the repetition *is* rather than what it rhymes with, and it separates the moral structure from the mechanical one. The b01 chunk substitution ("builds something that rhymes with Khepri in the only register left to her") is better than the direct miniature-architecture framing: it admits uncertainty about the shape of the thing she is building, and "in the only register left to her" carries the sense of capability constrained by circumstance rather than deployed at will. These changes are real and they move the signature in the right direction.

What they do not resolve: the trajectory deltas still describe the capability arc in operational confidence language. D04 says she builds the network outward and routes information "derived from those observations through people who do not know they are being observed" — the sin is named but the operation itself reads as executing cleanly. D12 says she "push[es] the network to full coverage, reading and routing more bodies through more wards without their knowledge than at any prior point" — again, the moral weight is present but the mechanics read as reliable. The watch-item from pass 1 was specifically about whether bones will show *the texture of uncertain operation* — routing through people who resist the read, blocks where insect density triggers the witch-label requiring workarounds. The trajectory does not foreground this friction; it names the moral shape and describes the operational outcome. At the signature level, that is acceptable — trajectory is not the bone-gate. But the watch-item is not relieved; it is carried forward with higher specificity.

**On social-tether-as-trap and position-as-visibility — Westerosi political mechanism or authorial telling?**

The b01 chunk language "community-as-tether and community-as-trap braid the same rope" lands as stated irony rather than demonstrated mechanism. The chunk is descriptive-of-result, not evocative-of-physics: it names the outcome of the tether/trap braiding but does not show the Westerosi-specific texture of how that operates — what the embedding looks like to witnesses, what becoming "known to one court layer as a functional asset" costs in Planetos social terms. The position-axis `one_means`/`five_means`/`nine_means` entries do the structural work cleanly. The chunk's prose gloss over that structure reads as the author's summary of what happened, not as the world's mechanics applied to a specific actor. This is not a new flag — it is the same concern as pass 1's "world as set dressing" proximity — but it is now more visible because the capability language has been refined and this piece is comparatively softer. At the bone-gate, position-as-visibility bones need to show the Westerosi witness-vocabulary responding to Taylor's increasing legibility, not Taylor internally noting her position has changed.

**Net assessment:** The pass 2 revisions are substantive but scope-limited. The magic-fence pressure is partially relieved at the signature level; the watch-item is refined, not closed. Social-tether-as-trap is named but not yet demonstrated as Westerosi-specific mechanism. The signature still passes as a planning document — the trajectory is structurally sound and the irony is load-bearing where it needs to be. SUBSTANCE-FELT carries forward because the watch-item is now a bone-gate obligation, not a signature correction, and one new watch-item is added.

Capability bones that describe the outcome of the operation without the grain of its uncertainty are a flag.

Taylor internally noting her position has changed is not sufficient.
