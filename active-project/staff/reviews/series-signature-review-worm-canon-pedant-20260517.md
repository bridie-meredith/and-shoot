## Primary Fault — Capability Axis Framing Under-Prices the Architecture Rhyme

The b01 chunk says "threading bodies through routes without their knowledge." The trajectory at d04 labels the state shift "Khepri-architecture-in-miniature."

`cond-khepri-residue-122ac` has permanently sealed multi-shard hijack — coordinating or routing human nervous systems is a hard fence violation. What survives is insect control and a degraded pattern-precog that runs as ordinary cognition.

"Threading bodies through routes" is ambiguous in exactly the wrong direction. If it means Taylor uses insect-feed surveillance to map streets and then identifies which human couriers follow predictable paths she has already observed — that is the permissible reading. If it means Taylor's insects or pattern-recognition is influencing or directing human movement, that is the sealed capability sneaking back through a metaphor.

The planning documents are using "Khepri-architecture in miniature" as the capability label across d04, d07, d12, and the b01 chunk summary. That label is doing conceptual work it isn't licensed to do. Khepri coordinated human bodies. Taylor in this project cannot. The architecture she is rebuilding is an intelligence-network architecture — insect surveillance + pattern-reading + information routing through people who do not know they are being observed. That is meaningfully different from Khepri and the label is blurring it.

The rhyme the story is going for — that what Taylor built for atonement repeats the shape of what she atoned for — is canonically correct and is the right dramatic spine. But the rhyme only lands if the gap between the two is priced. Khepri hijacked thousands of human nervous systems simultaneously across a continent. The miniature is: Taylor's insects observe human bodies without those bodies knowing, and she routes information based on what she observes. The sin is surveillance and instrumentalization without consent, not override. That is a real Khepri-rhyme. But calling it "the same architecture" at d12 — "the scale is smaller than Gold Morning; the architecture is the same" — flattens that gap and risks either (a) prose that treats this as morally equivalent to Gold Morning in ways it isn't, or (b) prose that needs the gap to be real for the tragedy to work but has the signature claiming they are structurally identical.

The signature needs to name what the architecture actually is: insect-network surveillance + unconsented instrumentalization of observable patterns in human movement, not control of human movement. That is a genuine Khepri-rhyme and it is cheaper than Khepri — that cheapness is the point. The tragedy is that a lesser repetition is still a repetition. If the signature says "same architecture," bones-gate will pass content it should not.

## Secondary Fault — Otto Acceptance Rate at d03

d03 has Taylor calculate and accept on first presentation. The trajectory notes "the first trade is the auditable mistake beat" but there is no prior beat where Taylor attempts refusal and the refusal collapses — she arrives at the offer, runs the arithmetic, and takes it.

Post-Gold-Morning Taylor's operating rule at story open is explicit: "no one becomes an instrument again." This is not a mild preference. In Worm-canon terms this is Taylor post-Khepri, post-removal of her own agency by the Entities, post-the-thing-she-did. The prohibition against control is load-bearing to her as a person.

A single-beat accept at d03 — however calibrated Otto's ask — under-prices the collapse. Taylor should have a beat that reads as her trying to find a path that doesn't require accepting. She doesn't find one. Then she accepts. The beat does not need to be long. But the absence of it means the moral-framework axis starts its collapse not from prohibition-under-pressure but from prohibition-that-wasn't-really-that-binding. That is not post-Gold-Morning Taylor.

This is not a hard canon error — the trajectory does establish that the prohibition exists and the calculation overrides it. But "the calculation is clear" as the complete explanation for d03 acceptance is thin for this character. Bones-gate needs at least one delta sequence where the refusal is attempted and what makes it fail is named.

## What Tracks

The contempt-without-refusal endpoint is correct. "She has not been wrong about them once" at d13 is the specific register of post-Worm Taylor — the cost of accuracy as its own trap. That is this character.

The capability axis measured as willingness-to-deploy rather than power-growth is also correct and canon-consistent. Taylor's insect-control did not grow over Worm; her operational scope expanded because she deployed more. The 3→7 ramp needs bones to carry this as deployment-willingness, not shard-potency increase. The signature's start-state description — "she is keeping it dormant as penance" — names this correctly. As long as the delta descriptions in subsequent chapters do not write this as "her power grew," the axis is in good shape.

The Earth-Bet noun fence is well-handled at signature level. "Khepri" appears as a planning-document label, not as Taylor's self-reference. The trajectory uses operational descriptions ("insect-network," "compound-eye observation," "intelligence delivery") without activating the banned vocabulary in the register that would propagate to prose.

The moral-legibility-to-self axis (7→4, inverted baseline, recognition-too-late) is the correct canon shape for this character. Taylor starts self-aware and ends with her self-awareness as the trap rather than the escape. That is not generic tragic-protagonist. That is her.

## What Bones-Gate Needs to Watch

1. Any bone that uses "threading" or "routing" human movement must be reviewed against `cond-khepri-residue-122ac`. Insect-surveillance of human movement is permitted. Influencing human movement through any mechanism is a fence violation.

2. d04 label "Khepri-architecture-in-miniature" should be treated as a planning-document label only. Prose at d04 must describe what the architecture actually is: surveillance-and-inference, not control. If the prose reaches for "she moved them through the streets," that is the violation.

3. d12's "the scale is smaller than Gold Morning; the architecture is the same" needs to be qualified in the bones that carry it. The architecture is the same in shape (unconsented instrumentalization of observed persons for a strategic goal). It is not the same in mechanism (insect observation vs. Khepri override). The tragedy requires the gap to be visible alongside the rhyme.

4. d03 bones should include at least one beat of Taylor attempting a path that avoids the accept. The calculation wins. But the beat needs to be there for the prohibition-collapse to price correctly.

---

## Summary

The signature is not flat — the axes are in motion, the costs are ledgered, and the endpoint is right for this character. The fault is in the "Khepri-architecture" labeling blurring the gap between what Khepri did and what Taylor is doing in this story. That gap is load-bearing. The rhyme works because the lesser repetition is still a repetition. The signature needs to name the architecture accurately so that bones-gate does not pass content that claims equivalence the canon does not support — and so that the tragedy's specific moral weight (surveillance and unconsented instrumentalization, not control-override) is preserved through to draft.
