## Why winner won (3-5 sentences)
P1 counterweights the bones' parallel-clause infrastructure with genuine variance — short declaratives ("The ceiling." / "I pushed the range.") punch through longer observational clauses, and the body enters the prose at concrete points (wall against right shoulder, back of the skull, the slow approach to the lane-mouth). The setup→payoff at @9–@11 is staged unspoken: the dropout arrives, the reader registers it as cost, and Taylor names the decision to spend more without the prose announcing the metaphor. P1 trusts the reader to feel the bill being paid; P2 hands them a receipt. The voice across P1 holds a particular intelligence pricing the work, where P2's voice is closer to the system humming — observation of mechanism rather than a mind inside it.

## Why runner-up lost (2-3 sentences)
P2 amplified the bones' clipped parallel rhythm instead of counterweighting it, and the recursive count sentences ("The count was even. The count was the gauge by which the count stayed even.") tipped into metronome tic-regularity exactly where variance was needed. The "back of the bill, signed in advance" announces the metaphor P1 leaves implicit, and "the hands of the work were not the hands" gestures at dissociation rather than dramatizing it — a tell that the voice is reaching for significance instead of letting the events carry it.
