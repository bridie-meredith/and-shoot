## Re-rendered span (person-first, same content, bones 23-35)

By evening I had the room-floor to myself, the lamp unlit because the window's last light was the working light I preferred, and the air in the lodging room held the still that an indoor room keeps when no through-transit moves it — a different stillness from the lane's, dense in the corners, the boards underfoot dry where the lane had been damp at the wall. I sat with my back to the bedframe and set the day on the floor in front of me the way a person lays out coin to count it.

I ran the morning's lane back through me first, the coverage below the Hook returned in the same shape it had gone out, and I took the count and the body-flags and the gait-records and let each one close against its return, every entry resolved, the lane settling into the form a place keeps when nothing about it has asked to be remembered.

Then I ran the junction back through me the way I had run the Hook. I crossed the provisioner-train at the junction again at the hour it had crossed, carried it east the way I had carried it east in the morning, and let it clear. I kept on into the courier-window, into the three figures in the side-alley, into the report-entry I had delivered at the line where my own filings close.

I could not let the junction go quiet. The color that had been on it at the morning hour was on it at the evening hour, present when I brought it back the way it had been present when I read it live, the route carrying the provisioner-train through the same architecture and the architecture coming back with the train. I ran the junction a second length to learn whether it was my read and not the route; the color persisted.

My head tilted toward the held color. I stopped.

The stopping was the body's, before I had a word for what the body was doing. The hand I had laid flat on the boards came up off the boards. I had been keeping the read neutral, an instrument with a use and nothing it was permitted to want, and now I would not carry the route as only a route — not the lane, not the courier, but the architecture the route went through, the architecture I had not asked myself to mark. The discipline I had been keeping was the discipline of a neutral read; the discipline was what I was no longer willing to support.

I closed the evening review. The window's light had thinned to the line above the sill, and I closed the day's coverage out into the form it keeps when the day is done, the entries delivered, the count resolved.

I filed the courier's body-record. The face the alley had returned went into the body-map under the recurring-body anchor I had opened that morning, the enforcement-incident attached, the entry going in the way I put a body into the record when the record is the route I will move it along later, and I held the face there a length and then I closed the file.

I ran the junction flat, with nothing in it but the count.

I could not. I crossed the provisioner-train at the junction again at the morning hour and brought the crossing back and the architecture came back with it; I could not run the junction the way I had run the Hook, could not close it clean against its return, could not take it as a count and only a count. The door I had turned did not open at the turn.

I ran it flat again, slower this time, taking the junction at a length deliberate enough to let the neutral read seat if it would seat.

## Diff note — before → after

1. **"I ran the Hook-feed first, the morning's coverage of the lane below the Hook brought back through me in the same shape it had gone out, and the feed delivered the count and the body-flags and the gait-records and closed itself out clean"** → "I ran the morning's lane back through me first... and I took the count and the body-flags and the gait-records and let each one close against its return." The feed stops being the thing that *delivers* and *closes*; I do the taking and the letting-close.

2. **"The rushwick-feed did not settle."** → "I could not let the junction go quiet." Apparatus-as-subject (the feed fails to settle) becomes person-as-subject (I cannot quiet it). Same beat (bone 28, "holds the color"), no new content.

3. **"I stopped the rushwick-pass."** → "I stopped." Drops the apparatus-object; the source already makes the next line ("The stopping was the body's") the subject of the act, so removing "the rushwick-pass" invents nothing.

4. **"the feed was an apparatus I had been keeping neutral, an instrument with a use, and the instrument was recognizing the route it carried as the route"** → "I had been keeping the read neutral, an instrument with a use... and now I would not carry the route as only a route." The recognition moves from the instrument to me. This is the load-bearing beat of the scene (the goal: "the feed stops being neutral"), and person-first makes the refusal *mine* rather than the tool's.

5. **"I ran the rushwick flat-read."** → "I ran the junction flat, with nothing in it but the count." / **"The provisioner-train held the rushwick-pass."** → "I could not." The flat-read's failure routes through "I could not" rather than through the train holding the pass.

## Honest assessment

The gain is real and not marginal. The shipped prose is airless mostly because the apparatus is the grammatical agent in nearly every load-bearing sentence — the feed delivers, the feed does not settle, the instrument recognizes, the train holds the pass. A person reading it has no one to stand behind; the "I" is a spectator to her own equipment. Routing those same beats through "I" — I could not let it go quiet, I would not carry the route as only a route, I could not run it the way I ran the Hook — restores an agent who wants and refuses, which is exactly the scene's drama (a discipline breaking). No content was added: every verb maps to an existing bone, and where the source named an apparatus-object I either dropped it (the next sentence already supplies the subject) or recast the feed-action as a first-person mental act ("ran X back through me"), which the c05 idiom already licenses ("brought back through me" appears in the shipped line).

One genuine **[EMBODIMENT-BLOCKED]** beat: the final line, bone 35 "the courier-walk holds the rushwick-pass." Person-first would require me to assert what the walk *does to her* — and the source gives only the inert fact that the courier-walk is what now occupies the pass. Rendering it "I walked the courier instead" is the closest faithful person-first move, but it loses the source's point that the walk *holds* (persists involuntarily) rather than being chosen, and choosing it would invent a volition the bone denies. I marked it partial rather than force it.
