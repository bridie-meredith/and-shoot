## Fault 2 — d03 Acceptance Too Thin: Resolved

The refusal-first-then-accept structure is now in place. The revised d03 cause has a two-beat structure: Taylor refuses first, names the prohibition explicitly ("atonement-via-refusal-of-control, no one becomes an instrument again"), holds it — Otto does not press — and then the calculation does the work. The cause explicitly states: "The refusal holds long enough to make the second answer legible as a choice, not as an inevitability. She accepts on the second answer."

The b01 chunk carries this forward at the summary level: "She refuses first; she names the prohibition that is supposed to hold; the prohibition holds long enough to make the second answer audible as a choice."

The prior review's objection was that a single-beat accept made the prohibition read as not-really-that-binding. The two-beat structure addresses that: the prohibition holds through the first answer, which is what makes the second answer readable as a crossing rather than a non-event. That is the canonical post-Gold-Morning weight the character requires. It is not schematic — "the calculation does" is not the complete explanation; the structure shows the refusal existing, holding, and then being overtaken by the arithmetic, which is the specific failure mode appropriate to this character. The prohibition tried. That it tried is what makes the crossing cost.
