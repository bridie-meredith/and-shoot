## Why winner won
P2 inverts the bones' default cadence rather than amplifying it: long compound-noun sentences are interrupted by short declaratives ("The lamps were lit.", "I turned from the alley-mouth.", "The insects filed the ward-junction contact.") that produce real length-variance and let the peak @27 land standalone as the scene-map prescribes. It is more embodied — hands carry, the head is elsewhere, the narrator does not look up — where P1 stays in a ledger-schema register. It honors restraint: each figure (the yield-as-receipt, the hand that knew the motion) gets one pass and is dropped, where P1 doubles its key figures ("the pattern read as one body. The pattern read as one body"). Scene-map compliance is cleaner — @27 is not fused with mem:2 or @17, and the verbatim opener is given its own paragraph rather than being absorbed into the second clause.

## Why runner-up lost
P1 amplifies the bones' default — compound-noun saturation runs through nearly every sentence and parallel-clause triples ("same foot first, same pause at the sill, same sequence under the same lintel") create the metronome tic the rubric penalizes. It fuses peak @27 into the same sentence as the hand-mem:2 figure, flattening what the scene-map marks as the peak landing. It also names its own discipline ("The discipline held.") — a soft theme-as-statement P2 avoids.
