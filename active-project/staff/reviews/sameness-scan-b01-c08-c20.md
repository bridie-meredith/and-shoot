**Detector prototype + live measurement.** Run 2026-06-10 against the no-ledger rebuilt drafts (DEC-0115, 2026-06-08). Bones are the structural skeleton; four drafts (c10/c13/c16/c19) + two flank contrasts (c07/c20) spot-checked at the rendered-prose layer.

**Headline:** SAMENESS-HIGH on a contiguous core. **8 of the 10 mid-book chapters (c10–c19) run the same central scene-move** — the "feed-desk transaction" template. Two of the ten (c13, c16) break it. The repetition is partly load-bearing (the grinding sameness IS thematic), but the *template runs unbroken across too long a span* and the breaks are not placed where a reader needs them. Recommendation: **a light structural cohere pass**, not a restructure. Touch 3 chapters.

## 2. Sameness matrix / clustering

**Cluster T (feed-desk transaction):** c08, c09, c10, c11, c12, c14, c15, c17, c18, c19 — **10 chapters**, with c08 a thin/transitional member and c15/c17 variants (no-packet / write-false-entry) that still pivot on the *withhold-the-name-at-the-desk* beat.

**Of the 10 mid-book chapters c10–c19: 8 run Template-T as their central move** (c10, c11, c12, c14, c15, c17, c18, c19). The two breaks are **c13 and c16** — both Halvard/observation chapters that resolve through a **two-voice argument** instead of a desk transaction.

**Is it literally the same beat-sequence, or a family with variation?** A **family with a hard invariant**. The invariant — present in every T chapter — is the **withhold beat**: a protected name (almost always Wren; Norren-as-false-cover in c17; "the word/Khepri" in c12; Daven's loss in c19) is *deliberately left off the page*. The mechanical scan confirms it: the "leaves-off / blank / unwritten / no-entry" vocabulary fires in c08, c09(in-head), c11, c12, c14, c15, c17, c18, c19. The variation is in the *frame around* the withhold (which packet, which observed scene precedes it), not in the move itself. The "lift the stylus / set the hand off the surface" terminal beat recurs literally in c08, c11(×2), c12, c14, c15, c19 — the diagnosed "the hand came off the surface" tic is **still present in the rebuilt bones**, exactly as the brief predicted (register fixed, structure faithful).

**Genuine breaks:**
- **c13** — observe-two-condemnations + *named contempt* + Halvard water-trough argument. No feed-desk.
- **c16** — pure Halvard-foreclosure two-hander; the shortest chapter (2 scenes), no desk, no withhold-a-name.
- (Flanks c07 and c20 confirm the template is mid-book-specific: c07 is the dialogue-argument prototype, c20 is the event cascade.)

**Spacing problem:** the breaks are mis-distributed. c13 and c16 sit only 3 apart, leaving an **unbroken T-run of c17→c18→c19** at the very end of the mid-book (three transactions in a row, the last of which, c19, *explicitly narrates its own repetitiveness*: "the same shape I always get… the feet wear the same track into the stone"). The reader hits the densest sameness right before the c20 payoff.

## 3. Severity for a reader

**Both readings are real; the honest verdict is "intentional accretion that has overrun its safe span."**

- **The case that it builds (the repetition IS the point):** This is a story about a woman who has rebuilt the Khepri surveillance-apparatus and cannot stop running it; the *grinding identical-morning sameness of the routine is the substance*. c19 makes this textual ("eleven months… the same reckoning… the same shape"). The withhold-the-name beat accumulates *meaning* across the run — it's a different name each time (Wren, then a false name for Wren, then the column simply left blank, then Daven's loss unwritten), so the pattern is designed to gain weight, not just repeat. The c20 payoff (the flies don't come back from the one lane she always kept clear) only lands because the reader has watched her withhold-Wren at the desk eight times. **Cut the repetition and you cut the payoff's foundation.**

- **The case that it dulls (déjà vu blunts the stakes):** c10 (Corwick taken) and c14 (a man confirmed-to-be-taken) and c18 (city-scale deployment) are the three highest-stakes transactions, and they share so much beat-DNA with the lower-stakes connective chapters (c09, c11, c15) that a sequential reader's escalation-sense flattens. Each chapter's "this is the one that costs something" risks reading as "another morning at the desk." The unbroken c17→c18→c19 run is where this bites hardest — three consecutive withhold-at-the-desk chapters with no structural palate-cleanser.

**Does the no-ledger rebuild make it better or worse?** **Worse for detection, better for survivability — and on net it raises the stakes of acting.** The brief's hypothesis is confirmed: the rebuild fixed the *register* (c10/c13/c19 drafts now render person-first, concrete, breathing prose — genuinely good at the line level) but rendered each chapter *faithful to its existing structure*, so the structural sameness is now **more legible, not less**. Previously the cold ledger-register *masked* the sameness behind uniform opacity (every chapter equally hard to parse, so equally hard to compare). Now that the prose is readable, a reader can actually *see* that c11 and c12 and c14 are the same scene with different furniture. **The rebuild converted a hidden structural problem into a visible one.** That is the correct outcome for a detector, and it is why this scan should be acted on now rather than deferred: the prose quality that makes the mid-book shippable is exactly what makes the repetition perceptible.

## 4. Detector verdict + what a productionized gate should emit

**VERDICT: SAMENESS-HIGH — cluster T spans c08–c19 (12 chapters), with an 8-of-10 core across c10–c19 and an unbroken terminal sub-run c17→c18→c19.**

A productionized detector should raise:

```
SAMENESS-HIGH  scope: b01 c08-c19
  template: T (feed-desk-transaction: packet→read→transcribe→withhold-name→lift-stylus)
  members: c08 c09 c10 c11 c12 c14 c15 c17 c18 c19  (10)
  breaks:  c13 c16  (2)
  longest_unbroken_run: c17-c18-c19  (3)
  invariant_beat: withhold-the-protected-name  (fires in 9/10 members)
  terminal_tic: "lift the stylus / hand off the surface"  (literal recurrence in 6 members)
  → flag for /and-cohere structural pass
```

**Threshold that distinguishes harmful repetition from intentional accretion** (proposed gate rule):

> A shared central scene-move template across N consecutive chapters is **intentional accretion (OK)** if BOTH hold: (a) the longest unbroken run ≤ **3** chapters before a structurally-different chapter breaks it, AND (b) the invariant beat *changes what it means* each instance (different withheld object, escalating stakes), not merely changes furniture. It is **harmful repetition (FLAG)** if EITHER: (a) the unbroken run ≥ **4**, OR (b) ≥ 2 consecutive instances are interchangeable (same withheld object + same stakes-tier + no plot advance between them).

Against this rule, b01 mid-book is a **borderline-to-flag**: the *meaning-accretion* test (b) PASSES (the withheld object evolves Wren→false-name→blank-column→Daven, stakes escalate c10<c14<c18), but the *run-length* test (a) FAILS at the c17–c18–c19 tail (run of 3 that, combined with c15's T-variant, reads as 4–5 lightly-broken). The accretion is real but the **circuit-breaker spacing is out of tolerance.** This is precisely the DEC-0124-style "design-inherent at most N=2 consecutive" logic from CLAUDE.md Rule 22 applied at the *structural* layer instead of the prose-register layer — and it's the check the pipeline currently lacks.

## 5. If sameness is real: the cheapest fix shape

It is real but it is *load-bearing*, so the fix is **circuit-breaker insertion + entry/exit variation**, NOT collapse or restructure. Collapsing T chapters would gut the accretion the c20 payoff depends on. Minimum intervention:

**Primary (do this): break the c17→c18→c19 terminal run.** This is the single highest-value, lowest-cost move. The run of three identical-shape transactions immediately before the c20 payoff is where the déjà vu does the most damage.
- **c18** is the natural break candidate — it's the "city-scale max-density deployment" climax and is *already* the most differentiated T instance (it opens every lane at once rather than running one desk transaction). **Push it further off-template at the scene-entry/exit:** open on the *watched court-as-one-machine* observation (bones @18–22 — the groom/maid/knight/septa) rather than on the cipher-note-at-the-desk, and close on the deployment's physical scale rather than on "stylus beside the closed ledger." This converts c18 from a T-member into a T-break by re-anchoring its frame, at the cost of a `/and-write revise` on entry/exit bones only — no substance-delta change. Result: c17 → **[break]** → c19, no unbroken run ≥ 2 at the tail.

**Secondary (cheap, high-leverage): vary the terminal tic.** The "lift the stylus / hand off the surface" close recurs literally in c08, c11, c12, c14, c15, c19. Direct `/and-stitch` (or a `/and-write revise` on the final bone) to give **at least 3 of these 6** a different chapter-exit gesture. This is a prose/final-bone touch, not a structural change, and it removes the most *audible* sameness signal a reader notices.

**Tertiary (optional): vary the entry of one mid-run T chapter.** c11 and c12 are the two purest, most-interchangeable transactions (c11: 5 packet-refs + lift-hand bookends; c12: full transaction + suppression coda). They sit adjacent. **Vary the scene-entry of c11** — open it on the cloth-merchant burning the paper (bones @9–13, already in the chapter) rather than on the courier taking the packet — so c11 and c12 don't open with the same packet-arrives beat back-to-back.

**Chapters to touch: c18 (primary — entry/exit re-anchor), c11 (tertiary — entry swap), and the terminal-tic varied across the c11/c14/c19 subset (secondary — final-bone/stitch touch).** Leave c13 and c16 alone (they are the working breaks). Do **not** touch the withhold-the-name invariant — that beat is the accretion engine and the c20 payoff's foundation.

**Estimated cost:** 1 `/and-write revise --from-signals` on c18 (entry/exit bones), 1 light revise or stitch-touch on c11, and a terminal-gesture pass — well inside a single `/and-cohere b01 c11-c19` structural iteration. Not a restructure.
