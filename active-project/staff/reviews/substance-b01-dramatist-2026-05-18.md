The refusal is load-bearing.

Taylor files the encounter closed: "prohibition held; function declined; position unchanged." The chapter ends on the refusal having held. The text explicitly names that she can see both answers clearly, has not been coerced, and the door is still open. She accepts on the second answer.

The two-chapter structure holds. The prohibition survives c04 intact, making c05's acceptance legible as a choice rather than inevitability. The reader sees both the moment the door was closed (c04: refusal stated and filed) and the moment it was reopened and walked through (c05: calculation returns, second answer chosen). This is the book's structural irony and the chapter pair supports it.

## Findings

**1. c09 scene count under-dense for a designated d07 hinge (SIGNAL, non-blocking).**

The charge specifies that c09 (d07 hinge) should carry 4-5 scenes per the climactic-density rule. c09 carries 3 scenes. d07 is the chapter where the arrangement is formalized — Taylor becomes Otto's unofficial instrument, the delivery cadence is regular and named, reversibility closes. The chunk text itself names this as a qualitative shift: before c09, the arrangement was something she entered for specific reasons and could in theory exit; after it, it is what she is. Three scenes is within the 3-5 range and is not a HARD-fail, but this chapter is doing hinge-level structural work and is under-dense for it. The "fluency is auditable" moment — the ledger running without pain as the signal that something has changed — deserves more room than a standard rising chapter. Address at `/and-substance chapter b01c09`: consider a fourth scene covering the aftermath accounting and the ledger's new fluency as its own beat, separate from the delivery scene.

**2. Aemond walk-on in c11 does not register an axis shift in substance_delta (SIGNAL, non-blocking).**

The cast roster specifies: "each appearance must shift a named plot axis." Aemond's c10 appearance shifts the knowledge axis (succession-pressure from inside the Green faction confirmed). His c11 appearance is referenced in the handoff as "active; succession-pressure registered" but c11's substance_delta records no axis move attributable to Aemond. His presence in c11 functions as background context for the sustained intelligence sequence that drives the contempt articulation, but the contempt is driven by Taylor's observation of the court elite in aggregate, not by Aemond's specific action. Address at `/and-substance chapter b01c11`: either assign Aemond a specific action in c11 that moves a named plot axis (knowledge or position are candidates), or reduce his c11 presence to a reference to the c10-registered signal rather than treating it as a new walk-on.

**3. d03 capability shift "systematic-reading-begun" is floating between c04 and c05 without a chapter claiming it in substance_delta (SIGNAL, non-blocking).**

The d03 trajectory declares three shifts: position, moral-framework, and capability (systematic-reading-begun). The moral-framework shift and acceptance land in c05 (correct for the split). c04's chunk prose states Taylor "begins reading the Flea Bottom political layer more systematically, which is the first step toward the surveillance architecture" — attributing the capability shift's initiation to c04. But c04's substance_delta records only position (+0.5) and knowledge (+0.5); it does not record a capability move. c05's substance_delta also does not record a capability move. The d03 capability shift is attributed to neither chapter in their respective substance_deltas. The book-level roll-up still passes because capability c02 already moved 3→4 and the roll-up's +4.0 total is met through other chapters. The issue is a labeling gap: at `/and-substance chapter b01c04` or `/and-substance chapter b01c05`, the d03 capability note should be explicitly claimed by one chapter's substance_delta with a magnitude, or the d03 capability language in series-trajectory.md should be reconciled against what the chapter actually delivers. This prevents a bone-gate ambiguity at `/and-write b01c04` or `/and-write b01c05`.
