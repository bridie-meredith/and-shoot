## CHECK 3 — COLD-READ-RISK

### COLD-READ-RISK @14 — HIGH CONFIDENCE — SOFT-BLOCK

**Span (draft lines 27–33):**
> The third pinned him to the stone.
>
> The side-alley returned the sound. The walls of the alley turned the noise of a body working against stone inward and then outward at the mouth, carrying past the threshold **below the register I would have called human** until the alley delivered it, and the feed had no field for what the alley delivered.
>
> The courier raised his spine.
>
> He found his feet under him and stood.

**(a) MISREAD VECTOR (the cold-reader's interpretation):**
A first-time reader, given the cluster of cues, will plausibly read this as a sexual assault rather than faction enforcement. The compounding cues:
1. "**The third pinned him to the stone**" (@13, FAIL #2-era pin-against-wall verb) — a single assailant holding a body against a wall, after the other two "**sealed**" the alley-mouth (line 25), is the staging vocabulary of a cornering/assault, not the staging of a beating.
2. "**a body working against stone**" — "working against" reads as struggle/grinding rather than blows landing.
3. "**below the register I would have called human**" — a non-human, sub-vocal sound emanating from a pinned body reads, to a cold-reader, as a sound of violation/distress of a kind the narrator recoils from naming; the deliberate not-naming amplifies the suggestive vacuum.
4. "**The courier raised his spine**" / "found his feet under him and stood" — recovering by getting up off the ground / straightening after being held against stone fits an assault-aftermath posture.

The narrator's clinical refusal to name the act (substance-correct as instrument-discipline) reads, to a cold-reader, as the narrator declining to name a sexual act — the not-naming *is* the misread accelerant.

**(b) SUBSTANCE-CORRECT READING (chunk + bones + facets):**
@13 is authorized as coordinated ENFORCEMENT violence directed at the courier — faction-enforcement, explicitly NOT robbery and NOT sexual (per the brief and s02 opposing_force: "coordinated, directed at a named body for a named reason; the content has named itself as faction-violence"). The two figures sealing the mouth + the third applying force = enforcement geometry (exit-blocking before contact). The sound at @14 is the dark-fantasy "gap-instrument" beat: the alley emits an effortful body-sound the insect-feed has no categorization field for; the sensory facet names it "courier-effortful-body-sound" and the interest-narrator facet names the read as "the alley returns a sound the feed has no field for; the gap is registered as gap, not as the body inside it." The substance is: the feed cannot file faction-violence the way it files foot-traffic, and the not-naming is Taylor's *instrument*-discipline failing to have a category — not narrator-squeamishness about a sexual act.

**(c) MISREAD CONFIDENCE: HIGH (would-likely-fire-at-Phase-9).**
Three of the four cue-words ("pinned... to the stone", "a body working against stone", "below the register I would have called human") independently push toward the assault reading, and they stack in four consecutive short paragraphs with no counter-signal disambiguating the violence-type *in the prose itself*. The substance-correct frame (enforcement, not assault) lives in the facets and the @0 preamble's general "faction" architecture, but NOTHING in the @13–@16 span tells a cold-reader "this is a beating / enforcement strike" rather than "this is a sexual assault." The "not robbery, the approach was wrong for robbery" line (line 25) explicitly rules OUT robbery while leaving the assault reading wide open — it narrows the misread toward, not away from, the dangerous interpretation. This is precisely the class of defect a cold-read gate fires on.

**(d) ROUTING RECOMMENDATION: bones-revise of the @13 verb (primary) + stitch-revise of the @14 prose (secondary).**
- **Primary — bones-revise @13:** The FAIL #2-era "PIN against stone" verb is the root cause. The pin/hold-against-wall geometry is what makes the whole cluster read as cornering. Recasting @13 to a clean directed blow — "the three figures STRIKE the courier" — removes the static hold-against-stone staging that the assault reading depends on. (NOTE: this is exactly the recast the CURRENT on-disk bones already carry — @13 now reads "the three figures strike the courier." This validates that the bones-layer fix is the correct routing: the recast at the bones layer is what dissolves the misread at its source. Phase 8.5 would have routed here and pre-empted the Phase 9 catch.)
- **Secondary — stitch-revise @14:** Even with a strike verb, the @14 prose "a body working against stone" + "below the register I would have called human" retains residual suggestiveness. Recommend the stitcher revise the @14 sound-rendering so the *source* of the sound reads as impact/blows-and-effort against a struck body rather than a body "working against stone" (which connotes a body pressed to/grinding against a wall). The sensory facet ("courier-effortful-body-sound") supports either rendering; the stitcher should choose surface that disambiguates toward the enforcement reading.
