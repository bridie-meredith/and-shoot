## STRUCT-031 (HARD) — Circuit-breaker declared in Rule 22 has no schema field or command-body enforcement

**Affected files:**
- CLAUDE.md Rule 22 (declares the circuit-breaker)
- `schemas/showrunner-memory.schema.md` (no tracking field)
- `.claude/commands/and-stitch.md` Phase 9 Step 4 (no auto-promotion logic)
- `.claude/commands/and-postop.md` (no dispositioned-verdict count tracking)

**Problem:**

CLAUDE.md Rule 22 declares:
> "a single defect class may be dispositioned 'design-inherent / accepted-caveat' at most N=2
> consecutive chapters; the (N+1)th auto-promotes NOTE → BLOCKING and forces a depth-pass or
> explicit principal escalation before further ships."

This is a binding production rule. It requires:
1. **A state field** tracking per-defect-class consecutive-design-inherent-disposition counts
   across chapters (e.g., `chapters[*].cold_read.design_inherent_dispositions[]` per
   defect-class label, or a book-level aggregate in `books[<slug>].disposition_streak` map).
2. **A check at the disposition site** (Phase 9 Step 4 in and-stitch.md, or and-postop.md
   verdict record) that reads the streak count and fires the auto-promotion when N+1 is reached.

Neither exists. The only reference to N=2 in command bodies is in and-stitch.md Phase 9
FOLLOW-FAIL (line 886: "consecutive-capped at N=2 even where it is available — see Step 4 /
PROP-0048"), but:
- PROP-0048 is referenced but never defined in any command body or proposal record.
- The reference is specific to FOLLOW-FAIL, not the general defect-class circuit-breaker.
- "See Step 4" implies Step 4 implements it, but Step 4 (lines 918–960) has no streak
  tracking or auto-promotion logic.

**Impact:** The root cause of the b01 airless cohort was 16 consecutive "design-inherent"
dispositions of the same defect class with no circuit breaker. CLAUDE.md Rule 22 was written
to prevent recurrence. Without state tracking and auto-promotion, a post-b01 chapter stream
that re-learns the b01 pattern will not trigger the breaker.

**Severity:** HARD — a rule with no enforcement mechanism is not a rule.

**Owning agents:** and-stitch.md Phase 9 (enforcement site), showrunner-memory.schema.md
(state field), screen-writer (if /and-substance or bones authoring is the disposition site).

**Parking-lot item:** pl-2026-06-11-001 (target: `/and-stitch`, scope: `*`, severity: HARD)
