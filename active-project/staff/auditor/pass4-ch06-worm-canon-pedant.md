The chapter does not assert the cost or skip to it — it builds it. This is the chapter's strongest mechanical accomplishment.

The bones show parallel control doing what parallel control should do: she manages discrete channels independently, releases when objective is achieved, and does not spend load on resolved problems. No overreach asserted.

**Cost terminal.** Taylor drops after she releases the second group (line 103), not before. She completes the operation. The drop is the cost arriving after the function is done. This is canonical Taylor's operational discipline rendered in the bones correctly.

**Voice register (structural).** The bones show Taylor in control of sequence throughout: she instructs, she positions, she releases. Even after the nosebleed she is still issuing active directives. The voice register that will emerge in prose is structurally supported.

**Document sequence.** The writing sequence (W7–W10) shows institutional procedure done with care: case → parchment → exchange → dip → write → pause → write closing → lift → blow → fold → seal → tuck. The prop custody lands in Rowan's possession correctly.

### What the prose pass must resolve

**Wardship category (W8) — DEMAND.** The bones have "ward entry," which is category-neutral. The STM carried this flag from the episode plan review. At prose level, the document must use the correct Westerosi institutional category — ward-of-administration or the period-appropriate equivalent. This cannot be left to generic "ward" language. If the prose pass uses personal wardship language instead, that is a lore flag.

**Raven-lift ambiguity (W3).** The ravens lifting when Taylor exits into the yard (line 11) is either an active release or an ambient startle. The e06 shoot established the raven-as-primary-instrument baseline. The prose pass must resolve this explicitly — either she releases them as she exits (active, costs nothing at this stage, sets up the vantage sequence) or they startle (and are then called back or gathered before the vantage sequence). The bones permit both; the prose must commit.

**Scene break transition (W12).** The transition from Taylor watching Rowan depart (line 53) to Taylor exiting the sept yard (line 108) is the chapter's least-supported transition in the bones. The prose pass must make the repositioning legible without summary. The transit bones (108–109) are present; the prose needs to make the time compression intentional rather than invisible.
