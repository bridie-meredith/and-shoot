## Findings

### What works

Chapter 09 has the complication-density structure this persona demands and voted for. Two competing claims arrive simultaneously (W4), escalate through a three-way argument (W6–W7), reach adjudication (W9–W11), and are immediately superseded by a third authority (W12–W15). The board gets worse, then worse again, then worse a third time at a different entry point. The escalation is structural, not tonal — each new party's arrival changes what the previous resolution meant.

Taylor's surveillance posture (two birds repositioned mid-scene at W8, third animal dispatched at W14) is the correct POV mechanism — she is not passively watching, she is actively managing her observation assets under a legibility constraint. That constraint (she cannot respond visibly to what her feed detects without revealing she is watching) is live throughout and generates micro-escalations inside the larger contest.

The chapter ends at a board state that is genuinely worse than the chapter opened: whatever the castellan decided, a royal authority has now taken control of both documents, and Taylor knows what is in them from having watched through her birds. That is the chapter's payoff and it lands.

### Weak points

**W9 and W11** are the chapter's soft spots. W9 (adjudication comparison) decelerates the board without adding a new complication. W11 (ruling delivered, knights exit) is the payoff of a five-window argument but the SVO bones do not capture the resolution's weight — both men exiting in sequence reads as administrative rather than dramatic. These are the windows most at risk of going flat in prose.

**Prose-pass flag on W11:** The bone layer needs at minimum one physical reaction from the losing party before the exit sequence. A Bracken SVO that registers the ruling — even one line — would upgrade W11 from TOLERATED to ENGAGED.

### Hard gate status

- Gate 1 (SVO comprehensibility): PASS. Three-party document contest legible from bones. Taylor's bird deployment and withdrawal clearly mark chapter structure.
- Gate 2 (no consecutive TOLERATED pair): PASS. W9, W11, and W16 each isolated by ENGAGED windows on both sides.
- Gate 3 (zero BORED): PASS.

### Overall

Chapter 09 is the strongest complication-density chapter in the season by structure. The three-party escalation with Taylor as observer rather than participant is exactly the board-geometry this persona voted for at the series planning stage. Prose pass should protect W11's payoff and W9's adjudication — those are where flat prose could lose what the SVO architecture earns.
