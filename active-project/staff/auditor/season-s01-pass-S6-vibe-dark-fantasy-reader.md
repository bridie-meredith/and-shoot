## Vibe-Cloud Summary (season s01)

The season operates on these vibe registers:
- World in motion before Taylor: impressment, castellan consolidation, septon dying — none wait for her
- Administrative machinery as grinding force: the ledger, the record, the stamp — impersonal and automatic
- Power with physical cost: fauna control punishes her body (nosebleeds, temples, physical collapse)
- No comfort, no catharsis: being right does not protect; being effective is what exposes her
- Planetos-specific wrongness: animal behavior that disturbs because it is against nature, not because it is useful
- Fast, pulpy, escalating: each chapter must worsen the board; stalemate is the failure register

## Per-Chapter Verdicts

### Chapter 01
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

The census machinery arrives on its own schedule — the riders crest, the officer dismounts, the scroll produces itself. The septon rises and falls. The notation is made regardless of what Taylor says or does. The chapter's kinetic register is correct: arrival → confrontation → notation → departure, no pause for Taylor's needs. The ravens operating as ambient wrongness (they flush before the riders crest; they resettle after departure) grounds the chapter in Planetos-specific unease without naming it. The dying septon as structural failure — a protection already collapsing before the threat arrives — is the correct dark-fantasy register. The machine does not accommodate him; it refuses his attestation as insufficient and moves on.

### Chapter 02
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

This is the vibe-cloud chapter: behavioral coherence detected before anyone can name a cause. Plumm's man notices the rat-cleared grain shed, the ravens roosting against pattern, the girl present at three anomalous locations — not because he is looking for Taylor, but because he is doing his job. The wrongness accumulates in his ledger through notations, not through comprehension. The chapter interleaves Taylor's POV (cost: nosebleed, pinched nose, temples pressed) with Plumm's man's documentary pass — the structure itself makes the point that she cannot see what he is recording. The ledger closes before she knows it has opened. Power-as-uncertain-force is present: the nosebleed triggers at the blood-lip beats, the release is a relief, not a victory. The world records her without her knowledge. Correct register throughout.

### Chapter 03
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

The shortest chapter (47 proto-lines) and the most compressed in its register. The raven disorientation beats — lift, lift, press temples, hold hands, blood at lip, hold chin, hold eyes — accumulate without explanation. Something has closed upstream and she cannot reach what caused it. The chapter is almost entirely interior-cost: she cannot locate the disturbance, she uses the fauna to probe and the bird drops on return. The world is not responding to her; she is responding to something the world did that she missed. This is the correct Planetos register for foreknowledge's inverse: knowing something happened, not knowing what. The brief chapter structure is appropriate — she is not acting here, she is absorbing. No vibe drift detected.

### Chapter 04
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

The witnessed anomaly chapter. The raven descends and perches on Taylor's arm in a posture no trained bird takes with a stranger — concrete physical wrongness, not labeled. The castellan's reaction is the critical vibe test: `oc-castellan-harrenhal steps back` (line 56), `oc-castellan-harrenhal faces the raven` / `the raven swings the head` / `oc-castellan-harrenhal retreats` (lines 62–64). This is discomfort and retreat, not curiosity or clinical interest. Plumm `grips the stylus` and `marks the page` — he is documenting, not analyzing. The chapter's goal statement explicitly cites the dark-fantasy-reader requirement (discomfort and unease, not curiosity or wonder), and the proto-lines honor it. The paperwork machinery continues through the anomaly: the ledger is marked, the page rolled, the riders depart. The world did not pause. Vibe holds.

### Chapter 05 (Interlude — septon-rowan POV)
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

The interlude registers as dark-fantasy-aligned through its mechanism: Rowan's good-faith attempt to intercede broadens Taylor's documented exposure rather than narrowing it. His name goes into Plumm's record book at line 48 (`ser-harwick-plumm writes the name`). The bureaucratic exchange — Plumm drawing the record book, opening it, numbering the entry, writing the sept entry, pocketing it — is the correct machinery register. Rowan speaks, argues, tries again; none of it matters because Plumm's record already has what it needs. The chapter ends with Rowan stopping, turning, then going to find Taylor with news that is not good. The Planetos register holds: correct action does not protect you; it marks you. The interlude does not soften the vibe — it documents how the net tightened from a perspective that didn't know it was helping the net.

### Chapter 06
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

The succession-clock chapter opens on the septon's death (line 2: `the breathing stops`) without framing it as loss — it is the trigger that starts the administrative clock. Taylor moves immediately: she crosses to Rowan, they draft the claim document, she deploys the raven network to slow the courier. This chapter has the highest kinetic register in the season: courier disruption, two raven groups, nosebleed mid-operation, the second courier requiring a second deployment. The physical cost is live throughout — nosebleed at line 82, the release of the second group followed immediately by `taylor-hebert-westeros drops` and `taylor-hebert-westeros presses the hands`. The chapter earns its action by making the cost explicit during the action, not deferred to afterward. Vibe-cloud requirement: fast, pulpy, physical cost present in real time. Satisfied. The ravens do not function as a reliable tool; Taylor is operating at capacity and the chapter shows it.

The refusal chapter. The recorder stamps the refusal entry (line 10) before Rowan has finished speaking. Plumm enters and files immediately (lines 17–27). The counter-claim is already logged before Taylor and Rowan clear the gate. The chapter's structure enacts the machinery: refusal → cross-reference entry → Taylor checked against the duty roll → exit. The emotional register is correct Planetos dark-fantasy: Taylor in the cottage with the document (lines 62–64: `taylor-hebert-westeros flips the document / taylor-hebert-westeros sets the document down`), the raven launching into an empty gesture (line 68), the gripping of the wall (line 71), the retreat into the sept (lines 87–92). No catharsis — she kneels and rises and the document is still on Plumm's counter. The chapter does not let the defeat arrive with drama; it arrives with a stamp.

### Chapter 09
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

The contested-capture chapter. Taylor is the object being disputed; she watches through fauna. Plumm and Bracken argue over her in the gatehouse while she repositions the raven on the sill (lines 34–36) — present in the room through a bird, not in person, which is the correct register: surveillance without participation, agency without standing. The castellan covers Bracken's page before Plumm can read it (line 32). No one acknowledges Taylor; the document is what matters. Celtigar's arrival (lines 67–76) is institutional escalation on its own schedule — he arrives from a separate logic that did not originate in this dispute. The chapter closes with Taylor withdrawing the fly, pressing the palms, and the ravens circling the central tower: passive fauna action, not a meaningful response to what just happened. Vibe holds throughout. The world does not organize around her; it processes its own business while she watches through borrowed eyes.

### Chapter 10
**VIBE-ALIGNED**

The season close. The ward-of-administration entry is written in the record (line 24: `ser-harwick-plumm writes the ward-of-administration entry`). The chapter uses ward-of-administration language throughout, not personal-ward language. Taylor speaks twice (lines 18, 34) and holds the chin and holds the feet — the physical tells of controlled stillness under pressure. Celtigar exits without speaking to her directly (line 30). The hall empties: Celtigar out, castellan out, Plumm out, Rowan out, Taylor alone with a sealed scroll on a counter she did not choose. The final image — `taylor-hebert-westeros exhales` — is not catharsis; it is the recognition that the mechanism has completed. The season's opening question (can she remain invisible?) is answered with finality: no, and the mechanism that removed her invisibility is the same one that consumed her. The ending does not offer a re-formed position. It offers a function label she did not write. Correct Planetos dark-fantasy register.

## Aggregate Verdict

**Chapters aligned:** 10/10
**Chapters drifting:** 0/10

**ACCEPT**

All ten chapters sit inside the season vibe-cloud. The dark-fantasy-reader's specific requirements are satisfied consistently:

- Planetos-specific wrongness: present in every chapter with fauna (ravens flush, perch wrong, drop on return; Plumm's man records behavioral anomaly; castellan retreats)
- Power as uncertain force with physical cost: nosebleeds, temples-pressing, physical collapse under sustained deployment — present in chapters 02, 03, 06, and implicit in 07–10 through body-still discipline
- Machinery that does not pause: the ledger, the stamp, the roll, the entry — administrative process is automatic throughout; no beat where the system waits for Taylor
- World in motion before Taylor: chapter 01 establishes this and all subsequent chapters maintain it; Celtigar's arrival in chapter 09 is the clearest test — an institutional logic that never knew Taylor existed arrives and resolves the dispute she was trying to survive
- No false catharsis: the closest thing to a win is chapter 06's courier disruption — paid for immediately with a nosebleed, a physical drop, and hands pressed to the floor; the delay it buys is not acknowledged as a win anywhere in chapter 07
- Enemies who don't pause: Plumm files within the same window Rowan's claim is refused; the recorder stamps the refusal before the conversation ends

Aggregate: **ACCEPT (10/10 — exceeds 12/14 threshold at 100%)**
