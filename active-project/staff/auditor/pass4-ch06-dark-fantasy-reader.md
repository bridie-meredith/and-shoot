## Findings

**F1 — Comprehensibility flag: W4 (lines 15–19)**
The choice to go to Rowan rather than back inside is made in the blank line 14 and is invisible from the bones. More critically, "Taylor speaks to Rowan" in line 19 is the initiating ask for the document, but the SVO gives no signal of what she is requesting. Prose rendering must supply the ask within the first exchange or the scene's purpose is opaque until Rowan picks up the stylus (line 30).

**F2 — Comprehensibility flag: W10 (line 114 — "Rowan tucks the parchment")**
The tucking location is load-bearing for document custody. The bones are ambiguous on where the parchment goes. Prose rendering must make this specific and must make clear whether Taylor registers who holds it and whether that satisfies or concerns her.

**F3 — Consecutive TOLERATED violation: W8–W9 (lines 36–45)**
The four write-lines (36–39) followed by the closing-line write (41) with speak exchanges on either side creates a two-window transit stretch where the SVO is transcription and negotiation-aftermath. Both windows are tolerated on established stakes. Gate 2 is technically not violated (only two consecutive, not three), but this is the weakest zone in the chapter. Prose rendering must supply texture — what Taylor hears in the scratching of the stylus, what she reads in Rowan's posture as he writes — or this zone will be the place readers drift.

**F4 — Strong positive: The raven operation (W13–W21)**
The nine-window operational sequence is the strongest sustained run in the chapter. The persona's specific tests — power as uncertain force, enemies who adapt and don't stop, cost arriving during not after the action, world moving on its own logic — are all satisfied. The nosebleed mid-operation (W17), the second courier's arrival before the first operation resolves (W18), the question of whether the gate-speech constituted message-delivery (W20), and the dropped ending (W21) are all correct register. No false catharsis. The persona does not feel manipulated into caring; the stakes are structural.

**F5 — Document-scene register (W5, W7, W11)**
The three windows where Rowan's body language is the primary signal (steps back, takes stylus, takes cloak and goes) land correctly. A man who knows the risk and acts anyway without being heroic about it — who simply picks up the instrument and moves — is the right Planetos flavor. These windows counterbalance the inert write-sequence in W8–W9.

**F6 — Opening (W1–W3)**
The death sequence and its immediate aftermath are the chapter's second-strongest zone. The candle flame straightening, the raven lift, and the double-facing are all correct unlabeled Planetos texture. No grief beat, no pause for Taylor to process. The world does not accommodate her loss. This persona accepts that as earned bleakness, not coldness.
