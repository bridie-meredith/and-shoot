## Method note

Windows are approximately 10 active bones each, counted by bone number sequence. Gaps in the number sequence (bones 24, 35, 47, etc.) are structural separators and do not count against the window. Verdict per window: ENGAGED / TOLERATED / BORED. Sustained-BORED trigger: two consecutive BORED → REVISE flag. Sustained-TOLERATED trigger: three consecutive TOLERATED → REVISE flag. Cap rule: TOLERATED may not exceed 10% of total windows.

Total active bones: approximately 480. Approximate window count: 48 windows.
TOLERATED cap: ≤4 windows (≤10% of 48). Zero sustained BORED allowed.

Hot-buttons applied hard:
- Hope without cost
- Enemies who pause at convenient moments
- Magic as reliable tool
- Violence without aftermath
- World as set-dressing
- Wrong-register Westerosi shock
- Catharsis before weight

Fatigue watch: action sequences with fake stakes, environmental description when setting stops feeling hostile.

## REVISE flags — enumerated

### REVISE-1: Sustained BORED (windows 10–11)
Bones approximately 108–135. The maester's first two presences in Taylor's range are absorbed by the logging refrain without friction. Window 10: maester speaks to the room, beetles relay, Taylor writes. Window 11: maester crosses, speaks, laughs (506), Taylor straightens and immediately logs. The most critical early-introduction of the season's key variable (the broken maester) is being processed as ambient data rather than as an anomaly. The maester needs a bone where he has not been written yet.

### REVISE-2: TOLERATED cap breach (12/34 windows = 35.3%, cap is 10%)
The TOLERATED windows cluster around three recurring patterns:
- **Log-refrain density:** open/write/close fires as a frame-closure device so frequently that it loses meaning. In the first 135 bones, the refrain appears approximately 8 times. By window 17, it is a rhythm the reader has learned to skip. Recommendation: reduce log-refrain instances in low-event windows; reserve the full triple (open/write/close) for windows where the log entry carries specific narrative weight (the maester's naming, the two-apparatuses-simultaneously entry, the first range expansion). In transit or ambient windows, single "taylor writes the entry" without framing triple is sufficient.
- **Multi-species spread bone clusters:** flies/wasps/beetles/spiders deploying to assigned zones appears with near-identical syntax in windows 09, 16, 20, 25, and 31. The range-expansion spine depends on these sequences but they have not been differentiated. Recommendation: each expansion-event spread should contain at minimum one bone specific to the new geography (e.g., "the beetles reach the apothecary ground floor" as a distinct event bone, not a list item). The expansion that crosses a milestone is not the same as the overnight maintenance spread.
- **Taylor exhales / taylor writes back-to-back:** the exhale bone is used as a soft period in 8+ windows and is frequently followed by a write or log sequence. The pairing is now predictable. Recommendation: introduce variant closes (a posture bone, a spatial movement, a relay bone that is not an insect) in windows where exhale has already appeared.

### REVISE-3: Denouement structural failure (window 34)
Bones 472–494. The closing perimeter walk and two-log-entry beat are formally identical to mid-season perimeter sequences. The content beat (beat 26) is the reader-asymmetry culmination: "the architecture has changed; she knows that; she does not know what file she is in." This requires at minimum one insert-numbered bone that marks the moment differently — a posture, a stop, a specific location reference that is not a traverse verb. The double-write (bones 492–493) must be visually or structurally distinguished from every prior consecutive-write occurrence. If these are the season's final bones, they must not read as routine.

### REVISE-4: Westerosi superstition register — not flagged as a hard problem but watch-listed
No specific hot-button breach detected at bone level; the bones do not render dialogue or emotional response. However: the "wrong-register shock" risk is highest at the dock-runner exchange (windows 12–13), the clerk sequences (windows 06–07, 25–27), and any bone where Taylor responds to the maester.
