The season's administrative machinery (census, ward-record, claim ledger, assessment roll) is legible throughout. A reader who pauses between chapters may not connect Taylor's cost-display (nosebleed, temple-press, raven-drop) to the riders arriving in Chapter 04. The dependency flag above covers this.
