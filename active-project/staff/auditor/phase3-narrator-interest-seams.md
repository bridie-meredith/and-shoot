## 3. Curve-Level Seam

**STRONG.**

The Phase 2 file claims eight channels exercised and full doubled-register visibility. Both claims carry more fragility than the Phase 2 audit surfaced.

On channel diversity: cost-tracking fires on 7 of 18 entries (38.9%). Eyes-to-exits fires on 4 entries, but in 3 of those 4 cases it is co-cited with cost-tracking (@23, @57, @60). The two channels are effectively a composite across most of the file. When the composite is counted as one channel rather than two independent channels, actual independent channel coverage drops from eight to six (passive fauna-feed, cost-tracking/eyes-to-exits composite, pre-calc, refusal-to-look, age-mismatch, foreknowledge-clamp/mask-thin). Six channels still clears the rubric's three-channel floor, but the claim of eight independent channels overstates the diversity, and it means the file's behavior-pack coverage is less varied than the writer's Pass 2 audit presents.

On doubled-register visibility: the foreknowledge-clamp register fires on only two entries (@63, @64), both in the last quarter of the episode, and @63 is already a revise-target (form failure). The @48 SKIP-MISSED (flagged in Phase 2) means the foreknowledge-clamp register is absent from the middle two-thirds of the episode. The writer claims doubled-register visibility is achieved because all three register-modes appear somewhere in the file. But if foreknowledge-clamp is compressed to two adjacent entries in the final quarter — with the stronger early-episode clamp (@48, "provisional") absent — the register is not distributed across the episode but concentrated at the end. The curve-shape rubric requires the doubled-register to be visible across the episode, not merely present at the closing cluster. The file's doubled-register distribution is back-loaded in a way the Phase 2 shape verdict (SHAPE-OK) did not flag as a risk.

These two weaknesses (channel-composite overstating diversity; foreknowledge-clamp back-loaded due to @48 absence) compound: the channel most capable of distributing the doubled register across the episode's middle section (foreknowledge-clamp at @48) is the one that is missing, and the channel that dominates the middle section (cost-tracking/eyes-to-exits composite) does not carry doubled-register content.
