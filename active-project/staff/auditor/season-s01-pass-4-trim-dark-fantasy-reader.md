## FILE-LEVEL VERDICT

REVISE-early-baseline-domestic-weight-exceeds-tension-yield

**Reason:** The season aggregate delivers strongly through the census and ignition beats (IDs ~329–518), the Mira interlude (~565–641), and the maester visit (~802–912). But the early-baseline domestic stretch (IDs 1–149, roughly 15% of the aggregate) accumulates insect-texture and household routine at a rate that outpaces its tension yield for dark-fantasy-reader taste. Multiple windows in that stretch register TOLERATED, and the run 31–80 produces two consecutive TOLERATED windows before the swarm-sense pattern solidifies enough to pull forward. The parents-act-in-concert interlude (IDs 700–800) has a similar problem: the approach to Rowan is correctly weighted, but the subsequent movement sequence (730–747) marks time through a transit without friction. These are addressable with targeted deletions and the new-beat insertions already flagged — they do not require structural revision. The deletion candidates are catalogued below.
