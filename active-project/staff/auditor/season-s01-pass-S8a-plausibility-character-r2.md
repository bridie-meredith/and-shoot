## Re-evaluation: IDs 738–746 — oc-craftsman-mother reeve visit

### What ID 934 establishes

ID 934 ("the town reeve faces oc-craftsman-father") is inserted at the census dock scene (between IDs 354-355), establishing that the town reeve has noted the Ashford family during official census duty. Prior acquaintance between the reeve and the Ashford household is now documented.

### Why this does not resolve the r1 finding

The r1 finding was not about whether Elara knows the reeve. In a market town of Fairstead's scale, mutual recognition between the reeve's household and a craftsman's household is assumed. Clem Ferris (oc-child-peer) is the reeve's son; social contact exists.

The r1 finding was about Elara's risk calculus when choosing information channels. The town reeve is active in every institutional transaction in Fairstead: he approaches the maester (IDs 805-812), gestures to the maester (ID 824), passes the maester the folio (ID 923), receives the post rider's letter (IDs 545-548). He is not a informal social contact insulated from Pryor's monitoring network — he is the local administrative function that sits directly within it.

ID 934 shows the reeve noting the Ashford family at a Pryor-run census event. This confirms the reeve is within Pryor's institutional orbit, not outside it. Prior acquaintance established under a Pryor-adjacent institutional event does not convert the reeve into a safe informal channel for Elara. If anything, the census-dock framing of ID 934 reinforces that the reeve is a monitored-network node.

Elara's card: "someone making a formal note or record about Taylor → she becomes very still and tracks every subsequent interaction with that person." The reeve is within the category of persons whose interactions Elara would track, not approach.

### What would resolve it

The r1 routing offered two valid paths:
- **Path A (dialogue layer):** The shoot-phase content establishes Elara's visit as covert information-gathering — she goes to hear what the reeve knows, and manages the visit so that she reveals nothing and leaves. This requires the impersonator/dialogue layer to carry the full register explicitly: Elara initiating for intelligence, not for help. Not bone-level work; shoot-phase content-pass criterion.
- **Path B (fixer):** Reroute IDs 738-746 to a market-wife contact — a channel Elara controls and that sits outside Pryor's institutional orbit.
