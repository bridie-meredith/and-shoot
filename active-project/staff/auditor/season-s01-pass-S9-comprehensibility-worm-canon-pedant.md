The aggregate is structurally canon-honest on the following points: passive-sense is correctly rendered as zero-cost ambient awareness across all Taylor-POV beats; the involuntary-swarm distinction is structurally set up; the POV transitions (Mira-interlude, Elara-interlude) honor the swarm-sense zeroing; the Shard behavioral weight is not improperly externalized anywhere; Taylor's mid-canon failure modes (self-isolation, control-as-substitute-for-trust) are structurally present in the family-management beats and the Mira-alley architecture.

The comprehensibility risk is concentrated in two places: (1) the IGNITION trigger chain, where the proto-line beat ordering creates ambiguity about whether the involuntary swarm has a sufficient stimulus at the moment it rises, and (2) the ID 906 folio-beat clustering with swarm-sense beats, which risks implying a perception Taylor does not have. Both are shoot-phase risks, not structural revision demands — but both are load-bearing for the Worm reader tracking the cost-mechanic, and both require explicit prose handling. The verdict is COMPREHENSIBILITY-RISK-POWER-MECHANIC-OPACITY because the mechanic's trigger and the mechanic's cost ceiling are both under-legible at the aggregate level in the IGNITION window.
