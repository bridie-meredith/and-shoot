## Specific feedback (REVISE)

**Beat 9 — family visit, city arrival**

The beat's structural job is to show the village-claim follows Taylor to King's Landing and the transactional surface doesn't close with distance. That job is real and necessary. But the emotional content — mother's face working through something unresolved, father counting tells again — is the same register as beats 3 and 4, delivered to the same two people, producing the same non-transaction. Beat 9 needs to pay a cost that beats 3 and 4 have not already paid. Options: the father registers something new (a behavioral tell from the Flea Bottom environment that the village frame doesn't have a category for — not the old tells, but a new one that unnerves him in a different direction), or the mother catches something from the way Taylor holds herself among strangers that shifts her grief into something sharper than the vigil-grief of beats 3 and 4. The visit should end with the transactional surface confirmed open AND with something the family has learned that they didn't have when they walked in.

**Beat 17 — mother arrives alone**

The withheld-touch gesture (mother reaches toward the girl's hand and stops) has already been paid in beat 3. Paying it again is not accumulation — it's repetition. The beat's correct structural work is to show the claim persists independent of mechanism, and the informal visit without bundle or trade goods is the right vehicle. But the emotional content needs different currency. The long silence, the cold-months question, the no — all of that is correct. What the beat cannot use again is the paused hand. Replace with something that shows what the mother has done with the eighteen beats of unresolved grief since beat 3 — what has she tried, what has she stopped trying, what is she doing here that she was not doing in beat 3.

**Beat 19 — range crosses 500m**

Delete or rewrite the sentence: "The range arithmetic is internally consistent; the 300m-to-500m expansion over approximately eight months of local reseed is within the shard's established recovery curve." This is author-facing assurance that the math checks out, not reader-facing content. The shard's credibility is already carried by the physical specifics — the geographic perimeter, the dates, the physiological cost per extension. Asserting internal consistency is what you do when you're worried the reader won't believe the specifics; trust the specifics. The sentence should be cut; the beat reads cleaner without it.

**Beat 21 — network position upgrade**

The beat shows Taylor's position changing from "placed by the elder" to "routed to by the network" — a real status shift. The problem is that within this beat, the exchange has no visible cost: she accepts, completes, gets paid, status upgrades. The Hightower apparatus is already building a file on her and her elevated network visibility is the surface that file will eventually key on, but that consequence is not present in beat 21. The beat needs to show either (a) a specific cost of completing the task — something she expends, risks, or exposes — or (b) a signal within the beat that the upgraded visibility has already reached someone who should not have it. The network making the change itself is the right observation; the beat should also show what the change costs in the same breath.

## What this draft gets right (load-bearing, do not revise)

- The Hightower apparatus is moving on its own schedule across beats 5, 18, 20, 23, 25. It does not pause. The file is opened, built, and miscategorized before Taylor understands what she's being filed under. The enemy learns.
- The shard's range expansion is metered, physiologically costly, and treated as data rather than triumph throughout. Beat 11's expansion-as-threshold-crossed and beat 24's "the log does not record the distance to the Red Keep" are particularly correct.
- Beat 12 (the eviction) with its forward-placed erasure announcement is the strongest single beat in the draft. Do not touch it.
- The broken maester's arc (beats 15, 16, 22) is correctly sequenced: ambient data → named subject → actively sharpening. Beat 22's six-hours-past-his-stop notation is the best version of "enemy who learns" in this draft.
- The season closes without resolution. Beat 25 is correct: four open bills, no connection drawn between them, two things that are not the same (knowing she is recorded vs. knowing the file exists).
