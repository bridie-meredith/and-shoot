## Specific feedback

- Beat 25 / terminal beat: the peak and the handoff are fused into one beat. The structural fact that both arcs have now closed around Taylor — the foreclosure of working unnamed — needs at minimum one beat after beat 25 where Taylor holds the new state. Not a summary beat; a scene beat. Taylor in the Flea Bottom base, or walking the perimeter, running the arithmetic in her log: what she now knows she is inside, what she cannot now undo, the gap between "I know I am being recorded" and "I do not know what file I am in." The beat should close on the open surfaces — the village-claim in the lord's-man record, the Hightower file dormant and miscategorized — without resolving them, but registering that Taylor knows the architecture has changed even if she cannot read the file. This is the falling stretch the season requires to have dramatic shape. The current beat 25 can carry the peak; beat 26 (or a restructured beat 25 split into two) carries the fall.
