## Attempt 2 (draft 2, 26 beats)

Structural check, draft 2.

**Season buildup: beats 1–24.** The arc builds cleanly: tanner-family closing around Taylor (1, 3, 4, 9, 13, 17, 23), range expansions as self-observed capability log (2, 7, 11, 14, 19, 24), Hightower/Hand's apparatus accreting toward Taylor (5, 18, 20, 25), maester as ambient variable (7, 15, 16, 22), network integration (6, 8, 10, 12, 21).

**Season climax: beat 25.** The Hand's apparatus sends a senior operative's formal written request through the elder — the most consequential institutional action yet, completing a file Taylor cannot see, under a framework that misreads her. This is the peak: she now knows she is being formally recorded by an apparatus she cannot identify, cannot access, and cannot correct. Beat 23 is the secondary close (village-claim externalized into lord's-man record). The peak lands in the correct structural position — back third, with both arcs now committed.

**Season denouement: beat 26.** Physical, located, doing something. Taylor walks the perimeter — a specific two-hour action in a specific geography (every alley, Fish Gate margin, south-wall beetle colony). She returns to base and writes two log entries side by side. The beat carries the falling stretch: the new state is registered, inventoried, and held without resolution. The open surfaces are named — village-claim formal, Hand's file dormant and miscategorized, maester pen-scratch audible, dock-side cluster thin. The season does not resolve these. It closes on Taylor knowing the architecture has changed while not knowing what file she is in.

**Denouement share: 1/26 = 3.8%.** This is low. The question is whether one beat is structurally sufficient falling stretch for a 26-beat season.

Assessment: yes, for this particular arc shape it is sufficient. The reason is that beat 26 is not a summary beat — it is a scene beat with physical duration, specific action, and the full inventory of open states delivered through the log entries. The falling stretch does not need to be long because the peak (beat 25) does not produce an explosive release; it produces a quiet, asymmetric closure — Taylor knows she is recorded, does not know what file. The fall does not need to decompress a violent climax; it needs to register the new architecture. Beat 26 does exactly that, in the correct scene-beat format (walking, writing, observing). A second denouement beat would risk over-explaining what the log entries already hold cleanly.

**Two-arc test.** Beat 26 explicitly preserves the independence of the two arcs: the lord's man holding the wage-claim record and the Hand's apparatus holding the file have no knowledge of each other. The beat reinforces rather than weakens the independence — Taylor herself documents the institutional separation. The two arcs closing around her are not converging into one story; they are two separate institutional tracks that happen to have Taylor at their intersection. This is the correct structural commitment and beat 26 confirms it without collapsing it.

**Inert-stretch check.** No three-beat consecutive run without escalation, meaningful character action, or new information. Clean.

**Try-fail integrity.** Partial payment at beat 13 formalizes rather than discharges the claim — load-bearing. Dock-side cluster burn-down at beat 21 costs her a passive observation surface for weeks — load-bearing. Clean.

**Antagonist agency.** The tanner-father operates through customary law, not emotion; he externalizes the claim in beat 23 through institutional registration, not confrontation. The Hand's apparatus operates through its own intelligence logic — clerks building population registers, a senior operative sending a formal written request — all independent of Taylor's behavior toward them. Clean.

**POV.** Single throughout. No fragmentation. Clean.

**Stakes-trajectory.** Rising continuously from beat 1 through beat 25. Beat 26 does not spike or collapse — it settles. This is the correct stakes shape for a denouement beat. Clean.

**Payoff fit.** The season set up two open recording events (village-claim, Hand's file) and closed both in the back third (beats 23, 25). Beat 26 inventories both without discharging either. The payoff discharges the climax beats, not different stakes. Clean.

## Verdict

CLEAN

**Strongest route:** beats 1–24 accumulate through behavioral isolation, range expansion, and institutional accrual. The peak arrives correctly at beat 25 with the Hand's formal written request — the most consequential institutional move, operating above Taylor's visibility. Beat 26 is a genuine scene-beat denouement: physically located, durational, carrying the open-surfaces inventory without resolving them. The two-arc independence is preserved through beat 26 and confirmed by Taylor's own log notation. Shape is rise-peak-fall. No inert stretches. No POV fragmentation.
