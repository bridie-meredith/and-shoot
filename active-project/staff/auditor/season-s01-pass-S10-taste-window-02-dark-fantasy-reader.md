No beat in the rise resolves the previous beat's weight. Cumulative.

Peak: IDs 266–313 (beats 14–16).

- Beat 15: visitor at maester's door — incomplete acoustic data, threat-shaped but unidentified. Stakes escalate: the maester is not safe ambient data.

Beat 16 is the right structural peak for a window about information accumulation: the moment when accumulation becomes naming, and naming becomes surveillance of a specific person rather than environmental cataloging. The maester's pen-scratch is now an event Taylor tracks, not noise she overhears.

Fall: IDs 315–328 (beat 17).
- Mother arrives alone, states the vigil is over, exits.

The fall is short — one beat, twelve bones. The dramatic question for the fall: does it earn its weight? The vigil's ending is not catharsis — the mother does not weep, Taylor does not feel relieved, the claim does not close. The fall works because the weight is changed, not resolved. The personal dimension of the village-claim has changed form. The institutional dimension is intact and about to harden further (beat 23). A fall that feels like completion would be unearned catharsis. This fall feels like a phase shift in an ongoing weight.

The shape is coherent but the fall is minimal. One beat covering the mother's visit and the log close. This is structurally sound — the fall should be short in a season where the denouement is Window 3's final beats — but the brevity creates a concern: does the reader exhale at the vigil ending? If the mother's declaration ("I stopped lighting the vigil candle") reads as a moment of completed grief rather than a change in mechanism, the reader might exhale before Window 3 complicates things.

This is an execution risk at prose level, not a bones-level flaw. The bones hold the correct shape. The mother exhales at 323 and exits at 324. The log opens at 326. No lingering. No editorializing. The bones do not allow the reader to exhale — the log closing immediately reframes the exchange as material to be recorded, not processed.

**SHAPE-COHERENT flag (soft):** The fall at beats 14-beat-17 has a pattern-repetition risk (beats 11 and 14 run nearly identical range-expansion bone sequences before the real peak at beat 16) that could read as shape-bloat in the rising action. The peak itself (beat 16) is correctly placed and correctly weighted. The two-iteration range-expansion (11, 14) before the maester naming may create a local flatline perception in the 266–278 stretch. This is a TOLERATED-window finding (noted above) that does not rise to a structural fault but is flagged for Window 3 awareness: beat 19 (third range expansion) should carry additional differentiation to avoid the expansion sequence becoming noise.

Verdict: **SHAPE-COHERENT — PASS with note**

No false catharsis. No resolution-before-weight. The fall changes form without releasing pressure. The peak earns its position through three-beat progression (beats 7→15→16). The two TOLERATED windows (beat 10's frictionless chain incorporation, beat 14's pattern-repetition) are within acceptable range for a ~163-bone window.
