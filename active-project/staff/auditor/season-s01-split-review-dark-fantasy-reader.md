Rowan's pastoral claim lands only if the reader feels what Taylor is giving up by accepting it. If the sept is described as a functional location rather than a place with cost, this reader starts skimming environmental description by line 180 and does not return.

2. **s01e05 Mira-interlude arithmetic.** Prior watch item elevated. Mira's withholding from Pryor (lines 608–624) must read as political calculation at visible cost — she is not doing Taylor a favor; she is spending something. If a single line tips into sympathetic-loyalty register, this persona flags it during shoot. Execution on notice.

3. **Swarm as passive-sense, not tool.** The ignition event (s01e04) is the only active swarm beat in the season. The insect threading throughout — flies in the workshop, the sept basin, the dock, the market — must read as a world that does not know it is being read, not as a surveillance apparatus Taylor is running. Lines 11–13 (clustering beetles and spiders in e01) set this correctly. Execution must hold the distinction through 912 lines. If any proto-line renders the insects as responsive to Taylor's attention rather than continuous and autonomous, flag during shoot.

4. **Superstition frame at ignition and after.** Series-level watch item remains active. Witnesses at the ignition must read the swarm through Westerosi superstition — Seven-as-ward, old-gods bleed-south register — not as curiosity or anomaly-noting. This activates specifically in the collection-retinue aftermath (s01e04 lines 480–519) and the maester's town-read (s01e06 lines 802–897). No structural fix available at split level; execution flag stands.
