**Flag 2 — W11 blank compression:** IDs 63–65 are three blanks before Plumm pockets the record. Prose authoring must give Plumm something to do in that space that reinforces the finality of the documentation. **Flag 4 — W14 close:** The ravens lifting + eyes-dropping close is affect-only at bone level. Prose authoring should ensure that at least one of these final beats (raven lift or pack-lift) contains a Taylor-read — something Rowan observes in Taylor before he leaves that confirms the damage is understood by both of them.
