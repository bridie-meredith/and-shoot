## Referral 1 — cond-pre-deployment-ritual-monument

**Source:** `active-project/theater/facets/memory.md` mem:3 @64. Advisory from worm-canon reviewer in `active-project/staff/auditor/facets-audience-gate-r2.md` §"Memory (structural rebuild)".

**Proposed slug:** `cond-pre-deployment-ritual-monument`

**Pattern definition:** A somatic-preparation sequence — postural shift and respiratory reset — that precedes the activation of a range-extended sensory-and-control apparatus. The sequence is habituated from prior-life operational use to the point of pre-conscious automaticity: the body initiates it on approach to a threshold-activation context before any explicit decision has been made. When this monument is active, the body's preparatory state is legible as data about the prior life's operational history to any observer with the right frame of reference; it is not legible to the speaker herself except as "what happens before the network goes."

**Cite-back form (if card existed):**
`mem:3 @64 the shoulder-set drops back and down and the breath leaves before the network goes -> cond-pre-deployment-ritual-monument`

**Expected callsites across the series:**
- s01: any scene where Taylor approaches a threshold-activation event (range expansion, first Hightower approach, escalating the broken-maester observation). The tic surfaces as a sensory-register note in the memory facet.
- s02: Khepri-mantle threshold crossing late in season — the pre-deployment ritual is likely to appear in the scene architecture at the Khepri activation moment itself, which is the highest-stakes activation in the series. Load-bearing monument at that juncture.
- s03: rider-control demonstration preparation. If the demonstration is staged with intentionality, the ritual will appear; if it is reactive, the ritual may appear despite no conscious preparation, which is a stronger form of the monument (automaticity persisting past the point where Taylor thought she was making a deliberate choice).
- s04: density-saturation experiment as a recurrence in the apparatus-log register. The ritual disappearing from the log would be a clinical-self-erasure-class absence.

**Risk notes:**
- Taxonomy question: the behavior card `taylor-hebert.card.md` §"Non-verbal tics" already names "The breath out before the swarm goes" as a tic entry. A monument-condition card covers the *structural register-rule* and *prior-life weight* of the pattern — what it means as a monument, how it surfaces in narrative, what it produces when adjacent — rather than the physical fact of the tic. If the distinction between tic (behavior-card content) and monument (condition-card content) is not clearly maintained in the card body, there is overlap risk. The monument card should be authored to cover the legibility-as-data and prior-life-origin aspects; the tic entry on the behavior card remains the physical-description locus. These can coexist without duplication if scopes are kept distinct.
- No existing condition card covers this. `cond-shard-behavioral-weight` covers escalation-bias; `cond-clinical-self-erasure` covers notation-drift. Neither addresses the pre-activation somatic sequence as a monument.
- The card directory `cards/conditions/memory-monuments/` referenced by `taylor-hebert.card.md` §"Memory monuments" does not yet exist. If this card is authored, a decision is needed: file under `cards/conditions/` flat (consistent with current library structure) or create the `memory-monuments/` subdirectory (consistent with the base behavior card's reference). That decision belongs to the promotion dispatch, not here.

## Referral 2 — cond-swarm-feed-cognition-monument

**Source:** `active-project/theater/facets/memory.md` mem:7 @159. Advisory from worm-canon reviewer in `active-project/staff/auditor/facets-audience-gate-r2.md` §"Memory (structural rebuild)".

**Proposed slug:** `cond-swarm-feed-cognition-monument`

**Pattern definition:** A cognitive-extension pattern in which the speaker's sensory-and-control network functions as a parallel processing track, producing behavioral inferences and ambient-register reads that the speaker's foreground cognition receives as conclusions rather than as reasoned steps. The feed runs ahead of deliberate analysis: behavioral data from observed subjects is weighed and filed before the apparatus registers that weighing as a choice. The pattern is a monument because it represents the specific epistemic architecture the prior life built around networked-sensor cognition — the learned capacity to trust inferences whose derivation is not introspectable.

**Cite-back form (if card existed):**
`mem:7 @159 the beetles relay the maester's second crossing and the behavioral-read-through is the kind of data the prior life learned to weigh before the apparatus knew what it was weighing -> cond-swarm-feed-cognition-monument`

**Expected callsites across the series:**
- s01: any scene where Taylor reads a person's behavior through the insect-network and acts on that read before she has consciously processed it (the broken maester's routine, the dock-runner's trust-test, the tanner-elder's conditional alignment). The monument is the prior-life origin of that cognitive habit — the learned epistemic trust in the feed.
- s02: the broken maester's naming of the Khepri threshold. He names what she is doing; she does not hear it as an accusation because the feed-cognition is too integrated for her to see it from outside. The monument is the structural explanation for why the naming is not heard.
- s03: faction-convergence engagement. The rider-control demonstration is a moment where the feed-as-cognition-extension is operating at maximum scale. The monument would anchor any memory entry about the prior life's parallel-track being what brought her to this capability.
- s04: the density-saturation experiment. The failure event — where the feed-cognition produces a false positive that drives the experiment past the safety threshold — is the foreclosure of this monument. If the card exists, the s04 entry could cite it as the monument that failed rather than authored a new gloss.

**Risk notes:**
- `condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology` covers what insects are available and how the feed's sensory properties differ in this ecology. It does not cover the cognitive-extension pattern or the monument register (prior-life epistemic architecture). No overlap; the two cards address different aspects of the same capability.
- The monument is arguably more behavior-class than condition-class. The behavior card `taylor-hebert.card.md` §"Memory monuments" is the canonical locus for how Taylor's voice references Earth-Bet monuments; this pattern could be documented there as a monument entry rather than as a standalone condition card. The argument for condition-card status: the pattern applies across multiple characters (any observer with the right frame can read its outputs) and it has ambient-modifier effects on scenes (it governs what behavioral data is available before dialogue begins). The argument for behavior-card-only status: it is a cognitive pattern native to a single speaker's prior-life architecture and its register-rules are all speaker-internal. Promotion dispatch should adjudicate taxonomy before authoring.
- If filed as a condition card, the `cards/conditions/memory-monuments/` directory question from Referral 1 applies here as well.
- No existing condition card covers this pattern. The promotion dispatch should check whether any behavior-card revision to `taylor-hebert.card.md` §"Memory monuments" already suffices before authoring a standalone card.
