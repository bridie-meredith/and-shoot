# Cold-read drag pass — Chapters 1–5

Naive reader. Range: Ch 1–5 of `active-project/draft/b01-manuscript.md`. Voice register (cold analytical first-person, ledger-flavored) is the book's signature and is NOT flagged as such — only flagged where density tips into genuine reader-fatigue.

## REPETITION ACROSS 1–5

- **"held the count" / "held the feet" / "held my feet" / "held the angle" / "I held the…"** — the *hold* verb is the narrator's signature physical tic and recurs ~15+ times across the five chapters (Ch1: "I held the feet," "I kept the feet"; Ch2: "I held the count at its edge," "held the breath"; Ch3: "I held my position," "I held my feet"; Ch4: "I held my feet" ×2, "held the four-ward feed"; Ch5: "the hold I kept at the wall-line," "I held the entry's face"). Load-bearing as voice, but the *literal* "held my feet" at a yard/junction appears so often it has gone invisible. Recommend thinning the bare "I held my feet" beats by roughly a third where no new stakes attach.

- **"the count" / "the feed" / "the map" / "the accounting" / "the ledger"** as the subject-actor ("the count carried her past," "the accounting closed," "the feed returned X," "the map assigned") — the apparatus-as-agent construction is the dominant sentence engine and appears dozens of times. Signature, not flagged per se, but note: when *every* sentence in a paragraph has an apparatus noun as subject (e.g. Ch2 lines 121–131, Ch5 lines 335–339), the prose goes airless because no human verb breaks the surface. Recommend the trimming pass break up the densest apparatus-subject runs rather than thin them evenly.

- **"the price-tag" / "the cost crossed before I registered it crossing" / "the cost did not appear in the closing"** — the *cost-paid-but-unfelt* beat closes Ch2 (line 131), Ch3 (line 214), and Ch4 (line 270). Three near-identical chapter-end "the cost had crossed before my hand registered it" landings. Recommend varying or cutting one so the device doesn't telegraph.

- **Wren / stitch-maker's ward re-identification** — her full descriptor ("the eleven-year-old whose hands had gone up first at the cart-morning") is re-appended on nearly every appearance (Ch1, Ch2 line 107, Ch3 line 146, Ch4). 4+ full re-introductions. After Ch2 the reader has her; the dossier tax should be paid once and then dropped to a bare "Wren" / "the stitch-maker's ward."

- **Self-justification intro aphorism** — each chapter's italic preamble ends on a variant of "the rule was still the rule / I had not crossed the line / the work was what I did instead" (Ch1 "the rule is what I have left," Ch2 "how it stayed permitted," Ch3 "the work was what I did instead," Ch4 "the rule had a price tag and a destination both," Ch5 "I had agreed to this"). Five chapters, five restatements of the same moral-ledger throat-clear. The device is fine once or twice; by Ch5 it is a tic. Recommend trimming the closing aphorism in at least two of the five intros.

- **"would not… would not… would not" negation cadence** — clustered triads of refusal-to-close (Ch5 line 349; Ch2 closing). Recommend reducing triads to pairs.
