## Hard-Fence Verification: @48

**Entry text:** "she has heard the shape of that word before in another tongue"

**Fence in question:** variant §"Memory monuments: Earth-Bet monuments: Hard fence: never named in any Westerosi-set scene." Specifically, does "in another tongue" name Earth-Bet?

**Ruling: LICIT. Not a hard-fence violation.**

The hard fence covers *Earth-Bet proper nouns*: the locker, the S9, Leviathan, Gold Morning, the Birdcage, PRT, Wards, Protectorate, Endbringer, Cauldron, Scion, Gold Morning. "Another tongue" is not any of these. It does not name English, does not name Earth-Bet, does not name any recognizable Earth-Bet institutional or monument term.

The variant card's authorized construction is: "The interior may circle. Free-indirect or close-third interior may approach a monument without entering it." The example given is: *This was not the year Bakuda lit the city. This was a different year.* That construction names Bakuda by name; "in another tongue" names nothing — it specifies only that the word was previously known in a different linguistic register than Westerosi. This is the displacement-cue pattern the variant card explicitly licenses: "Surface as displacement only... the interior knows; the spoken line does not name. The reader feels the weight without the cause being articulated."

"Another tongue" is not an affirmation. It is not a naming. It is a negation-by-implication: this word is being heard in *this* tongue, but she has heard its shape in another one. Earth-Bet is implied by the structure; the structure is the displacement, not the naming. The variant card marks "negation-by-naming" as permitted at low frequency. This construction is lower-register than negation-by-naming — it does not even reach the level of naming by negation. It circles.

**Foreknowledge-clamp on "provisional":** Does "she has heard the shape of that word before in another tongue" leak Dance specifics? The entry names: (a) that the word "provisional" triggers recognition, and (b) that the recognition has a prior-life source. It does not name: what the Dance-foreknowledge about "provisional" determinations contains, who the determination will affect, when it will matter, or any canonical event. "The shape of that word" clamps the content behind the perception. The Dance-foreknowledge is gestured at without being opened. The audit-fence — "any line of hers that reveals foreknowledge of the Dance is the deepest audit-trip in this card" — concerns *revealed* foreknowledge. This entry reveals only that foreknowledge exists. The content stays shut.

**WCP note on @48:** WCP's primary canon-pedant concern with foreknowledge-clamp entries is lore-leak: does the character know something they have no in-world path to knowing *in a way that is readable to another character in-world*? Here, the interior entry is not spoken. It is Taylor's closed interiority. No in-world character receives the information. The lore-leak concern is external-to-character-knowledge, not internal; the internal register-awareness is the licensed channel. ACCEPT.

**Final ruling on @48: VOICE-OK. Hard fence not tripped. Foreknowledge-clamp holds.**
