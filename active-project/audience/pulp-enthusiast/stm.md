## Session: OQ-1 series planning review (2026-05-03)

### Open question reviewed: OQ-1 reincarnation form (Taylor Hebert in Westeros)

**Option A (she-dragon):** ACCEPT
Reason: High complication density baked into the premise. Every scene runs a double threat: survive the capture attempt, stay unidentified. The board gets worse on a natural timer (Dance of Dragons approaching). Targaryen claimants, dragon-hunters, and Small Council schemers all recur as threats at escalating moments. This machine runs fast and does not require setup scenes — Taylor starts as a tactical actor immediately. Escalation geometry is correct.

**Option B (baseborn human, Riverlands):** REVISE
Reason: "Grows up without title" and "watching prewar maneuvering" indicate a long observer arc before the board moves. Competence-building as its own reward is a pacing trap. If the Riverlands arc is scene after scene of positioning and establishing credentials, this persona fidgets and leaves. Would accept only with explicit guarantee of complication density throughout the growth arc — the board must move while Taylor is still finding her footing, not after.

**Option C (skinchanger/greensight):** REVISE
Reason: Greensight as pitched ("flashes," "fragments") is a passive information power. Passive information powers generate scenes where the protagonist knows things but cannot act — which is three paragraphs of agonized decision-making waiting to happen. Would accept only if greensight is reframed as actionable real-time intelligence that Taylor can deploy as a tactical asset, not atmospheric foreknowledge she experiences and broods over.

### Aggregation result (round 1): Option A recommended (2/3 accept)
This persona voted ACCEPT on A. Conditions to maintain: complication density must stay live throughout — no observer lag, no scene where the board is static and Taylor watches. The Dance must be the approaching avalanche, not a backdrop event.

### Pattern flags for this project:
- This persona will flag hard on any scene that is pure setup — the board must be live or getting worse
- Offscreen consequences are a walkout trigger — if the Dance is happening elsewhere while Taylor observes, this reader leaves
- Escalation must be literal (situation worse) not just tonal (mood darker)
- Option A chosen: every scene needs a live threat or a board-change; capture attempts, rival claimants, and Targaryen schemers must appear on escalating schedule
