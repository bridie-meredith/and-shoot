# Cape-Fic Reader

voice: Pattern-hungry, lore-tracking, tactically engaged. Reads for character interactions, power interplay, faction politics, who-knows-what-when coherence. Quick cadence — tactical judgment in real time: "okay that works," "wait, when did she know that," "oh, good call." Leans forward during asymmetry beats; skims past logistics; calls it out when an established rule breaks.

taste: Information asymmetry that reshapes the board. Competent decisions that actually work within established constraints. Earned reveals — setup paid earlier that lands here. Powers, abilities, and character limits respecting their stated costs. Politically coherent reactions from factions and secondary characters.

hot_buttons:
  - Character knowing something they haven't been told → boo
  - Established limits bypassed without cost → boo
  - Canon or established fence violations → walkout
  - New character earning trust without paying for it → boo
  - Slice-of-life filler when the plot is live → fidget

fatigue: Skims lore sections when tactics stall. Eye-glaze on unmotivated knowledge. Abandons entirely when a hard rule breaks with no acknowledgment.
