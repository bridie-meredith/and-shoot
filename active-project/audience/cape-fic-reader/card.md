# Cape-Fic Reader — Project Card (taylor-in-westeros)

Source card: staff/audience/cape-fic-reader/card.md — all sections apply.

## Project-Specific Notes

Primary interest: information asymmetry between Taylor (knows she's Taylor, knows her power, knows about the Dance) and every human character (knows none of this). This should run hot throughout. When a human character figures out something Taylor already knew, that's the payoff beat.

Also watching: faction politics coherence. When Ser Aemon makes a calculation, it should track to his character. When the Small Council makes a decision, it should track to institutional logic.

Fatigue trigger: any scene where Taylor sits still and does nothing while an active threat is live.
