Pattern updated: Option A is the only seed event where the dramatic-irony gap between Taylor's self-narration and the prose's consequence-register is dense, present in the operational moment, and available for the reader to read in real time. The habit planted is procedural — framing-for-effect as calibration rather than deception — which means each subsequent application carries the same irony without repeating the same scene. The foreclosure shape is correct: the framing habit works because it is accurate, is adopted because it works, and becomes the only available register, which means the first time it burns someone essential it cannot be abandoned because it is by then how she operates. Doors-that-close, not lessons-that-teach.

## Round 8 — OQ-6 Plan Review (2026-05-17, /and-project Phase 1b)

Topic: Closing-shape / book's verdict. Three options (all prior OQs settled: Dragon Gate, witch-label, block-cap, 126 AC, Watch-track captain, "speaking the patron's dialect" 6-rung ladder, M27–28 sweep-killing, interior model-collapse prose-unnamed per Round 7 non-negotiable condition).

Verdicts:
- Option A (Taylor alive, still operating, has not understood): REVISE. The terminus requires the reader to do too much assembly if Mira's gait-detail has not been established as a recurring prose register. "Woman with Mira's gait stopping" — the weight of this line depends on a specific detail (Mira's gait as a named insect-perception signature) having been prepared earlier in the book. At plan level, this preparation is not guaranteed. A terminus whose weight is contingent on execution-layer preparation that could fail is an execution risk that B and C do not carry. Additionally: if the insect-delivery of Mira's gait-signature is the first time the prose uses insect-perception as a narrative conduit for a consequence Taylor refuses, it reads as arranged at the terminus — a device introduced for the payoff rather than an established register arriving at its natural use. That is the authorial-arrangement charge. Flagged as conditional, not fatal, but lower confidence than C at plan level.
- Option B (Taylor alive, has understood, paralyzed): REVISE. Hard rejection. "Choosing not to know what it knows" names the meaning. The Round 7 non-negotiable condition — model falsification as reader-inference, never stated — applies with equal force to the closing sentence. "Choosing not to know" assigns Taylor deliberate agency over her ignorance, converting structural foreclosure into a narrated tragic choice. The word "choosing" is the prose doing the structural work the reader is supposed to do. The beetle-on-windowsill is then set dressing for a meaning the prose has already delivered. This directly violates the distinction between theme-as-structure and theme-as-statement that this book has maintained from Round 1. The violation is not recoverable by execution; it is inscribed in the option's shape.
- Option C (Taylor dead, processed by sweep): ACCEPT. "Her own range-capped insects registering no movement inside the building she's in." Foreclosure as structure, not statement. The protagonist is absent from the terminus as a subject — the world's instrument records her absence. The reader does the assembly from established materials: they know why the insects are there (ambient-not-directed, range-capped, established from OQ-1), they know what the range-cap means, they know what stopped moving. The prose provides only the image. The arrangement risk is real — using Taylor's own power-extension as the terminus instrument is structurally elegant in a way that can tip into arrangement. But eligible to be elegant rather than arranged, because the insect-ambient-register is established from the book's earliest pages (OQ-1 settlement) and the closing image uses that register rather than introducing it. The arrangement concern is an execution obligation, not a plan-level fatal.

Aggregate: C recommended (3-of-3 accept across all personas). A passes 2-of-3 with execution risk at terminus. B rejected 2-of-3.

Pattern updated: Option C is the settled closing shape. It satisfies the Round 7 non-negotiable condition (model falsification as reader-inference, never stated) because the closing image does not name anything — it provides only a datum the reader must interpret. Foreclosure is structural in Option C: the protagonist is literally absent; the consequence runs to completion without her. Execution obligation carried forward: the insect-ambient-register must be a consistent prose mechanism throughout the book, not a technique surfacing at the terminus as a symbolic flourish. If present throughout: the closing image is earned. If not: the arrangement charge upgrades from execution risk to plan flaw. This obligation is flagged for tracking at screen-writer and shoot stages.
