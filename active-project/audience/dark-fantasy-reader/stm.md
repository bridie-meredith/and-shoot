## Round 2 — OQ-2 Plan Review (2026-05-17, /and-project Phase 1b)

Topic: Taylor Hebert arrival circumstance and identity-handle. Three options (block-capped, insects-only, ambient power-state settled from OQ-1).

Verdicts:
- Option 1 (Flea Bottom, "vagrant"): REVISE. World-invisibility is not world-hostility. A protagonist the world doesn't notice is not the same as a protagonist the world opposes. Early wins unrecorded means no friction — the world is indifferent, not dangerous. Indifferent worlds do not cost anything to move through.
- Option 2 (Dragon Gate, "witch"): ACCEPT. World makes its move before she does. Septon calls witch — that is Westerosi-specific superstition, not generic authority. Gold cloaks release her as lunatic — she survives by being dismissed, not rescued. The world's hostility is immediate, culturally grounded, and the resolution (dismissal) compounds rather than resolves: she is now marked and loose in the market block. Earned.
- Option 3 (Maegor's Holdfast, "spy"): REVISE. Two days detained, released as not-worth-paperwork. After detention, release reads as reprieve. The adjacent-to-power positioning feels author-placed — the world drops her near the machinery she needs. That is set dressing for the protagonist, not a world that existed before her.

Pattern updated: The arrival must be the world's first move, not Taylor's. Option 2 is the only arrival where Westeros acts first and the resolution (dismissal-as-lunatic) is not a softening. The witch-label compounds — it is not neutralized when she walks free, it follows her. That is the correct shape of earned bleakness: the first hostile contact leaves a mark that doesn't come off.

## Round 3 — OQ-3 Plan Review (2026-05-17, /and-project Phase 1b)

Topic: Taylor Hebert time-of-arrival within pre-Dance window. Three options (Dragon Gate arrival, witch-label, block-cap, settled from prior rounds).

Verdicts:
- Option A (126 AC late summer, ~3 years before Viserys dies): ACCEPT. Administrative normalcy means the hostility is granular — street-level, Watch-level, septon-level. World-weight is sustained by social texture before political temperature spikes. Three years of the world pressing on Taylor through ordinary Westerosi superstition and ordinary Watch hostility is three years of terrain that costs something to move through. The war off-page or barely-on at close means the first irrecoverable atrocity carries full weight: the reader has accumulated the slow hostile texture, and the atrocity lands as consequence of a causal chain they can reconstruct. Earned.
- Option B (127 AC early winter, ~18 months): REVISE. "Politically charged lull" is the specific phrase that triggers the set-dressing alarm. Faction temperature bleeding into streets adds spectacle pressure before the witch-label compound has done its work. The second half risks war-adjacent events that function as pageant unless every beat is grounded at individual cost — that is authorial counterpressure required on every page, not a structural solution. Two-act symmetry is a convenience, not an earned shape.
- Option C (128 AC autumn, ~6-8 months): REVISE. Open political crisis at arrival means the world the reader encounters is already in spectacle mode. Minimal witch-label compound time means the earned-bleakness structure is compressed into a period dominated by war events. War as majority of book triggers the catharsis-not-earned walkout: the weight hasn't accumulated for the consequence to register as loss.

Pattern updated: Sustained world-weight lives in the long pre-crisis stretch where Westeros is hostile at the texture level — not dramatic, not spectacular, just costly. Option A is the only option that structurally guarantees that stretch. Options B and C shorten it until the world stops being terrain and starts being backdrop. The war must arrive after the weight has accumulated; only Option A gives both the time they need.
