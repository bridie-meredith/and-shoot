## Round 5 — OQ-4 Plan Review (2026-05-17, /and-project Phase 1b)

- Option C (Velaryon merchant-track, Lyseni factor): REVISE. "Operationally clean Act 1" is a structural softening. This reader does not want an operationally clean Act 1 — the world must press on her from arrival. The earned-bleakness payoff in Act 2 (network re-reads as Black-adjacent enemy infrastructure) has maximum impact only if Act 1 cost is already accumulating. An invisible fuse means no cost is paid until the reveal. Reprieve, not terrain.

Pattern updated: The faction-gravity must be hostile infrastructure from the start — not a patron who protects or accommodates, but one who uses her at arm's length in a way that compounds without visible threat. Option A is the only option where the patron-relation is world-happening-to-her from day one, the Act 1 cost is already running, and the Act 2 re-read carries full weight because the reader has been accumulating the entanglement throughout.
