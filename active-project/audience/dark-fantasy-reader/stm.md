## Session: /and-project Phase 1b Batch A

### Bundle verdicts
- Bundle A: ACCEPT — embedded eight months with residual perception and no control; she is not in command of her situation; correct footing for a world that should feel dangerous.
- Bundle B: REVISE — three years embedded with full prescience reads as protagonist immunity; if she already knows the Dance, the world cannot push back against her in a way that costs anything real; earned-bleakness requirement not met.
- Bundle C: ACCEPT — involuntary precognitive flicker that pattern-matches catastrophe but lacks canon ASOIAF knowledge keeps her genuinely in danger; false certainty is more frightening than false safety; consequence-propagation enabled.

### Triad outcome
Bundle C: triad ACCEPT (3-of-3). Bundle A: triad ACCEPT (2-of-3). Bundle B: triad REVISE (2-of-3 reject).

### Carry-forward flags
If Bundle C is selected: the false-certainty engine must be used against Taylor, not for her — any scene where the flicker confirms her good intentions rather than undermining them is a cost-evasion, not a use of the mechanic.
