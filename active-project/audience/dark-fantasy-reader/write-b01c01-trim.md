# /and-write Phase 4 — dark-fantasy-reader trim — b01c01

## Per-bone proposals

### s01 — arrival

- bone b01c01s01n03 `the building-keeper pockets the copper-stars` — PROPOSE-DELETE — k+0.01; the transaction already landed with n02 (Taylor pays); n03 is the keeper's receipt of it, which adds no new information, no texture, no push-back from the world. The payment is done once the copper changes hands. A second bone to watch it pocketed is the narration nodding at itself.

- bone b01c01s01n11 `taylor-hebert-kl-122ac enters the yard` — PROPOSE-DELETE — marked bridge; k+0.01; this is pure locomotion between n02 (building-keeper) and n05 (crossing the yard). Two movement bones back-to-back (enter then cross) compress to one without loss. Bridge bones that do nothing but move a body across an unmarked threshold are filler by definition. The yard's geometry can be established in n05 itself.

- bone b01c01s01n06 `coll-net-mender-flea-bottom works the net` — KEEP-BUT-FLAG — feigned-indifference is the right beat, but this is the second bone in a row for Coll after n04 (lifts the eyes). The rhythm of watch / feign works dramatically. Flag only: if the prose author lingers here and gives Coll any internal explanation or softens the surveillance into curiosity, the register cracks. This bone's k+0.02 has to be earned by the *quality* of the indifference, not the fact of it. The dramatist shaped it correctly; the prose risk is sentimental treatment.

- bone b01c01s01n08 `taylor-hebert-kl-122ac drops the pack` — STRONG-KEEP — null axis-move, marked settle. On its face a deletion candidate. I want it kept. Taylor going dormant and still — choosing to hold the ground rather than perform — is the capability-held-not-deployed beat that earns this whole scene's patience tax. If this bone is cut, the scene ends on the speech (n09) and the hold (n10) without the physicality of the settle. The pack going down is the first image of Taylor as a fixed point, not a transient. That is load-bearing for the cold-accounting register.

- bone b01c01s01n09 `coll-net-mender-flea-bottom speaks to taylor-hebert-kl-122ac` — STRONG-KEEP — opposing force makes first direct contact; speech bone after full scene of surveillance and feint. This is where Flea Bottom vouching-physics become literal. No delete risk, calling it out explicitly because it's the pivot.

### s02 — working day

- bone b01c01s02n01 `taylor-hebert-kl-122ac lifts the basket` — PROPOSE-DELETE — k null, rhythm marker only. Rhythm bones earn their place when they establish or reinforce a pattern the chapter needs. But n02 (Coll pulls the net) already names the shared-labor rhythm. Having Taylor's rhythm-beat precede Coll's in the same beat-pair duplicates the establishment. One of the two rhythm bones carries the scene; the other is redundancy. If forced to choose: Coll's is the more interesting half (opposing-force in a rhythm-bond is stranger and harder to achieve), so Taylor's basket is the expendable one. If the prose author needs a parallel construction, the parallel can live in a single bone's language.

- bone b01c01s02n02 `coll-net-mender-flea-bottom pulls the net` — KEEP-BUT-FLAG — this is the rhythm-establishing half I'd keep over n01, but flagging: both n01 and n02 are k-null. If only one survives the trim, it must do the work of orienting the reader to the passage of days. Flag that the prose author needs to do something with the net that is specific to Flea Bottom — not generic labor but the kind of labor that has a smell and a sound and a calendar.

- bone b01c01s02n06 `the needle crosses the mesh` — KEEP-BUT-FLAG — k+0.03, parallel to n03 (Taylor threads the needle). Two needle bones in the same scene: n03 establishes the action, n06 tracks the progress of it. The flag is that this bone needs to be doing something more than confirming the needle has moved. If the mesh is a vehicle for Taylor's pattern-reading to be enacted — if the crossing of the mesh mirrors the way she reads bodies — then it earns its separate bone. If it's just "and then she kept sewing," it collapses into n03.

- bone b01c01s02n04 `the insects fill the block` — STRONG-KEEP — k+0.05, c null, opposing-force capability-available. This is the chapter's first explicit naming of what Taylor is not doing. The insects are present, the capability is visible, and it is held. This is exactly the beat the series contract requires at d01 (dormancy by choice, close-range only). Strong keep regardless of what else gets trimmed.

- bone b01c01s02n08 `the city-watch passes the hook` — STRONG-KEEP — k+0.05, the chapter's second-highest single-bone knowledge increment after n04 (insects). The watch passing is the institutional world applying its own surveillance to the same street where Taylor is suppressing hers. The structural irony of that simultaneity — her insects, the watch's boots — is load-bearing for the chapter's geometry.

### s03 — Wren enters

- bone b01c01s03n09 `wren-stitch-maker-flea-bottom-ward approaches taylor-hebert-kl-122ac` — PROPOSE-DELETE — k+0.01, marked bridge proximity. Bridge bones in s03 are a concern because this scene already runs nine bones and has a child who risks becoming cute. n09 is a pure locomotion bone: Wren moves closer. The proximity itself can be handled in n02's speech setup (she is there enough to speak, so she is already close enough). Keeping a dedicated approach bone adds a beat of anticipation that risks being read as the narrative warming the child up before the reader meets her. That is exactly the sentimental preparation the register cannot afford.

- bone b01c01s03n06 `taylor-hebert-kl-122ac holds the eyes` — KEEP-BUT-FLAG — c null, catch. The second "holds the eyes" bone in the chapter (n10 in s01 was dormancy-hold; this is catch after Wren's second speech). The action is correct — Taylor catching herself, recognizing the pattern-reading impulse — but two bones with nearly identical descriptions across the chapter risk feeling like a verbal tic rather than a deliberate echo. Flag for the prose author: the two holds need to be distinct in register. s01's hold is animal dormancy. s03's hold should be something colder — recognition, ledger-entry, something that names the failure-mode without naming it.

- bone b01c01s03n08 `taylor-hebert-kl-122ac lifts the needle` — STRONG-KEEP — c null, scene-close. This bone does the work the closing bone must do: the chapter goal requires Wren to land as "the ward it will fail to protect" without sentiment, and the way to close against a child is to cut back to the object (needle) not the face. Taylor picking up the needle after Wren exits is a cold-accounting close. If this bone becomes anything warmer — if the prose author lingers on Wren's retreating figure instead of the needle going up — the chapter fails. Calling it out as load-bearing because the prose risk here is highest.
