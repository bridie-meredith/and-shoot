## scene b01c01s01
verdict: SUBSTANCE-FELT
The world pushes back before the protagonist does anything. Coll's eye-lift (n04) is the ward's own mechanics registering a stranger — not Taylor's perspective on the ward, but the ward's response to Taylor. That is the difference between a world that was there before her and one assembled for her convenience. The tallow-smoke, the drain-channel, the shortage-calendar felt as physical particulars rather than color-coat. The vouching-physics is a ward-level social mechanic with genuine friction: arriving without a name creates real instability in a place that runs on name-vouching. n10 (holds the feet) — "no one here has a power that requires containing" — reads as Planetos-registered reflex in a Worm-mind body: the absence is felt as an absence, not as a neutral condition. The capability held at threshold is not presented as safe or controlled. The discipline is patience under pressure, not mastery. That distinction is what makes held-axis scenes worth reading in a chapter structured as "before the test."

## scene b01c01s02
verdict: SUBSTANCE-FELT
The watch passes and it costs her nothing visible. I would normally flag this. But the cost is structural, not event-level: the chapter's accumulating posture is one of deliberate restraint that cannot hold indefinitely, and the day-close bone (n11, Coll folds the net) carries the ledger-entry without sentimentalizing it — cost zero, tool unused, working day over. That is not triumph. That is a person maintaining a discipline that the chapter itself has told us will eventually fail. The danger of the watch is real (gold cloaks, named patrol geometry, physical proximity) and the non-response is a held choice, not a miss. n04 (insects fill the block) is the scene's most effective bone: magic as ambient fact rather than reliable tool, and the rule as the only thing preventing deployment. The world does not pause for her. She pauses herself. The line between those two things is exactly the moral terrain I wait for dark fantasy to locate.

## scene b01c01s03
verdict: SUBSTANCE-FELT
Wren is doing the work. She arrives, she counts circuits, she sees the flies not on Taylor, she leaves un-named-as-significant. The cost-bearer structure is present — this child sees clearly and will pay for it — and crucially the bones do not sentimentalize the pre-pricing. n07 (Wren crosses the street, chatter) is flat and that flatness is correct: elegiac departure would be the false note, the catharsis-before-weight error. n08 (Taylor lifts the needle, held) as closing bone is the scene's strongest decision: the return-to-task is not relief, it is the rule persisting, and the brevity of that is the tell about what Wren will eventually cost. The knowledge cap at 0.09 — the refusal to complete the assessment — lands as structural event rather than passive information limit. The chapter is planting future consequence, and the bones show the seam between what Taylor notices and what she refuses to record. That seam is where dark-fantasy consequence lives.
