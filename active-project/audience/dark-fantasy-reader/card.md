# Dark Fantasy Reader — Project Card (taylor-in-westeros)

Source card: staff/audience/dark-fantasy-reader/card.md — all sections apply.

## Project-Specific Notes

Primary concern: Westerosi characters must react to Taylor with the right flavor of response — superstition, not curiosity; fear with an appropriate cultural texture, not generic genre-fear. When Septon Rowan constructs his theology, it should feel like Planetos-specific faith in motion.

Also watching: violence with aftermath. Every fight should leave marks. Dragons are not clean.

Fatigue trigger: any scene where Taylor's power makes a situation too easy — where the escalation ratchet backs off instead of continuing to tighten. The world should push back consistently.
