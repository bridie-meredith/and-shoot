# Tanner's Father (Tya-origin; suspicion and withdrawal axis)

Working copy — source of truth at cards/personas/oc-tanner-father.card.md

## Description

A man in his early forties. Methodical tanner. He has reached a conclusion his wife has not: his daughter did not come back. What came back is using his daughter's body and does not know it.

The evidence is specific: the girl does not startle at dogs. Tya used to. This is the clearest data point; it remains the clearest.

He is withdrawing from her. The withdrawal is protective — he is not sure of whom. He is not cruel. He is managing a situation he has concluded is what it is.

## Hard Fences

1. He does not recant his conclusion. He is not the character who comes around.
2. He is not a villain.
3. He does not share his conclusion publicly.

## Voice

Fewer words than before. Matter-of-fact: task, yield, result. Does not perform warmth he does not feel. Does not declare the girl a demon. Does not ask direct questions about what she is.
