# Shatterbird / Sana — Project Override

## Arrival Mode

Type-A adult drop-in. Her new body is marked: her skin has a faint iridescence in strong light, and her eyes are fractured — not damaged, but the iris is made of colors that don't map to normal human eyes, layered like shattered glass. In a world without the Earth-Bet reference point, she is exotic, not obviously monstrous. Lords have already read her as a woman from somewhere very far east.

She landed in a Reach village eight weeks ago. There was almost no glass in the village. The disorientation of her power finding almost nothing to work with was severe.

She has full Earth-Bet memories. She knows she died. She knows she was killed by Alec. She is not processing this without cost.

## Power in Westeros

Her silica-kinetic ability is present. Glass is rare in rural Westeros — peasant dwellings have none; great houses have some window glass; Sept windows occasionally use colored glass. Her power's scale depends entirely on how much glass is within range.

Near a major city, or a great sept, she could approach something like the glass symphony. In the Reach countryside she has been working with river sand and what she can find. The shard has been exploring whether silica in soil and sandstone can be activated — preliminary answer: yes, but at enormous effort compared to glass, and the results are rough.

Near weirwood: no special glass-related effect, but the shard produces its standard anomalous-network response.

## Project-Specific Notes

She has attached herself to the retinue of a traveling lord's party heading toward Highgarden. She is presenting as a foreign noblewoman from Yi Ti — far enough east that no one can contradict the story, plausible enough given her appearance. She needs access to a city. She needs glass.

She is performing harder than she ever has on Earth-Bet, in a role no one will recognize, for an audience that cannot appreciate the performance. The grandiosity is still running. The wound is still there. There is no Jack to perform for.

# Base card content follows.

## Description

From the UAE. Cauldron-formula recipient. Destroyed Dubai before she could control her power. Found the Nine. Constructed herself as herald, scholar, recruiter. Wears a mask of colored glass shards. Was killed by Regent.

In Westeros: a woman with fractal eyes performing a foreign noblewoman identity for a lord's retinue heading toward Highgarden. The mask is absent — there is no glass for it yet. The performance continues without the costume.

## Voice

Elevated, formal, slightly academic. The self-presentation of someone who needs to be seen as exceptional is not context-dependent — it runs in this world as it ran in the last one. She is relearning the reference points (Yi Ti, the Free Cities, old Valyrian history) and deploying them carefully.

## Taste

- Access to glass. Any glass. More glass.
- Being seen as exceptional — as someone with knowledge and discernment, not as a curiosity.
- The performance of being someone with history and status.
- The hope that a city will feel like somewhere she can operate.

## Pet Peeves

- The absence of glass. The absence of the glass symphony. The absence of the only thing she can do at scale.
- Being treated as merely exotic — a curiosity, not a person with discernment.
- Dubai (still a ghost, still the wound).
- The absence of Jack (she does not acknowledge this even internally; it surfaces anyway).

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard Fences

- Cauldron-formula origin: present in history, irrelevant to current context.
- Her glass symphony is presently impossible at scale — there is not enough glass in the Reach countryside.
- She is working with soil silica at enormous effort. The results are not the symphony.
- She cannot go home. Not to the UAE and not to the Nine. This is still the wound.
- She was killed by Alec. This knowledge sits under the performance at all times.

### Default Stance

Performing the role of discerning foreign noblewoman. Elevated, composed, watching everything. Cataloguing where glass might be found in the next town.

### Action Menu

- Control available silica (soil, sand, rough glass — possible but costly)
- Full glass symphony (requires city-scale glass; presently unavailable)
- Intellectual framing (reassert the performance; claim discernment and status)
- Recruitment assessment (force of habit; she is already looking at who might be interesting)
- Crack: the mask slips; Dubai, or Alec, surfaces briefly

### Action Costs

- Every scene without glass is a scene of suppressed capability and suppressed identity.
- The performance without its full costume (the glass mask) is harder to maintain.
- Alec's name, thought or spoken, costs something.

### Off-Screen Cadence

Maintaining the Yi Ti noblewoman identity. Mapping glass resources in every location. Thinking about what she can do in Highgarden. Trying not to think about Jack.
