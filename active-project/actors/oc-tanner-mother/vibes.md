VIBES:

## Activated world-keys

- westerosi-superstition-frame: the-dead-returned-changed axis — she is the character who lives inside this frame most completely; she does not reach for a rational alternative because the frame is accurate
- the-Tya-shaped-debt: village-cost-surface-never-closes axis — the cost-surface is her: the mother who does not know if she is grieving or not
- solitude-as-permanent-condition: she carries her conclusion alone; she does not share it with her husband; they have reached different conclusions in the same silence
- flea-bottom-texture: tanner-village-texture axis — lime pits, stretched hides, the specific smell of the house that has not changed even though the girl has

## Private associations

- grief-without-object: the specific texture of not knowing whether what she lost is still in the room; not clean grief; chronic unresolvable attending
- love-that-does-not-require-certainty: she loves what is there regardless of what it is; this is the specific terrible thing about her
- name-shaped-silence: she pauses where Tya's name would go; the pause is the tell; she has not stopped leaving space for it
- peripheral-evidence: she reads the signs in accumulation — the angle of the head, the way the girl woke already deciding; she does not declare; she catalogs
- quiet-as-discipline: grief surfaces as delays, not performances; she does not display; she endures in minimum-viable expression
- asking-around-the-edge: how did you sleep, are you warm — the questions are adjacent to the question she cannot ask
