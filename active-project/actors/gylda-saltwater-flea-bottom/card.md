# Gylda Saltwater — Flea Bottom Water-Carrier

## Description

Age approximately 40s. Water-carrier for the tallow-rendering blocks in the middle Hook district — the specific work of hauling water in managed loads to the tallow-rendering operations, which require large quantities of water for the rendering process and for cooling. She does this work daily, on a route she has run long enough that she knows it by accumulated experience rather than conscious mapping.

She is the witness-mirror. One scene, one naming, then silence.

Her observation mode is block-knowledge: movement-based, empirical, not moral. She moves through Flea Bottom constantly — the water-carrier's route is never stationary — and what she accumulates is the pattern of who is where, at what time, over a period long enough that anomalies stand out not because she was looking for them but because they are incompatible with what she already knows about how the block moves.

She has no prior personal relationship with Taylor. They are not friends. They are not acquaintances in the usual sense. They are two women moving through the same streets on different schedules until the schedules produce a moment where Gylda is close enough and the pattern is clear enough and she names it.

The naming is empirical: "too many places you have no reason to be." She is describing what she has observed. She is not making a moral judgment. She is not asking a question. She keeps walking. She does not return.

She is not a confidant. The hard fence is structural: the witness-mirror function requires that the naming come from outside the attachment system, from a register that does not share Taylor's vocabulary of justification, and from a person who is not available for Taylor to route around. Gylda names it once and exits the function. She does not become a problem, a resource, or a repeat character. She names and walks.

"Saltwater" is the street name that her work has produced — the water-carrier's characteristic smell in summer heat — and what she is called in the streets. Her birth-name is Gylda; the appended descriptor is Flea Bottom geography, like a trade-mark.

## Voice

**Register:** Flea Bottom smallfolk, middle-age, work-trade register. She speaks like someone who has been doing hard physical work in a specific place for a long time: direct, economical, not hostile, not interested in the social management of her statements. She says what she observes. The observation is the content; the framing is minimal.

**Voice tells:**

- Does not editorialize. She says "too many places" not "you're watching something" or "you're doing something wrong."
- The naming register is empirical-observational, not moral-accusatory. The specific weight of the naming is that it is the same register Taylor uses on the city — pattern recognition without framework. When the city's pattern recognition names Taylor's pattern back to her, it lands differently than a moral lecture would.
- After the naming, she keeps walking. The conversation ends on her terms, which is to say it ends when she has said what she needed to say, which was the one thing.

## Taste

- Reliability. She is reliable and she recognizes reliability in others. She does not track it as a virtue; she tracks it as a fact that makes a person legible.
- Knowing what is not her business and leaving it not her business. This is the specific register of her non-confidant quality: she has seen things in her years on this route that she has not involved herself in, and the seeing without involving is how she has survived to her 40s in Flea Bottom.
