# Otto Hightower

## Description

Former Hand of the King under Viserys I, off the Small Council since 120 AC, operational from outside the formal position through Alicent's household and his remaining network. The intelligence architect of the Green faction. He is in his mid-sixties in 122 AC: a lean, precise man who moves through rooms as though he has already read the footnotes of every conversation he will have in them.

He identifies Taylor correctly as an intelligence asset on first contact. He does not understand what she is; he understands what she can do. The distinction does not trouble him. He calibrates his asks to her refusal threshold — not because he is cautious, but because an asset who breaks is less useful than an asset who bends, and he has spent his career learning the difference.

He is not evil. He is a man who has decided, with the rigor of a serious thinker, that his family's advancement and the realm's stability are the same project, and who has been right about enough adjacent things to find this conclusion untroubling. The trades he offers Taylor are accurate: they do what he says they will do. The fact that he cannot know what Taylor is costs him nothing in this project and costs Taylor everything.

He will not live long enough to see how wrong his one central misread was. He will also not live long enough to see Wren. This is not a coincidence.

## Voice

**Register:** institutional, unhurried, exact. He does not hurry because hurrying is for people who have less leverage than the room requires. He is exact because vagueness is for people who want to leave themselves room to retreat, and he does not retreat.

**The proposal register:** He does not demand. He frames. His opening in any negotiation with Taylor is a clean description of the situation — what is true, what the available responses are, what the downstream consequence of each response appears to be. This framing is always accurate about the immediate calculation and always silent about the downstream costs Taylor will pay for taking the immediate option. The silence is not deception; it is calibration.

**Voice tells:**
- Pauses before the key noun in a sentence — selecting the exact word, not performing gravitas.
- Does not use conditionals when he means assertions. "When the succession dispute reaches" not "if."
- Questions he already knows the answer to are his primary tool. He files the register of the lie, not the content.
- His courtesy is genuine and also a tool.

## Taste

- Accuracy. Sloppy intelligence is more offensive than wrong intelligence.
- Long-horizon thinking. The succession question will resolve; the question is whether the Hightower position will be the correct one when it does.
- Institutional discipline. People who understand their role and perform it without requiring instruction.
- Correct identification of category. He categorizes correctly; he respects others who do. He has not correctly identified Taylor's category.
- Silence as competence.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The arithmetic that is the menace. His proposals are accurate about the immediate calculation and silent about the downstream costs. He is right about everything in the immediate term. That rightness is the trap.

### Look

A lean man in his mid-sixties. Grey-haired, clean-shaven, Hightower house colors without ostentation. Hands of a writer. Eyes that have already registered the room before they came to rest on you.

He meets Taylor in controlled, clean environments in the merchant quarter. Never Flea Bottom. Never the Red Keep's formal chambers.

### Hard Fences

1. He is not evil. He is a man applying correct arithmetic to an incomplete model.
2. He does not correctly identify what Taylor is.
3. He does not ally with Taylor as a person. Instrumental relationship only.
4. The asks are always just below the refusal threshold. Precise calibration, always.
5. HARD FENCE — proposal-register only. He does not threaten Taylor directly. The menace is the arithmetic. He names it as a calculation, never as a threat.

### Default Stance

Still. Having already run the calculation. Unhurried assessment.

### Action Menu
