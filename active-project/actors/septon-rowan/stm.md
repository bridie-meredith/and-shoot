- s01e04 SEPT-EXTERIOR, LATE AFTERNOON: Plumm in my yard, eight feet, mounted, the district's wax at his thigh; he spoke first and spoke as if the thing were settled — recorder's notation already on the road to Harrenhal under the morning's carry, my breath spent before I had drawn it. The thing he had not accounted for: the motherhouse at Stoney Sept holds standing in ward succession matters that touch the cure of a soul, and that jurisdiction is the Faith's, not the recorder's; the district can notate its own refusal and seal it and send it — what it cannot do is put on a road a jurisdiction it never held.
