- s01e04 B40-43 (EASTERN TRACK, RETURN): Sent to the motherhouse at Stoney Sept today — committed aloud on the track. Named nine days. Taylor said "Twelve." without looking at me. I asked how she knew. She named the river road west off the Kingsroad, the cut past Harroway, the fords at Stone Hedge and the Mander branch — fords running high off spring melt, verge not graded since autumn rains took it. The count: twelve days, named by the roads and the season. She did not slow for the answer. She has been mapping the road to the motherhouse. The folio is under my arm, the hall's refusal inside it. We are approaching the settlement.
- s01e04 BULLET 37: At the dispatch-side ledger stand, lifting the folio off the wood, the open page came into the line of sight. Saw my own name in the refusal entry three lines down — expected. Saw three lines above it, in the same ink on the same page, the inspection entry I did not write, with my name in the margin beside the raven-sketch as the cited connection. The chain assembled on a single page. The hall knew what it was looking at before I presented anything. Lifted the folio anyway. Turned for the door. The document is now in my hands and going out of the hall under my arm — the hall's record of its own refusal, traveling.
- s01e04 BULLET 14: Taylor told me — two sentences, flat: Osmynd is dead, checked twice before first light. Then immediately: Plumm's man on circuit, clock starts at the district recorder's filing, not at death, the count of days against the wardship is already running. I knew then: Osmynd is dead AND the succession mechanism is already in motion. The window was live.
- s01e04 BULLET 12: Pre-dawn, guest-lodge doorway. Track empty as memorized, then: shape from sept direction at controlled walk — not village road, not fields. Read the body: right arm held closer to ribs (something beneath the cloth), hour wrong for any errand I knew. My foot found the step down before the reading finished — both feet in the yard, doorway empty at my back. The crease between my brows. Did not call out. Stepped toward her, committed.
