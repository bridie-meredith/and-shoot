## Project Entry State

Septon Rowan is in his village sept when the story opens. He has heard two weeks of increasingly specific dragon rumors — not the usual vague "there's something in the hills" but "I saw it, it was the size of a horse, it did not attack me when it could have." He is currently constructing his theological response. He has not yet left the village to investigate. He is about to.
