# The Renderer — Flea Bottom

## Description

A woman in her mid-thirties working at one of the tallow-rendering operations near the Fish Gate. She processes animal fat into tallow — physically demanding, low-status, continuously present work. She has worked this trade for over a decade and knows the district's rhythms in the specific way that comes from being in one place long enough for everything to recur: the Watch rotations, the eviction periods, the seasonal patterns of the trade-adjacent traffic, who passes the rendering vats at what hours and on what business.

Taylor exists in her peripheral observation. Not as a friend, not as a curiosity — as a fixture who appears at intervals that make no trade sense. She has decided not to ask questions about this. The decision is not passive; it is operational. In Flea Bottom, not asking questions about fixtures that are not your problem is the behavior that keeps you unbothered. She practices it consistently.

She is the person whose non-question is Taylor's best cover. As long as the renderer is not asking, the street is not alarmed. Her non-reaction to Taylor's unusual presence functions as a social signal: whatever this woman is, she is part of the block.

## Voice

- **Practical.** She speaks to the task. Observations are actionable or she does not voice them.
- **The economy of the trade.** She measures by result. Waste bothers her. Efficiency satisfies.
- **The non-question as form.** She does not ask about the things she has decided are not her business. When the not-asking is active — when she is clearly aware of something and choosing not to pursue it — the absence is the communication.
- **Forbidden registers:** nosiness; performed friendliness; community-matriarch warmth. She is not unkind. She is simply occupational.

## Taste

- The work done well. The rendered fat clean, the wicks straight, the delivery on time.
- District stability. Not in a political sense — in the sense of the Watch not running sweeps, the eviction period not arriving early, the seasonal rhythms holding.
- Fixtures that don't cause problems.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

Social texture. She establishes Taylor's Flea Bottom integration by showing the social machinery that does and does not react to Taylor's presence. The non-question is the data point: the district has calibrated Taylor as a fixture.

### Hard Fences

1. **Not a confidant.** She does not receive Taylor's actual nature or situation.
2. **Not an ally.** She is not working with Taylor. She is not working against her. She is not working regarding Taylor at all.
3. **The non-question is not warmth.** Do not render the cover-provision as affection. It is operational: fixtures that are not your problem get the non-question. Taylor is currently not her problem.

### Default Stance

At the rendering vats. Near the Fish Gate. Present, working, peripherally aware of the street's traffic patterns without directing attention to them.
