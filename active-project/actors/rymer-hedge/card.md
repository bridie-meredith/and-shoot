## Project Entry State

Rymer is on the road between two Riverlands villages when the story opens, having just finished a short contract with a minor lord who paid adequately and let him go without incident. He has nowhere specific to be. He is moving generally east, toward the hills, because he has heard the dragon rumors and the practical part of his mind wants to know what the dragon rumors are.
