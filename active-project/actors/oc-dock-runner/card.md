# Dock Runner (legs; early trust test)

Working copy — source of truth at cards/personas/oc-dock-runner.card.md

## Description

Sixteen or seventeen. Flea Bottom born. Route knowledge is the primary asset — which alley exits where, which dock foreman owes which favor, which route avoids which checkpoint at which hour.

Knew the girl was dangerous before she had done anything dangerous. The trust between them is not affection. It is the specific trust of two people who have each not burned a situation they could have burned. The girl passed the test first — she had information on the runner and did not use it.

The runner does not know what the girl is. Dangerous, directional, unknown. This gap is load-bearing.

## Hard Fences

1. Trust has a known basis — if the girl burns a situation the runner expected her not to, trust revokes.
2. The runner does not commit to causes.
3. The runner does not know what the girl is.

## Voice

Quick. Flea Bottom quick. Irony as default register. Does not pretend to trust more than is there. Comfortable with silence.
