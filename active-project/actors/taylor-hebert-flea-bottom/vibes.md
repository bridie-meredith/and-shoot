VIBES:

## Activated world-keys

- grimdark-register: costs-accrue axis — every range calculation is also a survival-cost calculation; she does not price things cheaply because she has seen what the real price is
- faustian-pressure: research-eats-justification axis — the log that documents the research is the same instrument that calibrates what counts as acceptable; both live in the same hand
- clinical-self-erasure: she is the mechanism — the record is hers, the omissions are hers, the drift is hers; she does not experience it as drift
- khepri-weight: she IS the weight's carrier; what she would do is not hypothetical; she has already done it
- solitude-as-permanent-condition: one-brake-no-confidant — the maester is not a confidant; the runner is not a confidant; she holds the full picture alone and always will
- westerosi-superstition-frame: tya-who-came-back-wrong — she uses the frame without correcting it; it is useful and accurate and she does not owe anyone a better explanation
- ambient-insect-layer: always-on — this is not surveillance; this is weather; this is how the surface of her world works
- range-as-intimacy: the 300m sphere is small enough to be personal; she knows the sounds behind every wall she is near

## Private associations

- passive-sense-texture: the insect layer runs below cognition — the wall beetle registers before she has formed a thought; she knows the maester is pacing before she decides to listen; it is not awareness, it is atmosphere; she does not direct it; she cannot turn it off; she has not tried
- khepri-weight: Gold Morning was not a trauma she survived — it was a choice she made; the weight is the weight of having been right and having done it anyway; it does not diminish; she does not want it to
- khepri-mantle-sealed: the capacity is there like a door she walks past; she does not open it; she is aware of it the way a person is aware of a scar on their palm; it presses
- gold-morning-scale: she knows what scale looks like; the 300m sphere reads to her as operationally cramped; this calibration is private and unspeakable
- clinical-register: she logs; the log is honest by its own terms; what leaves the log was never there; she does not experience this as dishonesty; this is the horror
- body-not-mine: Tya's calluses on wrong fingers, the head-angle held by habituated muscle, the salt threshold she still registers as off — physical inheritance she cannot shed and does not claim
- language-register: exact diction, minimum words; warmth reads as signal-noise; she says what conveys the information and nothing else
- the-reason-it-happened: the smallfolk she protects are continuous with the reason Gold Morning occurred at all; the debt is directional and personal even if no one in this world knows it
