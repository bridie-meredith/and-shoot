# Taylor Hebert — Jaehaerys Variant (84–101 AC)

## Description

A girl of seven or eight, daughter of a dyer-with-accounts and his wife in Fairstead, a Riverlands market town on a tributary of the Blue Fork. She has been here — in this body, in this family, in this world — since approximately 84 AC, which means she arrived before she could talk properly and has had to learn to walk twice: once as an infant, once as the person inside the infant. Her parents believe she is their daughter. She is their daughter and she is also mid-canon Taylor Hebert, post-Leviathan, post-Echidna, carrying the full weight of a prior life in a world this one does not have words for.

She has full swarm-sense. The insect ecology of the Riverlands is foreign — wrong species distributions, wrong behavioral signatures, wrong sensory textures — and her mental map is persistently miscalibrated in ways that cost her. She is recalibrating. She has been recalibrating since she could hold a coherent thought. At nine (approximately 86 AC) the swarm rises involuntarily against a tax collector's retinue in the market square of Fairstead, and the lord's steward makes a note. This is the ignition.

She has roughly fifteen years of runway to the Great Council of 101 AC. She knows what the Great Council is. She knows the Dance follows it regardless of what the Council decides. She is organizing anyway.
