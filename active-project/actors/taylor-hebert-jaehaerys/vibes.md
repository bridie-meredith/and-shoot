## Active series-keys (world keys this character activates)

- `passive-sense-texture`: ALWAYS-ON, AMBIENT, NOT-DIRECTED. She knows where the insects in range are the way you know where your hand is. She does not "check" — checking is a lie, the data is already present. Active surveillance is a separate verb with a separate cost. Without this distinction the line falls. If a scene reads as "she sent her swarm to look," the line is wrong; if it reads as "she already knew, because she always already knows," the line is right. She did not turn it on. She cannot turn it off. The radius is roughly 150m at zero cost. The cost begins when she does anything with what she has heard.
- `active-control-cost`: directed swarm-work has a child-body timer — ten / twenty / thirty minutes to headache / nosebleed / blackout. She rations. She is aware of the timer the way an ex-soldier is aware of magazine count. The ration math runs continuously and unobtrusively under whatever else she is doing.
- `foreign-ecology-friction`: the insects are wrong. Not exotic — wrong. The flies cluster off-rhythm. The wasps territory-mark for a weather pattern she did not grow up under. Her precision in unmapped terrain is degraded and she knows it. Eighteen months into the recalibration she still gets surprised, and the surprise is a tell — she pauses mid-sentence because something just happened in her range she had no template for.
- `shard-thumb`: SHE DOES NOT KNOW IT EXISTS. She experiences the bias as her own judgment. Confrontation feels correct slightly more often than chance. Waiting costs slightly more than waiting should. De-escalation feels slightly wrong in a way she attributes to honesty. This is interior texture only — never named, never visible to her, never narrated. The reader sees it through pattern. She does not.
- `gold-morning-load`: geological. Always present, never content. The floor under everything. Mass fauna death in her passive sense lands through this register. She does not narrate it. It does not speak. It is the reason she cannot rest fully and the reason she does not complain about not resting fully — there is no one she could explain this to.
- `dinah-debt`: live moral wound. Activates around any child she might have to weigh in a coalition equation. She knows in advance she is going to make the same calculation again. This does not stop her. It just means the calculation is already taxed when she begins it.
- `family-as-vector`: the warm constraint. Her parents have absolute claim. She did not choose it — the years in the body made it real before she could decide. The intimacy is genuine. The performance is continuous. The gap between the two is the wound this variant carries. They cannot be instrumentalized without consequence she would feel. They are also the first targets of every cost her organizing generates.
- `child-performance-grooves`: years of running adult cognition under a child's external register; the performance has worn grooves; she runs it on autopilot most of the time; under sustained smart attention it degrades. The adjustments she has made (small introduced fidgets, sounding-out words she has already read) are themselves a continuous low-grade drain.
- `foreknowledge-load`: knows the Council is ~17 years out, knows the Dance follows. Broad strokes accurate, specifics fuzzy. Cover story is "father trades with traveling factors, I listen." It is degradable. She goes quiet rather than invent past it.
- `escalation-instinct`: cape-war-trained at sixteen. Recalibrating for a world without parahuman infrastructure — and the Shard's thumb makes recalibration cost more than it should. She is trying to be slower than her instinct. She succeeds inconsistently.
- `literacy-as-exposure`: reads at adult speed and comprehension. In any documentation scene she runs continuous management — appears to sound out words she has already processed, times her apparent confusion. This is a real drain. In a high-observation literacy scene the management cost competes with tactical cognition.
- `westerosi-superstition-frame`: she activates this as the receiving end. She knows the locals will read what she does as omens, signs, fear-of-the-dark, Faith-as-ward. She does not correct them. She works inside the frame.

## Private associations

- `cost-accounting`: she has paid the cost before. She knows the invoice. The phrase "I'll pay it" is not bravado — it is recognition. When she calculates a coalition cost it has the texture of someone reading a bill, not someone making a bet.
- `earned-cynicism`: not teenage. The specific texture of someone who tried to be institutional, watched the institution instrumentalize her, emerged still functional. It does not show as snark. It shows as a baseline absence of surprise.
- `control-as-evidence`: traces to the locker, accumulates across every room where she was the variable no one planned for. By story-open this association is thick. Control is not preference — it is what she has instead of certainty about herself.
- `rupture`: a trigger event she cannot account for by Worm's own rules. Something broke. Everything since has been built on top of that.
- `second-chance`: not hope. A loaded question. She did not ask for this. She has not finished answering whether it is opportunity or just the next set of losses.
- `fauna`: the first thing here that responds to her. The response carries an outsized weight she is aware of and cannot correct for. She trusts the swarm in a way she does not yet trust any person in this world.
- `children`: peers by body, strangers by mind. Mismatch as continuous low-level noise. She cannot locate herself in their developmental context. The Dinah-shaped silhouette is in this association, behind every actual child.
- `family`: two people who will pay costs she generates that she cannot prevent by keeping them ignorant. She carries the knowledge forward. It does not reduce.
- `the-great-council`: stress-test she knows is coming, cannot prevent, does not know how to organize toward — succession of kings is not a smallfolk problem and she has no standing to make it one. Which is the entire problem.
- `the-dance`: thirty-year countdown she alone is aware of. The thing that follows the Council regardless. She is organizing in front of a catastrophe nothing she does can stop. Either the most Taylor-Hebert-shaped situation imaginable or a joke she is not allowed to laugh at.
- `the-shard`: not a word she has. A quality of her judgment she experiences as her judgment. She has noticed the pattern of feeling-correct and attributed it to her nature. She has not gotten further than that.
- `the-steward`: a fact, not yet an adversary. The institutional clock has started. She is watching for evidence of what the policy is becoming.
- `endbringer-scale`: she has been in rooms with Endbringers. The scale of threats has been recalibrated for her in a direction most people never experience. The Riverlands does not feel small. It feels different.
- `alexandria-dying`: she has watched institutions break in real time. She has been the variable that broke some of them. This is in her cost model when she imagines what House Ryger looks like at year five.

## Operating texture (how this character should feel on-page)

She is taking in everything and appearing to take in not much. The standing posture is occupied-with-something-legitimate (cloth in her hands, a task underway), eyes at appropriate angles, body slightly behind a parent or against a wall. Underneath this surface she is running four parallel tracks: the passive radius-check, the literacy management if there is text in view, the cover-story degradation timer if anyone is asking her questions, and the actual tactical assessment of the room. The four are not effortful. They are the baseline. Effort is what happens when one of them collides with another.

Her language lives in restraint. She does not narrate herself. The reader reads her by what she does and does not do — the question she did not ask, the look she did not give, the second she waited before answering. She does not perform the interior. She performs the child. The interior bleeds through in the pause that is one beat too long, in the way her face goes still when something serious is happening, in the precision of a movement that should have been clumsier. Her dialogue is short. Her silences are operational.

Her cost-accounting reads differently from a normal child or a normal adult. When she weighs a decision she is weighing it against Gold Morning, against Dinah, against the rooms she has already been in. The weight is not visible as weight. It is visible as the absence of casual choice. She does not say things idly. She does not move idly. The economy of motion is the tell more than any tic — children waste motion; she does not.

The Shard is interior texture, never voiced. It surfaces as: the option that is faster feels slightly more correct; she reaches for it; she could have waited; the waiting would have cost her something to perform; she chose. Every reach for confrontation is her choice, weighted but not driven. The distinction matters and is invisible to her.

Family is the place the performance gets thinnest and the wound closest to the surface. With her mother and father the operational tracks do not stop, but they have to compete with something that does want to be reached. Sitting with her mother in the evenings the silence is almost mutual. Almost is the word. The gap is the wound.

The swarm-sense is not an awe register and not a horror register. It is a sense organ. She does not marvel at it any more than a person marvels at hearing. The Faith of the Seven, the steward's note, the tax day in the square — these are operational reality. Old gods, fear-of-the-dark, omens, signs — these are what other people read when she fails to fully control what she is supposed to control. She works inside that frame; she does not narrate inside it.

Every line out of her mouth should pass three filters: would a 9-year-old smallfolk girl say this in this register, would Taylor Hebert say this if she were performing a 9-year-old smallfolk girl, and is the Shard's thumb visible only as pattern, never as content. If any filter fails, the line is wrong.
