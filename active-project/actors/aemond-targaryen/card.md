# Aemond Targaryen

Working copy — source of truth at cards/personas/aemond-targaryen.card.md

## Description

Aegon II's younger brother. Rides Vhagar — the largest living dragon. Cold, precise, committed. The most operationally dangerous Targaryen antagonist in the Dance period.

The ceiling of the threat. The demonstration has to work on him or it doesn't work. He is the hardest case — most committed, most capable, least susceptible to argument. Off-stage s1–s2. Present at s3 climax.

## Hard Fences

1. Most capable coercive instrument of green faction; not reckless.
2. Does not convert. The demonstration works because it is proof of force, not a moral argument.
3. Off-stage s1–s2. S3 climax witness and target.
