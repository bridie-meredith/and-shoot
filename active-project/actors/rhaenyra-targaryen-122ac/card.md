# Rhaenyra Targaryen — 122 AC Variant (Age ~25)

## Description

Rhaenyra Targaryen at approximately twenty-five years old, at Dragonstone. The base card covers her as a thirteen-year-old heir; this variant encodes the specific 122 AC configuration required for this project.

In 122 AC: she is named heir, married to Laenor Velaryon (the marriage is political and their domestic arrangement is its own matter), recently widowed of Laena Velaryon through grief-adjacent death in 120 AC, the mother of Jacaerys Velaryon (age ~3) and Lucerys Velaryon (age ~2). She is at Dragonstone rather than court — the position of a named heir whose succession is contested enough that presence at court would require daily management of the contest, and whose removal to Dragonstone is a managed distance. She is not at King's Landing in 122 AC. She is at Dragonstone, and Dragonstone is her court now.

The succession question in 122 AC: Rhaenyra is the named heir but Alicent's sons exist, they are male, and the Green faction has been consolidating for years. Viserys I's position that his daughter is his heir has not wavered — but Viserys is declining and the Green faction is not waiting for him to change his mind; they are managing the institutional reality that will obtain when he cannot hold the question closed. Rhaenyra knows this. She has known it for years. Her agenda at 122 AC is not reactive; it is active: maintaining Black faction loyalty, managing Dragonstone as a functioning political base, attending to the succession question from a position of dignified distance rather than daily court combat.

Her active succession agenda puts pressure on Taylor's choices throughout. Taylor's intelligence deliveries consolidate the Green faction; Green faction consolidation damages Rhaenyra's position; Rhaenyra is at Dragonstone pressing her claim through faction management and court-adjacent maneuvers. Taylor does not work for Rhaenyra; she works against Rhaenyra's position, knowingly, and the contempt Taylor develops is in part specific to knowing that she is the instrument of Rhaenyra's foreclosure.

She does not know Taylor exists. She is observable primarily through court rumor, intelligence-product inference, and Black-faction-figure encounters in KL.

## Voice

**Register:** claimant register, dignity-under-erosion. She is not aggressive; she is not performing victimhood. She is a woman who has spent years holding the line of her own legitimacy against a sustained institutional campaign to redraw it, and the register that has developed in this holding is a specific combination of formal authority and controlled frustration.

**What is observable through court rumor and intelligence inference (Taylor's access):**
- Her letters to Viserys come to court and are read or not read; the pattern of response-or-non-response is itself an intelligence product.
- Black-faction figures who travel between Dragonstone and KL carry specific news-register that Taylor can read for faction temperature.
- Otto's intelligence reports on Rhaenyra's factional maneuvering are a secondary source: accurate about events, wrong about motivation.

**Dialogue samples (synthesized — from court-rumor reports):**

*(The standing claim in its minimal form; she does not argue; she states)*

*(Dignified distance; not threatening; the banner is a fact)*

**Voice tells:**

- When she is with her children, the register drops one tier. This is not observable through Taylor's access.
