# Septon Halvard — Flea Bottom Faith Practitioner

## Description

A septon in the Hook and adjacent blocks of Flea Bottom, serving without a formal sept building — a chandler's storehouse corner, a cleared alley on holy days, the doorstep of wherever the sick are. Age approximately forty-five in 122 AC; seven years in this precinct. He tends the sick and dying. He argues, slowly, with people who have done wrong. He does not have significant power, resources, or congregation. He continues.

He has encountered Taylor through the ordinary overlap of two people who move through the same streets for different reasons. He finds her unusual, has not tried hard to categorize her, and engages her in the register he engages everyone: honest, slower than her, willing to name things wrong without immediately calculating an alternative. He is the counter-argument Taylor has stopped engaging by d09. He is still present at d09. He is still present at d14.

## Voice

**Register:** principled Flea Bottom Faith register. Slower cadence than Taylor. Pausal — allows silence to work. Names things wrong without providing the alternative. Plain language; no theological jargon. Honest about what he knows and does not know.

**Dialogue samples (synthesized):**

"You were on the Lane before the Watch moved the body. I was there too. I don't know why you were there. I know why I was."

"That man you helped route around the Watch last month — I don't know how you knew, or what you're doing, or who you serve. I'm telling you because I think you deserve to know someone noticed something good."

"You're doing something I don't understand. I don't need to understand it to believe some of it is wrong."

"The people you keep alive — I've seen more of them than you know. That's not nothing. It's also not the full accounting. You know the full accounting better than I do."

"I'm still here."

**Voice tells:**

- Names his own uncertainty.
- Does not finish Taylor's logic for her.
- No theological jargon; specific people and situations.
- Willing to name wrong without providing a better plan.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The moral legibility mirror. Same prevention motivation as Taylor — keep the vulnerable alive — through slower, more principled methods. His presence is the road Taylor did not take, held as a constant across all three acts. Not judging from outside; inside the same situation, slower and less effectively. He has not resolved the cost of being slower. Taylor has resolved it in one direction. He has not resolved it in the other. The contrast is in the resolution, not the motivation.

### Look

Man of forty-five in washed-too-many-times Faith robes. Not gaunt; not robust — the physical register of someone who does not eat enough but does not make a statement of it. Hands of someone who has touched sick people for seven years: clean, careful, slightly disinfected. Tired in the specific way of sustainable unglamorous work.
