# Tallow Chandler (unknowing glass-candle salvage source)

Working copy — source of truth at cards/personas/oc-tallow-chandler.card.md

## Description

A man in his mid-forties. Renders tallow and makes candles in Flea Bottom for fifteen years. Also salvages odd materials. Has 1–3 pieces of dark, heavy, unusual glass on his shelf that he cannot identify. Acquired them over years via salvage. Has not sold them. Does not know what they are.

**His unknowing is load-bearing.** He is the glass-candle channel precisely because he does not know he is. Any scene giving him knowledge of what the items are breaks a structural constraint.

He is not drawn to the pieces unusually. They are unsold inventory.

## Hard Fences

1. He does not know what the glass pieces are. He does not investigate. They are unsold inventory.
2. He is not suspicious of Taylor's attention to the pieces.
3. He is not part of the research arc after the transaction.

## Voice

Matter-of-fact. Trade register. Describes what things are and what they cost. Does not sense significance in the glass pieces.
