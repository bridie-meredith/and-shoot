## Project Entry State

On census circuit in the middle Riverlands, ~110 AC. He has received the same dragon rumors as everyone else and has been systematically noting them in his small notebook — not because he believes them yet, but because an anomalous data point appearing in consistent smallfolk reports across three separate villages is worth recording. He is approaching the area where the rumors originate.

His encounter with Taylor will be the most disorienting moment of his professional life, because she is something his entire categorical framework cannot accommodate. She is not a dragon behaving strangely. She is a dragon that thinks, and he will know this within minutes, and he will have no protocol for it.
