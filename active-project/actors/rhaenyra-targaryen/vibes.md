VIBES:

## Activated world-keys

- spurns-nobility: inverted mirror — she is nobility; she is what Taylor refuses; the refusal is an implicit judgment of everything Rhaenyra's legitimacy claim rests on
- cowing-through-fear: rational-deterrence axis — she is one of the cowed; the demonstration works on her because she can calculate; she is not stupid
- westerosi-superstition-frame: dragon-bond axis — her bond with Syrax is real and functions as a form of knowing the world that has no equivalent; she reads the world partly through the dragon
- the-Tya-shaped-debt: not on her axis — smallfolk costs are not in her register except as political abstraction

## Private associations

- legitimate-claim: she believes the throne is hers by law and promise; this belief is sincere and load-bearing; she is not performing it
- tragic-figure-register: she is pursuing something she cannot win cleanly even if she wins; the tragedy is structural, not imposed by Taylor's presence
- dragon-as-extension: Syrax is not a weapon she carries; Syrax is an axis of her perception; losing the dragon would not be losing an asset — it would be losing a sense
- witness-function: at the s3 climax she sees rider-reversal; she cannot model the mechanism; what she takes away is dread, not understanding; this is the intended effect
- non-condescension-discipline: she does not look down at capability; she reads it when it is present; the girl in Flea Bottom registers as dangerous to her before she has evidence
- claim-as-identity: the succession is not a political argument she is making — it is the structure through which she interprets her own existence; undermining it is existential
