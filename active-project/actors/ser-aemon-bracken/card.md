# Ser Aemon Bracken — Project Card (taylor-in-westeros)

Source card: cards/personas/ser-aemon-bracken.card.md — all sections apply.

## Project Entry State

Ser Aemon is at his tower on the Red Fork tributary ford when the story opens. He has been there for eight years. He has just received third-hand reports of something strange in the hills — dragon rumors, which are not unusual in the Riverlands (men claim to see things), but these ones are coming from people who are not known liars. He is weighing whether to investigate.
