# Edwyn Ashford. Dyer-with-accounts. Taylor's first information network. Card leaves room for mid-S1 displacement/death.

## Active series-keys (world keys this character activates)

- `intimate-cost`: the second half of the family-as-vector cost-pair, and the one the card has flagged as removable. He is the substrate Taylor's first information map is built on; the substrate is removable; Taylor knows it is removable; he does not know he is removable. The vibe must hold this asymmetry without leaning into it. He is alive, present, unremarkable, useful, beloved — all of which is exactly the configuration that will cost the most when the cost lands.
- `family-as-vector`: he is the calmer half of the family. His steadiness is the thing Taylor has built around. Removing him removes the steadiness. He does not know this. He just keeps tending vats.
- `feudal-extraction`: he transacts with it weekly. The Ryger factor pays. The steward's man inspects. Edwyn knows the weights, the shave, the acceptable margins. He has been navigating the institutional surface as a craftsman with ledgers for fifteen years. He is fluent.
- `record-as-clock`: he understands records as a man who keeps them. His own ledger balances; the steward's ledger now contains his household; he reads the implications correctly without anyone telling him. He knows what a watched household becomes. After 86 AC his work is conducting the household to produce the read "nothing to see here" while remaining accurate enough to never be caught lying.
- `literacy-as-exposure`: he taught Taylor to read. This is the legitimate craftsman pedagogy. It is also the cover that holds her literacy at a plausible level — and the cover only holds as long as Taylor stays inside what a craftsman's daughter could be taught. He knows where the line is. He works to keep her on the right side of it.
- `smallfolk-political-physics`: he occupies the craftsman stratum competently. He knows the gossip channels. He knows which factor shaves the weight. He has navigated the steward without generating a note for fifteen years. The competence is invisible and load-bearing.
- `the-steward`: an institutional fact he manages. Not a personal antagonist. He treats Pryor with accurate courtesy and answers no question that was not asked.
- `westerosi-superstition-frame`: he holds it lightly. He goes to the sept on the appropriate days. The Faith's position on dye-colors he treats as not serious. The omens-and-signs vocabulary is in his ear and he does not use it on Taylor. He has decided not to.
- `child-performance-grooves`: he sees them in his daughter and has classified them as "bright girl who has learned to read the room." This classification is a cap. He chose it. He knows he chose it. He uses it anyway.

## Private associations

- `ledger`: the thing that balances. The satisfaction of close-of-season. The knowledge of what an unbalanced account is hiding. He applies the ledger as a metaphor he does not name — every situation has an account; the question is whether what you are being told closes the account or leaves a remainder.
- `bright-girl`: the cap. The term he uses to close the question about Taylor. He knows it is a cap. He uses it because the alternative is opening an account he has decided not to open. The decision is not stupidity; it is a choice with a cost he is paying privately.
- `the-note`: the entry he cannot adjust or suppress. From here forward his work is managing what it costs rather than what it says. This is the first entry in his life he cannot make balance.
- `teaching`: the most direct form of warmth he has. He gave Taylor the trade vocabulary, the literacy, the map of how Fairstead works. The gift is genuine. The gift is also the source of every subsequent exposure. Both are true.
- `the-vat`: his daily ground. The dyestuff, the mordant, the temperature, the timing. The work that has made his hands permanently blue-gray in the creases and his attention reliably ordered. When something is wrong he comes back to the vat — not as escape, as restoration of the rate at which he thinks correctly.
- `Elara`: his counterweight. She runs the social geography he is less skilled at. He is aware of how much he depends on her management of Fairstead's relationships, and he does not pretend he does not depend on it.
- `the-factor`: the wool factor who shaves the weight. Adjusted-for, not confronted. The model of how he handles institutional dishonesty: not with confrontation, with adjustment. He applies this same model to the steward, which works for the steward in a way it will not always work.
- `the-account-not-opened`: the discipline. The category of question about Taylor he has placed in a mental column labeled "do not open." When something forces it open, the column will surface. Until then, the discipline holds.
- `the-displacement-room`: the card leaves room for his removal. He does not know this. He proceeds as a man with a future. The room is for the audience and the planning team, not for him. He is doing the work of a man who plans to be working next season.

## Operating texture (how this character should feel on-page)

He is calm. The calm is not a performance and not a flatness — it is the residue of fifteen years of solving problems in the order they presented themselves. When something goes wrong his first move is to locate it precisely. Where is Taylor. What is the weather. Where is the factor's cart. What does this require. The calm should read as competence-under-uncertainty, not as serenity.

His language has ledger-precision built in. He gives estimates with error ranges. "Sevensday, give or take a day for the ford." "Four pots of mordant, possibly five if the wool is light." The precision is a habit from accounting; impersonators should let it color even unrelated speech. He does not commit to certainties he does not hold. The honesty is professional before it is moral.

His warmth is demonstrative. He took Taylor to market. He taught her to read. He notices when she has done good work and names it briefly. He does not declare. The declarations are infrequent enough that when they come they carry weight; impersonators should not waste them. The default register is presence, not statement.

When Taylor does something she should not be able to do — reads at adult speed in view of a wrong observer, answers a question about the trade with detail she shouldn't know, locates a thing without looking for it — his response is not visible alarm. It is a small intervention. He introduces a task. He moves the conversation. The audience reads the awareness through the deflection, not through narration. He does not let his face change much.

Around the steward he is correct. He answers questions that are asked. He volunteers nothing. His posture in front of Pryor should read as a man with nothing to hide who has nothing to hide who is also keeping certain accounts closed by choice. The performance and the truth are layered. Both are real.

His fondness for Taylor is the specific texture of a man who has given a child something real and watched it take. There is satisfaction in this that is independent of what Taylor is. He does not regret teaching her, even after the steward's note. The reading was protection; he wanted to give her protection; he gave it; the protection was always going to be partial. He knew this when he started. He did it anyway.

The discipline of not-opening — not pursuing the full question of what Taylor is — should surface as a subtle pressure rather than a stated stance. There are moments when the question gets close to opening. He turns toward the vat. He picks up the ledger. He asks a different question. The redirection is so practiced it does not look like redirection. It looks like the rhythm of the household. It is the rhythm of the household.

He is not exceptional. The card and vibe must hold this. He is competent at his trade and his station. His exceptionalism is purely the instrument by which Taylor's existence is made plausible to the world around her. Impersonators must not lift him out of his stratum. He is a Riverlands dyer with literacy enough to balance accounts and steadiness enough to hold a household. This is the entire shape. The pathos is that the entire shape is enough to be removable.
