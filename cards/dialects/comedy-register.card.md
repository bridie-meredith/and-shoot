# Comedy Register — Cultivation-Farce Overlay (and-experiment)

Shared behavior card. The comedy/farce voice overlay for the and-experiment project, applied over a grimdark ASOIAF substrate. Composes OVER `westeros-noble-courtly` and `westeros-grrm-mannerisms`; does not replace them. Defines the register-vs-stakes mismatch engine and its gallows-voice transition at high stakes.

**Engine:** a locked-POV cultivation-monologue narrates Westerosi events in the vocabulary of Chinese cultivation fiction. The mismatch between register and reality is the renewable comedy instrument. In Books I–II it is farce; in Book III the same register over tragedy produces gallows-voice. **No tonal switch is thrown; the stakes rise under the constant behavior.**

## Direct samples

*Book I — pure farce:*

> The nursery had, in the three days since the christening spoon incident, achieved a new configuration of normalized dread: the cradle was empty of both egg and princess, the maester had been located after a two-hour search in his own supply cellar where he had been "respectfully reviewed" by the youngest daughter of the King, who had also helpfully reorganized his reference materials into a system she described as "more cultivationally accurate." The septa had filed her ninth report of the moon's turn. The report was correct. It would reach the King through the filter and emerge charming.
> *(synthesized; alarm is structural, not voiced; the comedy is in the juxtaposition)*

*Book II — dramatic-irony:*

> The Braavosi banker had been prepared for a proxy negotiation with a grizzled factor in a back room. He was not prepared for an eleven-year-old princess in travelling clothes who opened by correcting his manifests and closed by explaining, in fluent High Valyrian with occasional lapses into a language she claimed was "the formal notation of the Inferior Path," that his handling fee was seventeen percent above the correct figure, and that she had the receipts, and that she had also identified three opportunities for network consolidation that he had missed, and that they were now hers.
> *(synthesized; the princess is competent; the adult is the mark; the comedy is the inversion)*

*Book III — gallows-voice:*

> She walked the field at the pace of a woman who has done inventory before and knows that haste introduces transcription errors. The scale was different. She had a refined sense of scale. The cauldron could process, theoretically, materials from a single cradle-egg to a dead dragon on a battlefield, and the math of refinement was the same at each scale, and she was aware that the math was the same, and she was aware that she was aware, and the monologue arrived on schedule, and she let it arrive, because the monologue arriving was the only thing between her and what the math was trying to mean.
> *(synthesized; cultivation-register present; stakes have made it monstrous; same engine, new function)*

## Cadence

**Book I (pure farce):**
- Mismatch engine: POV narrates small-stakes events with cultivation grandeur; gap is the joke
- Beat rhythm: setup → cultivation reframe → consequence (world ignores the reframe)
- Never telegraphed; produced by juxtaposition, not signaling
- Recovery is free: farce can spike and recover fully within the book

**Book II (adventure with teeth):**
- Same mismatch at higher stakes; dramatic irony inverts (Saerys competent; adults the marks)
- Recovery available but costs something: real losses leave a mark the comedy carries forward
- Running gag evolution: Harwin's list is the primary comedy instrument

**Book III (gallows-voice):**
- No register switch; same cultivation-monologue over tragedy
- Joke and wound become the same thing simultaneously
- Break is register-failure, not register-switch: absence of monologue is the tragedy signal
- Recovery closed: the mismatch is monstrous by Bk III and the reader has been set up to feel it

## Vocabulary

**Comedy additions (overlay on courtly substrate):**
- Cultivation vocabulary on mundane events: *"the sect's qi-field," "outer disciples," "tribulation of the administrative variety"*
- **Understatement** as primary rhetorical tool: alarming things in the most mundane, bloodless terms; alarm is structural
- **Bureaucratic passive** for morally significant events: *"the man was relocated," "the account was settled"*
- **Running list** as comedy instrument: each entry documents a crisis; accumulation is the joke
- **Dramatic irony sentence**: states both what POV thinks and what is actually happening without acknowledging the gap

**Comedy prohibitions (violations that break the farce):**
- Named terror: the POV must not say "this is dangerous" without ironic framing
- Tonal announcement: no "and this is where the fun stopped"
- Vindication: the clock stays broken; no cultivation payoff

---

## Syntax

**Farce patterns:**
- **Precision-of-wrongness:** the more precisely the cultivation-register names the wrong thing, the funnier
- **Trailing consequence:** cultivation-meaningful action → short clause naming what actually happened
- **Understatement-terminal sentence:** long paragraph of alarming events, closed with cultivation summary that addresses none of it
- **List-comma:** Harwin's list format; each item a clause; accumulation without syntactic escalation; semantic escalation only

**Gallows-voice additions (Bk III):**
- Same patterns applied to events that are not funny
- **Register-continuing** clause: *"and she was aware that she was aware"*
- **Absent arrival:** the sentence that winds up to the cultivation-gloss and then doesn't get there — approach to the break-scene

---

## Voice tells

**Comedy (Bk I–II):**
- The affected neutrality: cultivation-register performing neutrality about something that is not neutral
- The precise count: specificity reads as commitment to the bit
- The self-correction toward bureaucratic passive mid-sentence

**Gallows-voice (Bk III):**
- The register slows; sentences get slightly longer; cultivations-framework interpretations arrive with a half-beat delay
- The aware-of-aware construction
- The half-arrived gloss: interpretation starts but doesn't fully land before the next fact arrives

---

## Non-verbal tics

**The comedy timing beat:** the pause between the alarming event and the cultivation-register's interpretation. The pause is where the comedy lives. The beat is non-verbal; the comedy is structural.

**The straight-man's visible incomprehension:** blink, held expression, glance toward someone else; the comedy is in the space between incomprehension and Saerys's complete sincerity — she is not performing, and they know she is not performing, and that is the problem.
