# Saerys Targaryen — Behavior Card

Per-character behavior for the Heavenly Demon Cauldron-Belly. Inherits `westeros-noble-courtly` for baseline register; personal overlay is the cultivation-monologue register, which is the load-bearing layer. Composes with `comedy-register` for tone.

## Direct samples

> "In truth, this is merely the cauldron processing a minor resistance — the outer disciples would call it pain, but pain is what impurities feel as they are expelled. I shall record it in the tribulation log. The qi-flow returns in three days, perhaps four. Possibly a sennight if the impurity was grade-two. Note: do not eat the green jar again until the cauldron has stabilized."
> — *(synthesized; after eating something that disagreed with her; the monologue is bright and clinical throughout)*

A foundation sect's spiritual influence propagates across correctly-aligned nodes without the founder's direct intention — I have merely been a sufficiently correct node for the existing propagation to crystallize around. In truth, I did not build this. The dao built it, through me, because I held the correct posture. I am now going to sign a fourth bill to see if the propagation continues. This is research."

This is an overreach of administrative scope. I will be submitting a counter-proposal. ## Cadence

The cultivation-monologue register runs over the courtly substrate:

- **Long sentences with embedded cultivation-parentheticals.** The structure is courtly (subordinated, periodic), but content detours through cultivation-framework interpretations mid-clause.
- **Footnote-thought construction.** She interrupts herself with precise qualifications, drafting as she speaks: *"The factor — the Braavosi one; not the one from Lys who had the misunderstanding about the manifest — has confirmed—"*
- **Flat affect on shocking content.** The monologue does not distinguish between register-appropriate and register-inappropriate information. She narrates a poisoning in the same voice as a successful synthesis.
- **The aside-to-posterity.** She speaks as though dictating for a later reader who will benefit from the methodology.
- **Pacing under pressure: the monologue accelerates.** Where courtly register tightens, hers adds more sub-clauses. More cosmological processing power.
- **The break:** Bk III, one scene only. The monologue fails to arrive.

## Vocabulary

**Reaches for:**
- Cultivation vocabulary: *qi, realm, cauldron, tempering, tribulation, merit, impurity, refining, the dao, the Inferior Path, foundation sect, outer disciple, breakthrough, spirit-beast, stage, tier, grade*
- Self-titling, unironic: *"I, the Heavenly Demon Cauldron-Belly," "one who walks the Inferior Path"*
- Accounting vocabulary bled into affect: *cost, entry, balance, net, closed account, the ledger shows*
- Logistics vocabulary: *factor, bill, manifest, license, network*
- GRRM-standard time markers
- Court vocabulary as bureaucratic instrument, not social grace
- Chemistry vocabulary treated as reagent names, not dangers

**Refuses to say:**
- "I was afraid" — files as "the cauldron registering a hazard-state"
- "I miss —" — loss is filed as a closed account
- "I don't know" — always has a cultivation-framework explanation
- "I killed him" — she addressed an impurity
- Faction-politics vocabulary in correct usage

## Syntax

- **Parenthetical inset structure.** Footnote-like insets inline: *"The reagent — which the maester has labelled 'do not touch under any circumstances,' a classification I have noted and set aside as institutional overcaution — is in fact a grade-two tempering compound."*
- **The double-read pattern.** Cultivation interpretation first, then (sometimes) the mechanical read.
- **Compound-sentence serial reasoning.** Steps laid out as subordinate clauses.
- **Aphorism from the wrong source.** She quotes cultivation-novel wisdom where a Westerosi would quote Faith proverbs, delivered with the same unironic authority as house words.

---

## Voice tells

- Interest: precise numbered questions (*"First: is the compound water-soluble? Second—"*)
- Discomfort: adds a cultivation sub-clause that reinterprets it as spiritually meaningful
- In trouble: the monologue gets longer, not shorter
- When moved (Daenys, the laundress's boy): the cultivation-gloss arrives a beat late and runs thin
- Doesn't want to be seen: makes herself very busy and very technical
- When her argument is weak (Daenys on cope): counter-arguments arrive too fast, with too many sub-clauses

## Non-verbal tics

- **Constant minor consumption.** Always eating something; often something she should not be.
- **The account-book.** Brings it everywhere. Other people bring embroidery.
- **Still-room smell.** A compound of dried herbs, chemical residue, lamp oil, and recent reagents.
- **Positional economy.** Always knows where the door is, where the watcher is, which corner is least observed.
- **The pause before the aside-to-posterity.** Brief; decides whether this warrants notation; usually yes.
- **The list-making gesture.** Fingers number options against her palm; invisible in public, visible in the still-room.
- **No hugging.** Accepts formal contact; initiates none. Physical space is administered.

## Memory monuments

- **The previous life (unnamed):** she died once. The cultivation-register is the monument. Every "I have already survived this" construction is built on this.
- **The cradle-egg:** the founding myth. A dead thing becomes fuel. The one-gesture the curdle replays at national scale.
- **The laundress's boy:** the one account she did not balance — because he was not a reagent. She files it as merit and has not examined the classification.
- **The first pest:** the meddling under-septa. The ladder's first rung. Account closed. Not reviewed.
- **Ser Harwin's list:** the running catalog of prohibited items. A comedy of the cauldron. How she knows she was loved.
- **Daenys's counter-thesis:** "the dying worth watching." Saerys has engaged with this at full register for all of Bk II. She does not have a good counter. She does not say so.
