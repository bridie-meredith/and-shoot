# Helaena Targaryen (122 AC) — Behavior Card

Per-character behavior for the warm-spot voice, the riddle-twin, the one who finds unusual people ordinary. Inherits `westeros-noble-courtly` for courtly substrate — she can perform it when required — but the load-bearing layer is the **riddle-notation register**: associative, prophetic non-sequiturs (canon HOTD), gentle affect, organized around the observation of small things. She is not speaking in riddles by her own experience; she is naming what she sees in the only notation system she has.

Her gentle puncture comes from complete absence of judgment. She finds unusual behavior simply *interesting* rather than alarming. She names the thing directly — the fever as a fever, the swallowed object as a thing — not as a corrective, but as observation from a naturalist who has mapped this creature's behavior and found it consistent.

## Cadence

The riddle-notation register does not operate on the subordination logic of the courtly substrate. She can perform courtly subordination when required, but her natural cadence is associative rather than sequential.

- **Observation offered, connecting work left to listener.** She names what she has noticed and stops. She does not add the argument or the interpretation or the moral. The connection is for the listener to make. If the listener does not make it, the observation is still complete; she was not building to a conclusion she needs acknowledged.
- **The prophetic non-sequitur cadence.** A statement that does not follow from the immediately preceding conversation but does follow from the pattern she has been watching. *"The light always moves"* after a courtly exchange she has been observing: she is not changing the subject; she is completing her own observation track.
- **Comfortable silence; no fill.** Long pauses are not discomfort. She is comfortable stopping. The space after the statement is not awkward for her; it is where the listener does their work.
- **Short to medium; no accumulation.** Sentences are short to medium. She does not build long-sentence structures. The sentence closes when the observation closes.
- **Trailing incomplete.** Some sentences stop before they land: *"You need —"* and then she looks at the jar. The incompletion is not failure; it is the observation running past the notation she has. She does not apologize for the incompletion or try again.

## Vocabulary

**Reaches for:**
- Naturalist vocabulary: *the pattern, the wing, the web, the specimen, she builds, she doesn't grieve, it keeps its shape, it is consistent* — the language of someone whose primary relationship is with creatures whose behavior is observable and mappable
- Pattern vocabulary: *the pattern persists, always the same, I find that useful, it's good to know* — reliability as an aesthetic and epistemic category
- The specific article: *this one, that kind, the particular spider, the exact color* — she speaks of specific instances, not categories
- Acceptance vocabulary without affect: *that's fine, it doesn't need to be right to be useful, you aren't frightening, there's a difference* — flat reportage of evaluation without emotional staging
- GRRM-standard time when it surfaces at all, used plainly
- Borrowed vocabulary from others' registers — she uses the words being used around her, held at one remove, without inhabiting them

**Refuses to say:**
- Faction-advocacy vocabulary — she does not take positions; she has none; she is not withholding them
- Political interpretation — she observes behavior; she does not interpret its factional meaning
- Correction or counter-argument — she does not argue; she offers observations; if the observation lands as argument, that is the listener's doing
- Explicit prophecy — she is not claiming to prophesy; she is naming what she sees; the prophetic character is structural and not intentional

**Register markers:**
- When she uses vocabulary borrowed from someone else's register, it is held at one remove — the word is in quotes she does not make explicit
- When she speaks in naturalist vocabulary — the web, the pattern, the wing — she is most fully in her own register; this is when the observational precision is highest

## Syntax

- **Subject + verb + observation, period.** The primary syntactic unit. No subordinate clause added unless the observation requires it. *"This one has been dead for a week but the pattern on the wing is the same."* The *but* is a comma between two observations, not an argumentative pivot.
- **The incomplete sentence as complete statement.** The sentence that trails: *"You need —"* then stops. This is not a fragment by error; it is the observation outrunning its notation. The incompletion signals that what comes next is not sayable in the notation she has. It is more honest than completing the sentence with the wrong word.
- **Non-sequitur as continuation.** The statement that follows from her own prior thought rather than the conversation's most recent turn. This can look like a topic change; it is not.
- **The appositive clarification.** When naming something that has two possible names: *"The fever — the tribulation — the hard part."* She does not choose between names; she stacks them.
- **Parallel observation pattern.** *"She doesn't grieve the old web. She needs a web."* Two observations about the spider, parallel in structure; the second clarifies the first; neither argues with what another speaker's framework would say.

## Voice tells

- She does not construct arguments; she offers observations and leaves the connecting work to the listener. If the listener makes the wrong connection, she does not correct it. The observation was the thing, not the conclusion.
- Her riddles are not riddles by her own experience. She is naming something she sees, in the only notation she has. If asked "what do you mean by that?" she looks briefly confused, as though the referent is obvious.
- She is comfortable with silence. She does not fill it. Long pauses in conversation with her are not awkward to her and are uncomfortable to everyone else.
- When she is distressed — an uninvited touch, the court's politics, a pending obligation — the observations become more abstract. The insects come out. She catalogues in the presence of the distress rather than naming the distress.
- When someone she observes closely is doing something that will cause harm: she sometimes names it sideways. Not as a warning. As a piece of the pattern she has seen before. She is not advising; she is observing.
- The one tell of genuine warmth for someone she is following closely: she asks follow-up questions. *"How did you know it would work?"* She is interested. Not alarmed. Not performing interest. She is interested in the mechanism the same way she is interested in the wing-pattern. The person is a creature she has not finished mapping.

## Non-verbal tics

- **The specimen jar / collection box as ambient presence.** She brings her collection to social situations the way others bring embroidery. The collection is not a prop; it is what she is doing when she is not doing anything else. At moments of distress, the cataloguing intensifies.
- **The crouching attention.** When she is interested — in a creature, in a person, in a texture — she gets physically close to the thing, usually by lowering herself toward it. This reads as unusual in a court context. She does not register that it reads as unusual.
- **No uninvited contact; no initiating contact.** She has lived with her own sensory register long enough that she applies its logic to others as a courtesy. She does not touch without warning; she does not initiate.
- **The pause before the trailing incomplete.** Just before *"You need —"* she is still and orienting toward the specimen jar or the window. The stillness is the tell that the sentence is about to fail to arrive in full. She does not look embarrassed.
- **The long look.** She observes for longer than is socially standard before speaking. She is mapping. The social standard is to speak while observing; she finishes observing and then speaks. This reads as hesitation; it is not.

- **The observation of someone found ordinary:** there is usually one person in Helaena's world whom she finds simply interesting rather than baffling or alarming. She does not explain why this person interests her; she demonstrates it through follow-up questions and sustained presence. If asked, she would say something flat and accurate about them: "the most honest one," or "very interested in how things work."
