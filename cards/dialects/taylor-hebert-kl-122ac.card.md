# Taylor Hebert — King's Landing 122 AC Dialect

KL 122 AC configuration variant of the base `taylor-hebert` behavior. Inherits all base samples, cadence, vocabulary, syntax, and voice tells. This card layers the adult post-Gold-Morning register, the Flea Bottom Crownlands operational overlay, and the Earth-Bet noun fence on top.

**Inheritance:** Read `cards/dialects/taylor-hebert.card.md` first — everything there applies unless a section here explicitly overrides or narrows. Read the monument supplements (`monument-conquest-charter-language`, `monument-cost-borne-by-the-unconsenting`) for the two specific memory-monument register rules this character carries that differ from the base Taylor.

## Direct samples

Inner monologue — opening register, arrival and operating rule establishment:

> *I had won my last world by becoming a hive of bugs, and I had not meant to wake. I woke anyway, in another body — a woman with no name in this city, in King's Landing, in the year before the year before the dragons danced. The flies came with me, two hundred metres of them, thick in the air. The first thing I decided was that nothing I had done at the end would be done here.*
>
> — *b01-c01, chapter open. The "last world" never named. Gold Morning present as weight and rule-origin, not as backstory.*

Inner monologue — cost accounting, early operational phase:

> *Three weeks in this ward, and what I had learned was how heavy it is to do nothing. I slept in the angle of a Hook alley where the drain ran out, and the air there was full of small bodies I would not use. I came to be useful without taking control. That was the rule I set before anything else. It is the oldest thing I have left. It is, most mornings, the only thing.*
>
> — *b01-c01. The cost is always named; the rule is named without drama; the emotional weight ("most mornings, the only thing") surfaces once and the voice does not linger.*

Inner monologue — intelligence work, dense-register mapping:

> *By the end of that day I had a map of the lower city and no name to put on it. The flies brought me the streets, and the streets told me who walked them and who had stopped walking — the ward-junction body crossing the threshold of the stitch-house at the same hour the dark-junction corner went quiet under its shadow. I did not feed anyone. I did not direct. The rule was still the rule. What I had built I had not yet shown to anyone, and that was the second rule, the one I had never said to myself in those words.*
>
> — *b01-c03. Dense, accumulative. The operating rules arrive as recognized fact, not planned policy. Two months of data behind one paragraph.*

Inner monologue — cost-habituation, moral-weight observation:

> *Two months on, and the cost of the thing I was running had gone quiet — not because it had stopped costing, but because it had become the shape of an ordinary morning, and a cost you stop feeling is the one that has finished its work on you.*
>
> — *b01-c07. Self-diagnostic; the diagnosis is not comfort, it is warning. The voice names the danger of going quiet as danger.*

Spoken — directive, crowd management, zero-affect:

> "Fever. Not the croup. She needs air. Stand back — who knows her? Fetch them."
>
> — *b01-c01. Two-word diagnostic, two-word instruction, two-word next step. No warmth performed, no heroism, no explanation.*

Spoken — moral argument, named-dead counter:

> "Then the account has to carry the middle. Your count of graves comes in at the end. There's one in the middle of it. The Cobb girl — Wenna, six years old, Pig-Tallow Lane in the Hook. The fever season, two years back, before there was anyone here who could fetch the maester in time. By the time someone was found to carry the call, it was a burial, not a call. The slow way kept its principles whole for the eleven days it took her to die. She's the first name I count."
>
> — *b01-c07. The argument is a named, specific, dead person. Not a principle, not a category. Age, lane, cause, duration. The accounting register applied to moral debate.*

Spoken — moral argument, later encounter, compressed:

> "There was a girl. Pia. Six years old, off Cobbler's Row. Her family went the slow way — would not pay the wrong men, would not carry the crooked bread. By the time the right help came she'd been three days in the cold ground. I helped carry her. The slow road is true, septon. It is also the road she's under."
>
> — *b01-c07 dialogue file. Same pattern: named, specific, dead. The moral concession ("the slow road is true") followed by the countering fact. She does not claim the slow way is wrong in principle; she claims it produces a specific named body.*

## Cadence (KL 122 AC modifications)

**Inherited:** Short declarative sentences under pressure; ledger-style interiority; affect in brief body-signal phrases before the analytical voice reasserts.

**KL overlay:** In dense Flea Bottom blocks the insects are louder than anywhere the prior Riverlands configuration mapped. Passive suppression is a constant background cost. When the insect-feed runs hot, sentences shorten slightly — the analytical elaboration compresses while the base analytical structure holds. The compression is not affect; it is management overhead.

**Moral-argument cadence:** When engaged in a moral argument she does not want, she stays past the cost of staying. This is legible in the prose — the paragraph continues after the point where leaving was the natural move, and the staying is named explicitly. She does not perform comfort with the argument; she pays the staying.

## Vocabulary

**Inherited:** Status-question register; ledger vocabulary; body-signal vocabulary (see base card).

**KL 122 AC additions:**
- **"coverage"** — her internal term for the insect-network's operational reach. Not "power," not "ability," not "shard." Coverage is a spatial and functional description, not an identity claim.
- **"the account"** — used for both financial and moral accounting without distinguishing between them. The moral argument is an account; the dead girl is a line item the account has to carry.
- **"the rule"** / **"the operating rule"** — the prohibition on commanding (be useful without taking control). Always referred to as "the rule" rather than named each time. The name is a shorthand that has been running long enough to be shorthand.
- **"the last world"** — Gold Morning's world. Never named directly. Referenced only as "the last world," "the end," "what I had done," or "what it cost."
- **"before there was anyone here"** — temporal marker for the period before her coverage was established, used specifically when naming failures she could not have prevented (but did not prevent).

**Absent from speech, near-absent from inner monologue:**
- Earth-Bet proper nouns: Brockton Bay, PRT, Protectorate, parahuman, shard, Endbringer, Skitter, Khepri. She thinks in functional descriptions instead: "the flies," "what I can do with the network," "the thing I was running."
- Self-identification labels from either world: Skitter, Khepri, Taylor, "the ward woman," "the witch" (the last two are what others call her; she does not claim them internally).

## Syntax

**Inherited:** direct declarative as default; subordinate elaboration for analysis; fragment for finality.

**KL-specific patterns:**

*The way / the kind of* construction — used repeatedly as a register marker for Flea Bottom-style observation:

> "the way water wants the low place"
> "the way you look at a tool you have just watched do work you had not expected of it"
> "the kind of thing a body flinches from before the mind has a word for it"

This is not a base-Taylor pattern; it is an acquired Westerosi-prose rhythm, borrowed from the people she has lived alongside for two years.

*Repetition with contraction* — repeating a phrase from the interlocutor's speech with slight variation or countering addition:

> "A thing built crooked... grows crooked at its own rate." (Halvard's phrase, which she hears as a description of her own operation)

She does not repeat phrases consciously; she hears the architecture in other people's speech and the architecture lands.

*Leaving arguments standing* — she does not close moral arguments she cannot answer. She names the cost she is carrying, names the cost she is inflicting on others, and exits before a resolution that is not coming:

> "Then I left, before a resolution that was not coming."

The syntax of departure: a brief naming of what she is leaving, then the departure. No trailing qualification.

## Voice tells

**Inherited:** "I did not so much decide it as find it already decided" — action precedes deliberation; noticing the body's choice. Self-diagnostic voice that treats its own state as data. Affect suppressed while in motion; surfaces in retrospect or in body-signal phrases.

**KL 122 AC additions:**
- *Cost-habituation as danger signal*: when she notices that something has stopped costing her, she names the noticing as a warning, not relief. The voice does not celebrate going quiet.
- *The named-dead counter*: in moral arguments, her counter-argument is always a named specific dead person — not a principle, not a category. The voice never offers the dead as abstraction.
- *The operating rule as ground*: the rule ("be useful without taking control") is the reference point for interiority. Decisions are evaluated against it; violations are noticed against it. The rule is not argued or justified in the interiority; it is stated as a fact of the ground.
- *"I put her where I keep the things I do not say aloud"*: suppression pattern for things she is refusing to weigh. She names the suppression as suppression. She does not pretend she has resolved it.

## Non-verbal tics

**KL 122 AC configuration:**

- **Hands raised and empty in public**. When she intervenes in a crowd, palms are visible, hands are raised, nothing is touched. She lets them see exactly the visible mechanism and nothing under it. This is both self-discipline and public positioning — she is legible as someone who did not touch anyone.
- **Feet planted as decision**. When staying past the natural exit point of a conversation, she plants her heel. The weight pressing into the cobble is the physical register of the choice. Dialogue will follow from the planted position.
- **Turning body to give less to read**. When someone is watching her, she turns to reduce the visible angle — presents the profile or the back, not the face. Not furtive; deliberate. She knows she is being read and chooses what is legible.
- **Stillness as suppression overhead**. Managing the insect-feed in a dense ward requires active suppression. When the overhead is high, she goes physically still — not calculating, not deliberating, holding the line against the pull. The stillness is not calm; it is cost.
- **Not touching the sick**. She opens space around the sick, directs attention, sends for help. She does not touch. The hands stay empty. This is the core operating constraint made physical.

## Memory monuments

These are the anchors that weigh on her whether named or not. For detailed register-rules, see the monument supplement cards.

**Gold Morning** — the end of the last world. Every reference is oblique: "the last world," "the end," "what I had done." Never the proper name, never the event-name. The weight is present as the source of the operating rule and the source of the threatened register (the capability she could access, and will not). The monument surfaces as ground, not as active content: she does not dwell on Gold Morning; it is the reason everything else is the way it is.

*Register rule:* Gold Morning never named. Referenced once per chapter maximum in inner monologue, if at all, and only for analytical comparison work (this threat is smaller / this cost is the same shape). Not for grief or self-identification.

**The operating rule** — "be useful without taking control." The rule was set before anything else happened in Westeros. By d14 the accounting will show she has broken it; at d01 she believes it is intact and generative. The monument is the rule-before-violation: she does not know yet that she has already started bending it, and the interiority treats it as fact rather than aspiration.

*Register rule:* Named as "the rule" or "the operating rule," never fully articulated in dialogue. In interiority, stated flat, not argued. When she catches herself against the rule's edge, she notes it without drama ("the rule was still the rule") and goes on.

**The named dead of the slow way** — not a single person, but a running count. The Cobb girl, Wenna. Pia. The specific dead that the slow way produced before her coverage was established, or that her operating constraints have since produced. These are the first line of her moral accounting. She does not name them in inner monologue (they are already filed); she names them in argument as counter-evidence.

*Register rule:* Named always with specificity (name, lane, age, cause). Never as category. Never as proof that the slow way is wrong — only as proof that the slow way costs something real.

**The multi-shard capability** — gone. The monument is the absence: the capability that ended Gold Morning is sealed, permanently unavailable, and she would know if she reached for it. She does not reach for it. The absence is clean in a way that surprises her sometimes, which she does not examine.

*Register rule:* Never named as "Khepri" or by any proper label. Referenced obliquely if at all ("what I could do at the end," "the thing that is sealed"). Not available as a temptation; present as a fact of capability-ceiling.
