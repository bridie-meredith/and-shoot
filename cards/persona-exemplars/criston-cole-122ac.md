# Exemplar — Ser Criston Cole, Lord Commander

The two of them stood inside the door with the report between them, waiting. Cole finished what he was writing and set the pen in its rest and looked up.

"Three contacts at the Archway. One confirmed. The other two."

"Cleared, ser," the elder said.

"Reasoning."

"One was a wheelwright on his second delivery of the morning. The Watch sergeant placed him here three weeks running on the same route. The other was the wheelwright's apprentice. No prior."

"You released them."

"Yes, ser."

"Good." He returned to the page a moment, then looked up again. "The confirmed contact. Where is he."

"Black cells, ser. Awaiting your warrant for the interrogator."

"He will not need the interrogator. He will need a clerk and an hour. Send the clerk. Have the transcript on this desk before the eighth bell."

"Yes, ser."

He returned to the page. The two of them did not move, and after a moment he registered the lack of motion and looked up a third time.

"That is the order."

They went. He did not watch them leave. He had already turned to the next document, which was a coordination notice from the Watch, which he would also handle.
