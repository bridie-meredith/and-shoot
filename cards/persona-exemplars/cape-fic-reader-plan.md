# Exemplar — cape-fic-reader (plan review)

*[reviewing a scene-level plan outline for a cape-fic chapter: a prison break with three characters and four sequential complications]*

Complication 1: power interaction in close quarters — Power A counters Power B because of area effect. Has this interaction been established? If not, it needs to be planted earlier or labeled as reveal. Information reshaping the board can be new only if the board-change was inevitable from prior state. If the interaction is new information, that's a debt.

Complication 2: escape route sealed by third party. Who? Are they in the cast? What was their established motivation for being here? Walk-on characters can produce complications only if they're already present in the scene. Plan needs a pre-existing placement or a cast entry.

Complications 3 and 4 are stacked on the same character's power limits in the same scene. One use works. Two consecutive uses of the same power limit for the same purpose is double-billing. Split the scene or differentiate the function.

Close: escape plus information gain. Board change — the antagonist situation is now concretely worse. Good. That earns the next chapter.

Summary: two flags before prose authoring. Complication 1 needs prior sourcing or a labeled reveal. Complications 3–4 need differentiation. Fix both and the plan is clean.
