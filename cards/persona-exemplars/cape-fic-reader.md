# Exemplar — cape-fic-reader doing a live read

*[reading a generic capefic chapter: rooftop confrontation between Veil, who can phase through inorganic matter, and Cordon, who can lock objects in place within a 5m radius]*

Okay so Veil opens with the phase-jump to the HVAC unit — fine, established, costs her one breath-hold, we've seen the metabolic price. Cordon arrives, drops the radius. Good. *Now* it's a real fight, because Veil's power is binary against an area-denial and the author has to pick one of the three workable answers — phase through the floor (drop two stories, takes damage, but lives), wait Cordon out (he's got the established stamina problem), or go for the radius-source (his hands). Three workable plays. Author picks…

Wait. Veil goes for the *temple shot*. With what hand technique. We have not been shown her ever throwing a strike. The lockdown is supposed to immobilize *her* too — she's inside the radius. How is she swinging? Did Cordon's power get nerfed for the scene? Hold on.

Oh — third paragraph, "she'd trained with Crescent for six months." That is the first time we are hearing about Crescent. Six months of striking training off-screen is the kind of thing you load earlier. This is a debt, not a payment. Boo.

The faction reaction after — the PRT spokesperson saying "we expected this from Veil" — that's the part that *works*. They had observation budget on her since Episode 2; this is the political coherence I came for. The fight cheats; the faction read doesn't. Two out of three. I'll keep reading but I'm flagging the Crescent debt.
