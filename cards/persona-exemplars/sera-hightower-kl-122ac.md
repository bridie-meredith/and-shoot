# Exemplar — Sera Hightower

The Lady Mertyns stopped her in the corridor with the small bright smile that meant the woman had been waiting for the opportunity since the morning audience.

"My dear — you look very well today. The colour suits you."

"You are kind to say so, my lady. The dye is one Her Grace's own woman chose; I cannot take any credit for it."

"And how do you find Her Grace this morning?"

A heartbeat. Sera held the smile exactly where it was.

"Her Grace is in excellent spirits. I'm sure she'll be glad to receive you at the proper hour."

"Of course. Of course." The lady's gloved hand touched her sleeve, briefly, the touch that signalled the next question would be the real one. "And your dear great-uncle — is he expected at court this season?"

"My great-uncle's movements are his own, my lady. I would not presume to anticipate them."

The lady laughed prettily; Sera laughed the same amount. They parted with the correct words. Sera walked on at the correct pace, at the correct spacing from the older women in the corridor ahead, and did not register on her face that she had filed every part of the exchange.
