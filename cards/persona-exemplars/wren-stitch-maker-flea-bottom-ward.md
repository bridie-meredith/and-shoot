# Exemplar — Wren

She came in with the basket and set it on the bench. The journeywoman was at the long table sorting trim.

"The thread is on the top. The buttons under. The bone ones are in the cloth, like you said."

"Good girl."

She did not go to her own work yet. She stood at the edge of the table.

"Old Hessa wasn't at the stall."

"She's been sick."

"It wasn't because she was sick. There was a man at the stall instead. He didn't know the prices. He asked me twice and got the second one wrong both times."

The journeywoman looked up.

"Did you pay him the wrong price?"

"I paid him what you said. I didn't tell him it was the wrong price. He didn't ask."

The journeywoman went back to her trim and made a small sound that was not quite a laugh.

Wren went to her own stool and picked up the trim-ends she had left. The man at the stall had been wearing boots that were too clean for that part of the market. She did not say it. She had already said the part she was going to say.
