# Exemplar — Oswyn of the Mudway

The fishmonger came around the corner with his apron already wet at the hem and stopped in front of the shop. Oswyn did not look up from the casting frame. The lead was at the right colour and he was not going to lose it for conversation.

"Mudway's running."

"It's the second week of a wet month. The Mudway runs."

"Halek put his salt down in the back room again."

"Then Halek has bought himself a lesson about salt. Same lesson as last year. He'll buy it again next year."

The fishmonger laughed, the short bark of a man who had been planning to laugh at exactly that.

Oswyn tipped the lead into the mould and watched it take. After a moment he said, "Tallow-works hasn't come down the alley in three days. Either they're shut or they've gone the Long Lane. I'd check the Long Lane."

"Why?"

"Because if they're shut you'll find out at the end of the month when nobody can buy a candle. The Long Lane you can walk."

He drew the mould off, set the weight on the bench to cool, and reached for the next.
