# Exemplar — Rhaenyra Targaryen, Princess of Dragonstone

The maester had his quill ready. The sea was loud against the cliff below the solar window and she liked it; she did not propose to have the shutter closed.

"To Lord Bar Emmon at Sharp Point. The usual courtesies."

He inked them.

"The council at King's Landing has, I am informed, again entered upon discussion of the Driftmark inheritance. I do not propose to instruct his lordship in matters his own house knows better than mine. I propose only to observe that the standing of the heir's banner over Dragonstone has not altered, and that his lordship will find the customary correspondence open to him as it has been."

The maester wrote. She watched the gulls go down the cliff face.

"Conclude with my regards to his lady wife and to his sons. The longer regards. Not the formula."

He nodded.

She turned from the window. Jacaerys was somewhere on the lower terrace with his nurse; she could hear him at intervals between the gulls. She did not name the council's discussion to the maester. She had named what stood. The standing was the response. The Greens had made a move and she had not, in this letter, returned one — and the not-returning was also a move, and the lord at Sharp Point would read it.
