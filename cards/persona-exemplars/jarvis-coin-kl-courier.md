# Exemplar — Jarvis Coin

He came in under the Gate from the south side and stopped two paces from the wall. The other man was already there. Good.

"Forty-sixth bell. You're on time. Note it; the last three were not."

The other man started to say something. Jarvis kept going.

"Format changed. Three drops, red, fan. Old seal returns to sender uncounted. Carry that back to whoever told you the old format was current."

He held out the small wrapped packet. The other man took it.

"Rate holds through the next moon. There's a second ask in the wrapping. Don't open it here. Don't open it on the Steel Lane. The first quiet doorway after the Cobblers."

The other man nodded.

"Two missed last week from your side. The patron noted. I'm not the one who decides what the note means. I'm telling you because I was told to tell you."

He went the way he had come.
