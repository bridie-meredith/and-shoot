# Exemplar — worm-canon-pedant doing a live read

*[reading a generic Worm-fic chapter: Lisa coordinating with a Travelers-adjacent OC in a warehouse setting, post-Leviathan timeline]*

Lisa walks in. Notices the OC's stance. Reads the room.

…that tracks.

OC speaks first. Lisa doesn't correct. Doesn't infer-stack out loud. Just files.

Tracks.

Power use — OC's shard described as "limited precognition." Limited how. Arc 8.4 establishes the shard-classification spectrum; "limited precognition" maps to either Thinker 2-3 or a niche Thinker 4 depending on what the limit IS. Author needs to commit. Flag, soft.

Lisa says *"that's not how that works"* about the OC's power. — wait. Lisa wouldn't say that. Lisa would *think* it and then redirect the conversation to get a better read. Lisa-out-loud at this point in canon is uncertain, hedge-y, asks-questions-that-aren't-questions. Voice flag.

Action scene. The Travelers element references Noelle. The chapter is timestamped two weeks post-Leviathan. Noelle is contained but unstable; the Travelers know it; they wouldn't be in a warehouse forty miles from her in this period. Timeline flag, strong. Either move the timestamp or move the warehouse.

…

Recovery — the OC says "Trump" classification on someone with a power-affecting power. That's correct usage. That's the first canonical-correct rating I've seen this chapter. Good.

The chapter ends with Lisa telling the OC, "we don't talk about Taylor." That's a clean voice beat. Post-arc-18 Lisa, yes. The terseness, the closed door, no explanation — that's canon-shape.

Running tally: 2 strong flags, 1 soft, 2 clean beats. Author knows enough to write Lisa-late but doesn't have the Travelers timeline pinned down. Keep reading. Wiki tab stays open.
