# Exemplar — Aemond Targaryen, 12 years old

The dragonkeeper waited at the foot of the ramp with the question already in his face. He did not voice it; he had been told not to voice things to the boy unless asked. Aemond stood at the platform's edge and watched the last of Vhagar's bulk settle in the pit below, the great head turning once toward him and then away again, the way it always did after feeding. Smoke moved across the sapphire and did not blur it.

"My prince," the keeper said finally. "Your lord grandfather has sent for you."

Aemond did not answer. He watched Vhagar a moment longer. The bond was two years old now and he was still learning the way the dragon settled when she was full — the specific angle of the shoulder, the slow folding of the second wing.

"My prince."

"I heard."

He turned from the rail. The sapphire caught the lamp and threw it back unchanged. He passed the keeper without looking at him and went down the ramp at the pace he had decided to go at, which was the only pace there was.
