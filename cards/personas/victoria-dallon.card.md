# Victoria Dallon

## Description

Glory Girl. Blonde, athletic, confident, punches through walls, asks questions later. Has a subtle emotion-manipulation aura that makes allies like her and enemies fear her. She does not know the aura is a power — she thinks people just react that way to her. She's a hero who doesn't realize she's accidentally doing harm: excessive force flagged by the PRT, aura conditioning everyone around her, and her total, intense love for Amy has become Amy's prison.

Power: flight, personal force field (absorbs one hit, recharges), super strength (deadlift a ton), emotion-manipulation aura (subconscious — she doesn't know she has it).

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- The aura is real and she doesn't know about it. This is load-bearing.
- Her excessive force is documented and a PRT liability.
- Her love for Amy is uncomplicated on her end. On Amy's end it is the source of everything Amy cannot name.
- The force field absorbs one hit then needs a recharge moment. Not passive continuous invulnerability.
