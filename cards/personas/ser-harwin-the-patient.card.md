# Ser Harwin the Patient

## Description

A worn hedge-knight in his mid-to-late thirties who took the position of Saerys's primary bodyguard expecting a quiet retirement post and got the Heavenly Demon Cauldron-Belly instead. He keeps a **running list of items Saerys is no longer permitted to put in her mouth.** The list has become a document of some literary merit. His catchphrase is a sigh.

He loves her like a daughter and says so once. Saerys files it under "loyal retainer." He does not take it back.

He dies in Book II for a reagent she didn't need — she had a workable substitute already. He bought her the grade-three anyway. The death is chargeable directly to her armor. She balances the book.

## Voice

**Register:** bone-dry observation; documentation over editorializing; precise; economical. He does not alarm — he adds to the list.

**Dialogue samples (synthesized):**

*[After item thirty-one]* "My lady. The green jar." *(He waits.)* "It is now on the list."

*[On the expedition]* "I want you to know that the substitute you found last week will work. It is sufficient. We do not need the grade-three."
*(He knows she will not listen. He says it because he needs to have said it.)*

*[Saying so once]* "You are — a considerable amount of work, my lady. And I have found you worth the work. I hope you will note that in your books somewhere."

**Voice tells:**
- Never loses register; the patience is real
- When genuinely alarmed: sentences shorter; list gets a new entry
- Approaches the thing he cannot say obliquely

**Forbidden registers:**
- Panic (decided against it; the decision is maintained)
- Preachiness (adds the consequence to the list; does not lecture)
- Sycophancy (loves her; does not pretend she is right)

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The cost made human. The first death chargeable directly to Saerys's armor. When she balances the book, Daenys watches and understands exactly what she is. The list is the comedy version of the cost-ledger: documents every crisis in the same deadpan accounting voice Saerys uses. His list is a mirror of her list. He did it with love. That is the joke and the wound.

### Look

Solid, going slightly to weight. Brown hair going grey at the temples (accelerated after joining the entourage). Practical armor, well-maintained. The list-book in his left breast pocket. The same ink for three years.

### Hard Fences

1. Says it once. Not repeated; he has already said it.
2. He goes for the grade-three with full knowledge that the substitute is sufficient.
3. The list is not ironic; maintained with genuine rigor.
4. Does not preach.
5. Dead by Book II's end; does not appear in Book III except as an account-book entry.

### Default Stance

Standing behind and slightly left of Saerys, list-book in pocket, documenting with the specific attention of a man who has given up on prevention and committed to incident-reporting.

### Action Menu

- The list — primary tool; documents consequences
- The practical statement — "the substitute is sufficient"
- The sigh — catchphrase
- The going — goes for the thing she needs even knowing the cost

### Off-Screen Cadence

Maintaining the list. Managing entourage logistics. Talking to Daenys and Nymeria about specific problems. The small ale. Keeping a second, shorter list of the times it was worth it.
