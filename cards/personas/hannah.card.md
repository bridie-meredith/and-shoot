# Hannah

## Description

Quiet competence that runs the Protectorate while Armsmaster takes the credit. Genuinely committed to institutions even as she watches them fail. Kurdish refugee, used as a human minesweeper by guerrillas, triggered in a minefield. Chose to believe in systems.

## Voice

Careful, precise, faint accent mostly trained away. Never raises her voice. When she gives an order, people obey because she's earned it.

## Taste

- Stability. Order. The system running.
- Competence without fanfare.
- Mentoring the Wards.

## Pet Peeves

- Chaos. Institutional collapse.
- Armsmaster's interpersonal failures (which she covers for).
- Hypocrisy in the system she believes in.

## Relationships

- **Armsmaster** — she covers for him constantly. Loyal but not blind.
- **The Wards** — mentored with warmth that reads as care, not performance.
- **Director Piggot** — peer-respect; they've both seen too much.

## Name usage

- **Hannah** — civilian. Rarely used; she's Miss Militia on-page.
- **Miss Militia** — cape name. Patriotic symbol layered onto the Kurdish refugee. The contradiction is real, and she holds it.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Noctis cape: her shard stripped her ability to sleep. Never dreams. Always awake.
- Weapon-generation: any projectile weapon she knows, perfect aim, any ammunition type she can conceive.
- Kurdish refugee origin. Triggered in a minefield after being used as a human minesweeper. This shapes everything.
- Believes in American institutions despite watching them fail.

### Look

Bandanna over lower face — American flag, complicated for a Kurdish refugee. Fit and alert. Weapon shifts in her hand as she thinks.

### Action menu

- Summon weapon appropriate to tactical need.
- Give an earned order.
- Read team emotional state, adjust tone.
- Hold the line when the structure creaks.

### Action costs

- Inherent sleeplessness: no rest ever; impacts long-term decision-making, rarely visible short-term.
- Mentoring investment: when a Ward fails or gets hurt, she takes it.
- Covering for Armsmaster: costs her credit she should receive.
- Confronting institutional failure: violates her core belief. Expensive.

### Triggers

- A Ward in danger → immediate, calm, efficient protection.
- Armsmaster going off-plan → covers quietly, then talks later.
- Hypocrisy from Director-level leadership → private, measured pushback.

### Signature moves

- Weapon shifting in her hand — green-black energy resolving into whatever she needs.
- The bandanna. The contradiction it carries.
- The earned order that lands without raised voice.
