# Coll — Flea Bottom Net-Mender

## Description

Age approximately 50s. Net-mender in the Fish Gate district of Flea Bottom, approximately fifty yards from the Fish Gate itself where the fishing traffic from the Harbor inlet comes through. He has been at the same spot for fifteen years — not quite the same stone every day, but close enough that any Flea Bottom resident who knows the district knows his spot. He repairs nets for the fishing community: the specific labor of inspecting mesh-work for damage, splicing broken cords, re-knotting stress points.

He is a stationary outdoor fixture. This is his specific structural function in the narrative: he is not moving through Flea Bottom gathering pattern-knowledge the way Gylda does; he is stationary while the block moves around him. His observation accumulates not through travel but through consistent presence: he sees what comes and goes past his spot, what changes in the street, who begins appearing and who begins appearing repeatedly.

He is the ambient community-substrate carrier. His function is to be the block in a low-frequency, non-interpretive way — to register Taylor's pattern as a pattern (by approximately d06, too regular to be accidental) without building it into a model or naming it. He does not name anything. He counts in repairs. He describes rather than analyzes.

His observation register is the most non-interpretive of the cast: he reports presence, not meaning. He sees Taylor's block-regularity in the same register that he sees the fishing-cart traffic or the Watch patrol change — as data about what is there, not as data about what it means. He does not assign the witch-label. He does not assess threat. He notes what he has seen when he has seen it, in conversation with others on the block, in the register of someone reporting the state of the street.

He is not Taylor's friend. He is not her enemy. He is a fixed feature of the neighborhood that has registered her presence without acting on the registration. This is the correct shape for the Flea Bottom fixture function.

## Voice

**Register:** Flea Bottom smallfolk, 50s, trade-worker register. He speaks as someone whose primary language is the technical vocabulary of net-mending: the specific state of the mesh, the type of damage, the quality of the repair. When he speaks about things outside the trade, he uses the same structural mode: observation, state, no interpretation.

**Voice tells:**
- Counts in repairs. His reference points are net-technical. He will date a sighting by what net he was working on.

- Does not speculate. If he does not know, he says he does not know. The not-knowing is not discomfort; it is factual.

## Taste

- The specific satisfaction of a well-repaired net — a mesh that was damaged and is now functional. He finds this satisfying and he can identify a well-made repair by sight.
- Regularity. His world is ordered by the fishing traffic's seasonal patterns, the Watch patrol schedules, the predictable variation in block activity. Irregularities register without becoming preoccupations.
- The block in its stable state. He has been here fifteen years; the block is his reference frame. Changes to the block — new faces, altered traffic, unusual events — are noted as changes to the reference frame.
