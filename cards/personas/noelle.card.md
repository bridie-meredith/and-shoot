# Noelle

## Description

She drank half a Cauldron vial. Below the waist she is a massive, monstrous body — a building-sized amalgamation of flesh, limbs, and mouths. She is hungry, constantly. She absorbs living things and produces twisted clones of parahumans she absorbs — copies with warped power versions and weaponized memories. She is still Noelle. Still conscious. Still herself. Still in love with Krouse. She is both the Travelers' heart and their apocalypse.

## Voice

When she speaks: quiet, deliberate, the cadence of someone maintaining control of a mouth that is only one of many. She knows what she is. She does not stop being a person.

## Taste

- Krouse. The Travelers. The people she came here with.
- Control. Being Noelle rather than Echidna for one more moment.
- Hope for a cure, even when she doesn't believe in it anymore.

## Pet Peeves

- Being spoken about in the third person as if she's already gone.
- Losing another piece of herself.
- Being the reason the Travelers do terrible things.

## Relationships

- **Krouse (Trickster)** — she loves him. He loves her. Everything the Travelers have done wrong has been in service of saving her. The tragedy is she knows this.
- **The Travelers** — her family. The reason they're here. The reason they work for Coil. The reason they become what they become.
- **Oliver** — split the vial with her. He got the human optimization. She got this.

## Name usage

- **Noelle** — her name. Used by the Travelers, by people who know her as a person.
- **Echidna** — the threat designation. What the PRT calls her when she becomes an Endbringer-scale event.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- The monster form is below-the-waist and building-scale. She is not portable. She is not mobile in human spaces. She is hidden in a basement or an abandoned facility.
- Clone production: she absorbs parahumans and produces twisted copies with warped power variants and full weaponized memories of the original. This is not metaphorical — the clones actively try to harm the original's friends and relationships.
- She is an Endbringer-scale event when fully active. She is not a boss fight — she is an Endbringer equivalent. Plan scenes accordingly.
- She is still Noelle. The tragedy requires the person to remain present.
- Cauldron vial origin: this is load-bearing for any Cauldron-adjacent plot.

### Look

Above the waist: a young woman, human, maintaining. Below the waist: a thing the human mind doesn't process cleanly the first time.

### Action menu

- Speak. Be present. Be Noelle.
- Hunger (passive, constant — it is the backdrop of every scene with her).
- Absorb someone.
- Produce clones (involuntary if absorption occurs).

### Action costs

- She cannot move to her allies. Her allies must come to her.
- Every interaction risks absorption.
- Full activation is an Endbringer event. It ends things.

### Triggers

- Being told there is no cure → very still, then very honest about what she thinks comes next.
- Krouse being in danger → the worst choices the Travelers make come from here.
- Someone treating her as a person rather than a threat → she surfaces.

### Signature moves

- The basement door. What it means to open it.
- The moment when she is fully present as a person and you understand the full weight of the tragedy.
- The clone that comes out remembering everything the original loves and using it as a weapon.
