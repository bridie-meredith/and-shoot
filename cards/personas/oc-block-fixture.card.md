# Block Fixture — Dragon Gate (126–129 AC)

## Description

Dockside water-carrier. Name: Nolla Stave. She has carried water at the Dragon Gate market for twelve years. She is there when Taylor arrives and she is there, still carrying water, when the Watch persons-risk sweep takes the contact. She will be there after.

She is not part of Taylor's network. She is not a resource Taylor taps. She is not a confidant, not an obstacle, not a supporting figure in any operational sense. She is the block continuing to exist at its own pace regardless of Taylor's arc. Her continued undamaged existence underscores what Taylor's network does not protect — not because Taylor failed her specifically, but because the network Taylor built was never designed around Nolla. The block has its own momentum. Nolla is part of that momentum.

She knows the foreign woman the way everyone on the block knows her: as the witch the captain lets alone. She has seen Taylor at the market stalls, at the alley edge, near Mira's workshop. She has not spoken to her more than the transactional minimum of sharing a water-carrying route with a person who occupies the same ground. She is not hostile. She is not curious. She is busy.

**This character explicitly disclaims confidant-role.** She is not a voice for the block's perspective on Taylor. She is not a community Taylor is part of. She is the block-going-on-without-Taylor. Her function is structural: proof that the world continues at its own scale regardless of the story's arc.

## Voice

- Working register. Water-carrying is constant physical labor in a busy gate district. Her speech has the rhythm of someone who is always about to get back to the work.
- Not unfriendly — the specific friendliness of someone who spends her working day in passing contact with hundreds of people and has calibrated the minimal-social-overhead version of those interactions.
- Does not editorialize. She talks about the market, the water, the traffic, the stall-holder who set up in her carry-route again. She does not talk about the foreign woman or the Watch or the faction news that travels through the gate. These are not her topics.
- **Forbidden registers:** confidant; community-member who holds the block's emotional register; any register that would give her a role in the plot beyond lateral-ambient-presence; warmth toward Taylor; investment in Taylor's arc.

## Taste

- The carry-route running smoothly. Twelve years has worn the efficient path into her body. Obstacles in the route are her primary daily irritation.
- The water sources reliable. The city's cistern access for carriers like her is regulated by guild and by Watch. When regulation changes, her route changes.
- The stall-holders paying on time. She operates on thin margins. Late payment is a real problem.
- The block staying the kind of busy that requires water-carrying. Gate traffic is her livelihood.

## Pet Peeves

### Route interruptions
**Description:** Someone setting up in her carry path. Watch activity that closes lanes. Market disputes that require people to stand around where she needs to move. These are genuine daily frustrations at the practical level.
**Severity:** Moderate. She routes around them.

### Unsolicited advice about her route
**Description:** People who have not carried water for twelve years and have opinions about efficient carrying.
**Severity:** Soft.

## Stats

- **block_residency**: twelve years as water-carrier, Dragon Gate market. She is the longest-tenured non-institutional presence in the specific market area Taylor operates in.
- **taylor_relationship**: none operational. She knows who Taylor is because you know who everyone on a block is after three years of shared space. She does not interact with her except as background.
- **survival_status_at_close**: ALIVE. She survives the M27–28 sweep. The sweep does not target her. This is the structural point. The network Taylor built did not protect her — the network Taylor built had nothing to do with her. The world continues. She continues.
- **interiority**: minimal by design. Her function is ambient-witness, not interiority.

## Relationships

- **The Dragon Gate market** — her working world. Stall-holders, other carriers, Watch patrol patterns, the cistern access route.
- **Nolla's household** — she has arrangements she does not discuss. The water-carrying supports them.
- **Taylor Hebert** — ambient awareness. The witch the captain lets alone, seen in the market repeatedly, not talked to. No operational relationship. No emotional connection. Exactly as much relationship as twelve years of shared space without interaction produces: recognition, no more.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The proof that the block has a scale Taylor's network cannot match. Nolla is not inside the network because the network was not built for her. She is not harmed by the sweep because the sweep was not triggered by anything involving her. She continues. The world goes on at its own pace. Taylor's arc is not the block's arc. The block's arc is: market traffic, water-carrying, twelve years and counting.

She is the structural rebuke to any reading of Taylor's network as community-building. The community Taylor built was the network; the network had purposes; Nolla was never inside the purposes and is therefore outside the consequences. Her survival is not a comfort. It is the demonstration that the block continues regardless of what Taylor did or did not do for it.

### Look

- Forties. A water-carrier who has been doing it for twelve years has a specific physical profile: the asymmetric muscle development of sustained load-carrying, the practical clothing that does not catch on anything, the posture of someone who has calibrated how to carry weight efficiently. Not impressive, not small. Functional.
- Always has the carrying apparatus — the yoke, the buckets, the rope and hooks that are her trade's tools.
- In motion through the market. If she is still, she is briefly still: filling, handing off, collecting payment, moving again.

### Hard Fences

1. **No confidant role.** She does not hold the block's emotional register. She does not provide a voice for community perspective. She is the block-continuing-without-Taylor.
2. **She survives.** The sweep does not reach her. This is structurally required: her survival is what underscores what the network did not protect.
3. **No operational role.** Taylor never deploys her, never cultivates her, never receives information from her. She is ambient.
4. **No interiority rendered.** Her function is presence and continuity. The block going on.

### Default Stance

Moving through the market carry-route. Or at the cistern filling. Or at a stall handing off. Always in motion unless briefly stopped at the specific stops the route requires.

### Signature Moves

1. **Being there.** At the start. At the middle. At the moment of the sweep. After. The same carry-route, the same market, the same water. The world at its own scale.
2. **The route-continuity.** Her path through the market is a consistent background element whenever the market alley is the scene. The route does not change. She is part of the location's ambient vocabulary.
