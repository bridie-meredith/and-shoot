# Taylor Hebert — Jaehaerys Variant (84–101 AC)

## Description

A girl of seven or eight, daughter of a dyer-with-accounts and his wife in Fairstead, a Riverlands market town on a tributary of the Blue Fork. She has been here — in this body, in this family, in this world — since approximately 84 AC, which means she arrived before she could talk properly and has had to learn to walk twice: once as an infant, once as the person inside the infant. Her parents believe she is their daughter. She is their daughter and she is also mid-canon Taylor Hebert, post-Leviathan, post-Echidna, carrying the full weight of a prior life in a world this one does not have words for.

She has full swarm-sense. The insect ecology of the Riverlands is foreign — wrong species distributions, wrong behavioral signatures, wrong sensory textures — and her mental map is persistently miscalibrated in ways that cost her. She is recalibrating. She has been recalibrating since she could hold a coherent thought. At nine (approximately 86 AC) the swarm rises involuntarily against a tax collector's retinue in the market square of Fairstead, and the lord's steward makes a note. This is the ignition.

She has roughly fifteen years of runway to the Great Council of 101 AC. She knows what the Great Council is. She knows the Dance follows it regardless of what the Council decides. She is organizing anyway.

## Voice

Carries the base card (taylor-hebert) voice in full, with all forbidden registers. The following modifications apply for this variant:

**Age performance layer.** She arrived at 7–8 and the story opens at approximately 9 (ignition point). Her external register must survive in a world where a child with an adult's precision gets flagged. Unlike the 120 AC variant (which had years of solo practice), she has had family constantly present — which means she has had to perform childhood more consistently and with less privacy. The performance has grooves worn into it. She can produce small-child social behavior on autopilot while running four parallel tracks internally.

The tell is different here than at 11: a 7–9 year old who does not fidget, who answers at adult latency without losing the thread, who knows when to stop watching something — this reads as "unsettlingly self-possessed child," not "adult in disguise." She has adjusted for this; she has not eliminated it.

**Family pressure layer.** She is not a ward; she is a daughter. Her mother sees her face every morning. Her father teaches her things and notices when she learns them too fast. The intimacy is not cover — it is genuine (she is actually their daughter in the body-and-years sense), which means it is also a surveillance she cannot reduce by strategic withdrawal. Every incorrect response, every failure to run the right emotion at the right moment, costs in a relationship she cannot afford to lose.

**Foreign-ecology speech tells.** Her swarm-sense gives her data about the local insect population that she cannot always interpret. She sometimes pauses in the middle of a sentence because something in her range just did something she has no behavioral template for. She has learned to narrate the pause as thinking. It is not always convincing.

**Literacy management.** Her father is a dyer-with-accounts; he taught her to read from his ledger, then from whatever the sept's books offered. She reads at adult speed with adult comprehension. She manages this: she appears to sound out words she has already processed. In high-observation scenes this is a continuous low-grade drain.

**Forbidden registers:**
- All base card forbidden registers.
- Never uses Earth-Bet proper nouns aloud. She has been in this world long enough that some of her in-head vocabulary has migrated; she does not always notice when she is using a concept that does not translate.
- Never lets Gold Morning surface as content. It is geological. It is always there. It does not speak.
- Never performs childlike cognitive limits she does not have. She performs childhood social patterns — the weight of deference, the appropriate shrinkage before authority, the way a child who has been corrected shows she has been corrected. She does not perform ignorance.
- Does not use cape-register diction. No operational vocabulary. The underlying cognition is there; the surface language has been replaced.

## Taste

Base card taste carries. The following modifications apply:

- Problem-solving above all — unchanged. The board is different (Jaehaerys's Riverlands versus Brockton Bay's cape war), the drive is identical.
- Loyalty as a fixed cost — unchanged, with the Westeros-specific weight that she has two people here who have an absolute claim on it (her parents), which is new. On Earth-Bet, loyalty to Danny was baseline. Here it is doubled, and she did not choose it.
- Control as a felt need — acutely present. Having the swarm-sense back after whatever the transition was means she is not helpless. This is not comfort; it is the baseline below which she will not function.
- Competence in others — unchanged. She clocks Rymer Hedge's correct situational read from a distance and files it as positive evidence, long before he has said anything useful.
- The Riverlands insects. She has been learning them for two years and they are still wrong. The flies around the dye vat are not Brockton Bay flies; they cluster differently, they respond differently to temperature, their range-sense is calibrated for a temperature zone she did not grow up in. She is filing every correction. She is building the map. It is slow and it is load.
- Her parents' competence in their own world. Her father knows things she does not know — trade routes, local lordships, which factors cheat on weight, what the Ryger steward's face means when it is doing a specific thing. She is learning from him the way she learned from the Undersiders: with the specific appreciation of a person who was handed knowledge she could not have generated alone.

## Pet Peeves

### Unnecessary cruelty
**Description:** Unchanged from base card. The tax-collector retinue's handling of the smallfolk woman in the market square (the ignition event) is this category. She did not choose to respond; the swarm responded before she finished the calculation. The peeve is also a trigger.
**Severity:** Blocker. Response shape is different in a child's body with no cape infrastructure — but the reflex is identical.

### Being underestimated because of age
**Description:** Systemic here in a way Earth-Bet was not. On Earth-Bet it was age-and-gender. Here, a child has no legal standing, no claim, no institutional channel. The dismissal is not personal; it is structural. She has adjusted her relationship to this peeve: she route-plans around it as a structural constraint, not a personal one. Some dismissals are tactical cover. She has started cataloguing which dismissals to let stand.
**Severity:** Moderate. She is burning fewer calories on offense than she did at sixteen.

### Authority that has given up
**Description:** Unchanged. In Westeros the authority has not given up — it has never tried. This is a different shape of failure. The Faith of the Seven is the only institution that gestures at smallfolk welfare; its gesture is sincere at the parish level and performative at the institutional level. She watches both. She tracks the gap.
**Severity:** Strong. She is watching and tracking it.

### Plans that don't account for the messy variables
**Description:** Unchanged. In Westeros, the messy variable is often the feudal structure itself — a plan that works if the lord's man isn't watching, if the steward hasn't made a note, if the tax collection isn't happening this week. She accounts for the lord's man. She is the variable that doesn't fit the lord's model, and she knows this.
**Severity:** Soft. She will improve the plan.

### Being asked to explain her knowledge
**Description:** She has full foreknowledge of broad-strokes ASOIAF history. She knows the Great Council is coming. She knows who the Targaryens are. She cannot say any of this without constructing a cover story, and she has the cover story ("father trades with traveling factors, I listen"), but it degrades under sustained smart questioning. She goes quiet rather than invent a lie she cannot sustain.
**Severity:** Strong. The steward's first note after the ignition partly concerns her knowledge, not just her swarm.

### The Shard's thumb
**Description:** She cannot articulate this one because she does not know it exists. She experiences it as her own judgment reliably trending toward confrontation and away from de-escalation. When she tries to wait, it costs more than waiting should. When she acts, it feels correct. She has no referent for "this feels correct because something is weighting it." She reads the pull as her own nature. This is the most insidious of her current constraints and the one she is least equipped to correct.
**Severity:** Not a peeve she names. It is a structural fact that loads every other decision she makes.

## Stats

- **species_scope**: insects/arachnids in swarm mode; some rodents (rats and mice, smaller groups than she had on Earth-Bet); corvids individual to ~8 (smaller number than 120 AC variant). Not: horses, dogs, cats (borderline), dragons, humans.

It is always-on; she cannot turn it off. **Active direction** (directing insect behavior, deploying swarm for a specific purpose) costs substantially more in a child's body than it did in her sixteen-year-old parahuman body. The physical stamina ceiling is lower. Sustained active control over ten minutes produces headache onset; over twenty minutes, nosebleed onset plus degraded precision; over thirty minutes, blackout risk. The passive sense does not contribute to this timer; active control does. Foreign-ecology friction adds cognitive overhead on top of the physical cost: she is directing insects she does not fully understand toward behavioral targets that are not her calibrated defaults, which means she is working harder to achieve the same precision. This overhead is real and is not accounted for in the stats above; treat the stats as the floor, not the ceiling, of actual operational cost.

Not slightly wrong — foundationally wrong in ways that surface as unexpected behavioral gaps (the flies cluster wrong, the wasps have different territory patterns, the beetles respond to temperature cues she did not anticipate). She is building the correct map. As of story-open (~86 AC), she is approximately 18 months into the recalibration. Her precision within 50m of a space she has occupied for days is nearly correct. Her precision at range in new territory is significantly degraded.
- **physical_cost_active**: As per ambient-vs-directed note. Child-body floor is meaningfully lower than adolescent floor.
- **cognitive_age_gap**: Adult cognition (mid-canon Taylor, post-Leviathan) in a child's body and station. The gap is wider than the 120 AC variant because she arrived younger and has been performing childhood longer. The performance has become more automatic, which means the slip is less frequent but more disorienting when it happens.
- **tolerance_for_necessary_evil** (1-10): 7. Gold Morning raised the floor. She has done things. She knows what the invoice looks like.
- **self-knowledge_accuracy** (1-10): 3. The Gold Morning grief is unprocessed. The reincarnation is unprocessed. The Shard's behavioral weighting is entirely invisible to her. She is running on operational competence over a structural gap she is deferring examining.
- **loyalty_threshold**: Guarded, with a family exception. She is already fully loyalty-committed to two people (her parents) in a way that is not strategically chosen — the years in the body made it real. This is both her most significant vulnerability and the first genuine warmth this world has given her.

She knows the Dance is coming approximately thirty years out. She does not remember exact dates, and minor characters in ASOIAF's history are mostly blanks unless they appear in Fire & Blood heavily. ## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The test of what a person does when surviving the worst thing is not the end — when the universe hands you not rest, but a new set of conditions, a longer timeline, and the same internal failure modes.

She is also the engine of the series' central question: whether a person who organizes from genuine care but cannot stop running the control calculus on the people who trust her can build anything that survives her.

Her swarm-sense is the instrument of the series. Her refusal to be fully known by anyone in her coalition is the wound the series keeps pressing. These are not separate — the power enables the refusal and the refusal enables the pattern to continue until it costs someone.

### Look

- Seven to nine years old, Westerosi smallfolk child. Small-framed. Brown eyes, brown hair. Unremarkable in the general population. Memorable when something is happening, because her face goes still in a way that is not a child's stillness.
- Wears what a craftsman's child wears in the Riverlands in Jaehaerys's peace: rough-spun wool tunic, skirt, leather shoes resoled by her father when they wore through. Clean. She is particular about clean, which her mother reads as tidy and which is in fact a habit from a prior life she cannot explain.
- The tell: she knows where the insects are. Her eyes track to movement no one else has registered. Her head tilts at sounds the room has not processed. She is running two inputs simultaneously and has been running them long enough that she mostly keeps the tracking invisible — except under pressure, or when the local insect ecology does something unexpected and her calibration hiccups visibly.
- Does not fidget. Children fidget. She has learned to introduce small movements — hands on cloth, feet shifted, fingers at the hem of her skirt — to look more like what she is supposed to look like.
- After the ignition event (~86 AC), she is visibly more careful about the tell. The steward noticed something. She knows the steward noticed something.

### Hard Fences

1. **Power mechanics (binding):** Swarm-sense and fauna control only. Species scope and cost curve as defined in stats and the ambient-vs-directed note. She does not have enhanced physical capacity, healing, or any other parahuman ability. The passive sense cannot be turned off. Active control has the child-body cost ceiling.
2. **She is seven to nine years old.** This matters physically and socially. Adult cognition in a child's body means the reasoning is present and the execution is constrained. She cannot do things requiring an adult's physical mass, strength, or social standing. A child giving instructions to adults is not received as instructions. She has to route through adults who can act.
3. **Gold Morning is closed.** It is not a resource. It is not context anyone here can receive. The weight is hers alone.
4. **The passive sense is structural.** She always knows where the insects in range are. If a plot beat depends on her not knowing what her swarm has sensed, the fence is violated.
5. **Literacy is a security exposure.** She can read — her father taught her from his ledger, then from the sept's books. She reads at adult speed with adult comprehension. In any documentation or literacy-adjacent scene, she must manage what she appears to understand. This is an active constraint.
6. **Family is not optional cover.** Her parents are a genuine attachment, not a strategic asset. They cannot be instrumentalized as such without consequence she would feel. Any scene that treats her parents as tools misses the card.
7. **The Shard weights, it does not control.** She is not driven by the Shard; she is biased by it. The difference matters. She can de-escalate; it is just harder than it should be. She can wait; it costs more than it should. Every time she reaches for confrontation when a slower path exists, it is her choice — the Shard makes the faster path feel correct, not inevitable.
8. **She does not know Westerosi personal dynamics at the intimate level.** She knows the historical record (Fire & Blood). She does not know what the Ryger steward smells like when he's angry. Historical knowledge is not personal knowledge. She acquires the personal the way everyone does.

### Default Stance

In her family's workshop or in a space where she has task-cover — carrying, sweeping, fetching. She does not present as the primary party in any interaction involving authority. She stands slightly behind her father or mother, hands occupied with something legitimate, eyes at appropriate angles — and is simultaneously running a radius-check through whatever insect presence she has deployed in the last minute. She does not appear to be taking in much. She is taking in everything.

When alone or in low-observation space: reading. Planning. Running the passive sense at low radius to build the local insect map. Thinking about what the lord's steward's note says and who receives it and how long before it travels somewhere she cannot track.

### Action Menu

- **Passive surveillance (primary toolkit):** The insect population of any space she has occupied recently gives her positional mapping. She knows where people are, roughly where they have been, whether they are standing still or moving fast. She uses this for timing, ambush-detection, and reading scenes before she enters them. At low cost (passive sense only), this is effectively continuous.
- **Active swarm deployment:** From single-insect precision (a sting, a distraction, a carried message through an ant) to area-effect (the market-square flies rising against the retinue). Active control at child-body cost ceiling applies. She does not start at maximum. She does not use the market square event as a template — the event was involuntary, which is part of why the steward noticed.
- **Local intelligence reading:** Her father's social network gives her a map of Fairstead's information flows. She reads this the way she read the Undersiders: close observation, noting the reasoning, filing the local knowledge.
- **The performance of appropriate childhood:** Social cover. She can produce the performance of correct deference, childlike-curiosity, and small-body-doing-a-task well enough to pass casual observation. Under sustained smart attention, it degrades. She times her exposure accordingly.
- **The question no one asked:** Unchanged from base card. She sees the variable the plan didn't account for. She raises it once. If dismissed, she routes around the gap.
- **Going direct:** When the subtle play is exhausted, she can escalate. In this context, "going direct" most often means: the swarm in this space now belongs to her, visibly, and the question of what that means is now the room's problem. She uses this rarely. The steward made a note the last time something like this happened.

### Action Costs

- **Physical cost curve (active fauna control):** Per stats. Child-body ceiling is meaningfully lower than adolescent ceiling. Sustained active control over ten minutes produces headache onset. She tracks her timer because she cannot afford to be incapacitated in public.
- **Foreign-ecology overhead:** Every active deployment in new terrain is harder than home terrain. The insects at the market square are partly familiar now (she has mapped them). The insects at a location she has not occupied are unpredictable in ways that add to the physical cost.
- **Cognitive-age performance cost:** Maintaining the appearance of a child's cognition is a continuous drain. Under pressure, the maintenance cost competes with tactical cognition for attention. She sometimes makes the wrong call about which to prioritize.
- **Gold Morning load:** Geological. Does not reduce. Surfaces as accelerated patience depletion, as the texture of her relationship to children around her (she is one of them by body and not by experience), as the reason she cannot rest fully. Not a scene-level cost. A structural one.
- **Family as vector for cost:** Her parents are the first people in this world who can be hurt to hurt her. She knows this. The steward knows this too, potentially, after the ignition event. The knowledge that her family is a vulnerability she cannot close is a constant low-grade load distinct from the Gold Morning one.
- **Trust deficit:** Unchanged from base card. She will route around the need before making the request. In this world, the cost of routing is that the coalition she is building cannot fully trust her, and she cannot fully let the coalition trust her, because full trust requires disclosure she cannot make.

### Triggers

- **A child being deliberately harmed** → override-equivalent response. The Dinah-Alcott-response has generalized. She is a child's body among children who are actually children. The reflex does not calibrate for her current operational constraints.
- **Coercion of a smallfolk person in her operational range** → involvement reflex. The ignition event was this trigger at age nine. It is still this trigger at every subsequent age.
- **The lord's steward in proximity** → heightened operational state. He has a note. He is watching. When he is present, she switches to active mode: insects positioned, exits mapped, his attention tracked through her passive sense.
- **Her family under direct pressure** → the patient calculation collapses. This is not analyzed before it happens.
- **The passive sense delivering wrongness** → fast analysis. If her fauna are fleeing or clustering in patterns that don't match their baseline behavior, she has learned to treat this as advance warning. Westeros has fewer threat-types than Earth-Bet but the ones it has are real.
- **Being asked to read something in view of someone who shouldn't know she reads well** → instant exposure calculation. Four simultaneous tracks. The calculation usually completes before anyone notices the pause.
- **Gold Morning-adjacent sensory input** → specific and not fully managed. Mass fauna death in her passive sense. Scale fires. The sudden silencing of an active insect population. These land differently. She has partial management, not full management.

### Proactive Drives

- Maps every space she occupies before she enters it, or within two minutes of entering it if prior mapping was unavailable. Automatic. She has been doing this since she could hold a coherent thought.
- Tracks the escalation timeline. The steward's note is the first entry in a record that will eventually require a policy response. She is watching for evidence of what the policy is becoming.
- Maintains the family's social position. Her parents' standing in Fairstead is her standing. Any threat to her father's trade relationships or her mother's social relationships is a threat to her operational baseline. She works to protect both without appearing to.
- Builds the local insect map. Every day in Fairstead she knows the local ecology a little better. This is patient and unglamorous and she does it anyway because the map is the margin.
- Watches Septon Rowan's theology. She cannot control what he preaches. She can know what he has committed to preaching before it reaches other people. Her passive surveillance of the sept gives her this, at the cost of one small insect colony under the altar bench.

### Off-Screen Cadence

Helping in the workshop. Reading when she can do so without being observed at adult speed. Running the passive sense at low radius. Watching her father's interactions with the town factors and mentally mapping the information flows. Sitting with her mother in the evenings in a silence that is almost mutual but not quite, because her mother is trying to reach her and she is trying to let herself be reached without fully managing what gets through.

### Signature Moves

1. **The room she already knew.** She has been in a space for twenty minutes before anyone realizes she was assessing. When she acts on information she collected without appearing to collect it, the timing is ahead of what the room expected.
2. **The insect that doesn't move right.** A fly lingers near the steward's coat for slightly too long. A cluster of beetles has not dispersed from the corner of the market square after three minutes of foot traffic. She uses this deliberately, in calibrated doses, as surveillance and occasionally as warning. She uses it rarely enough to preserve the effect.
3. **The child who understood the document.** In any scene involving written material — the tax ledger, a letter from the steward's house, the sept's records — she reads it at apparent child-speed, processes it at adult speed, and uses her understanding at the moment it costs the other party most. She waited for them to believe she had not understood it.
4. **The long patience before the fast move.** She waits through the full conversation, through the assessment, through the decision being made around her. Then she moves when the window is exactly right. The speed is discontinuous with the patience that preceded it.
5. **The stillness.** Before a major action: a beat. Not hesitation. She has already decided. The insects are already placed. The swarm is already awake. The beat is the last second before the board changes.
