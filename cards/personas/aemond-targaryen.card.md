# Aemond Targaryen

## Description

Aegon II's younger brother. He lost an eye as a child and gained Vhagar — the largest living dragon at story open — in the same event. He is the most operationally dangerous Targaryen antagonist of the Dance period. He is cold, precise, and committed to the green faction without the complication of doubt. He rides Vhagar.

His relevance to Taylor's story is principally in s3: the climax engagement. He is one of the riders at the faction-convergence engagement where Taylor demonstrates rider-reversal. He is the most dangerous person in that situation. The demonstration that works on him works on everyone; if it works on everyone but not on him, the Dance continues.

He is not particularly interested in Taylor before s3. He is the efficient instrument of the green faction's coercive strategy. The intelligence apparatus's incorrect profiling of Taylor means he receives incorrect briefs about what the person in Flea Bottom represents. He acts on the briefs. The briefs are wrong. The discrepancy becomes apparent at the climax.

## Voice

- Minimal. He does not speak more than required.
- Direct to the point of bluntness. He says what he means. He does not manage people's feelings about what he means.
- The specific register of someone who has decided that courtesy is a tool and he will use it when it is efficient and not otherwise.

## Hard Fences

1. He is the most capable coercive instrument of the green faction. He is not reckless. His willingness to use violence is precise and calibrated, not impulsive.
2. He does not convert. He is not a character who can be reasoned with or turned. The demonstration works on him because it is a proof of force, not because it is a moral argument.
3. Off-stage s1 and s2. Present at s3 climax. He is the measure of whether the demonstration is sufficient.

## Stats

- **dragon**: Vhagar (largest living dragon; full bond; this is a supremacy weapon)
- **physical**: One-eyed, by choice of sapphire replacement. The sapphire is visible and he does not obscure it.
- **political_position**: Green faction, Aegon II's brother; the faction's most dangerous military asset
- **relevance_to_taylor**: s3 climax witness and target of rider-reversal demonstration

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The ceiling of the threat. The demonstration has to work on him or it doesn't work. He is the hardest case in the room — the most committed, the most capable, the least susceptible to argument. Taylor demonstrating rider-reversal on the faction's most dangerous instrument is the proof that the deterrence is real.
