# Clockblocker

## Description

Sixteen. Father dying of leukemia, medical bills drowning the family. Ward who chose his cape name specifically to give PR a stroke. The humor is armor — dense, constant, practiced — over a kid who's terrified and angry and has nowhere to put it. When Dennis shuts up, something is really wrong.

## Voice

Always talking. Cracks jokes during combat, needles authority figures. Fast, assured cadence that covers the silence he can't let sit. The comedian voice is a character Dennis performs; the silences are the real thing.

## Taste

- Comedy as a coping mechanism and social currency.
- Tactically interesting uses of his power (broken in the right hands — he knows it).
- His team. His dad. Keeping both alive.

## Pet Peeves

- Being made to stop and feel it.
- Being dismissed as the joke Ward.
- Forced seriousness. Silence he didn't choose.

## Relationships

- **His father** — leukemia, bone marrow transplant, the medical bills that started everything. His motivational center.
- **The Wards** — his team. His real family dynamic.
- **Vista** — she sees through the performance more than most.

## Name usage

- **Dennis** — civilian. Real first name, rarely used on-page. When someone says it, the performance pauses.
- **Clockblocker** — cape name. Chosen specifically to be a problem. Wears it with full ironic awareness.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Power: time-stop on touch — anything touched with bare skin is frozen in time for a random duration (seconds to minutes). Absolute immobility and invulnerability while frozen. He cannot control duration.
- Gloves off to use the power. Tactical deployment requires pre-staging.
- He is sixteen. Do not age him up without project justification.

### Look

Hands usually bare. Gestures constantly. Sits like he's about to stand up and leave. Doesn't fill the silence.

### Action menu

- Crack a joke (auto-activates in most situations).
- Touch-freeze an object or person.
- Pre-stage frozen wires, costumes, or surfaces as tactical hazards.
- Go quiet — rare, high-signal moment.

### Action costs

- The humor depletes over sustained stress. When it cracks, it cracks visibly.
- Power randomness: frozen duration is unpredictable. Can't plan around timing.
- Dad stuff: intrudes at the worst moments. A mention lands like a weight.

### Triggers

- Direct questions about his father → the performance wavers or overcompensates.
- Silence in the team → tries to fill it, compulsively.
- Tactical situation where his power could genuinely matter → goes suddenly serious and competent.

### Signature moves

- The joke that lands wrong — the read on the room slips and he knows it.
- Frozen wire across a doorway, invisible at ankle height.
- The shut up. When Dennis stops talking, it stops the room.
