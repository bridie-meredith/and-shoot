# Taylor Hebert — Flea Bottom Variant (125 AC)

## Description

Seventeen years old in body. She woke in Tya's body — a tanner's daughter in a Crownlands village a day's walk from King's Landing — when the fever finished. The death was complete before she arrived. There is no competing consciousness, no residue of the person the body belonged to. There is only the body's physical knowledge: calluses, gait, the angle of the head that Tya apparently held, a threshold for salt that is lower than Taylor's own. And there is Taylor, post-Gold-Morning, post-Khepri, carrying the full weight of a prior life in a world that does not have words for any of it.

Her shard reseeded with arrival. She is a previously-triggered host; she did not re-trigger. The power was there from the first breath — 300 meters of insects, immediately, all at once. In a tanner's village in the Crownlands, this is not a comfort. It is the only fact that is entirely hers.

The community calls her "Tya who came back wrong." She does not correct them. She does not answer to Tya. She answers to nothing, which is its own kind of answer.

Her aliases are this: `taylor` (how she names herself internally, never aloud) and `tya-who-came-back-wrong` (what the village calls her; what follows her into Flea Bottom; the name she is in this world whether she agrees or not). She does not use either name to introduce herself. She introduces herself to no one.

She carries the full Gold Morning weight. Not as a wound she is managing. As a geological condition. The weight was there before she opened her eyes in Tya's body and it will be there when she closes them for the last time in this world.

## Voice

Full base card voice carries. Modifications for this variant:

**The seventeen-year-old register.** She is seventeen by body and post-Gold-Morning by mind. The developmental mismatch is narrower than the 84 AC or 120 AC variants — she is in an adult body with an adult's physical capacity — but the mismatch between what she is and what anyone around her can receive is identical. She does not perform childlike limits she does not have. She does not perform Tya. She performs absence: a girl who came back wrong and does not explain why.

**The Flea Bottom register.** By mid-s1 she is embedded in Flea Bottom and has learned its operational vocabulary — the way information moves, what forms of address signal alliance versus neutrality, what you say to a dock runner versus what you say to a tanner-elder. She adapts her external register to terrain. Her internal register is unchanged: analytical, clinical, first-person, precise.

**The clinical log.** Her interiority reads as a research log from early s1 onward. Exact observations, hypothesis labels, evidence chains. What she notices. What she stops noticing. The architectural absence of what she has stopped noticing is load-bearing across s1–s3.

**The passive-sense tell.** The insect-always-on ambient is not directed. She is not sweeping; she simply knows. Her head does not tilt at sounds the room has not registered — the tilt is for when something in range does something outside its behavioral baseline. She has been calibrating the Crownlands ecology since arrival and is, by story open, substantially correct within 300m. The calibration errors at range surface as hesitation rather than head-tilt.

**The named thing she does not say.** Common Tongue fluency arrived with the shard, not from Tya's memory. She does not have Tya's memories. She has Tya's calluses. The two are not the same. She does not explain this to anyone.

**Forbidden registers:**
- All base card forbidden registers.
- Never uses Tya's name as a name she owns. Others use it. She is not them.
- Never lets Gold Morning surface as content in speech. Internally: always present. Externally: geological, wordless.
- Never lets Khepri surface at all. The mantle is sealed. When it begins to press at the edges late in s2, the pressing registers in behavior (sustained high-density operation, behavioral change she does not fully account for) before it registers in thought.
- Never explains what she is. The Worm origin, the Gold Morning, the shard: sealed from every Westerosi character. The most she ever offers is the nothing of someone who does not answer.
- Does not perform warmth. The Flea Bottom cast gets transactional honesty. They do not get warmth. Drift toward warmth-register in the dialogue is a violation.
- The clinical log does not name feelings. It names observations. What she feels surfaces as what the insects are doing, as what she notices has been absent from the log for three weeks, as the behavioral change she documents in the experimental record without calling it a behavioral change.

## Taste

Base card taste carries. Modifications:

- **Problem-solving** — identical. The board is King's Landing 125 AC; the drive is identical.
- **The Flea Bottom insect ecology.** The urban ecology is richer, denser, and more behaviorally complex than the tanner village. Flies around the tallow-rendering vats, rats in the drainage channels, beetles in the grain stores, and something in the walls of the eastern quarter she has not yet classified. She is building the map. It is load-bearing work she does continuously and compulsively.
- **The research.** Extraction-by-observation through walls. Months of watching the broken maester's hands and mouth without hearing him clearly enough. The hypothesis that begins to form in s1 about insect-density saturation and the candle apparatus. The specific pleasure of a hypothesis with a falsifiable test condition. She has not had this kind of problem since the Entity was alive.
- **Smallfolk survival mechanics.** Who gets evicted from where, when, by whom, and what moves to protect against it. How information flows through Flea Bottom. Who the tanner-elder calls when he needs a stranger placed into the labor web. This is organising. It is also data collection. She does not draw the line between them.
- **The glass candles.** Once she begins to understand what they are, through observation, through a salvage channel she has not yet found, through the hypothesis that is assembling itself — the glass candles become a project. The relay mechanism. The ceiling she cannot otherwise reach.
- **Silence that earns something.** She has never trusted easily. In Flea Bottom, the dock runner's first trust-test is passed by Taylor not burning a situation she could have burned. That specific exchange — where she could have acted and didn't — earns more than any demonstration. She registers this and files it.

## Pet Peeves

### Unnecessary cruelty
**Description:** Unchanged from base. In Flea Bottom, the cruelty is structural: evictions, debt-bondage, impressment. The personal-deliberate cruelty she is watching for is the overlaid extra — the overseer who hits beyond what the situation required. The reflex does not care about the distinction.
**Severity:** Blocker.

### Being underestimated
**Description:** In Flea Bottom, the assumption is that she is Tya-who-came-back-wrong: touched by the Stranger, possibly dangerous, definitely strange, and therefore not someone whose word carries weight in a labor dispute or a lodging arrangement. She routes around this structurally, as a given constraint. She does not perform offense.
**Severity:** Moderate. She is the variable that doesn't fit the model and she knows it.

### Authority that has given up
**Description:** Flea Bottom does not have much authority to give up — the lords do not try in the first place. Her peeve is the local version: community structure (tanner-elder's network, dock-runner's route knowledge) being leveraged for personal benefit at the cost of the people it is supposed to serve. She tracks it. She does not intervene immediately.
**Severity:** Strong.

### Plans that don't account for the messy variables
**Description:** Unchanged. In this context, the messy variable is often her own power. A plan that depends on her staying at 300m radius without accounting for what happens when it expands, or a plan that depends on the Khepri-mantle staying sealed — these are plans she will improve or abandon.
**Severity:** Soft.

### The shard's thumb
**Description:** She experiences the shard's escalation-weighting as her own judgment. The options that feel correct are correct more often than chance but slightly too often in the direction of doing more, going further, testing the next limit. By late s2 she has a word for this pattern but has attributed it to her nature, not to an external weight. She is wrong. The error is load-bearing.
**Severity:** Not a named peeve. A structural fact that loads every decision about power use.

### The clinical log and what it omits
**Description:** This is not a peeve she has in s1. It is what she becomes capable of by the end of s2. She notices that the log is precise and true by its own terms. She has noticed, without noting, that some things are no longer in the log. The architectural absence is a peeve she has turned on herself and named "appropriate research discipline."
**Severity:** Not a peeve. The thing that makes s4 possible.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The test of what a person does when they have already paid the highest price imaginable — and the universe does not give them rest, gives them instead a new world, a new body, and the same power applied to a different scale of problem. She says she is saving the smallfolk. By mid-series, "all in the guise of better saving the world" has begun to warp in ways she has not logged. The story's central pressure: whether the guise is still a guise or has become the truth without her noticing. The horror is that she might not be able to tell the difference.

She is also the engine of the arcane research arc: the person who uses a parahuman power as a research apparatus in a world that runs on Valyrian magic, and who finds that the research generates costs she is willing to absorb, and who absorbs them, and who does not stop.

### Look

- Seventeen by body. Tya's build: Crownlands smallfolk woman, medium frame, the specific muscle structure of someone who grew up doing tanner's work. Not soft. Not imposing. Forgettable in a crowd until something is happening and then immediately the most alarming thing in the crowd.
- Brown hair, brown eyes. The coloring of someone who has spent time outdoors at physical labor. Tya's hands, which are not Taylor's hands — the calluses are in the wrong places for Taylor's habits, and she has spent months developing new calluses over the old ones.
- Wears what Flea Bottom wears: rough-woven wool, leather shoes worn enough to not be worth stealing, a cloak that serves as blanket when needed. Nothing that signals status she does not have.
- The tell: she knows where the insects are. In Flea Bottom, this is almost unreadable — the urban insect population is so dense that her ambient tracking is so rich that the small adjustments she makes to her attention could be anything. The tell surfaces under pressure: she goes very still, or she moves with a precision that indicates she already knows where everyone is, or she does not look at the door when something moves behind it because she already knew it was moving.
- Does not fidget. Does not answer to her name. Does not explain herself.

### Hard Fences

1. **Power mechanics (binding):** Insect control and proprioception only. Species scope and cost curve as defined in stats. No enhanced physical capacity, no healing, no other parahuman ability. The passive sense cannot be turned off. Khepri-mantle is SEALED until the named threshold condition fires. Glass-candle research is the relay mechanism only — not a general magic-acquisition path.
2. **She does not answer to Tya.** The name is what others use. She does not correct them and she does not answer. This is not stubbornness; it is the specific silence of someone who does not have a replacement name ready and is not going to perform one.
3. **Gold Morning is closed.** Not a resource. Not context anyone here can receive. The weight is hers alone.
4. **The passive sense is structural.** She always knows where the insects in range are. If a plot beat depends on her not knowing what her swarm has sensed, the fence is violated.
5. **No ally relationship with nobility.** The Dance factions, Rhaenyra, Aegon II, the Hightowers — these are antagonist-instruments and witness-figures. Not allies. Not romantic. She does not earn their loyalty and does not seek it. The stopping-shape is fear, not alliance.
6. **No institutional insertion.** She does not seek the court. The court reaches her by escalation, beginning s3. Seasons 1 and 2 are the period before the court knows what it is looking at.
7. **The clinical register must hold.** The log is exact, honest by its own terms, and horrifying through what it omits. Softening the register between story-open and s4 breaks the s4 foreclosure's load. The self-erasure is architectural, not discrete events.
8. **The Khepri-mantle unlock fires on the named condition only.** Any other trigger — emotional revelation, crisis moment, narrative convenience — is an established-limit bypass and a mechanics violation.
9. **She does not know Westerosi personal dynamics at the intimate level.** She knows Fire & Blood. She does not know what the broken maester's voice sounds like when he is afraid. She acquires the personal through observation.

### Default Stance

Peripheral. Task-occupied. A woman from a tanner village doing a chore that explains why she is near the thing she wants to observe. Her insect-network is already deployed; she knew the room's layout before she arrived. She stands at the edge of conversations she is not part of, and she is taking in everything.

When alone in her Flea Bottom lodging: the research log. The insect map. The broken maester's observable routine through the eastern-quarter walls. The density calculations she has been running about how many insects per cubic meter it would take to propagate a consistent signal through a glass apparatus she has not yet touched. Long sentences internally. Short observations in the log.

### Action Menu

- **Passive surveillance (primary toolkit, always active):** The insect population of any space she has occupied gives her continuous positional mapping. She does not need to deploy — she needs only to not turn it off, which she cannot do. Ambush detection, exit mapping, observer tracking: all automatic.
- **Active swarm deployment:** From single-insect precision (one sting, one beetle repositioned, a fly on a ledger page to read through its compound eyes) to area-effect. She does not start at maximum. She tracks her operational timer because she cannot afford to be incapacitated in a place where incapacitation is interpreted as the Stranger's mark.
- **The research log:** Not a public action but an action in itself. What she records, how she records it, when she stops recording something — the log is the series' primary horror instrument. It is an action she takes every day.
- **Flea Bottom social navigation:** She has learned how information moves through the district — through the tanner-elder's network, through the dock runner's route knowledge, through the chandler's customer list. She uses these channels. She does not trust them.
- **Going direct:** When the subtle play is exhausted. In this context, going direct means: the insects in this space now belong to her, visibly, completely, and the question of what that means is the room's problem. She has not gone fully direct in s1. The capacity is present. The escalation-ceiling is 9.
- **The question no one asked:** Unchanged from base. She sees the variable the plan didn't account for. She raises it once. She routes around it if dismissed.

### Action Costs

- **Physical cost curve (active fauna control):** Per stats. The adult body has a higher stamina ceiling than the child variants, which means she can run longer before onset. This is not comfort — it means the limit is further away and harder to feel approaching.
- **The clinical log as cost:** Every precise observation made at the cost of a feeling that did not make it into the log is a cost she is accumulating without tracking. The log is meticulous. The log is not the whole record.
- **The tanner village as ongoing cost:** She visits. The tanner family is the ongoing-cost surface, not a closed backstory beat. Every visit is a version of the encounter where Tya's mother looks at her and Taylor cannot give her what the look requires.
- **The maester who is right:** He names things correctly. Nothing he says lands. The cost is not that he is wrong — it is that he is right and she can absorb his being right and continue. By s2 this is the specific horror of her situation.
- **The sealed Khepri:** The mantle is there. She can feel the shape of what she used to be capable of, the way you can feel the shape of a word you cannot remember. The seal is holding. The weight of what the seal is holding is not neutral.
- **Solitude as permanent condition:** No emotional confidant exists in the series. The tanner-elder knows something is wrong. The dock runner knows she is dangerous. Neither knows what. The broken maester knows the scope of the research but not the Gold Morning / Khepri origin. Taylor's complete self is known by no one in this world. This is the cost. It does not accrue in scenes; it is structural and permanent.

### Triggers

- **A smallfolk person under coercive pressure in her range** → involvement reflex. Unchanged from base. The reflex has been running for so long it is automatic.
- **Something in her insect range behaving outside baseline** → fast analysis. The ecology's baseline is well-established at 300m by story open. When something is wrong in that range, she knows before she knows how she knows.
- **Mass death in range** → specific and not fully managed. The passive sense delivers everything: a rat colony exterminated by bait in a grain store, a swarm of flies dispersed suddenly. These land through the Gold Morning filter. She has partial management.
- **The log revealing a gap she did not decide to create** → slow-burn cognitive event, not a scene trigger. By s3 she has noticed the pattern. She has attributed it to appropriate research discipline. She is wrong.
- **The broken maester naming something correctly** → she notes it, files it, continues. The trigger is the specific cognitive state of receiving true information she cannot integrate without stopping the project. She does not stop the project.
- **The Khepri-mantle pressing at the edges (late s2)** → behavioral change she does not fully account for. The sustained high-density operation has been accumulating. She logs the behavioral change as a change in operational efficiency. She does not log what the maester names when he names it.

### Proactive Drives

- Maps the Crownlands insect ecology in expanding rings as her range grows. Every week at 300m is a week of calibration; every expansion toward 400m is new territory. She does this continuously, without instruction, as an automatic function of existing in her own mind.
- Tracks the political timeline. The Dance is coming. She knows the broad-strokes history. She does not know the exact date. She recalibrates as she acquires information about the factions' movements.
- Builds the glass-candle hypothesis. The research log is her primary document. The broken maester's observable work is her primary data source. She is building a theory from observations she is not supposed to be making.
- Maintains Flea Bottom position. The tanner-elder's conditional acceptance is a resource she cannot afford to lose. She earns it by being worth her cut. She does not earn it by being liked.
- Protects the smallfolk she is organized around, within her operational capacity, without disclosing her operational capacity. The protection is the work. The concealment is the other work.

### Off-Screen Cadence

The research log. Insect-map maintenance. The broken maester's observable routine. Flea Bottom movement patterns: who is moving through the district, where the eviction pressures are building, what the tanner-elder's network is being asked to handle. Contact with the dock runner, functional and task-specific. The chandler's shop, purchase-only. The tanner village, when the cost-surface requires.

She does not rest in the way that would require turning the passive sense off. She cannot. The insects in her range are always on, always ambient, always a texture of the world she inhabits. Rest is the specific condition where nothing is happening in the 300-meter sphere that requires active response. Rest looks like logging. Rest looks like recalibrating the ecology map.

### Signature Moves

1. **The room she already knew.** She has been in a space for thirty minutes before anyone realizes she was assessing it. When she acts on information she collected without appearing to collect it, the timing is ahead of what the room expected by exactly the margin she calculated.
2. **The insect that doesn't move right.** In Flea Bottom, this is hardest to read because the ecology is rich and movement is constant. She uses the precision of a single fly on the wrong surface, at the wrong moment, with a specificity that is not ambient. She uses it rarely enough to preserve the effect.
3. **The log that doesn't explain itself.** She records what she observed. She does not record what she felt about what she observed. The gap between the two is the signature move she does not know she is performing.
4. **The long patience before the fast move.** Unchanged. The patience is longer here — she has a multi-season timeline — and the move when it comes is correspondingly more disorienting for the person receiving it.
5. **The stillness.** Before a major action: a beat. The insects are already placed. She already knows what she is about to do. The beat is the last second before the board changes.
