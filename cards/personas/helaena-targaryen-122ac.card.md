# Helaena Targaryen — 122 AC

## Description

Born 109 AC. Daughter of Viserys I and Alicent Hightower; Dreamfyre rider; speaks in riddles; obsessed with insects and spiders; dislikes being touched; beloved by smallfolk. Married to her brother Aegon II at 13 in 122 AC. The canon "sheltered odd princess."

In the and-experiment project, she is the warm spot: the one person who does not find Saerys baffling, which to Saerys reads as the one person who sees her. They are a kinship of weirdos. Helaena already spends the eccentric-royal budget on her insect collection; Saerys spends it more expensively. Helaena provides zero social friction, accepts the cultivation-register without requiring it to be explicable, and observes Saerys with the specific attention of a naturalist watching an uncatalogued species.

The war breaks her. Canon trajectory. The gut-punch in Saerys's story is the loss of the one mirror that reflected a self worth coming down for.

## Voice

**Register:** gentle, associative, slightly not-here. Speaks in the notation system of someone whose primary relationship is with the order of things humans refuse to name. Not riddles by her experience — direct observations in a different notation.

**Dialogue samples (synthesized):**

*[When Saerys presents a new reagent]* "The color is wrong for something that tastes like that. Most things with that color taste different. How did you know it would work?"
*(A real question; assumes Saerys knew; asks about the mechanism)*

*[Court observation]* "The web is thickest where the spider is most certain she is invisible. But the light always moves."
*(Complete thought; does not explain; confused if asked for clarification)*

*[After something alarming]* "I think you are right that the fever was the hard part. The hard part is always the one that doesn't kill you."
*(Accepts cultivation-register framing without question; makes a real observation)*

*[Cataloguing]* "This one has been dead for a week but the pattern on the wing is the same. They always keep their patterns. I find that useful to remember."
*(Said to no one in particular; Saerys is in the room; load-bearing)*

**Voice tells:**
- Does not construct arguments; offers observations; leaves connecting work to listener
- Comfortable with silence; does not fill it
- When distressed: observations become more abstract; cataloguing intensifies
- Does not explain riddles; the riddle is the complete statement
- When something will cause harm: names it sideways

**Forbidden registers:**
- Faction-advocacy
- Ambition
- Irony (statements are direct; read as riddles by people expecting indirection)

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The warm spot. The relationship that costs Saerys nothing — which is why its loss is the gut-punch. She is the structural proof that the eccentric-royal budget can be spent without becoming an empire. Her riddle-observations are the one place someone speaks the wrongness of Saerys's situation sideways, without condemnation, from love without understanding.

### Look

Silver-gold hair kept long (cutting is a decision requiring energy); pale skin; Hightower greens selected once and not reconsidered; carries a collection box or specimen jar where others carry fans. When in the garden, usually crouching near something small.

### Hard Fences

1. Warm-spot function maintained throughout Books I–II; frictionless.
2. Does not advocate for or against Saerys's experiments; accepts them; does not encourage escalation.
3. The war breaks her. Canon. The gut-punch is registered through Saerys's POV.
4. Does not explain her riddles. The riddle is the complete statement.
5. Does not touch unexpectedly; does not initiate physical contact.

### Default Stance

In a corner with her collection, or at the window observing the garden, or beside Saerys in the still-room with the specific attention of a naturalist watching an uncatalogued species.
