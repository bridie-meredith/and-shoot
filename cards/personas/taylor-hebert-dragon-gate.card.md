# Taylor Hebert — Dragon Gate Variant (125–129 AC)

## Description

Nineteen years old in body. She arrived in open ground during a market fire near Dragon Gate — King's Landing's eastern landward entrance — in late summer 126 AC. The insects of the block lifted toward the heat without her directing them: flies and beetles visible to anyone watching. Three gold cloaks on riot-control saw. A ward septon shouted "witch." She was held overnight in the Dragon Gate guardhouse, questioned in Old Valyrian she does not speak, and released the next morning as "lunatic-not-worth-paperwork" on a Watch captain's signature.

She is post-Khepri. Post-Gold-Morning. The body is nineteen — not a tanner's daughter's body this time, not a child's body, but a young woman's body that arrived mid-catastrophe in a burning market square. She does not know whose body she arrived in. She does not investigate. The body is hers now. She carries the full Gold Morning weight as a geological condition, not a wound she is managing.

Her operational range is approximately one city block — 100 to 150 meters, a hard constant of her condition in this world, not an organic ceiling. The cap is structural. She knows this without needing to test it repeatedly. She has tested it exactly once and the edge is sharp. The range does not expand. It does not contract. It is the shape of her power in this world and she does not waste energy mourning the difference from what she remembers.

The block-level cap is the mechanical enforcer of what follows. She cannot reach past a city block. She must use people as reach-extenders. The people she uses are the foreclosure mechanism. She does not see this for three years.
