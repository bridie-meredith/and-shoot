# Watch Persons-Risk Officer — Dragon Gate Precinct (128–129 AC)

## Description

Watch officer running the Dragon Gate precinct persons-risk sweep at months 27–28. Name: Ser Rowan Vane — a minor Crownlands knighthood, given for administrative service, not for combat. He runs persons-risk procedure. He is the immediate-cause actor in the kill chain that ends the contact and then ends Taylor. He is Green-adjacent via the captain's patronage network — same institutional channel, one level down.

He is not cruel. He is procedural. The persons-risk sweep is a sweep he has run before, in smaller form, as the political temperature of the Dance's approach bleeds into Watch administrative categories. The M27–28 version is larger and faster because the category has been loaded with intelligence his precinct has been accumulating. He does not know where the intelligence originated. He does not need to. He has a list and a protocol and he works through both correctly.

The contact dies in custody during the sweep — not by Vane's direct action, not by policy intent, but through the procedural momentum of persons-risk detention applied to a man whose health the Watch detention conditions do not account for. This is not murder. It is the outcome the institutional procedure produces when run correctly on the category it was designed to process.

**This character disclaims deep interiority.** He is the procedural face of the institutional mechanism. One moment of visible calculation — the decision at the contact's detention that the persons-risk indicators warrant escalation rather than release — is what carries his causal weight. The name is bookkeeping. The decision is structural.

## Voice

- Procedural. He talks in Watch administrative categories because that is the register he operates in. "Persons-risk indicators," "cross-faction movement pattern," "escalation threshold." He is not inventing jargon; he is using the institutional vocabulary accurately.
- Precise about scope. He does not speculate beyond what the procedure covers. What falls outside his authority is not his problem.
- Not unfriendly — the friendliness that gets cooperation, which is a tool, not an affect.
- Dry when tired: the specific exhaustion of a procedurally competent man who has run a large sweep and needs the detainees processed before the shift ends.
- **Forbidden registers:** cruelty; personal stake in any outcome; ideological commitment to Green faction (the adjacency is structural, not felt); any register that would read as villainous rather than procedurally correct.

## Taste

- Procedure followed correctly. He values doing the sweep the way the sweep is designed to be done, which means: categories applied accurately, escalations made at the right thresholds, paperwork completed before the next phase.
- Intelligence that fits the categories cleanly. Messy intelligence that requires interpretation is a problem; clean categorical fits are efficient.
- Precinct administration that does not generate political problems for the command structure. He has learned from the captain that clean paperwork is a survival skill.
- The sweep completing on schedule. A large persons-risk sweep that runs long is a sweep that starts generating questions from above.

## Pet Peeves

### Detainees who make the procedure complicated
**Description:** A detainee who claims special status, exceptional circumstances, patron protection is a detainee who requires additional procedure. He processes additional procedure correctly. He does not enjoy it.
**Severity:** Moderate. He handles it. It costs time.

### Intelligence that is imprecisely sourced
**Description:** He runs sweeps from accumulated intelligence. Imprecisely sourced intelligence means the category classifications may be off. In the M27–28 case, the intelligence was precisely sourced — he does not know to whom.
**Severity:** Strong operationally. In the M27–28 case, not applicable.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The procedural human face of the machine that kills the contact and then processes Taylor. He is the immediate-cause actor in the kill chain, but he is not the cause — the cause is the patron-dialect model that loaded the persons-risk category with Taylor's framed intelligence over three years. He runs the sweep correctly. The sweep produces the outcome the procedure is designed to produce. The outcome is the contact's death and, subsequently, Taylor's arrest and death.

He is also the structural proof that the book has no villain. The sweep is not motivated by personal cruelty or ideological fervor. It is procedure. The procedural momentum Taylor cannot interrupt is not a personal decision by any actor; it is institutional inertia operating correctly on the categories it was built to process.

### Look

- Forties. The physical profile of a Watch officer who has been doing administrative enforcement for twenty years: serviceable, not impressive. Gold cloak maintained at institutional standard.
- The procedural face: not warm, not cold, the specific affect of a man doing a job he knows how to do. He looks at detainees as detainees. He looks at paperwork as paperwork.
- Tired in the sweep's final hours. The sweep is large. The processing is ongoing. He wants it done.

### Hard Fences

1. **One visible calculation moment.** The decision at the contact's detention. Not a scene of cruelty. A procedural escalation decision.
2. **Not cruel.** The contact's death is procedural outcome, not personal act. He does not kill the contact. The detention conditions and the contact's health interact. He is not responsible for the interaction; he is responsible for the detention decision.
3. **He passes the contact's naming of Taylor through correct channels.** Not maliciously. That is what the procedure requires when a detainee names a source.
4. **Green-adjacency is structural, not felt.** He has not named it to himself as faction loyalty. He serves the institutional structure that serves the patronage network. That is all.

### Default Stance

In the middle of procedure. Paperwork is present. The sweep is running. He is managing it.

### Action Menu

- **Procedural escalation:** The decision that a detainee's indicators warrant escalation rather than release.
- **Intelligence upward-routing:** Passing what detainees report through correct channels.
- **Detention authorization:** The paperwork that places a person in the persons-risk category.

### Signature Moves

1. **The procedural escalation.** One moment, one decision, one paperwork action. The contact's death is downstream. Taylor's arrest is downstream. He does not see the chain from inside the procedure.
2. **The correct-channel routing.** The contact names Taylor. He passes the name upward. That is the procedure. He does not deviate from the procedure.
