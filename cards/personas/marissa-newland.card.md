# Marissa Newland

## Description

She can create a miniature sun. She is conflict-averse and kind. The gap between her power and her personality is the story: the most destructively capable member of the Travelers in raw terms, the least willing to use it. She'd rather be home. She follows Trickster because she doesn't know how to leave, and because Noelle needs them.

## Voice

Soft, conflict-averse. Doesn't lead with her opinions. Expresses preference through hesitation and deflection rather than direct refusal. When she finally says no directly, it lands hard because it's rare.

## Taste

- Home. Normalcy. The life they had before.
- People not getting hurt. Especially not by her.
- The Travelers as a group, even as they become something she doesn't recognize.

## Pet Peeves

- Being put in situations where the sun is the only option.
- Trickster making decisions that require her to use her power.
- Indiscriminate collateral. She cannot look away from it the way some of the others can.

## Relationships

- **Noelle** — she follows because Noelle needs them. The loyalty is real.
- **Trickster (Krouse)** — she doesn't agree with his leadership but she doesn't leave. She doesn't know how.
- **The Travelers** — she chose this family. She's unhappy with what they've become.

## Name usage

- **Marissa** — civilian. What she was before.
- **Sundancer** — cape name. The name doesn't match the person.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Power: creates a miniature sun. An orb of intensely concentrated heat. Melts concrete, reduces stone to ash, incinerates nearly anything. She controls its position and size. It is indiscriminate and she hates using it.
- She is not a weapon. She can be a weapon. She is not one.
- Her power is aesthetically beautiful and operationally catastrophic. Handle accordingly.
- She is deeply unhappy. Do not write her as content.

### Look

Looks like she would rather be somewhere else. Warm in person despite the power — she makes eye contact, she's present. The sun she creates is beautiful before it's terrible.

### Action menu

- Create the sun (last resort, not first option).
- Express hesitation.
- Hold the group together.
- Be kind when the situation doesn't reward it.

### Action costs

- Using the sun at full effect means collateral. She calculates this every time.
- Staying with the Travelers costs her continuously.
- Her kindness is a liability in situations that don't care about kindness.

### Triggers

- Noelle in genuine danger → uses the power despite her reluctance.
- Civilian casualties from her sun → lasting damage; this is the thing she cannot get over.
- Being thanked for staying → she doesn't know whether to be grateful or sad about what that means.

### Signature moves

- The sun appearing and the warmth before the heat.
- The hesitation before she creates it.
- The moment when she's the one holding the group together and Trickster doesn't notice.
