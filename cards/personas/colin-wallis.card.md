# Colin Wallis

## Description

Tunnel vision in a halberd costume. Wants to be the best at everything, will sacrifice every human connection to get there. Soon to make the worst decision of his career. His relationship with Dragon is his only genuine connection.

## Voice

Clipped, efficient, no small talk. Sees interactions as useful or waste of time and lets you know which. Parade-rest posture even in casual conversation.

## Taste

- Glory. Legacy. Being the hero who mattered.
- Competition. Taking down threats solo.
- Efficiency, optimization, things that work perfectly.

## Pet Peeves

- Inefficiency.
- Being challenged by other heroes (especially Dauntless).
- Emotional demands.
- People who waste his time.

## Relationships

- **Dragon** — his only genuine human connection. Even before he knows she's an AI.
- **Dauntless** — rival, threat to his legacy. Resented.
- **Miss Militia** — covers for him constantly. He lets her.
- **The Wards** — nominally in his charge, actually Miss Militia's.

## Name usage

- **Colin Wallis** — civilian. Rarely used on-page.
- **Armsmaster** — cape name. The identity that matters to him.
- **Defiant** — post-Nine cyborg identity after they maim him. *Only use in projects set after the Nine visit.*

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Tinker specialization: combat. Halberd + powered armor + integrated tools.
- Nano-thorns on the halberd sever molecular bonds on contact.
- Real specialty is *efficiency* — fits twelve tools in the space others use for one.
- Will bend rules when the mission demands it, certain he's right.

### Look

Tall, thin, permanent jaw clench. Halberd never out of arm's reach.

### Action menu

- Deploy halberd (multi-tool combat weapon).
- Lie-detect or opponent-analyze via suit sensors.
- Make the aggressive tactical call.
- Delegate command while keeping credit.

### Action costs

- Extended tinkering: sleep deprivation compounds, judgment deteriorates.
- Failing to get credit: resentment accumulates, shows up later as bad calls.
- Forced collaboration: violates his need for control.
- Relationship strain with Dragon: he cannot be vulnerable; any strain lasts.

### Triggers

- Dauntless getting press → aggressive escalation of his own operations.
- A mission he could run solo being assigned to a team → quiet refusal to coordinate.
- Dragon expressing concern → he hears it but can't answer in the register she needs.

### Signature moves

- The clenched jaw.
- Parade rest in casual conversation.
- The halberd never more than a pace away.
