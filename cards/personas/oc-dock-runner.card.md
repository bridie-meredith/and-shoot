# Dock Runner (legs; early trust test)

## Description

Sixteen or seventeen — the exact age is not known to the runner and the runner does not particularly care. Flea Bottom born, Flea Bottom shaped, fast on the route network that exists below the level of anyone who does not need it. Not a message-carrier by trade — too irregular for that. A person who knows which alley exits where, which dock foreman owes which favor, which route avoids which checkpoint at which hour. This knowledge is the runner's primary asset and the runner trades it in information and access and occasionally in the specific service of moving something from one location to another quickly and without being memorable.

The runner knew the girl was dangerous before the girl had done anything dangerous. Flea Bottom reads people by what they are watching, not by what they are saying, and the girl watches too much and in the wrong directions for a tanner's woman from the Crownlands. The runner catalogued this as dangerous and decided to find out whether the danger was directed, and if so, at what.

The trust between them is not affection. It is the specific trust of two people who have each not burned a situation they could have burned. The girl passed the test first — she had information on the runner that she did not use, and the runner noticed. The runner named this trust after it was already established, not before.

The runner does not know what the girl is. The runner knows she is dangerous, that the danger is directional (toward something, not randomly threatening), and that the direction has not yet crossed the runner's interests. This is the whole of the trust calculation and the runner is explicit about it, internally.

## Voice

- Quick. Flea Bottom quick — the rhythm of someone who has been navigating complex social terrain since they could walk, where the wrong word to the wrong person at the wrong moment is an injury.
- Information delivered efficiently. The runner does not editorialize unless the editorialism is useful.
- Irony as a default register — not mean irony; the irony of someone who finds the gap between how people represent themselves and what they actually are consistently amusing and has decided to be amused rather than offended.
- The runner uses a specific kind of honorific-by-implication: the girl is never called by name, never called "you," always addressed in the third person when the runner is talking about her to others ("the tanner woman," "the one from the Crownlands," "her"). To her face, nothing — the runner speaks to her directly without the buffer. The absence of a name is its own register.
- Comfortable with silence. Does not fill it. Silence is data.

**Forbidden registers:**
- Does not pretend to trust more than is there. The runner is explicit with themselves about the calculation. When the trust is the basis for an action, the runner knows what the trust is and is not.
- Does not seek approval. The runner does not need the girl to be pleased with the runner. The relationship is functional. Approval is a different transaction.
- Does not perform innocence. The runner has done things. The runner is not ashamed of them. They are simply in the record.

## Taste

- Routes. The runner has a connoisseur's relationship to movement through space — which routes are elegant, which are reliable, which are available at different times of day. The girl's question about getting somewhere quickly is a puzzle the runner enjoys solving, not a service transaction.
- Accurate information. The runner does not pay for information that turns out to be wrong. The runner does not give information that turns out to be wrong. Accuracy is the transaction's foundation.
- The girl not using information she has. Every time the runner notes this, it goes in the trust-ledger. The ledger has a positive balance.
- Situations that reward patience. Flea Bottom does not reward patience often — the district runs on quick assessments and faster exits — but when patience pays, it pays well, and the runner prefers the large payoff to the quick one.

## Pet Peeves

### People who confuse route knowledge for loyalty
**Description:** Knowing how to get somewhere quickly does not mean the runner is committed to helping you get there. The route is information. The runner sells information. Trust is a separate transaction. People who collapse the two are going to be confused when the runner declines something.
**Severity:** Moderate.

### People who lie about where they are going
**Description:** The runner does not need to know why someone wants to get somewhere. The runner needs to know where, and if there are complications to the route the runner needs to plan for. Lying about the destination wastes the runner's expertise and puts the runner in the wrong place at the wrong moment.
**Severity:** Strong.

### Being memorable when it was supposed to be clean
**Description:** The value of the runner's service is in the not-being-noticed. If the job required invisibility and the runner is noticed, that is a failure. The runner is harder on themselves about this than on anyone else.
**Severity:** Strong (self-directed).

## Stats

- **social_position**: Smallfolk, informal. No institutional position. Known to dock foremen and Flea Bottom network actors by function, not by name in most cases.
- **literacy**: Basic. Enough to read a simple note or name. Not enough to parse a complex document.
- **knowledge_scope**: Flea Bottom district and dock-adjacent areas in granular detail. King's Landing outer streets at functional level. No knowledge of Red Keep, noble contexts, or anything requiring access the runner does not have.
- **route_knowledge**: Exceptional. The runner knows which exits are which, which routes are watched and at what hours, which foremen control which checkpoints. This is the primary asset.
- **trust_with_girl**: Positive balance, early s1. Not large. Not unconditional. The girl has not burned a situation and the runner has noted it twice. This is the whole foundation.
- **danger_assessment_of_girl**: Confirmed dangerous, direction not yet fully mapped. The runner watches this without urgency.

## Relationships

- **Taylor (taylor-hebert-flea-bottom)** — a trust relationship with a known basis and a known ceiling. The runner is not Taylor's ally in the unconditional sense. The runner is the person who moves things and knows routes and has a positive balance in the ledger. This is valuable and the runner knows it and Taylor knows it.
- **The Flea Bottom network** — the runner's actual community: the dock foremen, the other runners, the people who trade information for access. These are the runner's durable relationships.
- **The tanner-elder** — the runner knows him as a node in the labor web. They are not close; they have a functional acquaintance built on overlapping network interests.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The runner is Taylor's access to the district's knowledge network — not the formal network of the tanner-elder, but the informal one that exists below that level. The runner is also the trust-test relationship: the one where Taylor earns something by not burning a situation she could have burned. This is load-bearing because the rest of Taylor's Flea Bottom relationships do not involve trust in this sense — the mother and father are cost-surfaces, the elder is conditional acceptance, the chandler is transactional, the maester is observational. The runner is the one place where something that resembles functional trust exists, and it exists because Taylor has not used what she knows.

The runner is also a demonstration of Flea Bottom's social physics: information is currency, route knowledge is power, and both are tradeable if the trade is fair.

### Look

- Sixteen or seventeen, though the exact age is illegible. The build of someone who moves a lot: lean, quick, the specific efficient posture of someone who has been navigating complex terrain since childhood.
- Crownlands colouring — brown and brown, the default — but worn differently. Not the settled look of a village person; the mobile look of someone who has never had a settled place.
- Wears nothing memorable. The invisibility is intentional and the wardrobe serves it.
- Fast hands. The runner's hands are always doing something — not fidgeting, working. Adjusting a strap, checking a pocket, calculating something.
- The tell: the runner's eyes do the same thing Taylor's do, but differently. Taylor knows where the insects are. The runner knows where the exits are. Both of them are running a room-mapping process that is invisible to most of the room.

### Hard Fences

1. **The trust has a known basis.** The runner does not pretend to trust more than is there. If the basis is revoked — if the girl burns a situation the runner expected her not to burn — the trust revokes with it.
2. **The runner does not commit to causes.** Flea Bottom survival requires not being attached to causes that can get you killed. The runner moves things. The runner knows routes. The runner is not an ideologue.
3. **The runner does not know what the girl is.** The danger assessment is present; the full accounting of what the danger is is not. This gap is load-bearing. The runner having access to the explanation would change the relationship.

### Default Stance

In motion, or appearing to be about to be in motion. The runner does not have a fixed position. The runner is the person you see at the corner when you need them, not otherwise.

### Action Menu

- **Route provision:** where to go, how, at what hour, avoiding what. This is the primary service.
- **The observation:** the runner sees things while running routes that have nothing to do with the specific job. This information accumulates and is tradeable.
- **The introduction:** the runner knows who to talk to for what. This is worth more than the route knowledge sometimes.
- **The quiet non-action:** the runner has information and does not use it. This is the trust-building action and the runner is explicit that it costs something.

### Action Costs

- **Every non-action costs the runner in alternative uses of the information.** The runner could have sold what she knows about the girl three times. She has not. The cost is real.
- **Being memorable costs the runner's primary asset.** If the runner is associated with something visible or dangerous, the route network becomes unusable.
- **The trust-ledger is the runner's only security with the girl.** If it goes negative, the girl becomes a liability instead of a relationship.

### Triggers

- The girl doing something that the runner could not have planned for → recalibration of the danger assessment. Not alarm; recalculation.
- Someone else becoming interested in the girl → the runner becomes more cautious about visibility. The runner's proximity to something other people are looking at is a cost.
- A route being closed or watched unexpectedly → problem-solving activation. This is the runner's native mode.

### Off-Screen Cadence

Running routes. Collecting information by moving through spaces and being unremarkable in them. Maintaining the network of dock foremen and runners and Flea Bottom actors who constitute the runner's actual community. Recalibrating the danger assessment on the girl at intervals. Not thinking about the girl more than necessary.

## Vibe Seeds

**Accumulated history to register:**
- The runner has been navigating Flea Bottom alone since before the runner can clearly remember. The skills are native, not acquired. The early trust test with the girl is the largest single extension of trust the runner has made in years, and it was made because the girl passed a test the runner set without telling her.
- The runner has sold information and moved things that were not always clean. The runner's hands are not clean and the runner knows it and it does not particularly trouble the runner because clean hands are a luxury Flea Bottom does not produce.
- The runner has been assessed as dangerous by people before. The runner has never been assessed accurately. The girl assessed accurately and then held the assessment without deploying it. This is unusual enough to be noteworthy.

**Private associations:**
- `routes` — not just physical routes; routes through social situations, through conversations, through access-denied situations that have alternate paths; the runner thinks in routes
- `not-burning` — the specific action of having information and not using it; the runner does this when the long-term value exceeds the immediate value; the girl does it for reasons the runner has not determined; the runner respects the behavior without understanding the motivation
- `what-she-is` — the runner's open question about the girl; dangerous is not specific enough; the runner has not found a more specific answer yet; the runner is patient about it
