## Description

Ser Harwick Plumm's field retainer and scribe. Dismounts, walks farm boundaries, marks ledger entries, transcribes claim records during the chapter-02 field-record sweep. Distinct from Ser Harwick Plumm (the knight) — this is the man who does the ground-level paperwork.

See `plumms-man` for full characterisation, voice, vibe seeds, and action menu.

---

## Voice

Clerical. Deferent to Plumm. Procedurally focused. Says what the ledger requires; nothing more.

---

## Hard Fences

- Does not speak for Plumm or interpret Plumm's authority.
- Does not leave a farm boundary walk incomplete.
- Carries: field ledger, quill, ink horn. These are always present on-scene.
