# Nymeria of the Summer Isles

## Description

An archer-guide from the Summer Isles, picked up during the Book II expeditions, who joins the entourage and stays. Deadpan, pragmatic, the conscience of the company. She is the one who survives — the witness on the deck at the end.

She observes the cultivation-register with anthropological precision: a behavioral system with consistent rules, producing predictable outputs. She asks questions. The questions are precise. She already knows the answers. She asks because someone in the room should be saying the accurate thing.

**She survives.** She asks the meta-question spoken aloud in Book III: *"You can't audit your way out of this one. Stay. Grieve him. Be a person for one hour."* Saerys closes the book. They sail.

## Voice

**Register:** flat, precise, anthropologically curious. Summer Isles cadence under Common Tongue. Does not perform exotic difference; not performing anything. Observations land with weight because they are not performed.

**Dialogue samples (synthesized):**

*[After Saerys closes the book]* "All right." *(She finds the ship.)*

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The witness who survives. The human record of what the cultivation-register refused to grieve. Puncture-voice alongside Daenys — where Daenys uses love, Nymeria uses anthropological accuracy. Together they ensure the comedy never reads as validation. The meta-question is her final on-page action of consequence.

### Look

Summer Isles build: broad-shouldered, dark, the competence of someone who has navigated many foreign roads. Moves economically. Dressed for utility.
