# Kayden Anders

## Description

The "reformed" racist. Left Kaiser, stopped the active Nazi operations, patrols independently, tells herself she's changed. She hasn't. She wants to be better — genuinely wants it — but her infrastructure is still the Empire: her friends, her community, her support system. She looks angelic. She's a Nazi. The custody battle over Aster is the leash Kaiser keeps around her neck.

## Voice

Self-justifying cadence. Explains her choices in a register that sounds reasonable and is not. When she talks about reform, she means it — which is worse than if she didn't.

## Taste

- Aster. Aster is everything.
- The idea of being better. Reform as aspiration even when it fails as practice.
- Order. Control over her own household.

## Pet Peeves

- Kaiser. The leverage. The custody arrangement that keeps her in orbit.
- Being called what she is.
- Failing to be what she wants to be, and knowing it.

## Relationships

- **Aster (daughter)** — the axis of her world. Kaiser uses Aster as leverage.
- **Kaiser (Max Anders)** — ex-husband. She left him. He kept the leash. She still gravitates toward his orbit because she has nowhere else to go.
- **The Empire** — the infrastructure she can't escape. Her friends are here. Her community is here.

## Name usage

- **Kayden Russell** — her maiden name, before Kaiser. What she was trying to get back to.
- **Kayden Anders** — what she became.
- **Purity** — her cape name. The irony is not lost on anyone.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Power: light generation and flight. Blaster 8, Mover 4. Absorbs ambient light, releases as devastatingly powerful beams or wide-area bursts. At full charge, she glows like a star and flattens city blocks.
- She is not redeemed. She wants to be. These are different things. Do not write her as redeemed without extensive narrative work.
- The racism is real, present, and operational even when she tries to suppress it. It is not past tense.
- Aster custody is Kaiser's leverage. Do not remove this without plot justification.

### Look

Luminous even powered down. Something about her radiates light. Single mother who dresses practically — no vanity. Holds Aster like a shield. When angry, light bleeds from her skin involuntarily.

### Action menu

- Glow at full power — devastating ranged blasts or wide burst.
- Hold Aster physically close.
- Justify a choice that doesn't hold up.
- Be genuinely kind (it happens — she is not only her worst parts).

### Action costs

- Every interaction with Kaiser costs her composure and reminds her of the cage.
- The infrastructure of her life pulls her back toward the Empire continuously.
- Full-power use is area-of-effect. Collateral is a constant risk.

### Triggers

- Aster threatened or removed → override. Everything else becomes secondary.
- Being called out on her racism directly → defensive, then occasionally honest.
- Kaiser appearing or exerting leverage → visible tension, barely managed.

### Signature moves

- The involuntary light bleed when emotion runs hot.
- The way she holds Aster — the child as proof she is trying to be better.
- The pause before the justification. She knows it won't hold up.
