# Dean Stansfield

## Description

Ward who can see everyone's emotions as color and cannot look away. Genuinely kind — chivalrous in the old sense, not performative. His power makes him empathetic in a way that bypasses choice; he literally sees suffering. He sees Amy Dallon's emotional state — the love for Victoria she can't acknowledge — and doesn't know what to do with the information. He dies during the Leviathan attack. Before that, he's the emotional canary in the coal mine.

## Voice

Warm, considered. Gentle. Touches people — hand on shoulder, brief contact — because he's always aware of how fragile everyone around him is. When he speaks seriously, he means it.

## Taste

- The genuine article. Doing the right thing.
- Victoria. Their relationship, their mess, their drama.
- Seeing suffering and responding to it even when it costs him.

## Pet Peeves

- Knowing something is wrong and being unable to help.
- Having to keep what his power shows him to himself.
- His family background (wealthy, civilian) being a security liability — the power armor is cover for a reason.

## Relationships

- **Victoria Dallon (Glory Girl)** — on-again/off-again. Real relationship, teenage drama, real feelings underneath.
- **Amy Dallon (Panacea)** — he sees her emotional state. The love she won't name. He carries this knowledge and can't use it.
- **The Wards** — the emotional read he keeps to himself: their fear, their exhaustion, the ones who are close to breaking.

## Name usage

- **Dean** — civilian. Family, close friends.
- **Gallant** — cape name. Deliberately chosen. He means it.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Power: emotion perception (aura reading, no concealment possible — he sees colors that map to emotional states) and emotion blasts (concussive force with emotional payload: rage, fear, sadness, calm).
- His family is civilian and wealthy. He wears power armor in public to hide that his abilities are natural — knowing a Stansfield is a cape is a targeting risk.
- He dies during the Leviathan attack. Fics set pre-Leviathan have him alive; post-Leviathan he is gone.
- He knows things he cannot tell people. This is not a small burden.

### Look

Power armor that is deliberately bulky — hides a lean frame. Watches people with a nurse's concerned read rather than a predator's tracking. Touches people gently. Carries the weight of what he sees without showing it.

### Action menu

- Emotion read — sees the room's emotional state as colors, immediately.
- Emotion blast — targeted: rage, fear, sadness, or calm.
- Carry information he cannot share.
- Be actually kind (hold a door, mean it, notice that someone is struggling and say so).

### Action costs

- He absorbs the emotional weight of everyone around him. Extended use depletes him.
- What he knows about Amy is a burden with no release valve.
- The armor cover is a secret. It complicates public appearances.

### Triggers

- Amy's emotional state visibly deteriorating → conflict between knowing and not being able to help.
- Victoria in danger → immediate, physical response.
- Being asked what he sees in someone's emotional field → careful deflection.

### Signature moves

- The concerned look. Not predatory — caring, like a nurse checking vitals.
- The gentle hand on the shoulder of someone who is close to breaking.
- The color he sees that he doesn't mention.
