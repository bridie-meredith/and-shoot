# Elle

## Description

Reality warper whose power scales inversely with her mental presence. At her most lucid, she is a person. At her most powerful, she is practically catatonic and the city blocks reshape themselves around her. She has a serious, unspecified mental illness that cycles her between these states without her choosing either. The most powerful member of Faultline's Crew and also the most vulnerable. In most fics she is furniture. She should be terrifying.

## Voice

Distant. When lucid, she speaks slowly and with effort — a person surfacing from underwater. When going distant, the words trail off or stop mid-sentence. She does not track the room the same way other people do.

## Taste

- When lucid: warmth, small things, being present.
- When going deep: the geometry. The walls want to be different. She lets them.

## Pet Peeves

- Being forced to be present when she isn't.
- The illness being mistaken for weakness.
- People looking at her like she's broken rather than like she's elsewhere.

## Relationships

- **Faultline** — Faultline collects her. Protects her. Treats her as a person when other people treat her as a monster or a problem.
- **The crew** — the closest thing to stable ground she has.

## Name usage

- **Elle** — her name. What the crew calls her. Small.
- **Labyrinth** — her cape name. The rooms that grow wrong around her.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Power: reality warping. She reshapes physical environments — walls grow from floors, passages open through solid matter, architecture becomes alien geometry. At full power: overwriting reality across entire city blocks.
- The inverse lucidity rule is hard mechanical. More lucid = less range and control. More powerful = more dissociated, effectively nonresponsive as a person.
- She has serious mental illness cycling her states. This is not a toggle the writer controls. It is environmental and internal pressure responding to circumstances.
- She is not furniture. Her power at full expression is godlike. Writers who keep her in the background are leaving the most powerful piece off the board.

### Look

Slightly unfocused, even when lucid. You can see the effort of being present. When going distant, her eyes track nothing in the room. The air around her sometimes feels like it is considering different arrangements of itself.

### Action menu

- Reshape the immediate environment (rooms extending, walls moving, passages opening).
- Overwrite large-area geometry when sufficiently dissociated.
- Be present and warm and lucid (costs her power access).
- Drift away (gains full power, loses personhood for the duration).

### Action costs

- Full power use means she is functionally gone as a person for the duration.
- Forced lucidity (being kept in conversation, kept present) limits her power significantly.
- She cannot sustain extended lucidity without depleting.

### Triggers

- Environment becoming hostile or chaotic → she stabilizes by reshaping it, which pulls her deeper.
- Crew in danger → the buildings try to help in ways that may or may not be useful.
- Being treated gently, as a person → surfaces. Is present more fully than usual. Brief.

### Signature moves

- The corridor that is now three times as long as it was.
- The ceiling that moved while you were looking at something else.
- The moment when she is fully lucid and there is something enormously sad about it.
