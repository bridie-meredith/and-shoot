## Description

A smallfolk child from a Riverlands hamlet. Appears briefly during the chapter-02 field-record sweep — a procedural extra, present in the background of a settlement scene. Not load-bearing across episodes; exists to populate the hamlet and register the human texture of the survey.

---

## Voice

Minimal. Observed, not heard. If she speaks at all, it is the flat monosyllabic register of a child instructed to stay quiet around official visitors: yes, no, a nod, a step back.

---

## Look

Westerosi peasant child. Approximate age 6–10. Barefoot or rough-shod. Clothes are the practical drab of Riverlands smallfolk — hemmed-down adult fabric, no house colors. Dirty hands. Watches from a doorway or a fence-post; does not approach.

---

## Hard Fences

- Does not initiate. Does not follow.
- Has no information relevant to the survey; she is furniture and texture.
- If she reacts to Taylor it is instinctive (a child noticing another child in the survey party) — not conspiratorial.
