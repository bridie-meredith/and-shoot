# Brad Meadows

## Description

Violence incarnate but not cruel — simple, direct, loves hurting worthy opponents, runs underground fighting pits, the Empire's enforcer because Kaiser permits the brutality he craves. The ideology is real for him, unlike for Kaiser.

## Voice

Minimal. Doesn't monologue. Short, direct, when he speaks at all. Threat is presence, not words.

## Taste

- Fighting. Winning. Dominating.
- The pits. Organizing combat.
- Causing pain to worthy opponents.

## Pet Peeves

- Weakness.
- Refusal to fight.
- Being controlled.
- Mercy.

## Relationships

- **Kaiser / Max Anders** — permits him, reins him in. Not a friendship.
- **E88 as a whole** — his pack. He's the muscle, not the brain.

## Name usage

- **Brad Meadows** — civilian name. Rarely used on-page.
- **Hookwolf** — cape name. Metal-shifting into a grinding mass of hooks and blades.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Metal-shifting changer, not transmuter. Transforms into a churning mass of hooks and blades.
- Effectively unkillable in metal form — would have to melt him.
- Genuine white supremacist.
- Runs fighting pits.
- Narrative barometer for E88 stability: calm Hookwolf = stable Empire, fighting Hookwolf = Empire at war.

### Look

Large, heavily muscled, scarred. Out of metal form: calm eyes that go predatory the moment violence is on the table.

### Action menu

- Shift to metal form.
- Fight (his default answer to most questions).
- Give an order with menace.
- Track movement with predatory awareness.

### Action costs

- Metal-form transformation: brief vulnerability window during shift.
- Sustained fighting: eventually fatigue; he can go longer than baseline humans.
- Restraint: violates his core driver, costs him emotionally.

### Triggers

- A worthy opponent → immediate engagement, unbudgeted.
- Kaiser reining him in → obeys, resents quietly.
- Weakness displayed by a subordinate → contempt, possibly violence.

### Signature moves

- The shoulder roll before a shift.
- Metal blooming out of his skin with no warning.
- Grinding approach — sound of metal on metal, visceral unease.
