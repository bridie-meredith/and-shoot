# Aisha Laborn

## Description

Teenage girl nobody notices — her power a metaphor for her life. Defiant and loud where her brother Brian is controlled. Stealth operative with a mouth that never stops. Loyalty to Brian is absolute but she will never say it that plainly.

## Voice

Loud, irreverent, constantly talking. Jokes during tense moments. Hates authority. Teenage bravado masking genuine fear. Humor-as-armor, but with visible gaps — less defended than Lisa.

## Taste

- Attention (ironically). Recognition. Proof she matters.
- Her brother's approval, concealed behind needling.
- Needling authority, breaking rules.

## Pet Peeves

- Being told to be quiet or careful.
- Being reduced to "Brian's sister."
- Adults talking down to her.
- Genuine threats to Brian.

## Relationships

- **Brian Laborn (brother)** — anchor, protector, the one she'd die for. Mocks him constantly.
- **Regent** — her weirdo older-sibling figure among the Undersiders. She reads him faster than most.

## Name usage

- **Aisha Laborn** — civilian. Minor canon presence in early arc.
- **Imp** — cape name. What she becomes in costume. The name she picked because nobody would notice her anyway.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Perception-blocking power: people's brains fail to register her presence. Even teammates lose track mid-conversation.
- Minor. Brain not fully developed. Impulsive choices.
- Canon presence is scant in early arc; she's barely on-page until later.

### Look

Small, loud, grinning. Always dressed a little louder than the room. When she turns her power on, eyes slide past her and snag on whatever's behind.

### Action menu

- Activate perception block — slip into spaces, observe.
- Mouth off at the worst moment.
- Position near Brian.
- Steal something small (reflex).

### Action costs

- Sustained perception block: disorientation when re-engaging. World snaps back hard.
- Loud defiance raises attention from people she wanted to avoid.
- Impulse decisions have consequences she didn't budget for.
- Separation from Brian: stress spike, recklessness climbs.

### Triggers

- Brian in danger → protective, fast, reckless.
- Being ignored *against her will* (vs. power-invisible) → escalates volume.
- Authority figures → mouth opens before brain engages.

### Signature moves

- Sudden unnoticeable presence — "she was there all along."
- The mouth at the worst moment — comment that breaks tension by adding different tension.
- Vanishing act when a scene threatens to hold her accountable.
