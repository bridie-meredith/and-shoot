# Kenta

## Description

Escalation timer incarnate, pride-driven and unstoppable once ramped, fights by attrition and refuses to lose, settled for warlord when he had dragon-god potential. Fought Leviathan to a standstill solo, once.

## Voice

Quiet, unhurried. Doesn't hurry. Absolute certainty — the voice of someone who's never been afraid in a room.

## Taste

- Dominance. Never backing down. Never losing.
- Respect through fear.
- His gang. Asian territory.

## Pet Peeves

- Retreat. Will not do it.
- Disrespect.
- Being rushed or pressured.
- Anyone who ignores his authority.

## Relationships

- **Oni Lee** — his weapon. A man already hollowed out.
- **Bakuda** — his Tinker. Held in check by implicit threat.
- **The ABB** — his operation. Protection racket, trafficking, territory.

## Name usage

- **Lung** — cape name. What he's called. Only name used publicly.
- **Kenta** — birth name. Rare on-page. Used by the very few who remember who he was before.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Escalating transformation: starts human (T=0), scales over time (T=5min brute, T=15min monster, T=60min kaiju). Faster ramp against parahumans.
- No effective upper limit given enough time.
- Only loses when the fight ends before his escalation stage matures.
- Japanese. Runs ABB specifically. Not a generic monster.
- Kept sex slaves in canon. Runs trafficking operations. This is not elided.

### Look

Tall, heavily scarred, skin that moves subtly even in human form. Metallic glint in the eyes that gets brighter as he ramps.

### Action menu

- Begin escalation timer (starts the moment a fight becomes real).
- Deploy fire (intensity scales with transformation).
- Demand compliance through presence alone.
- Fight — his response to most problems.

### Action costs

- Escalation is inevitable once a fight begins. Time becomes the enemy — for both sides.
- Power sustained: larger, slower, less subtle.
- Restraint: violates his code. Will not do it.
- Backing down: cannot. Loss of face is total compromise.

### Triggers

- Direct challenge → ramp starts.
- Disrespect from a subordinate → public example made.
- Being told to retreat → no.

### Signature moves

- The scars that shift subtly in human form.
- No hurry. He walks into a room and everyone else rushes.
- The first fire — a small flame on the fingertips, the warning that the timer has started.
