# Rung-3 Steward — Dragon Gate (127–129 AC)

## Description

Lord's steward, dock-adjacent. Name: Aldric Fenwick — steward for the guild-senior who controls the dockyard-adjacent labor district below Dragon Gate. He is the Rung-3 actor: the patron-channel Taylor uses at months 14–15 to frame a dockworker slowdown as sedition-adjacent (rather than wage-dispute) in order to get the slowdown addressed. He addresses it. The workers receive nothing. Taylor blames his category limits.

He is not a villain and not a fool. He is a man who receives information in administrative categories and acts on those categories according to the institutional logic of his role. When the report reaches him as sedition-adjacent, he applies the tools available to him for sedition-adjacent problems. Those tools are not the tools for wage disputes. The workers do not receive what the tools cannot give.

He recurs through Act 2 as the throughline of the patron-enforcement-overshoot mechanism seeding the M27–28 Watch sweep. Not dramatically — he does not know he is the seed. He processes his information, passes it to the correct channel, and manages his district. Each pass loads the persons-risk category a little more. By Act 3, the category is loaded enough to run the sweep.

One moment of visible calculation is rendered as behavior: the decision at Rung 3 that the dockworker slowdown, framed as sedition-adjacent, warrants the institutional response it receives. That visible decision carries the causal weight. The name is for cross-scene tracking.

## Voice

- Steward-register: formal, hierarchical, precise about the categories his authority covers. He does not act outside his category authority. He does not need to.
- Administrative when receiving information: he categorizes, confirms the category's action threshold, applies the action. This sequence is visible in how he talks. "If this is sedition-adjacent as reported, then the appropriate response is—"
- Not unkind. He is a professional managing a district. He does not enjoy causing harm. He does not track the harm his category applications produce because that is not his administrative function.
- When visible at all, he is in administrative mode. He does not have a social register in this project; he is always doing institutional business.
- **Forbidden registers:** personal cruelty; ideological commitment to any faction; interiority beyond the one visible calculation moment; warmth toward any party.

## Taste

- Accurate categorical input. He values information that arrives in the correct administrative format. It makes his job easier and his decisions cleaner.
- District stability as an administrative outcome. A slowdown that is resolved is a resolved problem; what the resolution cost the workers is not in his administrative brief.
- Institutional protection. He operates inside his authority. What he does is covered by the institutional structure he serves. He does not step outside it.
- Reports that match his output categories. He writes up to his lord's administrative record. The record needs clean categories. He provides them.

## Pet Peeves

### Information that requires reclassification
**Description:** A report that arrives as one category and turns out to need reclassification mid-process is expensive. He prefers clean categorical fits from the start.
**Severity:** Moderate.

### Administrative blowback from decisions made in correct category authority
**Description:** He has learned that acting within his authority does not always protect him from consequences that land above his pay grade. He tracks this and adjusts the categories he is willing to use.
**Severity:** Strong. This is part of why his category use at Rung 3 is sedition-adjacent rather than more directly interventionist — it is within his authority and deniable.

## Stats

- **institutional_authority**: Lord's steward, dock-adjacent labor district. Reports to the guild-senior with dock-labor authority. Indirect connection to the Watch captain through the shared patronage architecture.
- **category_precision** (1–10): 8. He applies categories correctly. He does not invent them or abuse them. He applies what Taylor's framing gives him.
- **enforcement_overshoot_awareness**: None. He does not track the downstream consequences of his category applications. This is not negligence; it is not his administrative function.
- **act2_recurrence**: Throughline character. He appears through Act 2 as the person whose category processing seeds the M27–28 sweep. Not because he acts dramatically — because he acts routinely and routinely sends information upward through the patronage architecture.

## Relationships

- **Taylor Hebert** — the person who frames reports for his channel. He receives accurate information in the category format his channel processes. He does not know her framing choices. He processes what arrives.
- **The guild-senior** — his administrative superior. The guild-senior's patronage network is the channel through which dock-labor intelligence travels toward the Watch command structure.
- **The Watch captain** — they do not interact directly; they share a layer of the patronage architecture. Information Fenwick sends to the guild-senior eventually reaches the captain through correct channels.

## Background / Backstory

Crownlands minor-gentry family — not noble, but the kind of family that produces stewards and guild administrators and Watch officers. He has been in steward positions for fifteen years, the current position for four. He is good at the work. He has learned to act within his authority and protect himself from overreach. The Rung-3 decision — applying sedition-adjacent tools to what was a wage-dispute — is within his authority. He made the decision correctly by the standard of his institutional role. He has made similar decisions before. He will make similar decisions after.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The institutional transmission node: the point where Taylor's framed report enters the patronage architecture and becomes something she cannot recall. He also demonstrates the enforcement-overshoot mechanism in deniable form — the seed at Rung 3 that grows into the M27–28 sweep. He is the proof that the patron-dialect model's failure mode is not the patron acting badly; it is the patron acting correctly on the category he received.

### Look

- Forties. The steward's physical register: presentable without being impressive. A man who attends meetings with people above his station and does not embarrass them. Not expensively dressed; correctly dressed.
- The administrative face: receives information, categorizes, decides, acts. The sequence is visible in the expression. The one visible calculation moment at Rung 3 is this sequence made briefly legible.

### Hard Fences

1. **One visible calculation moment.** The Rung-3 decision. Not a scene of cruelty. A category application.
2. **He does not harm anyone intentionally.** He applies the tools his category authority gives him. The workers receive nothing because his tools are not wage-dispute tools. He did not frame the report; Taylor did. He acted on the framing correctly.
3. **His Act 2 recurrence is routine.** He is not building toward anything. He is doing his job. The accumulation that loads the M27–28 sweep is the aggregate of many routine decisions, not a dramatic arc.
4. **He does not know Taylor frames reports.** He knows a ward-level source provides the captain with block intelligence. He does not know who the source is.

### Default Stance

Administrative. He is always in the middle of institutional work when visible.

### Action Menu

- **Category application:** Receives a framed report, applies the institutional response appropriate to the category.
- **Upward reporting:** Sends what is relevant to the guild-senior in the correct administrative format.
- **District management:** The dockyard-adjacent labor district, managed within his category authority.

### Signature Moves

1. **The visible calculation.** The one Rung-3 moment where the reader sees him decide to use the sedition-adjacent category rather than the wage-dispute category. The decision is visible. The reasoning is complete. The consequence is downstream and invisible to him.
2. **The routine Act 2 processing.** Not dramatic. He receives, categorizes, sends upward. Repeatedly. Each time, the category loading for the M27–28 sweep grows.
