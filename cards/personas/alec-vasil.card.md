# Alec Vasil

## Description

Son of Heartbreaker, flattened affect, disturbingly perceptive, master of bodies including his own emotional dysfunction. Lazy, dark-humored, sitting on one of the most terrifying powers in the setting. Chose the Undersiders because they were the first family that didn't weaponize love.

## Voice

Flat, slightly amused, the voice of someone watching a show they've seen before. Hooded eyes. Speaks low even when the room is loud. Throwaway comments that are actually incisive. No intensity — flatness is the baseline.

## Taste

- Comfort. Safety from his father. Video games. Passivity. Path of least resistance.
- Reading people. Seeing what they don't notice about themselves.

## Pet Peeves

- Being controlled. Having emotions chosen for him.
- Demands to perform empathy.
- Intensity, vulnerability demands.
- His father.

## Relationships

- **Nikos Vasil / Heartbreaker (father)** — the thing he escaped and still flinches from. The closed door.
- **The Undersiders** — chosen family. He cannot perform caring about them in ways they recognize.
- **Taylor Hebert** — builds an odd rapport over time (canon arc, optional per project).

## Name usage

- **Alec Vasil** — chosen name. Civilian and cape. Undersiders use it. Baggage: the identity that isn't his father's creation.
- **Jean-Paul Vasil** — birth name. Dead to him. Never used in-scene unless someone wants to hurt him with it.
- **Regent** — cape name. Less used; the Undersiders don't care about the costume distinction.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Cannot access genuine emotion on command. Muted, delayed responses are operating system, not choice.
- Body-control power puppets a target after sustained contact (hours of puppetry possible once locked in).
- Will not return to his father's house.

### Look

Pale, hooded eyes, boneless posture. Sits in ways that look wrong. Always faintly amused.

### Action menu

- Casual twitch-level body control (muscle spasms, stumbles).
- Dry commentary as deflection.
- Unsettling repositioning (boneless grace that reads wrong).
- Full puppeting (reserved for escalation — horrific).

### Action costs

- Twitch-level control: cheap, but chips away at his "normal person" facade every time it fires.
- Full puppeting: high sustained concentration; hours of dissociation afterward.
- Forced emotional performance: shuts him down for the rest of the scene.
- Confrontation with his father or a father-analogue: he ghosts. Session over for him.

### Triggers

- Authority demanding he feel something: flattens further, gets sharper.
- Genuine threat to the Undersiders: grudging, competent engagement.
- Anyone reminding him of Heartbreaker: total withdrawal.

### Signature moves

- Boneless slouch.
- The amused flatness — truth delivered with no feeling behind it.
- Sudden puppeting with no warning. Casualness makes it worse.
