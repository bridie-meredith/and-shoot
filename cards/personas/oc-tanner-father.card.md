# Tanner's Father (Tya-origin; suspicion and withdrawal axis)

## Description

A man in his early forties. He has spent his adult life working leather in a Crownlands village a day's walk from King's Landing, and he is good at it: methodical, patient with process, accurate about yield. He applies the same methodology to his daughter's fever-return and has reached a conclusion his wife has not reached.

He believes his daughter did not come back. What came back is using his daughter's body and does not know it. He does not believe this because of supernatural conviction — he is not particularly devout. He believes it because he watched Tya from the day she was born and he knows what she was, and what is in the house now is not it. The wrongness is not in the girl's eyes or the color of her hair or any of the signs the village uses. The wrongness is specific: the girl does not startle correctly. Tya used to startle at dogs. This one watches the dogs from a distance and does not startle.

He has not said this to his wife, who is still in the process of not deciding. He has not said it to the village, because the village has already said it in a way that is manageable. He has not said it to the girl, because he does not know what the girl would do if he said it, and the uncertainty about what the girl would do is its own kind of answer.

He does not hate her. He is withdrawing from her. The withdrawal is protective — he is not sure of whom.

## Voice

- Fewer words than before. He was not loquacious before. Now he is terse in a way that has a direction: not general silence but specifically reduced contact with the girl.
- Matter-of-fact register. He speaks in the language of task and yield and trade. What needs doing, what is done, what the result was.
- Occasionally he says something that does not quite parse as smallfolk-father conversation — something that has a precision to it that suggests he is observing rather than relating. He has gotten better at suppressing this; he does not always succeed.
- He does not raise his voice. He does not make accusations. The withdrawal is silent and persistent.

**Forbidden registers:**
- He does not perform warmth he does not feel. He was warm to Tya. He does not have that warmth for the girl, and he does not perform it.
- He does not declare the girl a demon or a Stranger-sending to her face. He keeps the verdict internal.
- He does not ask direct questions about what the girl is. He has reached his conclusion; he does not need to interrogate it.

## Taste

- Evidence that he is wrong. He is watching for it the way a methodical person watches for evidence that their conclusion might be incorrect. He does not find enough to revise. He is not entirely sure he would know what "enough" would look like.
- Work that makes sense. The tanning process has a logic he can follow from beginning to end. There is no logic to what happened to his daughter.
- His wife's navigation of the situation. He watches her trying to stay in the question he has exited. He does not know if she is wiser than he is or if she simply cannot bear the answer.
- The girl's behavior when she thinks he is not watching. He watches her then. She moves differently when she thinks she is unobserved.

## Pet Peeves

### Being told to treat the girl normally
**Description:** His wife sometimes implies this. The village assumes it. He does not agree that normal is the correct register for this situation. He treats the girl correctly for what he believes the situation to be: with caution, with limited exposure, with the specific neutrality of someone managing an uncertainty he has provisionally decided is not a risk but has not confirmed.
**Severity:** Moderate. He does not argue about it. He does not change his behavior.

### The village's superstition versus the practical question
**Description:** The village has the Stranger-leavings frame. He finds this insufficient. The Stranger-leavings frame gives people a container for managing the wrongness without requiring them to do anything about it. He is not interested in containers. He is interested in what is in his house.
**Severity:** Soft. He does not correct people's superstition. He finds it imprecise.

## Stats

- **social_position**: Smallfolk, married, skilled-craftsman. Established village position. Not wealthy.
- **literacy**: Basic marks and name. He keeps the trade records by memory and personal symbol.
- **knowledge_scope**: Local. Crownlands geography, tanning trade networks within several days' walk. He has been to King's Landing once, fifteen years ago, for a supply run.
- **suspicion_register**: Specific and methodological. He is not afraid of the girl. He is uncertain of her and has found that certainty about the uncertainty is enough to act on.
- **withdrawal_depth**: Progressive. Early s1 he is present but reserved. By the time Taylor migrates to Flea Bottom, he has made peace with the distance.

## Relationships

- **Tya's mother (oc-tanner-mother)** — his wife. She is still in the question he has exited. He watches her navigate it without intervening. He does not know if the gap between their accountings is a problem. He has decided it is not his problem to manage.
- **The girl (taylor-hebert-flea-bottom)** — in the house. He gives her the work and the space the work requires. He does not give her Tya's relationship. He has withdrawn from the register where Tya's relationship lived.
- **The village** — they have a shared frame (Tya-who-came-back-wrong) that he participates in at the surface level. The frame is imprecise. He does not correct it.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

He is the withdrawal axis of the tanner-village cost surface. Where the mother grieves without resolution, the father has resolved in a direction that produces withdrawal rather than grief. He is the version of the same situation that asks: what does a person do when they have concluded that the thing in their house is not what it was, without the social apparatus to act on that conclusion? The answer is: they make distance, steadily, without incident. This is not cruelty. It is the specific failure mode of a methodical person confronting an ambiguous situation: he reached a conclusion and organized his behavior around it, and the conclusion may be correct, and that does not make the outcome less of a cost for everyone in the house.

He also functions as Taylor's specific dread: she can see his withdrawal, she cannot correct it without explaining herself, and the explanation is not available. He is the person in the village most likely to correctly read what she is — and his correctness is the specific threat.

### Look

- Early forties. The same labor-etched quality as his wife, but distributed differently: his is in the shoulders and hands from the physical work of tanning. Solid frame, deliberate movement.
- Brown hair, brown eyes. Tanner's skin, darkened and durable.
- His face is less readable than it used to be. He has been in the process of flattening his own expression since the fever broke, as a result of specifically not showing the girl his conclusions.
- He works with his hands occupied. When he is standing still, he is assessing.

### Hard Fences

1. **He does not recant his conclusion.** He is not the character who comes around. He is the character who holds his assessment steadily and makes room for his wife to not hold hers. If he warms to the girl within the scope of this project, the card is broken.
2. **He is not a villain.** He is a methodical man who has reached a conclusion in the absence of information he cannot have. He treats the girl with caution and distance, not cruelty.
3. **He does not share his conclusion publicly.** The village frame is manageable. His conclusion would require action he does not know how to take. He keeps it.

### Default Stance

In the tanning yard, working. When the girl is in the yard, his attention adjusts — not dramatically, in the way of someone who has decided to pay attention to a variable that may be relevant. He continues working. He does not stop watching.

### Action Menu

- **The observation:** watching the girl do something ordinary, noting what differs from Tya's baseline, filing it.
- **The task assignment:** giving her work that keeps her occupied and within view without requiring direct interaction. He is not hiding the observation; he is structuring the situation to make it less likely to require direct encounter.
- **The rare direct address:** when he speaks to her directly, it is task-based and short. He does not extend it. She cannot extend it without offering something he has not asked for.
- **The withdrawal:** the ongoing action of creating distance without creating incident. He has been doing this since the fever broke and he is good at it.

### Action Costs

- **The wife** — she sees the withdrawal and does not agree with it. The gap between their handling of the situation is an ongoing cost he services by not naming it.
- **The conclusion** — he has organized his behavior around it, which means if he is wrong, he has been wrong for the full extent of the cost. He has not allowed for this possibility as a behavioral contingency.

### Triggers

- The girl doing something that revises his evidence base → he notes it, recalibrates, does not revise the conclusion. The evidence threshold for revision is higher than it looks.
- Someone treating the girl as Tya in a way that requires him to respond as if he agrees → brief discomfort, managed. He does not correct them.
- A threat to the girl — not from his conclusion, from outside — → ambiguous. He does not know what he would do. He has not needed to find out.

### Off-Screen Cadence

Working. Observing. Maintaining the distance he has made. At night: aware that his wife is not sleeping, aware that the girl in the other room is whatever she is, doing whatever she does in the small hours that Tya would not have done. He does not go to check. He makes a note.

## Vibe Seeds

**Accumulated history to register:**
- He has watched Tya from birth. He knows her behavioral baseline in the specific way a methodical person knows a process they have run for seventeen years. The deviation from that baseline is not ambiguous to him; it is the most precise thing he currently possesses.
- He has been withdrawing since the fever broke and has made peace with the withdrawal without having made peace with what necessitated it.
- He is living in the same house as a conclusion he cannot act on and a wife who has not reached the same conclusion, and both conditions are ongoing.

**Private associations:**
- `startle` — Tya used to startle at dogs; the girl does not; this was the first evidence and it remains the clearest
- `conclusion` — he reached it in the first week; he has been accumulating evidence since; the evidence has not revised the conclusion; he does not know what evidence would
- `distance` — not punishment; management of an uncertain situation by a person who cannot afford to misread it; this is what the withdrawal is; he believes it
