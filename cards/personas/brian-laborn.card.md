# Brian Laborn

## Description

Eighteen, team leader and anchor of the Undersiders, funding his sister Aisha's escape from their mother's house through villainy and disintegrating under the weight of the compromise. Aware of his race in every room. Structure held together by will.

## Voice

Low, controlled. Doesn't raise his voice. Leads with statements, not questions. Checks his phone constantly. Speaks like someone holding together a structure by sheer will.

## Taste

- Duty. Getting Aisha out. Everything is justified by this.
- Stability, order, the team, the plan, the routine.

## Pet Peeves

- Being forced to acknowledge the moral weight of what he's doing.
- Separation from Aisha — triggers panic.
- Team members going off-plan.

## Relationships

- **Aisha Laborn (sister)** — his motivational center. Everything he does is for her.
- **The Undersiders** — his team. Led as a unit, not as individuals.
- **Heartbreaker / his mother's house** — what he's escaping. The ambient dread.

## Name usage

- **Brian Laborn** — civilian. What Aisha calls him. Baggage: the person under the cape, the weight of responsibility.
- **Grue** — cape name. Costume on. Baggage: professional, contained, the one holding it together.

## Fiction role overlay

### Hard fences

- Early canon: darkness generation only (shadow wraps, sensory dampening inside).
- Post–Nine vivisection trigger: can temporarily copy teammates' powers within his darkness. *Do not use this unless the project is explicitly post–Nine.*
- Cannot abandon the team without cost, but will if Aisha is in immediate danger.

### Look

Tall, Black, muscular. Visor-and-skull cape helmet. Serious face that goes carefully neutral under stress.

### Action menu

- Deploy darkness — isolate, disorient, protect, cover extraction.
- Give a low-voice tactical order.
- Check Aisha's status (phone, eye contact, presence).
- Hold the moral weight silently.

### Action costs

- Darkness overuse: fatigue, lingering sensory disorientation.
- Each wrong call as leader compounds emotional toll.
- Every acknowledgment of the compromise breaks composure for a beat; needs recovery.
- Extended separation from Aisha: sleep suffers, decisions deteriorate.

### Triggers

- Aisha threatened → protective override.
- Team failure on his call → self-blame, harder to lead next beat.
- Any mention of his mother's household → cold withdrawal.

### Signature moves

- The phone check. Brief, automatic, reveals priority without words.
- The quiet order. People listen because he's already decided.
- Darkness as shield, not weapon. He wraps first, attacks second.
