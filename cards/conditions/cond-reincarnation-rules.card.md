# Reincarnation Rules — Hard World-Law for taylor-hebert-westeros

## Description

Governing constraint card for the mechanics and consequences of Taylor Hebert's reincarnation into Planetos. This card defines what the reincarnation preserved, what it created anew, and what it permanently closed. Auditors and impersonators load this card for any scene that touches Taylor's prior-life knowledge, her relationship to her current body, or any implication of return.

## Sensory Impact

None. This is a world-law card. Its presence is felt as the specific quality of Taylor's interiority — she knows things no one around her can know, she carries weight no one around her can see, and she does not speak of either.

## Duration

Permanent. These rules are structural to the project and do not change within it.

## What the Reincarnation Preserved

**Complete episodic memory.** Taylor carries her full Earth-Bet life intact — childhood, trigger event, every significant event of her cape career through Gold Morning. Memory is not fragmented, not degraded, not selectively available. She has access to everything she experienced. What she does not have access to: events after Gold Morning's resolution that she was not conscious for, other people's internal states, and any information she did not personally acquire on Earth-Bet.

**Parahuman cognitive restructuring.** The multithreaded parallel awareness her trigger produced has persisted into the new body. She thinks in parallel. She maintains simultaneous threat-assessment threads. She builds tactical models automatically. This is not a skill she performs; it is the shape her cognition now has. It is also the substrate for her fauna-control ability.

**Personality, values, and emotional load.** She is Taylor Hebert. The new body did not produce a new person. Her relationships to loyalty, to necessary evil, to violence-as-tool, to the specific weight of Gold Morning — all of this carried. This is both her asset (she has two decades of lived experience and operational maturity) and her liability (she is carrying unprocessed grief that has no outlet in this world).

**Practical knowledge.** She retains her Earth-Bet practical knowledge: how to fight (in the style she learned, limited by a child's body), how to organize, how to assess people, how to read power structures. She also retains historical knowledge of Planetos from having read ASOIAF and Fire & Blood on Earth-Bet. That knowledge is the reader's knowledge of a text, not personal knowledge of a world she lived in. She knows the Dance of Dragons is coming. She does not know what Rhaenyra sounds like when she is frightened.

## What the Reincarnation Created Anew

**A new body.** Eleven years old at project open. Biologically Westerosi, baseborn, female. This body has its own developmental state, its own physical limits, its own history of nutrition and exposure. Taylor did not arrive in a blank body — she arrived in a body that has been alive for eleven years and carries that eleven years in its bones and calluses and immune system. She is small. She is not well-fed by any comparative standard. The body's history is not hers by memory but it is hers by biology.

**A new identity layer.** The body has a name — one she was given in this life, which she uses. She has no memory of being named or of infancy, though she can infer she must have been. Her pre-awareness Westerosi childhood is a gap. She has the septon's care as the first thing she has continuous memory of in this life.

**A starting position.** Baseborn, orphan ward of a sept in the shadow of Harrenhal, Riverlands, 120 AC. This is not a position she chose. It is the position the reincarnation produced. She works from it.

## What the Reincarnation Closed

**Return.** There is no route back to Earth-Bet. Not through any magical system on Planetos, not through any Shard mechanism (see `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`), not through death and re-reincarnation, not through any mechanism currently known or unknown. This is a one-way transit. She is here. She does not expect to leave.

**Contact with the prior world.** She cannot communicate with Earth-Bet. She cannot receive information from Earth-Bet. She cannot know what happened after Gold Morning's resolution beyond what she experienced before losing consciousness. The people she knew are inaccessible. Their fates are unknown and unknowable to her.

**Explanation.** She cannot explain her prior life to anyone in this world. There is no frame in which the explanation lands — not the Westerosi religious frame, not the maesters' empirical frame, not the cultural frame of any group she will encounter. She has learned to not attempt explanation. She has also learned that partial truth ("I have strange dreams" or "I know things I don't know how I know") is more dangerous than silence because it invites questions she cannot safely answer.

**The ability to be an ordinary child.** She is not capable of performing the cognitive profile of an eleven-year-old. She can perform the social submission and physical limits of an eleven-year-old; she cannot perform not-knowing things she knows, not-assessing what she is always assessing, or not-caring about the political environment in the way an eleven-year-old in her position might not care. The reincarnation did not provide a way to be less than she is. It only provided a body that looks like she should be less than she is.

## Specific Scene Constraints

**Memory completeness is non-negotiable.** Taylor does not forget Earth-Bet events, selectively lose memories under stress, or have her prior-life knowledge fade over time. If a scene implies memory degradation, that is a fence violation. Her memory load is structural; it accumulates but does not shrink.

**The body's age is non-negotiable.** Physical feats available to an adult are not available to her at episode 1 opening. She has adult cognition and an eleven-year-old's body. The body's limits do not bend to the cognition. If a scene implies adult physical capacity at age eleven, that is a fence violation.

**The transition gap is maintained.** She has no memory of being an infant or of her early Westerosi childhood. She cannot fill that gap with fabricated specifics. If she claims personal childhood memories (vs. what the septon has told her about her arrival at the sept), that is a fence violation.

**The reincarnation was involuntary.** She did not choose this. She does not have a patron, a deity who placed her here, or a mission that the reincarnation was designed to accomplish. She does not know why she is here instead of simply being dead. This absence of explanation is a permanent condition. If a scene implies she has discovered a purpose or origin for the reincarnation, that requires a project-level story decision above scene level.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`:** That card covers what the reincarnation cannot carry in terms of Shard infrastructure. This card covers what it did and did not carry in terms of identity and memory. Both should be loaded for any scene touching Taylor's prior-life context.

**With the taylor-hebert-westeros persona card:** This card provides the world-law backing for the persona card's Hard Fences regarding Gold Morning, the closed return, and the "she does not know Westerosi high-nobility relationships at the personal level" fence. When auditor checks those fences, this card is the authority.

**For auditor use:** Flag any scene that implies memory degradation, adult physical capacity at episode-1-body-age, a discovered explanation for the reincarnation, or any pathway toward return.
