# Psyche-Profile Wing — Index

A library wing of **condition-class** cards, one per major psychological disorder, grounded in
**DSM-5-TR (APA)** and **ICD-11 (WHO)**. Each card colors a *character* — behavior, perception,
interiority — rather than a location, and carries **both** a clinical-reference layer and a
fiction-characterization layer (Presentation & Tells / Interiority / Dramatic Affordances / Fences).

All cards are `scope: library`, `class: condition`, universe-agnostic. Maintained by margit — update on
every store, quality change, or new authoring.

**Responsible-use note.** These cards render *characters who live with a condition*, not verdicts on
real people, and never an instructional manual. Each card's `## Fences — Responsible Use` section
carries the anti-caricature / anti-stigma rules and the no-operational-detail boundary. Honor them at
authoring and review time.

**A note on sourcing.** The request named "arxiv or equivalent." arXiv is a physics/CS/math preprint
server and does not catalog psychological disorders; the authoritative taxonomy of psychological
deviations and impairments is DSM-5-TR and ICD-11, which these cards follow.

**Out of scope (deliberate exclusions).** Sexual dysfunctions and gender dysphoria are not included —
the former are not "deviations/impairments" in the dramatic sense, and the latter is framed in current
clinical practice in a depathologized way. Paraphilic disorders are covered by a single responsible
*overview* card rather than individual cards.
