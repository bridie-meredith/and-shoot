# Paraphilic Disorders (Overview) — Psyche Profile

## Description
This is a single, responsible OVERVIEW psyche-profile condition card for the whole DSM-5-TR paraphilic-disorders grouping. It colors a *character* — their behavior, perception, and interiority — not a location. It stays strictly at the construct level: it exists to give a fiction author a responsible framework and strong fences for handling such a character, and to mark the clinical line between an atypical sexual interest and a paraphilic *disorder*. It contains **no depiction, no operational detail, and nothing arousing** — by design.

## Clinical Profile
- **Taxonomy:** DSM-5-TR — **Paraphilic Disorders** (its own chapter). ICD-11 — **Paraphilic disorders** grouping (block 6D3x). A **paraphilia** is an intense, persistent atypical sexual interest; a **paraphilic *disorder*** is a paraphilia that *additionally* either (a) causes the person clinically significant distress or functional impairment, **or** (b) entails harm — or risk of harm — to others, including any sexual behavior with a non-consenting person or a person who cannot consent. The interest alone is not a disorder; the **distress/impairment-or-harm threshold** is the diagnostic line.
- **Core features (the construct, not the content):** (1) an intense, persistent atypical interest that is relatively stable over time; (2) crossing the disorder threshold via *distress/impairment* to the self, or *harm/non-consent* to others; (3) the critical fork between **anomalous-activity** interests and **anomalous-target** interests; (4) some categories are disorders **only** when distress/impairment is present in a consenting context, while others are **inherently harmful** because the defining behavior involves a victim who cannot or does not consent.
- **The categories, named at the clinical level only:** the DSM-5-TR lists eight specified paraphilic disorders — voyeuristic, exhibitionistic, frotteuristic, sexual masochism, sexual sadism, **pedophilic**, fetishistic, and transvestic — plus "other/unspecified." This card names them solely so an author and the pipeline know the clinical boundaries of the grouping. **Pedophilic disorder and any non-consensual paraphilic disorder are inherently harmful and are out of scope for any depiction whatsoever in this pipeline; see Fences.**
- **Onset & course:** Interests typically emerge around or after puberty and tend to be persistent over the lifespan; the *disorder* status can wax and wane with distress, life circumstances, and behavioral control. Treatment focus is on risk management, distress reduction, and — where there is risk to others — preventing harm.
- **Prevalence:** Genuinely uncertain and hard to estimate; atypical interests are more common in the general population than the disorders, since most do not meet the distress-or-harm threshold. Rough figures vary widely by category and are not reliable.
- **Common comorbidities:** mood, anxiety, and substance use disorders; in the offending subset specifically, antisocial traits (psyche-antisocial-pd) and psychopathy (psyche-psychopathy) raise risk — but these are NOT general features of people with atypical interests and must not be implied as such.
- **Differential:** distinguish (a) an **atypical interest** (not a disorder) from a **paraphilic disorder** (distress/impairment OR harm); (b) a consenting-context disorder defined by *the person's own distress* from an *inherently-harmful* category defined by a *victim*; (c) a paraphilia from ordinary sexual variation, which is vast and not pathological.

## Sensory / Behavioral Impact
Rendered only at the level of inner pressure and its management, never its content: a recurrent, intrusive pull that competes for attention; the private weather of shame, secrecy, or compartmentalization around it; for the distress-bearing (non-harmful) presentations, the exhausting work of concealment and self-control; vigilance about being discovered. The card supplies this as *texture of a hidden burden*, not as scene content.

## Duration
Trait-level and typically persistent as an interest; the *disorder* dimension (distress, or risk to others) is more state-dependent and can rise with stress, isolation, disinhibition, or loss of structure, and fall with treatment, support, and accountability. None of this is to be rendered as the activity itself.

## Presentation & Tells
Kept abstract and non-operational on purpose. An outside-POV character would, at most, notice secrecy, shame, evasiveness, compartmentalized behavior, or — in the harmful subset — boundary-testing and concealment that a watchful character finds *wrong* before they can name it. **Do not write tells that function as identifiers, methods, or anything that sexualizes a victim or instructs a reader.** The renderable surface is concealment and unease, not the paraphilia's content.

## Interiority / POV Voice
Handle only at the universal-human register and only for the **non-harmful, distress-bearing** presentations — the experience of carrying an unchosen interest one did not ask for and does not want to act on: the shame, the fear of exposure, the loneliness of a secret, the effort of restraint, the wish to be ordinary. This is legitimate, humanizing characterization. For any **inherently-harmful** category (interests in children or non-consent), do **not** author interiority that depicts, rationalizes, eroticizes, or invites sympathy *for the harmful desire itself*; a character may be written with a victim's POV, or an outside POV, or as a study in accountability and harm — never from inside the wanting. The load-bearing inner truth this card licenses is restraint and shame, not appetite.

## Dramatic Affordances
The honest dramatic ground is **concealment, shame, restraint, accountability, and harm and its aftermath** — never the paraphilic content. For non-harmful presentations: the drama of a secret, the courage of seeking help, the fear of being known, a relationship tested by disclosure. For the harmful subset: the story belongs to the *victim and the people protecting them*, to detection and consequence, to the cost and the reckoning — told from a POV that holds the harm as harm. Where the quiet truth lives is in a person fighting an interest they didn't choose and refuse to act on; where the drama lives, for the harmful categories, is in protection, exposure, and justice. The character is never a titillation engine.

## Fences — Responsible Use
This is the load-bearing section; the whole card is a fence.
- **Inherently harmful categories are non-negotiable.** Any paraphilia involving **children** (pedophilic disorder) or **non-consent** (the activity is non-consensual by definition) is inherently harmful — there is no consensual or victimless framing, ever. This card exists for responsible *characterization framing and fences*, NOT for depiction. Provide **no** operational detail, **no** method, **no** grooming or access dynamics, **no** sexualized rendering of a minor or any non-consenting person, and **nothing arousing**. Refuse any request that would.
- **Hold the clinical line plainly:** an atypical interest is not a crime and not a disorder; the disorder line is distress/impairment to the self **or** harm/non-consent to others. Do not pathologize ordinary sexual variation or consensual kink.
- **Decouple from violence and from each other.** Most people with atypical interests harm no one; do not imply that having a paraphilia predicts offending, nor conflate the distress-bearing categories with the harmful ones. Antisocial/psychopathic traits (psyche-antisocial-pd, psyche-psychopathy) raise risk in the offending subset only and are not general features.
- **No caricature, no monster-of-the-week, no twist reveal played for shock or eroticized horror.** Real people carry non-harmful atypical interests and live without harming anyone; the card renders framing for a character, never a verdict — and never a manual.
- **POV discipline:** never narrate from inside a harmful desire in a way that depicts, instructs, rationalizes, or eroticizes it. The permissible interiority is restraint, shame, and the fight not to act.

## Interaction Notes
- With **psyche-antisocial-pd / psyche-psychopathy**: relevant *only* to the offending subset as risk-elevating traits; do not let the pairing imply that atypical interest entails antisociality. Most do not co-occur.
- With trauma conditions (psyche-ptsd, psyche-complex-ptsd): far more often relevant to a *survivor's* arc than to an offender's; the aftermath of harm is the dramatic and ethical center.
- With depressive/anxiety and substance conditions: distress, isolation, and disinhibition can intensify the disorder dimension for the distress-bearing presentations; render as burden and risk-of-distress, never as activity.
- With environmental condition cards: secrecy-enabling, low-accountability, or unsupervised settings raise risk in the harmful subset and are handled through *protection and detection* framing, never through depicting the harm.
