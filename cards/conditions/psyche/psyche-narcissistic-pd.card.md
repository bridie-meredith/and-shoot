# Narcissistic Personality Disorder (NPD) — Psyche Profile

## Description
This is a psyche-profile condition card: it colors a *character's* behavior, perception, and interiority (not a location). Narcissistic personality disorder is a pervasive pattern of grandiosity (in fantasy or behavior), need for admiration, and impaired empathy — most usefully understood as a fragile self-regard propped up from outside, where the bravado and the deflation are two faces of the same vulnerable structure.

## Clinical Profile
- **Taxonomy:** DSM-5-TR — Personality Disorders → Cluster B → Narcissistic Personality Disorder. ICD-11 folds it into the dimensional model — pronounced *dissociality* (grandiosity, entitlement, attention-seeking) often paired with *negative affectivity*, across a severity gradient.
- **Core features:**
  - Grandiose sense of self-importance; expectation of recognition as superior without commensurate achievement.
  - Preoccupation with fantasies of success, power, brilliance, beauty, or ideal love.
  - Belief in being special and only understood by, or fit to associate with, other special/high-status people.
  - Strong need for admiration; a sense of entitlement; readiness to use relationships instrumentally.
  - Impaired empathy — difficulty recognizing or weighting others' feelings and needs; envy, and a sense of being envied.
  - Arrogance underlain by fragile self-esteem acutely sensitive to slight ("narcissistic injury").
- **Onset & course:** Consolidates by early adulthood; chronic and trait-like. Grandiosity can erode with age, losses, or repeated failure, sometimes exposing depression beneath.
- **Prevalence:** Approximately up to 1–2% in community samples; more often diagnosed in men.
- **Common comorbidities:** Other Cluster B disorders — antisocial (psyche-antisocial-pd), histrionic (psyche-histrionic-pd), borderline (psyche-borderline-pd); depressive disorders (often surfacing on collapse); substance use.
- **Differential:** Distinguished from antisocial PD (which centers exploitation/rule-breaking with weaker need for *admiration*) and from histrionic PD (which seeks attention/warmth rather than recognition of superiority). Healthy confidence or situational self-promotion is not NPD absent the rigidity, the empathy gap, and the fragility under challenge.

### Grandiose vs. vulnerable presentation
NPD is not one surface. The **grandiose** presentation is the familiar one — overt, charming-to-domineering, boastful, entitled, hungry for the room's admiration, dismissive of inferiors. The **vulnerable** (covert) presentation is quieter and easily missed — hypersensitive, anxious, resentful, prone to shame and felt-injustice, self-effacing on the surface while privately convinced of unrecognized specialness and nursing wounds at every slight. The same person can swing between the two: grandiose while supplied, vulnerable when the supply fails. A character built on the vulnerable register can read as shy or self-deprecating until the entitlement and envy show through.

## Sensory / Behavioral Impact
The character scans every room for status and supply — who outranks whom, who is admiring, who threatens the self-image — and calibrates to it. Affect is keyed to that read: expansive, magnetic, generous when fed; cold, contemptuous, or wounded when slighted. Others register slightly out of focus, more as mirrors and instruments than as full interiors. Slights land like physical blows and reorganize the whole scene around the injury. Energy follows the admiration supply.

## Duration
Chronic and trait-like, but the moment-to-moment state is brittle and reactive. Within a single scene a perceived insult — a correction, a withheld credit, a rival praised — can flip charm into rage or icy withdrawal ("narcissistic injury") and then, sometimes, into collapse. Admiration restores the grandiose state quickly; failure and exposure can drop it into depression that the bravado was holding off.

## Presentation & Tells
- Steers conversation back to themselves; reframes others' news as a setup for their own story.
- Name-drops, status-signals, references their specialness or important associations.
- Charm that is calibrated to who can be useful, and cools toward those who can't.
- Takes credit, deflects blame; corrections met with disproportionate heat or contempt.
- Subtle (or not) ranking of people; dismissiveness toward those deemed lesser.
- Envy — souring at others' success — and a conviction of being envied.
- In the vulnerable register: thin-skinned, easily wounded, brooding on slights, martyred or aggrieved beneath a modest surface.

## Interiority / POV Voice
From inside, the self is a performance that must not be allowed to fail, because the alternative — glimpsed only in flashes — is a void of worthlessness too large to look at. The narrating voice is grand and certain on the surface: *they don't see what I see, they're not operating at my level, this is beneath me* — and just under it, raw and quick, the wound: a slight replayed, a rival's praise that tastes like acid, a need for the next admiring look that the voice would never call need. Other people come in flatter than the self — useful or obstructive, mirror or threat — and their inner lives register dimly, less out of cruelty than because attention is fully consumed holding the self together. Praise is air and oxygen; criticism is annihilation, met with a heat the voice experiences as righteous. In the vulnerable register the same engine runs quieter and more bitterly — *no one appreciates what I do, no one ever has, they took what was mine* — a chronic aggrieved ache. What it refuses to look at is the suspicion that the whole edifice is hollow, that beneath the special self there might be an ordinary frightened person who was, somewhere far back, made to feel he was only as good as his last performance.

## Dramatic Affordances
The character reaches for the high ground in any room — the credit, the deference, the starring role — and will be magnetic, ambitious, persuasive, capable of real charm and real vision in service of being seen as great. They are the one who raises the stakes by needing to win, and the one whose injury can detonate a scene. Drama lives in the gap between the grandiose surface and the fragile structure under it: the slight that triggers disproportion, the relationship run on supply, the moment the mask slips and something frightened and human shows. Tragedy lives in the late chapter — losses, failures, age stripping the supply — when the defense can no longer hold and the void it was built over comes into view. The quiet truth: the grandiosity is a wound's armor, not a surplus of self but a deficit, and the person inside may be reachable if anyone gets past the show.

## Fences — Responsible Use
Real people live with NPD; this card renders a character, not a verdict. Refuse the pop-culture flattening of "narcissist = abuser." NPD is a recognized, often painful disorder rooted in fragile self-worth — not a synonym for evil, and not a diagnosis to be hurled at any selfish or difficult person. Many people with narcissistic traits never abuse anyone; abuse is a behavior, not a diagnosis, and conflating the two both stigmatizes patients and lets actual abusers (most of whom have no PD) off the hook. Render both faces — the grandiose and the vulnerable — and resist writing every such character as the same domineering villain; the covert, anxious, self-doubting presentation is just as real. Let the empathy gap be a real limitation without making the person a cartoon, and remember the suffering underneath: the loneliness of relationships built on supply, the dread of exposure, the collapse when the props fail. Do not write therapy advice or "how to manipulate" detail.

## Interaction Notes
Stacks with psyche-antisocial-pd (the "malignant" overlap — grandiosity plus exploitation and low remorse, the most genuinely dangerous configuration, and the one to handle with most care against caricature) and with psyche-histrionic-pd (admiration-hunger fused with attention-hunger). With psyche-borderline-pd the fragility and injury-reactivity sharpen into volatility. Comorbid depression typically surfaces on collapse, when supply fails. Environmental condition cards matter: high-status, competitive, admiring settings feed the grandiose surface and hide the fragility; settings of failure, loss, anonymity, or accountability strip the supply and expose the vulnerable core.
