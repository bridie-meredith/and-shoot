# Condition: Dragon Presence

## Description

The ambient state produced by the physical proximity of a living dragon. Animals respond before humans do. The response propagates outward from the dragon's position and scales with the dragon's size, heat output, and movement state. A resting, concealed dragon produces a mild version; a flying or fire-breathing dragon produces the full version. Even a hatchling produces a detectable environmental shift.

## Sensory Impact

**At one hundred yards and beyond:**
- Horses and cattle become agitated without an identified source; they do not run yet but they orient toward the dragon's direction and pull against their leads.
- Songbirds in the area go silent in sequence, starting from the dragon's nearest point and spreading outward like a wave. The silence is the first reliable indicator of approach.
- Dogs bark and then stop. The stopping is more alarming than the barking.
- The air carries a faint sulfur note — distinctive, different from smoke or rot, not always consciously identified.

**At fifty yards:**
- Animals that can flee will. Horses will throw riders if not controlled by exceptional riders.
- The sulfur smell becomes unmistakable.
- Heat shimmer in the air around the dragon (a large dragon; less so for a hatchling).
- A low resonant sound below the threshold of clear hearing — more felt in the chest than heard with the ears.

**At twenty yards:**
- Human autonomic fear response regardless of prior cognitive state. People freeze, then flee, then freeze again. The sequence is not voluntary.
- The heat is physical and radiant.
- Scales visible as scaled patterning even in poor light — they catch light differently from any organic surface.

**For Taylor specifically (dragon-POV):**
- She is the source of this condition; she does not experience it as condition but as her own body's ambient state. She is aware that she is causing it. The insects in her range respond to her the same way they respond to any heat source and apex predator; their behavior is altered from baseline, which affects her reconnaissance.
- Insects within ten feet of her body are in a disrupted state — heat-affected and predator-adjacent — and provide degraded sensory feedback. Her reconnaissance dead zone extends several feet around her own body.

## Duration

Active as long as the dragon is present and detectable. Subsides within fifteen minutes of departure as animals calm; the silence lifts first, the sulfur smell last.

## Interaction Notes

**With condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology:** Taylor's bug reconnaissance is degraded near her own body due to insect heat-response and predator-behavior suppression. She compensates by deploying insects to the perimeter of her immediate presence rather than at arms-length range.

**With condition-riverlands-contested:** The dragon presence condition in contested territory immediately triggers factional response. Every lord in a ten-mile radius will receive reports within hours of a dragon sighting. The political clock starts immediately.

**With condition-targaryen-claim-disputed:** Every dragon sighting is reported to Targaryen agents eventually. The presence condition creates a trail of witnesses. Taylor cannot move through populated areas without leaving a record.

**With human characters:** Westerosi smallfolk have a mixed relationship with dragons — awe, terror, religious interpretation, and pragmatic fear. They will not approach a dragon voluntarily. They will report a dragon immediately. The Faith of the Seven has complex doctrine about dragons that is not primarily hostile (dragons are creatures of the world, not the enemy) but also not remotely reassuring (a dragon is a force of destruction that requires a rider to be a force of order). Septon Rowan's theological construction is working with this background.
