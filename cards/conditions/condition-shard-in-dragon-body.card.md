# Condition: Shard in Dragon Body

## Description

Taylor Hebert's Worm-shard (insect-control capability) persisted through reincarnation because it was bound to her soul-pattern, not her specific biology. In the dragon body, the shard is fully active and her power functions. However, the interface between a dragon's nervous system and a Worm-shard's control interface produces specific alterations to how the power operates and feels.

This card governs the mechanical and experiential specifics of Taylor using her power in a dragon body. It is a law card, not a narrative suggestion. Impersonators must check against it before writing any line involving Taylor's bug-control.

## Power Mechanics (Binding)

**What works identically:**
- Range of control: same soul-linked radius; she controls arthropods at the same effective range.
- Species scope: same; all arthropods, no non-arthropods.
- Simultaneous control count: same; she can manage millions of individual insects concurrently.
- Sensory uplink: she can feel through bug sensory systems as before.

**What is different:**

**Sensory integration difficulty:** A dragon's baseline sensory input (heat-sense, vision across a different spectrum, proprioception in a four-limbed winged body) is radically different from a human's. Integrating bug-sense into this alien sensory baseline is more disorienting than it was in a human body. In her first weeks, she experiences the dual-sense problem at high intensity: dragon-sense and bug-sense cross-wiring. This normalizes over time but never fully resolves. Running high bug-density reconnaissance while also executing dragon-scale physical action produces significant cognitive strain.

**Bug-sense dead zone near her body:** The dragon body produces heat and apex-predator pheromones that disrupt insect behavior within approximately ten feet of her. Insects in this zone are in a stress state that degrades sensory feedback quality. She compensates by deploying bugs to perimeter positions. This is not a critical limitation but it is a real one.

**Fire and swarm conflict:** If she deploys fire (a dragon physical capability), any bugs in the fire's path die. Mass-bug-death in a fire area produces the same sensory strike it did on Earth-Bet. She has developed tolerance; she has not developed immunity. Choosing to breathe fire in a dense-bug-deployment area is a tactical tradeoff she must make explicitly.

**Cold and power reduction:** Same as condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology's winter note: insect density drops in cold. Her effective capability is season-dependent. In winter she is primarily a dragon (fire, flight, physical force) with limited bug augmentation.

**Worm-pedant compliance note:** The shard persists because Worm's lore establishes shards as entities that bond to soul-patterns, not specific biology. The power's function follows the soul. This is explicitly the basis of the worm-canon-pedant's acceptance of this mechanic. Any extension of Taylor's power beyond arthropod control (toward other creatures) is a long-arc development and is not available at series open. Do not introduce it without explicit showrunner authorization.

## New Capabilities (Dragon Body)

Taylor's dragon body provides capabilities her power never covered on Earth-Bet. These are not shard-derived; they are biological:

- **Fire:** she can produce and project flame. Controlled, directable. The fire is real; it is not a shard effect.
- **Flight:** she can fly. The physical capability is available; dragon flight requires learning the body, which is a series of its own challenges.
- **Physical force:** a dragon is an apex predator with mass and scale. She is not operating in a small body. Physical confrontation is viable in ways it was not for human Taylor.
- **Sensory range:** dragon vision and heat-sense extend beyond human normal.

These capabilities are available. They are also visible. Using them publicly identifies her as a dragon with certainty, whereas her bug-deployment is invisible. She has a choice about which toolkit to show at any given moment.

## Duration

Persistent. This is the baseline mechanical reality of Taylor's existence for the series. It updates only if major shard developments occur (long-arc, showrunner-authorized).

## Interaction Notes

**With condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology:** Both conditions apply to Taylor simultaneously. Condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology governs what insects are available in Westerosi ecology. This card governs how they interface with the dragon body.

**With condition-dragon-presence:** Her own body produces the dragon-presence condition. Her bug-reconnaissance operates against the backdrop of disrupted insect behavior that her presence creates; she must deploy insects outward to escape her own presence-effect.
