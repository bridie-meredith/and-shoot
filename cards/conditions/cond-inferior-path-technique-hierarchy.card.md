# Condition — Inferior Path Technique Hierarchy

The Inferior Path's named techniques, their genre grade, what they actually are, and the broken-clock mapping. The consistency anchor for how Saerys narrates every action she takes — she never describes what she does in plain terms if she can narrate it as technique deployment. Bones authors reach for this card whenever she executes any of her operational moves so the naming stays consistent across all 30 chapters.

**Purpose of this card:** naming consistency + broken-clock enforcement. The biggest drift risk is bones authors inventing new technique names or varying the existing ones scene-to-scene. This card is the canonical technique vocabulary.

**Hard fence:** The broken clock stays broken. Every technique described here is her belief about what she is doing. The real-state column is what is actually happening. The genre-grade column is how a cultivation-genre-literate reader would classify the technique if it were real.

---

## Description

Saerys has named, graded, and documented every operational method she uses as a technique of the Inferior Path. The technique hierarchy is not a martial hierarchy — she has no combat techniques. It is a logistics-and-control hierarchy: the methods by which she manages information, materials, people, and access. In genre terms, she has produced a complete technical catalogue of a non-combat demonic path's toolkit. The techniques are real and effective. The cosmological framing is not.

---

## Technique Registry

### The Cauldron-Belly Method (Core Cultivation Manual)

**Her name:** Cauldron-Belly Nine-Revolution Body Forging  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade ascending; she expects it to become Earth-grade as she advances  
**What it actually is:** Systematic mithridatism — progressive, documented, dose-escalating poison tolerance work. The methodology is pharmacologically sound.  
**Her narration pattern:** "nine revolutions of the spirit-fire" = she is digesting; "the cauldron processes" = she is experiencing physiological response; "advancement achieved" = she has survived a harrowing ingestion and documented the outcome

**Broken-clock note:** Her self-description as a "body-cultivation variant of the nine-revolution body forging method" is genre-internally coherent. Body cultivators who temper through material ingestion are a real cultivation trope. The mechanism is mundane pharmacology. The trope is not wrong about the shape of what she does.

---

### The Account-Book Formation (Fabao and Formation dual classification)

**Her name:** The Natal-Intent Fabao; the Account-Formation  
**Her genre grade:** Tier 2 dao-artifact approaching natal-artifact resonance  
**What it actually is:** A ledger recording every transaction, contact, and operational note of her trade network. The formation eye — the controlling node — of her entire commercial operation.  
**Her narration pattern:** "consulting the formation's primary node"; "the account-formation records the current state of the network's qi-flow"

**Key consistency rule:** The account-book is never called a ledger or a notebook in her voice. It is always "the account-formation," "the natal fabao," or "the primary node."

---

### The Still-Room Domain (Domain Formation, Preliminary)

**Her name:** Cauldron-Belly Domain — preliminary formation; radius one chamber  
**Her genre grade:** Preliminary domain technique, mortal-grade  
**What it actually is:** A workspace organized for workflow efficiency and contamination prevention. In this space she has maximum operational effectiveness; in open, uncontrolled environments she is genuinely disadvantaged.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the domain's formation matrix suppresses impurity introduction"; "the formation is temporarily re-established at [new location]"

---

### The Document-Heist Formation-Break (Formation Breaking)

**Her name:** Document Eye-Destruction Method; the Talisman-Transmission Protocol  
**Her genre grade:** Earth-grade formation-breaking technique  
**What it actually is:** Legal document instruments — bills of exchange, charter-parties, manifests, sealed notes — drafted with sufficient precision that they pass through institutional control systems without triggering refusal.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the talisman has been activated and transmitted through the correct channel"; "the formation's eye has been addressed directly"

**Broken-clock note:** Her formation-breaking vocabulary maps exactly onto what she is doing. She identifies the control system's formation eye (the specific document type the inspector looks for), crafts a document correct at that node, and the formation's own qi-flow carries her through.

**Cross-books consistency:** "talisman" for a single-use letter, "formation-break" for a heist, "grand-formation disruption" for a complex Lock sequence. Same mechanism, register-level varies with stakes.

---

### The Below-Stairs Node Array (Formation Flag Deployment)

**Her name:** Inferior Path Outer-Disciple Formation; distributed detection array  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-to-earth-grade formation  
**What it actually is:** Trusted household members deployed at strategic positions as an intelligence network. Each member is a node. The network gives her coverage the official watchers cannot provide.  
**Her narration pattern:** "the formation-flag at the east corridor is reporting"; "I added a new node — the coverage gap has been addressed"

---

### Impurity-Expulsion (Impurity Removal Technique)

**Her name:** Impurity-Expulsion, Standard Protocol  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade; her own method  
**What it actually is:** Poisoning people who represent operational threats. She classifies them as impurities rather than as people.  
**Her narration pattern:** "an impurity in the operational field has been addressed through the standard protocol"; "the formation's contamination has been cleared"

**Broken-clock note:** The killing karma she does not log. The cultivation-genre's accounting would classify these kills as accumulating demerit. She has declared them karma-neutral. The genre does not accept this reclassification.

**Deployment rule:** Narrate with the same flat affect as reagent classification. The horror is in the equivalence, not in heightened prose.

---

### The Performed-Illness Defensive Formation

**Her name:** Sickroom-Exclusion Defensive Formation  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade; effective within its limited scope  
**What it actually is:** The managed appearance of illness that creates a social exclusion zone. The sickroom door is a real door; no observer can enter without transgressing the appearance of concern.

---

### The Precision-Question Interrogation (Spirit-Reading, Preliminary)

**Her name:** Preliminary Divine-Sense Technique, externalized through question-formation  
**Her genre grade:** Mortal-grade approaching spirit-grade  
**What it actually is:** Exceptional social intelligence trained by years of operating in a court where wrong information is fatal.

## Technique-Name Stability Rule

Impersonator and bones authors must use the canonical technique names from this card, not improvised variants. New technique names follow the grammar: [Method-type] + [Object/scope] + [Genre-grade qualifier, if stated].

---

## Sensory Impact

Active in every scene where she executes any operational move. Its sensory signature: a brief internal notation, narrated in register, before she acts. The technique-name is always deployed before the action, not after it.

---

## Duration

Persistent across all three books. New techniques are added in Bk II–III as her operational scope expands; existing techniques do not retire.
