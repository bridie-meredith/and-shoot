# Condition: War of the Five Kings — Riverlands

## Description

The ambient danger condition for the Riverlands during the War of the Five Kings, covering the period from Robb Stark's coronation through the Red Wedding's aftermath. The Riverlands is the war's primary killing floor — every faction has crossed it, burned it, or is burning it now. This condition makes the cost of every choice legible against a specific ambient threat level.

## Active Faction Breakdown

**Gregor Clegane's riders / Lannister outriders:**
The Mountain's men are the single most dangerous faction for unaffiliated travelers. They burn villages, kill smallfolk, and are operating under orders that provide significant latitude. They do not stop to ask affiliation; they stop to take and burn. Likelihood of mercy from them: near-zero. They are the condition's ceiling: this is what worst-case contact looks like.

**Lannister authorized forces:**
More dangerous than the Mountain's men in administrative terms if less immediately violent. They enforce the Lannister occupation through requisitioning, checkpoints, and the documentation apparatus. Travelers without acceptable documentation are detained. Detained strangers without affiliation are interrogated; interrogation outcomes are not predictable. They will ask for affiliation before acting; they will not ask twice.

**Frey checkpoints:**
Since the Red Wedding (which may have occurred by series open or may still be pending — project should specify), Frey men control significant river crossings and road segments. Their mandate is Lannister-aligned; their operational style is extortion-adjacent. They want coin or compliance. They will settle for compliance.

**Robb Stark's army / Northern forces in transit:**
Passing through rather than occupying. Their threat is displacement and resource scarcity: an army traveling eats whatever it passes through. Direct violence against smallfolk is not their pattern; collateral harm from sheer scale is.

**Brotherhood Without Banners:**
Operating as outlaws from the crown's perspective. Their actual practice: targeting Lannister outriders and Clegane-aligned forces; taking from villages when necessary but at rates that create resentment without the scorched-earth pattern of the Mountain's men. They announce themselves as protectors. The villages they operate near have a complicated relationship with this announcement.

**Scattered Riverlord remnants:**
Some houses are still fighting; some have already capitulated. Men from broken garrisons are moving through the Riverlands independently and present the variable threat of desperate armed men without institutional backing.

## Road Safety Assessment

**Open road without faction cover:** high danger. Armed travelers need identification or credible affiliation. Unaffiliated travelers are stopped, questioned, and subject to the disposition of whoever stops them.

**Village-to-village travel by day:** moderate danger, decreasing to high at dusk. Villages are being burned in patterns that make "recently burned" vs. "not yet burned" a meaningful travel calculation.

**Villages with faction checkpoints:** low-to-moderate physical danger, high administrative danger. You can likely survive the encounter; you may not survive the detention that follows.

**Forest routes (Brotherhood territory):** moderate danger, Brotherhood-specific. They stop travelers; they do not typically kill unaffiliated ones; they take resources and assess threat. An unaffiliated traveler who poses no threat to their operation may be allowed to continue.

## Detained Stranger Survival Odds

At series open, a stranger with no affiliations, no documentation, and no Common Tongue (Taylor's baseline) detained by any faction:

- **Mountain's men:** very low. They do not reliably maintain prisoners.
- **Lannister authorized forces:** moderate. They have institutional reasons to process detainees. Outcome depends on interrogation, claimed affiliation, and the officer's disposition.
- **Frey checkpoints:** moderate to low. Payment or service substitutes for affiliation in their calculus.
- **Brotherhood Without Banners:** moderate to high. They have a stated code; they follow it more reliably than other factions. Beric Dondarrion's verdicts are real.

## Faction Affiliation Demand

How quickly affiliation is demanded of travelers:

- **Mountain's men:** does not wait for declaration; does not care.
- **Lannister authorized forces:** at point of detention. First question after "halt."
- **Frey checkpoints:** at the checkpoint. Before toll assessment.
- **Brotherhood Without Banners:** after initial restraint, during assessment. They are gathering information, not executing immediately.

## Sensory Impact

The Riverlands during this period: smoke visible on the horizon in multiple directions simultaneously. The smell of burning that has been ambient long enough to be part of the air's texture. Villages with gaps in them — houses not rebuilt, fields not replanted, absences where people should be. Ravens overhead. The specific silence of a road that should have travelers and doesn't. Dogs that are not tied up because there is no one left to tie them.

## Duration

Persistent across the series in its baseline configuration. Specific beats — the Red Wedding, faction movements, the Brotherhood's changing territory — may shift local threat levels. The condition does not resolve within series scope; it is the ambient state of the setting.

## Interaction Notes

**With condition-language-barrier:** Taylor cannot claim affiliation, seek protection, or explain herself verbally at Stage 0–1. This makes her survival odds at any faction encounter significantly lower than a native speaker's. She cannot say "I am under the Brotherhood's protection" or "I have business with Lord Beric." She can only demonstrate, comply, and be assessed on behavior.

**With condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology:** Her power is real in this environment and constitutes a significant advantage — but using it visibly in front of faction soldiers is a high-risk action. The war has produced enough unusual deaths that an insect-associated fatality might be attributed to plague or witchcraft rather than to her. The interpretation depends on who is watching.

**With Ser Harwick Plumm:** The ambient condition is his operational environment. He uses the war's administrative chaos as cover for his extraction operation. The condition makes his threat real: behind him is the entire Lannister military apparatus, which is actively operating in this territory.

**With Mira Stonefield:** She has read this condition for years. Her survival network is calibrated to it. Her information about faction movements and burned village patterns is the product of surviving inside this condition.
