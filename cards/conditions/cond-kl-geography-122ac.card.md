# King's Landing Geography — 122 AC Reference

## Description

Physical geography and named landmarks of King's Landing at 122 AC. Canonical from ASOIAF and F&B. This card is the location-reference anchor for any scene set in KL — it governs what Taylor can and cannot see from where she is, what routes connect districts, and what landmarks define orientation. Not a full location card for any single place; a geography index for scene-setting consistency.

## Sensory Impact

KL's geography is felt in the body as gradient: elevation, smell-density, noise-density, and traffic-density all shift as one moves between districts. These gradients are the sensory texture of KL's geography beyond the named-landmark level.

## Duration

Persistent. The city's physical layout does not change within this project's scope.

## The Three Hills

King's Landing is built on and around three hills, each with a major structure:

**Aegon's Hill (central):** The Red Keep sits at its crown. The highest point of the city. The Keep's towers are visible from most of KL. The slopes of Aegon's Hill are the noble and administrative quarter — the houses of minor lords, merchants of substance, and the administrative buildings attached to the crown.

**Visenya's Hill (to the west of Aegon's Hill):** The Great Sept of Baelor sits here (canonical note: the Sept is not yet called "of Baelor" in 122 AC — Baelor I reigns 170s AC; in 122 AC it is the Grand Sept of King's Landing, the Faith's principal seat in the capital). The Faith's institutional presence — the High Septon, the Most Devout, the septons attached to the Sept — all cluster around Visenya's Hill.

**Rhaenys's Hill (to the east of Aegon's Hill):** The Dragonpit sits at its crown. Built to house the Targaryen dragons; in 122 AC it contains several bonded and unbonded dragons. The insect dead-zone described in `cond-dragon-proximity-122ac` centers here.

## Key Districts

**Flea Bottom:** The dense slum district that fills the lower ground between and around the hills, primarily south of Aegon's Hill toward the Blackwater Rush. Overcrowded, refuse-dense, warm. The Hook is a specific lane within Flea Bottom — a curved street near the waterfront where the most marginal Flea Bottom residents concentrate, including the gong farmers, tallow chandlers, and the lowest-end services. Taylor wakes in an alley off the Hook.

**The Hook:** Taylor's entry point. Within Flea Bottom but defined by its proximity to the Blackwater Rush waterfront — close enough for the tidal smell to reach, dense enough with transient and marginal smallfolk that a new face does not trigger immediate inquiry. The Hook's population is somewhat more fluid than inner Flea Bottom because of waterfront traffic.

**The Noble Quarter / Rhaenys's Hill approaches:** The streets between Rhaenys's Hill and Aegon's Hill are where noble households cluster. These streets are cleaner, Watch-patrolled, and socially surveilled by household servants. Taylor does not move freely here.

**The market districts:** Named streets function as market districts:
- **Street of Steel:** Armorers, blacksmiths, weaponsmiths. Noise and forge-heat.
- **Street of Silk:** Textile merchants, dyers, tailors. More prosperous trade; frequented by servants of noble households.
- **Street of Seeds:** Spice merchants, food vendors, apothecaries.
- **Street of Sisters:** Connects the Great Sept to the river approaches; gets its name from the two hills of Visenya and Rhaenys that it runs between at the city's lower level.

## The Seven Gates

KL's perimeter is defined by its walls and seven gates. These are the exits from and entries to the city — each gate has its own character, traffic type, and Watch presence:

- **Mud Gate:** Faces the Blackwater Rush; used by river traffic, fishermen, and smallfolk arriving from the water. Heaviest smallfolk traffic; least scrutiny.
- **Dragon Gate:** Faces east toward the Dragonpit. Primarily used for Dragonpit-related traffic (dragonkeepers, handlers, riders). High Watch presence.
- **Iron Gate:** Primary overland gate on the east side; one of the main approaches for traffic from the Crownlands and further east.
- **Gate of the Gods:** Faces north; used by religious pilgrims and traffic from the Faith-adjacent routes. Watch presence is present but often secondary to Faith-controlled movement management.
- **Lion Gate:** Faces west/northwest toward Lannister territories. Overland trade gate.
- **Old Gate:** One of the original gates; faces roughly west. Lower traffic volume than the Iron Gate or Lion Gate.
- **King's Gate:** The formal ceremonial gate; faces south toward the water and the royal harbor approaches. Used for official arrivals and departures.

## The Blackwater Rush and Blackwater Bay

The Blackwater Rush (river) feeds into Blackwater Bay (harbor) at the city's south face. The Rush flows along the city's southern edge; the Bay is the larger body. The city's river-gate approaches (Mud Gate, King's Gate area) open toward the water. The Hook is close to the Rush waterfront — the tidal and river smell is part of its ambient sensory texture.

## Orientation Principles for Scene-Setting

**From Flea Bottom:** The Red Keep on Aegon's Hill is visible as a silhouette from most of Flea Bottom on clear days. The Dragonpit's outline is visible from Flea Bottom's eastern edge. The Great Sept's dome is visible from high ground in Flea Bottom.

**Taylor's operational zone at story open:** The Hook and immediate Flea Bottom — a roughly 200m radius from her initial position. Her operational zone expands as she acquires intel routes and social contacts, but at story open she knows the streets within walking distance of the Hook.

**Navigation by smell and sound:** Flea Bottom navigation is as much olfactory as visual. The rendering yards, the tallow-chandlers, the midden-lanes, the fish-stalls near the Rush — each has a distinctive ambient smell that marks district and direction. Taylor's insect-network adds a layer of precision to this: she knows where the highest insect-density zones are, which tracks to food storage and refuse accumulation, which provides an involuntary geography overlay on top of the visual-olfactory one.

## Interaction Notes

**With location cards for specific places:** This card is the geography reference, not the scene-setting card. Specific location cards (the Hook alley, the Red Keep courtyard, the Dragonpit approach) carry the scene-level detail. This card provides the spatial relationship between those locations.

**For auditor use:** Flag any scene that places Flea Bottom on the east or north side of the city (it is south/central, below Aegon's Hill). Flag any scene that invents a gate not on this list. Flag any scene that puts the Great Sept on any hill other than Visenya's or the Dragonpit on any hill other than Rhaenys's. Flag any scene where Taylor's 200m range extends from Flea Bottom into the noble quarter (the distance is greater than 200m from the Hook).
