# Fauna Control Rules — 125 AC Config Addendum (flea-bottom-dance)

## Description

Project-scope addendum to `cond-fauna-control-rules`. All base card rules hold without modification unless explicitly overridden here. Load this card alongside the base card whenever checking Taylor's power mechanics. Where this card conflicts with the base card, this card wins.

The three primary overrides: (1) range opens at 300m not 200m, ceiling expands to ~1.5km by late s2; (2) Khepri-mantle is sealed at story open, unlocks as a discrete threshold event, and must be treated as Faustian crossing not power-up; (3) glass-candle relay is the sole mechanism beyond the local ceiling — it is a relay path only, not a general capability pathway.

## Sensory Impact

Same as base card. No ambient sensory expression from the constraint itself. The constraint is felt in what Taylor cannot reach, what she must physically move to access, and what she registers as the edge of her sphere.

## Duration

Persistent. Range parameters expand through use, but within the ceiling defined here. The Khepri-mantle unlock is a one-time threshold event, not a progressive unlock. The glass-candle ceiling is permanent absent relay infrastructure.

## Override 1: Range at Story Open

**Story-open range: 300m radius.** The base card specifies 200m normal / 400m extended. This project opens at 300m. The higher baseline reflects a previously-triggered host arriving with a shard that has already run one complete career — the shard's initial seeding is above baseline for a first-trigger host. The 300m figure is the normal operational range without concentration effort.

**Extended range at story open:** Concentration extends range proportionally. At story open: ~500–600m under active concentration, displacing other cognitive tasks. This does not change the base card's concentration-cost rules; it shifts the numbers.

**Ceiling:** Organic ceiling through use is ~1.5km by late season 2. This is not a number Taylor can push through training or willpower. It is a hard local-only ceiling imposed by the air-gap degradation described in `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`. Beyond 1.5km, no fauna-control is possible without external relay infrastructure.

**Range expansion is organic and legible to the audience.** The prose must register range expansion as it happens — through scenes where Taylor reaches something she could not reach before, through operational notes in her internal register, through the broken maester noting the change. Range expansion is not a quiet background process. It is a visible power-progression with associated behavioral cost (the accumulation of high-density operation building toward the Khepri threshold).

**Taylor knows her range precisely at all times.** This is a carry-over from the base card but bears restating at this config: she has exact proprioceptive awareness of her sphere's edge. She does not guess. She knows. Prose implying uncertainty about range boundary is a violation.

## Override 2: Khepri-Mantle — Sealed at Open, Threshold Unlock

**Status at story open: SEALED.** Khepri-mantle (multi-body human coordination) is not available at episode 1. It is not partially available, not suppressedly available, not available under stress. It is sealed. There is no version of season 1 or early season 2 in which Taylor coordinates human bodies.

**What sealed means:** Taylor cannot extend control-grade awareness into human nervous systems. She can observe humans through her insect-network (reading breath, body temperature, proximity, physical state from insect-level perception). She cannot direct humans. She cannot sense human intention. The mantle-of-Khepri register — the ambient awareness of many human bodies moving as a coordinated mass — is not present. Its absence is felt as a relief she cannot name. She ran that level of awareness once, at Gold Morning's cost. She does not have it. She knows she does not have it.

**Unlock condition (mandatory):** Sustained high-density bug-control operation building sufficient local-processing capacity — informational threshold, not emotional revelation, not willpower, not narrative climax. The shard self-optimizes local processing when the operational load is sufficient to trigger the optimization. This is the only valid unlock condition. Any scene, plan, or story beat that implies a different unlock condition is a constraint violation.

**Expected unlock timing:** Late season 2 peak, triggered by the sustained relay-load operation that is simultaneously building toward the s2 arc's Khepri-threshold crossing. The two events are causally linked: the relay-load operation is the triggering operation; the threshold is what fires.

**Unlock register:** Faustian threshold crossed, not power-up, not relief. The cost of the triggering operation must be visible in the prose before the mantle fires. The audience must see the cost coming before Taylor names the capability. Any exhale beat, any sense that a problem has been solved, any triumphant register at unlock is a violation.

**Unlocked ceiling:** ~dozen humans at peak, not thousands. Gold-Morning-scale is permanently foreclosed (see below). The unlocked mantle is degraded relative to Gold Morning by the same air-gap logic that caps range. A dozen is the hard ceiling absent relay infrastructure.

**Visible sequencing with s3 demonstration:** The Khepri-mantle threshold crosses in late s2. The s3 demonstration (rider-reversal at faction-convergence) occurs in s3. These are explicitly separated by a season boundary. The demonstration is caused by the unlocked mantle — it cannot fire before the unlock — but they are not simultaneous or undifferentiated. Any story beat that collapses the threshold crossing and the demonstration into a single scene or single episode is a structure violation.

## Override 3: Glass-Candle Relay — Scope and Ceiling

**What glass-candle relay enables:** Extension of Taylor's operational range beyond the 1.5km local ceiling. The relay mechanism is a signal-propagation pathway, not a power-amplifier. It does not increase the density of Taylor's control, the sophistication of her perception, or the number of creatures she can manage simultaneously. It increases range only.

**What glass-candle relay does NOT enable:**
- Additional arcane capabilities unrelated to range extension
- Communication with other glass-candle users
- Any capability not directly traceable to "range of the insect-network extended"
- Khepri-mantle extension beyond the ~dozen-human ceiling
- Any retroactive explanation for capabilities Taylor uses that are not otherwise justified

Drift toward glass-candle work as a general-purpose magic acquisition pathway is a scope-creep violation. If a scene implies glass-candle research is producing Taylor capabilities that are not range-extension, that is a fence violation against this card and `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`.

**Mechanism (Taylor's working hypothesis — not confirmed truth):** Insect-density saturation produces a signal that the glass-candle apparatus can propagate. This is Taylor's hypothesis, tested empirically, partially falsified at s4. The s4 foreclosure event (density-saturation failure, log burned) reveals that the threshold is individual, not aggregate — the mechanism Taylor was working from was partially wrong. The relay path requires a different model she has not yet developed at series close.

**Acquisition timeline:** Glass-candles enter via the tallow-chandler fence / smallfolk salvage channel in the s2–s3 window. Taylor does not acquire them via institutional channels, noble patronage, or Citadel infiltration. The salvager (tallow-chandler) is structurally unaware of what they are passing along.

**Glass-candle research as load-bearing:** The research arc is not flavor. The late-series capability cap depends on whether the relay path is operational. The s4 foreclosure event forecloses the self-narrative of successful-relay as cleanly as it forecloses the scientific-caution self-narrative. By series close, Taylor has demonstrated that the mechanism partially works (s3 demonstration leverages whatever partial relay she has established), not that she has solved it.

## Physical Cost Curve — Adjustments for This Config

Base card cost curve applies. The higher story-open range (300m) does not reduce cost — the cost curve is calibrated to the capability level, and 300m at this config is the equivalent of 200m at base config. Time thresholds are the same.

**Range expansion cost:** As range expands organically toward the 1.5km ceiling, the passive-sense load increases. At story open (300m), the passive feed in Flea Bottom is significant but manageable. At 1.5km ceiling, the passive feed in Flea Bottom is substantial — the density of insect and rodent life in a crowded urban environment at that range produces a constant background that demands suppression energy. The cost of suppressing the passive feed at range-ceiling is itself a background drain that does not appear in the cost curve but accumulates.

**High-density operation cost (relevant to Khepri-threshold):** The triggering operation for the Khepri-mantle unlock is a sustained high-density relay operation. The cost curve for this operation is in the range of the "beyond 30 minutes continuous" tier — the triggering operation pushes into and past blackout risk, which is part of why the Faustian register is correct. The operation that fires the threshold is itself a dangerous operation run past the point of safety. The prose must show this.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-fauna-control-rules` (base card):** This card overrides specific numeric parameters and adds Khepri-mantle and glass-candle relay rules not present in the base card. All other base card rules (species scope, wrongness-perception, raven dispatch rules, capacity rules, concealment rules) apply without modification.

**With `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`:** The air-gap constraint is the root cause of both the degraded range ceiling and the sealed Khepri-mantle. Both this card and the no-parahuman-infrastructure card should be loaded together for any constraint check involving Taylor's power limits.

**With `cond-shard-behavioral-weight`:** The behavioral weight card describes the escalation bias the shard produces. This card describes the operational limits. The behavioral weight does not grant Taylor capabilities beyond this card's ceiling, and this card's ceiling does not reduce the behavioral weight.

**For auditor use:** Flag any scene where Taylor's range exceeds 300m in s1 without narrative justification of organic expansion. Flag any use of Khepri-mantle capability in s1 or early s2. Flag any glass-candle capability that is not range-extension. Flag the Khepri-mantle unlock if it appears to be triggered by emotional state, narrative climax, or any condition other than the named informational threshold. Flag any range expansion that is not registered in the prose.
