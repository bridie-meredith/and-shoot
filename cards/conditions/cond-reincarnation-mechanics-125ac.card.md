# Reincarnation Mechanics — Hard Rules for flea-bottom-dance (125 AC Config)

## Description

Governing constraint card for the terms of Taylor Hebert's reincarnation in the flea-bottom-dance project. Defines what carried over, what this specific arrival created, what it permanently closed, and what paths do and do not exist. All agents must load this card when writing Taylor's internal experience of her own continuity, her relationship to her prior life, her relationship to the tanner-family and tanner-village, or any scene that raises questions about whether she could return, escape, or access Earth-Bet resources.

This card supersedes and replaces `cond-reincarnation-mechanics` (taylor-hebert-westeros) for this project. The 120 AC / septon-ward / Harrenhal-adjacent config is the wrong config. The config here: ~17 years old at arrival, Crownlands tanner-family, fever-death body, no septon ward, no prior-Westerosi-childhood gap, community frame is Stranger-leavings not septon-care. All agents check this card, not the prior version.

## Sensory Impact

No ambient sensory expression. The constraint is felt from the inside: Taylor has complete episodic and semantic memory of her prior life, and a body that produces different sensory data than the one she died in. At 17 (rather than 11), the body-schema mismatch is less severe — a ~17yo body is physically closer to her remembered adult body, though still significantly smaller and weaker. The mismatch surfaces at moments of high physical stress, extreme danger, or sleep. It does not present as dramatic dissociation; it presents as the quiet persistent wrongness of hands that are slightly the wrong shape.

## Duration

Permanent. These rules hold for the full project duration. They cannot be altered by in-world events.

## What Carried Over

**Full episodic memory.** Taylor carries complete recall of her life as Taylor Hebert: childhood, trigger event, cape career, the Slaughterhouse Nine encounters, the Echidna incident, the S-class threat sequence, Cauldron, Gold Morning, her death. These are intact memories she can access as clearly as any other autobiographical memory. She does not experience them as a dream. They are her life, lived, complete.

**Semantic knowledge.** Everything she knew about the Worm universe — cape powers, Shard mechanics, the Ward structure, Earth-Bet geography, the political map of parahumans — is intact. Internally useful; externally unreferenceable. No one in Westeros can confirm, contradict, or use it.

**Planetos knowledge — text, not lived experience.** She retains her Earth-Bet knowledge of ASOIAF and Fire & Blood as a reader. That knowledge is a reader's knowledge of a text — she knows the Dance of Dragons is coming, knows the factions and their key figures, knows approximate historical outcomes. She does not know what Rhaenyra sounds like when frightened. The distinction between knowing a history and knowing a person is permanent.

**Tactical cognition and pattern recognition.** The cognitive restructuring produced by her trigger event — multithreaded parallel processing, tactical reading of conflict space, threat assessment instinct — is present in the new body. It is structural; it did not require the Shard to maintain.

**Core temperament.** The person she is — tolerance for necessary evil, discomfort with authority that does not earn itself, the reflex to protect people who cannot protect themselves, willingness to pay costs others will not — is continuous. She is Taylor Hebert. The body is different. The person is not.

**Shard, at 300m story-open config.** The shard reseeded with her arrival. Present from breath one. She is a previously-triggered host. The shard operates air-gapped from story open; see `cond-fauna-control-rules-125ac-addendum` and `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure` for operational details.

## What This Arrival Created Anew

**A body with its own history — approximately 17 years of it.** The body Taylor inhabits is a ~17yo Westerosi human female. It is not a blank. It has been alive for seventeen years and carries that in its bones, calluses, muscle-memory, posture habits, and taste-thresholds. She arrived with:
- The tanner's daughter's gait — long hours standing, leather-work posture, specific callus pattern on palms
- The body's habituated physical competencies: farm-adjacent labor, whatever physical tasks a tanner's daughter performed, the specific rhythm of the work
- Taste-thresholds: she knows what this body finds bitter, what it finds sweet, not because she remembers Tya tasting these things but because the body's responses are the body's
- The body's general condition: not well-fed by any comparative standard; pre-fever, this body was functional but not strong

What she does NOT have from Tya's history: **personal memories.** No episodic recall of Tya's life. No recognition of Tya's relationships. No access to Tya's fears, hopes, or self-understanding. The body knows how to tan leather in the habituated-motion sense; she does not know why Tya loved or hated the work. This gap produces specific moments: she can perform the work without fumbling, but she has no story about the work. She cannot explain preferences she has inherited.

**No transition gap — no prior-Westerosi-childhood gap.** Unlike the 120 AC config (where there was a pre-awareness childhood at the sept Taylor had no memory of), this arrival is into a dead body. Tya died. Taylor arrived in the dead body. There is no gap of Westerosi childhood she cannot account for — she simply woke in the body as it was the moment of death. She has no Crownlands-childhood memories to explain or conceal. Her cover story is the truth as the village understands it: she died and came back wrong.

**Named-by-absence.** Taylor does not take a Westerosi name. She does not answer to "Tya." The tanner-family calls her Tya because the body is Tya's body and habit is stronger than theology; she does not correct them and does not respond with warmth. In Flea Bottom, she is unnamed — not named differently, simply not named. She introduces herself as nothing. Over time she may acquire a working name from context, but this is not an authoring decision; it is a story decision the protagonist makes or does not make. No agent should provide Taylor with a Westerosi name or imply she has accepted one.

## What Did Not Carry Over

**Personal memories of Tya's life.** Taylor has the body's habituated knowledge — physical skills, taste-thresholds, posture patterns — but not Tya's episodic memory. She cannot recall Tya's relationships, fears, or self-knowledge. This is the cleanest version of "body's history without personal memory" possible.

**The original body.** Gone.

**Parahuman infrastructure.** Covered by `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`.

**Her Earth-Bet relationships.** Emma, Lisa, Brian, Rachel, the Undersiders, the Wards, Cauldron contacts — dead or unreachable. She knows they are dead. These are not relationships she is separated from; they are relationships that ended at Gold Morning and cannot be resumed. She is alone in a way that is total and non-negotiable.

**Her name and identity as claimable.** No one in Westeros knows who Taylor Hebert is. She cannot invoke her prior identity as any kind of claim.

## What the Reincarnation Closed

**Return.** No route back to Earth-Bet. Not through Planetos-native magic, not through any Shard mechanism, not through death and re-reincarnation, not through any mechanism Taylor has knowledge of or can conceive. This is not a rule she must reluctantly accept; it is a physical fact she has already accepted. Her internal model does not include a return scenario.

**Contact with the prior world.** She cannot communicate with Earth-Bet. Cannot receive information from it. The people she knew are inaccessible.

**Explanation.** She cannot explain her prior life to anyone in this world. The Westerosi frame cannot accommodate the explanation. The Stranger-leavings category is as close as the community gets to understanding the situation, and it is correct in its own terms: something came back in the body, and that something is not who the body belonged to. She has learned not to attempt explanation.

**The ability to be invisible.** She is ~17, which is closer to social legibility as an adult woman than the 11yo config. This creates different exposure: she is not a strange child who can be categorized as touched and managed; she is a young woman who is strange, and a strange young woman is more legible as threatening than a strange child. The categories available to her (touched, Stranger-leaving, old magic) still apply, but the social stakes of being wrong about her category are higher.

**The reincarnation was involuntary.** She did not choose this. She does not have a patron, a deity who placed her here, or a mission the reincarnation was designed to accomplish. She does not know why she is here instead of simply dead. This absence of explanation is permanent.

## The Body Is Real

Taylor's current body is not a projection or temporary vessel. It is a real biological entity that was born, lived seventeen years, died of fever, and is now occupied. It will age, can be injured, and will die again. She is not protected by prior-life status.

**Body condition at story open:** Better than the 11yo config in terms of physical competence (the body has adult motor patterns); worse in terms of recovery from the fever death. She woke in a body that had just undergone a lethal fever. The immediate story-open period involves the body's recovery from its own death — weakness, disorientation, the immune system's aftermath. She is not at full physical capacity in the opening episodes. This is not a long arc; it is a starting condition.

**Physical competence:** The body's seventeen years of labor give Taylor a physical starting point significantly above the 11yo config. She is not strong by any combatant standard, but she is not fragile. She has endurance from long work, a body accustomed to physical discomfort, and the manual dexterity of practiced trade work. Her adult cognition can make use of this in ways the 11yo config could not.

## The Fever-Death Body: Specific Constraints

**No competing consciousness.** Tya's death was complete before Taylor inhabited the body. There is no lingering Tya. No split-consciousness. No moments where Tya's personality intrudes. Taylor is the sole occupant. Any scene implying a second consciousness in the body is a fence violation.

**Tya's personal memories are absent, not suppressed.** They are not there to recover, unlock, or accidentally access. Taylor does not "remember being Tya" under any circumstance. Any scene implying memory-recovery of Tya's life is a fence violation.

**Common Tongue fluency arrived with the shard, not from Tya's memory.** Taylor's ability to speak and understand Common Tongue came with shard-seeding at arrival, not from inheriting Tya's language knowledge. She is fluent from breath one. This is not isekai-magic-translation; it is a specific property of shard-seeding at arrival. The distinction matters: she speaks Common Tongue as Taylor, not as Tya. Her speech patterns are not Tya's patterns. Her accent is slightly wrong in ways the village notices.

## Uniqueness

Taylor is the only displaced soul in this project. No other reincarnated Earth-Bet parahumans, no other isekai arrivals, no other persons operating with anachronistic knowledge. This constraint is absolute for the series duration.

## Taylor's Internal Experience of the Arrival

Taylor does not experience the reincarnation as miraculous, as reward, or as a second chance. She was thirty-two years old psychologically when she woke in a seventeen-year-old body in a room she did not recognize, in a community that was performing grief over her corpse. The reincarnation is simply where she is now. She has not forgiven it for happening. She is not waiting for it to reveal its purpose.

**What is different from the 11yo config:** She is closer in physical age to who she was. This is not easier — it is differently wrong. At 11, the mismatch between her cognition and her body was the central management problem. At 17, the management problem shifts: she can pass as an adult more easily, but the social constraints on a young woman alone in Crownlands/KL are different from the social constraints on a child. She has traded one class of exposure for another.

**What carries from the 11yo config experience (via prior-project Vibe Seeds):** The accumulated weight of having run this arc before — the 84 AC and 120 AC lives, the prior series of accommodation-and-escalation — is in the vibe seeds of the persona card. At the story-world level, this is her first Westerosi life. The vibe seeds inform register without producing in-world continuity.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`:** The no-return-channel rule on that card is the Shard-specific expression of the general no-return rule here. Load both for scenes where return or contact might arise.

**With `cond-crownlands-superstition-frame-125ac`:** That card governs how the community narrates Taylor's situation. This card governs what is mechanically true. The community's Stranger-leavings frame is not correct about the mechanism but is not wrong about the experiential fact: something is in the body that was not there before.

**With `taylor-hebert-flea-bottom` persona card:** The persona card's Hard Fences and Vibe Seeds carry the Gold Morning weight and the second-life-not-reward framing. This card is the structural source; the persona card is the operational expression.

**For auditor use:** Any scene where Taylor behaves as though return is possible is a constraint violation. Any scene where another displaced soul appears without project authorization is a constraint violation. Any scene where Taylor recalls Tya's personal memories is a fence violation. Any scene where someone provides Taylor with a Westerosi name she has accepted is a fence violation. Any scene where Common Tongue fluency requires explanation or effort is a violation. Any scene where the reincarnation is implied to have a discoverable purpose is a constraint violation.
