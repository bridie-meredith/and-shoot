# Earth-Bet Proper-Noun Fence — Hard Dialogue and Prose Ban

## Description

Hard authoring fence. Taylor Hebert's parahuman vocabulary — the specific proper nouns, institutional terms, and cape-register jargon native to Earth-Bet — is banned from dialogue and restricted in inner monologue. This is a world-law constraint, not a stylistic preference. No Westerosi character has any framework for these concepts; voiced parahuman jargon is incoherent to everyone in Taylor's environment and breaks the reader's immersion in the POV. This card governs what Taylor may not say aloud and what she may think only under defined conditions.

## Sensory Impact

None. A constraint card. Its expression is the absence of certain words from dialogue and the rarity of certain concepts in inner monologue.

## Duration

Persistent. Applies across all chapters, all scenes, all POV positions in this project.

## The Banned Terms — Dialogue (Absolute)

The following terms and categories are dialogue-banned. Taylor does not say them aloud to any Westerosi character under any circumstance, including extreme emotional pressure, injury-state, or scene-level shock:

**Power/entity vocabulary:** shard, entity, Queen Administrator, Khepri, trigger event, trigger, tinker, thinker, master, shaker, mover, striker, breaker, changer, blaster, stranger, brute, trump, any Worm-canon power-classification term.

**Institutional vocabulary:** PRT, Protectorate, Parahuman Response Team, Wards, Undersiders, Cauldron, Slaughterhouse Nine, Birdcage.

**Cape-name vocabulary:** Skitter, Weaver, Khepri, any cape's cape-name. Also: Taylor Hebert is the only name Taylor has in this world — she may use it; what she does not use is the cape-name register around it.

**Earth-Bet geography and institution names:** Brockton Bay, Earth-Bet, Gimel, Aleph, any shard-world designation.

**Gold Morning as explicit concept:** Taylor may reference *the event that ended her world* in inner monologue; she does not name it "Gold Morning" in dialogue. She has no word for it that would mean anything to a Westerosi listener.

## Inner Monologue — Restricted

In inner monologue (first-person Taylor POV), parahuman proper nouns may appear *sparingly* and under defined conditions only:

**Permitted:** When Taylor is actively comparing a Westerosi phenomenon to an Earth-Bet analogue and the comparison is doing analytical work (e.g., she is reading a political structure through the lens of a known group's organization). In these cases, the Earth-Bet reference is her internal cognitive shorthand, not an expressed concept.

**Frequency cap:** Inner-monologue parahuman proper nouns should not appear more than once or twice per chapter. If the project's inner monologue contains frequent parahuman jargon, the POV has drifted toward Taylor-as-Earth-Bet-protagonist rather than Taylor-as-displaced-person-in-a-foreign-world.

**Hard cap:** Taylor never thinks of herself as *Skitter* or *Khepri* or any cape name in inner monologue in this project. Those identities are closed. She is Taylor Hebert, and the closed identities are weight she carries, not names she reaches for. Any scene where a cape name re-activates as Taylor's inner self-reference is a fence violation.

**Gold Morning:** May appear in inner monologue as a named reference. It is the organizing trauma of her experience and the analytical lens through which she reads political instability. It is not suppressed from her own thinking. It is suppressed from any communication she makes outward.

## Substitution Register — What Taylor Uses Instead

When Taylor thinks or talks about her capability, she uses functional descriptions without source-material naming:

- "My insects" / "the swarm" / "what I can do with the flies" — not "my power" or "my ability" in the abstract (which sounds like cape-register)
- "The way I see" / "what the bugs show me" — for the passive awareness sense
- "What I did at the end" / "the thing I did" / "that last day" — for Gold Morning references, if she needs to externalize
- Silence is often the correct choice — Taylor does not explain herself

**Westerosi-language description:** Taylor has had time to accumulate some Westerosi framing for what she does. If a patron or ally asks what she can do, her answer is in their language: "I know where the rats are. I know what the flies see. I know before you do." She does not explain the mechanism.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`:** That card governs the world-law (no parahuman infrastructure exists on Planetos). This card governs the authoring fence (Earth-Bet vocabulary is banned from dialogue). They are complementary and both must be loaded for any scene where Taylor's capability is discussed or implied.

**With `cond-westerosi-witness-vocabulary`:** That card governs how Westerosi characters describe Taylor's capability in their own terms. This card governs what Taylor herself may say and think. Together they form the complete vocabulary fence for Taylor's power-set in this project.

**For auditor use:** Flag any dialogue line by Taylor that contains a Worm-canon proper noun. Flag any inner monologue where cape names appear as Taylor's active self-reference (as opposed to historical reference to a closed identity). Flag any scene where Taylor explains her capability using Earth-Bet conceptual vocabulary (power classification, shard mechanics, trigger events) to a Westerosi listener.
