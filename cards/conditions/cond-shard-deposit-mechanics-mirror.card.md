# Shard Deposit Mechanics Mirror — Taylor's Arrival Mechanism (This Project)

## Description

The arrival mechanism specific to this project: Shard-mediated involuntary deposit. A dying Shard fragment carrying residual Worm-logic deposited Taylor as a byproduct of its own death-process. She did not choose this; the Shard did not choose her specifically. What carried over, what closed permanently, what the "no local identity" condition creates, and what makes this arrival config different from the body-occupation variants used in prior projects.

**This card supersedes `cond-reincarnation-mechanics-125ac` for this project.** The body-occupation mechanics are not applicable here. Taylor arrived as herself, not in a prior occupant's body.

## Sensory Impact

The arrival mechanism has no ongoing sensory expression — Taylor's current state is the result of the deposit, not a continuing process. The arrival's effects are present in what Taylor carries and what she lacks: she carries full episodic memory and her temperament, but she has nothing from the arriving context (no documents, no clothing from Earth-Bet, nothing external to her body that would identify her origin). She is physically herself, but in a foreign world with nothing.

## Duration

Permanent state. The deposit is complete; the Shard is dead; the transit is over. Taylor is here. She is not in a transient state waiting to resolve.

## The Arrival Mechanism

**A dying Shard fragment's residual death-process deposited Taylor into Westeros as a byproduct — not as a mission, not as a selection.**

*From world-notes.md (binding):* "Mechanism of arrival is Shard-mediated involuntary deposit — a dying Shard fragment carrying residual Worm-logic deposited Taylor as a byproduct of its own death-process."

This means:

- Taylor did not volunteer for transit
- The Shard did not evaluate Taylor as the best candidate for a Westerosi deployment
- There was no purpose-driven selection mechanism
- The transit was the Shard's death-throes, not a directed action

**Why this matters narratively**: Taylor arrived without orientation, without preparation, and without any framework for understanding what happened to her. Her weeks-long calibration period in Flea Bottom is the process of reconstructing a functional operational framework from scratch. She is working with Gold Morning survival instincts and nothing else.

## What Carried Over

**Full episodic memory**: Taylor has complete memory of her life through Gold Morning. Everything she knows, she knows. Her Worm-canon endgame state is her starting state here: post-apocalypse-survivor, post-Khepri, post-escalation-training, post-trauma.

**Tactical cognition**: The analytical, threat-assessment, tactical-planning capability she developed through two years of cape escalation and the Scion event. This is the primary survival resource she arrived with. She has no cape power to apply it through, but the cognition itself is intact.

**Core temperament**: Her fundamental character — the zealot-of-prevention orientation, the willingness to commit bad acts for good reasons, the specific emotional texture of someone who has survived the worst and believes she is uniquely positioned to prevent it happening again — arrived with her.

**The flicker** (see `cond-flicker-discipline-mirror` for complete rule-set): The Shard-transit deposited an involuntary precognitive residual — fragmentary, unreliable, involuntary near-future glimpses. This is the transit's sole parahuman residual.

## What Did Not Carry Over

**Queen Administrator**: Gone. The power-to-aggregate is gone. Taylor cannot control insects, cannot receive their sensory input, cannot direct them as a weapons platform.

**Khepri**: Gone. The state-change that Taylor underwent during the Scion fight is not present. She is Taylor Hebert, not Khepri.

**Any other parahuman capability**: No Worm-universe powers present except the flicker.

**Parahuman infrastructure recognition**: There are no capes in Westeros. No Cauldron, no Dragon, no Contessa, no Parahumans Online. No one in Westeros can read Taylor as a powered individual in any canonical sense. Her flicker is invisible as a power-act; it expresses only through involuntary behavioral tells.

*From world-notes.md (binding):* "No parahuman support structure exists in Westeros — no Contessa, Dragon, Cauldron, or Worm-context peer who can read what Taylor is. Her errors cannot be corrected by a system that understands her."

## What Closed Permanently

**Return to Earth-Bet**: Closed. There is no return mechanism. The Shard is dead. The transit is over. Taylor is in Westeros permanently. She knows this.

**Contact with the prior world**: Closed. No communication channel to Earth-Bet exists or can be constructed.

**Parahuman infrastructure**: Permanently absent. Taylor cannot acquire parahuman support in Westeros because the infrastructure does not exist.

**The prior life**: Taylor Hebert of Earth-Bet is a memory and a formative history, not a recoverable state. She carries what she was; she cannot be what she was in this world's terms.

## The No-Local-Identity Condition

This is the critical difference from the body-occupation config:

**In body-occupation projects**: Taylor arrived in a prior occupant's body, with that occupant's local relationships, family, name, and history (even if she had to learn them retroactively). She had a social identity waiting for her. The "came back wrong" frame was the primary witch-label mechanism — people who knew the prior occupant recognized that something was wrong with the familiar face.

**In this project**: Taylor has no prior occupant's body. She arrived as herself — unfamiliar face, unfamiliar accent, no recognizable family connections, no one who looks at her and sees someone they know. She is entirely foreign.

**What this produces**:

- No name that is hers in local terms (she uses "Taylor" but this is not a name the Westerosi cultural context finds legible — it neither sounds Westerosi nor connects to any local family)
- No documented origin (she cannot claim to be from a specific place in Westeros without inconsistencies; her accent and cultural-knowledge gaps make any such claim easily challenged)
- No prior social ties that create automatic trust or automatic status
- A Flea Bottom life constructed entirely from scratch in weeks, using Gold Morning resourcefulness and nothing else

**The witch-label formation consequence** (see also `cond-kl-witch-label-formation`): The label does not form around "came back wrong" because there is no prior occupant's normalcy to violate. The label forms around "appeared without origin" — someone who is simply here, with no documented arrival story, no family, no prior. The Westerosi superstition frame has vocabulary for this: the stranger who appears without explanation is already in the category of potentially-touched-by-something. Taylor's behavioral tells (the flicker's expression) add the knowing-strange layer onto an already-unusual foundation.

## No Other Worm-Universe Entities

*From world-notes.md (binding):* "Taylor is the only displaced soul. No other Worm-universe entities present."

This is the uniqueness clause. There is no other cape, no other Shard-deposited person, no Worm-universe faction operating in Westeros. Taylor is a singularity in this world. Her errors cannot be caught by a peer who understands her framework. Her successes cannot be confirmed by anyone who knows what she knows.

This is the structural basis for the "no one can correct her" axiom: without a peer who shares her frame of reference, Taylor's Gold Morning pattern-recognition is self-validating. She sees the signals she expects to see, and there is no one who can say "that's not how this world works."

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-flicker-discipline-mirror`:** The flicker is the transit's sole parahuman residual. This card describes what the transit was and what it deposited; that card governs how the deposit operates.

**With `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`:** That library card establishes the general absence of parahuman infrastructure in Westeros. This card establishes Taylor's specific arrival state — she is a foreign-physics intrusion in a world with no category for what she is.

**With `cond-kl-witch-label-formation`:** The no-local-identity condition shapes which witch-label mechanism applies. The "appeared without origin" foundation is this card; the label's mechanics are the witch-label card's domain.

**For auditor use:** Any scene where Taylor uses Queen Administrator or any other Worm-universe power (other than the flicker) is a hard violation. Any scene implying Taylor has a return path to Earth-Bet is a violation. Any scene where another Worm-universe entity appears in Westeros is a uniqueness-clause violation. Any scene where someone in Westeros correctly identifies Taylor's framework without patron mediation is a violation.
