# Condition — Maester's Cabinet (Quick Reference)

Condensed 60-second bones-author lookup for the 15–20 most-used still-room substances. Each entry: maester-register name · one-line real property · Saerys's cultivation-tier gloss. Distilled from `maesters-cabinet-glossary.md` (~90 entries). For fine pharmacological detail (dose→lethality windows, tolerance ceilings, mithridatism efficacy), load `cond-westerosi-poison-pharmacology.card.md`. For Tier 0–4 table, load `cond-westeros-reagent-tier-map.card.md`.

**Hard fence:** Saerys's cultivation-tier gloss is in the belief column. Real properties are in the right column. The broken clock stays broken.

**Cross-reference:** `maesters-cabinet-glossary.md` is the exhaustive 90-entry version of this card. Reach for this card at bones/facets time; reach for the full doc only when authoring a scene requiring precise dosing, specific compound interactions, or a substance not on this list.

---

## Description

The maester's still-room cabinet: what is in it, what it does, what she calls it. Every still-room scene, every apothecary visit, every poisoning or healing beat requires knowing what is physically available. This card is the bones-author's fast answer.

---

## Sensory Impact

The still-room's ambient sensory register (from `loc-still-room`): layered chemical smell — the sharper sulfur-and-acid top note from mineral acids and alchemical preparations, beneath it the herbal middle (dried poppy, rue, pennyroyal, nightshade berry — bitter and vegetal), and beneath that the base of preserved fats and beeswax from ointment stocks. When specific compounds are in active use, their scent rises above the ambient layer. Loading this card alongside `loc-still-room` gives bones authors both the physical space and its contents.

---

## Duration

Persistent across Bk I (still-room at Red Keep, primary). Portable subset carried in the kit across Bk II–III (load `prop-still-room-kit` for the travel form). Cabinet composition changes as stocks are used and restocked; the core 15–20 listed here are always present unless explicitly depleted.

## The 18 Most-Used Substances (Quick-Reference)

### Sedatives / Analgesics

**Milk of the poppy** `[canon]`
Real: Opium preparation — morphine/codeine; analgesia, sedation, respiratory depression at overdose; dependency risk.
Her gloss: "Tier 0–1 Water/Yin qi-calmer; the vital fire at low dose, extinguished at high."

**Sweetsleep** `[canon]`
Real: Powerful sedative; steep dose-response; small dose = sleep, larger dose = unstoppable sleep, death is quiet and facially peaceful.
Her gloss: "Tier 1 Yin-suppressant; the most efficient technique for producing simulated natural sleep in a counterparty."

**Valerian** `[real-world]`
Real: Mild anxiolytic and sleep aid; *Valeriana officinalis*; non-addictive; strong smell when dried.
Her gloss: "Tier 0 Yin-herb; adequate for blending; insufficient as primary reagent."

**Henbane (black henbane)** `[real-world]`
Real: Anticholinergic (hyoscine, hyoscyamine); sedative and antispasmodic; hallucination at moderate dose; death at high; narrow window.
Her gloss: "Tier 1 early — overlaps with nightshade in attribute profile; supplementary when primary nightshade series is in tolerance consolidation."

### Poisons / Toxins (Primary Series)

**Tears of Lys** `[canon]`
Real: Slow dissolution of vital organ tissue; odorless, colorless, tasteless; mimics natural wasting; death 72–120 hours.
Her gloss: "Tier 1 peak — primary operational reagent; Water-attribute qi-disruptor; the most-documented substance in the dose-log (entries 100–115)."

**Nightshade / Belladonna** `[canon]`
Real: *Atropa belladonna*; atropine/scopolamine; low dose anticholinergic, moderate dose hallucination/delirium, high dose death; 10–20 berries lethal for adult.
Her gloss: "Tier 1 early-to-spirit — Earth attribute with Void side effect (the hallucinations). Training reagent series (dose-log entries 3–15)."

**Wolfsbane / Aconite** `[canon]`
Real: *Aconitum*; aconitine; opens sodium channels — tingling mouth, cardiac arrhythmia, convulsions, death; lethal at very small quantities.
Her gloss: "Tier 1 early-to-spirit — Fire/cold duality; Foundation training reagent. Tolerance ceiling: ~8-grain-weight; further escalation contra-indicated."

**Hemlock** `[real-world]`
Real: *Conium maculatum*; ascending paralysis; mind clear as body fails; death by respiratory paralysis.
Her gloss: "Tier 1 early — Metal-attribute restriction technique; the consciousness-field remains active; documented as 'complete mobility tribulation.'"

**Widow's Blood** `[canon]`
Real: Stops bowels; progressive gastrointestinal shutdown over 3–7 days; agonizing slow death; appearance of natural illness.
Her gloss: "Earth attribute — attacks the cauldron's operational pathway (elimination cycle); professional interest in resistance."

**Greycap / Death Cap** `[real-world]`
Real: *Amanita phalloides*; amatoxins inhibit RNA polymerase II; 6–24 hour symptom-free lag, then GI crisis, "walking ghost" apparent recovery, liver failure, death 5–10 days; no pre-modern antidote.
Her gloss: "Tier 1 — Earth-attribute slow dissolution; the lag mechanism noted as methodologically interesting for temporal deniability."

**Arsenic (white arsenic)** `[real-world]`
Real: Arsenic trioxide; low-dose tonic use in maesters' practice; slow-poison via progressive accumulation; chronic exposure: neuropathy, skin changes; acute: vascular collapse and death. Historical arsenic tolerance precedent (Mithridates) is the foundation of her entire program.
Her gloss: "Tier 0–1 Earth-attribute mineral; the Mithridates precedent cited in dose-log as foundational justification."

### Wound Care / Antiseptic

**Vinegar** `[real-world]`
Real: Acetic acid solution; cleaning wounds; reduces bacterial contamination; painful on open tissue.
Her gloss: "Tier 0 Water-acid reagent; cleaning-attribute; no cultivation value."

**Spirits of wine (distilled alcohol)** `[real-world]`
Real: Ethanol at sufficient concentration; antiseptic; wound-cleaning; also extraction solvent for herbal preparations; the primary solvent in the still-room.
Her gloss: "Tier 0 Fire-attribute extraction medium; the essential solvent of the Inferior Path's preparation work."

**Honey** `[real-world]`
Real: Antimicrobial due to osmotic pressure, low pH, and hydrogen peroxide production; wound dressing standard; also base for preparations.
Her gloss: "Tier 0 Earth-attribute healing medium; acceptable for coating wounds; the base material for the simplest medicinal blends."

---

### Herbal / Preparatory

**Willow bark** `[real-world]`
Real: Contains salicin (precursor to aspirin); anti-inflammatory and fever-reducing; the maester's standard for pain and fever.
Her gloss: "Tier 0 Wood/Water attribute; fever-reduction technique; unremarkable but consistently functional."

**Feverfew** `[real-world]`
Real: *Tanacetum parthenium*; historically used for fever, headache, migraine; anti-inflammatory effects; some evidence for migraine prevention.
Her gloss: "Tier 0 Wood attribute; fever technique; milder than willow bark; suitable for recovery-phase use."

**Foxglove / Digitalis** `[real-world]`
Real: Cardiac glycosides; therapeutic dose strengthens and slows heartbeat; toxic dose causes arrhythmia and cardiac arrest; extremely narrow window.
Her gloss: "Tier 0–1 Fire/Water balance reagent — acts on the heart's qi-circulation; dual-use profile; tolerance work has been careful."

**Pennyroyal** `[real-world]`
Real: *Mentha pulegium*; historically used as an emmenagogue (stimulates or increases menstrual flow); abortifacient at high doses; hepatotoxic at the doses required for abortion; can cause death.
Her gloss: "Tier 0–1 Wood attribute; the women's-medicine column; the cauldron has professional familiarity with its operational envelope; not used in personal regimen."

## What Is Not On This List (At Bk I Baseline)

- **Manticore venom** — Essos only; aborted at early onset (entry 133); protocol revision pending
- **The Strangler** — rare, Essos-origin; not yet sampled; flagged as protocol gap
- **Yi Tish exotics** — the poison-master's case (acquired Bk II M2); see `prop-still-room-kit` for integration
- **Shade of the evening** — Qarth; unavailable; she knows of it; she has not pursued it
