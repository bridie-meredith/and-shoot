# Condition: Riverlands Contested

## Description

The Riverlands are the geographic center of every war Westeros has. No natural defenses; navigable rivers that serve as invasion corridors rather than barriers; no dominant house since the Teagues were destroyed and the Tullys installed as lords paramount (and the Tullys are paramount in name more than fact). At any given time, multiple lords have overlapping claims, boundary disputes, and competing loyalties to major houses. The Brackens and Blackwoods have been feuding for centuries. The roads run through everyone's territory and no one's territory.

At ~110 AC, the contested condition is present at a low simmer: no active war, but the structural conditions for conflict are always in place. The Targaryen peace has suppressed major conflict for decades; the peace does not remove the underlying tensions. The lords are polite to one another in formal settings and have been trying to kill each other's tenants over boundary markers since before any of them were born.

## Sensory Impact

The condition shows in:
- **The landscape:** fields and villages that have been burned and rebuilt, sometimes multiple times in living memory. Stone keeps with fresh timber scaffolding where wood was added hastily. Farmsteads with defensive positioning that no pure agricultural logic would explain.
- **The people:** smallfolk who have a practiced assessment reflex when strangers arrive on the road. Not paranoia — pattern recognition. Someone's banner on the road means something. What banner, which direction, how many men.
- **The infrastructure:** roads that are maintained to military movement standards in some stretches and let fall to ruts and holes in others, depending on which lord's territory they run through and whether that lord has any reason to care about road quality right now.
- **The politics:** every conversation has a subtext about allegiance. Who do you serve. What house are you sworn to. These questions are always somewhere in the room even when no one asks them.

## Duration

Persistent. This is the Riverlands' baseline condition. It escalates during wars (Dance of Dragons will make it catastrophic) and subsides during long peaceable reigns (Jaehaerys I reduced it; it returned). It never goes away.

## Interaction Notes

**With condition-dragon-presence:** A dragon in the contested Riverlands is a political emergency for every lord in the region simultaneously. It will produce both competition and cooperation among lords who are normally in opposition — common interest in either claiming or removing the dragon overrides some of the usual faction dynamics, at least initially.

**With ser-aemon-bracken:** He is a product of this condition. His holding exists in the contested borderlands; his entire strategic practice is built around navigating the low-grade conflict that never fully stops. He understands the condition from the inside.

**With mira-stonefield:** Her survival network is a direct product of this condition. The information flows she has built exist because people in contested territory need information to survive.

**With condition-targaryen-claim-disputed:** The combination of contested regional politics and Targaryen claim creates a specific dynamic: local lords have their own interests in the unclaimed dragon that may or may not align with the crown's. Cooperation with crown agents is not automatic.
