# Flea Bottom — District-Level Card (Operational Anchor s1 mid through s3)

## Geography

The poorest district of King's Landing, occupying the lower ground south of the Street of Steel, north of the Hook, and running toward the Fish Gate at the southern wall. The district is densely built on the incline where the city's slope meets the harbor-adjacent lowland, and the drainage has never been adequate to the population. Flea Bottom is not a neighborhood that was planned; it is a neighborhood that accumulated around the industries and populations the rest of King's Landing did not want.

Borders: Fish Gate to the south (primary exit toward the docks and the harbor road); the Street of Steel to the north (where the city's more respectable trades begin); the city wall to the west at the Muddy Way; and the eastern quarter, where the apothecaries and smaller medical trades occupy a slightly less dense section, to the east.

Taylor's operational radius of 300m at story open, centered on her Flea Bottom lodging, reaches: the full block of the immediate district, the Fish Gate and the margin beyond it, and — critically — just into the eastern quarter where the broken maester's apothecary stands. By late s2, the expanded radius reaches toward the Red Keep's outer ring.

## Layout

Dense, irregular, built up over generations without plan. The streets are mostly alleys — narrow enough that two people passing requires one to turn sideways, and wide enough for a handcart if the handcart is small. The structures are two and three stories, built with additional stories added by owners over time, creating a skyline that leans and overhangs and blocks light at the street level.

Key interior geography:
- **The Fish Gate margin:** the edge of Flea Bottom where it meets the gate and the road south toward the harbor. This is where the chronically-evicted and camp-follower population lives — the most transient part of the district. The s4 surviving experimental subject lives here permanently by series end. Taylor's insect network covers this margin.
- **The market-side junction:** where the tanner-elder operates. A slightly wider meeting of alleys that functions as informal market space. The densest point of labor-web activity.
- **The eastern quarter adjacent:** the part of Flea Bottom that edges toward the eastern quarter proper. The streets are slightly less dense here, the buildings slightly more maintained. The apothecary that the broken maester lives above is at the edge of this boundary.
- **Taylor's lodging sector:** see `loc-flea-bottom-base`. Located within this district approximately 250m from the eastern-quarter apothecary, keeping both within the 300m operational radius.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**Smell:** Flea Bottom smells of density — the specific compound of many bodies in enclosed spaces, inadequate drainage, coal smoke, tallow rendering, animal waste, and the salt-adjacent smell of proximity to the harbor. The smell is layered and changes by location within the district: the rendering yards carry the tallow note; the Fish Gate margin has more of the harbor; the market-side has food smells layered over the baseline. Taylor's insect network has made this smell-map part of her operational picture — atmospheric conditions that shift the smell pattern at her range can indicate changes in wind direction, new activity in a closed space, or movement of large groups through the alleys.

**Sound:** Constant at daytime. Multiple registers: the raised voices of market activity at the junction; the mechanical sounds of the trades (hammer on metal from the Street of Steel to the north, which filters down as a background percussion); the specific sounds of overcrowded housing (arguments through walls, children, animals, the creak of stairs designed for lighter loads than they carry). At night: quieter, but not silent. The harbor sounds carry up. Dogs. Occasional raised voices. The specific silence of alleys being watched, which is a different quality of silence than alleys being empty.

**Visual:** Dark at street level even in daylight, because the overhanging upper stories block the sun in the narrower alleys. Laundry strung between buildings. Refuse at the edges of alleys — there is no formal collection. People constantly in motion during daylight: small trades, children running routes, water carriers, the visible labor-web traffic that the tanner-elder manages from his junction.

At night: torchlight in the main alleys, darkness in the side alleys. The Fish Gate is lit by gate-guard torches and by the lanterns of people who can afford lanterns. Most of Flea Bottom navigates by familiarity, not light.

**Texture:** The ground in Flea Bottom is packed earth in the narrower alleys and rough cobbles in the wider ones. Both are perpetually damp in the lower sections. The walls of the buildings are rough stone or timber-frame with wattle, and the textures that insects navigate are rich — crevice, mortar gap, timber grain, the specific surfaces that a Flea Bottom insect ecology has evolved to exploit.

## Fixed Props

None card-level. Location-specific props are runtime state.

## Exits

- **Fish Gate (south):** The primary exit toward the docks and harbor road. The gate is staffed; passage is routine for smallfolk during daylight and requires specific reason after dark. Taylor's operational range covers the Fish Gate margin.
- **Street of Steel (north):** The transition into the city's more prosperous trades. Not a natural exit for Flea Bottom residents but accessible.
- **Eastern quarter (east):** The adjacent district where the apothecaries and smaller medical trades operate. Within Taylor's 300m radius. The broken maester's apothecary is at the eastern edge of this transition zone.
- **Muddy Way (west):** City wall adjacent. Leads to a minor gate used for specific trade traffic. Not a convenient exit.

## Hazards

- **The density saturation constraint (late s2, project-specific):** As Taylor's range expands toward 1.5km and as she begins sustained high-density operations within the district, the population density of Flea Bottom becomes a load factor in the relay-mechanism experiments. The district is the primary operational substrate.
- **Lord's awareness:** Flea Bottom is technically within a lord's jurisdiction, but the practical administration is minimal. The City Watch makes periodic passes. The Watch's relationship to the district is enforcement of obvious crime rather than surveillance; they are not watching for supernatural operators.
- **Social intelligence:** Flea Bottom is a dense social network. Information travels faster than a person can walk. Taylor's operational movements within the district are visible to the district's network even when she attempts concealment. She has adapted by operating within the expected patterns.
- **The Fish Gate margin vulnerability:** The most transient part of the district is also where the s4 surviving subject will eventually live. This area is within Taylor's operational range and the subject's presence-in-surveillance but absence-from-log is a s4 horror element.
- **The Hightower intelligence network (s2):** As Otto Hightower's apparatus begins incorrect profiling of Taylor, the district will receive attention from agents whose presence Taylor will detect through her insect network before they have identified her. This creates a specific kind of operational pressure: she knows she is being watched before the watchers know who they are watching.
