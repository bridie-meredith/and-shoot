# Reach Smallfolk Village

## Geography

A village of perhaps eighty people in the heart of the Reach — grain fields on three sides, a stream to the north, a road that carries occasional traffic from a more significant town a half-day's ride away. Not poor by Westerosi standards; the Reach is fertile and these people eat. Not wealthy either. The lord whose land this is visits twice a year to collect dues and is otherwise absent. There is a godswood at the village edge — a stand of weirwood, heart tree and its attendants — maintained out of old habit by a family whose grandparents came south from the Riverlands.

## Layout

A single main road through the village, the common at its center, the septon's house at one end, the godswood path at the other. Dwellings are mostly wattle-and-daub with thatch. The midwife's house is slightly larger than most; she has a workroom attached. The mill sits at the stream. The common has a well, a pen for market-day animals, and the lone tree where notices are read aloud.

## Sensory Vocabulary

Bread and turned earth in the morning. Animal smells throughout the day — chickens, goats, draft horses, the smell of dung in the pen. The stream is cold and fast enough to hear from the road. Summer evenings: fires, the smell of tallow candles, children running in the last light. The godswood at the edge smells of old bark and something that doesn't have a name — colder than it should be, older than the village, the kind of quiet that has a texture.

## Fixed Props

None catalogued.

## Exits

- Main road north → major town (half-day's travel)
- Field paths → open Reach farmland
- Godswood path → the weirwood grove (adjacent; outside village edge)
- Stream path → the mill and the woods beyond

## Hazards

- The godswood: the heart tree is old; the villagers keep a distance; there are stories about children who went in at night; shard-active capes find the proximity produces involuntary responses
- Winter: the Reach is mild but not immune; a bad harvest year produces desperation
- Travelers on the road: not all traffic is benign

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- The midwife called urgently to a difficult birth or fever case
- A traveler stopping at the common — rare enough to produce attention
- The godswood making a sound at night that the elder villagers recognize and the younger ones don't
- The lord's tax collector arriving ahead of schedule
