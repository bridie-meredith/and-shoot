# Red Keep — Interior (and-experiment configuration)

The internal spaces of the Red Keep as Saerys inhabits them: Maegor's Holdfast as cage and home; the royal apartments; the maester's still-room as the primary operational space; and the Great Hall as the occasion-space. The geography of daily operations for a dragonless princess running a charity apothecary network from a fortified box inside a fortified castle.

---

## Geography

The Red Keep stands on Aegon's High Hill, the highest point of King's Landing. At its fortified heart: **Maegor's Holdfast** — twelve-foot walls, spiked dry moat, single drawbridge, royal nursery within. The outer castle contains the Great Keep (throne room, formal spaces), Tower of the Hand, Maester's Tower, sept, servant quarters, gardens. The distances between zones are short but strictly governed by social geography.

---

## Layout

### Maegor's Holdfast

The cage. Bridge (almost always down) controls access; the real containment is the septa-and-chaperone structure.

- **The nursery / Saerys's chamber:** starts as standard nursery; becomes progressively less nursery and more laboratory as she grows; by Bk I end contains an unauthorized account-book and a smell the household has stopped investigating.
- **Alicent's solar (adjacent):** the administrative center; Saerys attends correctly; uses the time to assess household concerns.
- **The drawbridge:** chain-and-counterweight mechanism; audible throughout the holdfast; she can tell from the sound whether it is raising or lowering.

### Royal apartments

Formal chambers and connecting corridors between Maegor's Holdfast and the Great Keep.

- **The connecting corridor:** primary movement space; watched during daylight; specific unwatched hours she has mapped.
- **Helaena's room:** adjacent; Saerys visits freely; household treats this as normative and stopped tracking it.
- **The maester's consulting room:** attended for lessons; empty at known hours.

### Maester's Tower / still-room

The operational center.

- **The still-room proper:** stone-floored; cold-water basin (one wall); drying-racks (other wall); dried herb bundles; ceramic jar rows (labeled by the maester; her relabelling system parallel and invisible); small brazier. Smell: dried herbs, recent compound work, unidentified third register she has introduced.
- **The maester's supply cabinet:** initially locked; solved within a moonturns of access. She knows its inventory in detail; the maester knows his in approximation. She restocks items she removes.
- **The maester's library:** she rearranged it at age five. The rearrangement is cultivationally accurate by her taxonomy. The maester accepted it because his most-used texts are now somehow more accessible. He does not know why.
- **The back stair:** a servants' stair connecting the still-room level to the supply delivery area on the outer ring. She found this at age six. The Septa does not know it exists. No light source; she has memorized the step count.

### The Great Hall / throne room

She attends when required, observes sightlines and exit locations. The gallery above has its own access and useful acoustics.

### The sept of the Red Keep

She attends devotions; is observed attending; is not cognitively present in the way the Septa would prefer. She has mapped the sept's specific sound-carry directions over years of sitting in the same spot.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**Maegor's Holdfast:** Cold stone (warms slowly near hearths). Wax-and-soap smell of a maintained children's space. High windows producing directional light at specific hours. The audible drawbridge mechanism — chain-and-counterweight — as a location-signal.

**The still-room:** Primary sensory location in the project. Dried herbs (complex, season-variable). Recent compound work (sharper; varies by current project). The third chemical register she has introduced: clean-acid under the herbs, faintly metallic when the brazier is active. The cold-water basin's trickle is a work-session timer. The back stair: stone, dust, no light.

**Royal apartments and corridors:** Beeswax candles (not tallow; maintained throughout). Cedar and lavender from stored clothing. Tapestries absorb sound; formal corridors quieter than servants' passages. Specific echo pattern: sound attenuates at approximately twelve paces.

**The Great Hall:** Stone and open volume. High ceiling with different acoustic properties. Gallery acoustics favor upward listening. Rushes (ordinary) or sanded stone (formal occasions). The Iron Throne's visual weight at the far end.

- Back stair (undocumented in household plans)
- Drawbridge chain-and-counterweight (audible throughout Maegor's Holdfast)

## Exits

**Maegor's Holdfast:** The drawbridge (only official exit; always monitored). She escapes the system by operating within it (the loopholes), not by finding alternate physical exits.

**Still-room:** Main door (to the maester's tower corridor). Back stair (servants' delivery access; connects to outer ring supply area; Septa does not know it exists).

**Royal apartments:** Formal monitored corridors; Helaena's room (low surveillance by household convention).

**Great Hall:** Multiple mapped exits; gallery access via located stair.

---

## Hazards

**The watcher problem (Bk I primary hazard):** The cage is the attendant, not the door. The hazard is observation, not architecture. Every space requires mapping who is watching what when. The hazard escalates if a watcher cross-references accounts.

**The septa's report-filing:** Correct observations correctly reported, reaching the King through the "spirited-child filter" and accomplishing nothing. The hazard is filter-failure: enough reports in close enough succession that the pattern becomes undeniable.

**The supply cabinet:** She raids it; she restocks it. The hazard is careful inventory by the maester — which her ledger-competence prevents by making herself operationally necessary to him.

**The back stair:** No light source; structural soundness uncertain in places. Utility ends if anyone else discovers it.

**Maegor's Holdfast isolation (double-edged):** Protects the royal family AND protects still-room work from external inspection. Both functions are real.

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- Still-room compound failure producing unexpected smell, color change, or audible reaction — question is whether it was heard outside
- Maester noticing a supply gap; his theory about it is wrong but involves proximity to the accurate answer
- The drawbridge going up unexpectedly for unscheduled reasons, cutting off a planned route
- A Kingsguard on a corridor she considered lightly watched, with forty minutes of sensitive account-books to move
- Helaena in the still-room doorway for an unknown duration, making a sideways observation of uncertain accuracy
- The maester searching for a rearranged text with escalating irritation; she has approximately one hour
- A factor's letter arrived via the below-stairs delivery route, sitting under a flagstone at the back stair's base
- Court function scheduled with less notice than usual; three hours to move the current project to a safe location
