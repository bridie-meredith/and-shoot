# Dragon Gate Market Alley — King's Landing (126–129 AC)

## Geography

The inner alley system of the Dragon Gate market zone — the specific work-day geography Taylor moves through repeatedly over 36 months. Not the broad approach to the gate arch (which is the outer market zone with the traveler-transit stalls) but the one row in: the established trader row where the guild-registered operations run, where the craftsmanship shops have permanent ground-floor frontage, where the daily commerce of the gate district happens in its most routine and legible form.

The alley is roughly 80 meters of primary working frontage on each side, with several branching side-passages connecting to the courtyard spaces behind the permanent buildings. It is within Taylor's range cap from any position in the alley. She can stand at the east end of the alley and have the west end at the edge of her ambient awareness. She can stand centrally and have the full alley in range.

This is where Rung-1 (month 4, tanner's apprentice's grain-watering) and Rung-2 (months 9–10, merchant short-weight) play out. This is the ambient block-life geography — the grain store, the merchant stalls, the Watch patrol turning point, the water-carrier's route, Mira's workshop entrance, the passage to Daveth's counting-house.

## Layout

**East end (gate-approach adjacent):** Where the inner alley meets the outer market zone at the gate approach. Heavier foot traffic from arriving gate-traffic. The grain provision stalls are here — including the stall with the tanner's apprentice connection in the Rung-1 context. This end of the alley is noisiest and most watched by the Watch patrol's turning-point near the staging yard.

**Central alley:** The established trade row. The cloth trader, the metalmonger, Mira's workshop (south side), the general-provisions merchant (Rung-2's short-weight operator), the dye-goods trader, the ironmonger's front. The permanent commercial infrastructure that the block has maintained for decades. The buildings here are older than the outer-zone stalls; their ground-floor frontage has been refined by generations of use.

**West end (interior-city-street adjacent):** Where the inner alley opens toward the city interior. Quieter foot traffic — the people moving through here are residents and regular traders rather than gate-transit traffic. The water-carrier's (Nolla's) route runs through the west end toward the residential-layer well.

**Side passages:** Multiple narrow passages off the inner alley connecting to the courtyard spaces. Daveth's counting-house is accessed through one of these passages. Other courtyards serve as goods-movement and storage space for the permanent traders.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**Smell:** The layered smell of a working market alley. The grain-area at the east end: dusty-sweet grain smell, the specific sour note of grain that has been waterlogged and dried (the Rung-1 context). Moving west: the metalmonger's coal-and-hot-iron smell, the cloth trader's fabric smell, the rope-workshop fiber-and-lanolin. General: cooking from the outdoor provisions stalls, animal smell from gate-traffic animals that drift through, the damp-stone smell of the passages.

**Sound:** The continuous percussion of a working alley. The metalmonger's hammer is the loudest regular sound; it establishes the alley's acoustic baseline. The gate arch's traffic (cart wheels, animal calls, gate-keeper's voice) arrives from the east end as a muted background. The water-carrier's yoke-and-bucket assembly makes its specific route-sound (creak and slosh) on a schedule Taylor has had mapped since month two. The Watch patrol's footstep rhythm — two officers, the eastern turning-point, the western turning-point — is Taylor's primary institutional-presence indicator.

**Light:** Morning: eastern light floods the gate-approach end and works its way west over the first two hours after dawn. Afternoon: the wall's shadow falls on the outer alley first, then creeps into the inner alley from the east; by mid-afternoon the inner alley is in mixed-shade. Evening: the rope of amber lanterns across the alley provides commercial light until the traders close. The passage openings are always darker than the alley proper; side-passage ambient light is the single lamp the resident or trader at the other end maintains.

**Temperature:** The inner alley runs warmer than the passages in summer (the stone walls collect heat during the afternoon shadow period and hold it into the evening). In winter the alley runs cold from midday, with the wall-shadow providing no warmth benefit.

**The insect register:** Taylor's primary ambient operational surface. Insects in the inner alley are distributed across the full ecosystem: grain-area flies and beetles at the east end, the metalmonger's forge-adjacent insects (which thin near the heat and thicken at the passage edges), the fiber-insects of Mira's workshop, the food-vendor flies, the rats in the passages and the residential-layer above. At 150m diameter with Taylor positioned centrally, the full alley is in range. She has individuated the alley's population to the creature-by-creature level within three months.

## Fixed Props

- Grain provision stalls (east end)
- General provisions merchant stall with short-weight scale (central, north side)
- Cloth trader's ground-floor frontage
- Metalmonger's ground-floor frontage
- Mira's workshop frontage (south side, central)
- Dye-goods trader
- Rope of amber lanterns (strung across the alley width between buildings, for evening commerce)
- Resident well (west end approach, serving residential layer above shops)
- Multiple passage entrances to rear courtyards

## Exits

- **East end** — outer market zone, gate-approach. Connects to the Watch patrol staging yard boundary and the main gate-transit traffic.
- **West end** — interior-city street. The alley's connection to the broader King's Landing street network, outside Taylor's range if she is positioned at the alley's east end.
- **Side passages** — multiple. The primary relevant exit is the passage to Daveth's counting-house courtyard. Other passages connect to residential courtyards and goods storage.

## Hazards

- **The Watch patrol's turning-point:** The patrol turns at the east end of the inner alley, near the grain stalls. Any activity at the east end that deviates from the market's baseline is observed by the Watch officer's turning-point scan. Taylor has timed her grain-area work to the patrol interval from month one.
- **The Rung-1 grain-store:** The specific grain stall where the tanner's apprentice's waterlogged supply is held. Taylor's report to the Watch captain — framed as riot-risk, not illness — is the first deployment of the patron-dialect model. The physical location of the event is the grain stall's back-area storage.
- **The Rung-2 merchant:** The short-weight merchant's stall is a fixed commercial presence on the north side of the central alley. His short-weight practice is real; Taylor's report of it as guild-stability risk is accurate; the guild fine and partial restitution is the outcome. The stall remains.
- **The fire risk:** The evening lantern-rope is an open-flame risk running across the full alley width. In a wind event or careless lamp-maintenance situation, the lantern-rope is the ignition source for an alley-wide fire. The market has fire-response protocols. Their speed is not reliable.

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- Water-carrier's route: Nolla's twice-daily route through the alley (morning fill, afternoon secondary run) is a background constant; her schedule is the most reliable civilian rhythm in the alley
- Metalmonger's work cycle: the hammer starts and stops on a roughly four-hour work-session schedule; the acoustic baseline of the alley changes when the hammer is idle
- Market-day versus off-day density: the alley's foot-traffic density varies enough to register — on heavy market days the alley is shoulder-to-shoulder; on quiet days it is half-empty; Taylor's insect ambient thins and thickens accordingly
- Patrol interval: the Watch patrol's east-end turn is approximately every forty minutes; Taylor could give the interval in seconds by month three
- Grain delivery: a periodic cart delivery of new grain to the east-end stalls involves a cart in the alley approach; the cart blocks the east end for twenty to forty minutes and disrupts the Watch patrol's turning-point
- Evening lantern lighting: the transition from natural light to lantern light changes the alley's sensory register completely — the amber light, the shadow-filling, the acoustic shift as the ambient sound of late-market commerce replaces the daytime working sounds
