# Dragon Gate Block — King's Landing (126–129 AC)

## Geography

The Dragon Gate block is the mixed-use market and transit zone surrounding the Dragon Gate — King's Landing's eastern landward entrance. The gate itself is the city's primary connection to the eastern Crownlands road network, and by extension to the river-road that runs to the dockyard approaches. The block occupies roughly 300 meters in each cardinal direction from the gate arch, bounded on the west by the city-interior streets, on the east by the gate arch and the road beyond, and on the north and south by adjacent ward boundaries.

**Relationship to the city:** Dragon Gate is not Flea Bottom (which is southwest and landward, a different social register entirely). It is not the Red Keep district. It is not the major trade piers, which are at the western harbor. Dragon Gate's specific position: eastern gate, landward road access, with river-dockyard-adjacent goods traffic coming through on the interior side of the gate. The block is mixed-class in a specific way — it is neither smallfolk-density-landless (as Flea Bottom is) nor noble or guild-senior. It is the transit zone: travelers, caravan-adjacent merchants, gate-registered traders, the Watch presence of a gate station, a ward septon's sept, a scatter of craft operations that serve the gate-traffic needs (rope, metal fittings, provisions, animal handling).

**Taylor's operational territory:** ~150m radius from wherever she is standing within the block. The gate arch, the market alley, Mira's workshop, the contact's premises, the market stalls — all within or at the edge of her operational range when she is positioned centrally. The guardhouse is at the gate's immediate margin. The block is Taylor's entire world for three years.

## Layout

**The gate arch and approach:** The Dragon Gate arch itself: stone, two cart-widths across, with the gate-keeper's watch platform above. The approach road from the east enters the city here. The market zone immediately inside the gate is the first commercial contact for arriving travelers — stalls, vendors, animal-handlers, water-carriers, the ambient chaos of a working transit point. The Watch patrol staging yard is immediately adjacent to the gatehouse on the south side of the arch.

**The market alley system:** Running south from the gate-approach along the wall's interior face, a network of semi-permanent stall rows and permanent ground-floor shop spaces. The outer alley (closest to the wall) handles travelers' immediate needs: food provisions, animal fodder, water-carrying. The inner alley (one row in from the wall) has the more established traders: cloth goods, dyes, hardware, rope. Mira's workshop is in the inner alley. The contact's counting-house is in a side courtyard off the inner alley.

**The ward sept:** North of the gate arch, a modest Faith structure. The ward septon's administrative and pastoral center. Small charitable-function space attached. The sept has a small graveyard/bone-yard behind it — the Faith's minimal provision for gate-district dead.

**The dockyard-road edge:** The southern boundary of the block opens toward the river-road approach. This is where the dockyard-adjacent labor district begins — the steward's administrative territory, where the Rung-3 incident is set. Not exactly the block; the edge of it, where the market traffic transitions to labor-district traffic.

**Residential texture:** There is permanent housing in the block, built over and behind the ground-floor commercial spaces. Cramped, poorly ventilated, multi-occupancy. Mira occupies something in this layer. Taylor's own lodging is here, acquired through a transactional arrangement that was not warm and has remained not warm.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**Smell:** The foundational smell of the Dragon Gate block is gate-traffic: animal (horses, mules, the occasional cattle drive passing through), the dust and dried-dung smell of an eastern road brought through an arch, mixed with the market's cooking (grain-paste vendors, a fire-roast stall near the inner arch) and the craft smells of the alley shops (rope-fiber and lanolin from Mira's workshop, metal and oil from the ironmonger, the dye-smell of the cloth trader). Underneath: harbor salt, always, from the eastern wind. The guardhouse smell is distinct — see location card — and registers differently inside Taylor's range as an institutional-building note in the ambient.

**Sound:** The gate arch amplifies and compresses. Cart wheels through the arch are loud; the gate-keeper's call carries far into the market zone. The market's ambient sound is the continuous wash of commerce: vendor calls, animal noise, the specific percussion of a working transit market. The Watch patrol's foot-traffic rhythm is distinguishable from merchant-and-traveler foot-traffic for anyone who has been listening for three years. Mira's workshop is audible from the alley outside during working hours: the regular thud and drag of rope-braiding, the foot-pedal's rhythm.

**Light:** The block has the light quality of a walled city's gate district — mornings bright on the eastern approach, afternoons in the shadow of the city wall, the market zone's interior in partial shade through most of the day. The rope of lanterns the market traders string across the inner alley gives the evening commerce a specific amber quality. The guardhouse's patrol yard is the one permanently well-lit zone at night: the Watch keeps it lit.

**Temperature:** Eastern exposure means the block catches morning sun early and loses it to the wall's shadow by early afternoon. In summer the gate district runs hot until the afternoon shadow; in winter the wall's shadow is longer and the market alley runs cold from midday. The gate itself channels wind from the east whenever the wind is favorable — a regular feature that market traders manage with their stall arrangements.

**The insect register:** Dense, mixed, gate-traffic-driven. Flies follow the animal traffic through the arch and distribute through the market zone. Beetles colonize the wall-seam spaces along the old stone. Rats occupy the storage areas behind the permanent shops and in the residential layer above. The ambient insect-register of a busy gate district at 150m is approximately twenty thousand creatures in continuous contact — Taylor knows each of them at the individual level within her range. The register is never quiet. Deviations from baseline are immediately apparent.

- Ward sept (stone building, small, modest)
- Well at the north end of the inner alley (the primary water source for the residential layer above the stalls)

- The dockyard-road approach, south boundary

## Exits

- **Dragon Gate arch (east)** — the city's eastern exit to the Crownlands road. Primary transit exit. Watch-overseen at all hours.
- **Interior city streets (west)** — the block connects to the general King's Landing street network at the western boundary. These streets lead toward the center of the city. Not within Taylor's range from the block's center — she must position centrally to reach the western boundary.
- **North ward boundary** — the block transitions to the adjacent northern ward. The ward septon's authority boundary runs here; the Watch precinct boundary runs approximately here.
- **South to dockyard-road** — the southern boundary opens toward the labor district and the river-road approach. This is Rung-3 territory and the edge of the contact's operational geography.

Taylor's range cap means she cannot track anyone who passes through the gate arch and keeps moving, or who turns west at the interior city streets. The exits are where her awareness ends. The block is contained within her cap when she is centrally positioned.

## Hazards

- **The Watch omnipresence:** The Dragon Gate block is Watch-surveilled as a routine matter. The gate station is always staffed. Patrol patterns cover the market alley on predictable schedules. Taylor has the patrol patterns mapped to the minute after three months. Anyone who does not have this map is operating without it in a surveilled environment.
- **The gate-traffic instability:** Travelers carry news, faction temperature, and the ambient escalation of the Dance's approach into the block. As the political temperature rises through Act 2, the gate-traffic register changes — more faction-sigil carriages, more armed escort, more Watch-attention on non-standard movement patterns. The block's ambient political temperature bleeds in from outside via the gate.
- **The witch-label weather:** The septon's pastoral record and the Watch's management note exist simultaneously. A new Watch officer, a new septon, a new political context could change the management note without Taylor knowing. The label is institutionally stable but contingently stable.
- **The persons-risk sweep protocol:** Once activated, the sweep runs through the block at Watch-administrative speed. It does not stop for protests, personal relationships, or individual appeals. The sweep is the block's primary hazard in Act 3 — not an external threat but an internal institutional mechanism that Taylor's own intelligence-building made more capable.

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- Caravan arrival: a substantial caravan through the gate generates a temporary disruption of the ambient insect register (animals clearing insects from a path), a noise increase, and a Watch-alertness increase
- Market day versus off-day: the block's ambient traffic density varies; Taylor's insect register is thinner on off-days and thicker on market days
- Faction-sigil carriage passage: the Hand's carriage in Act 2 (the single canon glimpse) passes through the gate arch identifiable by sigil — the Watch clears the staging yard; the market alley goes quiet; the carriage does not stop; the block resumes
- Weather event: the eastern gate channels wind; a strong eastern wind in winter brings a specific cold that the market traders manage with visible adjustments (stall coverings, braziers); the insect ambient thins in cold weather
- Dockyard-labor trouble: when the dockyard-adjacent labor district generates disturbance (the Rung-3 context), the sound carries to the block's southern edge; Taylor registers it through insect-ambient before it registers audibly
- Night-watch transition: the shift change at the guardhouse ripples through the patrol yard; the patrol yard lanterns stay lit; the market alley lanterns come down as traders close; the block at night is the Watch's territory more than the merchants'
