# Flea Bottom Base — Taylor's Specific Lodging (Operational Sub-Anchor)

## Geography

Taylor's specific lodging within Flea Bottom. A single rented room on the second floor of a three-story building in the mid-district, situated approximately 250 meters from the eastern-quarter apothecary where the broken maester lives. This places both locations within Taylor's 300m operational radius from story open.

The building is between the market-side junction and the eastern quarter edge: a deliberate positioning that keeps the tanner-elder's labor web and the broken maester simultaneously within observation range. The positioning was not accidental; she mapped the district for several weeks, using insect reconnaissance, before committing to this location.

The distance calculation (critical to the project's mechanics): 250m to the apothecary's upper room at a direct line. The alleys between require more travel distance on foot but the insect network does not follow alleys — it saturates through walls. Taylor's operational awareness of the maester's upper room is continuous from story open at her 300m radius. As her range expands through s2 toward the Red Keep's outer ring, the maester remains within range until Taylor physically leaves the district.

## Layout

The room itself: approximately four meters by five. One window on the alley side. A sleeping pallet, a small table, a wooden stool, a corner shelf that came with the room and that she has filled with her working materials. The door locks from inside and outside; the key is her landlord's and hers.

The building structure: three stories, stone ground floor and timber above. Her room is second floor, which gives her an elevated line-of-sight down the alley and access to the roof via a stair at the end of the corridor. She has used the roof for observation. The roof access is known to the building's other residents and is not private; she uses it during hours when others do not.

Adjacent rooms: two other tenants on the second floor, separated by the thinnest of timber partitions. She knows their schedules from passive ambient insect sensing. They are not threats; they are noise variables.

The stairwell connects to the ground floor and to a small internal courtyard at the back of the building where the privy and water access are located. The courtyard has three entry points: the interior stairs, a gate to the side alley, and a low window from the ground floor storage space. She has mapped all three.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**The room:** The smell of the building — timber, old fire smoke, the ambient Flea Bottom density — overlaid in her room with the specific smell of the insects she runs. Not a strong smell; insects do not have a strong smell unless there are very many of them in a confined space. At sustained high-density operations in the room itself, there is a particular quality to the air that she has stopped noticing and that a visitor would notice immediately.

**Alley sound:** The room's window gives direct acoustic access to the alley below. She can tell time of day by the sound pattern: early morning water carriers, midday market-side activity, evening neighbor-conversations, late-night quiet with specific exceptions. The alley sound is mapped in her operational picture; deviation from baseline is a trigger.

**The maester's ambient:** Through the walls and at 250m range, the insect network in the corridor between Taylor's lodging and the eastern-quarter apothecary is maintained at low density for continuous connectivity. She knows, at any hour, whether the maester is awake or asleep, whether he has a visitor, whether he is working at his table. This is always-on ambient awareness; it does not require directed attention.

**Night texture:** The second-floor room is dark at night without a candle. She does not usually light a candle after the street noise drops, because the light through the window is visible from the alley. She works in the dark, which is not a limitation for someone who has insect-sense throughout the room.

## Fixed Props

None card-level. She keeps working materials on the shelf — notes written in a personal cipher she has not shared with anyone, materials relevant to the research log, and whatever she has acquired for operational purposes. These are runtime state.

## Exits

- **Door to corridor:** Primary exit to the building stairwell and the alley entrance.
- **Window to alley:** Observable but not a practical exit at normal conditions. An emergency exit option.
- **Roof access:** Via the corridor stair. Requires passing the other tenants' doors. She times this.
- **Back courtyard:** Via the ground floor, through the internal stairs. Gate to the side alley.

**Radius note:** From this room's location, Taylor's 300m sphere at story open covers: the full immediate block and surrounding alleys; the market-side junction (tanner-elder's position); the Fish Gate margin at the southern edge; and the eastern-quarter apothecary where the broken maester lives (approximately 250m, within the operational radius). The radius just reaches the apothecary's upper room; the maester's street-level entrance is closer. As the radius expands through s2, coverage extends north toward the Street of Steel and eventually, at near-ceiling range (~1.5km), toward the Red Keep's outer ring.

## Hazards

- **Thin walls and informed neighbors:** The neighbors' schedules are known to Taylor, which means her own schedule is potentially observable by them. She manages her unusual activities — insects in volume, night-work that produces no normal sound cues — within the patterns her neighbors expect.
- **Landlord access:** The landlord retains a key. She is aware of this. She does not keep anything in the room that would be intelligible as dangerous to someone who found it — the research notes are in cipher, the materials are ambiguous, the insects are ambient.
- **The density cost:** When she runs sustained high-density operations from this room in s2 in preparation for the Khepri-mantle threshold, the room's air quality changes in ways that are noticeable if someone opens the door unexpectedly. She manages timing.
- **Observation from the alley:** The room is second floor with an alley-side window. A persistent watcher in the alley below would have a partial view of her movements. She has mapped who uses the alley and at what hours.

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- A neighbor's schedule deviation — coming home at an unexpected hour when Taylor is running a high-density operation
- The landlord knocking for rent when the room's ambient insect density is elevated
- A watcher in the alley below who does not match any known alley-user profile (s2 Hightower intelligence agent)
- Sound from the maester's upper room that deviates from his working-pattern baseline — a visitor, a crash, extended silence where there should be movement
- The alley below going suddenly quiet during a normally active hour (Watch movement, factional incident elsewhere in the district)
- The corridor neighbor asking, casually, what the smell is — not investigating, just asking; the wrong answer is available in several forms
