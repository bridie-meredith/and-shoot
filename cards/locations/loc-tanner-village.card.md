# Tanner Village — Crownlands (Story-Open Location; Ongoing Cost Surface)

## Geography

A village in the Crownlands, on a secondary road between King's Landing and the agricultural interior. A day's walk from King's Landing — approximately fifteen to eighteen miles along the road, closer to twelve as the crow flies. The terrain is gently rolling Crownlands farmland: not the dramatic landscape of the Riverlands, not the coastal exposure of the Reach, but the specific moderate agricultural geography of land that has been under Targaryen rule long enough to be organized and not long enough to be comfortable.

The village sits at the junction of the secondary road and a smaller track leading to a nearby stream. The stream is used for the tanning process. The smell reaches the road before the village does.

Population: approximately eighty to one hundred, including the tanning families, a small farming contingent, a farrier, and the usual smallfolk service trades. Not large enough to have a septon's presence on site — the sept is a half-day walk toward a larger market town. Not small enough to be irrelevant: it produces leather goods that reach King's Landing markets.

## Layout

The village is organized around the road junction and the tanning yard, which sits slightly downwind of the main settlement. The tanning yard is the economic center: several large pits, a rendering area, drying racks, and a workshop where finished leather is cut and traded.

The residential structures are built on the road side of the yard, arranged loosely around a common area that functions as market space on trade days. The tanner family's dwelling is the largest, reflecting established position: two main rooms, a connected workshop space, a small yard where hides are sorted before processing.

Peripheral structures: a barn at the southern edge, a farrier's workshop at the road junction, several smaller one-room dwellings for the laborers.

The stream runs about fifty meters from the yard's edge. The path to the stream is well-worn and passes through the back of the tanning area.

## Sensory Vocabulary

**Smell:** The tanning process is the dominant sensory fact of this village. Lime-pits and bark-tanning produce a specific compound of chemical sharpness and organic rot. It is not overwhelming at the road if the wind is correct; at the yard itself, it is simply the air. The tanner family has stopped noticing it. Visitors have not.

**Sound:** Moderate livestock ambient — a few goats, chickens, the farrier's work. The specific wet sounds of the tanning pits when active. Wind through the drying racks. Dogs somewhere off to the south.

**Visual:** The drying racks are visible from the road — tall wooden frames with stretched hides at various stages of processing. The color of the village is the color of Crownlands working places: brown, grey, the occasional white of fresh lime on the pits. Mud at certain seasons. Dry dust at others.

**Texture:** The hides at various stages of processing: raw, treated, partially dried, finished. The ground near the pits is permanently saturated. The road itself is maintained enough to be passable. The village feels used, functional, without aspiration.

## Exits

- **Road north** — toward King's Landing, approximately fifteen to eighteen miles. Full day's walk. The road is maintained at the Crownlands standard: usable in good weather, difficult in heavy rain.
- **Road south** — toward the agricultural interior and further market towns.
- **Stream path** — eastward, fifty meters, to the stream used for tanning process. Not a travel route.
- **Secondary track** — westward, toward additional farmland. Dead end for non-farmers.

## Hazards

- **Tanning chemistry:** Lime and tanning agents are caustic. The pits are a physical hazard for anyone unfamiliar with the yard's layout. Taylor's insects around the pits operate with adjusted behavioral expectations — the chemical environment suppresses certain species and concentrates others.
- **Lord's attention:** The village pays rents and occasional labor services to a minor Crownlands lord. The lord's man comes through quarterly. Any unusual event in the village — including the return of a dead girl under strange circumstances — will reach the lord's record eventually. The lord's man is not present continuously, but his awareness of the village is persistent.
- **The smell as social exposure:** Visitors notice. Taylor, arriving in Flea Bottom, carries a faint residual of the tanning process that marks her origin to people who know the smell. This is a minor but real operational tell.
- **The ongoing cost-surface:** The tanner family's relationship to the girl is a live social fact in the village. The village's handling of it — Tya-who-came-back-wrong as managed category — is stable at story open but vulnerable to events that shift the category from strange toward dangerous.
