# Red Keep — Outer Ring (Range-Reachable Late s2 / Early s3)

## Geography

The outermost accessible layer of the Red Keep's physical structure — the outer walls, the gatehouse approaches, the baileys immediately inside the outer gates, and the servant and garrison quarters that ring the inner castle structures. Not the throne room, not the council chambers, not the dragon pits below — the exterior skin of the castle and the zones immediately inside it.

Taylor's 300m range does not reach the Red Keep from Flea Bottom at story open; the distance from loc-flea-bottom-base to the Red Keep's outer wall is approximately 800–1000m. As her range expands organically through s2 toward the ~1.5km ceiling, the outer ring comes within operational reach without physical proximity — she does not have to be near the Red Keep; she has to be somewhere within 1.5km of it with her range ceiling extended.

The outer ring is therefore available as a surveillance location beginning late s2 / early s3, accessed without Taylor moving from her established Flea Bottom anchor. Range expansion is the mechanism; geographic proximity is not required.

## Layout

The outer ring includes:
- The main gatehouse approach (guarded; heavy foot traffic during daylight)
- The outer bailey (stables, garrison quarters, servant access)
- The outer wall circuit (walkway; guard posts)

The inner castle begins beyond this ring. Taylor's range at ceiling does not penetrate meaningfully into the inner castle structures; she has the outer ring and the approaches, not the throne room's ambient or the private quarters.

## Sensory Vocabulary

The outer ring carries the specific ecology of a military-administrative structure that processes a large population continuously: horses, stable fauna, the insect ecology of the granaries and kitchens at the outer ring level, the rat population of the castle foundations (large, established). For Taylor's insect network: rich substrate at the outer ring's level. The inner castle is thinner — cleaner, more maintained, less rat-and-beetle dense.

## Fixed Props

None card-level.

## Exits

Not applicable — this is a surveillance reference, not a traversal location.

## Hazards

- **Range ceiling constraint:** Taylor's operational ceiling is ~1.5km. The outer ring is reachable at near-ceiling range. She cannot sustain maximum operations at this distance for extended periods; the cost curve applies.
- **The Red Keep's ecology:** The castle's insect population is managed — fumigation, pest control — more aggressively than Flea Bottom's. Her network has a thinner substrate to work with at maximum range. Passive ambient awareness is possible; directed precision at 1.5km is degraded.
- **Targaryen magical residue:** The Red Keep was built with Valyrian involvement. Taylor's insect network at maximum range within the outer ring may register anomalous data — behaviors she cannot attribute to normal ecology. This is observable but not interpretable at story open.

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- Court-level political information reaching Taylor through the outer ring's servant population (conversation overheard by her range-edge network)
- A Hightower intelligence agent recognizable by movement pattern from prior Flea Bottom contact appearing in the outer ring's approach — confirmation of the profiling operation
- Factional movement visible in the outer ring's garrison activity — the Dance beginning to register in the physical organization of the castle's military resources
