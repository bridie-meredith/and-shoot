# Stormlands Coastal Cliffs

## Geography

The eastern coast of the Stormlands where the land breaks hard against the Narrow Sea. High limestone cliffs, scored by centuries of storms, riddled with caves and ledges wide enough to nest on. The cliff face runs for miles. The top is heath and gorse, mostly unfarmed. Below: a narrow shingle beach only accessible at low tide, when the waves withdraw far enough. The nearest settlement of any size is half a day's ride inland.

## Layout

Three tiers: the clifftop heath (accessible by foot from inland), the cliff face itself (ledges, caves, vertical faces — accessible by climbing or flight), and the beach at the base (accessible at low tide only, dangerous at high). The cave system in the lower cliff face runs deep; most of the caves are too narrow for anything large, but one chamber two-thirds of the way down opens wide enough to shelter something horse-sized.

## Sensory Vocabulary

Salt and cold iron smell — the sea here doesn't smell clean, it smells mineral, like something that hasn't stopped working. Wind that finds gaps in cloaks regardless of how they're fastened. The sound of storm birds (grey and white, large, perpetually angry). Spray on the face even on calm days. The cliffs above are bone-colored in daylight; at dusk they go grey-brown and the shadows in the cave mouths look occupied. At night: the sound of the sea below is constant and enormous.

## Fixed Props

None catalogued.

## Exits

- Clifftop path eastward → Stormlands coastal road (half-day walk)
- Clifftop path westward → wilder heath, no road for miles
- Cliff face → beach at base (only at low tide; dangerous approach)
- From the large cave: flight exits directly over the Narrow Sea

## Hazards

- High winds; updrafts that can assist or impede flight unpredictably
- The cliff face is unstable in places — sections calve into the sea during storms
- Storm birds mob large intruders with persistence and coordination
- The beach at the base is cut off at high tide; anything caught there is trapped against the cliff for four to six hours

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- Hunting parties from inland lords, appearing on the clifftop path with bows and torches
- Ships in the Narrow Sea, visible from the clifftop
- Storm season: high storms that make the cliff face uninhabitable for days
- A Targaryen dragon rider in the distance, scouting the coast from altitude
