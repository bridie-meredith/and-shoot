# King's Landing — Exterior and Approaches

## Geography

The city sits at the confluence of the Blackwater Rush and the Narrow Sea, on a hill dominated by the Red Keep and the Dragonpit. From the road approaching from the west (the Reach road), the city is visible for an hour's ride before arrival: walls, the towers of the Red Keep, and smoke from a hundred thousand hearths. This card covers the city gates, the approach roads, and the outer city streets — not the Red Keep interior (separate card required) and not the Dragonpit (separate card required).

## Layout

Three hills inside the walls. The city is walled but the walls are not maintained as a fortress — this is a living city, and the gates are open during daylight. The outer city districts near the gates are the poorest; the city's wealth concentrates uphill. The Gate of the Gods (northern road), the Dragon Gate (eastern road toward the Narrow Sea), and the King's Gate (western Reach road) are the primary entry points. Inside the walls, the streets are narrow, irregular, and smell of everything.

## Sensory Vocabulary

Scale shock on first arrival: the city is large by any standard that Westerosi travelers bring to it, and the smell arrives before the walls — sewage, cooking, animals, smoke, the Blackwater itself. Noise is constant. The Red Keep is visible from most of the city, and the presence of dragons there in this era means occasional passes overhead — the shadow of a dragon flying from the Dragonpit produces a specific sound from the crowd below, a held-breath then a noise that isn't quite a cheer. Underfoot: cobblestones near the city center, dirt and refuse near the gate districts.

## Hazards

- The gate districts: poverty, desperation, theft, violence that doesn't get investigated
- City watch (gold cloaks): present, corrupt at the street level, occasionally competent when it matters to someone important
- Informers: the city is dense with people who sell information
- Dragon passes overhead: crowds freeze or flee; someone with unusual powers could be seen in the wrong moment

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- A lord's procession requiring the street to clear
- A city watch patrol moving through the gate district with purpose
- A rumor from the Dragonpit: a dragon behaved strangely; no one knows why
- A smallfolk crowd gathered around something — execution, market dispute, herald's announcement
