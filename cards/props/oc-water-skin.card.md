# The Water-Skin — Halvard's Sick-Tending Water-Carrier, Hook Ward

## Physical Description

A standard westerosi water-skin: a cured-leather bag with a tied neck-opening and a carry-strap, capacity sufficient for several cups. Halvard's skin shows regular use — the leather is softened and darkened from handling, the strap worn where it crosses the shoulder. It is a working implement, not a decorative one.

## Affordances / Uses

- **Water-carrying for sick-tending circuit.** Halvard fills the skin at the Hook lower water-trough on his circuit rounds for delivering water to ward residents who are sick and cannot fetch their own. This is the skin's sole function and the reason Halvard is at the trough.
- **Scene-close anchor (b01c13 s04 @31).** Halvard fills the skin at the trough after Taylor departs. The filling is the final beat of the chapter — Halvard still present, still in his circuit, still the unanswered counter-argument, the water-skin being filled while Taylor walks. The skin's filling is the physical continuation of Halvard's witness function after the foreclosure.

## Sensory Hooks

- Leather smell of the water-skin, warmed from carry
- The sound of water entering the skin at the trough — gurgle and fill, then the neck-tie pulled shut

## Portability

Portable. Halvard carries it on his person during the sick-tending circuit.

## Carry State

Carried on Halvard's shoulder or in hand during the circuit. At b01c13 @31: being filled at the trough — transitioning from empty to full.

- `fill_state`: `empty` at b01c13 @24 (Halvard finds the trough to fill it); `filling` at @31 (filling after Taylor departs)
