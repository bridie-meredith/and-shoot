# Prop — Dose Log

The mithridatism record. Simultaneously the most scientifically sound document Saerys has ever produced and the most cosmologically incorrect.

---

## Physical Description

A narrow ledger, smaller than the account-book — close in format to a maester's apothecary record but purchased blank and filled in by her hand. Soft calfskin cover, no title on the spine. The entries run in columns: date (GRRM time-keeping, moonsturns and name-day counts from a personal epoch she established at age six), substance name (her tier classification, not the maester's pharmacological name), dose (in units of her own devising — "grain-weights of the first measure," "three drops of the second preparation"), observed response ("the cauldron registered a hazard-state at double dose; the tribulation processed without cultivation deviation"), and conclusion ("tolerance threshold advanced; impurity expulsion confirmed; the tier holds").

The handwriting shrinks as the years pass — she found her adult hand by eight; by twelve the entries are dense, small, and uniform. The earliest entries have occasional blotted corrections. The middle-book entries are clean. The final pages are the most precise.

She writes in the log in a cipher that is not a true cipher: she uses cultivation-register vocabulary that is meaningless to anyone who does not know the framework, which is everyone. The septa has read it and concluded it is a religious devotional in an unfamiliar tradition. She is not entirely wrong.

By Book III the log has roughly 200 documented exposure events across approximately 40 substances. The margin of some late entries is annotated: a small cross-reference in a different ink pointing back to an earlier entry — "cf. entry 47: the tolerance established then carried here."

---

## Affordances / Uses

**As a pharmacological record:** It is one. The methodology is mithridatism, correctly executed — progressive low-dose exposure with documented response tracking and controlled escalation. A Citadel-trained master of poisons who read it without the cultivation gloss would recognize the methodology immediately, be alarmed, and then be impressed. The dose escalation is cautious by historical standards. The response notes are accurate. The tolerance conclusions are correct. Twelve years of disciplined mithridatism, documented.

**As the Inferior Path's technique manual:** In her framing, this is her primary cultivation text — the only technique manual the Inferior Path possesses, authored in real-time by the practitioner it documents. Each entry is a realm-advancement record. The log is to the Inferior Path what a sect's founding scripture is to an orthodox sect. She knows it is also a poison-tolerance record. She does not separate these interpretations. They are the same document, read in two registers simultaneously.

**As a double-blind accountability mechanism:** She notes every entry. Failed experiments, near-misses (the wildfire-shard incident is entry 31 — one line, no drama), recoveries. The log is not self-congratulatory. It is a scientist's record. When an entry shows she went too far, the next entry records the adjusted protocol. The log documents her methodology across the boundary between what the register calls "advancement" and what a pharmacologist would call "severe adverse event."

**As a Bk III payload trigger:** The dose-log becomes load-bearing evidence in Book III Movement 1, when a specific assassination attempt deploys a poison she has documented tolerance to. The assassin used what should have been lethal. Entry 44 in the dose-log shows she had already established tolerance at triple the dose. The log is the broken clock's clearest legitimate ring: she was right for the wrong reasons. The cauldron was genuinely tempered. The clock kept time.

## Sensory Hooks

- Smells of nothing; she has been careful not to work near the log when compounds are active
- The calfskin cover has a slight waxy texture; she applied a preservative at Year 3, again at Year 7
- The binding is sturdy but has swollen slightly from humidity — she has moved it through four locations, including a sea voyage
- Weight: light, deceptive — it looks like a devotional; it reads like a weapon
- The earliest pages have a faint discoloration at the edge from a liquid spill she notes in entry 12 ("the cauldron sustained a minor administrative tribulation; the record survives")

## Portability

Carried on her person when she is in the still-room or working. Stored in a specific concealed location otherwise — not with the account-book (deliberately separated; one document is her identity, the other is her methodology; losing both to a single search would be catastrophic). Travels with her on the Essosi voyage, wrapped in oilskin inside the Yi Tish poison-master's case. By Book III it lives in her cabin on the ship, under a floorboard she has not technically modified.

---

## Carry State

Thin enough to be carried in a doubled sleeve without obvious bulk. She does not carry it at court — the risk of search outweighs the access benefit; she has the relevant entries memorized. In the still-room it is on the table. At sea it is within arm's reach at all times.

---

## Functional State

The log is a growing document. It gains entries. It does not lose them. There is no correction mechanism beyond a note — errors are documented as errors, not erased. The log's completeness is its primary value: a broken chain of entries would invalidate the tolerance-threshold conclusions. She has never missed a documented exposure event.

**Book I state:** approximately entries 1-60; the early training reagents; the wildfire incident (entry 31); Foundation Establishment misread documented on the following page as a major advancement note in red ink, the only color used in the log.

**Book II state:** entries 61-140; the Essosi substances appear; basilisk venom (entry 89, a cautious preliminary); Tears of Lys full-concentration series (entries 100-115); the first manticore-venom preliminary (entry 133, aborted at early-onset symptoms with a note reading "the cauldron was not yet prepared; this is documented correctly; we shall return").

**Book III state:** entries 141-present; the log enters its darkest register here. Entry 157 is a single line. Entry 163 is blank — the quill stopped mid-column. Entry 164 resumes as though 163 did not happen.
