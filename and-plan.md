# and-shoot

## purpose
author creative fiction for entertainment, human-orchestrated, all else ai

## general info
modular memory system houses information per schema called **cards** (import from brighid-creative-writing)
margit's workshop houses assets for crafting, improving, and pruning cards
active-project houses all relevant directories for the sole active project
projects houses all projects in sub-directories; receives active-project contents as subdirectory at conclusion
**series** general story in the broadest course from beginning of story to conclusion
**season** significant arc within a series (of seasons)
**episode** single chapter within a season (of episodes)

## constraints
### **law** of world, universal constraints if non-standard
### **lore**, background and history of world
### **behavior** of persons (in general and in specific for possible cast)

## plot
### (why) **reason** of story
### (what) **elements** of story
### (how) **shape** of story

## authoring
### screen-writer (TBD agent), personas optimized to craft disparate story elements
### audience (critic), personas optimized to review and describe entertainment felt
### dramatist (critic), personas optimized to apply form **categorical** to arc


## active-project prep
planning and prework to enable story shoot in roughly this order

1. initiate context handler: 
2. identify constraints, request cards, build context (focused on notable laws, lore, behaviors), **vibe-cloud**
3. generalize plot components and structure
4. author loose plan for narrative at **series** level
5. detail constraints of first season **vibe-cloud**
6. describe plot components and structure of first season
7. author plan of first season
8. audit constraints (keep notes)
    * world's lore is accurate
    * universe laws are obeyed
    * characters have motivations
9. audit plot (keep notes)
    * is it plausible
    * is it entertaining
    * is it dramatic

### in case of rejectable audit findings
if rejected for reason (constraints disobeyed, bored audience, ...) decide if problem is constraints, elements, or story. if story, provide feedback to screen-writer for correction at **series** or **season** level. if elements or constraints, revise elements or constraints as necessary, then provide updates to screen-writer for correction at **series** or **season** level.
