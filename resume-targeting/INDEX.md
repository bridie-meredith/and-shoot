# resume-targeting

Hiring-manager / screening-recruiter personas for targeting Catherine's resume at the eight opportunity categories in `Catherine_Opportunity_Report.md`.

Each persona card models one gatekeeper skimming a high-volume pile for a single coveted slot. Each card carries a `## Reading Catherine` section — the actionable payload: how that screener would react to Catherine's actual profile, the screen-out risks, and what her resume / one-pager / pitch must surface to survive the skim.

Each persona was authored by an isolated fork (no cross-persona visibility) to keep their reads independent.

## Card structure

Each persona card uses the `class: persona` schema (`schemas/card.schema.md`), adapted for this project's screening purpose:

- **Description** — one-line read of the screener.
- **The Position** — what they fill/buy, comp band, how the screen works, what a "yes" means.
- **Voice** — how they sound while screening.
- **Taste** — what makes an artifact jump to the YES / shortlist pile.
- **Pet Peeves** — graded blocker / strong / soft.
- **The Skim** — first-8-seconds mechanics, scan order, instant rejects.
- **Reading Catherine** — the actionable payload: strengths seen, screen-out risks, what the artifact must surface.

Roles 6 and 8 have no literal hiring manager; those personas screen the analog artifact (consultant one-pagers / proposals, expert pitches / book proposals).
