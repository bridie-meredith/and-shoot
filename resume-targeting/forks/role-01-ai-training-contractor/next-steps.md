# Next Steps — Role 1: AI Training Contractor

Concrete, sequenced actions for landing AI-training-contractor work
(Mercor / Scale Outlier / Surge / Invisible). All steps are achievable within
1-3 months while Catherine keeps her early-childhood teaching job — the work
itself is async, evenings/weekends. Each step is tied to the persona question
or screen-out risk it neutralizes.

The current resume is already submission-ready (both screeners advance it).
The steps below are about *converting an advance into placement and
band-promotion* — clearing Priya's funnel, then clearing Ravi's sample-task
gate at the top of the band.

---

## Weeks 1-2 — Foundation: apply, and close the LLM-fluency gap

### Step 1 — Apply to the marketplaces and complete the AI interviews
Apply to Mercor, Scale Outlier, Surge, and Invisible as a SpEd / education
expert. Complete each platform's automated profile parse, "AI interview," and
domain skills assessment. Apply to all four — placement is project-by-project,
not platform-by-platform, so multiple pipelines maximize match odds.
- **Neutralizes:** Priya Q1-Q3 (gets the profile into the funnel so the
  tagline can do its work); the only way any later step matters.
- **Realistic:** ~3-5 hours total; entirely evenings/weekends.

### Step 2 — Build genuine power-user LLM fluency (5-8 hrs/week)
Use Claude, ChatGPT, NotebookLM, and one AI-for-teachers tool (MagicSchool,
Brisk, or SchoolAI) intentionally and daily for two weeks. Focus on the
workflows the resume already claims: drafting, summarizing source documents,
and stress-testing rubrics. Keep a short log of three concrete
rubric-stress-testing sessions — e.g., feeding a compliance rubric edge cases
to find where the criteria are ambiguous.
- **Neutralizes:** Ravi's "stress-test rubrics" watch-item — the AI interview
  may ask her to substantiate it; the log makes it lived practice, not resume
  garnish. Also closes the opportunity report's weak-spot #1 (LLM fluency).
- **Realistic:** A defined 5-8 hrs/week, sustainable alongside teaching.

### Step 3 — Rewrite the LinkedIn headline to match the resume tagline
Replace "Education and Administration Leadership Expert" with specialty-first
language: *"EdD — special education specialist: complex/PDA autism, IEP &
IDEA compliance auditing, behavior support."* The automated profile parser
reads LinkedIn; the headline must hit the scarce keywords as fast as the
resume tagline does.
- **Neutralizes:** Priya's instant down-tier trigger (district/leadership
  positioning; buried specialty). The parser auto-tiers off LinkedIn — a
  mismatched headline can sink her before the resume is ever read.
- **Realistic:** A 20-minute edit.

## Weeks 3-6 — First sample tasks: clear Ravi's gate at the top of the band

### Step 4 — Pre-study the spec-over-standard discipline before the first sample task
Before submitting any paid sample task, internalize the single behavior Ravi
offboards good experts for missing: when a personal/IDEA-based judgment
diverges from the project's gold answer, **flag the divergence explicitly in
one line** ("scored per the rubric; note field best practice would weigh this
differently") rather than scoring silently to her own standard. Draft a
personal one-page checklist: (a) score to the project rubric, not IDEA;
(b) flag every divergence; (c) match the spec's format and length exactly;
(d) commit a score plus one tight line from the rubric, never an essay.
- **Neutralizes:** Ravi Q2-Q3, Q9-Q10 — silent divergence, best-practice
  import, essay-instead-of-a-score, credential-as-argument. These are the
  instant-offboard triggers; the checklist is the only thing standing between
  her and the top of the band.
- **Realistic:** A few hours of preparation; no new credential needed.

### Step 5 — Practice scenario-as-probe construction
Before the first sample task, draft 4-5 practice scenarios from her
autism/PDA and compliance expertise where the *wrong* answer is tempting and
fluent — e.g., a model response that confidently offers an accommodation
where the scenario requires a modification, or a fluent answer subtly harmful
for a PDA learner. The deliberate skill: build the trap, not the quiz.
- **Neutralizes:** Ravi Q4, Q6 — "scenario with no failure mode" (strong
  reject) and the teaching-item-vs-probe risk. This is the promote-tier skill;
  it moves her from band floor to the $90-110/hr work.
- **Realistic:** Uses only existing expertise; ~3-4 hours of practice.

### Step 6 — Complete the first paid sample task(s) and aim for $4-8k/month
Once matched, execute the sample task using the Step 4 checklist and Step 5
practice. Target $4-8k/month of contracting income within six weeks. Treat the
first submission as the highest-leverage artifact — Ravi's keep/promote/
offboard call is fast and predicts steady-state.
- **Neutralizes:** Ravi Q1, Q5, Q7-Q8 — proves calibration, observable rubric
  authoring, the adversarial catch, and spec-format discipline on the actual
  artifact. This is the only gate the resume cannot pass for her.
- **Realistic:** Async, project-based, 15-20 hrs/week as the resume states.

## Months 2-3 — Build the portfolio narrative and durability

### Step 7 — Publish one public AI artifact
Write and publish one LinkedIn article or Substack post: *"Five Compliance
Questions Every District Should Ask Before Buying AI Tools"* (drawn directly
from the opportunity report's suggested pieces). This is a low-lift,
high-credibility signal that she engages with AI seriously — and it doubles as
proof for the AI interview and any re-match conversation.
- **Neutralizes:** Priya's "fake AI expert" worry — a substantive piece reads
  as genuine engagement, not garnish; closes weak-spot #2 (public AI
  artifact). Reinforces, doesn't replace, the contractor framing.
- **Realistic:** One article in months 2-3; modest time cost.

### Step 8 — Tighten the resume's OSPI entry (the last page-level residue)
Apply the one remaining critic-flagged edit: lead the OSPI experience entry
with the "defined format and reporting constraints" framing and demote the
"qualitative inquiry / synthesizes findings into program-design
recommendations" language. Add a half-line under EDUCATION & RECOGNITION
tying the EdD/dissertation to structured-criteria or measurement work, if
truthfully supportable.
- **Neutralizes:** Ravi's faint "essay-instead-of-a-score" residue (the OSPI
  entry's qualitative vocabulary); converts Priya's inert-prestige EdD into a
  band argument.
- **Realistic:** A five-minute edit (reflected in the projected resume).

### Step 9 — Secure a re-match by performing, and add a second pipeline
After the first project, use strong sample-task performance to request
re-matching ("we'll re-match you if a relevant spec opens"). Explicitly flag
availability for *behavioral/clinical* projects, not only education — the
mental-health case-management background opens a second spec bucket.
- **Neutralizes:** Priya Q2 (two buckets from one yes — her favorite outcome)
  and the gig-economy "no long-term arc" con; turns one placement into a
  durable, multi-pipeline contracting income.
- **Realistic:** Follows naturally from Steps 4-6; no new credential.
