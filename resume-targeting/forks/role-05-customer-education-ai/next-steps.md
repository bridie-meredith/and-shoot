# Next Steps — Role 5: Customer Education at an AI Company

Concrete, sequenced moves for Catherine to take over the next 1-3 months while keeping her teaching job. Every step is drawn from the opportunity report's Section 4 (Weak spots) and Section 6 (Recommended next steps), and is tied to a specific persona question or screen-out risk it neutralizes. The current resume already *survives* both screens; these steps convert "survives" into "wins" — closing Sofia's missing-artifact gap and Marcus's missing-metrics gap, and deepening the AI-fluency evidence both demand.

The two highest-leverage moves (per the round-2 critic synthesis) are: **(1) publish one clickable AI artifact** and **(2) inject current outcome digits into the consulting blocks**. Everything else supports those.

---

## Weeks 1-2 — Foundation: prove the AI is real and gather the numbers

### Step 1. Deepen and document AI power-user fluency (5-8 hrs/week)
- **Do:** Use Claude, ChatGPT, and one AI-for-teachers tool (MagicSchool, Brisk, or SchoolAI) daily in real consulting and PD-design work. Keep a short running log of concrete use cases — drafting compliance guidance, summarizing IEP source documents, stress-testing rubrics, building PD materials.
- **Neutralizes:** Sofia's blocker ("has she touched the technology with her hands?") and Marcus's "can she demo without freezing?" The log also pre-empts Sofia's interview question "walk me through a session you ran" — the resume bullet must have a real session behind it.
- **Report basis:** Section 4, weak spot #1 (LLM/prompting fluency); Section 6, Weeks 1-2.

### Step 2. Pull every honest number out of the consulting and OSPI history
- **Do:** Sit down with engagement records and reconstruct: exact count of OSPI contract cycles renewed; number of repeat district/school clients out of total engagements; educators reached and sites served across AI-literacy and compliance PD; multi-year client count. Write each as an integer.
- **Neutralizes:** Marcus's #3 ("where are the digits?"), #5 (renewals as a pattern), and #10 ("does 'proven' earn its place?"). This is the raw material for the resume's most-needed fix.
- **Report basis:** Critic feedback round 2, Marcus fixes #1-#3.

### Step 3. Rewrite the LinkedIn headline and profile to bridge AI + education
- **Do:** Replace any generic headline with a dual-fluency line, e.g. *"EdD specializing in inclusive leadership, complex autism, and AI integration for K-12 special education — helping districts and AI products deploy responsibly."* Mirror the resume tagline. Ensure the profile names AI tools and the compliance angle in the first lines.
- **Neutralizes:** Sofia's #1 and #6 (bridging positioning) and #3 (she clicks through to LinkedIn — it must not read education-only).
- **Report basis:** Section 4, weak spot #3 (positioning language); Section 6, Weeks 1-2.

---

## Weeks 3-6 — First signals: publish the artifact, name the scale

### Step 4. Write and publish ONE clickable AI artifact, and link it on the resume
- **Do:** Publish a short LinkedIn article or a 3-post Substack series. Best title, directly in her wheelhouse and requiring no new skill: **"Five Compliance Questions Every District Must Ask Before Buying AI Tools."** Optionally add a second: "What AI Education Products Get Wrong About PDA Autism." Keep it modest and practical, not deep.
- **Neutralizes:** Sofia's #3 — her single highest-value remaining gap. "Sofia will click it; right now there is nothing to click." This converts her from MAYBE-to-YES to a calendar invite. It also doubles as a public proof of dual-fluency for Marcus.
- **Report basis:** Section 4, weak spot #2 (public-facing AI artifact); Section 6, Weeks 3-6; critic synthesis top edit #1.

### Step 5. Quantify the AI-literacy PD and compliance training bullets
- **Do:** Tag the AI-literacy PD with a real headcount and site count — "delivered AI-literacy PD to N educators across M sites." Do the same for OSPI training and compliance work, using the integers gathered in Step 2.
- **Neutralizes:** Marcus's #3 and #7 (scaled, countable program design) AND Sofia's #10 — a headcount proves the PD is a real delivered session, not a résumé line, pre-empting her interview question. Serves both screeners with one edit.
- **Report basis:** Critic feedback round 2, Sofia fix #2, Marcus fix #1, synthesis edit #3.

### Step 6. Submit an AI-themed conference talk proposal
- **Do:** Submit a session proposal on AI + special education compliance (or AI policy for districts) to AERA 2027 and/or ISTE Live 2026. Even a submitted/accepted proposal can be cited.
- **Neutralizes:** Sofia's remaining soft gap — her speaking record is currently all education-side; an AI-tagged session upgrades the speaking card from education-only to dual-fluency. Reinforces Marcus's "can carry a room in front of buyers."
- **Report basis:** Section 6, Weeks 3-6 (AI-themed talk proposal); critic feedback round 2, Sofia fix #3.

### Step 7. Soft-launch district outreach to build current client signal
- **Do:** Email 15-20 superintendents and SpEd directors in WA, offering a free 30-minute consult on their AI policy. Aim to convert 1-2 into a near-term scoped engagement.
- **Neutralizes:** Marcus's #4 and #5 (a current, repeat-able client relationship) and gives a *recent* engagement to anchor the consulting block — countering his note that the only hard number is from 2010-2012.
- **Report basis:** Section 6, Weeks 3-6 (soft-launch consulting offer).

## Months 2-3 — Convert and apply

### Step 8. Land one current paid AI-policy or compliance engagement
- **Do:** Close one new district AI-policy or inclusive-AI-implementation review engagement (workshop or short project) from the Step 7 outreach. This produces a current, AI-tagged, district-facing line item.
- **Neutralizes:** Marcus's #2, #3, and #9 — a recent engagement that is explicitly about AI deployment counters the "reads as a teacher" risk and gives a current outcome to point to. Reinforces Sofia's "has she deployed an AI tool / led an AI-policy engagement."
- **Report basis:** Section 6, Months 2-3 (land first paid consulting engagement); Section 2, Role 6 product list (district AI policy authoring, inclusive AI implementation reviews).

### Step 9. Build a one-page consulting / professional site and link it
- **Do:** Stand up a simple template site (or a clean LinkedIn "Featured" section) that hosts the AI artifact, the positioning line, the speaking record, and the AI-policy service. Link it in the resume contact header.
- **Neutralizes:** Sofia's #3 (gives her a richer clickable destination) and Marcus's #6/#10 (a public professional presence reads as an operator with a P&L flavor, not a teacher).
- **Report basis:** Section 6, Months 2-3 (build a simple website / one-page consulting site).

### Step 10. Apply to 3-5 targeted customer-education roles at AI companies
- **Do:** With the artifact published and metrics in place, apply to "Customer Education, Education segment" / "Field Engagement, Education" / "Partner Education, K-12" roles at AI companies and SpEd-focused edtechs. Use the projected resume.
- **Neutralizes:** N/A — this is the payoff step; it is only worth doing once Steps 1-9 have closed the screen-out risks.
- **Report basis:** Section 6, Months 2-3 (apply to 3-5 carefully selected full-time roles, customer education at AI labs).
