# Role 7 — Ed+AI Startup — Persona Analysis

Catherine is being evaluated as **education lead / domain-expert co-founder** at a seed-stage AI-IEP company. Two gatekeepers screen her: Devin Park (founder hiring employee #8) and Arjun Mehta (technical co-founder courting the domain half). They share a thesis — *compliance is the moat in AI-IEP* — but their decisions differ in magnitude: Devin is making a hire he can de-risk with a paid trial; Arjun is betting 40-50% of his cap table on a seven-year partnership.

---

## Persona 1 — Devin Park (Founder & CEO, ed+AI startup)

### What Devin is looking for — the bar for a YES

Devin reads the resume in two minutes; its only job is to **earn the 30-minute call** plus a paid trial (audit a sample AI-drafted IEP, find the compliance holes, write the spec). A YES is a candidate who:

- **Makes the product more trusted on day one.** Someone he can put in a room with a skeptical SpEd director and not need to be in the room himself.
- **Carries a scarce, unfakeable wedge** — SpEd compliance auditing, IDEA fluency as a working practice, complex/PDA autism. This is "the missing organ of his company," not a nice background.
- **Reads as a builder, not an operator** — created things from zero (a consulting practice, a program, a won contract), not maintained institutional roles.
- **Moves at startup velocity** without a permission structure above her, and **treats ambiguity as the job**, not a defect in it.
- **Took the equity bet with clear eyes** — wants four years on the problem regardless of whether the equity pays out.

### Instant-reject triggers

- A purely institutional employee history with **no self-started anything**.
- Needing the role **pre-defined** — a clear mandate, stable org chart, a manager who sets priorities.
- Treating the "equity may be worth zero" warning as a **dealbreaker** rather than a known cost.
- **Title-chasing** — every bullet a "leadership" bullet, padded scope, vague big-team language.
- **AI tourism** — "passionate about leveraging AI" with no evidence of having used the tools or formed an opinion.
- **Credential recital with no verbs** — degrees and awards stacked with nothing built.

### Top questions Devin will be asking

1. **Is there a wedge in the first third of this resume?** Does the top line say "special education compliance auditor, complex autism, IEP/IDEA across multiple districts" — or a mushy generalist label? *(The Skim, first 8 seconds; Pet Peeves — credential recital.)*
2. **What would you do in week one?** He asks this before he asks about your past — he is testing whether she thinks in shipped actions, not initiatives. *(Voice.)*
3. **Are her recent roles operating or building — salary job or self-made?** Did she found/launch/win something, or coordinate/support/participate? *(Scan order 2-3; Taste — evidence of building.)*
4. **Has she lived with real risk?** Is there an independent / 1099 stretch with no salary floor that proves she didn't flinch? *(Taste — skin in the game; Scan order 4.)*
5. **Can she run a six-week cycle and ship something imperfect?** Or is her metabolism set to the school-year clock — multi-year initiatives, phased rollouts, committee consensus? *(Pet Peeves — district-pace prose; Reading Catherine — sharpest fear.)*
6. **Does she have a specific, slightly contrarian point of view about AI in education that is hers?** Not "I'm excited about AI" — a take like "most AI tutors are built for the median student and that's a compliance liability." *(Taste — a point of view; Pet Peeves — AI tourism.)*
7. **Has she actually touched the tools?** Used Claude/ChatGPT/AI-for-teachers products, shipped or written anything? *(The Skim — AI signal; Reading Catherine — must close this gap.)*
8. **Can she sell trust to SpEd directors and superintendents?** Does she already have that network and speak their language — compressing his go-to-market by months? *(Taste — customer fluency.)*
9. **Is she a builder who wants to ship a flawed v1, or a researcher who wants to study the problem?** The EdD and AERA papers make him admire her — and worry. *(Reading Catherine — researcher identity; this is the question he probes the whole call.)*
10. **Does she ask about ownership, runway, and the cap table — or only base salary and benefits?** Equity-illiteracy shifts how he reads everything else. *(Pet Peeves — equity-illiteracy.)*

## Persona 2 — Arjun Mehta (Technical Co-Founder seeking the domain half)

### What Arjun is looking for — the bar for a YES

Arjun is **not hiring; he is courting**. He has a working AI-IEP prototype, two design-partner districts, ~18 months of personal runway, and no domain half. A YES is **not a job offer** — it is entry into a 90-day unpaid, structured "dating" period working the prototype and first three district conversations, with a written co-founder agreement drafted before either tells their employer anything. He binds 4-7 years of his life and his cap table to this person. His five assessment axes, in order:

1. **Domain depth he genuinely, structurally lacks** — she can explain *why a thing is hard* (the mechanism), not just that it's hard.
2. **Can own the entire non-technical half** — go-to-market, fundraising narrative, design-partner relationships, business-side hiring, public CEO — without him backstopping it.
3. **Founder-grade resilience and real appetite for the financial cliff-edge** — a number, not a feeling.
4. **Complementary, not overlapping, skills.**
5. **Can he argue with her at 11pm about a hard call and come out still trusting her** — the one he can't put in a spreadsheet.

### Instant-reject triggers

- **The advisor in disguise** — wants the title and equity but flinches at the cliff, the cold sales calls, the no-salary quarter; describes herself as a "strategic" partner. *(Blocker.)*
- **Domain expert who won't be wrong** — a researcher who treats the credential as the end of the conversation, can't say "I don't know," defends her field's status quo against the product's need to change it. *(Blocker.)*
- **Pure institutional career** with no independent operating history and no signal she wants one. *(Skim deal-breaker.)*
- **A profile optimized for climbing an org chart** rather than for ownership.
- **Zero AI engagement plus no curiosity signal** — the conversation starts from below zero.
- **Identity hedging** — "researcher, but also builder, but also leader." *(Strong.)*
- **Vague risk talk** — "I'm comfortable with risk" with no month-count attached. *(Strong.)*
- **Outsourced GTM** — assuming a "head of sales" hire will do the selling. *(Strong.)*

### Top questions Arjun will be asking

1. **Is there a domain moat here — and is it the exact moat I lack?** He scans for "SpEd / IDEA-compliance / complex autism" named outright. A generalist "education leader" gets a polite no. *(Skim, check 1.)*
2. **Can she explain why IEP compliance is hard — the specific mechanism?** The seventeen ways an IEP goes legally wrong, the litigation exposure — not just "it's hard." *(Taste — a wedge he can feel the edges of.)*
3. **Has she shipped or sold anything outside an institution?** An LLC, paid engagements, a self-funded project — proof she's operated without an employer's scaffolding. *(Skim, check 2; Taste — skin in the game.)*
4. **Has she watched the same problem repeat across many sites?** "I have audited IEP non-compliance at N sites and it fails the same three ways every time" converts consulting history into founder evidence. *(Taste — owned, repeated exposure; Reading Catherine — must surface this.)*
5. **Is there one concrete AI artifact — critical, not promotional?** A teardown of an existing AI-IEP tool's compliance failures, or a written piece. This is the artifact that tells him she can be his **argument partner**. *(Skim, check 3; Reading Catherine — point 3.)*
6. **Can she carry the entire non-technical half — GTM, fundraising, design partners, the CEO seat — without him backstopping it?** "If she needs me to backstop the business half, the company has no business half." *(The Position; Reading Catherine — his biggest fear; Pet Peeves — outsourced GTM.)*
7. **What is her runway number?** "I can operate for X months on $0-60k" — a number, or he assumes the real answer is three. *(Pet Peeves — vague risk talk; Reading Catherine — point 4.)*
8. **Is she a builder today — or a researcher who secretly wants tenure and will leave at month fourteen?** A Dissertation of the Year is a credential *and* a warning light: proof she excels at the slow, citation-bound mode a pre-seed startup punishes. *(Pet Peeves — identity hedging; Reading Catherine — the identity question.)*
9. **Will she give up the net?** Did she rehearse the founder cliff *with a stable W-2 underneath* — and is she now willing to run without it? *(Reading Catherine — equity-risk appetite.)*
10. **Will she disagree with me in the first meeting — and hold the position when I push back?** When he shows her the prototype, will she find the real compliance flaw and tell him plainly? "The partnership is decided in that argument, not in the resume." *(Taste — productive disagreement; Reading Catherine — point 7.)*

## Synthesis — What the two personas jointly demand

Devin and Arjun want the **same candidate**; they differ only in how much proof they need before committing.

**They jointly demand four things:**

1. **Wedge-first, not title-first.** Both skim for "SpEd / IDEA-compliance / complex / PDA autism" in the top third. A generalist "education leadership" label fails both instantly. The niche is the entire reason either takes the call — it must lead.

2. **Consulting reframed as company-building.** Both read "founded and ran an independent practice — solo lead-gen, proposals, closed engagements, lived on 1099 income" as the closest thing she has to a startup story and the proof she can own GTM. It must be foregrounded as building, not described as a service.

3. **A builder identity that is *evidenced*, not just *announced*.** Both want the "I am done writing about it — I want to build the fix and sell it" sentence. But Arjun specifically will not accept an asserted builder identity without **one shipped, public, critical AI artifact** behind it — for him, a declared identity with no artifact reads as the advisor-in-disguise pattern. The single highest-leverage gap is one short, public teardown of an AI-IEP tool's compliance failures.

4. **Cooler packaging.** Both are allergic to performance. The all-caps banner and "I bring the unfakeable half" register read a notch hot. They want the strong facts stated flat so they can reach the co-founder conclusion *themselves* — the only version of that conclusion either will trust.

**Where they diverge — and what it means for Catherine:** Devin can defer the hardest question (builder vs. researcher) to a paid trial, so a "credible AI step + I'll test the rest" resume earns his call. Arjun cannot defer it — he is buying an argument partner for half a decade — so he needs the AI artifact *and* a forward-looking runway number *and* an explicit claim of the CEO/GTM seat before a fast yes. **The resume that satisfies Arjun automatically satisfies Devin.** Catherine should write to Arjun's bar.
