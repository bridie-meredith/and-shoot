# WHAT I DO FOR SCHOOLS AND DISTRICTS
17 years as a special education practitioner — complex autism, PDA, trauma-informed practice, early childhood — now in my sixth year of independent consulting. I help K-12 districts keep special education compliant and defensible under a state monitor's review, and I author board-adoptable district AI policy. Compliance is my day job, which is why I am the right author for whole-district AI governance: a policy that has to survive a board member, a parent FOIA request, a journalist, and a state compliance reviewer is the same discipline as a federal IDEA findings report. AI tools are built for the median student; for a child with a PDA profile or a complex communication need, that median assumption is exactly where the bias and the IDEA exposure live — and that is the section of any AI policy I get right. I work with superintendents, special education directors, principals, and instructional leadership teams.
