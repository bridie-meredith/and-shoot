The bones around peaks look like fusion candidates from prose shape alone but the tensometer disqualifies them.

**The missing rule**: the brief says "All anchors are tens=1" but some annotated lines span multiple anchors (e.g. L3 fuses @5/@6/@7/@8; L10 fuses @16/@17; L67 fuses @105/@106). When a single rendered line covers multiple anchors with mixed tens, the line's effective tens should be taken as the maximum of its constituent anchors. This was not stated in the brief's condition 2. It would have mattered for L67 (the brief reader might see "L67 = one sentence, check its trace" and find the Phase 7 FUSE notation but miss that @106=3). Recommend adding: "For rendered lines that span multiple anchors (Phase 7 FUSE lines), the effective tens is the maximum of all constituent anchor tens values."
