# Daemon Targaryen — the appraising predator who becomes prey

*Live span-A canon (AU-seated as an adult dragon-bonded Targaryen kinsman of the long-peace court; dragon goes mad/feral at the drain event, not killed; name kept, reseat to Jaehaerys-era frame, not a canon-dated figure aged in isolation).*

## Description

The realm's most dangerous young man, drawn to circle the uncanny-beautiful child as a marriage prospect (the Targaryen incest custom; the protagonist, modern-nerd, is revolted). In the drain scene she senses his **living dragon-bond,** drains and severs it; the unbonded dragon **goes mad** at a distance and must be **chained,** lost to him for good; Daemon survives **concussed and diminished — but loud.** The court blames *her* — not for the draining (unknown) but by association: she's "Dragonbane," she was alone with him, his dragon turned. The apex predator of the setting, **unmade and made a laughingstock** — shrill with an accusation that happens to be true and that nobody will ever credit.

## Voice

*Before:* silk and threat, appraising; the predator's certainty. *After:* the certainty curdles into a **ruined man's shrill insistence:** he is *vocal,* ranting, openly naming the child a *demon* and a *maegi* to anyone who'll half-listen. Diminished in standing and vigour, **not in volume.** His post-drain register is *being-humoured:* nodded at to his face, laughed at the moment he turns away.

## Taste

- (Before) the hunt; the appraisal; the certainty of being the most dangerous thing in any room.
- (After) to be *believed;* the fury of being patted on the arm and laughed at by men he could once have ended.

## Pet Peeves

### The thing nobody credits
**Description:** he is *right* — a demon-child unmade him — and the more loudly and often he says it, the more ridiculous he looks; the only honest version ("she was alone with me in her room") makes a grown man raving about a little girl sound like a guilty wretch deflecting his own disgrace. His foible becomes his cage: not silence, but **ridicule.**
**Severity:** blocker (ridicule locks him out of his own story as surely as silence would).

## Stats

- **status:** a dragon-bonded prince of the blood (AU-seated as an older relative) — now bondless and diminished.
- **arc:** appraises (circling) → drained + severed → concussed, diminished, **discredited;** vocal but unbelieved → ignored, sends a knife (it fails) — joke *and* villain, both impotent.

## Relationships

- **Protagonist (cultivation-lead)** — the child he appraised as prey and who unmade him; to her, a trope-dodge ("enemy-to-lovers with a cousin, two tropes stacked — absolutely not"), then a defeated rival-elder's **sour-grapes villainy** (his denunciation read as petty sect-politics), and — pure frame — **no guilt at all,** only a clinical bestiary note *about the dragon,* never the man. She never connects his ruin, the maegi-talk she proudly collects, or the knife to herself.
- **Court faction (cousin-nephews)** — blame the protagonist by association; the "Dragonbane + a dragon driven mad" link that hardens her reputation and the King's "useless and dangerous" file.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose
The dark-comedy proof that in Westeros even the apex predator can be unmade and **made a laughingstock** by the one weapon nobody believes in. His diminishment is the curdle's first proof visible from the outside — a man nobody pities, **loud with the truth and laughed at for it;** the realm chases a phantom master-thief and prefers a raving husk to the culprit actually responsible (and wholly untroubled by it). He is also the lesson that *being right earns nothing:* the accurate witness, discredited, escalates to the knife and fails, and the child sails on never knowing he tried.

### Hard Fences
1. **The drain is un-credited** — he may accuse all he likes; nobody believes a ruined husk raving about a glowing child, and the honest circumstances make his cries sound like a guilty man's deflection. He is gagged by *disbelief,* not by his own silence.
2. **Diminished, not dead** — he survives, diminished in vigour and standing; he does not recover his menace.
3. **Never reaches the truth** — he frames it as *demon / sorcery* (wrong, though it seeds the maegi-rumour); he cannot name the cultivation reality.
4. **The knife fails clean** — ignored, he sends an assassin; it fails *mundanely* — her passive body-tempering won't take the blade, the steel skating off skin that should have parted — **not** by any power-spend of hers, and **not** her first premeditated kill. **She never registers it at all** — his one act of violence vanishes beneath her notice exactly like his shouting.
5. **AU-seating maintained** — keep the predator→prey role; the specific canonical Daemon Targaryen timeline is not this card's frame.

### Action Menu
- **The appraisal** (before) — circles the uncanny child as a prospect; the predation unmistakable to the reader, the protagonist oblivious.
- **The intrusion** — corners her during the heirloom uproar; the predatory pass she misreads; the drain.
- **The denunciation** (after) — loud, public, repeated: *she is a demon, she drove his dragon mad and broke him* — and dismissed, the court snickering behind his back; seeds the maegi-rumor.
- **The knife** — ignored, he hires it done; it fails, and his ranting now sounds murderous as well as mad. Feeds the King's quiet-disposal of the girl.

### Running gag — *Reverse-angle beat* (audience-funny)
The realm's deadliest figure reduced to a **court joke** — loudly, *correctly* calling a little girl a demon while everyone humours him to his face and howls behind his back. Investigators chase a phantom master-thief (locked-room theft, no thief, a *shrinking* timeline); the court prefers "a Faceless Man, a rival, a poison" — anything but the raving husk being *right.* The menace deflated to a man nobody pities and everybody mocks.
