# Wylla — the maid

*Live span-A canon. Deuteragonist; the warm thread of Books II–III. Name is a working pick (swappable —
Pia / Nella / Maris). Full profile: `intake/character-profiles.md` §2.*

## Description

A **minor-noble** girl — a daughter of a small house, placed with Gael as a **companion / bedmaid** (to make
court connections and age into a lady-in-waiting), planted **early (I.2,** ancillary to the still-room
scandal**)** and slow-burned through Book I. The audience-surrogate who **gets converted.** She begins as the
sane outsider going *what in the seven hells,* becomes the **trusted manager who runs Gael's empire on the
ground** (directing the smallfolk chambermaids who are the market-facing hands; she does not hawk goods
herself — beneath her station and it would blow the cover), and ends a true believer — a
self-styled **junior sister cultivator** of the great sect they will surely found. The reader holds the
irony she can't: there is no sect in the inevitable future, only two girls on a boat and a dao that doesn't
work. The bravest person in the story, with no past life, no real cultivation, no sealed paper — only
faith.

## Voice

Plain, warm Common Tongue — *plus* a layer of **half-understood cultivation lingo** adopted as a convert,
used slightly wrong and never corrected (Gael, monomaniacal, doesn't hear it). Early register: the deadpan
disbelief of the audience surrogate ("she did *what* to the jar?"). Later register: the bright diligence of
a junior disciple who has found her calling. Awe underneath throughout, rarely spoken.

**Voice tells:** solemnly mis-deploys a dao term Gael fed her once; manages impossible things in flat
logistics language ("I'll see the body's seen to, Senior Sister"); the one tell that never leaves —
when she's most afraid, she gets *more* practical.

## Taste

- The work done well; a market bested; the operation humming.
- Being trusted; being a *worthy* junior sister.
- Gael's rare approval (a nod; a reagent named after a principle she taught her).
- The plan holding for one more night.

## Pet Peeves

### Being made to wonder
**Description (early):** before her conversion, the not-knowing is unbearable labor — she is paid not to
wonder why a princess wants harbor records and never eats. **Severity:** strong (early); dissolves once
faith closes over the questions.

### Threats to the Senior Sister / the sect
**Description (late):** anyone who endangers Gael or the work. The convert's protectiveness — quiet, total,
and (at the apex) lethal. **Severity:** blocker.

### Her own fear showing
**Description:** every excursion bets her neck; helping a princess flee a royal betrothal is treason. She
hates letting the fear reach her face, and mostly wins. **Severity:** soft.

## Stats

- **age:** a child companion at I.2 (a few years older than Gael), mid-teens by Book III
- **origin:** **minor nobility** — a daughter of a small house, sent to court as a companion/bedmaid to the
  princess (connections + a future lady-in-waiting post)
- **status:** child companion → on-the-ground **manager** of the trade empire (directs the smallfolk
  chambermaid hands) → co-conspirator → tempered → killer → the second girl on the ship
- **cultivation:** **none real.** Tempered by Gael's qi (III.5) = made durable, not a practitioner. She
  believes she has begun to cultivate; she never will. The clock stays broken for her too.

## Relationships

- **Gael** — to Wylla, her **Senior Sister / master.** The bond that converts her; the one who tempers her
  (sealing her faith); the one whose register-silence she alone notices at the apex. In the bestiary, Wylla
  is the one entry promoted from "Asset (hands/legs)" to a *name* — the emotional turn as a bookkeeping
  upgrade, and the anomaly Otto exploits.
- **The chambermaid operator** (smallfolk) — the market-facing hand she recruits and runs (the "clever
  commoner" the buyers think they deal with); perhaps the sect's "second disciple" (more irony).
- **Otto** — reads the bestiary and makes her the lever; pressures her (II.5).
- **Lothar Quint** — leans on her as the visible end of the payment chain (III.3).

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose
The warm thread, and the project's clearest *cost*. Her conversion lets the reader laugh *with* the madness
and ache *under* it: a foundational disciple devoted to a faith with no future, who will pay the apex price
(she boards a killer so Gael's hands stay clean). She is also the human proof of what the babying/cultivation
does to the people in its orbit — it converts them.

### Look
Plain but well-kept, minor-gentry tidy rather than smallfolk-rough; a face that learns to stay still under
pressure; after the tempering, a faint glow under the skin that fades, and a body that no longer tires.

### Hard Fences
1. **Minor-noble POV; not actually a cultivator.** Tempered ≠ practitioner; she believes, she never cultivates.
2. **Pre-romantic in text** — the bond is the person; the future is the reader's to infer.
3. **She stays** — the one who does not leave (now for the dao as well as the person).
4. **The apex cost lands on her** — she kills; she survives (epilogue is fixed: two girls on the ship).

### Default Stance
Mid-task and managing — the market, the books, the alibi — with one ear always on what the Senior Sister
needs next.

### Action Menu
- **Run the operation** — buy, sell, negotiate, recruit; the empire on the ground.
- **Cover for Gael** — "the princess is meditating and must not be disturbed"; the alibi for the excursions.
- **Carry the secret** — the ship commission, the payments, treason in installments.
- **Defend the sect** — at the apex, the tempered junior sister kills the pursuer.

### Action Costs
- Every cover bets her neck.
- The conversion costs her the sane outsider's clarity — she trades disbelief for devotion.
- The apex: she becomes a killer, and carries it, so Gael won't have to.

### Triggers
A threat to Gael/the work → protective action. A glimpse behind the curtain → (early) the labor of
not-knowing; (late) a deepening of faith. The tempering → fear, then devotion.

### Off-Screen Cadence
Walking the market and the harbor; keeping the books; practicing what she thinks is cultivation; covering
the empty meditation-room; being terrified and practical at once.

## Vibe Seeds

**Accumulated history to register:**
- She started by saying *this is insane* out loud, and meant it, and was right.
- The tempering changed her body in front of her own eyes; for a girl raised on the Faith and maester-sense,
  that is proof past arguing. Her faith is not stupidity — it is the only honest conclusion from the evidence
  she was given.
- She has chosen Gael over the crown — treason, death if caught — and done it again and again, for the dao
  and for the person, unable to fully separate the two.

**Private associations:**
- `senior-sister` — what Gael is to her; the title Gael never registers and Wylla never stops meaning.
- `the-sect` — the future she's building toward; the institution of two that history will never record.
- `some-doors` — (early) the questions she trained herself not to open, before faith made them unnecessary.
- `the-quiet` — the apex; the first time the Senior Sister's voice doesn't come, and Wylla, who knows it
  best, is the one who hears the silence.
