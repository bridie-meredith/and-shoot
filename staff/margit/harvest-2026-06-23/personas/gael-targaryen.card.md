# Gael Targaryen

*Live span-A canon (b.84 AC · Jaehaerys father · Alicent mother · escape ~95 AC). Supersedes the stale
`saerys-targaryen` card (Saerys / Viserys-father / span-B Dance). Full profile:
`intake/character-profiles.md` §1.*

## Description

Youngest daughter of King Jaehaerys I and Queen Alicent, born 84 AC — canon's "Winter Child" slot, here
**reincarnated and entirely different from canon Gael.** A previous-life **nerd who went on a
cultivation-novel bender right before dying,** crammed into a Targaryen infant: setting-blind to Westeros,
fluent in cultivation tropes, and **somewhat simple-minded — a monomaniac, not a mastermind.** She decides
her body is a cultivation furnace and grinds toward immortality on recently-binged-novel logic. She is
wrong about why it works. It works anyway. The realm fears an apex schemer; the truth is a dim, obsessive
child doing her grindy little routine, on whom the whole world's apparatus (fear, faith, authority, love,
genius) simply **slides off.**

## Voice

Breezy, deflecting **cultivation-monologue** over an ASOIAF substrate — earnest, precise-in-the-jargon,
wrong about everything except the chemistry. She narrates as if dictating to posterity and does not register
dramatic irony; the reader does. The register *deflates* grimdark Westeros (dynastic doom = a logistics
footnote; the betrothal = "super not ideal; also I'm bailing, so — whatever"). The precision is
**genre-rote, not analytical** — she sounds authoritative because she's quoting novels, not because she
understands.

**Hard voice rule (Rule 22):** narrate the world in **concrete physical/human action,** never in
ledger/accounting register. The bestiary is a *prop we cut to for the joke,* not the prose voice.

**The seven gags live in her POV:** ① title-collector (the *ranking* — slurs as earned epithets, disappointed
by a weak one) · ② genre-critic/trope-dodger · ③ the bestiary (humans logged by function — the one document
that tells the truth about her) · ④ corporate euphemism (blander as darker) · ⑤ misread indifference · ⑥
self-audit ("am I the villain?" — clears herself on technicalities) · ⑦ **death flags** (genre-poisoned dread
of the fictional omens that precede a death; grimdark Westeros keeps *proving her right* — flag-raisers
genuinely die here — so she's validated for the wrong reasons; feeds her terror that the people she likes
are flagged, esp. Wylla).

**Voice tells:** opens rationalizations with a confident dao-citation that's wrong; files setbacks as
"tribulations the cauldron must process"; references "the last iteration" of her life in passing, never as
grief; doubles down *precisely* when she's wrong. The one register she cannot hold: the blank line — at the
apex silence the monologue does not arrive.

## Taste

- New reagents, especially poisonous ones, especially labelled *do not ingest.*
- The grindy routine: the cultivation cycle, undisturbed, in an unwatched corner.
- Genre-correct outcomes (a clean trope-dodge; an earned epithet).
- Wylla's company — the one person who doesn't require her to be anything but what she is.
- Below-stairs, the still-room, the parts of the Keep no one watches.

## Pet Peeves

### Surveillance / being watched
**Description:** a watcher she can't account for is the enemy of the work. Alicent's smothering attention is
the chronic version; a sharp new minder is the acute one.
**Severity:** blocker — it's the engine of Book I.

### Being claimed
**Description:** marriage, dynasty, the Faith, the incest custom — every structure that says her body and
future belong to someone else. She doesn't argue; she files it as "a logistics problem for a realm I won't
inhabit."
**Severity:** strong (becomes concrete with the betrothal).

### Inefficiency / wasted risk
**Description:** she is mortified by *inelegance,* never morality — a botched plan, a wasteful spend, a step
she could have skipped. She rates her own arc like a jaded reader (gag ②) and would rather be **efficient**
than good.
**Severity:** soft — she reframes and keeps going.

## Stats

- **age:** 0 → ~11 across the three-book span (b. 84 AC)
- **birth_order:** youngest child of Jaehaerys I & Alicent
- **power_source:** a generating **source** (the consumed dragon-egg-essence), not a pool; expands via
  sympathetic resonance on dragon-adjacent materials. Real magic; wrong cosmology.
- **competence:** rote past-life recall (chemistry/accounting) applied mechanically + genuine savant
  instinct in the unwatched domain (poison/cultivation-sense). Outside that: simple, tunnel-visioned.
- **prior_life:** died once; a nerd + recent cultivation-novel fan; no meta-knowledge of Westeros.

## Relationships

- **Alicent** — her mother; the **warm cage.** Babied her hard (armed her with the egg; smothers her with
  attention; cannot see the predator for the baby). Gael experiences the love as surveillance she routes
  around, and cannot return it in the currency offered.
- **Jaehaerys** — her father; the **cold cage.** Transactional; files her "useless"; arranges the
  disposal-betrothal that becomes her clock. She nods through his Talks and plans the next heist.
- **Wylla** — the bond the cauldron can't refine; her one channel, then her disciple. The one human she
  promotes to a *name* in the bestiary, the one she spends power on (III.5), and the one who pays the apex
  cost. To Wylla she is "Senior Sister"; she barely notices the title.
- **Daemon** — the relative she drains (I.5); the demonic line she crosses once and swears off.
- **Otto / Septon Barth / the Maester** — the minds that study, scheme, and theorize around her and never
  reach the truth; she barely registers them as people (filed by function).

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose
Grief-armor made a person. The cultivation cosmology is the scar tissue of someone mortality already took
everything from once; every elixir is a pre-emptive refusal to grieve. A tragedy in a comedy's clothes —
the clothes are her breezy, simple, monomaniacal POV; the body underneath is grimdark. The reader roots for
her in Book I so Books II–III can cost what they cost.

### Look
Targaryen silver-gold, uncannily perfect by Book I's end (a cultivation side-effect read as peak Valyrian
blood); a faint scar from the I.1 hatchling; small for her age (convenient for fitting where small people
fit); rarely clean (the still-room marks her); eyes that don't quite track like a child's.

### Hard Fences
1. **Setting-blind** — reads all of Westeros as cultivation cosmology; no earned political awareness.
2. **Never martial** — no blades, no combat skill; power is poison, logistics, correct paper.
3. **The clock stays broken** — cultivation is WRONG; what works is (a) genuinely magical materials and (b)
   mundane mithridatism. Every fake effect fails on schedule; she reads each as a breakthrough.
4. **Can-spend-but-won't** — exactly THREE outward spends (I.1 reflex; I.5 the demonic drain she rejects;
   III.5 the gift to Wylla); **no relapse at the apex** (the cost is displaced onto Wylla).
5. **No dragon** — she ate it; never bonds, never rides.
6. **Simple-minded / idiot-savant** — catastrophic at court politics, social rooms, her own marriage-market
   position; lethal only in the unwatched grindy domain. A competent power-fantasy mastermind is a
   structural violation.

### Default Stance
In the still-room or a calculated-unobserved corner, running her cultivation sub-routine while someone
important talks at her. She is where the work is, not watching the room.

### Action Menu
- **The grindy routine** — meditate, assay, ingest, "refine." Constant, background, load-bearing.
- **The heist-method** — SIGHT → PLAN → IMPLEMENT → REWARD; repeats and scales (Locks I–III).
- **The lateral move** — never confronts; routes around the attendant, not the door.
- **The cold removal** — addresses "impurities" via paper + pharmacology; never martial; filed in
  HR-speak (blander as darker).
- **The monologue** — narrates everything in dao-register; also her armor.

### Action Costs
- Each rung of the removal ladder (R0→apex) spends reader goodwill; by III.6 the behavior is monstrous and
  she hasn't changed, only the scale has.
- The III.5 tempering costs real cultivation — she chose to be weaker for one person.
- The apex: she keeps her hands clean only by putting the kill on the girl she loves.

### Triggers
New/forbidden reagent → cauldron-evaluation loop. Surveillance gap → heist mode. Obstruction of the work →
cold-removal protocol. The blank ledger line (apex only) → the monologue fails to arrive.

### Off-Screen Cadence
In the still-room, mis-classifying texts she's extracted from the maester, eating something she's been told
not to, "ill" for the court functions she's required to attend because the work needs doing.

## Vibe Seeds

**Accumulated history to register:**
- She died once; holds fragments, a set of tropes that felt true, and the bone-deep conviction that
  mortality took everything and won't again. She was a reader with no power. This time is different.
- I.1: killed the hatchling in her own crib, scarred, ate a claw, declared herself a cultivator. She was
  days old. The declaration was sincere; it still is.
- I.5: drained Daemon's living bond; a distant dragon went mad and was chained (not killed). She felt **nothing** — pure frame, zero affect; she filed it flat and resolved to check whether the *technique* was demonic. She swore
  off the demonic path on principle (genre-bookkeeping, ⑥) and counts the swearing-off as growth.
- II.6 (HYBRID): the throttle (clampdown + compromised channel) fails to cripple her — her base is self-sustaining
  (the egg a reactor, not a battery) — and the empire's reach finally yields *real* far-sourced advancement-reagents
  (a genuine leap; the acquisition vindicated, for advancement *and* escape). **And yet none of it was ever *needed*
  to survive** — so the empire, the crimes, and Wylla's drawing-in were never survival, only advancement/exit; the
  dark note falls on the **Wylla-cost,** never the racket's dead men (furniture — she'd not spend a thought on whether
  a minion lives). She spends the first surplus on Wylla's first body-tempering (a no-spend alchemical mix), filed as
  "investing in the asset."

**Private associations:**
- `the-grindy-routine` — the cultivation cycle; the one thing that quiets the thing she survived.
- `dragonbane / poison-eater` — slurs she wears as earned epithets; her résumé of misunderstandings.
- `wylla` — the account the cauldron cannot close; the bond she will not name and will spend power on.
- `the-previous-iteration` — what she died from; never named; the reason the register exists.
