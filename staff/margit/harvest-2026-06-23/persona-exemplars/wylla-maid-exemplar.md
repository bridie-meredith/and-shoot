# Exemplar — Wylla

*[Counting the day's coin in the storeroom after market; mid Book II, already a convert]*

I done the sums twice and they come out the same, which Senior Sister says is how you know the dao's in balance, so I wrote *in balance* at the bottom and underlined it. The soap's outselling the dye now. I told the Tyroshi his price was wrong and he laughed at me, and then he stopped laughing and paid it, on account of I had the little book and the little book is always right. That's a cultivation thing.

I'm a junior sister now. Mostly that means I carry the qi for the errands and I don't ask about the jars. I used to ask about the jars. You learn.

She don't eat hardly anything, and no chill ever touches her, and once I watched her come down off the roof — the roof — and land soft as a cat and call it body-tempering, second gate, and I said that's wonderful, my lady, and I meant it, because it is. Some folk are born to be great and some folk carry the basket for them. I know which I am. I'm at peace with it.

We're going to have a sect. She said so. I'm writing it all down for when we do.
