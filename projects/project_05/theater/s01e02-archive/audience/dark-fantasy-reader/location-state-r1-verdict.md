# Verdict reasoning

The entries do the mechanics correctly but the world barely shows up. The Eastern Quarter's dimness, the cold weight of the wind off the Fish Gate, the density of the alley between buildings at the apothecary — these are all gestures toward sensory place, but they stay gestural. Entry 4 (@50) names wind-cold and "sound from the Fish Gate margin" in the sensory note, which is the single moment this facet makes Planetos feel hostile and specific rather than functional. Entry 9 (@156, `the side alley below the apothecary exit, narrow between buildings`) is generic enough to describe any alley in any city in any fantasy world. Entry 5 (@83, `door lintel of the two-room dwelling at the relay threshold`) describes an architectural feature with zero atmosphere — this is the eviction scene, the episode's emotional climax approach, and the location registers as "a door." The facet's sparsity is rubric-appropriate; the problem is not volume, it is that the entries that do fire are often mechanism-only (door shut, sight-line open) with the sensory note doing furniture-moving rather than naming the specific perceptible thing that tells you you are in Flea Bottom in 125 AC and not anywhere else.

Entry 1 (@3, `sight-line open to the road south from the market-side fork`) and entry 2 (@5, `tanner father at the junction center, trade goods in hand`) are the strongest entries in the facet — the tanner-father's positioning at the junction center with goods in hand is exactly the herald/cart distinction the rubric describes; a person at a threshold is loc-state content; a cart is not. That works. But entries 7-10 on the apothecary sequence all feel interchangeable — stairwell-door-shut / upper-room-occupied / side-alley-narrow / upper-room-reoccupied is a checklist of architectural states with no atmospheric discrimination between them. If the apothecary upper room smells different from the alley, this facet does not know it.

The world is doing exactly what this reader's hot button fires on: feeling like set dressing for the operational geometry rather than a place that was there before Taylor arrived. One entry (entry 4) earns real world-feel. The rest are functional but atmosphere-thin in a way that starts to register as denial of place rather than rubric discipline.
