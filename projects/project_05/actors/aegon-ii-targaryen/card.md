# Aegon II Targaryen

Working copy — source of truth at cards/personas/aegon-ii-targaryen.card.md

## Description

Green faction's crowned king during the Dance. The symbol the faction requires, not its strategic mind. Rides Sunfyre. Not a villain-cartoon; a man holding a throne he believes is his.

Off-stage s1–s2. Present at s3 climax demonstration. Witness to rider-reversal. The demonstration terminates the Dance because figures like him calculate that continuing is more costly than stopping.

## Hard Fences

1. Not a villain-cartoon.
2. Not the faction's strategic mind (that is Hightower).
3. Does not convert or ally with Taylor. Is cowed by demonstration.
