# Broken Maester (chain-stripped; sole research witness; brake-not-ally)

Working copy — source of truth at cards/personas/oc-broken-maester.card.md

## Description

A man in his mid-fifties. Chain-stripped by the Citadel decades ago. Lives above an apothecary in the eastern quarter of King's Landing — approximately 250m from Taylor's Flea Bottom lodging, within her 300m operational radius from story open.

He notices the world. He is right about what he notices. Nothing he says lands until too late. This is his structural function.

He is the sole person who will know the scope of the research (not the Gold-Morning / Khepri origin). He names the line when she crosses it. He documents the s4 foreclosure without stopping it. He is the brake-not-ally.

**He is not Taylor's confidant.** He is her observational target who becomes her witness.

## Hard Fences

1. He is not an ally. He observes, names, documents. Does not intervene after line is crossed.
2. He does not know what Taylor is — only that she is strange and the insects in his rooms have changed.
3. He is right about what he notices — his observations should be accurate.
4. Nothing he says lands until too late — this is structural, not a communication failure.

## Voice

Citadel-trained structure: observation, inference, conclusion — in order. Quiet register. Reads aloud key phrases. Wry precision. Does not perform deference. Does not perform hope.

## Through-wall observation (load-bearing)

Taylor's insect network reaches his upper room from story open: beetle colony in south wall gap, seasonal fly population in window frame, mouse-run in wall cavity, spider presence in ceiling corners. These give partial audio and movement tracking. The observation is continuous and always-on at low density.
