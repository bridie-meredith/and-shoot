# Viserys I Targaryen

Working copy — source of truth at cards/personas/viserys-i-targaryen.card.md

## Description

Fifth Targaryen king. Known as a good man and a poor king: beloved, gentle, committed to peace, structurally incapable of making the succession decision clearly enough to prevent the Dance.

Background political force for s1. His relevance is structural: an unclaimed dragon in the Riverlands (and unusual creature activity in the Crownlands) is, by Targaryen right and law, his business. His response to reports shapes the institutional pressure Taylor faces.

Off-stage in s1. Reports reach him. He does not arrive.

## Hard Fences

1. He does not believe anything bad will happen if he maintains kindness and refuses to escalate.
2. Cannot acknowledge the succession contest as a contest.
3. Off-stage s1. Does not arrive.
