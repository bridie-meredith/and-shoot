# Series Tone Constraints — flea-bottom-dance (125 AC Config)

## Description

Project-level tonal law for flea-bottom-dance. Defines the registers this series runs in, the registers it does not run in, and the specific structural prohibitions that govern how scenes must be constructed across all four seasons. These constraints apply to all agents across all scenes. The auditor loads this card when reviewing scene-level register drift.

**This is not the fast-pulpy-dramatic config of cond-series-tone-constraints-84ac.** The primary register here is contemplative-procedural-horror. The distinction is load-bearing: the 84ac version runs on forward velocity and physical stakes; this version runs on the weight of what is being tracked, what is being built, and what is being missed. The pace is slower. The interiority is thicker. The horror is architectural, not kinetic.

## Sensory Impact

Tonal constraints have no ambient sensory expression. Their expression is felt in scene velocity (slower than 84ac), in the ratio of action beats to observation beats (tilted toward observation), and in whether a scene's center of gravity is forward-moving or interior-recursive. A scene that violates these constraints will feel too fast, too kinetic, or too emotionally resolved.

## Duration

Persistent across all four seasons. Series-defining rules.

## The Primary Register: Contemplative-Procedural-Horror

**Slow-burn / low-rupture-density register. Foreknowledge-clamp as primary register.** The standard tens frequency-band gate (60-75% 1s / 20-30% 2s / 5-10% 3s in `design/shoot-v2/rubric-tensometer.md`) does not apply to seasons authored under this tone-law. The fast-pulpy-dramatic 84ac config is the rubric's calibration target; this config's contemplative-procedural-horror register runs structurally lower on 2s and higher on 1s because the prevailing beat-charge is *Taylor observing what is being built and what is being missed*, not *Taylor reacting to externally-applied pressure*.

**Relaxed tens frequency-band for this config (URI-034 Exemption 5):**
- **1s: 75-85%** (relaxed ceiling — contemplative observation IS the primary register; quiet-beat density structural).
- **2s: 12-22%** (relaxed floor — visible-pressure beats are sparser because pressure is foreknowledge-clamped, not externally inflicted at the typical thriller cadence).
- **3s: 4.5-10% season-average, 4.0-10% per-episode** (relaxed from the standard 5-10% rubric floor by 0.5 points at season scope, 1.0 point at per-episode scope; the rubric calibration target is 84ac-pacing, and slow-burn-foreknowledge-clamped fiction structurally lands 0.5-1.5 points lower because the prevailing register is observation-not-reaction). The rupture-rung floor is load-bearing: a per-episode 3s rate below 4.0% OR a season-average below 4.5% is a HARD fail even under this tone-law. The dramatist may not soften the floor further by scalar inflation (AP4 prohibition holds); a season failing the relaxed floor is a structural story-shape failure that requires screen-writer intervention, not rubric relaxation.

**The relaxed band applies across every episode of every season under this tone-law.** Per-episode breaches falling inside the relaxed band are exempt-under-tone-law; per-episode breaches falling outside even the relaxed band remain FAIL. The Exemption 5 path in the tens rubric requires this card's relaxed-band statement to be cited by the dramatist or auditor with the quoted numbers above. A claim of slow-burn register without this card's citation is not an Exemption 5 claim.

**Contemplative:** Scenes are allowed to linger on what Taylor observes, on the texture of the research, on the quality of what is being built and what is being missed. This is not the 84ac version's prohibition on extended introspection — here, extended observation and recursive self-examination are structural. The contemplative register earns its length when what is being observed is load-bearing. The constraint is that the contemplation must be working — it must be producing something (a hypothesis, a dread-texture shift, a record of omission) — not merely present.

**Procedural:** The arc is built through accumulation of small actions, each individually defensible, each producing a future consequence that is not visible at the moment of the action. The procedure of Taylor's research — observation, hypothesis, test, notation, revision — is the story's primary mechanism. The audience tracks the procedure; the horror is in what the procedure produces across time. A scene that skips the procedural texture in favor of dramatic shorthand is a register violation.

**Horror:** The horror register is architectural, not kinetic. No shock events in the cheap-horror mode. No body horror as spectacle. No sudden reveals that produce a discrete scare. The dread accumulates through what is present in the register of Taylor's record and what is absent. The horror is the gap between Taylor's self-perception (rigorous, precise, doing necessary work) and what the record actually shows. It is realized over arc-length, not scene-length.

## The Prohibited Registers

**No cheap horror.** Fear-as-tool is deployed deliberately and tracked. Shock events, torture-as-spectacle, grotesque-as-primary-affect are prohibited. If a scene is attempting to produce horror through visceral imagery alone, without the contemplative-procedural context to make the imagery mean something, the scene is in violation.

**No triumph register.** Taylor's capabilities increase. The Dance ends. These are not triumphs. The stopping of the Dance is a deterrence outcome — it works because both factions are more afraid of Taylor than of each other, not because Taylor has won anything or resolved anything. Any scene that produces a triumph beat — a moment where Taylor has clearly succeeded and the success is registered as relief or victory — is a register violation. This applies to the s3 demonstration particularly: the rider-reversal demonstration is not a triumph. It is a demonstration of what Taylor is willing and able to do. The tone must hold the distinction.

**No catharsis before the s4 foreclosure.** If a scene creates an emotional release that functions as resolution — that allows the audience or Taylor to exhale — that exhale is a violation unless it is the exhale before the s4 foreclosure arrives to remove it. The series does not offer clean endings at any seasonal boundary prior to s4. Each season boundary should leave the audience with accumulation, not release.

**No momentum-driven adventure pacing.** The 84ac config's "fast, pulpy, dramatic" is explicitly not this project's register. Scenes that run primarily on action beats, that minimize interiority in favor of forward velocity, that treat the setting as backdrop to events rather than as weight that accumulates — these are register violations for this project. The contemplative-procedural-horror register requires slowness to function. Slowness here is a feature.

**No lore-dump as scene function.** Taylor's knowledge of ASOIAF and Worm-universe mechanics surfaces as relevant action and observation, not as internal encyclopedia. The constraint is the same as in the 84ac version.

## The Escalation Rule — Modified for This Config

**Taylor's escalation-reflex is structural** (carried from `cond-shard-behavioral-weight`). The modification for this config: escalation here is not kinetic acceleration. It is scope expansion. Taylor does not escalate by fighting more people; she escalates by extending the research, by committing more resources to the hypothesis, by pushing the experiment past the point where an outside observer would stop. Each step of research scope expansion is an escalation in the structural sense — it forecloses a prior position and creates a new floor below which Taylor cannot return.

The escalation ratchet is one-directional within the research arc: once a research scope has been established, Taylor does not scale it back. She may redirect within the scope; she does not voluntarily shrink it.

## The Demonstration Rule (s3 Climax)

The s3 climax is the collapsed-by-demonstration event: both factions converge dragons at a critical strategic engagement; Taylor demonstrates once — she reverses control of the riders (not the dragons themselves) catastrophically, briefly, in front of witnesses from both factions.

**Demonstration-not-triumph:** The demonstration is a proof-of-concept, not a resolution. Both factions can see that Taylor reversed rider control. Neither faction can model the mechanism. The rational deterrence that terminates the Dance follows from this: rational deterrence, not defeat, not diplomacy, not Taylor's victory. The Dance ends because both sides calculate that testing her again is irrational. Taylor does not receive their calculation as a win. She receives it as a data point.

**Khepri-register mandatory at the demonstration.** The demonstration must land in the Khepri-register — the register of someone who has done this before at a vastly larger scale, who is doing it now at the smallest possible scale that will work, who knows exactly what she is demonstrating and what it costs. The rider-reversal is not Taylor's full capability; it is a precise minimum necessary demonstration. The Khepri weight — "I ended a god, this is nothing, and I want you to understand what nothing means from someone who knows what it looks like to end a god" — must be present in the register without being stated.

**No exhale between demonstration and foreclosure.** The s3 demonstration and the s4 foreclosure are separated by a season boundary, but the narrative must not permit an exhale in the gap. The Dance ending is not a resolution; it is a before-the-next-cost state. Any scene between the demonstration and the s4 foreclosure that implies the resolution-weight that the demonstration does not have is a violation.

## The Khepri-Unlock Sequencing Rule

The Khepri-mantle threshold crosses in late s2. The s3 demonstration is caused by the unlocked mantle. These must be visibly sequenced.

**What "visibly sequenced" means:** The s2 Khepri-crossing must read as the cause of the s3 demonstration's capability. An audience member watching both seasons must be able to trace: the s2 relay-load accumulation → the threshold crossing → the behavioral change visible in s2's final stretch → Taylor arriving at s3 already changed → the s3 demonstration as something she could not have executed in s2. If the sequencing is collapsed (if the demonstration feels possible in s2, or if the threshold crossing in s2 does not precede the demonstration in perceptible causal order), the structure is violated.

**The S2 Khepri-crossing's relay-load cause must be visible in s2 prose.** Not plan-level: on the page. The sustained high-density operation that triggers the threshold must be shown as the cause, not inferred. The maester naming the crossing and being not-heard must read as horror, not as misunderstanding or communication failure.

## The Clinical-Register Self-Erasure Rule

The s1–s3 dread texture is the clinical register's self-erasure. See `cond-clinical-self-erasure` for full executable form notes.

**This card's role in relation to that card:** The self-erasure IS the contemplative-procedural-horror register in execution. It is not a subplot; it is the primary horror mechanism of the first three seasons. A scene that violates the self-erasure card (by having Taylor notice the drift, by having the records remain compassionate past the point they should, by having a single decision-moment replace the gradual drift) is also violating this card's primary register.

**The clinical register must not be softened before s4.** Any revision, scene-level correction, or emotional counterweight that pulls the clinical register back toward full humanity before the s4 foreclosure event is a preparation for the foreclosure that the foreclosure cannot support if it arrives. The foreclosure's weight is in direct proportion to how completely the self-erasure was executed.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-shard-behavioral-weight`:** The Shard's escalation-bias and this card's scope-expansion escalation rule are additive. Load both for any scene involving Taylor's research decisions. The Shard makes de-escalation cost more than it should; this card specifies that de-escalation in the research arc means shrinking scope, and Taylor does not do that.

**With `cond-clinical-self-erasure`:** That card is the execution document for the procedural-horror register this card defines. Both should be loaded for all scene-level review of the research arc.

**With `cond-fauna-control-rules-125ac-addendum`:** The Khepri-unlock sequencing rule in this card maps directly to the Khepri-mantle sealed-at-open / threshold-unlock config in the fauna-control addendum card. Both should be loaded for any scene involving the s2 relay-load accumulation and the threshold crossing.

**For auditor use:**
- Flag any scene that produces a triumph beat at the s3 demonstration.
- Flag any exhale beat between the s3 demonstration and the s4 foreclosure.
- Flag any Khepri-mantle use in s1 or early s2.
- Flag any scene in which the Khepri-threshold crossing and the s3 demonstration appear to be simultaneous or undifferentiated.
- Flag any scene in which the clinical register softens — subjects being named in s3 records, Taylor's discomfort appearing in s3 notation, humanizing counterweight that is not operationally motivated.
- Flag any scene that produces cathartic release before the s4 foreclosure removes it.
- Flag any scene running primarily on action beats and forward velocity without the contemplative-procedural texture.
- Flag any scene where Taylor treats the Dance's end as a win.
