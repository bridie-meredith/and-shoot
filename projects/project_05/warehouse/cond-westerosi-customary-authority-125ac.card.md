# Westerosi Customary Authority — Crownlands / King's Landing Smallfolk Social Physics at 125 AC

## Description

The behavioral constraints governing how Crownlands smallfolk — and specifically a 17-year-old unattached female smallfolk in King's Landing — interact with authority figures at 125 AC late-Viserys-I era, transitioning into the Dance of Dragons. This card defines the social physics of Taylor's operating stratum in her King's Landing configuration: the default postures, the available compliance behaviors, the specific costs of deviation, and what various kinds of wrongness look like in a young woman in this environment.

**Key differences from the era-general pattern (see `cond-westerosi-customary-authority-jaehaerys` for reference):** Authority in KL is Crown-direct, not paramount-lord-mediated. There is no Tully suzerainty layer, no House Ryger, no steward-as-local-face. In King's Landing, the operative authority structures are the City Watch (Gold Cloaks), the Hand of the King's administrative apparatus, and the Master of Laws' jurisdiction — with the Faith of the Seven present as a soft-power overlay. Smallfolk who register as anomalous do not begin climbing a Tully → bannerman → lord chain; they begin moving through Gold Cloak intake → watch-house log → Hand's administrative office, with potential elevation to the Master of Laws. The chain is shorter and moves faster.

This is the ambient behavioral layer that everything in Taylor's Flea Bottom life runs against. It is not a special circumstance; it is the water.

## Sensory Impact

The social physics of deference are physically embodied. When a Gold Cloak patrol moves through Flea Bottom, the smallfolk body-language response is not the retreat-and-eyes-down of a rural encounter; it is a more practiced urban calibration — a slight turn away, continued movement at adjusted pace, no eye contact, no pause that would look like waiting to be noticed. Flea Bottom denizens are experienced at being minimally legible to watch patrols. The deference is performed through absence — not present in the patrol's record means not visible.

Taylor's tell in this environment is not that she fails to perform deference; it is that she performs it incorrectly for a Flea Bottom native. Rural deference postures (the full eyes-down, the weight-shifted-back stance, the formulaic address pattern from `cond-westerosi-customary-authority-jaehaerys`) are recognizable in KL as not-from-here. A young woman who looks down and steps aside like a village girl reads as displaced, not compliant. Displaced reads as notable. Notable is the wrong outcome.

## Duration

Persistent. This is the ambient social structure. Era-stable for the 125 AC project window. The Dance's outbreak (season 3) escalates the Gold Cloak's operational intensity but does not change the underlying social physics — it only raises the probability that those physics apply to any given smallfolk on any given day.

## Authority Architecture — King's Landing at 125 AC

**City Watch / Gold Cloaks (primary street-level authority):**
Taylor's principal point of contact with coercive authority is the Gold Cloaks, not the lord's men of a rural administration. The Gold Cloaks are a standing city watch of approximately two thousand, established by Jaehaerys I, under the command of the Commander of the City Watch (currently reporting upward to the Master of Laws). At 125 AC their reputation for corruption is established: Gold Cloaks at lower ranks take coin, extort market stalls, and are selectively blind to Flea Bottom's black-market economy. This corruption is simultaneously a hazard (Taylor can be shaken down) and a resource (attention can be bought off). The price of invisibility is typically compliance with informal tolls; the price of formal investigation is much higher.

A Gold Cloak street patrol does not initiate formal records on a young smallfolk woman who shows correct minimal-legibility posture. Records begin when: (a) a complaint is lodged against the person by name or description, (b) the person is present at a flagged event, (c) the person is detained for any reason and a watch-house log entry is made, or (d) an anomalous behavior crosses the threshold for patrol-captain discretionary notation.

**The Hand of the King's administrative apparatus:**
At 125 AC, Otto Hightower holds or has recently held the Handship. The Hand's administrative office manages the city's governance at the executive level — tax rolls, guild oversight, census-adjacent records when conducted, special inquiries. Smallfolk do not interact with the Hand's apparatus directly unless a watch-house referral elevates to that level. Elevation occurs when: the Gold Cloak Commander receives a pattern-flagged entry from multiple watch captains, or an unusual-person investigation is initiated at petition, or the Hand's network flags an intelligence interest independent of watch reporting. Taylor's risk profile escalates toward Hand-level attention when she acquires the glass-candle (the tallow-chandler contact creates a traceable commercial interaction at a level unusual for her apparent stratum) and when insect-relay behavior becomes observable from outside the 300m cone.

**Master of Laws:**
The Master of Laws holds jurisdiction over criminal matters of sufficient severity that the watch-house cannot adjudicate locally. This is not a threshold Taylor reaches for behavioral deviation at stratum; she reaches it only if detained and charged with a crime that exceeds Gold Cloak summary jurisdiction (murder, sedition, crimes against Crown persons, practiced sorcery under the Faith's formal complaint mechanism). The practical path to Master of Laws attention is: watch-house log → Hand's office noting an intelligence interest → Master of Laws notified as jurisdictional authority for the formal investigation. Taylor's trajectory reaches this threshold in the s2-s3 period when the broken maester's documentation begins to cohere.

**The Faith of the Seven in King's Landing:**
The Great Sept of Baelor is under construction at this era; the Faith presence in KL is large, institutionally present, and operating in soft-power mode consistent with post-Faith-Militant accommodation. In KL specifically, the Faith maintains a larger charitable infrastructure than in a rural market town — alms for the poor, sick care, the Poor Fellows' successor organizations operating as charitable lay brothers (not armed). A septon in KL has more institutional weight than a rural septon (access to the High Septon's network, presence at court functions) and correspondingly more channels to transmit an anomalous-person report to someone who acts on it. Taylor's relationship to the Faith in KL runs through the charitable-access axis: a young woman who needs food, work-certification, or shelter has reasons to interact with the Faith's charitable arms. This is also an observation axis — the lay brothers who run the alms distribution know the faces of the people who come regularly. An irregular face is noted.

**No paramount-lord intermediary:**
There is no Tully suzerainty, no bannerman lord, no steward-as-administrative-face for the Crownlands smallfolk of King's Landing. The Crownlands are directly under the Iron Throne. This means: escalation moves faster (fewer administrative hops to reach Crown-level attention), the machinery is less patient (KL authority is city-scale, not rural-stable), and there is no local lord whose personal knowledge of a subject's household history provides informal cover. In the Riverlands config, Aldric Pryor knew every family in Fairstead. In KL, no one knows the families of Flea Bottom with that granularity; the Gold Cloaks know patterns and recurring faces, not household histories.

## Flea Bottom Tied/Landless Distinction

Flea Bottom is populated almost entirely by landless smallfolk — no indenture to a lord's land, no attached status through a recognized craft guild, no claim documented in any lord's record. The relevant distinction within Flea Bottom is between:

**Labor-attached:** A person working for a recognized employer (tannery, chandlery, dock authority, city guild affiliate) has a traceable day-labor or employment relationship. This is thin protection but it is something — a Gold Cloak who detains such a person has to account for their absence from an employer who may complain. Taylor acquires a thin labor-attached status through the tanner-family connection (she is working at the tannery in a semi-formal arrangement) and later through the tallow-chandler relationship. Neither is sufficient to constitute legal personhood; both are sufficient to give her a reason-to-be-present argument in a watch-house encounter.

**Wholly landless / unattached:** A person with no employer record, no family documentation, no guild contact, no demonstrable reason to be in KL at all. This is legally the most exposed stratum in the city. The Gold Cloaks have summary authority over unattached persons — they can detain, press into city labor gangs, or expel from the city. An unattached person who cannot account for themselves to a patrol captain's satisfaction has no procedural recourse except appeal to the Faith's charitable institutions. Taylor begins her KL period in this category and moves toward thin labor-attachment through the tanner-family.

## Taylor's Specific Legal Position — 17-Year-Old Unattached Female

At 17, unmarried, unattached, with no kin claim documented above her own person, Taylor occupies the most legally exposed smallfolk category the KL authority structure recognizes. The elements:

**Unattached female, working age:** A young woman of working age who is neither in documented service, nor married into a documented household, nor under a recognized guardian's formal custody, is a category the law does not protect. She has no claim on a lord's protection, no guild membership that confers rights, no husband's household that makes her legally visible. Her presence in the city is not illegal in itself, but her presence gives the Gold Cloaks no reason not to treat her as a wholly unattached person subject to summary authority.

**No kin claim above her own person:** In the customary authority framework, a smallfolk woman's primary legal visibility runs through her male relatives (father, husband, brother, recognized guardian). A woman who can name no such person is invisible to the protective apparatus and maximally visible to the coercive apparatus. Taylor's cover story positions her as the tanner-family's dependent — this is her primary kin-claim substitute. When that connection frays or is tested, she loses the thin protection it provides.

**Unmarried at 17:** At this age in this social world, an unmarried young woman is expected to be: in service in a recognized household, in the tanner-family's documented household, or seeking marriage. An unmarried young woman who is independently resident in Flea Bottom without a clear household attachment reads as fallen, widowed (with documentation), or — in the worst reading — a prostitute operating without guild registration. None of these categories are favorable for invisibility.

**What acts cross Taylor into Crown attention:**
- Detention at a watch-house for any reason, even if released without charge (creates a log entry with physical description)
- Complaint by name or description from a recognized person (the broken maester's documentation eventually produces this)
- Presence at a formally investigated event (a fire at the tallow-chandler's, a death that is investigated, any Gold Cloak action that produces names)
- Commercial transaction at a level anomalous for her apparent stratum (the glass-candle acquisition is the clearest example — glass candles are not Flea Bottom commodities)
- Demonstrable literacy in a formal encounter (signing a document, writing in view of an authority figure)
- Behavior that a watch captain reports as anomalous without a prior log entry (insect-behavior at visible scale crosses this threshold)

**What protections she has (effectively none beyond customary):**
Taylor has no legal protection that a Gold Cloak officer is required to honor beyond the customary limits on outright murder and permanent property seizure. The Gold Cloaks cannot permanently imprison without charge; they can hold for up to three days on suspicion. They cannot sell her into formal slavery (abolished under Aegon I); they can press her into temporary city labor. They cannot execute her without a conviction; they can beat her and release her without record if the encounter does not generate a complaint. Customary protection runs through social pressure (neighbors who will talk, the Faith's charitable monitoring, the employer who notes the absence) rather than legal right.

## Punishment Jurisdiction

**City Watch summary jurisdiction (Gold Cloak captain's discretion):**
- Fines for disorderly conduct, market-rule violations, unlicensed commercial activity
- Detention up to three days for unattached-person suspicion
- Expulsion from the city (escort to a gate and release) for persons with no demonstrable reason to be present
- City labor press for unattached persons of working age (rare; used during infrastructure projects or siege preparation)
- Physical correction for minor resistance during lawful detention — beatings administered without formal charge, not recorded as punishment, logged as "resistance during detention" if logged at all

**Watch-house referral to Hand's apparatus:**
- Persons whose detention generates an anomalous-person notation beyond what the captain can adjudicate (anomalous literacy, anomalous commercial connection, anomalous behavior that pattern-matches an intelligence interest)
- Persons detained multiple times — three or more watch-house entries creates a pattern-case referral in the Gold Cloak record-keeping system

**Master of Laws jurisdiction:**
- Crimes against Crown persons or Crown interests
- Practiced sorcery under formal Faith complaint (requires the High Septon's formal involvement or a maester's formal report through the Citadel-to-Hand channel)
- Murder, sedition
- At this threshold, the process includes a formal hearing, documentation, and potential forwarding to the King or the Hand for final disposition

**For auditor use:**
- Any scene implying the Faith can field armed investigators or compel detention is a fence violation (post-Faith-Militant accommodation holds at 125 AC)
- Any scene implying Taylor has enforceable legal rights against Gold Cloak summary action is a social-physics violation
- Any scene where Taylor is processed through a lord's-steward authority chain (rather than Gold Cloak → Hand) is an authority-architecture violation for KL scenes
- Any scene where Taylor's anomalous literacy or insect-behavior passes without Gold Cloak notation in a formal encounter is a plausibility violation
- Any scene implying a watch-house detention does not create a log entry is an administrative-realism violation

## Available Resistance Modes (KL Configuration)

**Urban-invisibility posture:** Move correctly for the environment — Flea Bottom pedestrian rhythm, not rural deference posture. Not stopping, not making eye contact, not pausing when a patrol passes. This is the base-state resistance mode; it keeps encounters from initiating.

**Labor-attachment claim:** In a watch encounter, naming an employer is the first defense. "I work at the tannery off the tallow-run" is a thin claim but it requires the officer to verify, which is effort, which ends encounters if the officer has no specific reason to press.

**Faith charitable access:** Presenting at a sept's alms line or shelter creates a documented contact point with a protective institution. The Faith does not detain people; it notes them and provides care. A person who is in Faith contact is not wholly unattached in the operative sense — but this protection is social, not legal.

**Delay:** As in the rural configuration — move slowly, need to fetch someone, have to speak to the employer first. Buys time without creating a record of active refusal.

**Disappear before contact:** Requires advance notice of a patrol's attention. Converts the problem into a location-problem (where is she?) rather than a status-problem. Higher risk than in a rural configuration because KL has multiple Gold Cloak districts and disappearance from one district does not mean disappearance from the city.

**Direct refusal:** Not viable. The Gold Cloaks have immediate coercive authority; refusal in a formal encounter results in force. The encounter then produces a log entry, a physical description, and a resisted-detention notation.

The Gold Cloak visibility threshold is lower than 300m — a patrol that sees insects behaving strangely in a 30m radius around a specific person will notice before the full range is deployed. Taylor's operational constraint is therefore tighter than the range ceiling in urban conditions.

**With `otto-hightower`:** The Hand's apparatus is the escalation destination for anomalous-person cases that exceed watch-house scope. Otto Hightower's intelligence-architect function means his apparatus is more actively interested in unusual persons than a passive administrative apparatus would be. A Taylor who shows up in Gold Cloak records gets faster attention from the Hand's level than she would under a less intelligence-minded Hand.
