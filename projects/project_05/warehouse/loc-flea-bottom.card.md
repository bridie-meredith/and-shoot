# Flea Bottom — District-Level Card (Working Copy)

Source of truth at cards/locations/loc-flea-bottom.card.md

## Geography

Poorest district of King's Landing. Lower ground south of Street of Steel, north of the Hook, running toward Fish Gate at southern wall.

Taylor's 300m operational radius at story open covers: full immediate block, Fish Gate margin, and into eastern quarter where broken maester's apothecary stands (~250m direct line).

## Key Notes

- **Fish Gate margin:** most transient part of district; s4 surviving subject lives here
- **Market-side junction:** tanner-elder's position; densest labor-web activity
- **Eastern quarter adjacent:** slightly less dense; apothecary at edge of this zone
- **Exits:** Fish Gate (south), Street of Steel (north), eastern quarter (east), Muddy Way (west)

## Active Hazards

- Density saturation constraint builds in late s2 as high-density operations accumulate
- City Watch makes periodic passes; not continuous surveillance
- Hightower intelligence apparatus active in s2 — Taylor detects watchers before they identify her
