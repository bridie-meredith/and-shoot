# Tanner Village — Crownlands (Working Copy)

Source of truth at cards/locations/loc-tanner-village.card.md

## Geography

Crownlands village, day's walk from King's Landing (~15–18 miles by road). Junction of secondary road and stream used for tanning. Smell reaches road before village.

Population ~80–100. Tanning families, small farming contingent, farrier.

## Key Notes

- **Ongoing cost surface** — not closed backstory. The tanner family's relationship to the girl is a live thread across seasons.
- **Lord's man** arrives quarterly for rent collection. Village events reach the lord's record.
- **Tanning chemistry** is a physical hazard; insect behavior around lime-pits is altered.
- **Exits:** Road north to KL (day's walk); road south to agricultural interior; stream path; secondary track west.
