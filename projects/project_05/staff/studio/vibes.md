# Season vibe-cloud — s01 (deltas from series)

## Deltas from series vibe-cloud

- **grimdark-register** → **first-season register**: costs are not yet detonating; they are being *catalogued*. The bill is being itemized, not paid. No closures yet — every transaction opens a tab the series will collect on later. Demonstration-not-triumph holds, but s01 is pre-demonstration: the *capability* is what accumulates, not the use.

- **faustian-pressure** → **research-onset, not research-eats-justification yet**: the line-crossings of s01 are small enough that the log can still hold them honestly. The drift starts here but is not visible to Taylor as drift. The "guise" is still operationally indistinguishable from the protection it claims.

- **clinical-self-erasure** → **calibration-still-aligned-with-world-baseline**: cond-clinical-self-erasure is active but the log omissions of s01 are smaller in magnitude than s02+. What stops being data in s01 is the village-debt surface (the Tya cost-line). The shard's gentle behavioral bias is present but reads as "preference" not "drift."

- **khepri-weight** → **present-but-sealed**: the mantle is felt as silence, not as pressure. Khepri is what Taylor refuses to consult on *anything* in s01 — including whether what she's doing now counts. No one else knows the weight exists.

- **solitude-as-permanent-condition** → **solitude-as-present-condition** (not yet permanent): no brake is named yet (the broken maester arrives mid-s01 / s02). What hardens in s01 is the habit of not-confiding; the structural impossibility of confidants comes later.

- **westerosi-superstition-frame** → **active-at-village-AND-Flea-Bottom**: the tanner-village reads Tya as "came back wrong"; Flea Bottom reads the bug-network as stranger-leavings before it reads it as protection. Two superstition frames running in parallel on the same body.

- **range-as-intimacy** → **range-as-expanding-front**: s01 is the season the range climbs from village-scale to Flea-Bottom-scale. The shard reseed is recent; every meter of new range is a fresh capacity. Range-arrivals are events.

## New / sharpened keys (s01-specific)

- **first-misreading-as-event**: the Hightower file opens on Taylor as a *miscategorization*; the precise wrong category is load-bearing for s03–s04. The file's existence is the s01 close-state.

- **the-village-claim-as-active-surface**: the tanner-village's grief is not background — it is an ongoing transactional surface; Tya's parents make claims on the body Taylor walks in. Recurs across the season, does not close.

- **organism-texture-as-novelty**: Flea Bottom's smallfolk political-physics (cond-smallfolk-political-physics) is *new* to Taylor's research register; she is calibrating to a substrate she has not worked before. The texture should feel observed-for-the-first-time, not assumed.

- **air-gap-as-baseline**: cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure is the s01 floor. No PRT briefings, no Dragon, no Tattletale. Capability rebuild is slow because the only multiplier was social network, and the network has to be built from nothing.

- **before-the-broken-maester**: the s01 mid/late arrival of the maester is a *new* sensory key (the eastern-quarter vertical, through-wall listening). Pre-arrival, the maester does not exist as a presence; post-arrival, he is a watcher who has not yet sharpened. The brake-that-does-not-stop-her is not yet operational because the brake has not yet seen enough to name anything.

- **the-file-opens-but-does-not-close**: the Hightower miscategorization is filed and *left*; no follow-up in s01. The season closes with the file existing, not with the file acted on. This is the structural fact the season hands to s02.
