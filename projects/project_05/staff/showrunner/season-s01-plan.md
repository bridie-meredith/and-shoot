# Season s01 plan — flea-bottom-dance

## Season chunk

Taylor's insect-range expands through Flea Bottom while the tanner-village's ongoing claim on the body she inhabits and the Hightower apparatus's first miscategorized intelligence file close simultaneously around her; the season forecloses the possibility of working unnamed — she is already a misread fact in someone else's record before she understands what she is building.

## Season drama

The tanner-village's claim on Tya's body and the Hightower apparatus's first miscategorized intelligence file close around Taylor on the same season-timeline, with neither knowing the other exists and neither closeable from inside. What cannot survive the season is the possibility of working unnamed: by season close, Taylor is already a misread fact in someone else's record before she understands what she is building, and the village's grief-debt remains an open transactional surface she cannot pay off.

## Structural commitments

- Two parallel arcs converging independently — village-claim arc (beats 1, 3, 4, 9, 13, 17, 23) and Hightower-file arc (beats 5, 18, 20, 25). Neither apparatus knows the other exists. Both close around Taylor by season end without resolving.
- Range expansion is a continuous spine — 300m → 330m → 400m → 500m → 600m across the season (beats 2, 11, 14, 19, 24), with physiological cost logged at each step.
- Broken maester arrives mid-season as ambient (beat 7), as anomalous variable (beat 15), then as named log entry (beat 16); his pen-scratch sharpens through beat 22 but does not name the line in s01.
- The Hightower file opens (beat 5, lord's man on quarterly pass) and stays open — neither apparatus closes it in s01. Final entry beat 25 is the file completing in the Hand's records under the wrong framework.
- Village grief-debt does not close — formalizes via customary wage claim (beat 13), externalizes into lord's-man record (beat 23), remains open transactional surface at season close.
- Khepri-mantle is felt as silence, not pressure — not consulted in any beat; not referenced; sealed.
- No capability demonstration to factions — range expansions self-observed and logged; no faction observes a demonstration.
- No titles offered yet — refusal posture operates only as unspoken refusal-of-being-seen.
- One POV: Taylor (taylor-hebert-flea-bottom).

## Content beats

Source-of-truth file (full beat prose): `active-project/staff/screen-writer/s01-content-beats-draft.md` (draft 2, 26 beats, converged 1e attempt 2).

Continuous numbered beats covering s01 arc start to end. Drama-sized. No episode delimiters. No slugs. No predicted episode count. Episode boundaries are an output of Phase 7 mechanical write-out, after Phase 3 review and Phase 6 verdict.

1. Taylor wakes in Tya's body three days post-fever; tanner-father runs behavioral-tell audit at the morning meal; the village category "Tya who came back wrong" closes before she has said ten words.
2. Taylor inventories 300m of insects from inside the tanner-family's yard; walks the perimeter twice in the first week; the shard runs local-only — no network, no multiplier bandwidth, no external reference.
3. The tanner-mother begins three notes of a private Tya-only song; Taylor does not finish it; mother's face closes on a new fact (whatever came back does not have the song) and the foreclosure from the mother's side completes — distinct mechanism from the father's tell-audit in beat 1.
4. The tanner-father reorders the labor distribution across four weeks so Taylor occupies a functional slot but touches nothing central; the withdrawal is structural fact before Taylor identifies its shape.
5. The village's "came back wrong" category reaches the lord's man on his quarterly pass as background information from a reeve; he records "tanner's daughter, fever-return, behavior irregular" before Taylor knows he visited — the file opens in the most minor possible register.
6. Taylor chooses King's Landing; tanner-elder places her in the Flea Bottom labor web as "reliable and strange" via trade-reference; she maps her 300m radius in 48 hours and identifies the eastern-quarter apothecary's upper room as a surface worth watching.
7. The 300m sphere catalogs Flea Bottom block, market-side junction, Fish Gate margin, apothecary upper room at 250m, broken maester's working pattern via south-wall beetle colony; everything beyond perimeter is dark — the dark is the primary operational fact of s01.
8. The dock-runner moves cargo through a shifted Watch pattern Taylor's network already knows; she does not warn, watches the runner recalibrate; two days later runner approaches via the tanner-elder for Watch-pattern information — first transactional exchange in Flea Bottom network; runner does not know what she is.
9. The tanner-family visits King's Landing; father registers the half-second strategic-scan Taylor does before turning to a speaker — a Flea-Bottom-specific tell with no village-frame category; mother is quieter on the walk home; family leaves with a new problem they cannot name.
10. Taylor maps the Flea Bottom whisper chain across three weeks of network-ambient listening; begins routing weather-pattern data and Watch-movement timing through the chain anonymously; chain accepts and transmits; she reads as "odd but functional" to Flea Bottom (stranger-leavings frame has not reached here yet).
11. Range expands ~30m in all directions during a sustained overnight network operation; threshold-event, not continuous; she marks new edges in her log with invented grid notation; physiological cost logged (headache, four-hour-onset, six-hour duration). Range is expanding.
12. A Flea Bottom family is evicted from a two-room dwelling for unpaid rent; Taylor's network covers the event in full; neighbors watch from doorways and do not intervene; she logs children's ages and rent debt with full notation — this entry will not exist in the log by s03 (a fact with no weight yet).
13. Tanner-family second visit; father states the family's customary wage-claim under Crownlands practice — accurate amount, accurate mechanism, no raised voice; Taylor makes partial payment; the transactional surface does not close, it formalizes; village-claim arc has an instrument.
14. Range crosses 400m during sustained autumn relay operation; new radius pushes north toward Street of Steel and northeast toward eastern quarter; log entry clinical (new perimeter, date, ambient conditions, headache duration); maester's upper room remains inside the radius with margin.
15. The broken maester's side-alley door is used at an unusual pre-light hour by an unidentified heavier-tread visitor; 40-minute low-register conversation; Taylor logs as anomaly in maester's access pattern; first time the maester registers as more than ambient data — he is now a variable.
16. The broken maester arrives in Taylor's operational picture as a named presence: chain-stripped by Citadel (not retired), keeping systematic record in his upper room; she cannot read the record but recognizes the rhythm of decades-organized information; logged as "subject: chain-stripped maester, eastern quarter, consistent high-density notation activity."
17. Tanner-family third visit, unannounced — mother alone, no trade goods; cold-months question, Taylor declines; mother reports she has stopped lighting the vigil candle, as practical decision not confession ("waiting for Tya was not the same as waiting for what came back"); the village-claim recurs without requiring formal mechanism, but the grief has changed shape — the vigil is over.
18. A Hightower-apparatus low-tier clerk maps the Flea Bottom labor web on a routine population-register pass; speaks to tanner-elder, records "trade-referenced from the Crownlands, reliable, strange, no known affiliations"; Taylor's network covers the exchange and logs as "lord's-apparatus intelligence contact, junction" — she does not know what apparatus.
19. Range crosses 500m in an incremental expansion through early winter as shard reseed stabilizes; broken maester's full vertical (upper room + ground-floor apothecary) now inside the radius; log specific (dates, perimeter markers, physiological cost per extension).
20. A second clerk asks the apothecary owner below the maester's rooms about unusual recent residents; describes a behavioral profile matching Taylor; the owner names her; the clerk records and leaves; Taylor's log: "second lord's-apparatus intelligence contact, eastern-quarter apothecary." The file entry will be categorized under the wrong framework — she has no path to knowing this. The possibility of being wholly unknown to the apparatus operating out of the Red Keep is gone.
21. Tanner-elder routes a task requiring Fish Gate Watch-pattern knowledge and invisible information-handoff via Flea Bottom labor web; Taylor accepts; routing the hand-off through coverage she controls requires burning through a portion of her dock-side insect cluster (passive observation surface); cluster will need weeks to reseed. Network-position upgrades from "placed by the elder" to "routed to by the network" — and the node that made it possible is now thinner.
22. The broken maester walks to an eastern-quarter market stall selling dried beetles and preserved specimens; speaks with stall-keeper about insect coordination anomalies in Flea Bottom-adjacent alleys; buys nothing, returns; pen-scratch continues six hours past usual stopping point; Taylor logs the event but does not record what she thinks the extended session means.
23. Tanner-father appears at the Flea Bottom labor-web junction without mother, alone, mid-trade-day, without goods; states to tanner-elder that the wage-claim is on customary record with the lord's man; elder routes information to Taylor. The village-claim has closed around her from the outside — open transactional surface and also a formal record she cannot access or modify.
24. Range reaches ~600m in late winter via a third extension event during a sustained overnight operation covering Fish Gate margin comprehensively; log records expansion, headache duration, specific new geography; maester's upper room now 200m inside sphere edge; Red Keep's outermost approach still 400m beyond her ceiling — the distance is known, the math is in her log, the log does not record the distance to the Red Keep.
25. A third more-senior operative in the outer-ring service approach of the Red Keep sends a messenger to the tanner-elder requesting a written account of the Crownlands girl's behavior and affiliations; elder delivers via middleman three days later; Taylor's network observes messenger and elder's response but cannot observe what is written or where it goes. The Hand's file completes under the wrong framework — she does not know the file exists, knows she is being recorded; the two are not the same. (PEAK)
26. Taylor walks the Flea Bottom perimeter three days after the junction messenger — full two-hour circuit, every alley, south-wall beetle colony; returns to base; writes two log entries side-by-side (village-claim in lord's-man record she cannot access; Hand's-apparatus contact in a file she cannot read); explicitly notes the two apparatuses have no knowledge of each other, are on different institutional tracks, are not cross-referencing; she has no model explaining the four-day simultaneity as anything other than coincidence; log does not speculate. The architecture has changed; she knows that; she does not know what file she is in. (DENOUEMENT — reader-level asymmetry registers what Taylor cannot.)
