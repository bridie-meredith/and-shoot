- **board-move:** Acknowledge the visitor's prefatory remark (whatever was said at @137), settle them into the room, and open the examination/consultation. The line transitions the encounter from greeting into the work the visit is for.
- **register state:** *at-home* (private upper room above the apothecary); chain-stripped; Citadel-trained voice *held selectively by reflex, not by claim*. The forms of institutional speech surface — double-affirmation tic, institutional-we, polite-brief cadence — without the maester reaching for the chain or the Conclave or the Citadel as named authority. He does not perform; the register is structural to how he thinks.
- **rung within card:** This speaker's load-bearing axis is *formal register held vs dropped*. The chain is gone but the cadence persists. He holds the cadence at the opening because the cadence is the working mode for an examination — it is the *only* gear he has for "let us see." - **distance:** professional-courteous. Not intimate; not dismissive. Consultation-distance.
- **public-vs-private:** private upper room; visitor + maester only (Taylor's beetles unknown to either party).

### Drafts

**Draft A (CHOSEN):**

```
"Yes, yes. Come, sit. Let us see what you have brought."
```

- Cadence: double-affirmation tic *yes, yes*; polite-brief / polite-brief / polite-brief stutter. Three short sentences, each contracted and courteous.
- Register hold: institutional *let us see* (institutional-we) deployed without claim — he says "let us" because that is the working mode; he is not claiming the institution.
- Opening-an-examination canonical pattern: the §Direct samples line *Yes, yes. Let us see.* is the load-bearing canonical phrase the card flags. Chosen draft preserves it verbatim at the spine ("Yes, yes" + "Let us see") with a sit-instruction inserted in between — the insertion is the at-home variant, the canonical core is intact.
- Restraint: no chain-grasping, no Citadel-naming, no my-lord, no folk-warmth, no emotional declaration, no hyperbole. The line is exactly what a chain-stripped maester in working-mode would say at a stair-top admission.

**Draft B (REJECTED):**

```
"Indeed. Sit, if you would. The matter — well, let me hear it set out plain."
```

Rejection: em-dash spine is Taylor-chassis contamination (rubric pattern 7 / explicit anti-pattern); "set out plain" leans toward simplification-warmth that flattens the Citadel register, weakening the §Cadence anchor; the line fires §Vocabulary only weakly (single "indeed" without the doubled tic).

**Draft C (REJECTED):**

Rejection: over-commits the scene by presuming a document (the proto-line bones don't license that load); reintroduces em-dash chassis; the "if there is a page" subjunctive is in-register but the scene-load it carries (an inferred page-on-table) is not in the bones and would force downstream facets to play to it.
