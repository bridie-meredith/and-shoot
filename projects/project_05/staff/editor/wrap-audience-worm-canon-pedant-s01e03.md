# Audience Wrap Review — s01e03 — worm-canon-pedant

## Flags

flag-001 "the way it used to leave before the network went"
  paragraph: line 59 — "the way it used to leave before the network went"
  category: voice
  severity: high
  suggestion: "the network went" has a double register. In-episode it could mean the insect-network going dormant overnight, but the displacement-clamp framing of the sentence ("the way it used to leave before") marks this as a prior-life body-memory comparison — and "before the network went" in that register reads as Gold Morning / Entity-collapse memory bleeding through the prose surface. The displacement-clamp discipline requires Taylor's prior-life experience to surface as body-knowledge without Worm-proper-noun tokens — this avoids named tokens but carries the correct semantic weight of the collapse event anyway, which is the fence the discipline exists to prevent. Rephrase to anchor the comparison to something anatomically or procedurally grounded in Westeros experience, or excise the comparison clause and let the breath-left-before-the-answer stand without the justifying subordinate.

flag-002 "the interior had the count of years for what the seat above the city was going to be"
  paragraph: line 65 — "the interior had the count of years"
  category: confusion
  severity: high
  suggestion: "the interior" has no legible antecedent in this sentence. The sentence reads: she faces the Red Keep, and "the interior had the count of years for what the seat above the city was going to be." Interior of what — the Red Keep, the city, Taylor herself? The intended meaning (Taylor's body-memory carrying the weight of what a seat of power above a city will become, referencing prior-life knowledge of such architectures of authority) is the right displacement-clamp move, but it misfires because "the interior" parses most naturally as the Red Keep's interior, which produces a nonsense reading: "the Red Keep's interior had the count of years for what the Red Keep was going to be." Rewrite to make the locus of the body-knowledge explicit — "the body had the count" or "something in the chest had the count" anchors the displacement-clamp to Taylor's interior, not the building's.
