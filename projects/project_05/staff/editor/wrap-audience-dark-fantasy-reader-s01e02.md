## Summary
total flags: 14 (of 28 entries; 14 anchors noted)
by category: pacing=3, variance=3, voice=4, entertainment=2, confusion=2

high-level concerns:
The clinical-self-erasure register the OQ-3 watch demanded is broadly landing — log-trio refrain, fauna-relay-refrain, cardinal-quartet, three-note buildup are doing architectural work, and the closing "closed it on a name" + the mother's "Whatever you are, you are warm" are the episode's earned peaks. The grimdark register holds at the structural level.

Two recurring execution problems weaken the texture: (1) **inline glosses** (Fish Gate em-dash, customary-wage parenthetical, vigil-candle em-dash) read as tutorial inserts that break first-person POV and steal weight from load-bearing dramatic beats — the customary-wage gloss specifically interrupts the wage-precedent peak. (2) **generic procedural verbs** (pivoted, turned, possessions, trade goods, placement-look) leave gaps where Westeros-specific texture should sit; in a first-person POV that's supposed to feel like a Crownlands tanner-girl operating in King's Landing, the prose currently hovers in a third register that reads neither feudal-medieval nor clinically-modern.

The tanner-family scenes (A and I) carry the highest grimdark stakes and are the weakest at the sentence level — Taylor's dialogue formulas ("Goodman. I have not forgotten." / "Goodwife. The road is yours.") are inert where the Tya-shaped-debt requires the wrongness to be audible in her speech. The eviction scene (H) and the maester naming scene (K-M) are doing this work well; the family scenes need a closer pass.

No catharsis-creep flagged — the episode does not offer a relief beat and the closing log-line correctly refuses to.
