### L19 — lord's-man / his man stutter (pronoun antecedent fuzz)

### L23 — stylus-drop sequencing (cause/effect inverted)
**Personas:** cape-fic-reader, worm-canon-pedant

### L25 — visitor / maester pronoun reference (Scene K)
**Personas:** cape-fic-reader, worm-canon-pedant
**Sentence:** `The visitor entered the side alley, then the stairwell, then the upper room. ... He went out by the upper room, by the stairwell, by the side-alley door.`

Let us see what you have brought."), making "he" read as the maester for a moment.
