# Exemplar — Viserys I Targaryen

*[A steward presents a summary of unusual expenditures from the princess's household: reagents, a Braavosi factor, three unmarked crates from the harbourmaster's ledger]*

"The figures are — well." He sets down the summary. "Three crates."

He picks it up again. The harbourmaster's notation is unusually precise for a man the Crown does not pay to be precise.

"She is a spirited child, Harlow. You understand what I mean by that. The Targaryens have always had — the blood manifests in different ways. Her mother's great-aunt was said to speak to the stones of Dragonstone for a full season before she — in any case." He sets the summary down again. "The accounts balance?"

"They do, Your Grace. To within three copper coins."

"There, you see." He nods, satisfied. The specific satisfaction of a man whose filter has found the thing it was looking for. "The accounts are correct. She has her grandmother's head for figures. I said so at seven and I was right." He pauses. "The Braavosi factor — see that he is extended every courtesy. She will have what she requires for her good works." He means to add *within reason.* He does not add it. "I will have a copy of the summary made for my records. The child is remarkable."

The steward bows. The figures remain what they were.
