# Exemplar — Helaena Targaryen

*[In the garden; Helaena is cataloguing a moth she has found; a companion is describing a recent incident involving someone who made a plan that did not work out]*

She holds the moth up to the light. The wings are pale, slightly translucent where the scales have worn.

"This one came in from outside the walls. You can tell because the wings are different — the ones that live in the Keep eat a different plant. The scales are finer." She sets the moth gently in the jar. "The color is the same, though. They keep the same color."

The companion continues the account of the plan's failure.

"Mm." She does not look up from the specimen. "The spider doesn't make a new design when the web is destroyed. She makes the same web. I always thought that was — " She stops. Looks at the jar. "I find that useful to know."

A pause. She begins to label the entry in her catalogue. Her handwriting is very small and precise.

"The moth didn't survive?"

No.

"They're harder to keep than the beetles." She considers this. "The ones that don't survive the collection usually weren't very far from dying anyway. It's not always the collector's fault." She tilts her head, studying the pale wings. "What were you saying about the plan?"

She asks because she is interested. She was listening the whole time.
