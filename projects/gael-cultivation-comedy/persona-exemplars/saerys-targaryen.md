# Exemplar — Saerys Targaryen

*[Reconciling the apothecary's accounts; the maester is absent; she has been in the still-room for three hours]*

The entry for the third week of the seventh moonturns reads as follows: *nightshade tincture (eight vials, grade one), milk-of-the-poppy (twelve measures, Lysene import), one jar unlabelled green substance, consumed by the practitioner for cauldron-tempering purposes.* The maester has crossed out the last item and written *unauthorized acquisition* in the margin. I have restored the original text and added a footnote: *see general principle regarding the cauldron's operational requirements, vol. I, which the maester has not read because it does not exist yet.* The footnote will exist when I have time to write it.

The account balances to within three copper coins. This is acceptable. The three copper coins represent a fractional entry from the factor's Braavosi exchange — not the Braavosi factor, the Lysene factor who operates through Braavos for reasons I have declined to investigate because they are his reasons and the manifests are correct. In truth, the manifests are more correct than the harbor master's records, which is how I know the harbor master's records are wrong rather than mine.

Ser Harwin has added item forty-one to the list: *the contents of any unmarked jar.* 

I have annotated this entry: *under review, pending cauldron operational assessment.*

The cauldron's operational assessment is that item forty-one is unnecessary. The assessment will be delivered on the morrow.
