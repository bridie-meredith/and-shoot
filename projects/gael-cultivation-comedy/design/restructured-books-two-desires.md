# Restructured spine — the two desires (run-02, principal directive)

**Principal note (re-axis):** *"Each book needs to be focused on acquiring resources and
freedom to be left alone to cultivate. The conflict needs to be against these two desires.
Focus less on other stuff."*

This supersedes the FRAMING of the converged outlines (`convergence/round-03/fusion-v2.md`,
`convergence/chapters/round-02/fusion.md`) — the mechanics (Cauldron-Belly, poison-path, three
Locks, curdle ladder, heist, the one break) survive and are RE-FRAMED, not torn down. What
changes is what each book is *about* and what gets foregrounded vs. demoted.

---

## The engine

Saerys wants exactly two things, and only these:
1. **RESOURCES** — reagents to cultivate (an escalating ladder).
2. **TO BE LEFT ALONE** — solitude/freedom to cultivate, undisturbed.

**The conflict is against these two desires.** The world (a) withholds the resources and
(b) refuses to leave her alone. And the spine's secret engine: **the two desires fight each
other** — acquiring resources requires *engaging* the world (costing solitude); protecting
solitude requires *withdrawing* (costing acquisition). Her whole apparatus — the sick-house,
the factors, the paper, the poison, the empire — is **one machine built to resolve that
tension:** make resources flow to her *while keeping the world at arm's length.* Everything
she builds is, at bottom, an elaborate "do not disturb" sign with a supply chain attached.

### Demoted to pressure / cost (the "other stuff")
- **Relationships (Daenys, Harwin, Nymeria, Helaena)** — no longer a foregrounded romance /
  found-family / grief plot. They are **ties that threaten her solitude, and people she spends
  (or loses) to her two desires.** They are the *price the engine extracts,* surfacing only
  where they bill. (Pull any back up on request.)
- **The Dance / faction politics** — no longer foregrounded plot. It is **the maximum
  obstruction:** the event that delivers the ultimate resource and makes being-left-alone
  impossible, at once.
- **The grief theme** — survives strictly as the *cost* of the two desires; it is loudest at
  the one break, and otherwise stays under the comedy.

### Antagonist pressure, sorted by which desire it attacks
- **Against SOLITUDE:** the septa & handlers → the marriage market / betrothal → her mother
  Alicent & Otto (who want to *use* her) → the factions (who forbid a useful neutral to stay
  neutral). The world keeps trying to claim her time, her body, her allegiance.
- **Against RESOURCES:** the watched fortress & the guarded Dragonpit → rarity, cost, distance
  (Essos/Yi Ti) → the Alchemists' Guild gatekeeping → and finally the cruel fact that the best
  reagent (dead dragons) only comes from catastrophe.

## BOOK I — *scraps & a locked door* (the Red Keep · age 3–9 · farce)
- **RESOURCE goal:** Tier-0 — scavenge eggshell / dragonglass / weirwood / dung; raid the
  still-room for poisons. Temper the cauldron.
- **SOLITUDE goal:** carve unsupervised hours out of a fortress where a princess is *never alone.*
- **Conflict (against both):** the household — septa, lessons, marriage-grooming, the cage of
  princess-life — denies her time *and* her access. The reagents are right there (the Dragonpit,
  the apothecary) and she is *watched.*
- **The two desires at war:** to get supply + solitude she must *engage* (charm the maester,
  found the sick-house, buy the below-stairs) — every tool she builds makes her more visible.
- **Resolution / LOCK I:** she invents the **two-desire machine in miniature** — the charity
  sick-house (solitude + lab + supply at once, under unattackable piety) and the **sealed
  King's-hand note** (legal ungovernability = the ultimate "leave me alone"). *Paper makes her
  unbotherable in the Red Keep.* Both desires won, at local scale.
- **Cost (demoted):** the first "pest" poisoned (a threat to the machine, removed); the small
  warm ties (Helaena, the laundress's boy) are planted as things she'll later spend — present,
  not foregrounded.

## BOOK II — *far supply & a wider perimeter* (the roads · age 9–13 · adventure with teeth)
- **RESOURCE goal:** local scraps exhausted; she needs Tier-2/3 reagents (Essos → one far reach,
  Yi Ti).
- **SOLITUDE goal:** defend her autonomy at scale — the **betrothal** (a real marriage = the
  maximum violation of "leave me alone") and her own usefulness (everyone now wants to claim the
  medicine / trade / poison) close in.
- **Conflict (against both):** the far resources can only be reached by *engaging the world*
  (travel, trade, factors, an entourage) — every reach for supply *costs solitude*; and every
  success makes her more valuable, drawing more claims on her person. **The two desires are at
  open war this whole book.**
- **Resolution / LOCK II:** she perfects the **remote-acquisition machine** — the cross-ocean
  paper extraction proves she can pull *any* resource to herself through paper and factors,
  *without being present or claimable.* "Acquire without being disturbed," solved at world scale.
- **Cost (demoted):** Harwin dies as the price of *reach* (needing people to extend her grasp);
  the betrothal is the book's antagonist; Viserys — the shield over her solitude — is dying on
  the last page.

## BOOK III — *the motherlode & the only true solitude* (the Dance · age 13–15 · tragedy→cold flight)
- **RESOURCE goal:** the ultimate reagent — the Dance kills dragons (the motherlode, finally
  available); she reaches for the forbidden black stone (Tier 4).
- **SOLITUDE goal:** absolute freedom — the war makes neutrality impossible; both factions refuse
  to let the useful princess be left alone. The only solitude left is *off the map entirely.*
- **Conflict (against both):** the war hands her the resources she always wanted **at the exact
  moment it makes being-left-alone impossible,** and strips away everyone who shielded her. The
  two desires reach terminal form: unlimited supply is available, but only *escape* buys solitude.
- **Resolution / LOCK III (the heist / ending):** she steals the ship on paper and **sails off
  the edge of the world** — the total satisfaction of *both* desires at once: maximum resources
  (the ship, the reagents, the black-stone horizon) **and** absolute solitude (beyond every claim,
  marriage, faction). *She gets exactly what she wanted.*
- **The thesis (re-axed ending):** the total fulfillment of "resources + to be left alone" is
  **indistinguishable from total isolation.** She wins both desires completely — and whether that
  is victory or tomb is the open question. The deaths were the *price* she paid; the blank-line
  break is the one moment the cost of "left alone" becomes visible even to her.

## What this changes in the chapter outline (re-axis, not teardown)
**Survives (re-framed):** the Cauldron-Belly, poison-path, mithridatism, the three Locks (each
Lock = winning *freedom* at a rising level: household → world → Crown+death), the resource ladder
(the *acquisition* arc), the curdle, the one break, the paper-heist. The PLANT/FIRE causality is
intact.

**Reweight (the actual edit):** make every book's A-plot visibly **get-the-resource /
defend-the-solitude**, with relationships and war as the *cost/pressure* lane, not a parallel
plot. Concretely:
- Foreground each book's **resource target** and **solitude-threat** in its opening and climax.
- Compress the foregrounded-relationship chapters (II.3 Daenys's hall; III.6 Daenys's war) so
  they read as *claims-on-her-solitude / costs-of-reach,* not romance beats.
- Re-pitch the entourage chapters around **function** (they exist to extend her reach for
  resources) over found-family warmth.
- Re-pitch the Dance chapters around **the assault on her supply + her solitude** over faction
  detail (the "supply-chain disruption" gag is already exactly this — lean in).
- The two-desire **tension** becomes the explicit through-line: every chapter, she's trading
  some solitude for some supply, or vice versa, and resenting the trade.
