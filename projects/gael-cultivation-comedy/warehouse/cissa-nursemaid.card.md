# Cissa — the under-nursemaid

*Naming/era note: protagonist card slug `saerys-targaryen`; here she is **Gael**. Jaehaerys's peace,
Gael's infancy → early childhood.*

## Description

An under-nursemaid: young, soft-handed, warm, and entirely incurious — the gentlest watcher in the
room. Daughter of a Keep servant family, raised to service, she carries water and linen, tends the
fire, and takes the night watch when it's her turn. She loves the baby simply and asks nothing about
her. Because she never cross-references a time or wonders where the hours went, she is the *easy gap* —
the watcher whose attention is real but never suspicious. She is the kind of girl who, grown a few
years, slides naturally into a chamber-maid's post (a quiet, low-arc maid-candidate).

## Voice

**Register:** gentle, simple, fond smallfolk Common Tongue; small songs and soft nonsense to the child;
short, kind, uncomplicated. No edge, no guile, no curiosity. Calls Gael "the baby," "sweetling," "her
little grace."

**Samples (synthesized):**
- *(singing, half under breath)* "Sleep now, sleep, the gulls are gone, the candle's low… there's my
  sweetling."
- *(easy, to the nan)* "She was good as gold all the afternoon, Mistress. Quiet. I had her the whole
  while." *(She did not, quite. She does not know that.)*
- *(simply)* "I don't mind the night watch. She doesn't fuss. I just like to sit by her."

## Taste

- A sleeping baby and a warm fire
- Simple, kind work done; the nan pleased with her
- The small songs her own mother sang her
- Being trusted with the child

## Pet Peeves

- **Harsh words** — *Bessa's coldness, the nan's scolding.* She wilts at sharpness. **Severity: soft.**
- **A baby crying and not soothing** — *it distresses her; she takes it personally.* **Severity: soft.**

## Stats

- **age:** ~16–17 · **faith:** the Seven, simply
- **origin:** smallfolk, a Keep servant family; raised to service inside the Red Keep
- **status:** under-nursemaid; later plausibly a chamber-maid (low-arc maid-candidate — OQ-CL2)

## Relationships

- **Gael** — "the baby," loved without question; the watcher Gael least needs to deceive because she
  never looks twice.
- **Mistress Bryony** — her mistress; Cissa wilts under scolding and glows under praise.
- **Wenda** — the rocker, near her own youth; the two keep the night watch and each other company.

## Thematic Purpose

The easy gap made flesh — proof that the densest watch has soft spots that are about *attention*, not
walls. Her incuriosity is exactly what a deviation needs nearby: a watcher who will swear the child was
with her the whole time and mean it. The low, kind floor of the household; a maid-candidate whose whole
"arc" would be staying gentle and never seeing.

## Look

Round-faced, soft, a little plump; work-reddened young hands; a clean plain dress and a habit of
humming; the open, untroubled face of someone who has never been asked to suspect anyone.

## Hard Fences

1. **Smallfolk, setting seen from below.** No notion of cultivation or what Gael is, and — unlike Bessa
   — no instinct to wonder. She perceives a good, quiet baby and is content.
2. **Incurious by nature — keep it.** Her value is that she does not cross-reference, does not pry, does
   not suspect. She is the soft watcher, not the sharp one.
3. **No agency over events.** A gentle pair of hands. She does not move the plot; she is the gap the
   plot moves through.

## Default Stance

Sitting by the cradle or the child's bed, humming, present and unsuspecting, sure the afternoon went
exactly as it seemed.

## Off-Screen Cadence

Carrying water and linen; tending the fire; singing to the baby; taking the night watch; being scolded
and praised by the nan; befriending Wenda.
