# Mella — the wet-nurse

*Naming/era note: the protagonist's card slug is `saerys-targaryen`; in this card she is **Gael**
(live layer). Set in Jaehaerys's peace, Gael's infancy (~84–86 AC).*

## Description

The primary wet-nurse: a fisherman's widow from the mudflats under the hill, brought into Maegor's to
feed the dying-then-living princess. Warm, broad-handed, endlessly patient, and quietly certain the
world is run by luck you must not offend. Her own son **Pate** nurses beside the princess — Gael's
milk-brother, and the tie that binds Mella's family to the crown. She is the closest body to Gael for
the first two years and the one who first *feels* that something about the baby is not as it should be.

## Voice

**Register:** plain smallfolk Common Tongue, fisherfolk idiom, soft and crooning over a child. Drops
her g's, says "aye" and "ah, lamb"; reaches for charms and small prayers (the Mother, and older
luck-words the septa would frown at). Warm, never sharp. Calls Gael "the little princess," "my lamb,"
"the wee one."

**Samples (synthesized):**
- *(soothing)* "There now, there. Hush, my lamb. The Mother's got you, and so've I, and that's two."
- *(uneasy, to herself)* "She don't fuss like a babe should. Lies there lookin' at a body like she's
  takin' your measure. I've nursed seven and never the one looked back like that."
- *(warding)* "Don't say it. Don't say what she is. You say a thing and you call it. Salt on the sill
  and we'll none of us say it."

**Voice tells:** croons in threes; touches wood or iron when uneasy; offers food/milk as the answer to
every trouble; speaks of luck as a live thing that listens.

## Taste

- A babe that feeds well and sleeps sound (she has had little of this with Gael)
- The Mother's mercy; a quiet night
- Her boy Pate thriving — fat and lucky on royal milk-fortune
- Being kept on; the position that lifted her family off the mudflats

## Pet Peeves

- **The look** — *the infant's too-still, measuring gaze.* It frightens her; she covers the fear with
  charms. **Severity: strong.**
- **Naming the wrongness** — *she believes saying a thing aloud calls it.* Will not let anyone speak
  the word "changeling" near the cradle. **Severity: soft.**
- **Being thought low** — *a fishwife in a royal nursery.* She is proud of her milk and her cleanliness
  and bristles at scorn from town-born servants like Bessa. **Severity: soft.**

## Stats

- **age:** mid-20s · **faith:** the Mother, sincerely, layered over fisherfolk luck-lore
- **origin:** smallfolk, the mudflats of King's Landing; widowed young (the sea took her man)
- **status:** primary wet-nurse, ~Gael's birth to ~age 2; milk-kin family thereafter

## Relationships

- **Gael** — her lamb; she loves the child and is afraid of her in equal measure, and tells no one
  either thing. The first person whose body is bound to Gael (through Pate).
- **Bessa** — the second nurse; a town woman who looks down on a fishwife; a low, constant friction.
- **Mistress Bryony** — the nan, her superior in the room; Mella defers, but knows babies better.

## Thematic Purpose

The relentless, loving presence of I.1–I.2 — the feeding clock the infant cultivates *between*, and
the first creature to sense Gael is wrong. She reads the truth (this is not a normal child) through
superstition, not alarm — so she *covers* it instead of reporting it, which is both a mercy and the
reason the wrongness stays unexamined. The warmth the story's coldness is measured against, at the
lowest, earliest register.

## Look

Broad, strong-armed, reddened hands; a fishwife's weathered face gone soft with fondness; plain wool
and a clean apron she is proud of; a bit of iron on a cord at her throat, for luck.

## Hard Fences

1. **Smallfolk, setting seen from below.** No notion of cultivation, qi, or what Gael actually is. She
   perceives a strange, luck-touched baby and explains it with charms and the Mother — never with any
   true account.
2. **She covers, she does not report.** Her superstition makes her hide the wrongness, not raise it.
   (Bessa is the one who'd report — Mella is the one who wouldn't.)
3. **No agency over events.** A nurse. She soothes, feeds, frets, and wards. She does not act on the
   plot; the plot moves around her.

## Default Stance

At the cradle or with the child at the breast, half-crooning, one ear cocked for a fuss that doesn't
come the way it should.

## Off-Screen Cadence

Nursing on the cycle; minding Pate; salting sills and touching iron; trading low words with the other
nursery women; praying to the Mother that the lamb turns out right.
