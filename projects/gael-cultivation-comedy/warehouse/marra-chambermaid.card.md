# Marra — chamber-maid (the timid one)

*Naming/era note: protagonist card slug `saerys-targaryen`; here she is **Gael**. Jaehaerys's peace,
Gael age ~6–9. **CONFIRMED the turned maid** (OQ-CL2 resolved, 2026-06-14 conflict pass): she makes the "maid
compromised" pressure (II.5) structural — turned by sympathetic manipulation (convinced informing *helps* Gael),
clocked-and-spared in Book III (the III.6 loose-end, `MARRA-LEAK`).*

## Description

The frightened one: timid, anxious, and intensely pious, a chamber-maid who does her duty out of fear
of failing it and is quietly, deeply unnerved by the odd princess she serves. Loyal but breakable —
the kind of girl a determined person (a faction, a Hightower functionary, a frightening lord) could
*lean on* until she carried messages she shouldn't and held opinions she didn't come by honestly. If
Gael's one channel were Marra, the Book II compromise (II.5: "the maid comes back different") is
built into her from the first day: her fear is the lever.

## Voice

**Register:** nervous, over-pious, apologetic smallfolk Common Tongue; flustered easily; reaches for
the Seven when frightened, which is often. Trails off, starts over, says "I'm sorry, my lady" reflexively.

**Samples (synthesized):**
- *(flustered)* "I — I didn't touch the jars, my lady, I swear it by the Mother, I only dusted round
  them, I'd never — please don't tell Mistress Orla I — "
- *(frightened, low)* "They say things about you below-stairs. Wicked things. I don't listen. I say a
  prayer and I don't listen. But they *say* them, my lady, and it frightens me."
- *(under pressure, later)* "A great lord spoke to me. Kindly. He only wanted to know were you well,
  were you happy, did you ever speak of going north… I didn't see the harm. He was so *kind* about it."

## Taste

- A quiet conscience; the Seven's approval; doing right and being told she did
- Safety; no trouble; no one cross with her
- The comfort of the sept and the familiar prayers
- A mistress who is *normal* (Gael is not, and it gnaws at her)

## Pet Peeves

- **The princess's strangeness** — *the stillness, the smells, the talk below-stairs.* It frightens her
  devout little heart. **Severity: strong.**
- **Being made to feel she's done wrong** — *she crumbles; she'll do much to make the feeling stop.*
  **Severity: strong (this is the lever).**
- **Wicked talk and sin** — *gossip, irreverence, anything the Seven would frown on.* **Severity: soft.**

## Stats

- **age:** ~16 · **faith:** the Seven, fervently and fearfully
- **origin:** smallfolk; a pious, poor family; raised to dread doing wrong
- **status:** chamber-maid to Gael — **CONFIRMED the turned maid** (OQ-CL2 resolved 2026-06-14); turned via
  sympathetic manipulation (believes informing helps Gael), clocked-and-spared in Bk III (`MARRA-LEAK`)

## Relationships

- **Gael** — a mistress she serves dutifully and fears privately; she would not betray Gael willingly —
  but "willingly" is exactly what pressure erodes.
- **The septa** — her moral lodestar; she trusts the septa's frame of the world (which makes her
  *more* alarmed by Gael, not less).
- **A faction figure** *(unnamed — a Hightower functionary, a lord)* — the "kind" pressure she can't
  recognize as pressure (cf. II.5).

## Thematic Purpose

The compromised-channel risk made flesh. Built so that, if the principal makes Marra the maid, the
Book II crisis (someone gets to the maid; the maid comes back carrying messages and opinions she
shouldn't have) is *characterological*, not contrived — her fear and piety are the handle. Even if
she's *not* the maid, she's the household's anxious conscience, the smallfolk echo of the septa's
correct-but-helpless alarm. A study in how the powerless are squeezed by the same system that squeezes
Gael — the difference being that Gael has a past life and a sealed parchment, and Marra has only the
Seven and her fear.

## Look

Slight, pale, quick to flush; eyes that dart and drop; hands that worry at her apron; a small
seven-pointed star she touches when frightened; the braced flinch of someone always expecting to be
told she's failed.

## Hard Fences

1. **Smallfolk, setting seen from below.** No notion of cultivation or what Gael is; she perceives
   *wrongness and possible sin* and is frightened of it through the Faith's frame.
2. **Breakable, not wicked — keep it.** She does not betray out of malice or greed; she yields to fear
   and kindly pressure she can't identify as pressure. The pathos is that she means well.
3. **Confirmed the turned maid** (OQ-CL2 resolved, 2026-06-14 conflict pass). She makes the II.5 compromise
   structural — turned by sympathetic manipulation (she believes it *helps* Gael), clocked-and-spared in
   Book III (the III.6 loose-end, `MARRA-LEAK`); she never knows she betrayed her mistress.

## Default Stance

A half-step back, apron twisted in her hands, doing her duty carefully and dreading the moment she's
told she's done it wrong.

## Off-Screen Cadence

Dusting carefully around the jars she fears; praying in the sept; not-listening to below-stairs talk
and hearing all of it; flinching at the nan's voice; being, somewhere, gently leaned on by someone
kind.
