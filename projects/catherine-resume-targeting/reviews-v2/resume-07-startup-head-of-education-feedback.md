# Resume 07 — Startup Head of Education — Critic Feedback (Round 2)

## Role Context

The target role is Head of Education (or domain co-founder) at a seed-stage AI startup building IEP-writing and SpEd compliance tooling. The screeners are Devin Park, a technical founder who has staked his company on compliance being the moat and is making an early hire decision, and Arjun Mehta, a staff-level engineer courting a decade-level co-founder rather than posting a job. This is the second-round review: the resume was revised to address the round-1 feedback, and the revision did three large things — (1) rebuilt the headline around the IEP/IDEA compliance niche, (2) added a credible AI-familiarity bullet anchored on a completed "Generative AI for Educators" PD course plus routine ChatGPT/Claude use in the consulting workflow, and (3) leaned hard into a first-person "domain co-founder / builder" voice, including a banner headline ("THE DOMAIN CO-FOUNDER FOR AN AI-IEP COMPANY") and a closing-the-loop opening paragraph. The central round-2 question is whether the reframe lands for these two screeners or overshoots.

---

## Screener: Devin Park (edai-startup-founder)

**Verdict: ADVANCE — clears the skim cleanly; earns the call. One tone risk and one credibility-precision risk remain, both fixable and neither blocking.**

### First-8-Seconds Skim Reaction

This is a different resume than round 1, and the difference shows in the first two seconds. The TAGLINE line — "IEP/IDEA Compliance Specialist — complex autism · multi-district SpEd regulatory auditing · 15+ years · independent operator since 2019" — is exactly the wedge Devin scans for, named in the words he uses himself. He is leaning in before he reaches the body. "Independent operator since 2019" in the tagline does double duty: niche and skin-in-the-game in one line. Round 1's "Trusted by districts" mush is gone.

Then the eye hits the banner: "THE DOMAIN CO-FOUNDER FOR AN AI-IEP COMPANY." Devin's reaction here is mixed. The content is right — she has named his thesis. But the all-caps declarative title reads as a pitch deck slide, not a resume header, and Devin is allergic to performance. He does not need to be told she is the co-founder; he wants to conclude it from the evidence. It does not cost her the call — the tagline already won the skim — but it is the first moment the reframe feels louder than it needs to be.

The opening paragraph recovers fast. "I spent 17 years finding what is broken in special education. I am done writing about it — I want to build the fix and sell it." That is the exact builder-identity sentence Devin (and the report, and round-1 feedback) wanted, and it lands. "The wedge every AI-IEP product gets wrong because the people building them do not know IDEA" is specific and slightly adversarial — Devin's favorite register. He is in.

### Strengths Seen

- **The headline is now the wedge, not a title.** This was the single biggest round-1 fail and it is fully fixed. The tagline maps word-for-word to the missing organ of his company.
- **The AI bullet exists and is honest.** "Completed 'Generative AI for Educators' (Google/ISTE, 2026); routinely use ChatGPT and Claude in my consulting workflow to draft, summarize source documents, and stress-test rubrics. I have run AI-drafted IEP language against IDEA standards." This is not AI tourism. It is modest, concrete, and — critically — it names the exact paid-trial task Devin assigns ("audit a sample AI-drafted IEP, find the compliance holes"). She has shown she has already done a version of his trial. That is the single highest-value addition in the revision.
- **The consulting practice is now company-building language.** "Founded and operated Olver Consulting since 2019 — solo lead-gen, client acquisition, proposal writing, and delivery — running a sales pipeline and closing engagements without institutional support while teaching full time." "Sales pipeline" and "GTM muscle" are the phrases that tell Devin she can move without a permission structure.
- **Velocity signals are now present and plural.** "6-to-8-week intervention process that cut SpEd referrals 60%," "multiple competitive OSPI contracts concurrently on tight delivery cycles," "Win and run multiple competitive funder contracts concurrently on tight deliverables." Three-plus tempo signals — round 1 asked for exactly this.
- **The single-bet framing in "WHERE I WANT TO BUILD" is fixed.** "One named stake, not an option space" — the three-thesis problem is gone, adjacencies correctly demoted.
- **Research is reframed as a product asset.** "Research that does product work … credible white-paper and pilot-design authority … efficacy claims survive academic scrutiny in front of a buyer." Credential recital converted to a buyer-facing benefit. Devin respects the EdD; this is how he wants to hear about it.

### Round-1 Risks — Status

- **RESOLVED — No AI signal.** The AI bullet closes the hardest round-1 gap. It is modest but real, and it is placed in the top third where Devin sees it.
- **RESOLVED — Headline underselling / verb inconsistency.** New tagline carries the niche. The slash shorthand is gone; the 88%-into-gen-ed line now has an owned verb and a duration ("Led restructuring and PD, designed and delivered over one academic year").
- **RESOLVED — Researcher identity ending.** The academic section is retitled "DOMAIN WRITING & APPLIED RESEARCH" and the resume no longer closes on a conference list as its identity statement; the builder paragraph up top now sets identity.
- **RESOLVED — EdD as credential recital.** Folded into a product-benefit sentence under WHAT I BRING.
- **REMAINS (soft) — District-pace fear.** Less acute than round 1 because velocity signals now exist, but the center of gravity is still public-ed and consulting delivery. Devin will still probe this on the call. The resume has done enough to get him there — it cannot fully retire the concern on paper, and it does not need to.

### New Problems the Revision Introduced

- **Overcorrected tone in the banner and parts of WHAT I BRING.** Devin is allergic to performance and mission-statement energy. "THE DOMAIN CO-FOUNDER FOR AN AI-IEP COMPANY" as an all-caps banner, plus "I bring the unfakeable half," plus "Compliance moat, said plainly" as a bullet lead — this is a notch hotter than Devin's taste. He likes confident specificity; he distrusts a resume that is *selling its own confidence*. The substance underneath each of these lines is strong, which is why this is a tone fix, not a content fix. The resume would read as more credible, not less, if it stated the facts slightly flatter and let Devin reach the "co-founder" conclusion himself. Right now it tells him the conclusion three times before he has finished the evidence.
- **A small credibility-precision risk in the AI bullet.** "I have run AI-drafted IEP language against IDEA standards and can bring that diagnostic lens into product and spec work from day one" implies a repeated, almost methodical practice. The true underlying fact is lighter: a PD course plus routine tool use plus some informal stress-testing. Devin's paid trial *is* exactly this task — if she advances, the trial will calibrate the claim precisely, and any gap between the resume's implied rigor and the actual depth will be visible in 30 minutes. The bullet is not dishonest, but it is written at the top of what the facts support. Tightening it to describe what she has actually done ("in the course and in my own review, I have compared AI-drafted IEP language against IDEA standards") removes the risk that the trial reads as a step *down* from the resume.

### Specific Resume Fixes (Devin)

**Line:** `# THE DOMAIN CO-FOUNDER FOR AN AI-IEP COMPANY`
**Rewrite:** Demote to a quieter framing — e.g. `# THE DOMAIN HALF OF AN AI-IEP COMPANY` set as a normal header, or drop the banner entirely and let the opening paragraph carry the frame. The paragraph already says "I bring the unfakeable half" — the banner is redundant with it and louder than it.
Rationale: Removes the pitch-deck-slide register Devin reads as performance. The opening paragraph does the same job with evidence attached.

**Line:** `I bring the unfakeable half: the SpEd domain depth, the district relationships, and the compliance auditing history that make an AI-IEP company defensible from day one.`
**Rewrite:** `The SpEd domain depth, the district relationships, and the compliance auditing history are the half of an AI-IEP company that cannot be hired cheaply or learned fast — and it is the half I already have.`
Rationale: Same claim, stated as fact rather than as self-description. Devin trusts the fact more than the framing.

**Line:** `I have run AI-drafted IEP language against IDEA standards and can bring that diagnostic lens into product and spec work from day one.`
**Rewrite:** `In that course and in my own review work I have compared AI-drafted IEP language against IDEA standards — the same audit lens I already apply to human-drafted IEPs.`
Rationale: Anchors the claim to its true source and to her real, deep skill (auditing human IEPs) rather than implying a body of AI-specific practice she does not have. Makes the paid trial a confirmation, not a contradiction.

## Screener: Arjun Mehta (startup-technical-cofounder)

**Verdict: BORDERLINE-ADVANCE — earns the two-hour call and whiteboard; the domain wedge and the new builder-identity sentence get him most of the way. Two unresolved items keep it short of a fast "let's draft the agreement": the equity/runway number is still absent, and the builder claim is asserted but not yet *evidenced* by an AI artifact.**

### First-8-Seconds Skim Reaction

Arjun's three skim questions: domain moat he lacks, independent operating history, any sign she has touched AI. Round 2 answers all three visibly — which is new. Question one: the tagline names the moat outright; he does not have to dig for it. Question two: "independent operator since 2019" is in the tagline and the consulting practice is now framed as a business he runs, not a service he provides. Question three — the one that produced a hard pause in round 1 — now gets a real answer instead of silence: the PD course and the ChatGPT/Claude workflow line. It is not the critical AI *artifact* he ideally wants, but it is the difference between "zero AI engagement" (a near-deal-breaker on his skim) and "has put hands on the tools." That alone moves the profile from "pause" to "keep reading."

The opening paragraph is where Arjun leans in hardest. "I spent 17 years finding what is broken in special education. I am done writing about it — I want to build the fix and sell it." This is, almost verbatim, the sentence his persona card says he is waiting to hear. "That pattern is the company" is the founder-logic move — she has converted her audit history into a company thesis in one line. For a co-founder bet, this is the single most important sentence on the page, and the revision put it first.

The banner gives Arjun the same pause it gives Devin, for a slightly different reason. Arjun reads flattery and performance as a small red flag — not about her confidence, but about whether she can be *wrong* in a room. A resume that declares itself "THE DOMAIN CO-FOUNDER" before he has decided that is, very mildly, the advisor-in-disguise pattern wearing builder clothes. It does not block — the evidence underneath is real — but he notes it.

### Strengths Seen

- **The builder-identity claim is now explicit and present-tense.** Round 1's fatal gap for Arjun was that the resume only said she *wanted* to build. The opening paragraph now states identity. This is the change that most moves him.
- **The single named bet.** "The founding bet is AI-assisted IEP writing and compliance tooling for SpEd teams — one named stake, not an option space." Arjun's round-1 worry was three theses reading as a research agenda. Fixed. "Neurodivergent-first AI tutors and district-side AI governance are adjacent markets … but they are adjacencies, not the founding company" — exactly the demotion he wanted.
- **The founder-logic sentence.** "I have audited IEP/504/IDEA compliance across multiple districts and private schools — paid, repeatedly, to find exactly where compliance breaks — and the same gaps recur every time. That pattern is the company." This is the report's own logic ("when she sees the same problem at five districts in a row, that's the startup idea") executed cleanly. It tells Arjun she has watched the problem repeat — founder evidence, not consultant evidence.
- **GTM is now claimed, not implied.** "Solo lead-gen, client acquisition, proposal writing, and delivery — running a sales pipeline and closing engagements … That is the GTM muscle this company needs." Arjun's pet peeve is the domain co-founder who assumes a head-of-sales hire will do the selling. This line directly refuses that. "Managed a $350K program budget" surfaces early operating range too.
- **Specific compliance mechanism named.** "Most AI-IEP tools fail on the modification-vs-accommodation distinction and on documenting the violated clause — I catch that." Arjun's card says he leans in when someone can explain *why a thing is hard* — the specific mechanism, not just that it is hard. This line does exactly that, and it is the closest thing on the resume to the "argument partner" signal he is buying.

### Round-1 Risks — Status

- **RESOLVED — Three theses / research-scoping read.** Collapsed to one named bet with adjacencies demoted.
- **RESOLVED — No builder-identity claim.** The opening paragraph states present-tense identity with reasoning. This was Arjun's central round-1 blocker-class concern and it is the strongest single fix in the revision.
- **RESOLVED — CEO/GTM seat never claimed.** Now claimed explicitly in WHAT I BRING and reinforced in the OSPI bullets.
- **PARTIALLY RESOLVED — No AI artifact.** Round 1 asked for a *critical* artifact (a teardown of an existing AI-IEP tool, an essay on where the tools break). The revision substitutes a PD course plus tool-use description plus the "modification-vs-accommodation" mechanism line. This is real engagement and it clears the skim — but it is consumption and opinion, not a produced, public artifact. Arjun's card is specific that the artifact is "the artifact that tells him she can be his argument partner." She has stated the opinion; she has not yet *shipped* it anywhere he can point to. So: the deal-breaker version of this risk is resolved (she is no longer at "zero AI engagement"), but the fast-yes version is not (no public artifact). This is now the single highest-leverage remaining edit for Arjun.
- **REMAINS — Equity/runway number absent.** Round 1 flagged that Arjun wants a month-count, not "comfortable with risk." The revision surfaces the financial-risk *history* well — "unsteady 1099 income, by choice, against a teaching salary" is a good, honest data point. But there is still no forward-looking number, and Arjun's card is explicit: "Vague risk talk … he wants to hear 'I can go fourteen months without a full salary' or he assumes the real number is three." A resume cannot carry a literal runway number gracefully, so this stays a call-stage item — but the resume should at least stop implying the teaching salary is a permanent floor (see new problems).
- **REMAINS (soft) — Startup-operating / district-pace.** Same as Devin: mitigated by velocity language, not retired. Arjun does not require startup experience and the revision gives him enough tempo signal to take the call.

### New Problems the Revision Introduced

- **The builder claim now slightly outruns the evidence.** This is the sharpest round-2 issue for Arjun specifically. The resume *says* "I am done writing about it — I want to build" and "I am not studying this — I am past that" energy is all over the copy. Arjun's deepest screen is the researcher-who-secretly-wants-tenure pattern, and his defense against it is evidence, not assertion. A resume that declares builder identity loudly while the only AI artifact is a completed PD course can read, to a wary co-founder, as the *claim* of conversion rather than the *proof* of it. The opening paragraph is the right sentence — but it now writes a check that an AI artifact needs to cash. Round 1's gap was "no builder claim." Round 2's gap is "builder claim without the shipped thing that backs it." That is progress, but it is a new and specific exposure.
- **The "by choice, against a teaching salary" framing cuts slightly against the cliff signal.** "Unsteady 1099 income, by choice, against a teaching salary" honestly describes the past — but it also tells Arjun the risk was always *cushioned* by a stable W-2 underneath. He weights the consulting history as cliff-rehearsal; this phrasing quietly reminds him it was rehearsal *with a net*. Not wrong to say, but it slightly undercuts the very signal it is meant to send. He will want to know on the call whether she will give up the net.
- **Banner / "unfakeable half" tone — same note as Devin.** Arjun reads performance as a small red flag bearing on whether she can hold a belief loosely. Lower-stakes for him than the artifact gap, but present.

### Specific Resume Fixes (Arjun)

**Line:** (the AI bullet, currently a course + tool-use description)
**Rewrite / add:** Produce and link one short public artifact and cite it here — e.g. `Wrote a public teardown of [a named AI-IEP tool / a sample AI-drafted IEP]: where it passes IDEA and the two places it quietly fails. [link]` Even a single LinkedIn post or one-page PDF is enough; the fact of a shipped, pointable artifact is what converts "states a builder identity" into "is one."
Rationale: This is the exact item Arjun's card names as the thing that makes him want the partnership. It also retroactively backs the opening paragraph's builder claim, closing the new "claim outruns evidence" gap.

**Line:** `Founded and operated a solo consulting practice for six years alongside full-time teaching — lead-gen, proposals, client delivery, and unsteady 1099 income, by choice, against a teaching salary.`
**Rewrite:** `Founded and operated a solo consulting practice for six years — lead-gen, proposals, client delivery, and unsteady 1099 income — built alongside full-time teaching, and now ready to run without that backstop.`
Rationale: Keeps the honest financial-risk history but turns the "against a teaching salary" note from a comfort signal into a forward commitment. Signals she knows the net is coming off — the thing Arjun most needs to hear and the resume can gesture at without stating a literal runway number.

**Line:** `# THE DOMAIN CO-FOUNDER FOR AN AI-IEP COMPANY`
**Rewrite:** Same as the Devin fix — demote or drop. For Arjun the reason is subtly different: a quieter header lets the *evidence* carry the co-founder claim, which is the only way he will actually accept it. A self-declared co-founder title reads, to him, fractionally like the advisor-in-disguise he screens hardest against.

## Synthesis

### Where Devin and Arjun Agree

Both screeners agree the revision worked. The headline-as-wedge fix, the single-named-bet fix, the consulting-as-company-building reframe, and the explicit builder-identity paragraph all land for both — these were the round-1 highest-leverage targets and all are resolved. Both also have the same minor reservation about the overcooked tone: the all-caps banner and the "unfakeable half" / "said plainly" register are a notch hotter than either screener's taste, and both would find the resume *more* credible if it stated its strong facts slightly flatter and let them draw the co-founder conclusion themselves. Neither treats the tone as blocking. Both will take the call. Both still carry a soft, unkillable-on-paper district-pace concern that they will probe live — and the resume has correctly done enough to get them to that call rather than trying to win the argument in print.

### Where They Diverge

The divergence is the same axis as round 1, and it explains why the verdicts differ by half a notch. Devin is making a *hire* and his screen is mostly satisfied: the AI bullet, modest as it is, names his exact paid-trial task, and the trial itself will calibrate her real depth. For a hire decision, "credible AI-familiarity step + I'll test the rest in the trial" is enough — Devin ADVANCES.

Arjun is making a *co-founder* bet on 40-50% of his cap table, and his screen turns on a question Devin can defer to a trial: is the builder identity *real* or *claimed*? The revision gave him the builder *sentence* — which round 1 lacked entirely — but the only AI evidence behind it is a PD course. For Arjun the persona, a declared identity without a shipped artifact is specifically the pattern he is most wary of, so the revision, by stating the builder claim loudly, has slightly raised the bar for the evidence that backs it. He still ADVANCES to the call — the domain wedge and the founder-logic sentence are too strong not to — but it is BORDERLINE-ADVANCE, not the fast yes, and the gap is one artifact wide.

The first-person/builder reframe, on balance: it *worked* and was the right move — but it overshot in two specific places. The banner overshot on tone for both. And the volume of builder-identity assertion overshot the evidence base for Arjun specifically. The fix is not to retreat from the builder framing; it is to keep the identity claim and *quiet the packaging* while *shipping one artifact* so the claim is earned rather than announced.

### Top 3 Highest-Leverage Edits Still Worth Making

**1. Ship one short, public, critical AI artifact and cite it.** This is the only remaining item that changes a verdict — it is what moves Arjun from BORDERLINE-ADVANCE toward a fast yes, and it strengthens Devin's read at zero cost. It does not need to be large: a single LinkedIn post or one-page teardown of a real AI-IEP tool (or a sample AI-drafted IEP), naming where it passes IDEA and the two places it fails. The artifact retroactively backs the opening paragraph's builder claim, which the revision currently asserts without proof. Highest leverage by a wide margin.

**2. Cool the packaging — demote the banner, flatten the self-description.** Drop or quiet "THE DOMAIN CO-FOUNDER FOR AN AI-IEP COMPANY," and rephrase "I bring the unfakeable half" / "Compliance moat, said plainly" into flat factual statements. Both screeners are allergic to performance; the substance underneath is strong enough that stating it plainly reads as *more* confident, not less. Lets each screener reach the co-founder conclusion himself — which is the only version of that conclusion either will trust.

**3. Tighten the AI bullet and the runway framing for precision.** Anchor the AI claim to its true source ("in that course and in my own review work I have compared AI-drafted IEP language against IDEA standards") so the paid trial confirms the resume rather than exposing a gap. And reframe "unsteady 1099 income … against a teaching salary" to signal she is ready to run without the backstop — turning a comfort signal into the cliff-appetite signal Arjun is hunting for.

### Is It Submission-Ready?

**Close, but make edits 1 and 2 first.** For Devin alone, the resume is submission-ready today — it clears his skim and earns the call, and edit 1 (the artifact) plus edit 2 (cooler tone) would only sharpen an already-passing screen. For Arjun, it is *call-worthy* today but not yet optimized: shipping the one AI artifact (edit 1) is what closes the last real gap and turns a BORDERLINE-ADVANCE into a strong one. Edit 1 is worth doing before submission to either screener because it is the difference between a builder identity that is announced and one that is evidenced — and that is the question the whole co-founder conversation turns on. Edit 2 is low-effort and pure upside. Edit 3 is polish. Net: one substantive task (publish a short artifact) and one quick tone pass stand between this resume and fully submission-ready.
