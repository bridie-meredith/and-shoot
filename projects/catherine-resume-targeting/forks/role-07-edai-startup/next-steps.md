# Role 7 — Ed+AI Startup — Catherine's Next Steps

Concrete steps to make Catherine a fast YES for Devin Park (founder hire) and Arjun Mehta (co-founder bet). Each is achievable in 1-3 months while keeping her early-childhood teaching job, drawn from the opportunity report's Section 4 (Weak spots) and Section 6 (Next steps). Each step is tied to the persona question or screen-out risk it neutralizes.

The governing principle: **the resume already clears Devin's skim. It does not yet clear Arjun's bar.** Arjun needs the asserted builder identity to be *evidenced*. Every step below is sequenced to close that gap.

---

## Weeks 1-2 — Foundation: close the AI-tourism risk and prepare the artifact

### Step 1 — Build genuine LLM power-user fluency (report §4 weak spot 1)
Spend 5-8 hrs/week using Claude, ChatGPT, NotebookLM, and one AI-for-teachers tool (MagicSchool, Brisk, or SchoolAI) daily. Specifically: run several real (de-identified) IEP scenarios through each, and have each tool draft IEP goals, accommodations, and present-levels statements.
- **Neutralizes:** Devin Q7 ("has she actually touched the tools?") and Q6; Arjun Q5 and the "zero AI engagement" instant-reject. Moves her from AI tourism to demonstrable hands-on practice.

### Step 2 — Pick one named AI-IEP tool and run a structured compliance audit on it
Choose a real, namable AI-IEP product (e.g., an existing IEP-drafting assistant). Generate 3-5 sample IEPs from it and audit each against IDEA using the same lens she applies to human-drafted IEPs. Document, in writing: where it passes IDEA, and the two or three specific places it quietly fails (modification-vs-accommodation conflation, undocumented violated clause, present-levels gaps). This is the raw material for Step 4.
- **Neutralizes:** Arjun Q2 (explain *why it's hard* — the mechanism) and Q10 (will she find the real compliance flaw and hold it?). This is the dry run for the in-meeting disagreement test the partnership turns on.

### Step 3 — Rewrite the LinkedIn headline and "About" (report §4 weak spot 3; §6 weeks 1-2)
Replace any generalist "Education and Administration Leadership Expert" framing with a wedge-first headline, e.g. *"EdD · IEP/IDEA compliance auditor specializing in complex autism — building the compliance layer for AI-IEP tooling."* Mirror the resume's positioning so a warm intro and a LinkedIn glance tell the same story.
- **Neutralizes:** Devin Q1 and Arjun Q1 (is the wedge named in the first thing they see?). Arjun arrives almost always via warm intro and LinkedIn glance — this is his skim surface.

---

## Weeks 3-6 — First signals: ship the artifact, name the runway, do the math

### Step 4 — Publish ONE short, public, critical AI artifact (report §4 weak spot 2; §6 weeks 3-6) — HIGHEST LEVERAGE
Turn Step 2's audit into a single public piece: a LinkedIn article or a one-page PDF teardown titled something like *"I audited an AI-drafted IEP against IDEA: where it passes, and the two places it quietly fails."* Critical and specific, not promotional — name the mechanism. One post is enough; the fact of a *shipped, pointable* artifact is the point.
- **Neutralizes:** Arjun Q5 — the single verdict-changing gap from the critic feedback. Converts the resume's asserted builder identity into an *evidenced* one and retroactively backs the opening paragraph. Also strengthens Devin Q6/Q9 at zero cost. This is the one step that moves Arjun from BORDERLINE-ADVANCE to a fast yes.

### Step 5 — Write a second short piece naming the recurring failure pattern (report §6 weeks 3-6)
Publish a companion piece, e.g. *"Five compliance questions every district should ask before buying an AI-IEP tool"* or *"The same three ways IEP compliance breaks — what I've seen across multiple districts."* This makes the "I have watched the problem repeat" claim concrete and public.
- **Neutralizes:** Arjun Q4 (has she watched the same problem repeat across N sites?) and Devin Q6. Converts consulting history into founder evidence — the report's own logic ("the same problem at five districts in a row — that's the startup idea").

### Step 6 — Do the runway math and decide a number (report §6 decision points)
Sit down with finances and determine honestly: how many months can she operate on $0-60k. Land on a real figure she can state plainly ("I can go X months without a full salary"). Pair it with an explicit decision about giving up the teaching salary.
- **Neutralizes:** Arjun Q7 and Q9 (the runway number; will she give up the net?) and Devin Q10. The resume can gesture at this; the call requires the actual number. The critic feedback flags this as a still-open item.

### Step 7 — Quantify the audit history (report §6; persona "watched the problem repeat")
Pull her own records and pin down concrete counts: how many districts/private schools audited, how many IEPs reviewed, how many years. Replace "multiple districts" with a real number wherever she truthfully can.
- **Neutralizes:** Arjun Q4 and Q3; Devin Q3. A counted history reads as founder evidence; "multiple" reads as soft.

## Months 2-3 — Conversation-ready: warm intros, cooled packaging, calibrated claims

### Step 8 — Cool the resume packaging and tighten claims for precision (critic feedback edits 2 & 3)
Demote the all-caps banner to a quiet header (or drop it); rewrite "I bring the unfakeable half" and "Compliance moat, said plainly" as flat factual statements; anchor the AI bullet to its true source ("in that course and in my own review work I have compared AI-drafted IEP language against IDEA standards"); reframe "1099 income against a teaching salary" into a forward commitment ("built alongside teaching, now ready to run without that backstop").
- **Neutralizes:** Devin Q9 and the performance pet-peeve; Arjun's "advisor in disguise" and "won't be wrong" wariness. Lets both reach the co-founder conclusion themselves.

### Step 9 — Activate warm intros into the ed+AI startup world (report §4 weak spot 4; §6)
Schedule the working call with the user (project context) to clarify positioning and identify 5-10 warm intros. Show up in the AI-for-Education community, ISTE's AI strand, and Stanford Accelerator for Learning events. Arjun "almost never" finds co-founders cold — he comes through a mutual contact or investor intro.
- **Neutralizes:** Arjun's "warm intro only" reality (The Skim). Without a warm-intro path, the strongest resume never reaches him.

### Step 10 — Submit an AI-themed conference talk and prepare the GTM/CEO narrative (report §6 months 2-3)
Submit an AI-IEP-compliance session proposal for AERA 2027 or ISTE Live 2026. Separately, rehearse a tight verbal pitch that explicitly claims the GTM and CEO seat — framing the consulting practice as "I have run a sales pipeline and closed five-figure engagements solo" and stating she *wants* to own sales, fundraising, and the public face.
- **Neutralizes:** Arjun Q6 (can she own the non-technical half? does she want the CEO seat?) and his "outsourced GTM" instant-reject; Devin Q8. The talk also adds a forward-dated public AI signal.
