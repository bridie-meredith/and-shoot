# Role 2 — Frontier-Lab Full-Time — Catherine's Next Steps

All steps are achievable in 1-3 months while keeping the Kent School District teaching job (evenings/weekends, async). Each step is tied to the persona question or screen-out risk it neutralizes. Drawn from and consistent with the opportunity report's Section 4 (weak spots) and Section 6 (next steps).

The current resume (round 2) is *effectively submission-ready* — it banked all three round-1 fixes. These steps close the **three remaining gates the resume itself cannot fix**: artifact quality, AI/tech-policy depth, and the warm-intro channel.

## Weeks 1-2 — Foundation: prove tool fluency, harden the existing artifact

### Step 1. Verify and deepen the cited LinkedIn artifact
**Do:** Confirm "Five IDEA compliance questions every district should ask AI vendors" is genuinely substantive. If it currently reads as a listicle, rewrite at least one of the five questions into a *worked policy example* — a real regulatory requirement (e.g., a FERPA/IDEA data-retention obligation) set against a competing interest (vendor model-training opt-outs vs. district service needs), ending in a recommendation.
**Neutralizes:** The shared load-bearing dependency — all three personas click this link. Naomi's bar is "a policy memo with judgment, not résumé SEO." A thin piece reverses every gain. (Maya Q9, Devang Q9 caveat, Naomi Q10.)

### Step 2. Reach daily power-user fluency with AI tools
**Do:** 5-8 hrs/week using Claude, ChatGPT, NotebookLM, and one AI-for-teachers tool (MagicSchool, Brisk, or SchoolAI). Keep notes on failure modes you observe in education contexts — these become raw material for Step 4.
**Neutralizes:** Devang's blocker — "AI claimed with nothing to verify reads as a tell she hasn't used the tools." Backs the resume's honest "use Claude and ChatGPT routinely" claim with real depth she can defend in interview. (Devang Q8, Q5.)

### Step 3. Rewrite the LinkedIn headline and profile
**Do:** Replace any residual district framing on the LinkedIn profile with the resume's tagline language: "EdD; special-education / IDEA compliance specialist working at the intersection of K-12 and AI." Mirror the resume's positioning so a recruiter cross-checking LinkedIn finds the same retargeted story.
**Neutralizes:** Maya's "was this sprayed or retargeted" check, and her concern that the artifact link must land on a profile that confirms the resume's story. (Maya Q2, Q4.)

## Weeks 3-6 — First signals: publish the eval-flavored artifact, open the referral channel

### Step 4. Publish a second, eval-flavored artifact
**Do:** Write and publish one short piece (LinkedIn article or Substack) that *sketches an actual eval*: "How would you test whether an AI tutor handles a 7-year-old with an IEP appropriately?" Lay out a small rubric — criteria, ground-truth examples, edge cases, false-positive concerns. Draw on the failure-mode notes from Step 2.
**Neutralizes:** Devang's residual gap — the existing artifact is policy-flavored; an eval-flavored piece converts his ADVANCE into an *unarguable* yes for his specific req, and gives Maya a second clickable link. (Devang Q1, Q2, Q9; Maya Q1.)

### Step 5. Source one warm referral into a lab
**Do:** Hold the 60-minute positioning call with the user (per report Section 6); identify 5-10 warm intros from his network. Also engage the AI-for-Education community, ISTE's AI strand, and Stanford Accelerator for Learning events to surface direct contacts. Goal: one researcher or policy person inside Anthropic / OpenAI / DeepMind who will pass Catherine's name to the recruiter.
**Neutralizes:** Maya's single remaining gating risk — a cold apply to a scarce req without a referral is a practical blocker; she will *fight* for a referred borderline candidate. This is the one thing standing between her "second pass" and a clean forward. (Maya Q6.)

### Step 6. Add an AI-themed conference session to an existing venue
**Do:** Submit an AI-in-special-education talk proposal for AERA 2027 and/or ISTE Live 2026 — venues Catherine already presents at. Even an accepted (not-yet-delivered) proposal is a citable line.
**Neutralizes:** Reinforces the "visibly did the thinking about AI" signal across all three; gives a third public touchpoint. (Devang Q1, Naomi Q4.)

## Months 2-3 — Depth: a worked policy artifact, stakeholder evidence, application

### Step 7. Publish or draft a worked tech-policy memo
**Do:** Write one piece that demonstrably models a *stakeholder fight on a technology question* — e.g., a one-page position on whether a consumer AI assistant should let a 12-year-old with an IEP use it unsupervised, showing the COPPA/FERPA tradeoff, the competing interests (child autonomy vs. parental access vs. institutional liability), and a defensible recommendation.
**Neutralizes:** Naomi's hardest probe — she advances Catherine to a writing exercise that is exactly this. A published worked memo proves the writing exercise won't waste her panel's afternoon, and demonstrates she can hold competing constituencies in one hand. (Naomi Q3, Q4, Q5.)

### Step 8. Sharpen one resume bullet into visible assessment work
**Do:** Per Maya's optional fix, convert "Served as compliance monitor across 3 school sites" to "Served as designated compliance monitor across 3 school sites — site-level IDEA/504 documentation review." Confirm all child-development range language reads as specific developmental reasoning, not generic K-12.
**Neutralizes:** Maya's residual mild-district-vocabulary flag; Devang's duty-vs-built-thing scan; Naomi's decision-vs-duty line check. (Maya Q8, Devang Q6, Naomi Q9.)

### Step 9. Apply to 3-5 targeted reqs, each paired with a referral attempt
**Do:** Apply to specific frontier-lab education reqs — model-behavior evals lead, education T&S policy, human-data program designer. Anthropic's Seattle hub is the geographic best fit. Pair *every* submission with a referral attempt from the Step 5 contacts.
**Neutralizes:** Converts the banked work into actual pipeline; ensures no cold submission goes in without the warm-intro multiplier Maya requires. (All personas.)
