# Role 8 — Productized PD / Courses / Community / Book
## Catherine's Next Steps (1-3 month horizon)

### Framing — read this first

The opportunity report is explicit that Role 8 is a **slow compounding play and a
supporting move, not a first move**. Productized PD takes 1-3 years to monetize
meaningfully. So these next steps are **not** "launch a profitable course." They are the
*early audience and IP foundation* — the layer that, a year from now, lets Catherine
walk into a Camille conversation with receipts, and lets a Denise-type buyer find a free
sample and a named pilot.

The Round-2 feedback split cleanly: **Denise's blockers are a one-week copy/design pass**
(price, deliverables, free sample, pilot proof) — reachable now. **Camille's blocker is a
real-world action gap** (an audience, a publishing archive) — a deliberate 12-month build
that these steps only *begin*. Every step below is realistic for someone keeping a
full-time teaching job, and each is tied to a specific persona question or screen-out
risk it neutralizes.

---

## Weeks 1-2 — Foundation: positioning, the first artifact, the publishing rail

**1. Launch the Substack / LinkedIn newsletter and publish post #1.**
Stand up a named newsletter on inclusive leadership + AI + SpEd compliance. Publish the
first issue this fortnight — the report names a perfect opener: *"What I Wish AI
Education Products Understood About PDA Autism."* Each issue is a thesis-with-an-enemy,
not a syllabus.
- *Neutralizes:* Camille Q1 ("what's your list size?") and Q2 ("have you shipped, or just
  'building a cadence'?") — week one of the dated archive must exist. Closes the Round-2
  blocker "no proof of week one."

**2. Convert the newsletter from "building a cadence" to a real, dated archive — set the
schedule.**
Commit publicly to a weekly cadence and schedule the next 6 issues now (titles + a one-line
thesis each). The artifact must stop saying "building a cadence" (Camille's tell) and start
saying "Weekly newsletter — issue N, published [date]."
- *Neutralizes:* Camille Q2 (cadence-untested expert, blocker) and the Round-2 fix
  "replace 'building a weekly cadence' with a real number — or stop asserting it."

**3. Publish the free one-page download: "Five Compliance Questions Every District Should
Ask Before Buying an AI Tool."**
The report names this exact artifact. It is implied to already exist as cohort material;
pulling out a one-page free version is near-zero extra work. Host it as a newsletter
sign-up incentive (this also feeds list growth).
- *Neutralizes:* Denise Q8 ("can I taste it before I pay?") and the Round-2 blocker "no
  free sample" — this is how an unknown creator earns a tired buyer's trust.

**4. Rewrite the LinkedIn headline and surface a real audience number.**
Replace any vague title with the report's positioning: *"EdD specializing in inclusive
leadership, complex autism, and AI integration for K-12 special education."* Pull the
honest existing LinkedIn connection count and SpEd-sector segment into the artifact's
first screen.
- *Neutralizes:* Camille Q1 (no number in the first screen halves her interest) and
  Q3 (the wedge / one-liner); Denise Q1 (sees herself named).

**5. Set the cohort price and a PO-friendly team rate; write a one-line drop/refund
policy.**
Decide a per-seat number ($1,200-2,800 band per the buyer persona) and a 2-3-seat team
bundle, plus a plain refund line. This is a decision, not a build — it costs an afternoon.
- *Neutralizes:* Denise Q5 ("what's the price, visible without a call?" — now her largest
  gap, strong-severity) and Q10 (real refund/drop policy).

## Weeks 3-6 — First signals: more posts, the pilot recruited, conference proposal

**6. Reach 6 consecutive weekly newsletter issues; publish the second report-named post.**
Keep the cadence unbroken — issue #2 of the named pair: *"Five Compliance Questions Every
District Should Ask Before Buying AI Tools."* Six dated issues is a visible habit, not an
intention.
- *Neutralizes:* Camille Q2 and Q8 — builds toward the "twelve consecutive weeks" bar she
  buys; converts the cadence claim into a countable archive.

**7. Recruit and schedule a free or low-cost pilot cohort of 6-8 SpEd directors /
coordinators.**
Use Catherine's existing OSPI and WA/OR district network to fill a small pilot run of
"AI Compliance for Special Education Directors." Free or low-cost — the point is the
receipt, not the revenue. Schedule it within the 1-3 month window; recruit it now.
- *Neutralizes:* the single highest-leverage gap — Camille Q5 ("if it's this built, why
  hasn't it run?") *and* Denise Q7 ("name one peer who's done this"). One move, both
  personas (the Round-2 synthesis names this as the #1 edit).

**8. Submit an AI-themed SpEd session proposal to AERA 2027 / ISTE Live 2026.**
A proposal is a 1-2 hour task and gives the artifact a forward-looking, AI-anchored line.
Where Catherine has *delivered* talks, capture room counts and any follow-up requests.
- *Neutralizes:* Camille Q8 ("did anyone lean in — room counts, DMs?") — converts the
  engagements list from credentials into evidence of pull; Denise Q9 (current, AI-aware).

**9. Build a one-page cohort landing page (template, not custom) with price, deliverables,
honest total time math, and the free download.**
Per the report's months-2-3 step and the Round-2 recommendation that this be its own
document, separate from the speaker sheet. List the four deliverables as a noun-list; state
session length *and* a ~30-minute homework cap; show the price.
- *Neutralizes:* Denise Q2, Q4, Q5, Q6 (nameable outcome, honest time math, visible price,
  deliverable noun-list); Round-2 fix "bound the total weekly load, not just session
  length."

## Months 2-3 — Run the pilot, capture proof, tighten the franchise

**10. Run the free/low-cost pilot cohort to completion and capture named peer proof.**
Deliver the 4-week pilot. Collect, with permission, participant names by role and region
("7 SpEd directors and coordinators, WA/OR districts") and 1-2 plain-spoken practitioner
quotes ("That's useful" energy, no hype).
- *Neutralizes:* Denise Q7 (the peer she can phone — the steep unknown-creator bar) and
  Camille Q4/Q5 (proof of demand at repeatable scale; the cohort is no longer a syllabus
  with no buyers).

**11. Reach ~12 consecutive newsletter issues and report the honest subscriber count.**
By month 3, a steady cadence plus the free-download incentive plus pilot-participant
sign-ups credibly yields a few hundred subscribers. Report the real number — "~250
subscribers, 12 weekly issues" — not a rounded-up fantasy.
- *Neutralizes:* Camille Q1 and Q2 — twelve consecutive weeks is the cadence bar she
  *buys*; a modest honest number beats no number. (Note: this *begins* closing Camille's
  audience gap; it does not finish it — that is the 12-month build.)

**12. Separate the franchise spine into its own block and refine the IP backbone.**
State the spine once, cleanly: newsletter → Cohort 1 (SpEd directors on AI compliance) →
Cohort 2 (principals on inclusive AI leadership) → the book as the same argument. Keep it
*out* of the cohort offer block so Denise reads the SpEd-directors cohort as cleanly hers.
- *Neutralizes:* Camille Q6 ("repeatable franchise, not a one-off") and the Round-2 fix
  "move the 'Cohort 2 / the book' franchise lines out of the cohort block."

## What is deliberately NOT in scope (1-3 months)

- A launched, profitable, full-price cohort — the report calls Role 8 a 1-3-year monetize
  horizon. The pilot is the foundation, not the business.
- A book deal or proposal — premature; the book is the far end of the franchise spine.
- A large audience (thousands of subscribers) — a few hundred in 3 months is credible;
  more is not, and overclaiming triggers Camille's "show me the receipts" reflex.
- Pitching Camille for a course slot now — the honest verdict is still "no today." These
  steps build toward the *next* Camille conversation, 12+ months out.
