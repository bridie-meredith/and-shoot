# Season 1 Plan — Dead Capes in Westeros

## Season Slug: s01

## Season Drama Statement

Each arrived cape is isolated, surviving on incomplete information, and concealing what they are from a Westerosi society that would process them as monsters, sorcerers, or divine omens. The season's pressure: the shards are reacting to Westerosi magic with increasing intensity, cover stories are fraying, and the capes are beginning to leave traces in the world — traces that Westerosi people are noticing. By the season's end, each cape has made their first contact with another arrived cape (or knows that contact is imminent), and the question of whether to find each other deliberately has been forced by external events.

The season ends with the convergence engine engaged: someone in King's Landing is collecting stories of impossible things, and the stories are all about the same six people.

## Episode Chunk Statements

### s01e01: "Drop"
The first episode is three simultaneous arrivals, close-cut. Jack, three months in, makes his first mistake — a Reach lordling notices that the traveling merchant seems to know things he shouldn't. Dennis, day one, lands in a Stormlands market town, uses his power publicly, and starts the time-witch rumor. The Crawler-dragon is seen from a ship in the Narrow Sea and the sighting is reported to King's Landing.

### s01e02: "The Midwife's Question"
Riley heals a child with fever using a preparation that no one in the village recognizes. The midwife asks her where she learned it. Riley doesn't have an answer that holds. First sustained scene in Riley's perspective. The village elder goes to look at the weirwood grove and does not come back until morning.

### s01e03: "Cover and Run"
Dennis's time-witch reputation has spread far enough that the local lord sends a man to look into it. Dennis has to move. The episode is his first experience of real Westerosi peril — not the abstract shock of arrival, but a specific man on a horse with a specific mandate. He freezes a doorway and runs. He ends up on the Kingsroad heading north. He makes the wrong call about which direction is safer.

### s01e04: "The Noblewoman from Yi Ti"
Shatterbird arrives at Highgarden in the lord's retinue. She sees the sept windows. She cannot perform the symphony — too many people, wrong time — but she practices in the sept at night with the small quantity of glass available. A septon catches her. She convinces him she was praying. The lie is almost too easy, which tells her something she doesn't want to know about who she's becoming here.

### s01e05: "What the Hunger Wants"
Noelle reaches the edge of the Reach, two days from King's Landing. She stops at a godswood. The hunger spikes harder than it ever has near a weirwood — the shard is pushing, something about the network, something she cannot read. She sits outside the godswood for four hours and does not go in. When she stands up, a child from the nearby holdfast is watching her from the tree line. The child asks her if she is a witch. She says no. The child doesn't believe her.

### s01e06: "The Dragon Expedition"
A Targaryen minor household sends a ship to investigate the sightings. The ship has two knights, a maester, and a junior Targaryen (a legitimized bastard, no dragon of his own, trying to make a name). They find the cliffs. The Crawler-dragon watches them from the cave. The maester names what he sees correctly as a dragon and incorrectly as a young Targaryen bloodline runaway (his theory). The junior Targaryen disagrees. The disagreement saves the ship — neither camp wants to act without consensus. The ship withdraws. The Crawler watches them go. The shard registers: high-value targets, novel threat potential. He follows the ship at altitude, invisible against the night sky.

### s01e07: "Traces"
The episode cuts between five separate people in Westeros who are telling stories about impossible things. A lord's steward (Jack's trace). A septon (Shatterbird's trace). A child from the holdfast (Noelle's trace). A market guard (Dennis's trace). A ship's maester (the Crawler's trace). All five stories reach King's Landing. None of them reach the same person — yet. Someone, unnamed, is beginning to collect them.

### s01e08: "The First Signal"
Jack's broadcast shard, which has been firing into noise for three months, produces its first coherent output: a specific sensation, specific direction, specific distance. Not a parahuman read — he doesn't know what he's getting — but something his shard recognizes as a shard. The signal source is in King's Landing. He sells his cover identity, packs what he needs, and gets on the road. He does not tell the lordling he is leaving. The lordling will notice by morning.

## Key Cast

- jack-slash: the psychological operator learning to perform without instruments
- bonesaw: the child who does not know what she is but who the village is beginning to suspect
- crawler: the dragon in the cliffs whose threat-ceiling is about to be raised dramatically
- clockblocker: the Ward on the wrong road, running from the wrong problem
- noelle: the woman walking west, suppressing the hunger, choosing not to
- shatterbird: the performance without glass, getting better at a role she never wanted

## Season Convergence Engine

By episode 8, Jack is moving toward King's Landing on the signal. By episode 6, the Crawler is following the Targaryen ship (which is also heading toward King's Landing to report). Noelle will arrive in King's Landing in episode 9. Shatterbird, in Highgarden, is three days from the Roseroad — when she hears what's being collected in King's Landing, she will move. Dennis is on the Kingsroad — wrong direction, but the Kingsroad leads everywhere eventually.

## Next Season Sketch (horizon rule — one sentence only)

Season 2 is the season they are all in the same city, looking at each other for the first time, and having to decide whether they are a problem or an asset.
