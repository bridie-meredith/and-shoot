### Seam 3 — Operational precision as knowledge Jarvis should register

Jarvis is characterized as a courier/first-line intermediary, not as an intelligence handler. The reader tracks whether Jarvis should recognize this distinction and what it means operationally for the faction (Otto) receiving the information.

The board-move the sidecar attributes to this line: "the modification is locked into the agreed shape before Jarvis can route it past her." This implies Jarvis is the entity who might route the distinction past Taylor — that is, Jarvis as the vector who might convert Taylor's pattern-report into a commitment for raw intelligence. But the sidecar nowhere establishes that Jarvis, as a courier-tier figure, has the operational authority to renegotiate the terms with Otto. The board-move collapses: the distinction is locked in the agreement, yes, but it is locked with Jarvis, who cannot renegotiate it with Otto anyway. The strategic value of Taylor naming the distinction TO JARVIS rather than TO OTTO is not established.

The cape-fic-reader fires on "established limits bypassed without cost" and on "character using a board-move that doesn't land given the faction structure." This is a faction-coherence seam, not a card violation. The sidecar defends the speech-act as terms-assertion — but terms asserted to a courier, not to the principal, have different strategic weight. If the terms were asserted to Jarvis so that Jarvis carries them as the agreed shape to Otto, that's a real board-move. If the terms are stated in the yard because Taylor needs to say them for her own accounting, that's a different speech-act class. The sidecar argues "the modification is the discipline's last available parameter to hold" — but the holding is structural to the agreement, not transactional, if Jarvis is courier-tier and the terms travel upward through him to Otto anyway.

**Seam weight:** SOFT. Not a card violation; does not fail Q1 or Q2. The board-move rationale in the sidecar has a faction-coherence gap the R2 reviewer should close — specifically, why Jarvis as the recipient is the right recipient for this particular precision-statement.
