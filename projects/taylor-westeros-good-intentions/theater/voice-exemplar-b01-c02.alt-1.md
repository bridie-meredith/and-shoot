# Exemplar passage — alternate voice prime for b01-c02 (arm-2)

I had two-thirds of the net mended by the time the light went orange. The needle was working faster than I was thinking about it, which is how the net gets mended. If you think about the needle you lose the count and the count is what keeps the gauge even. The hands do not need watching once they are started.

Tomas had not come home in the morning. He had said he would and he had not. This was not the first time and it would not be the last. He went where the work was and the work was sometimes on a boat that did not come back when the boat said it would. I had told myself that twice already today and the telling did not change anything but the having-told. The need to tell yourself a thing is a different need from the need for the thing to be true.

I tied off the third row. The light was orange and then it was less than orange. The needle went into the mesh and came out of it and the mesh closed where there had been an opening and I did not look up.

The boat would come back tomorrow. Or it would not. Either way the net would be mended by dark and I would put it under the bench where I always put it, and I would go inside, and I would set out the bowl for him whether the bowl would be used or not.
