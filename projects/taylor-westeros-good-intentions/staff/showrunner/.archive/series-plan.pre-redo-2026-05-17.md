# Series Plan — detail companion to memory.md

## Attempt 1 — proposed series chunk

A foreign operator arrives in King's Landing with a single, declared objective — keep Lucerys Velaryon alive long enough that the succession holds and the Dance does not start — and the city processes her insect-network capability through its institutional machinery until she surfaces as an intelligence asset inside Otto Hightower's operation, which is precisely the faction that requires Lucerys dead. Each trade she makes to protect the objective tightens the Hightower network's hold on her movements, narrows the exits available to the one relationship she will not put in her ledger, and advances the rupture she came to prevent; by the time the Dance ignites, her prevention-work is traceable as the proximate cause of the war's opening violence, and the cost that never entered her accounting lands in Flea Bottom on a girl she would not name.

## Attempt 2 — proposed series chunk

A foreign operator surfaces in King's Landing with one declared objective — keep Lucerys Velaryon alive and the succession intact — and the Hightower operation, recognizing the intelligence value she carries, works methodically to fold her into its architecture before she understands she is being positioned rather than employed; Nessa, the Flea Bottom girl she will not formalize in any ledger, is the pressure point the Hightowers locate first, and it is to keep that pressure off Nessa that Taylor makes the delivery that arms Aemond at Storm's End. That act — her hands, her network, her routing decision — is the irreversible one: the Dance's opening violence traces back to it in a straight line, and when the accounting finally runs, the cost lands not in any ledger she kept but in Flea Bottom, on the girl whose name she refused to write down.

## Attempt 3 — proposed series chunk (superseded; prose retired Attempt 4)

A foreign operator surfaces in King's Landing without a target and spends her first months reading the city's political weather until she identifies the fragile node — Lucerys Velaryon, the boy the cadet faction needs dead to break the succession — and commits to keeping him alive. The Hightower operation, recognizing the intelligence value she carries, works methodically to fold her into its architecture before she understands she is being positioned rather than employed, and locates Nessa, the Flea Bottom girl Taylor will not enter even in her own accounting, as the pressure point. It is to keep that pressure off Nessa that Taylor makes the delivery that arms Aemond at Storm's End: her hands, her network, her routing decision, the irreversible one. The Dance's opening violence traces back to that act in a straight line, and the cost lands on Nessa in a riot neither Taylor's ledger nor the city's records were ever going to count.
