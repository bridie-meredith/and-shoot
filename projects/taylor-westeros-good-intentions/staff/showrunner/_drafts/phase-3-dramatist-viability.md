## Q4 — TENSION RANGE ACROSS THE ARC: CONDITIONAL PASS with one chapter-level specification

**d01–d04:** Ward-elder + Wren + intermediary (active from d04) + Otto (offer at d03). Full setup roster available. Sufficient.

**d05–d09:** Alicent/Aemond/Criston feed content drives political_register-prot from resentment (d05) through articulated contempt (d09). The foil's continued existence as the counter-argument Taylor has stopped engaging is required at d09 — the contempt-articulation lands as a choice only if the slower-principled method is demonstrably still available and Taylor is demonstrably no longer engaging it. Brief's spec satisfies this ("at least one direct encounter per act; implicitly available as counter-argument by d09"). Sufficient.

**d12–d14:** Full-coverage push (d12) and ignition + Wren death + Taylor removal (d14) are cast-covered. Wren carries d14. Otto carries position/tether dissolution. Taylor carries all collapse axes. Sufficient.

**Chapter-level specification required — d10's opposing-faction courier.**

d10 is the arc's structural pivot: non-extractable confirmed, extraction path foreclosed, moral_legibility_to_self first-recognition-suppressed. The mechanism is specific per trajectory: an opposing-faction courier is detained because Taylor's route-map reached Otto's apparatus. Taylor runs the accounting on a person, confirms protect-target's benefit outweighs the courier's harm, closes the ledger, registers what she did, and files the calculation. This is the first clear Khepri-rhyme in the moral_framework axis and the "rationalizing-each-trade → first-recognition-suppressed" shift.

The courier has no cast slot and no prior history in the roster. If the courier is an abstraction — a function rather than a person — the moral_legibility beat loses its weight. Taylor ratifying an accounting is not a dramatic event. Taylor ratifying a specific harm to a specific face she has been tracking through the insect-feed across prior chapters is.

This does not require a new cast slot. It requires prior insect-feed observation of this figure — their movement patterns in Taylor's coverage map, their routes in her data — distributed across the d05–d09 observation sequences so the d10 accounting has a body to close on. The chapter contracts for acts 1-2 observation sequences should accumulate this courier as a named background figure in Taylor's observation record.

Carry to screen-writer as a staging obligation distributed across the middle arc. Not a roster gap. A chapter-level contract specification that must be imposed at /and-substance book chapter chunking.

## Q5 — FOIL DISTINCTNESS: PASS in both branches

World-notes carry-forward: "Place naive-idealist foil as a distinct character OR declare load-bearing fusion into Rhaenyra; implicit fusion not sufficient."

**Branch A — Rhaenyra not elevated:** Foil is a required original character in the minimum-viable roster. Distinct from every other cast member. Carry-forward satisfied.

**Branch B — Rhaenyra elevated:** Foil remains a distinct original character with its own slot. Rhaenyra carries world-embodiment:black-faction-claimant — the faction whose survival Taylor's intelligence deliveries undermine, the faction-road-not-taken, an additional irony channel for political_register-prot and political_register-world. These functions do not overlap with the foil's function (moral-register mirror; principled slower method; counter-argument Taylor has stopped engaging). The two roles are structurally orthogonal. No implicit fusion present. Carry-forward satisfied.

**Conditional note if Rhaenyra is elevated:**

World-notes carry-forward also specifies Rhaenyra must have "an active agenda putting pressure on Taylor's choices throughout — not only a trust to be violated at the end." With Rhaenyra at Dragonstone and Taylor in Flea Bottom, direct contact is unavailable. Her pressure on Taylor's choices must arrive through mediated channels — court rumor, intelligence-product inference, Black-faction figure encounters in KL. This is deliverable under single-POV, but requires explicit staging at /and-substance book chapter level: Rhaenyra as a named pressure-source whose agenda Taylor registers and factors into her accounting, not merely background. The base `rhaenyra-targaryen` card does not carry a 122 AC addendum for this project; margit will need to author `rhaenyra-targaryen-122ac` variant at Phase 4 if elevated.

This is a staging specification. The optional slot is structurally viable if elevated; the pressure-staging mechanism must be named at book substance.
