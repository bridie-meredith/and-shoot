# Open Questions

Dependency-ordered. Each upstream OQ feeds the next.

## OQ-1 — Taylor's power-state at story-open
**Why first:** every later choice (entry-point, identity-posture, prevention-mechanism) depends on what Taylor can actually do at scene-zero. Worm-canon-pedant audience reviews this.

**Admissible values:** intact-Khepri (full multi-shard control) / Khepri-residue-range-limited (control of insects + minor brain-effects only) / Queen-Administrator-sealed-but-precog-thin (no insect control; weak organizational precog) / shard-malfunctioning-intermittent / mundane-with-residual-instinct.

**Constraint:** must support a tragedy-shape where Taylor's INFLUENCE is sufficient to bend events but INSUFFICIENT to control outcomes. Pure-mundane breaks the slow-prevention shape; intact-Khepri breaks the tragedy (she could just win).

## OQ-2 — Mechanism of arrival in Westeros
**Depends on:** OQ-1 (power-state shapes admissible mechanisms).

**Admissible values:** shard-residue-portal (Worm-explained) / Entity-fragment-displacement / authorial-fiat (cold-open: "she woke in an alley off the Hook") / parallel-Earth-with-magic-where-Westeros-is-real.

**Constraint:** must not consume narrative budget. The brief frames Westeros as the SETTING, not the puzzle. Resolution should be efficient enough that a reader accepts and moves on within the first scene or two.

## OQ-3 — Year within "before Dance of Dragons"
**Depends on:** none directly; constrains every downstream cast availability decision.

**Admissible values:** Jaehaerys's late peace (post-101 AC Great Council, pre-103 AC) / Viserys-I middle reign (105-120 AC) / late Viserys-I (120-129 AC, Alicent-Rhaenyra cold war hardening) / immediate pre-129 AC ignition window.

**Constraint:** F&B-canonical cast availability. Story-type (slow-prevention-tragedy) benefits from immediate-pre-ignition because prevention pressure is highest; risks compression of the road-to-hell chain. Mid-reign gives more rope for the chain but dilutes urgency.

## OQ-4 — Taylor's social entry-point in King's Landing
**Depends on:** OQ-1, OQ-3.

**Admissible values:** Flea Bottom anonymous / smallfolk-attached (single relationship anchor from scene-zero) / Faith-attached (taken in by a septon) / household-attached (servant in a lordly household) / merchant-broker-attached / direct-Targaryen-orbit (implausible without OQ-1 = intact-Khepri).

**Constraint:** must let Taylor reach the patron-with-agenda (Rhaenys / Rhaenyra register) within the first quarter of the book. Flea Bottom anonymous is the deepest entry — maximum runway — but requires a mechanism to surface. Direct Targaryen-orbit shortcuts the climb and forecloses the true-ally / attached-smallfolk relationship the lyra-cost requires.

## OQ-5 — The specific "good intention" driving Taylor
**Depends on:** OQ-1 through OQ-4.

**Admissible values:** prevent the Dance entirely / protect a specific person from a known fate / feed Flea Bottom through coming famine / prevent a specific named atrocity (e.g. Lucerys's death, Helaena's collapse, the Riot of King's Landing equivalent) / "do less harm than the version of her that arrived would have done".

**Constraint:** must be reducible to a single declarative sentence the cold-utilitarian protagonist would actually say in inner monologue. Vague intent breaks the moral-accounting register.

## OQ-6 — The specific "bad thing for a good reason"
**Depends on:** OQ-5.

**Admissible values:** assassination / coercive intelligence-feeding (to Otto Hightower) / sabotage of a dragon / network-building that becomes Otto's tool / kidnap-as-protection / a specific betrayal of the true-ally / multiple smaller bad-acts whose aggregate is the bad-thing.

**Constraint:** the bad thing must be auditable — the reader must be able to point at the specific page where Taylor crossed the line. Aggregate-bad-thing risks defusing the structural demand.

## OQ-7 — Cost-bearer (lyra-register ward) identity
**Depends on:** OQ-3 (year fixes which children exist), OQ-4 (social entry-point fixes accessibility).

**Admissible values:** F&B-canonical child within reach of Taylor's entry-point — e.g. a young Targaryen ward, a smallfolk child Taylor takes in, a Hightower-circle ward, a septon's foundling. Specific name pinned in 2.4.

**Constraint:** must be a person Taylor has plausibly seen weekly by the time the closing-image cost lands.

## OQ-8 — End-place locus (Taylor / realm / both / third-party)
**Depends on:** OQ-5 through OQ-7.

**Admissible values:** Taylor solo (she pays and the realm escapes the worst) / realm-only (Taylor escapes; the cost is borne by people she meant to save) / both (full tragic alignment) / third-party (the lyra-cost lands on the witness or the opposite-number).

**Constraint:** must satisfy the retroactive-revelation structural demand — the closing image must let the reader run the chain backward.

## OQ-9 — Book structural ranges (chapters / scenes-per-chapter / bones-per-scene)
**Depends on:** OQ-5, OQ-6 (chain length).

**Admissible values:** these are typically resolved at /and-series; surfaced here only if OQ-5/6 demand a length floor.
