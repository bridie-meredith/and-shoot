taylor hebert, post gold morning, in westeros king's landing before dance of dragons. theme should be "road to hell is paved with good intentions" where taylor does bad things for a good reason which later turns out to be bad. one book long series

## Story-type (taste-judge pick at Phase 1.8)
Slow-prevention-tragedy (mirror-of-canon)
reason: forces the end-requirement to be earned structurally — Taylor's interventions become the proximate cause of the rupture she prevented, so the "road to hell" chain is reconstructible beat by beat rather than announced at the close.

## Archetype picks per role (taste-judge picks at Phase 1.8)
- protagonist characterization: cold-utilitarian — theme's irony bites hardest when Taylor's moral accounting is explicit and systematic; every trade narrows the exit.
- foil: naive-idealist — survival/death calibrates Taylor's cost without mirror-image flattening.
- false-ally: patron-with-agenda (rhaenys / rhaenyra register) — protection-for-use puts Taylor inside the dynastic machinery she is trying to forestall.
- true-ally: attached-smallfolk — single relationship Taylor will not sacrifice anchors the cost chain; the test the thematic spine names.
- antagonist-as-person: hightower-intelligence-architect (otto) — opaque institutional opponent; legible function without apex-chain polemic.
- antagonist-as-institution: Targaryen dynastic logic itself — the war is coming regardless; no face to bargain with.
- victim-by-which-cost-is-paid: specific-ward-child (lyra register) — named closing-image cost-bearer makes systemic loss particular and irreversible.
- witness: maester-chronicler — coda-only archival voice; confirms retroactive revelation without theme-narration.
- opposite-number: a canon Dance-igniter whose path Taylor's choices accelerate — visible convergence between prevention-logic and ignition.
