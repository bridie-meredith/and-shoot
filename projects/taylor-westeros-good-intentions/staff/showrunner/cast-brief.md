## 2. POV Constraint Recap

Series POV is single first-person Taylor. Every chapter is narrated by Taylor from inside her perceptual radius. Characters who are never in Taylor's direct field — who exist only as names in her accounting, as insect-feed data, as court rumor, as intelligence products — are valid cast members whose on-page presence is mediated entirely through Taylor's observation.

Non-Taylor chapters must be marked interludes. Given the 1-book, 18–22 chapter structure and the single-POV hard fence from memory, interludes are possible but not assumed; they require dramatist approval for any chapter using a non-Taylor narrator. Default assumption for /and-cast is that all 20 chapters are Taylor first-person.

Consequence for cast: every cast member's substance load must be deliverable through Taylor's perceptual access — direct scene presence, insect-feed coverage, court rumor relayed by informants, Otto's disclosed or inferred reactions. A character whose role requires information Taylor cannot observe is either underspecified (needs a delivery mechanism named) or belongs in an interlude.

## 5. Tonal/Voice Register Requirements

The signature's emotional axes load on contempt (political_register-prot, 8-rank monotonic rise), recognition (moral_legibility_to_self), un-priced anchor (relational_anchor_status), and cold-utilitarian interiority (protagonist register throughout). The cast voice requirements that follow from this load:

**Cold-utilitarian ledger register (protagonist):** Taylor's voice sets the tonal floor. Every other voice is heard through her accounting. Characters who speak in affect-heavy, declarative-emotional registers will be flattened by Taylor's perception; the cast should not require sustained emotional naturalism to deliver their substance load. Register contrast is achieved through Taylor's observation of what she will not feel, not through characters emoting at each other.

**Transactional precision (antagonist / intermediary):** Otto's voice must read as utilitarian-affinity — close enough to Taylor's own register that the relationship's uncanniness is available as a note. The intermediary reinforces this: intelligence-transaction vocabulary, no surplus. These two roles give the cast its institutional-dryness baseline above the Flea Bottom tier.

**Flea Bottom specificity (ward-elder, Wren, naive-idealist-foil):** The smallfolk-gallows register requires vocabulary grounded in neighborhood scale — blocks, coin, weather, who has been seen. Wren's observer-training habit is the critical differentiator: she names what she sees, plain and accurate, which is why her observations are useful and why Taylor hears them without perceiving them as instruments. The ward-elder's voice is seasonal and community-oriented. The foil's voice is principled and slower.

**World-embodiment opacity (Alicent, Aemond, Criston):** These figures are primarily observed, not voiced. Their register requirements are behavioral and postural rather than dialogue-weighted. Alicent: managed dynastic-maternal affect. Aemond: physical economy, controlled precision, nothing surplus. Criston: operational framing, procedural rather than cruel. The contempt that accumulates in Taylor's political_register-prot is driven by observing these registers as expressions of entitlement and consequence-blindness, so their voices must be readable as oblivious to their own power physics — not sinister in their own framing, merely indifferent to what Taylor can see.

**Anomalous register (Helaena, nice-to-have):** One voice that does not fit the contempt-accumulation model. Dreamspeak at an angle. Registers as a filing error in Taylor's accounting. Serves the vibe-cloud key "residue not spectacle" — the one figure whose strangeness is the same kind of strangeness as Taylor's insects, observed without wonder.
