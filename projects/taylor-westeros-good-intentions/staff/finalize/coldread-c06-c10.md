# Naive Cold-Read — Drag Pass, Chapters 6–10

Reader brief: dark literary fantasy, cold analytical first-person "ledger" narrator. The clipped, accounting register is the signature and is NOT flagged as such. Flagged only where density tips into genuine fatigue — abstraction without anchor, restatement, idea-repetition, passages where nothing advances.

---

## Chapter 6

- CHAPTER: 6
- ANCHOR: "because reading a thing twice is how I make sure"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The narrator literally reads the form twice and then tells us reading-twice is her method — the gloss restates the action we just watched.
- TRIM: Cut "and then I picked it up and read it again, because reading a thing twice is how I make sure I have the size of it." Keep "I read it through and set it down." The double-read is gilding the same beat.

- CHAPTER: 6
- ANCHOR: "both weighed before the hand ever reached for them"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The "verdict already reached, hand only catching up" idea is stated three times in one paragraph (Para. starting "The stylus stopped") — the stillness "was not a question," "the hand catching up to a verdict the count had reached three entries back," "weighed before the hand ever reached." Same thought, three angles.
- TRIM: Keep one statement of it. Cut "both weighed before the hand ever reached for them — so the stillness was not a question being asked." Let "the hand catching up to a verdict the count had reached three entries back" carry it alone.

- CHAPTER: 6
- ANCHOR: "the same hand had made both. I closed the notes."
- SEVERITY: FLAG
- WHY: The final beat (blank field + dispatched names made by "the same hand") re-states the moral equivalence the chapter has already fully built; it is load-bearing as a closer but slightly redundant after the ledger paragraph.
- TRIM: Note only. If trimming, "The two records sat complete... The same hand had made both" could lose "and I read them without comment" without harm. Leave the closer.

## Chapter 7

- CHAPTER: 7
- ANCHOR: "A thing built crooked doesn't come straight"
- SEVERITY: FLAG
- WHY: The septon's "crooked house / leaning compounds at its own rate" metaphor is the chapter's spine, but it is then immediately paraphrased back by the narrator ("A crooked house does not come straight by leaning it gently; the leaning compounds at its own rate") almost verbatim two paragraphs later.
- TRIM: Cut the narrator's echo sentence "A crooked house does not come straight by leaning it gently; the leaning compounds at its own rate." in Para. "I went still." The next line ("That was the architecture of the thing I had built. He was reading my own ledger back to me") makes the point without the verbatim repeat.

- CHAPTER: 7
- ANCHOR: "staying was the more expensive option, and I paid it"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The "instinct to leave / I stayed and paid the cost" beat is performed four separate times across the chapter ("I planted my feet," "staying was the more expensive option," "My feet had begun to shift toward the lane, and I settled the heel back," "Then I left, before a resolution"). The physical fidget-and-stay is dramatized once too often.
- TRIM: Collapse the two middle staying-beats. Cut "My feet had begun to shift toward the lane, and I settled the heel back instead, the weight pressing down at the cobble's edge." — it duplicates "I planted my feet... staying was the more expensive option, and I paid it" from earlier.

- CHAPTER: 7
- ANCHOR: "Two accountings stood in the cold and neither was paid"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The dueling-ledgers summary ("his slower way carried a body it could not answer for; mine carried a coverage that... compounded the corruption at its own rate") restates the exchange the dialogue already delivered cleanly. The septon then says it again in dialogue ("take the quick way and watch the debt go on growing").
- TRIM: Trim the narrator's "Two accountings stood in the cold..." paragraph to its first sentence; let the septon's reply carry the symmetry rather than pre-summarizing it.

- CHAPTER: 7
- ANCHOR: "and the knowing was not the same as the taking"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: "The road I was not taking / knowing is not taking" is stated twice in the closing two paragraphs ("the road I had chosen not to take" / "This was the road I was not taking, and the knowing was not the same as the taking").
- TRIM: Cut one. Drop "This was the road I was not taking, and the knowing was not the same as the taking." since "the clean knowledge of the road I had chosen not to take" already landed it.

## Chapter 8

- CHAPTER: 8
- ANCHOR: "the sightlines resolved as a coverage already in place"
- SEVERITY: FLAG
- WHY: This chapter is the densest abstraction stretch in the range — three watcher-figures, "overlay slots," "gap-corridors," "the geometry completes itself; the coverage-matrix subsumes." A naive reader has no concrete stake yet and the apparatus-register runs nearly anchor-free for the first half. Load-bearing (it's the surveillance net tightening) but fatiguing.
- TRIM: Note only — do not gut. But see next two entries for the specific airless lines.

- CHAPTER: 8
- ANCHOR: "Then my step landed at the close, and the circuit closes; nothing files"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The "circuit closes / nothing files / geometry updated without an entry" idea appears three times in the chapter near-identically ("the circuit closes; nothing files; the geometry has updated without a corresponding entry," and at the end "The geometry completes itself; the coverage-matrix subsumes... the water-point approach holding its feed-weight unchanged"). The unrecorded-update motif is over-rung.
- TRIM: Keep the closing instance (it ends the chapter). Cut or compress the mid-chapter "nothing files; the geometry has updated without a corresponding entry in any column I keep" to "nothing files."

- CHAPTER: 8
- ANCHOR: "the body pausing before the filing closed"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The "hand/eye pauses a beat before filing" micro-gesture is the chapter's only emotional tell and it fires four times ("my body refusing the next step," "my hand set on the next entry. I set the stylus down," "my eye held on the page a beat past the perception," "my step came shorter than the last"). By the fourth it reads as a tic, not a tell.
- TRIM: Cut one of the two desk-pause instances — drop "My hand set on the next entry. I set the stylus down. The hand stayed where it set, palm to the worked wood of the desk, the stylus released along the edge of the page." OR the bread-price "my eye held on the page a beat" — they do identical work.

- CHAPTER: 8
- ANCHOR: "the standard channel's standard receipt at the standard hour"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: Triple "standard" in one clause, and the chapter leans hard on "standard"/"the way it opens every morning"/"the standard nodes" elsewhere. The sameness-of-routine is established; the word is overworked.
- TRIM: Reduce to one "standard": "the standard channel's receipt at its hour." Same effect, less drone.

## Chapter 9

- CHAPTER: 9
- ANCHOR: "a thing with waypoints is no longer a body that happens to be in range"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The "Wren has been indexed without my asking / the keeping did it on its own" idea is the chapter's thesis and it is stated four times: epigraph ("the keeping had indexed her on its own"), "a thing with waypoints is no longer a body," "the same fold and set the hand gives a supply cart or a gate," "the keeping has indexed her without once asking." That is the same realization rephrased across one paragraph.
- TRIM: Cut "and the keeping has indexed her without once asking" at the end of the Wren paragraph — it restates the epigraph and the "supply cart or a gate" line verbatim in import. One indexing-statement per paragraph is plenty.

- CHAPTER: 9
- ANCHOR: "a direction, not a name... a direction I did not infer so much as recognize"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The Corwick/lower-gate read is delivered, then immediately re-explained in three near-synonymous clauses ("resolved to a direction, not a name," "read the gate's loyalty the way a person reads a thing already written," "a direction I did not infer so much as recognize"). Over-qualified.
- TRIM: Keep one phrasing. Cut "the split's ending held at a remove no one standing in this lane could hold, a direction I did not infer so much as recognize" — "resolved to a direction, not a name" already says it.

- CHAPTER: 9
- ANCHOR: "for the length of the press I was only a body standing in a cold lane"
- SEVERITY: FLAG
- WHY: The "for a moment I was only a body in the cold" opening grace-note recurs as a structural device (also opens the Wren beat and echoes the c10/c11 "I was a person standing in a cold place"). Load-bearing as the humanizing pulse, but it is becoming a formula across chapters — see Repetition section.
- TRIM: Note only within c9. Address at the cross-chapter level.

## Chapter 10

- CHAPTER: 10
- ANCHOR: "the geometry of him laid down circuit by circuit until it was as fixed in me"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The "I carried Corwick's body in the feed-record, circuit by circuit, nine passes, never routed" idea is stated in the epigraph, then the first body paragraph, then twice more in the closing paragraph ("withheld through nine circuits," "one more pass of a body I had held without his leave," "the months of him"). Four full restatements of the same withholding.
- TRIM: The epigraph and the second paragraph ("I had carried it for months in the feed-record... the way the record had held that posture nine times running") say the same thing. Compress the second-paragraph recitation — cut "the errand-corridor and where it emptied, the posture-classes, the gate that backed the wrong side, the geometry of him laid down circuit by circuit" since the epigraph already enumerated exactly these. Replace with a short pointer back.

- CHAPTER: 10
- ANCHOR: "the way the channel had closed over the entry"
- SEVERITY: TIGHTEN
- WHY: The closing paragraph re-runs the entire chapter's accounting a second time — "the crossing came back through the feed the way the posture had come back nine passes running, one more pass of a body I had held without his leave, and the crossing was one more entry in the months of him." This re-itemizes the months we were just walked through three paragraphs earlier.
- TRIM: Compress the ledger-close paragraph. Cut the re-enumeration "the crossing came back through the feed the way the posture had come back nine passes running, one more pass of a body I had held without his leave, and the crossing was one more entry in the months of him" — keep "the architecture that does this to bodies it never asked, doing it again in my hand." The architecture line is the payload; the recount in front of it is padding.

- CHAPTER: 10
- ANCHOR: "the posture squared itself again in the feed-record after the entry was closed"
- SEVERITY: FLAG
- WHY: The very last beat — Corwick's gait/posture persisting in the feed after the ledger closes, the narrator's gaze staying on it — is the chapter's strongest image and is load-bearing (the cost that won't file). But it slightly over-explains itself with "the lower-gate face holding in the register where the ledger kept no column for it" plus "The ledger did not ask me to stay."
- TRIM: Note only — keep the image. If anything, cut the explanatory tail "where the ledger kept no column for it"; the persistence speaks for itself.

## REPETITION ACROSS 6–10

Counts are conservative (instances I can point to verbatim or near-verbatim).

1. **"the way it ran / the way the X always ran" + "standard" routine-sameness** — pervasive. "the circuit ran the way the circuit always ran" (c8 epigraph), "the convention ran the way the convention ran" (c10), "the road outside ran the way it ran" (c10), "the way it always did now" (c11), triple-"standard" (c8), "the way it opens every morning" (c8), "marked it every cold morning" (c10). 7+ instances. The routine-drone is a deliberate texture but is over-deployed in this stretch; thin by ~30%.

2. **"a person/body standing in a cold lane/place" humanizing grace-note** — c9 "I was only a body standing in a cold lane," c10 "I was a person standing in a cold place reading a page," plus the cold-stone-under-palm/wrist/hand sensory beat in EVERY chapter (c6 court, c8 junction-stone + desk-wood, c9 lane-stone + feed-station, c10 morning-stone + feed-station-stone-under-wrist, c11 station surface). The cold-stone-grounding gesture appears 6+ times. Load-bearing once or twice per chapter as the body-anchor; currently it is the default scene-opener and has lost surprise. Vary or thin.

3. **"the hand/eye/body pauses a beat before the filing/stylus" micro-gesture** — c6 (hand stands above the board), c8 (×4: refusing the next step, set the stylus down, eye held a beat, step came shorter), c9 (gaze held a beat before she reached the corner), c10 (breath shortened once, hand turned the months). 8+ instances. This is the narrator's one emotional tell and it is being asked to carry every chapter; by c8 it reads as tic. Cut at least one per chapter.

4. **"nothing files / the geometry updates without an entry / a thing kept off the deliverable, never written down"** — c8 (×2–3), c9, c10 epigraph ("kept off the deliverable... never once written down"), c10 close ("the channel kept no column for it"). The unrecorded-cost motif is the book's thesis but is rung 5+ times in this five-chapter stretch nearly verbatim. Keep the strongest instance per chapter; cut the echoes.

5. **"crooked house / compounds at its own rate / debt grows past where I can see"** — c7, stated by the septon, then echoed near-verbatim by the narrator within two paragraphs, then a third time in the closing "compounded the corruption at its own rate." 3 instances in one chapter. Cut the narrator's echo (flagged above).

6. **"node / fixed point / feature of the ground / logistics object among logistics objects"** (person-reduced-to-object) — c7 ("a node on a map," "a feature of the ground"), c8 ("logistics object among logistics objects"), c9 ("supply cart or a gate"). 4+ instances. This is thematically central and varies its phrasing well; flagging for awareness only, NOT for cut — it earns its recurrence.

## TRIM-PASS SUMMARY
- Draggiest chapter: **Chapter 8** — densest anchor-free apparatus-register and the "pause-before-filing" tic fires four times; least concrete-stake-per-line of the five.
- Runner-up for restatement: **Chapter 10** — closing paragraph re-itemizes the whole chapter's accounting a second time.
- Cleanest: **Chapter 6** — tight, one genuine double-statement (the verdict-already-reached paragraph).
