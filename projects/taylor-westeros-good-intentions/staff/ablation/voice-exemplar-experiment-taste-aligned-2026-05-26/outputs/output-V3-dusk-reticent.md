*The crowd had broken open around the stitch-maker's ward, and the hands had gone up before mine did. After that I went back to the drain angle and did not feed anyone for three weeks. I held the count at its edge by the cost of holding it. What I did instead was start to listen — at first because subsistence-reading is what survives in the Hook, and then because the listening had a shape I had begun to extend on purpose. The rule was the same rule. Reading was not directing. I had not crossed the line.*

What I had built I did not yet have a name for. The flies and beetles told me where heat clustered and where foot-traffic knotted. I held a count of bodies in my head — who was sick, who was hungry, which alley junction emptied before dark and which one never did. I called it a coverage map when I was being plain with myself; I called it an accounting when I was not. The ledger was where it closed at end of day. The rule was that I read and did not direct. That was the line. The instrument was harm-reduction. That was how it stayed permitted.

The flies brought the heat-signatures back first. The beetles brought the threshold-count — who had crossed into the lane, how many times, with what weight on the stone. I was standing at the drain angle when the first of the three fever-signatures came in from across the alleys. Three bodies above ambient, and the day had not yet decided what to do with them. I fanned the insects out along both alley-runs to get a better read.

The ward gave me the signatures again at the alley-mouth. I put myself where the lane opened onto the Hook and let the insects cross the threshold-stones into foot-traffic. The feed filled with four hundred bodies and held. It was a number I had learned the ceiling of, not the number I had chosen. What I did not do was push it.

The alley-back went silent before I turned my head. It did not go silent because the bodies left. It went silent because I had taken the range past where the feed could carry it without dropping a sector. The dropout arrived before the intention did — which meant the range I had not yet decided to take had already been priced and charged. I was at the ceiling and one step past.

I extended the range.

There are things you decide after the fact. The prohibition was the same prohibition I had set in the same hand that was now extending. The line holds at the ceiling and not past it. I had just been past it. I drew the line there, at twelve, at the alley-wall where the Hook's full grid came into the feed — and the line drawn here is the one that will be asked of me again; I did not know that yet, but I drew it in the same hand that would be asked.

The insects filled the Hook. The foot-traffic knotted at the ward-junction — a corner in the Hook where three or four alleys gave onto one another under a single precinct-ward's reach — and the morning's pattern began to take shape in the way that patterns do when you have been holding the same count long enough that the count starts to tell you things you did not ask.
