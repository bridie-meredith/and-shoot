The tallow-damp off the lane-caulking reached me before the yard did, which is the way of that yard in the third-bell quiet, when the smallfolk-hours murmur of the cooper's workers at the near shed had not yet broken into the daylight register and the predawn held everything at the same low pressure, and I walked into the cooper's yard and took the shed-wall at my back, putting timber behind me and the open ground of the yard between me and the lane-mouth across.

The feed had him a span before the lane-mouth did. The gait that came in through the count was the gait already mapped, settled in its weight and step-interval, and a moment after the feed returned him to me the lane-mouth gave him over to my eye also, and Jarvis Coin entered the lane-mouth.

I said, "Yes. The terms hold with two changes. You will have patterns — what moves through the streets, what does not, where the seams sit — not raw report of who said what to whom. The interval is mine to set. The volume is mine to set."

The yes went across the yard-air in the cadence of a settled count, and the modification carried only the parameter; the prohibition still held. My hand closed on the shed-wall at my back.

I held my feet.

He said, "The man I serve takes those terms. The volume yours. The interval yours. The substance pattern-reports from the wards you walk. Same place. First bell. Three days."

The cadence carried receipt without the conditional that would have left the yes open, and the receipt-form settled before my body had finished holding.

The feed held the hook-range around him.

Jarvis Coin exited the lane-mouth.

I exited the cooper's yard.
