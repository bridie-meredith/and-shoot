## Pairwise differential

P1 vs P2 reveals the prime mechanism is about **where the protagonist's cost lands syntactically**. P1's prime trains the renderer to let the peak fall on a *quiet short line* — the costly recognition collapses into "The feed returned Wren. / I held my feet." The trap-and-protection identity is enacted by withholding the count, not by naming the withholding. P2's prime trains the renderer to *suspend* the recognition across a long subordinated chain that surfaces the ledger metaphor and names the paradox aloud at @19. The counterweight shape P2 achieves is real — it does invert the bones' short-stack default — but the cost is theme-as-statement at exactly the bone where restraint matters most. P1 trades cadence-counterweight for register-discipline and wins the chapter goal: the protection and the trap read as the same operation because the prose refuses to separate them, not because it describes their sameness.
