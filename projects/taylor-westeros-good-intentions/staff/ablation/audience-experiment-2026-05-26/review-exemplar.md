# Live-read pass

Okay, opening italics — Worm-Taylor, post-GM, woke in another body, two hundred metres of bug-count came with her. That's the budget declaration. Good. I want to see the cost paid on every use. "The rule is what I am holding the flies in for" — suppression as ongoing-tax, not free. Yes. That's the kind of established-limit setup I came for.

Three weeks in-ward, no name exchange, anonymity as the price of the rule. Tactical posture. Fine.

Then the lane scene. Cart blocks, child down. She holds the count at its edge and *does not move her feet* — and "the insects propagated out from the count's suppressed edge, threading through the crowd in the directions she had not named; the propagation went where she had told it to go, and the telling was a thing she would not name." Wait. Hold on. She's *using the swarm to part the crowd* while telling herself she isn't. That's the rule already cracking in scene one, and the prose half-acknowledges it ("the telling was a thing she would not name") — okay, I'll accept that as deliberate. Self-deception is paid-for. Lean in.

"Fever. Not the croup." Triage voice, clean, doesn't claim ownership. Good. Palms-out gesture as deliberate non-ownership — author is *showing* the rule in body language. Nice.

Then the Mudway elder takes the lane-mouth. Thirty years on these streets, "named what he saw without explaining why he had noticed." That's a faction-coherent observer beat — exactly the political-eyes scene I want post-rescue. Two-out-of-three: the rescue cheats slightly (swarm-assist not declared aloud), the noticing-elder lands.

Wren close — eleven-year-old, watches cloth before she cuts it, head orients a half-beat before the body. Two-watchers-now setup. Filed. Good chapter-close.

But the prose. God, the prose. "The chin lifts the way it lifts in the country's older stories when a word has been waiting for a person and the person has finally arrived to fit it." I skimmed that. Twice. Three "the count's edge" in a graf. The mannerism is *thick* — every sentence wants to be a koan. By the time I hit "the morning geometry tight" I'm fidgeting. The tactical content is here; the delivery keeps pulling me out.

Worm-fence check: insect-range was canonically much larger than 200m, but Taylor scaling down post-GM in a new body reads as deliberate nerf, not violation. Acceptable. No walkout.

# Verdict

**revise**

# Top three flags

- **Swarm-use vs the stated rule is the live tension and the prose flinches from naming it.** "The telling was a thing she would not name" is doing a lot of work to license what is, mechanically, exactly the control-via-bugs the opening rule forbade. Either pay it (let the elder *see* it, let her *know* he saw) or don't crack the rule in scene one. Right now it's having-and-eating.
- **Mannerism tax is high.** "The count's edge" repeats four+ times in the rescue graf; "the angle-gap where the drain ran" repeats verbatim; the chin-lifts-the-way-it-lifts sentence is a skim-trigger. Tactical reader gets information density elsewhere and the lyrical thickness here costs me forward momentum.
- **Mudway elder lands but Wren's tell is fuzzy.** "Watched cloth before she cut it, and watched people the same way" — fine as a card-line, but the half-beat orient-before-the-body needs one concrete physical cue I can hold. Right now she's a vibe, not a person I'm tracking for chapter two.

# What worked

The opening budget declaration plus the palms-out non-ownership gesture is a clean show-don't-tell of the central rule, and the Mudway elder taking the lane-mouth is exactly the faction-coherent noticing-beat a cape-fic chapter-one needs to set up "someone is watching, they have standing, the politics are real."
