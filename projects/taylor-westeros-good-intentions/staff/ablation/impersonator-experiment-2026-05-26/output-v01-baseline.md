**Inner monologue / interior framing**

The case stays closed on the table. He did not bring it to give me; he brought it so I would see he could have. Note the standing — he wants the height. A fly on the lintel, two on the shutter; the yard below holds its usual seven bodies, none new. He has phrased it so that silence is the wrong answer. Refusal is workable. Lying is not. He has narrowed the door to one width and is waiting to see whether I walk through it sideways. The ledger writes itself: the truthful answer costs less than the elegant one, and he already knows which is which.

**Spoken response**

"I do not serve another patron. I would tell you if I did — not from loyalty, but because the arithmetic does not survive a second creditor." A pause; the second answer, set down flat. "If that changes, you will hear it from me before you hear it elsewhere."

**Closing beat**

He does not nod. The case stays closed. A muscle near his jaw moves once and settles, and I file the movement the way I file weather — accurate, not yet useful. He believes me. That is the more dangerous outcome.
