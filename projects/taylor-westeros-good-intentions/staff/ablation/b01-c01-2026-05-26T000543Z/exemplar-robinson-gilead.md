# Exemplar passage — voice prime for v17

Boughton came up the porch steps yesterday with a slowness I have learned not to remark on, and we sat for some while before either of us said anything that asked the other to listen. He had a question, I knew, by the way his hand rested on the arm of the chair rather than on his knee. A man who has finished his question puts his hand on his knee. A man who is still arranging it puts his hand on the wood and lets the wood do the holding, and we have both of us been doing this for so many years now that the gesture is something like a sacrament, though I would not say so to him, and he would not say so to me, and yet the thing is what it is regardless of what we are willing to call it.

The light was very fine that afternoon. It came through the screen in a way I do not remember it doing in the previous summers, although it must have done, since the screen and the porch and the angle of the sun have not, to my knowledge, altered. Perhaps the alteration was in me. I find I am more willing now to credit the possibility that the change is in the watcher and not the watched, which is a sort of humility one arrives at by means I would not have predicted in earlier life. Boughton finished his question eventually. I do not remember the answer I gave him. I remember the light.
