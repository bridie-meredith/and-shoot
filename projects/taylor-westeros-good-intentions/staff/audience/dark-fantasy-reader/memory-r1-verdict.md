Three entries; all accumulate rather than resolve.

mem:1 @15: "the number the count runs past is one she reached once before, in a different architecture, in a different place, for reasons that did not hold." The "for reasons that did not hold" is the dark-fantasy form of the echo: the prior act was done for reasons that collapsed. The recurrence is not hope; it is the structure of repetition. The reasons didn't hold then; no indication they hold now.

mem:2 @25: "the shape has had her own name in the ledger since the day the work stopped being only the reading of bodies." The day named only by what it changed in the work's nature. No sentimentality, no grief-annotation. The cost was filed that day and the ledger has carried it since.

mem:3 @45: "the lane she held blank all fortnight is screened by a name she set down herself that was never the true one, and the column that would say so stays empty." The protection and the suppression are the same act. The column that would name this stays empty not because she can't write it but because she chooses not to. Cost-recorded-not-paid.

No catharsis. No resolution. Accumulation throughout.
