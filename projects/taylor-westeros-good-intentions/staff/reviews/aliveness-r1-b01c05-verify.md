## Comparison: does readability agree with FOLLOWABLE?

The two axes diverge cleanly, and the divergence is the point. Completeness was
right on its own terms — the plot IS followable: courier sighted, beaten, filed,
feed later refuses to read the route neutrally; every causal link is present and
legible. But "followable" measured the *graph*, not the *experience*: a reader can
trace what happens precisely because everything is reported in the same flat
instrument register that makes it followable — and that same register is exactly
what makes it AIRLESS. The aliveness axis catches the dense/apparatus problem
completeness structurally cannot see, because legibility-of-process and
absence-of-person are produced by the *same* property of the prose. The c05
cold-read's "abstracted into feed and count, dense to the point of obscurity" is
real and the readability axis surfaces it where completeness passed it over: the
chapter is followable precisely *because* there is no one in it to get in the way.
