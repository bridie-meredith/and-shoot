# Naive cold-read — b01 c06–c12 (seven-chapter stretch)

First read, reading pace, no outside context.

---

## Q1 — voice/register consistency — CAUTION

It is unmistakably one narrator across all seven chapters — same first-person, same compulsive double-entry mind, same trick of pricing a gesture before the hand moves. The *consistency* is not in doubt. What drifts is the density of the apparatus-vocabulary: c06 and c07 give the count a human floor (a girl crossing a crowd, a septon naming a dead child), while c08 onward the same voice thins into "the feed completed the figure," "the geometry completes itself; the coverage-matrix subsumes the watcher-positions." It is the same person — but the register tightens from "narrator using an apparatus" toward "apparatus narrating itself," which is consistency in the wrong direction. (Ch.8, lines 99, 129.)

## Q2 — setup→payoff inventory (LOAD-BEARING) — CAUTION

Promises made / receipts delivered:
- **Otto's escalating asks (c06 opens "What Otto asks for next will not be" the same form).** PAID immediately — c06 itself is the ask for names. Lands.
- **The four ward-elder names dispatched (c06).** PAID c07: the four absences return, replaced by a different patrol-shape. Clean, chilling, lands.
- **The septon's "crooked house" argument (c07).** Set up as the moral spine; never literally revisited but its logic ("compounds at its own rate," "older shape") recurs as the narrator's self-indictment through c10–c12. Carried, not dropped.
- **Corwick the courier (c08 introduced → c09 watched → c10 routed → detained).** Fully PAID, the cleanest arc in the stretch. The Gold Cloak pair at the emptied corridor is a real receipt.
- **Wren the stitch-maker, kept off every deliverable.** Set up c09, reinforced c10/c11/c12, and PAID at the close: the lane-refusal makes her free movement the map's eastern boundary, settling "the second tranche of what had long been owed." This is the load-bearing payoff and it lands, though obliquely.
- **DROPPED / thin:** the burned-page-to-Dragonstone (c11) is set up as "the enemy done before I was in it" and then abandoned mid-stretch — no receipt within these seven. "Sera" is the named consideration the whole arrangement is owed against and we never once see her; she is a pure abstraction the ledger keeps invoking. The watcher-boy gone from the water-point (c08) is filed and never returns. Aemond Targaryen (c08) is dropped in as a giant and never recurs.

## Q3 — calendar/time legibility — PASS

Trackable, and one of the prose's quiet strengths. The sept-bells' "longer count" toward the Father's name-day (c06) gives a fixed point; c07 stamps "two months old"; the Crone's stretch (c07–c12) holds the season; "two days later" (c10), "nine circuits," "this morning / that evening / two days later" all chain legibly. Bay-wind turning to bay-damp to bay-cold to "morning gone warm" at the very close (c12) is a real seasonal arc. I always knew roughly where I stood.

## Q4 — character-presence accumulation (LOAD-BEARING) — CAUTION

- **Wren (stitch-maker):** Earned, mostly. She arrives as a body before a name (c06, the route past the cart), is named c09, and the narrator's protection of her is the moral throughline. But she is *felt* most in c06 and never again given a scene — after that she is a route, "indexed without once asking," which is thematically the point but starves her of warmth. By c12 she is more cipher than person. Carried thematically; cold dramatically.
- **Septon Halvard (the figure on the corner):** The most earned presence in the stretch — c07 gives him a child's name, seven years on the corner, a real argument, a real refusal. He carries.
- **Corwick (the courier):** Arrives cold (a posture-class, a silhouette) and *stays* cold by design — but the design works: the coldness is the indictment, and the emptied corridor lands the cost. Earned in reverse.
- **The patron up the hill (Otto):** Pure off-stage pressure. Never present, only "the line," "the apparatus." Unearned as a person but functions as a weather-system, which may be intended.
- **Cold arrivals:** Aemond (c08), Sera (never seen, all stretch). Sera especially — the entire arrangement is owed against her and she is a hole in the page.

## Q5 — sensory texture distribution — CAUTION

Front-loaded. c06–c07 are richly grounded: tallow smoke pooling at foot level, the wall of warmth and cloth, ground-cold up through the soles, the carter-breath. c12 also reopens the body (warm morning, damp burned off stone). The middle — c08, c09, c10, c11 — runs largely on geometry and register: "the feed-geometry met the coverage-gap," "the body-map advanced on the read." The wax-crack and the burned-page smoke are the recurring sensory anchors that keep the middle from going fully abstract, but they are doing heavy lifting almost alone. Texture is real at the edges, scarce in the belly.

## Q6 — apparatus-register cumulative load (LOAD-BEARING) — FAIL

It tips, and it tips hard in the middle three chapters. c06 and c07 earn the apparatus by anchoring it to bodies — the count is a tool a person holds. By **c08 the register has eaten the person**: sentences like "the feed completed the figure and I stood through the completion," "the geometry completes itself; the coverage-matrix subsumes the watcher-positions it has been adjacent to, smooth," and "nothing files; the geometry has updated without a corresponding entry in any column I keep" read exactly as data-entry in riddles — airless, agentless, no hand to hold onto. **c09** continues it (waypoints, posture-class, "I priced the posture-class and the price was small"). **c11** is the most overloaded: the threefold "arm closed / arm closed / arm closed" accounting at the close is structurally clever but reads as a spreadsheet recited aloud. The prose recovers a person at c12's open and close, but the cumulative load across c08–c11 crossed my tolerance: for roughly the middle half of the stretch I was decoding columns, not reading a woman. The technique that thrills in c06 (the blank field as the entry) is, by c10–c11, a tic.

**Tip chapters: c08, c09, c11.** (Ch.8 lines 99, 129; Ch.11 line 185.)

## Q7 — sub-section feel — CAUTION

It reads as a genuine sub-section in its *spine* — one continuous moral escalation (asks→names→courier→Wren-boundary) with real accumulation. But every chapter opens with an italic prologue that re-states the same standing conditions ("the arrangement holds," "the count runs the Hook at standard density," "the harm I can prevent is still the only column that closes"). Across seven chapters that refrain becomes a seam I can see — the chapters are genuinely connected underneath, but the prologues glue rather than flow, and their near-identical phrasing made the middle stretch feel recapped rather than advanced.

## Q8 — close-of-section pleasure — CAUTION

Yes, narrowly — because the final move (Wren's lane as load-bearing un-priced boundary, and "the older word... the thing I did when the world was ending") opens a real door I want walked through; but I want the *next* chapter to put a body back in front of me, because four of these seven left me reading the ledger instead of the woman keeping it.
