The `orchestrator-critic` run-judge card is built to judge a *live `/and-season`/cascade run*
(Category-A convergence, dispatch caps, F7 bone-gate). Those metrics do not apply to a post-hoc
re-read of an already-shipped book — there is no live run to score. So this is rendered as a
**narrative re-certification**: the load-bearing inputs are the naive cold-read of the rebuilt prose
(the direct test of the DEC-0115 fix) + the structural-sameness detector + the standing cohesion
queue. The honesty discipline (§Honesty discipline of the card) is binding: the ruling is the
evidence's, not the rebuild's self-report.

## The question DEC-0115 set: did the rebuild clear the airless-register failure?

**YES — cleared.** The naive cold-reader (no contract context, reading as a reader who picked the
book up) found: a person is present to inhabit; followability is STRONG (no reader gets lost); the
prose renders concrete human action, not bookkeeping; the ending lands. Per-third: **c01–c07 ALIVE**
(the c07 septon argument is the book's high point), **c08–c13 MIXED** (human spine holds; apparatus
nouns reassert), **c14–c20 MIXED** (concrete wins beside residual bookkeeping). The systemic
ledger-as-narration failure that made the prior version unreadable is **gone**. This is the thing
that mattered, and the rebuild delivered it.

## What did NOT clear (the honest reservations)

1. **Back-third structural monotony — NOT cleared (the original problem (b)).** The structural-sameness
   detector returned **SAMENESS-HIGH**: 8 of 10 mid-book chapters (c10–c19) run one scene template
   (`packet → transcribe → withhold-the-protected-name → lift-stylus → hand-off-surface`); breaks only
   at c13/c16. The cold-reader independently felt it: the back third reads as "the same scene seven
   times," and a real reader could "plausibly bail between c17 and c19" — the exact danger zone the
   detector flagged (unbroken c17→c18→c19 run before the payoff; c19 narrates its own repetitiveness).
   **Non-fatal:** the withhold-name beat accretes meaning by design (Wren → false-name → blank-column →
   Daven) and **c20 detonates the pattern and retroactively justifies it** — the book is finishable and
   the payoff lands. But the run-length overruns its safe span. **Named remediation:**
   `pl-2026-06-08-sameness-001` (a light, targeted 3-chapter cohere — c18 entry/exit + c11 opening +
   terminal-tic on ~3 chapters; NOT a restructure).

2. **Residual ledger-instinct on plot-reporting — render-layer creep.** Whenever the prose *reports*
   high politics (the Otto/Dance/Small-Council climax), it slips to italic prologues and abstract
   clauses rather than dramatized scene ("the succession channel was the Greens' now"). This is a
   bounded render-choice creep, not the systemic failure of before. Render-layer; addressable at
   targeted re-stitch if pursued.

3. **Standing cohesion item (unaffected by rebuild): Sera promise-payoff drop** — court-tier
   protect-target introduced c03, never staged as a person, c20 decommission doesn't confirm she was
   protected. Flagged by the (now-superseded) cohere run; carried as `pl-2026-06-06-cohere-002`,
   principal-deferred per **DEC-0108**. Distinct from the by-design on-page non-naming
   (`pl-2026-05-28-002`). Still open; still deferred.
