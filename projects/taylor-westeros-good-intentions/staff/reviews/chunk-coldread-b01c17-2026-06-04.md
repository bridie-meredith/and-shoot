**1. EVENTS**
- S1: Taylor retrieves a hidden cipher-sheet at the water-trough. A query asks her to identify an unknown woman moving through a coverage gap. She recognizes the gait as Wren's.
- S2: Taylor walks in the dark and weighs two options: name Wren (exposure) or pin the movements on someone else (a false record). She doesn't resolve it.
- S3: Taylor goes to her room, pulls her coverage log, and falsely attributes Wren's movement-signature to a different elder, "Norren." She writes three back-dated supplementary entries.
