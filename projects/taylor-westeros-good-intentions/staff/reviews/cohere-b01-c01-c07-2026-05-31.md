## Headline

Antagonist pressure (Otto-Jarvis-the-named-Hightowers) accumulates correctly. But three load-bearing failure modes break cohere:

1. **Naive Q4 — character-presence accumulation: Wren Stitch-Maker arrives cold at her c06 load-bearing moment.** She is named in c01 as "the stitch-maker's ward" (one line, line 67), referenced abstractly in c02 ("the ward-junction body" filed by the feed without being recognized as Wren, lines 100-115), spotted-and-not-reported in c04 (lines 275-278, 313), then in c06 walks into a load-bearing kindness — providing the route around the blocked lane — that triggers the entire confrontation Halvard names in c07. The reader has been given her *name* once, in c01, and never since. By c06 she is functionally a cold walk-on: the prose says "She found me before her feet turned" and asks the reader to register an established relationship that the prior five chapters have systematically un-named. This is the c01 register choice ("nobody here knows my name and I know nobody's name") executing exactly as Taylor narrates it — but the prose-layer effect is that Wren cannot carry the c06 hinge weight she is asked to carry. Load-bearing: a structural beat depends on her arriving felt-as-carried.

2. **Naive Q6 — apparatus-register cumulative load.** Each chapter individually passed `/and-stitch` Phase 9 PROP-0022 readability twin. Across seven chapters, the apparatus accumulates past sustainable density. The register is "the feed," "the count," "the ledger," "the accounting," "the architecture," "the routing," "the operational layer," "the file," "the column," "the receipt-form." Every interaction is mediated through accounting vocabulary. Character bodies appear as "body-counts," "node," "function-signature," "connector-type." This *is* Taylor's voice and *is* the substance contract (her instrument is the cost), but seven chapters of it without sufficient sensory-grounded counterweight strains the reader's tolerance. The single sustained grounded passages — Halvard's confrontation in c07, the Rushwick alley-strike in c05 — work specifically because they break register. The reader needs more such breaks earlier. Cumulative load-bearing: by c05-c06 the prose reads as accounting-document. By c07 Halvard's "crooked house" lands harder than it should *because* the apparatus had drained the prose first — which is a feature for c07, a bug for c04-c06.

3. **Audience SUBSTANCE-FLAT (cape-fic-reader rotation).** See Fork C below. The cape-fic-reader rotation reads c01-c07 as four chapters of coverage-buildup, two chapters of single deal, one chapter of moral argument. The asymmetry that "reshapes the board" — the cape-fic-reader's prime substance axis — happens only twice (c03 deal struck, c06 four names submitted). Two reshapes in seven chapters is below cape-fic-reader's sustainable rate. The substance contract claims six axes of motion across the stretch; the prose delivers ledger-bookkeeping on one axis (instrument-vs-rule) and accumulates the other five in narration without dramatic embodiment.

The PASS axes (dramatist promise-payoff, dramatist antagonist pressure, naive Q2 setup-payoff at the macro level) confirm the structural skeleton is sound. The FAIL axes are about prose-layer accumulation, not contract failure.
