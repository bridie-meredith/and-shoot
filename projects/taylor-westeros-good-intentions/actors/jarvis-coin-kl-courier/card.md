# Jarvis Coin — Green Faction Courier Tier

## Description

A courier-tier operative in the Green faction's informal intelligence network. Not a named agent; not a lord's man; not a sworn sword. He handles the physical exchange of intelligence products at the boundary layer between Flea Bottom and the city's upper-tier circulation. His function: arrive at designated location at designated time, receive or deliver a message-container, confirm receipt, depart. Thirty seconds to three minutes per exchange.

He is approximately twenty-eight years old, lower-city origin (broader bracket than the Hook). "Coin" is an acquired surname in the Flea Bottom practice for those of unclear parentage. Green faction tie is purely transactional — paid by a man named Pell three tiers below Otto; no ideological investment in the succession.

He does not know what Taylor is. He knows she delivers accurate products at a reliable rate. The mechanism is not his concern. The exchange is his function.
