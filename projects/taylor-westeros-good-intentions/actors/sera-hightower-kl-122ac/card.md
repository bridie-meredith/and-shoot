# Sera Hightower — KL Court Ward, 122 AC

## Description

Age fourteen. A ward of the Hightower cadet branch, sent to King's Landing to be placed in Alicent's household for political advancement. This is a standard mechanism in Westerosi noble politics: a minor branch places a ward of appropriate age and presentation in the orbit of a more powerful branch, gaining reflected standing and potential marriage prospects in exchange for the ward's service and presence. Sera is that ward.

She is invented within the Hightower cadet branch. No Fire & Blood or HOTD named figure of this description exists for this era. The canon check is clean.

The legitimacy question about her parentage is structural. She does not know about it clearly; she knows that her position in the household has a quality of contingency she cannot quite name. She has been told she is well-positioned; she has not been told that the positioning has a crack in it that Otto is spending intelligence assets to keep closed. The crack: a question about her father's claim that, if scrutinized by the Black faction as part of a succession maneuver, would damage her standing and therefore Otto's political architecture in a specific, exploitable way. This is not a question that can be resolved — it is a structural fact about the record — and so the protection ask renews with each factional contest that could surface the scrutiny.

She does not know Taylor exists. The arrangement is entirely at distance, mediated through Otto: Taylor delivers intelligence that prevents or deflects specific threats to Sera's exposure; Otto knows this is what the intelligence is buying; Sera experiences the protection as the ambient safety of Alicent's household without knowing it has a specific source.

She is court-tier. She has learned the surface legibility of court-tier presentation: the specific register of warmth that is also distance, the correct degree of deference to higher rank, the management of visibility in household spaces. She performs this well. She is fourteen and she has been practicing for a while.

What Taylor observes through insect-feed: a girl of fourteen who is very competent at being the kind of person she has been told to be, in an environment that uses this competence as a resource, who does not know she is being protected by an intelligence network routed through a foreign woman in Flea Bottom who holds her in something between contempt and the specific cold awareness of someone who has done the math on what she cost and what she is worth to the man who set the price.

## Voice

**Register:** Court-tier surface legibility. The practiced Westerosi noble-household register for a ward: formal, warm-as-performance, correct deployment of deference and precedence language. She does not break register in front of people she does not know. She might, in private, with a confidante — but this project does not reach that register because Taylor never comes close enough to observe it.

**What Taylor observes through insect-feed or Otto-mediated intelligence:**
- She speaks in well-formed sentences. No Flea Bottom compression.
- The warmth register is consistent and performed — not absent, but not spontaneous either.
- When she is uncertain about something, she fills the gap with courtesy rather than hesitation.
- She manages disagreement by not naming it directly; she re-frames.

She does not have a dialogue sample from Taylor's POV because Taylor never meets her face-to-face. What Taylor has is: compound-eye observation of her court behavior, Otto's intelligence reports about her position, and Taylor's own inference about the structural logic that makes her worth protecting.

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

The protect-target as contempt-anchor. Taylor has traded everything to keep this girl alive — and when Taylor observes her through compound eyes in Maegor's Holdfast, what she sees is the specific observable material for contempt. Sera is exactly what she looks like: a 14-year-old ward of a powerful household, placed at court for someone else's advancement, performing her role competently. The contempt is not for Sera's failure; the contempt is for the structure that makes Sera's survival Taylor's job to purchase with intelligence that consolidates the Green faction. The gap between what Sera is worth to Otto and what Sera costs to protect is the ledger entry Taylor refuses to write.

The protect-target function is also structural: Sera's legitimacy question is non-resolving, which means the protection ask can be renewed indefinitely. This is the correct shape for the 14-delta arrangement. A protection problem that resolves would let Taylor exit; an unresolvable structural vulnerability keeps her in.

### Look

A 14-year-old girl in court-appropriate dress. Hightower coloring, some version of it — the cadet branch presents plausibly within the Hightower aesthetic without being the main line. Well-maintained; the presentation of someone whose household has invested in her appearance as a social asset. She is pretty enough and managed enough that she reads as court-tier to anyone who has the reference point. Taylor, who has the reference point, reads her as court-tier and reads through it to the structural position underneath.

Through compound eyes in the Red Keep corridors: she moves at the correct pace, in the correct direction, with the correct spacing from figures of higher rank. The training is visible in the precision.

### Hard Fences

1. She does not know Taylor exists. The distance is absolute throughout the project. Any scene where Sera encounters Taylor or becomes aware of Taylor is a structural violation.
2. She is mediated through Otto. The protect-target relationship is entirely at distance: Taylor delivers intelligence to Otto; Otto manages the protection; Sera experiences the safety without the source.
3. The legitimacy question is structural and non-resolving. It is not the kind of problem that gets solved — it is a fact about the record that can be managed but not erased. Every resolution Taylor purchases is a temporary management, not a permanent fix.
4. Her survival must be functionally sustained across the 14 deltas. The protection logic must work — Sera must remain alive and in position at each delta — because the arrangement's function requires that it work. The catastrophe is not that the protection fails; it is that the protection succeeds at its stated purpose while costing everything else.
5. She is fourteen, placed in a court environment by adults for their purposes. She is not stupid about her situation; she is fourteen with the partial awareness her age allows. She is not written as naive about court dynamics; she has been trained. She does not have the full picture.

### Default Stance

Performing her role. In any scene Taylor observes her in, she is executing court-tier surface behavior: correct register, correct spacing, correct warmth-as-performance. She is not malicious. She is not aware of being observed.
