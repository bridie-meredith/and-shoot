### Does the moral_framework breach land felt through the concrete acts?

Yes.

The original bones passed the gate on substance-logic — the accounting ran honestly and arrived at delivery, the breach was at the seal, the architecture was correct. What they could not pass was the readability axis: "writes the first arm — names against Sera's protection" and "squares the jarvis-channel form" were apparatus-SVOs reporting their own operation. A reader tracking whether the accounting *costs* something had to supply that weight themselves. The bones were providing ledger entries, not acts.

The recast sequence changes the load-bearing relationship:

**Bone 1 (opens the ledger-board):** Physical. She picks up or unfolds a physical writing surface. Not a metaphor-container; a real object with real weight. The ledger-board is a thing in the room.

**Bones 2–3 (writes the ward-elder names / writes the sera-coverage entry):** Concrete inscriptions. Two distinct writing acts, two distinct categories of inscription. The split matters — the ward-elder names and the Sera-coverage entry are not the same kind of content being written in the same kind of gesture. The two-bone treatment enacts this as two separate acts the hand performs. The original "first arm" framing collapsed them into an accounting concept; the recast makes them sequential physical marks.

**Bone 4 (marks the red-keep coverage record):** A third distinct act at a different substrate. Three writing surfaces, three writing acts. This is a person doing precise, methodical work across institutional records — not a metaphor for that work.

**Bone 5 (holds the stylus — NEW verdict-pause):** This is the bone that earns the breach. The stylus held between the completed accounting and the sealing. Taylor has finished the ledger-work; the Jarvis form is ready; the ward-coverage record is current; the only thing standing between her and delivery is the physical act of sealing. She holds the stylus. This is not a pause for deliberation — Taylor's accounting has already deliberated. It is the body's record of the weight before the irrevocable act. A tool held still is the correct register: not drama, not hesitation labeled, not a beat that announces itself. The stylus is the right object because it is the writing instrument — she used it to complete the accounting that authorized this delivery, and now she is holding it before the delivery executes. The residue is enacted as physical posture. That is exactly the Taylor bone-gate the prior chapter work has been building toward.

**Bone 6 (closes the ledger-board):** The ledger closes. Both arms in, mark made, entry complete. Physical closure of a physical object.

**Bone 7 (lifts the jarvis-channel form):** The Jarvis form is a separate physical object lifted. The action separates the accounting from the send — they are not simultaneous; the ledger closes first, then the form is lifted. This sequence is correct: the accounting is not still open when she dispatches. She is not dispatching while still weighing. She has already finished weighing. That is the rationalize-each-trade horror.

**Bone 8 (seals the jarvis-channel form — moral_framework −1.0):** The breach is a sealing. A physical act with irreversible status. A sealed form cannot be unsealed. The axis-move lands here, on the physical irreversibility, not on any conceptual closure. The original "squares the jarvis-channel form" was apparatus-register: squaring a form is administrative prep, a pen-and-ruler act that precedes the real thing. "Seals" is the real thing.

**Bone 9 (the courier takes the jarvis-channel form):** The form leaves Taylor's hands. The handoff is physical and complete. The action is attributed to the courier — it is *taken*, not merely accepted. The moral weight passes with the object.

**Bone 10 (opens the ward-coverage notes — moral_legibility_to_self +0.5, realized +1.0):** The contrast mechanism. She opens a second record — the one that would contain Wren's name if Wren were in the accounting. She does not open it for Wren. She opens it as part of her normal record-maintenance. The juxtaposition is architectural: the form just dispatched carried four ward-elder names; this record carries no name in Wren's field. The irony is not stated. The opening enacts it.

**Bone 11 (closes the ward-coverage notes):** Closed. Entry complete (or complete in its incompleteness). Both records closed, both institutional substrates updated, the chapter's accounting done.
