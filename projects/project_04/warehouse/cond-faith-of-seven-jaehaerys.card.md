# Faith of the Seven — Institutional Behavior at 84–99 AC (Jaehaerys I Era)

## Description

How the Faith of the Seven operates as an institution at 84–99 AC. Covers: the post-Faith-Militant institutional structure, hierarchy and communication patterns, theological categorization of uncanny phenomena, the Faith's relationship to the smallfolk it serves, and what Faith institutional actors can and cannot do in this era.

**Critical era constraint:** The Faith Militant was suppressed by Jaehaerys I approximately fifty years before story open. The Faith at 84 AC operates with full awareness that it is not permitted an organized military arm. Any scene implying Faith-organized violence or armed Faith enforcement is a hard fence violation for this era.

## Sensory Impact

The Faith's ambient sensory presence in Fairstead: the parish sept's bell marking the canonical hours, the smell of tallow candles and cold stone, the sound of collective prayer on holy days, the septon's voice at market. The Faith is present as smell and sound and routine — background to daily life, not intermittent event.

## Duration

Persistent across the project window. The Faith's institutional structure does not change significantly within 84–101 AC. Septon Barth's death (~99 AC) begins a slow drift in the Citadel-Faith collaborative model.

## Post-Faith-Militant Institutional Character

**What the Faith cannot do at 84 AC:**
- Field soldiers or men-at-arms
- Compel compliance by armed force
- Arrest, detain, or physically enforce rulings

**What the Faith does instead:** Pastoral authority (moral influence), charitable redistribution (the septon controls who receives alms), communication network (letters through the septon hierarchy, circuit septons as traveling messengers), institutional records (births, deaths, marriages), and social pressure (excommunication, denial of ceremony, community religious opinion).

These are substantial tools. They are not swords.

## Hierarchy and Communication

| Level | Title | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Peak | High Septon (Oldtown) | Doctrinal authority; political relations with Iron Throne |
| Regional | Septons-Superior | Regional administrative authority; oversee diocese circuits |
| Circuit | Circuit septons (traveling) | Move between village septs on seasonal routes; carry information and authority |
| Parish | Local parish septons (resident) | Serve a specific community full-time — Rowan is this level in Fairstead |

**Communication flow:**
- Normal channel: circuit septons carry verbal reports and letters on their routes. Rowan's circuit visitor arrives in Fairstead approximately twice per year.
- Emergency channel: the Faith does not have a raven network of its own. A septon who needs urgency sends a road messenger, or asks a lord's maester to send a raven on Faith business (which may or may not be granted).

**How information about Taylor travels:** Rowan witnesses a swarm event → categorizes theologically → writes letter to circuit septon on next visit → Septon-Superior reads and decides whether to file, send an investigator, or escalate → this process takes months, not days.

## Theological Categorization of Uncanny Phenomena

**Available categories for Taylor's swarm events:**
- Divine intercession / the Seven's hand (favorable — Rowan's default reach)
- Blessing of the Stranger (unusual but not accusatory — governs the uncanny-but-not-evil)
- Curse or malefic working (adversarial — for when power serves a bad purpose)
- Old magic / pre-Faith forces (fits events that don't cleanly map to Seven-theology)
- Witchcraft or diabolism (adversarial extreme — requires evidence of agency and intent)

**What the Faith does with its categorization:** Categorization is a pastoral act, not a judicial one. The Faith cannot arrest Taylor. It can refer her to a lord's authority with a letter recommending investigation. The political weight of a septon's letter of recommendation is not nothing — it is exactly the kind of soft pressure the Faith has instead of swords.

## The Faith's Relationship to Smallfolk

The Faith is the most consistently accessible institution for smallfolk. The parish septon is the person smallfolk go to for charity, recording of births and deaths, management of grief, and resolution of minor disputes. Taylor's organizing will intersect with the Faith's charity network and record-keeping function at every level.

## Septon Rowan Specifically (Fairstead Parish)

Rowan is a resident parish septon — lowest level of the Faith hierarchy. Nineteen years in Fairstead. He receives circuit septons; reports to the circuit septon; writes letters that travel upward through the circuit.

Rowan's position: he has been constructing a theological framework around Taylor's swarm-sense since before the ignition. His framework: she is in a special relationship with the Seven, specifically aspects dealing with the natural order (the Stranger, the Smith-who-shapes-matter). He does not think she is a witch. He thinks she is blessed in a way she has not chosen to be.

This creates a specific dynamic: Rowan is an ally building a category around Taylor that she cannot control and may not want. His theological construction protects her from worse interpretations but makes her visible in ways she would not choose. His friendship is its own kind of constraint.
