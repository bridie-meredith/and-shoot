# The Fairstead River Dock and Ferry Crossing

The town's transit throat. Flat-bottom ferry, one crossing point on the tributary. Ferryman: Tomm Weyl (carry-forward character; knows everyone who passes). Garrison posts here during census and collection periods. Insect ecology: water beetles, mosquitoes, horseflies at the livestock transit pen. Taylor's aquatic-insect calibration is lower than her terrestrial calibration. Full card at library source.
