# Fairstead Market Square — Ignition Site

The commercial center of Fairstead. ~40x30 paces, open to the sky. Ignition event site (~86 AC). Livestock pens at east end: animal-pen flies are Taylor's primary offensive resource during the ignition. Garrison outpost at northeast corner has direct sightline to the collection table. Full witness-position and ignition-sequence blocking in full card at library source.
