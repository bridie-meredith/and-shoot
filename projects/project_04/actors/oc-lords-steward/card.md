# Aldric Pryor (Steward of House Ryger at Fairstead)

Ryger steward for the Fairstead district. Thorough, non-corrupt, non-cruel — which makes him more dangerous than a cruel man. He made the first institutional note after the ignition event (~86 AC). He is the spine of the antagonist-escalation through to mid-S3 suppression-as-policy. House Ryger of Willow Wood is the committed lord's house. Full card at library source.
