# Mira Stonefield — Jaehaerys Variant (~50, Fairstead)

A smallfolk woman of approximately fifty, thirty years in Fairstead. Informal community elder with network authority that unlocks community-level organizing. Not a war survivor — her background is economic precarity under Jaehaerys's peace. Skeptical of Taylor on two axes simultaneously: network access AND community legitimacy. Both must be earned from her simultaneously, which is harder and more interesting than sequential earns.
