## Coverage check (orchestrator's pre-review read)

- **Tension axes covered:** intimate (mother + father + Taylor), institutional (lord's steward + Rowan as Faith-witness counterweight), peer-authority (community elder, Mira), ground-witness (Rymer). 
- **Genre register served:** smallfolk-organizer canvas is dense; the Faith and the lord's apparatus both have early-episode presence; no aristocrat in the cast (correct per OQ-4/A; restraints against drift to noble-reformer narrative).
- **Worm-Taylor demands met:** the family is the test of Taylor's self-isolation. Mira and the elder are the test of her control-as-substitute-for-trust. The steward and Rowan are the institutional weight.
- **Gaps the screen-writer/dramatist may flag:** no aristocrat means the Targaryen / Great Council horizon is offstage until S2+; no child-peer in starter (Taylor's "performing as child" friction is carried only by family). May need to add `oc-child-peer` for early-episode dramatic range or accept that the friction is the family alone for S1.
