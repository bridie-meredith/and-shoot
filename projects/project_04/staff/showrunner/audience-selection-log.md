## Audience selection

brief axes: Worm-protagonist (Taylor Hebert) reincarnation isekai into Westeros at 289 AC (Aerys late reign, Rebellion-imminent grimdark feudal setting); core register named in brief is "social warrior escalation" — organizing-and-counter-escalation as engine; subject crosses two heavy source materials (Worm canon + ASOIAF lore).

selected:
  - worm-canon-pedant: Worm-canon axis — Taylor characterization, swarm-sense mechanics, escalation logic must read as Taylor and not Generic Isekai Heroine; brief explicitly invokes both protagonist and "escalation" as Worm-shaped concepts
  - dark-fantasy-reader: world-weight axis — Westeros 289 AC is grimdark with earned consequence (Aerys, Defiance of Duskendale aftermath, looming Rebellion); needs a reader who will catch when Westerosi political physics is being treated as a backdrop rather than as material constraint
  - pulp-enthusiast: momentum/escalation axis — brief names "escalation" as the engine; without a momentum reader the series can drift into 12 chapters of court manners; pulp pushes for stakes, pacing, the escalation actually escalating

rejected:
  - cape-fic-reader: overlaps worm-canon-pedant on character-stakes/rule-consistency without adding distinct axis
  - literary-snob: voice/theme axis already partially served by dark-fantasy-reader's world-weight read; pulp's momentum axis is the more pressing gap given the brief's stated register
  - all stubs: not selected; full personas cover the three primary axes the brief opens
