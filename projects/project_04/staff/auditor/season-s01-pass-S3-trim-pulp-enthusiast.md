## North Star (Season Drama Statement)

A reincarnated organizer spends two years building an invisible operational baseline inside a world too small to hide in forever, then loses her invisibility in a single involuntary act at the market square — and the four years that follow are the long cost of what gets built under the steward's growing file and what gets paid by the people she cannot keep at managed distance before a stranger in maester's robes arrives at Fairstead with someone else's letter in his bag.

**Pulp read on north star:** Clear escalation spine — INVISIBLE → EXPOSED → COST-PAYING. Complication density demands at minimum: one board-change per beat, no soft landings. Every non-blank line must serve IGNITION approach or IGNITION fallout.

## Sweep 1 — Per-line Trim Against Season Goal

### Method

Lines are deletion candidates when they:
(a) do not advance Taylor's operational-cover status, the swarm-sense ecology, or the institutional threat-level, AND
(b) are not voice-load-bearing (a gesture that carries interior register counts; a repeated position-hold does not).

Blank / gap lines are structural breathing room — not counted against density but noted when they inflate quiet sections past tolerance.
