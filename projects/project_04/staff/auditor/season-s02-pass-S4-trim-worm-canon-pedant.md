## Named structural faults

**FAULT-WCP-001: body-clock differentiation mandate — B4 passive-hold (critical fault)**

Location: bones 593–633 (W44–W45) and 731–760 (W50).

The season-plan H section commits explicitly: "Beat 4 passive-sense extended hold — show what the new ceiling returns that the old could not hold." The B4 passive-hold sequence runs at bones 731–760 (the second full garrison-grid extension after Rymer's behavioral delta). These 30 bones show the flies reaching the garrison waypost and perimeter at extended range — but the bones are coverage statements ("the flies cover the garrison waypost," "the flies cover the road's second stretch") that are formally identical in register to the pre-transition grid coverage bones. The canonical Taylor would register the extended reach as a cost-shape difference, not as a coverage expansion. The differentiation mandate requires a bone showing the physical cost of the extended hold — specifically, the season-plan notes that the reach Taylor now has (extended insect-grid coverage of the road corridor, sustained passive-sense through the garrison outpost's insect-wrong zone) is what makes the Rymer behavioral-delta logging possible, and "the aggregate writer must show the difference between what the old ceiling would have returned and what the new ceiling returns here — not as exposition, but as a bone showing the physical cost of the extended hold." No such bone exists.

Assessment: This is the cost-language vs. discipline-language fault named in the season-scope-adversarial: rendering shard-load as patience flattens the canonical distinction. The extended hold across 30 bones reads as patience (Taylor persisting), not as cost (the new ceiling extracted at the body level). The differentiation bone is absent.

Criteria for resolution: a bone within the B4 passive-hold sequence (roughly bones 731–760) must distinguish the new active-control cost from the pre-transition baseline; the bone must mark the hold's physical consequence, not merely the area covered; the area covered is incidental; the cost-register is the content.
