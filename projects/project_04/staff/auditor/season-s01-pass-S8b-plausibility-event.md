### Scene N (continued) — Inquiry rider (beats 542–553)

**542–553:** IMPLAUSIBLE-EVENT — cond-suppression-policy-progression / cond-riverlands-84ac-state

**id: IMPLAUSIBLE-EVENT-001**

The inquiry rider enters the market square (beat 542), speaks to the town reeve (beat 545), and the town reeve receives a letter (beat 548) — the same day or very shortly after the swarm event.

Per cond-suppression-policy-progression Stage 1: the ignition event produces a note in Pryor's file; the note costs nothing; a single incident is not worth formal escalation; Pryor begins actively monitoring only at Stage 2, triggered by a second anomalous event or accumulated pattern. The steward already drew his incident folio and marked it (beats 496–499) during the event itself.

An inquiry rider arriving at Fairstead on the same day or within hours of the ignition event implies an instantaneous institutional response at a level above Stage 1. There is no Fairstead raven rookery — per cond-maester-network-behavior, the raven network does not extend to market towns; Fairstead does not have a raven rookery; Pryor controls what enters the raven network from Ryger's end. Pryor was physically present at the event. He cannot have dispatched a rider to produce an inquiry simultaneously with marking his own incident folio.

The alternative reading — that the inquiry rider is arriving on a pre-existing, unrelated mission — is possible but is not established in the aggregate. The placement (beats 542–553) directly follows the family's post-ignition retreat (beats 521–540) and the rider's conversation with the reeve involves delivering a letter (beat 547–548), which reads as a response to the event rather than a coincidence.

**Why it matters:** If the aggregate implies the institutional apparatus is capable of same-day inquiry-rider responses to an incident in Fairstead, that collapses the Stage 1 / Stage 2 distinction in cond-suppression-policy-progression, mis-characterizes the pace of the long-peace administrative apparatus (cond-riverlands-84ac-state: "slower institutional escalation — Taylor has runway"), and breaks the plausibility of the Jaehaerys-era administrative physics. The suppression's horror is that it is methodical and slow and documents everything — not that it is fast.

**Criteria for correction:** The inquiry rider scene (beats 542–553) must be either (a) established as arriving for a pre-existing, independent administrative purpose unrelated to the swarm event, with a beat establishing its prior scheduling or nature; or (b) re-timed so that it occurs at a plausible interval after the event (minimum days, realistically weeks — the time for Pryor to return to his administrative seat, assess his note, and dispatch a rider via road). Same-day or hours-after timing is not plausible under the long-peace administrative physics.
