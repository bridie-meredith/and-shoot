## Notes for showrunner

- rhaenyra-targaryen is the highest-value deferred character: she is 13 at story open and grows up across the series, becoming the central Black faction figure as the Dance approaches. She is the most interesting human foil for Taylor and the only canon figure with both dragon-bond and political-heir status.
- The OC-lord slot (oc-lord-bracken-bastard) is load-bearing for S1 — Taylor needs a human character who makes the choice to back her rather than report her. Without this, S1 is Taylor alone vs. everyone, which limits dramatic range.
- The septon slot is optional for S1 but creates the cult-of-power-genesis layer the brief-expansion flagged as an interesting secondary engine.
