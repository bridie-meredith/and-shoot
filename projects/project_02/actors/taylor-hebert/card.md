# Taylor Hebert — Project Card (taylor-in-westeros)

## Project Entry Override

**Entry state for this project:** Post-Gold Morning. Taylor is dead — her human body is gone. Her consciousness has persisted through reincarnation into a dragon egg in the Riverlands of Westeros, approximately 110 AC. She has hatched. She remembers everything: the locker, the Undersiders, Gold Morning, the fact that she ended the parahuman age. She is now a dragon.

**Name usage in this project:** She has no name in this body. The Westerosi characters she encounters will try to give her one or assign her one through the categories they use for dragons. She does not have a cape name; she does not have a civilian name in this world. Internal narration uses "Taylor." External references will develop as the story develops.

**Power mechanics in this project:** See condition-shard-in-dragon-body. Her insect-control power persists. Her dragon body provides additional capabilities (fire, flight, physical force). She has two toolkits. See also condition-swarm-in-foreign-ecology for what insects are available in the Westerosi Riverlands ecology.
