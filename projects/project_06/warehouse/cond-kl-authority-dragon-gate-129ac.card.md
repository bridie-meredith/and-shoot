# KL Authority Architecture — Dragon Gate Territory (126–129 AC)

## Description

Project-scoped authority and institutional structure governing the Dragon Gate territory and the political context of 126–129 AC. Derives from `cond-westerosi-customary-authority-125ac` (flea-bottom-dance project card, itself the 125 AC KL authority architecture text) with adjustments for: Dragon Gate territory rather than Flea Bottom; Taylor ~19yo at arrival (not 17yo); the Watch-patronage / Green-infrastructure dynamic that is the spine of this project's foreclosure; and the 126–129 AC political temperature (the Dance's approach bleeds into street-level operations per OQ-3 Act 2).

For underlying legal structure, load `cond-feudal-hierarchy-law`. For smallfolk internalized physics, load `cond-smallfolk-political-physics`. This card is the institutional overlay for the specific Dragon Gate operational territory and the 126–129 AC political context.

## Sensory Impact

Authority in the Dragon Gate block is felt primarily as institutional presence: the Watch patrol's regular rhythm through the inner alley (distinguishable from merchant foot-traffic by anyone paying attention), the guild liaisons' market-day inspection rounds, the ward septon's pastoral rounds, and the occasional higher-authority passage (the Hand's carriage glimpse in Act 2 is the ceiling event of this presence).

The political temperature rise through Act 2 has sensory expression in the gate-traffic register: more faction-sigil carriages through the arch, more armed escort accompanying traveling merchants, Watch increased attention on non-standard movement patterns. These arrive in Taylor's insect-ambient as behavioral-baseline deviations before they arrive as audible events.

## Duration

Persistent throughout the project. The authority structure is stable with two specific changes: (1) the political temperature rise through Act 2 changes which reports receive institutional attention; (2) the Dance's beginning at the book's back end changes the regime context — Viserys I's off-page death and the Aegon II proclamation represent the terminal authority shift. Taylor is dead before the regime change has operational effects on her case; the persons-risk processing occurs under the late-Viserys Watch authority that has been her operating context throughout.

## The Dragon Gate Territory Authority Structure

**Gold Cloak Watch (primary street authority):**
The City Watch of King's Landing holds street-level authority over the Dragon Gate block. The Watch captain of the Dragon Gate precinct is the immediate authority figure — all patrol operations, detention decisions, persons-risk actions, and administrative records flow through the precinct station. The Dragon Gate precinct is structured heavier than an interior-city-ward precinct because gate districts present specific authority challenges: traveler traffic, mixed-class social register, guild-registered merchants with cross-institutional connections.

The Watch commander is above the precinct captain. The Watch command structure is connected to the Hightower patronage network — the Hand's authority flows through the Watch commander to the precinct captains. This is the specific Green-adjacency mechanism that makes the Dragon Gate precinct captain Green-adjacent: not personal commitment but institutional patronage architecture.

**The Gold Cloak's authority over Taylor specifically:**
- Taylor has no documented Westerosi identity. She arrived without documentation, was detained and released under the "lunatic-not-worth-paperwork" classification, and has no formal administrative status except the Watch management note the captain maintains.
- The management note is NOT formal legal protection. It is an informal administrative designation. The captain can revoke it unilaterally. Taylor has no appeal rights regarding the management note's revocation.
- The witch-label from the ward septon adds a Faith-institution flag to Taylor's administrative record, which the Watch's persons-risk category can incorporate as a "unusual-capability" indicator.
- As a ~19yo woman with no documented Westerosi identity, no property, no patronage, no guild registration, and a witch-label on record, Taylor has essentially no formal legal standing in the Dragon Gate precinct's authority hierarchy.

**Guild Structure (secondary commercial authority):**
The merchant guilds hold authority over commercial operations in the Dragon Gate block — market fees, weight-and-measure regulation, commercial dispute resolution. The guild liaison is the operational interface Taylor uses at Rung 2. The guild-senior with dock-labor authority is the Rung-3 steward's administrative superior.

Guild authority and Watch authority overlap in the gate district. The guild registers commercial presence; the Watch enforces order. A commercial activity that generates both a guild-compliance problem and a Watch-risk categorization is handled through both systems simultaneously. The systems share intelligence via the shared patronage network.

**The Faith (tertiary pastoral authority):**
The ward septon's pastoral authority covers the block's Faith functions: charitable distribution, sacramental services, pastoral record-keeping. The Faith's pastoral records include the witch-flag on Taylor from arrival.

The Faith cannot compel Watch action without Watch cooperation. The ward septon's witch-classification has been institutionally suspended by the Watch management note — he cannot act on his classification because the Watch-captain's informal protection makes Watch enforcement unavailable to him. This institutional friction (Faith classification vs. Watch informal protection) is the specific condition that makes Taylor's social position contingent: if the Watch management note is revoked, the Faith classification activates without a counter.

**The Patronage Architecture (Hightower-Green, one remove):**
The Watch command chain → Watch commander → Watch captain chain is the Green-adjacent patronage architecture in this project. Taylor does not see this architecture from inside her block-cap. She sees the Watch captain as an institutional resource whose institutional behavior she can read. She does not see the Hightower connection.

The patronage architecture becomes the kill mechanism when the contact names Taylor as a source: the persons-risk intelligence that flows upward through the captain's reports has been loading the Green-infrastructure's understanding of the Dragon Gate block for three years. When Taylor becomes a liability, she is processed through the same channel her intelligence traveled upward through. The architecture is symmetrical: it carries her reports up, then carries the persons-risk determination back down.

**The Hightower carriage glimpse (Act 2):**
The single canon glimpse: the Hand's carriage passes through the Dragon Gate arch in Act 2, identifiable by sigil, neither stopping nor acknowledging. The carriage passage requires Watch clearance of the patrol yard. The market alley goes quiet. The carriage does not stop. No canonical figure speaks on-page.

This glimpse is the only time the Hightower patronage architecture is visible to Taylor as a physical fact rather than an institutional abstraction. She sees the sigil. She knows what it means in the historical record she carries. The carriage does not know she exists. This is the event's structural point: the architecture passes through her territory and does not notice her. She is too small to be noticed by the architecture directly. She is the right size to be harvested by it.

## The 126–129 AC Political Temperature

**Act 1 (months 1–12, 126–127 AC):** Gate district is politically stable. Faction temperature is background noise — Green and Black faction affiliations exist in the merchant class and the Watch command structure, but street-level operations are not yet faction-inflected. Taylor's Act 1 work happens in the relative calm before faction temperature bleeds into street-level categories. The calm belongs to her perception, not to the world — the faction architecture is already present; she cannot read it from inside her range-cap.

**Act 2 (months 13–26, 127–128 AC):** Faction temperature begins to bleed into street-level operations. The Watch's persons-risk category starts incorporating cross-faction-access indicators. Guild compliance becomes more sensitive to Black-adjacent commercial connections. The Rung-3 steward's category applications (sedition-adjacent framing for a wage dispute) are symptomatic of the Act 2 temperature rise: the institutional category structure is expanding to accommodate faction-risk as a legitimate administrative concern. Taylor experiences Act 2 as terrain-tightening — the cost-per-intervention rises each quarter; Watch cooperation becomes more contingent and transactional. She does not read the faction-temperature explanation for the tightening because the faction-architecture is upstream of her range-cap.

**Act 3 (months 27–36, 128–129 AC):** The last viable pivot is available and missed (Rung 6 / M27–28). Viserys I's death is registered as a street-crier announcement in the block — Taylor hears it in the market alley. Aegon II's proclamation, if heard, arrives as ambient. The political collapse Taylor cannot reach is happening above her cap while the persons-risk sweep she loaded runs below it. Her arrest and death occur within weeks of Viserys's death, making her case one of the first processed under the administrative realignment the regime change produces — her paperwork reflects the new regime's intake numbers as administrative realignment.

## Taylor's Legal Exposure

As a ~19yo woman with no formal identity documentation, no Westerosi family name, no guild membership, no formally registered dwelling (she occupies lodging under an informal arrangement), and a Watch witch-classification under a management note rather than formal cleared status:

- **No formal legal protection.** The management note is the sum of her institutional protection. It is revocable.
- **No appeal channel.** She cannot petition against Watch administrative action through any channel that functions without Watch cooperation. The guild has no standing to intervene in Watch persons-risk procedure. The Faith cannot compel Watch action. There is no court accessible to her.
- **The persons-risk category fits her by criteria.** She has non-standard arrival and no documentation (criterion 1). Her block-network contacts include cross-faction-access individuals (criterion 2). The witch-label constitutes an unusual-capability indicator (criterion 3). She appears in multiple Watch intelligence reports over three years (criterion 4). The category is accurate by Watch criteria.
- **Her foreign origin reads as additional risk.** In the 128–129 AC political temperature, foreign-origin persons without documented Westerosi affiliation are more likely to generate persons-risk attention, not less. Her Earth-Bet origin is completely invisible; her observable foreign-affect (not a native KL register, not a recognizable regional accent, no family or guild affiliation that places her in Westerosi social geography) reads as foreign-without-affiliation in the category that needs to be attached somewhere.
