# Fauna Control Rules — Dragon Gate Addendum (126–129 AC)

## Description

Project-scoped re-spec of Taylor's fauna-control rules for the dragon-gate-foreclosure config. Derives from `cond-fauna-control-rules` (base library card) with the following critical overrides. Both this card and the base card must be loaded for any power-mechanics scene. Where this card conflicts with the base card, this card wins.

## Range Override (HARD CONSTANT — OQ-1 RESOLUTION)

**Dragon Gate config range: ~100–150m, non-expanding.**

This overrides the base card's 200m normal / 400m extended / 600m+ extreme range specification entirely.

- The 100–150m cap is a hard constant of Taylor's condition in this world. It is not a temporary debuff. It is not an organic ceiling that expands with practice or sustained use. It is a physical constant of the shard-seeding conditions that obtained at Taylor's Dragon Gate arrival.
- The cap does not expand organically. There is no version of "range grows through use toward a higher ceiling" in this project. Any scene implying organic range expansion is a mechanics violation.
- The cap does not contract. It is approximately 100–150m at arrival and approximately 100–150m at the book's close.
- Taylor knows her radius precisely at all times. The edge of her range is sharp. She has tested it once at arrival (within the first week) and does not re-test it; the test was definitive.

**Structural function:** The block-level cap is the structural enforcer of the foreclosure theme. It forces Taylor to use people as reach-extenders. The reach-extenders are the patron-channel nodes that constitute her block network. The block network, built on patron-channel foundations she cannot fully inspect, becomes the Green intelligence infrastructure the Watch captain harvests. The cap is the mechanism that makes the foreclosure available: without the cap, Taylor's direct-surveillance capacity would eliminate her need for patron channels, which would eliminate the patron-dialect model's operational necessity.

**Auditor enforcement:** Any scene implying Taylor's range exceeds ~150m without a patron-channel reach-extender is a power-mechanics violation. Any scene implying Taylor's range has grown organically over the three-year arc is a hard violation.

## Species Scope Override

None. The base card's species scope carries without modification.

## Physical Cost Curve Override

None. The base card's cost curve carries. The adult body's higher stamina ceiling means onset is slightly further away than in child-variant projects, but the curve structure is identical. The passive sense cannot be disabled; this carries from the base card.

## Khepri-Mantle Status (SEALED — FULL PROJECT DURATION)

**The Khepri-mantle is sealed for the full single-book timeline. There is no unlock condition in this project.**

This overrides the base card's implicit "unlock possible under named threshold condition" framing.

- The mantle is sealed. It stays sealed. This is not a plot device. There is no unlock. There is no threshold event in a 36-month single-book arc that meets the prior-project unlock conditions.
- The weight of the sealed mantle is present as a structural condition. Taylor knows the shape of what the mantle holds. The seal is holding. The weight of the seal is not neutral.
- Any scene implying the mantle is approaching unlock, has partially unlocked, or could unlock under some available condition is a mechanics violation.
- The mantle's sealed state is not suspense. It is the book's power-mechanics terminal state.

**Why sealed in this project:** The flea-bottom-dance config's unlock mechanism required sustained high-density bug-control operation building sufficient local-processing capacity across a multi-season arc. This is a single 36-month book. The block-level range cap (~150m) means Taylor's maximum operational density is significantly lower than the flea-bottom-dance config's. The accumulated operational pressure required for the unlock threshold is not available in this project's arc.

## No Glass-Candle Research Arc

This project has no glass-candle research arc. The flea-bottom-dance config's glass-candle relay hypothesis (insect-density saturation as a signal the glass-candle apparatus can propagate) is specific to that multi-season project. In the dragon-gate-foreclosure config:

- Taylor does not pursue a glass-candle hypothesis.
- Taylor's operational ceiling is the block-level cap with no relay mechanism.
- The absence of a research arc is load-bearing: the foreclosure in this project is driven by the patron-dialect model, not by escalating power-mechanics.
- Any scene implying Taylor is building a glass-candle research apparatus, acquiring glass candle materials, or developing a relay hypothesis is a project-scope violation.

## Urban Ecology Specification (Dragon Gate Block)

The specific urban insect ecology of the Dragon Gate block differs from the flea-bottom-dance and prior-project ecologies. Impersonators must use this specification for any power-mechanics scene.

**Gate-traffic insects:** The gate arch is King's Landing's eastern entry. The road through it carries cart traffic, livestock, and human transit continuously during operating hours. Flies follow the livestock traffic through the arch and distribute through the market zone. The gate-traffic insect population fluctuates with traffic density — heavier on market days, lighter at night.

**Market-area fauna:** The inner alley's permanent commercial operations support a stable insect population: grain-area beetles and flies at the east end, workshop-associated fauna (fiber-beetles in Mira's workshop, forge-edge insects in and around the metalmonger), food-vendor flies at the provisions stalls. Rats in the storage spaces behind the permanent buildings and in the residential layer above.

**Wall-ecology insects:** The Dragon Gate's old stone wall has centuries of mortar-gap colonization. Beetles in the wall-seams of the guardhouse, the Sept, the permanent commercial buildings. These beetles are the specific ambient-register fauna that Taylor knows most precisely — they have been in the wall longer than any current occupant and their behavior baseline is the most stable ecological reference she has. They are the specific insects registering her arrival and her close in the guardhouse's isolation cell.

**Approximate ambient population at 150m:** ~20,000 insects in continuous contact when Taylor is positioned centrally in the block. This is denser than a rural Riverlands village, sparser than Flea Bottom's dense smallfolk ecology, appropriate for a mixed-class gate-district urban environment. The ambient register is never quiet. Deviations from baseline are immediately apparent. Taylor has individuated the full block population to the creature-by-creature level within three months of arrival.

## Ambient-Not-Directed Presentation (LAW — OQ-4 Resolution)

Taylor's power MUST present as ambient-not-directed throughout this project. This is binding.

- The captain never witnesses a directed demonstration. The witch-label rests on the arrival incident (insects lifting toward heat in response to Taylor's distress — involuntary, not directed). The captain's cover of Taylor is premised on the label being "lunatic who insects respond to," not "woman who can direct insects."
- Any scene where Taylor explicitly directs insects in a manner that could be witnessed and identified as directed-command rather than involuntary-response is a violation if the witness is the captain or anyone in the captain's institutional chain.
- Taylor's active deployments must be rendered in ways that preserve plausible-deniability in the "ambient/involuntary" register. She does not openly deploy to witnesses who do not already have reason to believe in directed control. Mira knows something is wrong about the way animals respond to Taylor; Mira does not know the control is directed.
- The auditor must check: is this power use consistent with "lunatic who animals respond to strangely" rather than "woman who explicitly commands insects"? If not, the maintenance of the witch-label-as-cover is at risk.

## AU-Divergence Legibility (LAW — OQ-1 Resolution)

The ~100–150m hard cap is an AU divergence from canon shard behavior (canon allows organic range expansion; this project does not). **This divergence MUST be legible in the prose — named, observed, or acknowledged by a POV character — and must not be silently elided.**

- Taylor must register the cap as a limit at least once in the text, in a manner visible to the reader. A single clear moment of recognition (testing the edge, noting the hard boundary, acknowledging to herself the ceiling does not move) satisfies this requirement.
- The acknowledgment does not need to be a dedicated scene; it may be embedded in an action sequence or interiority moment. It must be present.
- Silently writing the cap as if it is simply "Taylor's range in this city" without any character-level recognition of its distinctness from prior operation is a violation of this constraint.
- Auditor check: is there at least one prose moment where the range cap registers as a limit, whether through Taylor's direct interiority, a test, or an acknowledgment to another character?
