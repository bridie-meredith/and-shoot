# Watch Persons-Risk Sweep Protocol — Dragon Gate (128–129 AC)

## Description

The Watch administrative category and operational protocol that functions as the kill mechanism at Rung 6. Defines what "persons-risk" means as a Watch administrative category in King's Landing 126–129 AC, how the sweep is triggered and escalated, the institutional momentum that Taylor cannot interrupt once the contact is detained, and the structural requirement that the sweep reads as institutional procedure throughout — not as personal cruelty, not as conspiracy, as bureaucratic momentum.

**The sweep is not a villain's tool.** It is a Watch administrative procedure functioning correctly on the categories it was built to process. The categories it was built to process have been loaded with three years of Taylor's patron-dialect reports. The loading is real. The protocol is real. The outcome is real. None of these are personal.

## Sensory Impact

The sweep's sensory presence in the text is indirect. Taylor experiences it through:
- The insect-ambient-register deviating from baseline in the area of the contact's premises (Watch presence, unfamiliar officer movement patterns, the passage blocked from the alley end)
- The specific acoustic of Watch administrative procedure at elevated tempo (multiple officers in the patrol staging yard when only the regular patrol should be there)
- The absence at the contact's premises after the detention — presence-to-absence in the insect-ambient register
- Second-hand Watch procedure in the log-room above, audible as sound-texture through the stairwell, when she is in the isolation cell at the book's close

The sweep is not announced. It does not have a dramatic arrival. It runs through the block's institutional infrastructure without Taylor being able to interrupt it because she was not present at the moment of detention initiation, and by the time she registers the deviation, the passage is blocked.

## Duration

The M27–28 sweep is a two-to-three-day operational event. The persons-risk protocol as a standing category is persistent throughout the project — the sweep is the acute activation of a category that has been building throughout Acts 1 and 2.

The sweep's institutional aftermath (Taylor's detention and death) is a further extension of the protocol. The protocol does not end when the contact dies; it continues to run on the detainees list. Taylor's name arrives on that list via the contact's report. The protocol processes Taylor the same way it processed the contact: correctly, efficiently, without personal intent.

## What "Persons-Risk" Means as a Watch Category (King's Landing, 126–129 AC)

The persons-risk category is a Watch administrative classification for individuals whose movement patterns, associations, or reported activities indicate a potential threat to persons or public order that does not fit the more specific Watch categories (theft, violence, riot-incitement). It functions as the Watch's administrative miscellaneous-threat-category.

**Who falls into persons-risk:**
- Individuals with non-standard movement patterns across ward boundaries (especially eastern gate entries and exits that do not match declared business purposes)
- Individuals with cross-faction associations (persons known to maintain access to both Green-adjacent and Black-adjacent commercial or social networks)
- Individuals flagged by ward-level intelligence as possessing unusual capabilities, foreign associations, or behavior patterns that Watch informants have categorized as security-relevant
- Individuals whose presence in the gate district has been noted in multiple intelligence reports across different patron channels over an extended period

Taylor falls into persons-risk category at the end of Act 3 via all four criteria simultaneously: her foreign origin and non-standard arrival (criterion 1); her block-network contacts include the cross-faction contact (criterion 2); the witch-label and insect-ambient behavior generate ward-level intelligence flags (criterion 3); three years of Watch captain intelligence reports reference her as a ward-level sensor (criterion 4). The category loading is cumulative across the full arc.

**What persons-risk detention entails:**
- Detention in the Watch precinct cell block pending administrative review
- Administrative review: the detained person's records across all Watch and guild intelligence categories are compiled and reviewed for escalation indicators
- Escalation threshold: if the records review produces sufficient indicators, the detention is elevated from administrative hold to formal persons-risk detainee status
- Formal detainee conditions: the Watch cell block, indefinite hold pending category resolution, no release on the detained person's appeal alone — requires administrative authorization from above the arresting officer level

**The health hazard:** The Watch cell block's conditions (cold stone, minimal light, inadequate provision for extended holds) are not designed for indefinite detention. The formal detainee conditions, applied to a person in fragile health or applying over an extended period, produce outcomes the protocol does not account for. The contact dies under these conditions during the sweep; it is not a torture death, it is a procedural-conditions death. Taylor dies under these conditions. Same mechanism, applied to Taylor's body, which is not in fragile health but has been in the cell for three days by the time of her death.

**Taylor cannot appeal persons-risk detention.** She has no documented Westerosi identity, no patronage relationship that can be invoked in formal procedure (the captain's management note is an informal arrangement, not a formal protection), no guild registration, and no Faith-institutional relationship that generates a formal appeal right. The persons-risk protocol processes her in the category she fits: foreign, witch-labeled, no formal standing, attached to a persons-risk intelligence chain.

## How the Sweep Is Triggered

**The loading:** The M27–28 sweep is not triggered by a single event. It is triggered by the accumulated loading of the persons-risk category with three years of intelligence from the Dragon Gate precinct's patron-channel network — including Taylor's framed reports flowing through the Watch captain, the Rung-3 steward's category applications feeding through the guild-senior to the Watch command structure, and the Rung-5 guild auditor's physician-suspension report generating a persons-risk adjacent flag.

**The immediate trigger:** Taylor's Rung-6 report — the Black-adjacent contact's border-crossing pattern framed as smuggling-of-persons-risk for record-review access — is the final loading event that activates the sweep. The contact's pattern, confirmed by multiple intelligence records, reaches the persons-risk threshold for formal action.

**The escalation:** The contact's detention triggers the wider sweep. The persons-risk protocol, once activated for a specific detainee, requires review of associated-persons intelligence to determine whether the sweep should expand. The contact's records connect to multiple persons in the Dragon Gate block intelligence database. The sweep expands to cover associated persons, including Mira (who appears in block-network intelligence via the Rung-4 channel) and Taylor herself.

**Taylor's name arrives via the contact:** Before the contact dies, he names Taylor as his source for the border-crossing intelligence report. (Not maliciously — under persons-risk detention, the protocol includes interview of detainees about their intelligence sources to assess network scope. The contact names her because names flow in that procedure.) The name travels up the correct channels to the captain, who recategorizes Taylor from administrative asset to persons-risk liability.

## Institutional Momentum Taylor Cannot Interrupt

Once the contact is detained, the following momentum cannot be interrupted by Taylor:

1. The detention is in progress: she is not present at the detaining; she has no persons-risk override capacity
2. The contact's interview produces her name: she cannot prevent the interview or the naming
3. Her name travels to the captain via correct channels: she cannot intercept correct-channel communication
4. The captain recategorizes her and signs the authorization: she cannot access the captain's calculation process
5. The Watch officers arrive at her lodging: she has approximately three days between the contact's detention and her own; this window closes before she identifies the scope of what is happening
6. Her own detention is processed correctly: she is in the category; the protocol runs

The insect-ambient register at 150m means she is aware of Watch presence in range. She is not aware of the specific administrative process that is assembling her detention. The patron-channel architecture that ran through the captain was invisible to her for three years; its final action on her is invisible to her until the officers arrive.

**What Taylor could theoretically do in the three-day window:** She could flee. She could approach the captain directly. She could appeal to the Faith. She does not do these things for reasons consistent with her established character and the patron-dialect model's final failure mode: the model tells her the situation is manageable at the patron-channel level; the model's final deployment is Taylor trying to frame the situation in a channel that no longer exists because the captain has already recategorized her. The model runs correctly one last time, on a situation that the model cannot fix, producing a framing that the captain does not receive as useful because the calculation is already complete.

## The Sweep Must Read as Institutional Procedure

**Auditor enforcement:**
- The sweep is never rendered as personal cruelty by any actor. The contact's death is a procedural-conditions outcome, not a murder.
- The sweep is never rendered as a conspiracy targeting Taylor specifically. The sweep targets a persons-risk category that Taylor happens to fit by category criteria her own reports helped define.
- The Watch officer (Ser Rowan Vane) running the sweep must be written as procedurally correct throughout. His actions follow the protocol. The protocol produces the outcomes the protocol is designed to produce.
- The captain's final calculation must be rendered as a calculation, not as betrayal. He signs the paperwork for the same reason he signed the release paperwork: the calculation, applied correctly to the current variables, produces the correct institutional output.
- The book's closing death must be rendered as bureaucratic processing, not as drama. Taylor is processed. The world's verdict, not Taylor's, is at the final image.

## The Final Image Requirement

**From OQ-6 (binding):** The book's last image is Taylor's own insects, range-capped to the block, registering no movement inside the building she is in. The image is external, observational, silent. The surveillance apparatus she built outlives her by minutes and reports back faithfully on her own disappearance.

This image requires:
1. The insect-ambient-register has been a consistent prose mechanism throughout the book (per OQ-6 and Taylor's Hard Fence 9 — the closing image must be earned by prior use)
2. The final image is rendered from outside the isolation cell, through the insect-ambient register at the wall's beetle-ecology level
3. The image is external and silent — not Taylor's interiority; the insects reporting from the outside
4. "No movement inside the building she is in" is the reporting-state; the reader knows what it means; Taylor is not articulating it; she is the absence being reported

The reporting is what ends the book.
