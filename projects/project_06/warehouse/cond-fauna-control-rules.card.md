# Fauna Control Rules — Taylor Hebert in Westeros

## Description

Governing constraint card for Taylor Hebert's parahuman fauna-control ability as it operates in Westeros, absent shard buffering. This card defines the exact mechanics, physical cost structure, species scope, and wrongness-perception rules that apply in this project. All impersonators, auditors, and constraint checks must load this card alongside `cond-fauna-control-rules-dragon-gate-addendum` when Taylor uses her ability. The addendum's range override takes precedence.

## Species Scope

**In scope (controllable):** insects and arachnids; rats and mice; corvids (ravens and crows specifically; also rooks, jackdaws); any bird without complex social cognition (sparrows, pigeons, starlings); any creature without higher-order social behavior.

**In Dragon Gate block specifically:** flies, beetles, moths, spiders, rats, mice, ravens, crows, common sparrows. See `cond-fauna-control-rules-dragon-gate-addendum` for block-specific ecology detail.

**Explicitly excluded:** horses, dogs, direwolves, dragons, humans, herd animals, creatures with complex social behavior. Cats: borderline, unreliable, avoided.

## Range

**OVERRIDDEN BY ADDENDUM.** Do not use the 200m/400m/600m+ figures from this card for this project. The Dragon Gate config range is ~100–150m, hard constant, non-expanding. See `cond-fauna-control-rules-dragon-gate-addendum`.

## Capacity

**Swarm-mode (insects):** Taylor can maintain simultaneous individual-level control of hundreds of insects without meaningful cognitive load. Thousands as a coordinated mass with somewhat less individual precision.

**Mid-size fauna:** Up to approximately 10–15 large creatures simultaneously with individual-level command.

**Cross-class management:** Running insects AND rats AND ravens simultaneously is possible but costs more.

## Physical Cost Curve

**0–5 minutes:** No meaningful cost.

**5–15 minutes:** Headache onset.

**15–30 minutes:** Nosebleed onset. Physical performance degrades.

**Beyond 30 minutes continuous OR multiple shorter sessions without adequate rest:** Blackout risk.

**Recovery:** 4–6 hours complete non-use. Partial rest (2–3 hours) provides partial recovery.

**The passive sense cannot be disabled.** Always-on within range. In Dragon Gate block at 150m: approximately 20,000 insects in continuous ambient contact.

## Wrongness-Perception

When Taylor directs fauna at scale or in coordination, observers register it as uncanny. The available Westerosi frames are: omen, blessing, curse, Old Gods' working, old magic, witchcraft. See `cond-westerosi-superstition-frame` for full vocabulary.

**Concealment:** Reduce wrongness-perception by: single animal or small group, operating at edge of range, passive reconnaissance rather than active control, ensuring directed animals maintain superficially natural behavior patterns.

**Cannot fully conceal in extended use.** A sustained operation involving multiple species in close quarters will eventually produce observable animal behavior that does not fit natural patterns.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-fauna-control-rules-dragon-gate-addendum`:** Range is overridden. Khepri-mantle sealed (no unlock). No glass-candle research. The addendum governs this project's power-mechanics. Both cards should be loaded together.

**For auditor use:** Power use exceeding the Dragon Gate addendum's range is a hard violation. Power use implying organic range expansion is a hard violation.
