# Mira's Rope Workshop — warehouse copy

See library card at `cards/locations/loc-miras-workshop.card.md` for full content.

## Critical facts for scene use

**Role:** Primary site of Taylor-Mira genuine-attachment relationship. Mira's place of business for nine years. Inner alley, Dragon Gate block.

**Layout:** Ground floor working space (~8m × 12m). Braiding frame (large, fixed, rear wall), fiber storage (north wall), work-table (center), counter/display (front). Half-loft above rear section (Mira's personal space, ladder access). Front door to inner alley — propped open during working hours.

**Sensory vocabulary (key):** Fiber-and-lanolin (dominant and persistent). Wood of the braiding frame. The braiding-frame sound when running: foot-pedal click-thud, rope-drag sibilance. When idle: comparative quiet. Metalmonger's hammer from shared wall (acoustic challenge for conversation). Evening: hanging lamp over work-table, alley dark outside.

**Light:** Natural light from alley front. Afternoon shadow from city wall reduces this. Evening lamp (amber, self-contained).

**Temperature:** Cool — flagstone floor, shared walls, no direct sun. Most comfortable space on inner alley in summer.

**Exits:** Alley door (front) only. No shared-wall pass-throughs.

**Taylor's visits:** Within range whenever Taylor is in inner alley. She knows the workshop occupants through insect-ambient before she opens the door. Has known for the full three years whether Mira is present.

**Hazards:** Loft single-exit issue (ladder only); fire hazard (fiber + lamp-oil + shared wall with cloth trader); guild-inspection exposure; Watch visibility from patrol coverage.
