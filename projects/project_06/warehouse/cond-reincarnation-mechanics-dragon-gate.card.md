# Reincarnation Mechanics — Dragon Gate Arrival Config (126 AC)

## Description

Project-scoped re-spec of arrival mechanics for the OQ-2 Dragon Gate arrival event. Derives from `cond-reincarnation-mechanics` (base library card, taylor-hebert-westeros project) with full-config override for this project's specific arrival conditions: ~19yo body, mid-catastrophe market fire, open ground, urban arrival, immediate Watch-encounter-at-arrival. The base card's fundamental rules (memory continuity, no return, no other displaced souls, body is real) carry without modification. What changes: the body, the age, the arrival context, the social physics of arrival.

Load this card alongside `cond-reincarnation-mechanics` (base). Where they conflict, this card wins.

## What Carried Over (Base Card Carries)

All base card content on "What Carried Over" applies without modification:
- Full episodic memory of Taylor Hebert's Earth-Bet life
- Semantic knowledge of the Worm universe and Fire & Blood as text
- Tactical cognition and pattern recognition
- Core temperament

## The Body: ~19-Year-Old Configuration

**Age at arrival:** Taylor arrives in a ~19yo body. Unlike the prior Westerosi lives (11yo westeros config; 17yo flea-bottom config), the Dragon Gate body is adult by Westerosi legal and social standards. She has arrived in a body that is not visibly a child, not legally managed as a minor, and not read by institutional authority as requiring parental or Faith-guardian oversight.

**Prior occupant status:** The body had no prior occupant at Taylor's arrival. It was not a tanner's daughter's body that had already lived seventeen years; it was not a craftsman's daughter's body with established social position. The body is — by available evidence — an unoccupied or recently vacated body that arrived in the world at the same moment Taylor did. She does not know whose body it was or whether it was ever occupied. She does not investigate. The body does not carry prior-life social relationships, prior-life community position, or prior-life family who expects something from it.

**The developmental mismatch:** The mismatch between body-age and cognitive-age is the narrowest it has been across Taylor's Westerosi lives. She is in an adult body with adult social legibility. She cannot be managed as a child. She cannot be dismissed as a child. The adult body is a genuine advantage in some operational contexts and a liability in the specific context where "young woman alone in a foreign city" reads as requiring institutional management — which is why the overnight detention at arrival is the Watch applying the institutional management framework available for her demographic.

**No prior-life body-schema:** Unlike the flea-bottom config, Taylor did not arrive in a body that had already learned callus-patterns she didn't cause. The ~19yo Dragon Gate body has the callus-patterns and physical habits of whatever life it had before Taylor arrived — but she has no access to what that life was (the prior occupant is absent, if there was one). The body's physical history is a blank. She registers this as less disorienting than the tanner-daughter config, where she had visible evidence of a prior person in every callus she didn't make.

## The Urban Arrival Context

**Location:** Open ground near Dragon Gate, the eastern landward entrance to King's Landing. Not a rural village; not a field at night. A busy market approach in a city of hundreds of thousands, during a market fire.

**The market fire:** A rendered-fat vessel overturned at a provisions stall ignited in the dry summer air. The fire was spreading through the stall cluster at the gate approach. The Watch was already on riot-control response. The market crowd was in mid-emergency dispersal. Taylor arrived in the middle of this.

**The arrival's involuntary insect response:** Taylor's distress-response at arrival — the cognitive disorientation of arriving in a new body, new world, mid-emergency — fired the ambient insect-sense before she had orientation to suppress it. The insects of the block, responding to her distress-input (not her directed control), lifted visibly toward the heat. This is the same mechanism as the base card's "distress-before-control" pattern, but it fired into a public emergency with Watch officers present. There was no private moment of adjustment. The arrival was already surveillance.

**The specific witnesses at arrival:**
- Three gold cloaks on riot-control: saw the insects lift; one filed the "animals went strange" report
- Ward Septon Aldyn at the sept's perimeter: saw the insects lift; shouted "witch" in a voice that carried through the smoke-filled approach
- Market crowd in emergency dispersal: many witnesses to the insect-lift, most categorizing it via the superstition-frame (omen, blessing, old-magic) rather than a directed-capability interpretation

**No controlled first impression:** Unlike prior Westerosi arrivals, Taylor had no period of adjustment before institutional contact. The arrival was immediately public, immediately observed, and immediately interpreted by the closest institutional authority (the ward septon) in the worst available category. The management of the first impression was retroactively impossible.

## The Overnight Detention

**The Watch guardhouse:** See `loc-dragon-gate-guardhouse` for full sensory vocabulary.

**The detention itself:** Taylor was held in the isolation cell from arrival day (late summer 126 AC, evening) to the following morning. Questioned in Old Valyrian — which she does not speak. Questioned in Common Tongue with Westerosi accent — which she understands but cannot respond to in a register that would distinguish "foreign but intelligible" from "foreign and incoherent." She said almost nothing. What she said was not oriented to clearing the detention classification; she had not yet assessed the institutional landscape enough to know what the correct communication strategy was.

**The experience:** Cold stone. Minimal light from the wall lamp at the stairwell base. The beetles in the wall-seams in continuous ambient contact — the specific insects she would know, three years later, as the last ones reporting. The Watch officers' footstep patterns above, audible through the stairwell. She spent the night mapping the building's ecology through the passive sense and assessing her situation.

**The assessment from inside the isolation cell:** She was in a Watch detention facility in a pre-industrial city where her power was already labeled as supernatural activity. She did not speak the local register fluently enough to communicate out of the detention. She had no documented identity, no social connections, no institutional cover. She assessed these facts correctly. She did not panic. She waited for the institutional process to produce an output.

**The release:** Morning. The captain reviews the case. Applies the "lunatic-not-worth-paperwork" classification. Signs the release. Taylor walks out of the guardhouse into the morning gate-district traffic as: the lunatic woman near Dragon Gate, the one the septon called a witch.

## What the Urban Arrival Configuration Changes from Prior Projects

**No tanner family.** In this project, there is no family that grieves a prior occupant, no family that looks at Taylor and sees the wrong version of their child. There is no ongoing-cost-surface from family contact. The social isolation of this project's arrival is complete and immediate.

**No community embedding path.** The flea-bottom config had a conditional-embedder (the tanner-elder) who placed Taylor into the district's labor-web. The Dragon Gate config has no such figure. Taylor's integration into the block is entirely self-generated: she finds lodging through a transactional arrangement that is not warm; she builds block familiarity through three years of presence and the insect-ambient register.

**No prior research context.** She arrives at Dragon Gate without the glass-candle research hypothesis that was building through the flea-bottom config. This is a fresh arrival — the project's operational constraint is the patron-dialect model, not a research arc. Taylor does not arrive with a research agenda; she arrives with a survival agenda that becomes the foreclosure arc.

**The witch-label from minute one.** In prior Westerosi arrivals, Taylor had a period before being identified as unusual. Here, the label arrived with the arrival. The social position that every other project-Taylor worked to maintain — the managed invisibility that kept institutional attention at bay — was foreclosed at arrival. She has been working with the witch-label as permanent infrastructure since the first morning.

## Fundamental Rules (From Base Card — Apply Without Change)

- **Return is closed.** No route back to Earth-Bet. Physical fact, not a reluctant acceptance.
- **The body is real.** Injury is permanent. Death is permanent. The body ages in real time.
- **Taylor is the only displaced soul.** No other isekai arrivals. Any scene introducing another displaced soul is a fence violation.
- **Memory completeness is non-negotiable.** Full recall of Earth-Bet life. No degradation over time.
- **The reincarnation was involuntary.** No discovered purpose or mission. The absence of explanation is permanent.
