# 1c — Candidate Menu

## Library survey summary

The library is well-stocked for Westerosi smallfolk projects but was built around three prior projects with distinct geographic footprints: `taylor-hebert-westeros` (120 AC Harrenhal-Riverlands), `taylor-hebert-jaehaerys` (84 AC Fairstead-Riverlands), and `flea-bottom-dance` (125 AC Flea-Bottom/King's Landing). This new project shares the 125–129 AC era-window and King's Landing setting with `flea-bottom-dance`, but its operational territory is the Dragon Gate market-and-gate block rather than Flea Bottom — a different quarter, different social physics (gate-adjacent mixed-class traveler traffic vs. dense landless-smallfolk labor-web), and a different cast structure. Most importantly, the thematic and structural shape differs: this project is a single-book foreclosure story driven by the patron-dialect 6-rung ladder, not a multi-season research-horror arc. As a result:

**What exists and is usable directly:** The base Taylor persona (`taylor-hebert-flea-bottom`) is the strongest candidate for the protagonist, with its 125 AC range parameters and Khepri-Khepri-sealed configuration, though her range was specified as ~1 city block in the resolved constraints (OQ-1: "approximately one city block") rather than the 300m of the flea-bottom-dance project — a significant distinction that may require a new Dragon-Gate-specific variant. Several library-scope condition cards (`cond-shard-behavioral-weight`, `cond-feudal-hierarchy-law`, `cond-smallfolk-political-physics`, `cond-no-parahuman-infrastructure`, `cond-westerosi-superstition-frame`) are directly usable. The `mira-stonefield` base card is the closest persona for the rope-braider role (Mira is described as Taylor's genuine attachment in OQ-7).

**What exists but is project-scoped to flea-bottom-dance:** The most complete and relevant condition cards — `cond-westerosi-customary-authority-125ac`, `cond-crownlands-superstition-frame-125ac`, `cond-fauna-control-rules-125ac-addendum`, `cond-reincarnation-mechanics-125ac`, `cond-series-tone-constraints-125ac`, `cond-clinical-self-erasure` — are all scoped `project: flea-bottom-dance`. They cannot be provisioned as-is into this project's warehouse. All six require either re-scoping to library (promote) or authoring of new project-scoped variants tuned to the Dragon Gate config.

**What does not exist at all:** No Dragon Gate-specific location cards exist anywhere in the library. No Watch-captain / Gold Cloak patron archetype exists. No rope-braider / KL-block craftswoman persona exists. No Black-adjacent foreign-merchant contact exists. No Dragon Gate precinct guardhouse location exists. No ward-septon (city-precinct level) persona exists. No condition card describes the patron-dialect operational model or the Watch-persons-risk sweep mechanism that are the spine of this project's foreclosure structure. These are all new authorings at 1d.

The project requires a new variant card (`taylor-hebert-dragon-gate`) that re-specs range at block-level, retains the Khepri-sealed mechanics and all carried Gold Morning weight, and adjusts the operational profile for gate-block mixed-class territory rather than dense Flea Bottom ecology. The existing card's Vibe Seeds, Voice, Taste, and Forbidden Registers carry forward unchanged.

The `mira-stonefield` base card's voice, trust-mechanics, practical register, and hard fences (no-admiration path, not a servant, own-agenda-binding) all map cleanly to the Dragon Gate version. **Reservation:** the base card is Riverlands-war-survivor backstory; the Dragon Gate version will need a King's Landing craft-worker backstory. This is a new variant (`mira-stonefield-dragon-gate`), not the base card used raw. The base card is the source; the variant is what gets authored at 1d.

No upgrade needed for this project; load as off-page-reference only.

- `viserys-i-targaryen` — Exists (scant quality). Off-page only. His death is registered as a street-crier announcement in Act 3; he is never dramatized. Existing card is sufficient for background constraint use. Flag as off-page-only.

- `loc-flea-bottom` — Exists (full quality, scoped library). - `loc-red-keep-outer-ring` — Exists (scant quality). Off-page reference only; the Red Keep is explicitly excluded from Taylor's operational territory. Not needed for provisioning; exists for geographic context if referenced.

### Recommended authorings

- `loc-dragon-gate-block` — PRIMARY. The operational territory for the entire book. Dragon Gate market-and-gate block: the eastern landward entrance to King's Landing, gate structure, the market space adjacent to the gate, merchant stalls, traveler-transit infrastructure, dockyard-edge traffic. This is where Taylor lives and operates for 36 months. Must include: geography relative to the city, gate structure and staffing, market layout, sensory vocabulary (gate traffic, merchant density, Watch presence overhead), exits, hazards, ambient interruption hooks. Mixed-class (not Flea Bottom smallfolk density — travelers, merchants, gate-adjacent guild fixtures, Watch patrols), surveilled-from-above as Watch routine.

- `loc-dragon-gate-guardhouse` — CRITICAL. The Dragon Gate precinct Watch station. Taylor's first location (overnight detention at arrival); Taylor's last location (died in custody at close). The book's structural frame location — it opens and closes here. Needs: layout (cells, captain's office or log-room, patrol staging area), sensory vocabulary (stone, torch-smell, the specific acoustic of a guardhouse in a busy gate district), Watch operational procedures visible in the space, exits and security. The fact that this is both intake-at-arrival and death-at-close means the sensory vocabulary of this space is the book's threshold register.

- `loc-miras-workshop` — Rope-braiding workshop or equivalent craft space on the Dragon Gate block. Mira's place of business and the primary site of Taylor's genuine-attachment relationship. Must be a credible gate-block craft space: workshop layout, the physical work of rope-braiding (a maritime-adjacent trade appropriate to a gate near dockyard traffic), sensory vocabulary (fiber, tools, smell of the work), exits, and the specific intimacy available in a craft space vs. market space.

- `loc-black-adjacent-contact-premises` — The contact's place of business. Gate-adjacent, appropriate for a foreign-trade merchant with Black-adjacent connections. Border-crossing context: the Dragon Gate is KL's eastward land gate; a merchant dealing in cross-Narrow-Sea or road-trade goods with faction flexibility would have a plausible fixed presence near this gate. Needs: layout appropriate for a small merchant/counting-house or storage operation, exits, hazard profile reflecting the contact's exposure.

- `loc-dragon-gate-market-alley` — The specific alley or market lane where the rung-ladder events play out: Rung-1 grain-watering incident, Rung-2 merchant short-weight encounter, ambient block-life scenes. Not the broad market but the specific work-day geography Taylor moves through repeatedly over 36 months. Sensory vocabulary of routine occupation.

## Notes

**Scope warning on flea-bottom-dance conditions:** Six condition cards that do substantial work for this project's mechanics are currently project-scoped to `flea-bottom-dance`: `cond-westerosi-customary-authority-125ac`, `cond-crownlands-superstition-frame-125ac`, `cond-fauna-control-rules-125ac-addendum`, `cond-reincarnation-mechanics-125ac`, `cond-series-tone-constraints-125ac`, `cond-clinical-self-erasure`. At 1d, showrunner should decide whether any of these warrant library promotion (via margit `promote` operation) before variant-authoring, or whether new project-scoped variants are always correct given the distinct operational config. My read: `cond-series-tone-constraints-125ac` and `cond-clinical-self-erasure` are so tightly coupled to the flea-bottom-dance multi-season structure that they should remain flea-bottom-dance-scoped; new project-scoped tone and horror-register cards should be authored. The other four (`cond-westerosi-customary-authority-125ac`, `cond-crownlands-superstition-frame-125ac`, `cond-fauna-control-rules-125ac-addendum`, `cond-reincarnation-mechanics-125ac`) contain content that would benefit the library generally if promoted, with the project-specific overrides extracted into new variant cards.

**Range discrepancy in Taylor variant:** The most operationally significant gap is between `taylor-hebert-flea-bottom`'s 300m opening range and this project's OQ-1 "approximately one city block" constraint (~100–150m). This is not a minor adjustment — the block-level cap is the structural enforcer of the foreclosure theme in this project ("forces Taylor to use people as reach-extenders"). The new `taylor-hebert-dragon-gate` card must be authored at 1d to get the stats right; using the flea-bottom-dance persona card raw would give auditors the wrong range ceiling for every power-mechanics check.

**Mira naming:** The OQ-7 binding cast uses "Mira" as both the character name AND specifies the role (rope-braider, genuine attachment). The existing `mira-stonefield` base card has "Mira" as an alias and operates on the same voice and trust-mechanics as required. The new Dragon Gate variant should be slugged `mira-stonefield-dragon-gate` and designated as variant-of: `mira-stonefield` to preserve the library chain. The name "Mira Stonefield" is a recurrence; the world-note constraint does not prohibit using the same name for a different-project OC — it is a common enough smallfolk name and no shared-universe continuity problem arises in a single-book standalone.

**The contact and business-partner pair:** OQ-7 specifies the Black-adjacent contact (Rung 6 victim) and the contact's business-partner (present at Rung 4, absent at Rung 6) as distinct named cast members. The business-partner's absence at Rung 6 is "the cost." These two cards should be authored as a pair at 1d, since their relationship is the load-bearing mechanism for the Rung-4 moment and its Rung-6 mirror.

**No existing location is usable for Dragon Gate precinct:** The `kings-landing-exterior` card names the Dragon Gate as an exit but has no interior geography, no guardhouse layout, no Watch-presence detail. All Dragon Gate-specific locations need fresh authoring. The guardhouse is the highest-priority single location given its structural role as both first and last setting.

**`cond-suppression-policy-progression` does not apply:** This library card governs the 84–101 AC Riverlands suppression arc (Aldric Pryor, House Ryger, Tully chain). The antagonist structure here is the Watch-persons-risk sweep (bureaucratic, urban, single-book compressed). Do not provision this card to the warehouse — it would introduce wrong-era and wrong-geography institutional logic into the project's constraint layer.
