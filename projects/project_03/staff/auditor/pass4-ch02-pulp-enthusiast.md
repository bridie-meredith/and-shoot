The beetle-pinch to nosebleed arc (cost engine from OQ-4) pays off correctly: she overextends, bleeds, loses multiple assets simultaneously. The incidental starlings at lines 62–65 are the sharpest complication in the chapter because Taylor cannot prevent what she did not cause.

**Weak spots:** Windows 4 and 5 are the chapter's soft middle. The physical-cost display and the two mid-operation transit sequences run for 12 lines without a board-change. The stall is structural (both operations are mid-run), but 12 lines is at the edge of this persona's tolerance. Window 7 (lines 45, 48, 55–57) is a third tolerated window but it does not come consecutively with 4 and 5, so the gate holds. If prose expansion of any of these windows adds atmosphere rather than complication, they become BORED windows immediately.
