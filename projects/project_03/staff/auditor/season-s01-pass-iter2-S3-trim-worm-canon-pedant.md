That is the second failure, differentiated from the first by: elevation change (deliberate move to the bell tower for greater line-of-sight), the raven gets further before dropping (it crosses the hedgerow; the first one stalled at the hedgerow), and then fails. This is escalation — she tried harder and failed harder.

Lines 44–47 (raven drives, presses temples, blood, releases): cost-marking for the third attempt — she goes past what the body will sustain before releasing.

Then: settle, exhale, sept, prayer, septon.

Assessment: the revision is correct. The structure is now failure / attempt from ground / failed attempt with escalated cost / resign. That is Taylor's problem-solving rhythm as canonized in Worm: she tries, she escalates, she stops at the wall when the wall is real. The repetition that was the prior complaint — three identical gesture clusters with no forward motion — is gone. Each pass in the sequence now costs something different and lands in a different position than the last one. The bell tower as the deliberate move is exactly what was asked for.

The sequence still reads slow at the chapter-plan level. It is still a chapter about a character who tries and fails to reach something. But it now has the shape of escalating failure rather than cycling failure, and that is the distinction that separates Taylor Hebert from a character who just spirals. She problemsolves. She escalates. She hits the actual wall. Then she goes to the sept.

Prior complaint addressed. Rating upgraded.

**POWER-MECHANIC SPECIAL CHECK — ch08 fauna observation at range**

*This is the worm-canon-pedant's primary responsibility in this pass. The special check is whether the fauna observation mechanic in ch08, where Taylor uses a pre-positioned raven to stream events from Harrenhal's hall while she is at the sept approximately 2.5km away, respects the 600m operational ceiling in cond-fauna-control-rules.*

**The distance:**

The s01e06 studio state establishes the sept as "half a league" from Harrenhal. A league in medieval reckoning is approximately 4–5km; a Westerosi/Fire & Blood league follows the same convention. Half a league = approximately 2–2.5km. The sept is at the near edge of this range; the Harrenhal main hall — inside the castle, set back from the gatehouse — would be the far edge. Call it 2–2.5km between Taylor's position at the sept and fauna placed inside Harrenhal's hall.

cond-fauna-control-rules sets a 600m operational ceiling.

**What ch08 actually shows:**

Lines 65–88: a fly is present in the hall ceiling, reaches the table edge, crosses the letter surface, crosses the beam, reaches the table again. Taylor receives physical-cost signals at lines 77 (presses temple), 79 (presses palms), 82 (presses nose bridge), 83 (blood reaches the lip). She is in the sept nave, having crossed from the cottage assessment area. The fauna in the hall — the fly — is streaming the letter-reading event: the Celtigar seal, the letter content, the castellan's voice, Bracken's physiological response.

The operational picture: Taylor has a fly pre-positioned inside Harrenhal's great hall. She is at the sept, 2–2.5km from the hall. She is receiving event-level intelligence from that fly — not just presence/position data, but the specific events of the letter being read aloud, the seal identified, a named character's physical response. She pays physical cost for it.

**The verdict:**

This is a range violation at the 600m ceiling established in cond-fauna-control-rules.

2–2.5km is three to four times the established operational ceiling. The nosebleed cost-marking is internally consistent as a signal that she is overextending — cost-marking is how Wildbow handled Taylor's overextension and the text has been accurate on this throughout. But cost-marking does not license a range exceeding the established ceiling; it marks the cost of what she is doing, not that she can do it. The ceiling is a hard limit on range, not a cost-scaling curve. At 600m she hits the operational wall. At 2.5km she is not struggling-with-range; she is operating outside the power's established parameters with no acknowledged reason for the departure.

Canon-Taylor's range was calibrated by Wildbow against operational context and physical cost. The 600m ceiling as codified here represents that calibration. A scene that requires Taylor to stream hall-level event intelligence from 2.5km is asking the power to do something it does not do, regardless of how much blood reaches the lip.

There is a compliant version of this sequence: Taylor has fauna at the gatehouse or in the outer ward — within or near the 600m ceiling from the sept edge of the range — and she gets positional intelligence (Celtigar arrived, someone left the hall, a document changed hands) but not semantic content. The letter's content is unknown to her. She gets mechanical events; she does not get the letter. That is what the power does at range.

The as-written version — where she is processing the Celtigar seal identification and Bracken's breath at 2.5km — is a power-mechanic error. It does not change the chapter's structural rating (the events need to happen; the chapter is correct to have them on-page; the problem is how Taylor's witness-position is constructed). But it does not comply with canon.

**Required fix at prose layer:** Taylor's fauna-witness in the Celtigar letter sequence must be repositioned to a range-compliant location, or her witness-position must be reconfigured so she gets proximity-level data (presence, movement, a sealed document received, the hall filling and emptying) rather than content-level data (the letter is from Celtigar, Bracken's specific physiological response). The alternative is a pre-positioned fauna relay — fauna at the gatehouse, then fauna in the outer ward, then fauna at the hall door — with acknowledged cost at each relay node, but this is above the power's established parameters and would require the AU-acknowledged-departure note that the worm-canon-pedant accepts. Unmarked contradiction does not earn tolerance.

**This flag is advisory for prose. It does not change the chapter rating, which reflects structural delivery. It must not be silently carried forward.**

**Range violation — ch08 fauna witness in the Celtigar letter sequence:**

The fly in the Harrenhal hall streaming the letter-reading to Taylor at the sept (~2.5km) exceeds the 600m operational ceiling in cond-fauna-control-rules by a factor of four. The cost-marking (nosebleed) is internally consistent but does not license the range breach. This is an unmarked contradiction of the established power ceiling — the type of error the worm-canon-pedant flags without generosity.

The prose layer must resolve this by one of three routes:
1. Repositioning Taylor's fauna to a range-compliant location (≤600m from the hall — which means she would need to be near Harrenhal, not at the sept).
2. Reconfiguring the intelligence she receives to positional-only at range (presence, document-receipt, hall filling and emptying) with semantic content unknown until she is closer.
3. Explicitly acknowledging the departure — an AU variant that names why this character's range ceiling has shifted — which earns tolerance per the worm-canon-pedant's stated taste.

Option 2 is the minimum-change compliant path. Option 3 requires narrative scaffolding. Option 1 requires a chapter reposition.

The ch09 half-league range question (fault-001 in showrunner memory) is a lesser instance of the same class — fauna at the gatehouse sill feeding speech-level data from half a league — and should be resolved in the same prose pass.

**Both range questions are advisory. They do not trigger a REVISE at season review. They are load-bearing for prose accuracy.**
