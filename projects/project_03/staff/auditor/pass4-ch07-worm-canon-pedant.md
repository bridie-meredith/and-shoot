Taylor is in the yard; all action in IDs 17-27 occurs inside the recorder's room. The SVO bones do not establish a perception path (fauna link, sightline, or window) that would make this action comprehensible from Taylor's POV. This is a Hard Gate 1 breach that was acknowledged by the user as a known residual.

**Options for prose pass:**
- Option A (link establishment): Add a bridging beat before ID 17 establishing that Taylor has sent a bird into the recorder's room or has a link running to an animal inside. The launch and subsequent speech then register through the feed.
- Option B (exit-read): Collapse IDs 17-26 from Taylor's direct perception. Let Plumm's exit (ID 27) be the moment of confirmation — Taylor reads what happened from how he carries himself, what he is holding, where he goes next. This is canonically consistent with Taylor's observational register and requires no link-establishment.
- Option C (retrospective inference, marked): Taylor does not witness IDs 17-26 directly; she reconstructs them later from other sources. The prose marks this explicitly as inference or reconstruction.

Option B is the most canonically honest path requiring the least bones revision. Option A requires an additional ID or prose bridge before ID 17. Option C requires care — Taylor does not present guesses as witnessed fact.

**W5 and W6 are both TOLERATED for this reason.** Hard Gate 2 reached its limit. If IDs 17-26 are reframed in prose without a perception path being established, W5 and W6 would require re-rating on the prose pass.
