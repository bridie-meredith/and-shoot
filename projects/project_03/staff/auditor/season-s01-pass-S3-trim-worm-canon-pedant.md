Not a canon error; a structural flag. Not blocking.

### chapter-03 — Disturbance she cannot reach

**TOLERATED.**

The chapter is mechanically coherent — Taylor senses disruption through fauna behavior (ravens lifting, lines 11-12), cannot localize it, costs herself trying (blood at lines 15, 20, 25), settles into futility. That pattern is accurate to source. The Manton-adjacent limitation of the power — she can sense and route fauna, but she cannot extract cause from their behavior when the cause is outside her range — is respected here.

What makes this tolerated rather than engaged: it is a compression chapter, and at SVO level it reads as a holding pattern. Taylor does the same gesture-cluster (press temples / hold chin / hold hands / blood) four times in 47 lines. Each repetition is individually correct but together they are the same beat cycling. Canon Taylor does not sit still in distress — she problemsolves her way through it, even if the problemsolving is wrong. The chapter has very little forward motion in terms of what Taylor tries versus what happens. She spirals. Then a cart passes (line 26). Then she goes to the sept and kneels. That is not nothing, but it is close.

This chapter could be trimmed without losing season-level stakes. Lines 1-25 could compress to half. The prayer sequence (lines 28-36) is thin — Taylor isn't a believer; she kneels at the altar as behavior, but the SVO doesn't tell us what it costs her or what she's doing there strategically. If it's cover behavior, the SVO should make that visible in some way. If it's genuine reaching-for-comfort, that's characterization that needs to survive into prose — and at SVO level it's indistinguishable from filler.

Not blocking. One tolerated is within budget.

## Final Verdict: REVISE

The season is not broken. Power mechanics are consistently accurate. Taylor's voice-via-action is correct throughout. The season-machine (census → file → report → refusal → counter-claim → assessment → decision → named) works as a structure, and chapter-10's landing is earned.

The two TOLERATEDs are the problem: chapter-03 and chapter-05 (interlude). They are adjacent chapters in the sequence, which means the season has a middle sag running from approximately chapter-03 through chapter-05 before chapter-06's kinetic recovery. A worm-canon-pedant does not stop reading, but does start tracking time-to-payoff and the answer from ch03 through ch05 is: slow.

### Proposed cuts / recasts

**Chapter-03 — trim the repetition cycle:**
Lines 11-25 contain three near-identical distress gestures (raven lifts / press temples / hold chin/hands / blood) with no differentiation between iterations. Compress to two instances maximum. Give the third slot to a different response — Taylor trying something (directing a raven toward the road, attempting to get positional info) rather than just registering failure. She does this eventually at line 45 (drives a raven toward the road), but by then we've had three identical beats first. Reorder: one failure cycle, one attempt, one failure of the attempt. That is Taylor's actual problem-solving rhythm.

**Chapter-05 (interlude) — cut the middle of the Rowan/Plumm exchange:**
The exchange runs lines 30-70. The load-bearing beats are: Plumm writes name (48), writes sept entry (52), numbers it (55), pockets the book (66). Everything else is back-and-forth that communicates the negotiation but does not add information the season needs. Propose cutting roughly 20 lines from the conversation block (lines 30-47 compress; 56-65 compress). Keeps the key documentation beats; removes the procedural drag.

**Chapter-08 (Taylor's thread) — compress the post-assessment sept return:**
Lines 61-95 (Taylor in the sept after the maester leaves, then back to the cottage, then the ledger, then palms pinned) is the season's fourth sept-circuit. The first three were justified. This one is the beat the audience already knows. Cut to: Taylor exits the cottage after the maester departs, crosses the yard, enters the cottage, sits. The raven striking the beam (line 96) is the correct end-beat — reach it faster.

No canonical errors flagged. Voice register held. Power mechanics accurate. Season accepts after the three targeted trims above.
