## Findings

### Structural finding — ID 37 ("taylor-hebert-westeros speaks to septon-rowan")

The chapter's chapter goal is "she senses the disturbance but cannot reach what caused it." The reporting of this to Rowan is the chapter's consequence-delivery beat. ID 37 is the only bone in the chapter that delivers zero specific content — "speaks to" names a speech act without naming what was said. At the chapter-goal delivery moment, the bone should carry the content of the thesis being communicated: direction of the disturbance, depth of the failure, or the cost Taylor is paying. Without this, the Rowan beat reads as transition scaffolding rather than consequence.

Recommendation: revise ID 37 to a content-specific bone before prose pass. E.g., "taylor-hebert-westeros names the direction to septon-rowan" or "taylor-hebert-westeros describes the stall."

### Flag — IDs 8, 9 referent ambiguity

"Holds the feet" and "holds the chin" in W4 (IDs 8, 9) read as ambiguous at bone level — the referent (self vs. bird) is unclear. Given that the same posture appears again at ID 42 (W10, clearly self-referential), the ambiguity in the earlier instance should be resolved in prose. Not a bone-level defect if prose clarifies, but flagged for editor awareness.

### Flag — Cost-posture formula (IDs 6-9, 68-69-39, 47, 42)

Temple press and hold-chin appear across multiple windows as the cost response signature. This is mechanically honest — the cost signature should be consistent. But the bones recur without differentiation across four instances (W4, W6, W7, W10). Prose must differentiate each instance by magnitude and texture, or the cost will read as a repeated checkbox rather than a meaningful escalating signal. Specifically: the W7 instance (blood at lip in W8 follows) must read as the most severe. If the W4 and W6 prose renders the temple press at the same register as W7, the blood-at-lip will feel disproportionate.

- Power mechanics (parallel fauna control, physical cost) consistent with OQ-4 ACCEPT throughout
- Escalating-then-collapsing attempt structure is correct Taylor procedure
- Cost ceiling (blood at lip) calibrated correctly: below blackout, above headache, consistent with four-attempt load
- Foreknowledge not invoked (correct for a chapter focused on operational failure — Taylor knows something happened upstream, not what)
- Voice register: operational, disciplined, non-triumphalist throughout bones
- No lore leaks
- No Worm-jargon misuse
