## Required Fixes

### Chapter 03 (BORED — mandatory fix)

Problem: No board movement. Taylor presses temples / holds chin / holds feet in rotation for 47 lines. The goal states she senses a disturbance she cannot reach — but sensing-without-action for a full chapter is a flatline. The pulp appetite has no entry point: there is no complication introduced, no clock, no attempt that fails, no cost attached to a decision.

Proposed recast: Taylor must make at least one active move that fails or costs her — attempt to push a raven further than she can hold and lose it, try to reach the upstream disturbance and hit a wall (nosebleed that stops her, not just accompanies her), send a rat toward Harrenhal and have it return wrong. Give the chapter a failed attempt so the inertia reads as defeat rather than waiting. The waiting-and-feeling structure needs one board event or it is not a chapter, it is a threshold scene that belongs attached to chapter 02 or 04.

### Chapter 02 (TOLERATED — address or justify)

Problem: Plumm marks the ledger across eight separate proto-lines (lines 16, 22, 27, 45, 46, 47, 48) while Taylor does not escalate. The parallel cut is structurally sound but the Plumm side runs too long without a counter-beat. The chapter's board moves in one direction only.

Proposed fix: Condense Plumm's ledger-marking sequences — the four consecutive marks at lines 45–48 (marks entry, marks description, marks location, marks date) can collapse to two without losing the beat. The recovered space should go to Taylor attempting a counter-action that fails or costs her (she tries to recall the raven following Plumm and it won't respond cleanly, costing her a nosebleed earlier than ch03). This connects the chapters and earns the ch03 cost.

### Chapter 05 Interlude (TOLERATED — address or trim)

Problem: The stylus-lifting and page-touching procedure consumes too much of the chapter — Plumm writes, lifts, touches, writes, lifts, numbers, writes again across 15+ lines. The irony (Rowan's good-faith move documents Taylor) is real but paced as bureaucratic transcription rather than trap closing.

Proposed fix: Compress the writing sequence by at least half. The entry beats (lines 47–55: uncaps, touches, writes name, lifts, speaks, touches, writes sept entry, lifts, numbers) can be reduced to: writes the name, speaks, writes the sept entry, numbers the entry — four lines. The chapter's irony lands in the closing (septon drops eyes at line 88) and does not need the full notation sequence to support it.
