The chapter's middle section sags.

The fix is not to remove an iteration. The fix is to give one of the three windows a beat that changes the reader's understanding of the situation — not just Taylor trying again harder, but something about the hedgerow or the tree line that tells the dark-fantasy-reader what is wrong upstream. W4 is the candidate: the body-cost retreat could carry a beat of realization or dread that engages rather than merely accumulates.

### F2 — Gate 1 partial failure: ambiguous physical-hold SVO

"Holds the feet / holds the chin / holds the hands" appears four times across the chapter (W4, W7, W10) without a resolvable referent from SVO alone. The dark-fantasy-reader persona reads SVO bones as the stripped load-bearing action: if the action's subject and object are unclear from the bone, the window loses comprehensibility. Three of the four instances are Taylor holding a body part — possibly her own, possibly Septon Rowan's in W7, possibly the loft-ladder in W10. The bones do not say.

This is not a FULL G1 failure because the surrounding context suggests damage-register self-posture in most instances. But it is a recurring ambiguity that compounds across the chapter. "Holds the feet" and "holds the chin" need SVO clarification — or the prose layer will need to make the referent clear immediately.

### F3 — No BORED windows; W6 and W8 are strongly ENGAGED

"The raven drops" (W6) and "blood reaches the lip" (W8) are the chapter's two strongest moments. Both are violence-with-aftermath in the correct Planetos register: cost made visible, the attempt failing harder than before. The chapter's peaks are correctly placed in its second half. The cost structure escalates correctly across the three iterations.

### F4 — Septon Rowan's breath-draw is a weak close for the disclosure

W10 closes on septon-rowan draws the breath. This is intake before response — reaction without content. For the dark-fantasy-reader, this is a legitimate choice if the chapter is designed to close before resolution. But "holds the feet" following the exit rather than any consequence-signal from the disclosure means the chapter closes on ambiguous posture rather than on the world having responded. The breath-draw promises a response that the chapter does not deliver. This is a structural flag, not a gate failure — but if the next chapter opens without a clear line from Rowan's response, the closure will read as deflation rather than deferral.
