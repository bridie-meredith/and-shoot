### Chapter 07 — The Refusal Logged

**ENGAGED**

The revision has resolved the prior TOLERATED verdict.

The bell strike / wall release compounding beat (IDs 97–98) is placed at the structural joint between the cottage processing sequence and the sept movement. Before the revision, that gap was a pure cycling digest: she moved through positions (chair, document, exhale, yard, wall, sept, kneels, rises, doorframe) with no external event arriving to break the interior register. Now the castle bell strikes while she is gripping the wall, and she releases the wall. The beat is small — it is one external event — but it does what the prior report required: the machine is marking time in institutional units while she is still processing the previous move. The grip and release are no longer self-generated interior actions; they have a trigger. The chapter's final image (Plumm takes the claim document, line 93) arrives immediately after, which means the bell beat is also a temporal marker — she releases the wall, the chapter's last external action completes, and she crosses to the doorframe knowing it has already happened.

The cottage sequence (lines 52–66) is still seven lines before the bell arrives. This is acceptable: it is not three iterations of the same sensation, it is one document-turn beat with words around it. A dark fantasy reader absorbs one digest pass; what they will not absorb is repeated cycling. The bell beat breaks the cycle before it can establish.

Prior complaint addressed. Verdict upgraded.
