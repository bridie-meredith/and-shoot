## Findings

**Finding 1 — Gate 2 breach: W3–W5 inert stretch (lines 9–29)**
The cottage sequence (Rowan reads the ledger) and the deliberation sequence (chancel + road) are SVO-structurally sound but mechanically inert at the bone level. The bones record procedure without resistance. The ledger reading needs a physical friction signal in the SVO layer — not what Rowan thinks (that is a facet) but a physical act that signals cost. The chancel sequence at single-action density (kneels, then takes road) is too compressed for a deliberation beat that the chapter depends on. Recommended repair: add one friction-bearing SVO line to the cottage sequence (a physical tell, a pause before the close, anything that registers in the body) and one to the chancel sequence before the road departure.

**Finding 2 — Gate 3 breach: Line 91 comprehensibility failure (W7)**
Line 91 is numbered out of sequence (chapter runs 1–88 with 91 appearing mid-chapter between the Plumm encounter set-up and the conversation body). From the bones alone it is impossible to determine which record book is being touched or where in the scene timeline this occurs. This is a bone-level comprehensibility failure, not a facet gap. The line must be renumbered to a position that gives it sequential context, or its placement must be clarified with a structural note. The four consecutive time-skips (37–40) following it compound the problem — the dead zone after a comprehensibility failure leaves the reader with nothing to anchor on before the next action.

**Finding 3 — Time-skip density in the Plumm encounter (lines 35–52)**
The record-book sequence (W8–W9) has the highest time-skip density in the chapter. Between line 41 (Rowan speaks) and line 56 (Plumm speaks to Rowan), there are seven time-skips against six action beats. The chapter's most consequential action — Taylor's name entering Plumm's record — is compressed between gaps that flatten rather than dramatize the sequence. This does not breach a hard gate but it is a structural note for the Pass 3 dramatist: the bones are underweight for this section's load.

**Finding 4 — Double eye-drop at close (lines 87–88)**
Lines 87 (septon-rowan lowers the eyes) and 88 (septon-rowan drops the eyes) perform the same physical action twice in sequence. This is a minor bone-level redundancy — lower and drop are near-synonyms in this context. One of these should be cut. The chapter's final physical beat should be singular and clean.

**Finding 5 — No canon-mechanical flags**
This is a Rowan-narrator interlude. Taylor's power mechanics are not on stage. No power-mechanics errors, no lore leaks, no voice-register violations detectable at the SVO level. The one canon-mechanical watch item (Taylor's fauna network) is not present in the bones and does not need to be — this chapter is correctly bracketed as Rowan's POV. The interlude flag in the header is correct and earns tolerance for Taylor's absence from the mechanical architecture.
