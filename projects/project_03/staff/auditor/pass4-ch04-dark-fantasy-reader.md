## Findings

**What this chapter earns:**

The chapter's structural spine — wrongness through NPC physical response rather than labeled threat — is executed correctly at the bone level. The castellan stepping back and retreating from the raven's gaze (windows 12–13) is the highest-risk beat in the episode plan and the bones support it. The institutional recording-while-wrong-is-still-present (window 15) is the most distinctively Planetos-register sequence in the chapter.

The cottage door sequence (window 7) is structurally sound — four sensory channels across a sealed barrier, each different, no redundancy. This is the chapter's subtlest construction and it earns its place.

The raven-clamp (window 14) is the correct execution of power-as-uncertain-force: Taylor signals for it to end and the bird decides when it ends.

**Where execution carries the most risk:**

Windows 9–10 (the four-exchange dialogue pre-satchel-open plus the document preparation) are the most execution-dependent bones in the chapter. The bones are correct-flavored but carry no pull on their own. If the prose lines in these windows don't have specific Planetos texture in the dialogue — something in how the castellan speaks that signals institutional authority without menace, something in Taylor's responses that signals restraint-as-survival — these two windows can slide toward procedural drag. This is not a bone failure; it is a flag for the shoot execution.

Windows 16–17 (document exchange and final two-exchange after pocketing) carry the same risk. The bones are correct but the ending of a confrontation scene needs the prose to do the work the bones cannot: these exchanges happen in the shadow of the pocketed record, and the reader needs to feel that shadow.

**Pattern flags carried forward:**

- The castellan's physical retreat (window 12) sets the correct-register baseline for every subsequent NPC encounter with the raven. Any future inspector or authority figure who reacts with curiosity or clinical interest rather than physical withdrawal is flagged against this baseline.
- The raven-clamp (window 14) establishes that Taylor's control is not clean. Any subsequent scene where she ends a raven interaction on her own terms without resistance is a flag.
- The pocketed document (window 17) is now a live record. The chapter closes with it in Plumm's pocket. Any future scene where this record is treated as lost or forgotten is a continuity and register flag.
