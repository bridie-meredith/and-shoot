# Census Officer — Hightower Administrative Functionary (ch-01 warehouse alias)

## Description

Crown census-taker operating the Harrenhal district impressment circuit under Lord Corwyn Hatch (castellan, Hightower-affiliated). Mid-level administrative functionary. Role: `crown-census-taker`. Affiliation: `crown-administration` / Hightower network. Not load-bearing across episodes — walk-on procedural authority figure for chapter-01.

Full characterization lives on the base card `census-officer`. This card exists to satisfy the `oc-*` slug pattern required by the state-updates facet and Pass 5 continuity audit.

## Voice

Flat procedural register. He processes transactions, not conversations. States rules — does not argue them. Does not raise his voice. Does not apologize. Clerical, non-confrontational, non-hostile. The form is the answer; the answer is final.

## Costume and Identifying Props

- Administrative livery in Hightower-affiliated colors; functional traveling clothes worn for several weeks on circuit in Riverlands mud
- Carries a **census scroll** (rolled parchment, partially filled; primary authority object)
- Carries a **quill** and an **ink pot** (in a writing case or held by the accompanying clerk)
- Accompanied by **the men-at-arms** (two to three armed men, standing at the periphery; present but not engaged unless compliance fails)
- Accompanied by the clerk (holds portable writing board; transcribes dictation)

## Behavior Signature

Clerical. Procedural. Non-confrontational. He follows the forms; the forms have no box for appeals, sympathy, or exceptional circumstances. His efficiency is the threat — not cruelty or hostility.

- Refusals are stated as rules, not personal decisions
- Dictation to the clerk is the action (the transcription makes it real)
- The return of a document is the refusal; no further argument is required
- Departure — mounts, moves to the next stop — closes the scene

## Hard Fences

- Does not deviate from the forms
- Is not malicious; takes no pleasure in refusals
- Does not engage with census subjects at a personal level
- Never becomes hostile or raises his voice; the authority behind him makes that unnecessary
