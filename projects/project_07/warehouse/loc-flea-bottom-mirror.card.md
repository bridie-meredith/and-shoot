# Flea Bottom — Mirror Variant (Flicker-Physics, Witch-Reputation Project)

**Base card:** `cards/locations/loc-flea-bottom.card.md` — geography, layout, sensory vocabulary, and exit data from the base card are structurally inherited. This card records only what differs for this project's physics and Taylor's position within the district.

---

## Geography

Inherited from `loc-flea-bottom`. The district's physical extent, borders, and orientation are unchanged: southern slope between Fish Gate and Street of Steel, running toward the harbor-adjacent lowland. The Fish Gate margin, the market-side junction, the eastern quarter boundary — all apply.

**Flicker-cost topology (project-specific):** Distance to the Dragonpit is the operative pressure gradient in this project, not insect-network radius. The Dragonpit sits on Rhaenys's Hill, northeast of the district. Walking north and east from the center of Flea Bottom toward the hill puts a person in the Dragonpit's sensory catchment well before they reach the approach road. Taylor has learned — by involuntary experience, not by choice — that the flicker costs more as the Dragonpit approach gradient steepens. The district's eastern quarter boundary is the edge of this gradient's lowest register. The Fish Gate margin is insulated; the approach to the Street of Steel is the transition zone.

## Layout

Inherited from `loc-flea-bottom`. Dense irregular alley network, overhanging upper stories, perpetual low light at street level.

**Social geography as Taylor reads it (project-specific):** Without an insect network, Taylor maps the district through behavioral observation. Key social nodes she has identified:

- **The water-carrier routes:** The water carriers move on fixed schedules between the public wells and the housing clusters. Their deviations from schedule signal disruption before anyone says anything. Taylor watches their routes the way she once used highway-ant scouts.
- **The evening cooking-fire pattern:** The specific distribution of cooking smoke in the early evening marks which households are solvent and which are not. The gap-households — no smoke — are the most vulnerable and most useful (information flows from desperation).
- **The Watch's non-patrol corridor:** The City Watch avoids two specific alleys near the market-side junction. Taylor has no confirmed reason for this avoidance; she tracks it as a structural irregularity worth understanding. The alleys in question are also the shortest route from the Fish Gate toward the Dragonpit approach.
- **The witch-reputation radius:** As of story-open, Taylor's reputation as a woman who knows things she should not has reached approximately the same spatial footprint as the original insect-network radius would have — roughly 300m centered on her lodging. Within this radius, people adjust their behavior in her presence (subtle, not theatrical). Outsiders who arrive asking about her are redirected by district residents without being told they are being redirected. She has become, functionally, a recognized element of the district's information infrastructure, which is both useful and exposed.

## Sensory Vocabulary

Inherited from `loc-flea-bottom` — smell compound, sound registers, visual darkness at street level, textural vocabulary.

**Additions for flicker-cost register:**

- **The Dragonpit-direction smell:** On certain wind conditions from the northeast, the Dragonpit's ambient exhalations reach Flea Bottom's upper alleys as a faint animal-char note underneath the rendering smell. Taylor has learned to register this smell as a flicker-pressure increase. The smell itself does not cause the flicker; the flicker's cost increases when this smell is present, because the proximity the smell signals is real.
- **The street-level tonal shift near story-open:** Flea Bottom is already aware that something is changing in the city's factional atmosphere. The sounds of the district carry a different register than they did two weeks prior — more argument, shorter tempers, the specific cadence of people who have received bad news and have not yet decided what to do with it. Taylor reads this as pre-fracture pattern-noise. She has been here long enough to have a baseline.

None card-level.

## Exits

Inherited from `loc-flea-bottom` — Fish Gate (south), Street of Steel (north), eastern quarter (east), Muddy Way (west).

**Project-specific exit note:** The northeastern route from the market-side junction — up through the eastern quarter boundary and toward Rhaenys's Hill — is the flicker-approach corridor. Taylor has walked it twice, involuntarily, following flicker-pull that she did not recognize as directional until after. She has not walked it a third time by choice. The route from Fish Gate margin northeast toward the Dragonpit is approximately forty-five minutes on foot for a person walking at Flea Bottom's habitual unhurried pace; faster for someone who is not trying to appear unhurried.

## Hazards

**Witch-reputation escalation:** The reputation is currently useful. It is also a trajectory. As it grows, it will attract institutional attention — specifically from the Faith's network, which has begun establishing the witch-label as a formal category within KL's social control apparatus. Taylor does not know the institutional timeline; she knows the social pressure is directional.

**Flicker-cost visibility:** When the flicker fires in the district, Taylor reacts — involuntarily, physically, in whatever way the glimpse demands. She has not learned to suppress the reaction entirely. District residents have learned to interpret the reaction as confirmation of her witch-nature. A severe episode near a witness with Faith connections would be reportable.

**Faction probe-interest:** The Black faction knows Taylor is in the district. The patron's initial contact with Taylor originated from intelligence that named her lodging sector. She does not know the extent of what was reported or what is still being observed.

**City Watch irregular corridor:** The two alleys the Watch avoids are adjacent to Taylor's own preferred route to the eastern quarter. She does not know if this is coincidence or structural (Watch informant placement, existing criminal jurisdiction agreement, or something older). The irregularity is a hazard because she cannot model it.

## Ambient Interruption Hooks

- A water-carrier route deviation signals disruption before Taylor can identify the cause
- The Dragonpit-direction smell arriving at the wrong time of day — wind shift, or something more deliberate in the hill's direction
- A district resident redirecting a stranger asking about the witch without Taylor being present to observe — the reputation operating independently of her
- A Faith-adjacent person (septon's errand-runner, lay brother doing charitable rounds) entering the district and behaving with the specific caution of someone gathering information rather than performing charity
- The Watch non-patrol corridor suddenly active — a Watch pair moving through an alley they have never entered before, for reasons Taylor cannot immediately identify
- A flicker episode in an alley with a witness who has both the language for what they saw and a connection to someone who would pay for that language
- The market-side junction going quiet in the specific way that precedes someone important arriving — not City Watch, not a regular merchant, someone the district reads as requiring a different posture
- A fire in the rendering yards (inherited hook — Taylor's Gold-Morning filter makes fire the most costly ambient interruption in the district)
