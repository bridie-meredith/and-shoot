# Flea Bottom Social Physics — District-Level Information, Labor, and Access (~125 AC)

## Description

The specific social physics of Flea Bottom as a King's Landing district: information velocity, who controls the labor web, the Watch's periodic relationship with the district, where Taylor falls in the existing taxonomy of strange-people, and the access constraints that govern movement from Flea Bottom to other parts of the city. This card is the district-specific layer beneath `cond-smallfolk-political-physics` — the general internalized-subjugation physics are the substrate; this card is what those physics look like in Flea Bottom's specific environment.

## Sensory Impact

Flea Bottom's social physics are felt in texture before they are understood analytically. The density of housing produces a specific acoustic quality — sound carries horizontally through shared walls, and a conversation at one end of a shared tenement is known at the other end by morning. The rendering yards and fishguts-processing make the district identifiable by smell before you enter it. The shared water-carrier points (stone cisterns, pump handles, the Flea-Bottom-adjacent wells) are the primary social exchange nodes — people are at them daily, and what is said at the water carrier is the district's fastest information channel.

Taylor reads this texture as a network topology. She cannot not see it. What she learns is which nodes are high-velocity (the water carriers, the corner where the labor brokers set up before dawn), which are high-trust (the apothecary, the women who manage the rendering-yard feeding schedule), and which are high-connectivity (the figures who move between multiple nodes — the carter, the night-soil collector, the girl who runs messages between households).

## Duration

Persistent throughout the project. Flea Bottom's social physics are the stable substrate of Taylor's operating environment.

## Labor-Web Structure

Flea Bottom's primary economy is physical labor — rendering, processing, hauling, the small-craft trades at the district's edges (ropework, net-mending, the low end of the tanning trade). The labor web is not employer-employee in any formal sense; it is brokered informally through several mechanisms:

**Labor brokers**: Individuals (usually men, but not exclusively) who know who needs workers on a given day and who needs work. They operate at fixed corners in the pre-dawn hours. Getting work on a given day requires either knowing a broker or having a reputation in the network. Taylor's early weeks involve learning this system and inserting herself into it as a reliable day-laborer.

**Obligation-based labor**: The informal credit economy described in `cond-smallfolk-political-physics` applies here. A neighbor who owes you watches your space while you work longer hours; a household that has received surplus rendering-scraps owes labor at your busiest time. This web of obligation is the district's insurance system.

**Trade-specific cohorts**: Workers in the same trade — all the rendering-yard workers, all the net-menders at the waterfront end of the district — form loose cohorts with their own information velocity. What happens at one rendering yard is known at the other within a day. These cohorts are not guilds; they have no formal organization. But they function as information channels and informal labor-allocation mechanisms.

**Taylor's position in the labor web**: Weeks in, Taylor has enough of a footing to get work through a broker and to have accumulated some informal obligations. She is not yet integrated into the deeper cohort networks — she is still a stranger (visibly foreign, not from any known KL background) but a stranger who shows up and works. This gives her access to labor-web information at the surface level without the deeper-trust access that would require longer presence or a known-family connection.

## Information Velocity

Flea Bottom's information moves faster than any individual event. The mechanisms:

**The water-carrier network**: The shared cisterns and wells are the district's primary information exchange. The women and children who make multiple trips daily are the most reliable high-velocity channel. A notable event before dawn is at the cisterns by mid-morning. Taylor learns to calibrate: what is said at the cisterns is the district's current state; what was said at the cisterns three days ago is already settled narrative.

**The rendering-yard shift-change**: At shift change, workers from the incoming and outgoing shifts overlap for a short period. This overlap is an information node — what the outgoing shift observed during their hours moves to the incoming shift in concentrated form. Taylor works to position herself at or near this exchange when she can.

**The apothecary as trust-weighted node**: The apothecary's assistant (or the apothecary themselves) is a high-trust information node — people bring their physical problems to the apothecary and talk while they wait. Information at the apothecary has a different quality from information at the cisterns: more specific, more personal, often more accurate because the teller has more reason to be precise about cause and effect. Access to this node requires established trust that Taylor does not have immediately.

**Message runners**: Flea Bottom has a loose economy of children and young teenagers who run messages between households for small coin or food. These runners are the district's connective tissue for information that needs to travel from one specific location to another without going through the general network. They are observable, and a person who watches the message-runner traffic can learn something about which households are currently in communication.

**Information velocity from Flea Bottom to outside**: Information that travels from Flea Bottom to the broader city travels primarily through two channels: the labor brokers who interact with factors from the noble quarter (a high-value channel because factors pay for useful information), and the Watch informants whose identity is not publicly known but whose existence is widely understood. Neither channel is fast in the way the internal network is fast; both involve deliberate decisions to pass information upward.

## The Watch's Relationship to Flea Bottom

(See also `cond-kl-feudal-physics-mirror` for the broader Watch jurisdiction framework.)

**Watch patrol pattern**: The Watch does not patrol Flea Bottom on a regular schedule visible to district residents. Watch presence is event-triggered: a report of violence above the minor threshold, a fire, a complaint from a higher-status neighbor, or a directed sweep (when Watch command has a specific person or category to look for). The district does not experience the Watch as a constant presence; it experiences the Watch as periodic incursion.

**What Watch visits to Flea Bottom look like**: When the Watch enters Flea Bottom on a sweep, the information moves through the district faster than the Watch can walk. The Watch knows this and relies on surprise (entering multiple points simultaneously) for most effective sweeps. But surprise is hard to maintain in Flea Bottom's acoustic environment.

**What the Watch is looking for in Flea Bottom**: Specific wanted persons, major violence that has created a Watch-incident record, or (under factional direction) persons of specific profile. The Watch is not looking for Taylor specifically, but a directed sweep with Taylor as the profile could reach her.

**The Watch and the witch-label**: A street-level witch-label in Flea Bottom does not trigger Watch interest on its own. The Watch cares about order, not superstition. A witch-label that has escalated to a formal complaint from a Faith institution, a noble household, or a Watch-adjacent administrative layer would trigger interest. The label alone is noise.

## Where Taylor Falls in the Existing Strange-People Taxonomy

Flea Bottom has its own classification system for persons who do not fit the ordinary social mold. The taxonomy matters because it determines how much trouble Taylor's presence generates and what the community's default management strategy is:

**The touched**: Cognitively atypical persons — the very old with visible confusion, the visibly mentally ill, people who talk to themselves or to absent parties. The community management strategy is integration through tolerance: they are assigned tasks they can do, given space by people who know them, managed by a specific household who holds informal responsibility. Not a threat; a permanent feature of the community.

**The returned**: People who have been away (prison, conscript service, life in a different city) and are now back. They are strange because they have been changed by absence; they carry references the district no longer shares. Reintegration is possible; it takes time. They are not a threat; they are a project.

**The foreign strange**: People from elsewhere whose strangeness is marked by accent, appearance, or cultural practice that is visibly non-local. The district's attitude is transactional: if they work, pay, and don't cause trouble, they can stay. If their strangeness generates Watch attention or threatens the district's semi-autonomous equilibrium, they become a problem.

**The knowing strange**: People who have knowledge that exceeds their apparent background — who seem to know things they should not. This category has the most witch-adjacent resonance. The knowing strange may be tolerated (if their knowing is seen as a resource) or feared (if their knowing seems to cost others). Flea Bottom has a few long-term residents in this category: the old woman who knows plants and their properties, the former maester's apprentice who left the order. These figures are integrated through known history — the community knows them and manages them by knowing them.

**Taylor's position**: She enters as foreign strange (appearance without local origin — not "the foreign woman from Myr" but "the woman who appeared"). She accumulates knowing-strange characteristics over weeks (the flicker's behavioral expression produces the pattern the community reads as "knows things"). She is not yet in the knowing-strange category's integrated tier because she does not have the long-term local history that makes the knowing strange safe; she is in the threatening liminal position of being a new knowing-strange whose history the community cannot verify.

**The difference from the prior config**: In swarm-control projects, the witch-label formed around a visible event. Here, the community is categorizing a person they have observed for weeks, not a single event. This makes the categorization process slower, more ambivalent, and more reversible than event-triggered label formation — which also means it is more resilient to a single counter-example.

## Access Constraints From Flea Bottom to the Wider City

**Movement within the lower city**: Taylor can move through the lower commercial districts without institutional constraint under ordinary conditions. She is identifiable as a Flea Bottom resident by appearance and bearing but this is not itself a gating factor for the market districts.

**The Sept of Baelor outer courts**: Accessible without escort. The outer courts of the Sept are nominally open to all faithful, and there is no practical checkpoint for smallfolk approaching the outer courts on a normal day. This is where a neutral meeting between Taylor and patron-level figures can occur without triggering the noble-quarter access constraints.

**The Dragonpit approach**: There is a Watch checkpoint at the Dragonpit road under ordinary operation. A Flea Bottom woman approaching the Dragonpit without escort will be questioned. The answer "I have business with [patron's factor]" may or may not satisfy the Watch, depending on whether the factor is known to the checkpoint Watch. Escorted access (the patron's retainer present) passes the checkpoint without question.

**Noble-quarter townhouses**: Not accessible without escort or a factor intermediary explicitly vouching for the visit. The noble-quarter Watch deployment is tighter than the lower city; an unescorted person who does not have recognized business will be stopped and questioned before reaching any household's gate.

**The patron's premises**: Whatever premises the patron uses for the consultation meetings are accessible to Taylor through the patron's own retainer escort. The patron does not receive Taylor at the Red Keep or in any formal court setting; the patron reaches down to Taylor's terrain or to neutral ground (Sept outer courts, a market-district factor house). This is the standard mode of operation for smallfolk-patron relationships.
