# Series Tone Mirror — Two-Register Architecture (Binding)

## Description

The binding tone architecture for this project. Two registers, structurally distinct, each with its own rules. Body: grim-literary close-third Taylor — compressed, tactical, emotionally honest but not sentimental. Coda: cold institutional chronicler — third-person, past-tense, archival, carrying the Citadel's documentation weight. The register-break is the irony mechanism, not a stylistic flourish. This card governs what each register can and cannot do, what is prohibited across both registers, and the hard structural requirements for the closing sequence.

**Source authority**: `active-project/staff/showrunner/world-notes.md` — all rules carried from `[1b]` and `[audience-triad]` are binding.

## Sensory Impact

Tone is not a sensory condition. Its expression is felt in the quality of language, sentence rhythm, and narrative distance — the way a scene registers as close or cold, tactical or archival. Agents working in the body register must maintain the grim-literary close-third throughout; agents working in the coda register must arrive at the cold-institutional without any residual warmth from the body.

## Duration

Persistent and structurally mandatory. The two-register architecture is not a default that can be varied for effect — it is the architecture the entire project depends on for its irony mechanism.

## The Body Register

**What the body register is:**

Grim-literary, close-third Taylor, exclusive. Taylor's interiority is the lens. The reader is in Taylor's perception and cognition at all times.

*From world-notes.md (binding):* "Body grim-literary, in Taylor's voice — compressed, tactical, emotionally honest but not sentimental."

**Compressed**: Sentences carry weight in proportion to their length. No sentence goes on longer than it needs to. The compression is a function of Taylor's tactical cognition — she does not editorialize; she observes, processes, acts. The prose reflects this.

**Tactical**: Taylor's close-third voice organizes experience into legible threat-and-resource maps. She reads people, spaces, and events as a military planner reads terrain. This is not authorial imposition; it is how Taylor's cognition actually works after two years of cape escalation and one apocalypse.

**Emotionally honest but not sentimental**: Taylor has emotions. They are present and accurately rendered. What is absent is dwelling — she does not let the narrative sit in feeling for its own sake; she moves through it. The emotional register is honest; the prose is not warmed by it.

**The Taylor-POV principle**: The body's close-third Taylor does not editorialize about her own choices. The narrative does not condemn or endorse from above the character's perspective. *From world-notes.md (binding):* "Narrative does NOT condemn in authorial voice and does NOT endorse her reasoning." The reader receives Taylor's reasoning fully and fairly; the reader's evaluation is their own. The irony is structural, not voiced.

## The Coda Register

**What the coda register is:**

A hard register-break. Different type-treatment recommended (indentation change, font change, or a clear break marker). Third-person, past-tense, archival. The voice of a maester-chronicler writing for the Citadel's record — institutional, cold, factually precise.

*From world-notes.md (binding):* "Chronicler appears ONLY as the closing coda — a hard register-break in different voice. The register-break IS the irony mechanism: body = Taylor's interpretation; coda = history's verdict."

**The coda's institutional weight**: The chronicler does not interpret Taylor. The chronicler records facts and consequences in the archival mode — what happened, what followed, what the historical record shows. The coda is not the author inserting moral judgment; it is the maester's parchment arriving in the story's final pages.

**What the coda must contain** (both requirements are hard structural requirements, not suggestions):

1. **The explicit counterfactual**: *From world-notes.md (binding):* "The coda MUST name the opposite-number's counterfactual. Not imply it — name it. 'Had [opposite-number] completed the bond, [outcome would have been].'" The chronicler names the path not taken in the declarative archival mode. This is the structural load-bearer of the moral register — the moment the irony becomes explicit, named, and irrefutable.

2. **The closing-image ordering**: *From world-notes.md (binding):* "Closing image = TWO BEATS in fixed order. (1) Chronicler entry first, explicitly naming the opposite-number's counterfactual. (2) Cost-bearer's (cousin's) death as the final image." The reader exits on the cost-bearer's death, having just had the counterfactual named. The ordering is a hard requirement. *"Reversal or omission of explicit counterfactual = automatic revise."*

**The coda does not mourn**: The chronicler's archival register does not grieve for the cost-bearer. The cold rendering of the cousin's death is the register doing its work — the reader supplies the pathos because the body has done its job. *From world-notes.md (binding):* "Death lands cold; the chronicler-frame did the moral work."

## Prohibited Registers

**Prohibited in the body:**

- Multi-POV: The body is Taylor exclusively. No other character's interiority is accessed during the body of the narrative. *From world-notes.md (binding):* "POV = close-third Taylor EXCLUSIVE in the body. No multi-POV; no frame-narration in body."
- Sentimentality: The body's close-third does not dwell in feeling. Emotional honesty is present; sentimentality (dwelling in feeling for its own sake, softening the register with warmth that the character's actual state would not produce) is prohibited.
- Authorial editorialization: No narrative voice above Taylor's layer weighs in on her choices. The irony emerges from structure and outcome, not from a narrator saying "she was wrong."

**Prohibited in the coda:**

- Warmth: The chronicler does not mourn, regret, or editorialize sympathetically. *From world-notes.md (binding):* "Warmth in the coda" is prohibited.
- Ambiguity in the counterfactual: The explicit naming is required. An implied counterfactual, a "one might argue that," a hedged phrasing — these are violations. The chronicler states the counterfactual in the archival declarative.

**Prohibited in both registers:**

- Melodrama at the bad act: *From world-notes.md (binding):* "The kill is rendered cold; the horror is in the cause-chain, not in visceral description." The bad act is not staged for maximum visceral impact. It is rendered at the register that the body's close-third Taylor would actually produce — which is not melodramatic, because Taylor has killed before, believes this is right, and her interiority in that moment is tactical, not horrified.

## The Register-Break as Structural Requirement

The register-break must be earned. *From world-notes.md (binding):* "The two-register architecture must EARN ITSELF. The coda register-break is NOT a tonal shift — it IS the chronicler voice arriving with the counterfactual the body could not yet name."

This means:

- The body must be strong enough that the coda's arrival is a distinct transition, not a gradual fade
- The body's close-third Taylor must be fully committed — no narrative voice above the character level has leaked in to prepare the reader for the coda
- The coda's arrival at the closing pages is the first time the reader has heard this register; it must feel like a different document arriving, not a different paragraph in the same document

The break is the story's structural irony engine: the body gives us Taylor's interpretation; the coda gives us history's verdict. The gap between the two is where the tragedy lives.

## Single-Book Scope Implications

This project is novel-scale (single closed arc, not episodic). The frequency-band and tensometer gates apply at chapter and book level, not episode level. The two-register architecture is sustained across the entire novel length, not reset at episode boundaries. The coda is a single arrival at the book's end, not a recurring register.

**Tonal exemptions**: Because the body must sustain the grim-literary register across novel length, minor tonal variance within the register (the specific quality of close-third prose in quieter scenes versus in action sequences) is permitted as long as the register's core characteristics (compression, tactical, not sentimental) are maintained. What is not permitted is register collapse — scenes that drift toward warm, soft, or omniscient authorial voice.

## Interaction Notes

**With `cond-flicker-discipline-mirror`:** The body's close-third Taylor renders the flicker's arrival and Taylor's interpretive process from the inside. The register must not give the reader more certainty about the flicker's accuracy than Taylor has — this is a joint requirement of the tone rules and the flicker-discipline rules.

**With Taylor's persona card**: The body register is a function of Taylor's actual voice and cognition — grim-literary is not an authorial imposition; it is accurate representation. The persona card's voice section should be consistent with this.

**For auditor use:** Any body section containing a POV that is not Taylor's close-third is a violation. Any body section where the narrative voice is warmer or more sentimental than Taylor's actual emotional state warrants is a violation. Any coda that does not explicitly name the opposite-number's counterfactual is a hard structural violation requiring automatic revise. Any coda that renders the cost-bearer's death before naming the counterfactual (reversal of the ordered beats) is a hard structural violation. Any scene rendering the bad act melodramatically (visceral horror-staging at the kill moment) is a violation.
