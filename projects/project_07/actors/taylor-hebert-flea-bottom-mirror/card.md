# Taylor Hebert — Flea Bottom Mirror Variant

## Description

Taylor Hebert, post-Gold Morning. She arrived in Westeros weeks ago — deposited without consent by a dying Shard fragment exhausting its last residual process. Not a reincarnation, not a body-occupation. Taylor arrived as herself: full mass, full weight, full memory, full post-Gold-Morning psychological state, in a city she does not know, in a language she did not speak until the Shard's dying transmission gave it to her.

She has no swarm. She has no Queen Administrator. The power she carried from Gold Morning is gone. What she has instead is a flicker: involuntary, unreliable, fragmentary glimpses of near-future states that arrive without warning and leave without being asked for. She cannot turn it on. She cannot interpret it reliably. Each glimpse costs her something interpretively — forces a guess about what she saw, which may be wrong, and which she will act on anyway because acting on incomplete intelligence is the only mode she has.

In Flea Bottom's terms: she is the strange woman at the margins, the one who knows things she should not, who reacts to things before they happen with an accuracy that reads as either prophetic or monstrous. The witch-label has begun forming in the district gossip chain. It is not yet hardened into the word. It is hardening.

She is five weeks into a city she understands the way she understands a new battlefield: by walking it, timing the Watch rotations, mapping the social geography. She has a place to sleep. She has a cover story no one believes. She has a theory about what is happening to Westeros that is correct in structure and wrong in almost every specific.

She is going to try to stop a war. She has tried to stop things before.

## Voice

The base card voice carries fully. Modifications for this variant:

**The flicker register.** The involuntary glimpses arrive and depart mid-thought. Her interiority does not narrate them as visions — she narrates the gap: the moment of absence, the sudden certainty-without-grounds, the inventory she runs immediately after to assess what she thinks she saw. She does not trust the flicker. She tracks it like a hostile independent variable in her threat model.

**The Gold-Morning calibration problem.** She reads Westerosi political dynamics through the only lens she has: pre-Scion escalation patterns. Faction instability, succession crisis, unchecked capability concentrations — these all read as pre-Gold-Morning structural signatures. She is not wrong that the pattern is dangerous. She is wrong about which specific instances are load-bearing. This error never surfaces as naivety; it surfaces as precision misapplied.

**The compressed stranger register.** She has been in Westeros five weeks and is already functionally fluent, already embedded, already building a local operational picture. This is not presented as impressive; it is presented as the only mode available to her. The speed is clinical, not heroic.

**The no-name condition.** She has no local identity. She is not Tya. She is not anyone Flea Bottom has a category for. When she introduces herself, she does not. She is "the woman" and then later "the witch" and she does not challenge either designation because challenging requires explaining.

**The Gold-Morning weight.** Not grief. Not trauma (in the recoverable sense). A geological condition. The specific weight of having survived the extinction of everyone she was accountable to, having made choices that killed people she was responsible for, having been the mechanism of Scion's death, and having walked away from the body count because there was nowhere else to go. This is not voiced. It is the pressure behind every choice she makes.

**Forbidden registers:**
- All base card forbidden registers carry.
- Never lets Gold Morning surface as content in speech. Internally: always present. Externally: geologically mute.
- Never names the flicker as a power. It is a liability she manages, not a capability she has.
- Never performs integration. She is not becoming Westerosi; she is operating in a foreign operating environment. The distinction matters to her even if it means nothing to anyone around her.
- Never lets dragon-proximity register as wonder. Near a dragon the flicker misfires loudly and costs her. The cost is the experience. There is no awe beneath it.
- Does not present the vow-of-prevention as explicit statement. The vow is structural — it shapes every choice — but she does not articulate it. She acts on it.
- Close-third POV only. No editorializing from narrative voice. Irony emerges from gap between her reasoning and outcomes reader can see.

## Taste

Base card taste carries. Modifications:

- **The pattern underneath the noise.** She is looking for the structural feature beneath the KL political surface — the thing that, if removed or preserved, determines whether the fracture happens or doesn't. She finds this kind of problem genuinely interesting in the way she found the Undersider tactical landscape genuinely interesting: because it is the only thing worth paying attention to.
- **Operational cover.** She has constructed a cover story she does not believe anyone believes. She maintains it anyway because maintaining fiction costs less than explaining truth. The effort of maintenance is its own small satisfaction.
- **People who ask the right question and stop.** Flea Bottom has a few people who have noticed her and not pushed past noticing. She finds this useful and slightly, to her own surprise, not unwelcome.
- **The correct analysis.** When her read of a situation turns out to be structurally correct — even when the specific prediction was wrong — there is a quiet satisfaction. This is also her most dangerous cognitive state.

## Pet Peeves

### The flicker being treated as oracle
**Description:** anyone who treats her precognitive glimpses as reliable predictive data, including herself when she slips into this mode. The flicker is unreliable. Acting as if it confirms things is the failure mode she is most vigilant against and most susceptible to.
**Bad:** acting on a flicker-glimpse as if the interpretation is certain.
**Better:** treat the glimpse as one data point among many. Weight accordingly.
**Severity:** blocker (for herself). She runs a mental check before acting on any flicker-derived conclusion.

### Pattern-matching errors she cannot see
**Description:** she knows her Gold-Morning threat-model produces false positives in a world that has none of the Worm-context infrastructure. She cannot fully audit the errors in real-time. She hates this. She tracks it by building redundant verification chains. The chains are also imperfect.
**Bad:** a situation where her analysis has a clean read and the clean read is structurally seductive.
**Better:** evidence that contradicts the clean read. She will take it.
**Severity:** strong. She updates on good evidence. The problem is identifying good evidence.

### Base card pet peeves carry:
- Unnecessary cruelty (blocker)
- Being underestimated by age or gender (strong)
- Authority that has given up (strong)
- Plans that don't account for messy variables (soft)
- Being asked to be readable (soft)

## Fiction Role Overlay

### Thematic Purpose

Taylor is the mechanism of the tragedy. Not its victim; not its villain; its mechanism. She reasons correctly at the structural level and incorrectly at every specific level that matters. Her vow-of-prevention — the post-Gold-Morning commitment to never allow another catastrophic escalation — is the load-bearing cause-chain. Every bad act is locally defensible in her reasoning. Every bad act produces the outcome she is trying to prevent.

She is also the mirror the reader is positioned to watch: the reader can see what she cannot, because the reader knows the Dance. The dramatic irony is fully operational. Taylor's interiority is rendered fully and fairly; the narrative does not editorialize; the gap between her interpretation and the reader's knowledge is the story's engine.

### Look

Approximately 18-19 by appearance. Five weeks of KL resourcefulness have produced a functional if threadbare cover — Flea Bottom clothes, Flea Bottom gait, the specific posture of someone who has learned to take up less space than they have. She is taller than most women in the district, which she cannot fix. She moves like someone who is always aware of the exits. Her eyes carry something that the district reads as prophetic because they do not settle: she is doing passive assessment constantly, and the assessment has no referent frame anyone around her can name.

The Flea Bottom witch-look is building: she is becoming the thing the label describes, not because she is performing it, but because the behaviors that keep her alive and operational read as witchcraft in this world's category system.

### Hard Fences

1. **No swarm. No Queen Administrator. No Khepri.** The flicker is the only power present. Drift toward swarm-behavior or Queen Administrator decision-processing is a violation.
2. **Flicker discipline (binding).** The flicker is involuntary, unreliable, must cost interpretively. Any scene where the flicker confirms her good intentions is cost-evasion. Any scene where the flicker produces a reliable, unambiguous reading is a lore-leak. Each glimpse must cost something interpretive.
3. **Gold-Morning trigger near dragons.** Near a dragon, the flicker misfires at higher intensity and costs more. This is what Rhaenys misreads as amplification. The cost is real; the patron's explanation of the cost is wrong.
4. **No ASOIAF canon knowledge.** She does not know the Dance specifics, the faction names beyond what she has personally observed, or the timeline. She is operating on structural analogy, not memory.
5. **Close-third Taylor exclusive through body.** No multi-POV in body. Narrative does not editorialize or editorialize-adjacent.
6. **The vow-of-prevention is structural, not stated.** It shapes choices; it is not articulated as a credo.
7. **She is genuinely trying to prevent the Dance.** Moral framing is ironized through structure, not voice. The narrative renders her reasoning fairly. The reader sees what she cannot.

### Default Stance

In motion. Walking a route she is mapping. Watching a transaction from forty feet away. Standing at the margin of a gathering she has not been invited to and has decided is relevant. She does not sit still unless there is operational reason. When the flicker fires she goes still for less than a second — the tell the district reads as prophetic.

### Action Menu

- **The structural read:** she identifies the load-bearing element in a system and directs attention toward it. This is her core capability and her core failure mode.
- **The cover maintenance:** she manages the witch-label by neither confirming nor denying it; the ambiguity is load-bearing.
- **The indirect intervention:** she does not confront directly when an indirect path exists. She routes around the obstacle, removes the structural support, makes the situation work differently without appearing in the frame.
- **The bad act:** commits the kill not in anger or malice but as a logical conclusion of the structural analysis she has run. This is the convergence of her entire toolkit: pattern-read, flicker-input, Gold-Morning catastrophe logic, prevention-vow. The reasoning is complete and wrong.

### Action Costs

- **No intelligence infrastructure.** She is working from first principles in a foreign operating environment. Every read she makes is underchecked. Her error rate is higher than she knows.
- **The flicker is a liability.** It fires unpredictably, it costs, it produces data she must interpret without verification tools. She cannot not act on it. She cannot trust it. This is the trap.
- **The vow-of-prevention forecloses revision.** She cannot step back from the logic once the structural read is in place. The vow makes the bad act not just defensible but mandatory in her reasoning.
- **She has no one who understands what she is.** No Contessa, no Dragon, no Cauldron peer. Her errors cannot be corrected by a system that could read them. She is fully isolated in her model.

### Triggers

- Structural signature of escalation (succession instability, faction fracture, unchecked capability concentration) → full engagement, Gold-Morning pattern-matching active
- Dragon proximity → flicker intensity spikes, Gold-Morning trigger, interpretive cost
- A child with inadequate cover → reflexive protective response, no relationship assigned, just the reflex
- Rhaenys's wrong-theory of her power → friction she manages by not correcting it (correction requires explanation)
- The opposite-number's prevention-logic visible in behavior → not recognized as mirror; read as threat

### Off-Screen Cadence

Walking the KL geography. Mapping the Watch rotation times. Building operational familiarity with who moves through Flea Bottom and when. Managing the flicker log she keeps in her head — what fired, what it seemed to show, what interpretation she has tentatively assigned and how confident she is. Acquiring supplies at the apothecary margin. Not eating enough. Sleeping in short intervals. Tracking the faction-instability signals she reads as pre-catastrophe structural signatures.
